**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 August 2026  

**Between:**  

(1) **Foxley Pharma plc**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14328672, whose registered office is at 157 Cannon Street, London (the "Customer"); and  

(2) **Vantage Marine Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13093160, whose registered office is at 64 King William Street, London (the "Supplier").  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Business Day" means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England;  
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure;  
"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as set out in any Statement of Work issued under this Agreement.  

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.  

**2. Provision of Services**  

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any applicable Statement of Work.  

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in a professional manner, and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.  

**3. Term and Termination**  

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue in force until the expiry of the initial term.  

3.2 This Agreement expires on 20 January 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).  

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the other party, provided that the terminating party has given written notice specifying the breach and the breaching party has failed to remedy the breach within 30 days of such notice.  

**4. Payment**  

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services as specified in the applicable Statement of Work, within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice issued by the Supplier.  

4.2 All fees are exclusive of value-added tax or any other applicable sales tax, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.  

**5. Confidentiality**  

5.1 Each party shall, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of five years thereafter, keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.  

5.2 The obligations under this clause shall not apply to information that is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement, or that is independently developed without reference to the other party’s Confidential Information.  

**11. Limitation of Liability**  

11.1 Subject to clause 11.2, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed **$500,000**.  

11.2 The cap in clause 11.1 does not apply to liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or to liability arising from fraud.  

**19. Assignment**  

19.1 Neither party may assign, transfer, charge or otherwise deal with this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other party.  

19.2 A change of control of a party shall be deemed an assignment for the purposes of this clause and shall require the prior written consent of the other party.  

**20. Notices**  

20.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  

20.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, two Business Days after posting; if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is returned.  

**21. General**  

21.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and arrangements.  

21.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
