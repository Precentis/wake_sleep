**DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT**

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Foxley Pharma plc ("Customer") and Vantage Marine Ltd ("Supplier") and forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 3 August 2026 between the parties.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Applicable Data Protection Law" means all applicable legislation in force from time to time relating to data protection and privacy, including but not limited to the Data Protection Act 2018 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), as retained and amended in the law of England and Wales.
- "Customer Personal Data" means any personal data (as defined under Applicable Data Protection Law) that is provided by or on behalf of the Customer to the Supplier, and which relates to employees, contractors, or agents of the Customer or its affiliates, including but not limited to names, contact details, identification numbers, and employment records.
- "Processing" shall have the meaning given in the Applicable Data Protection Law and includes any operation or set of operations performed upon Customer Personal Data, such as collection, recording, storage, use, or disclosure.
- "Security Breach" means any unauthorised or accidental access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of Customer Personal Data.

**2. Subject Matter and Duration**

2.1 This Agreement governs the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier in the course of providing managed IT services to the Customer under the Master Services Agreement.

2.2 This Agreement shall commence on the date of the Master Services Agreement and shall remain in force until the termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

**3. Obligations of the Supplier**

3.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, damage, or alteration. These measures shall be reviewed and evaluated at least every 12 months.

**4. Sub-processing**

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any third party to process Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 Any approved sub-processor shall be bound by enforceable contractual obligations providing data protection obligations at least equivalent to those set out in this Agreement.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

**6. Compliance with Data Protection Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

**7. Audits and Inspections**

7.1 Upon reasonable notice, the Customer may require the Supplier to permit audits or inspections of its data processing activities relevant to this Agreement, up to once per calendar year.

7.2 The Supplier shall cooperate fully with any such audit or inspection and shall provide all necessary information to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

**8. Data Subject Rights**

8.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing.

8.2 Assistance shall be provided at no additional cost to the Customer, provided that any requests are not manifestly unfounded or excessive.

**9. Security Breach Notification**

9.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay upon becoming aware of any Security Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

9.2 The Supplier shall provide details of the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, and the likely consequences, within 72 hours of awareness.

**10. Return or Deletion of Data**

10.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, the Supplier shall, at the Customer’s written election, either securely return or securely delete all Customer Personal Data.

10.2 The Supplier shall provide written certification of deletion upon request.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
