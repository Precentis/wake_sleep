SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE  
DATED 1 July 2026  
Between  
Foxley Pharma plc (the Customer)  
and  
Vantage Marine Ltd (the Supplier)  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Agreement” means the Master Services Agreement between the Customer and the Supplier dated 15 March 2024, together with all schedules and annexes thereto;  
(b) “Business Hours” means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
(c) “Monthly Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are accessible and operational, measured against total scheduled service hours;  
(d) “Monthly Fee” means the fixed fee of $65,000 payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services, as set out in Schedule 3 (Pricing) of the Agreement;  
(e) “Service Credit” means a credit applied against future invoices due from the Customer to the Supplier, calculated in accordance with Clause 4;  
(f) “Service Credit Cap” means the maximum aggregate Service Credits payable in any consecutive twelve-month period, limited to 50% of the total Monthly Fees paid during that period.  

2. Service Availability Commitment  
2.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Monthly Availability of the Managed IT Services is at least 99.5%.  
2.2 The Supplier shall monitor system uptime using agreed monitoring tools and provide the Customer with a detailed report of availability metrics within ten (10) Business Days following the end of each calendar month.  

3. Reporting and Verification  
3.1 The Supplier shall deliver monthly performance reports to the Customer by the 10th day of the following month, detailing system uptime, incident logs, resolution times, and any service interruptions exceeding fifteen (15) minutes.  
3.2 The Customer may, upon reasonable notice, audit the Supplier’s monitoring records to verify accuracy of reported availability, provided such audits do not unreasonably interfere with the Supplier’s operations.  

4. Service Credits  
4.1 If the Monthly Availability for any calendar month is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee applies.  
4.2 If the Monthly Availability for any calendar month is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the Monthly Fee applies.  
4.3 Service Credits are calculated solely on the basis of Monthly Availability as defined in Clause 1.3(c) and shall not be cumulative with any other remedies under the Agreement.  
4.4 Service Credits shall be applied automatically by the Supplier to the Customer’s next invoice unless otherwise requested in writing by the Customer within thirty (30) days of receipt of the monthly performance report.  

5. Payment Terms  
5.1 The Monthly Fee shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each month and is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.  
5.2 All payments shall be made in United States dollars to the bank account designated by the Supplier in writing.  
5.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.  

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice under this Service Level Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party.  
6.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: 157 Cannon Street, London, incorporated as Foxley Pharma plc (company number 14328672).  
6.3 Notices to the Supplier shall be sent to: 64 King William Street, London, incorporated as Vantage Marine Ltd (company number 13093160).  

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party arising out of or related to this Service Level Schedule.  
7.2 The obligations under this Clause shall not apply to information that is publicly available or independently developed without reference to confidential information.  

8. General  
8.1 This Service Level Schedule is governed by English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  
8.2 This Schedule forms part of the Agreement and shall be binding upon Oakhurst Foods Ltd (company number 15452136) only to the extent expressly permitted under the terms of the Agreement.
