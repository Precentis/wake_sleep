ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ROTHLEY ENGINEERING LTD

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
(b) “Buyer” means Ingleby Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10970081, whose registered office is at 93 Ludgate Hill, London;  
(c) “Company” means Rothley Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15883131, whose registered office is at 8 Old Broad Street, London;  
(d) “Seller” means Brightwater Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11067554, whose registered office is at 53 Gresham Street, London;  
(e) “Pemberton Energy plc” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10675846, whose registered office is at 18 Fleet Street, London;  
(f) “Shares” means the issued ordinary shares in the capital of the Company;  
(g) terms defined in the Companies Act 2006 have the same meaning when used in these Articles unless otherwise defined.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The issued share capital of the Company consists of 100 Shares.  
2.2 The rights attached to the Shares may be varied only with the written consent of the holders of not less than 75% in nominal value of the Shares, whether given in general or by class resolution.

3. Transfer of Shares  
3.1 Subject to these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of the member’s Shares by instrument in writing in any usual or common form or in any other form approved by the Board.  
3.2 No transfer of a Share shall be registered unless a proper instrument of transfer is delivered to the Company at its registered office or such other place as the Board may determine.

4. Directors  
4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all the powers of the Company.  
4.2 The Board may appoint one or more directors to serve until the next annual general meeting or until their successors are appointed.  
4.3 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution passed by not less than 50.1% of the voting rights.

5. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
5.1 The Board may meet to conduct business at such times and places as the Board may determine.  
5.2 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes.  
5.3 A director may appoint another director to act as proxy at any meeting and such appointment shall be in writing delivered to the Company prior to the meeting.  
5.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

6. Payment of Consideration  
6.1 Any payment due under any transaction involving the transfer of Shares shall be made in cleared funds to the bank account designated in writing by the recipient.  
6.2 The purchase price for the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be paid in full on completion, which shall occur on or before 2 December 2026.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given to a member, director or the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to, or left at, the registered office of the relevant party.  
7.2 Notices sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.  
7.3 Notices sent by electronic means (including email) shall be deemed served on the next business day following transmission, provided a delivery receipt is obtained.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the Company or any related party in connection with the affairs of the Company.  
8.2 The obligations under this clause shall not apply to information which is in the public domain (other than as a result of a breach of this clause) or required to be disclosed by law or a regulatory authority.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
