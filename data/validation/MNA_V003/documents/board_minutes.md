MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ROTHLEY ENGINEERING LTD

HELD ON 30 NOVEMBER 2026

1. CALLING TO ORDER  
The meeting was called to order at 10:00 a.m. at the registered office of Rothley Engineering Ltd, 8 Old Broad Street, London. The meeting was convened in accordance with the Articles of Association of the Company.

2. PRESENT AND APOLOGIES  
The following directors were present:  
- Mr Alistair M. Thorpe  
- Ms Eleanor J. Vane  

There were five (5) directors on the board of the Company. The attendance of 2 directors constituted a quorum under Article 14 of the Articles of Association. No other directors attended or participated, and no apologies for absence were received.

3. CONFIRMATION OF QUORUM  
It was confirmed that the meeting was quorate as 2 directors were present, satisfying the quorum requirement under the Articles of Association.

4. AGENDA ITEM 1: PROPOSED SALE OF SHARES IN THE COMPANY  
The Chair introduced Agenda Item 1, concerning the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Ingleby Capital LLP (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10970081, registered office 93 Ludgate Hill, London, from Brightwater Media Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11067554, registered office 53 Gresham Street, London.

5. AGENDA ITEM 2: REVIEW OF SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
The directors reviewed a draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 2 December 2026 (the Agreement) pursuant to which the Seller would transfer to the Buyer 100% of the issued share capital of the Company. The Agreement provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the shares in the Company, representing the entire issued share capital.

6. AGENDA ITEM 3: MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP  
The board noted that Pemberton Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10675846, registered office 18 Fleet Street, London, is a material customer of the Company. The board considered the potential implications of the transaction on the ongoing commercial relationship with Pemberton Energy plc and concluded that the change of ownership is not expected to materially disrupt existing contractual arrangements.

7. AGENDA ITEM 4: RESOLUTION TO APPROVE TRANSACTION  
After due consideration, and having taken legal and financial advice, the board concluded that the terms of the Agreement are fair and reasonable and in the best interests of the Company.

8. RESOLUTION  
Pursuant to section 4.1 of the Agreement, the board hereby RESOLVES as follows:  

RESOLVED THAT the Company, acting through its board of directors, hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 2 December 2026 between Brightwater Media Ltd and Ingleby Capital LLP for the sale of 100% of the issued share capital of Rothley Engineering Ltd, and that the directors be and are hereby authorised to take all necessary steps to give effect to such transaction.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION  
The Agreement is governed by English law, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising in connection with the Agreement.

10. ADJOURNMENT  
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
