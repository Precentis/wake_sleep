**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **16 November 2026**.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Brightwater Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11067554**, whose registered office is at 53 Gresham Street, London (the “**Seller**”);

(2) **Ingleby Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under registration number **10970081**, whose registered office is at 93 Ludgate Hill, London (the “**Buyer**”);

**AND**

(3) **Rothley Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15883131**, whose registered office is at 8 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Company**”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“**Closing Date**” means **19 February 2027**;

“**Closing Working Capital**” means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, determined in accordance with Schedule 1;

“**Target Working Capital**” means **$5,000,000**;

“**Purchase Price**” means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;

“**Shares**” means all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** The Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, all of the Shares, for a purchase price of **$35,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”).

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$5,000,000**. Any such reduction shall be applied as a downward adjustment to the Purchase Price payable at Closing. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, the Buyer shall pay the Seller the amount of such excess within ten (10) Business Days of the final determination of Closing Working Capital.

**3. CLOSING**

**3.1** On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of all the Shares, properly endorsed and accompanied by all necessary share certificates;

(b) resignations of the existing directors and secretaries of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

(c) such other documents as may be necessary to vest full legal and beneficial title to the Shares in the Buyer free from all Encumbrances.

**3.2** The Buyer shall, on the Closing Date, pay the adjusted Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.3 to the bank account specified by the Seller.

**4. NOTICES**

Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address or email address as a party may notify in writing. Notices shall be deemed received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, two (2) Business Days after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

**5. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each party agrees that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the other, disclose to any third party any confidential information concerning the other party or the transaction contemplated by this Agreement, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to professional advisers bound by confidentiality obligations.

**6. NON-COMPETE**

**12.1** The Seller covenants that for a period of **4 years** from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company, whether alone or in partnership or otherwise, within the United Kingdom.

**7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
