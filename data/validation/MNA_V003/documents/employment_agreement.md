**Parties**  
(1) Rothley Engineering Ltd (company number 15883131), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 8 Old Broad Street, London ("the Company").  
(2) [Employee Name] of [Residential Address] ("the Founder").  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Business” means the business carried on by the Company or any member of the Rothley Engineering group immediately before the termination of the Founder’s employment.  
“Effective Date” means 1 December 2026.  
“Termination Date” means the date on which the employment of the Founder under this Agreement is terminated, whether by resignation, dismissal, expiry of term or otherwise.  

**2. Position and Duties**  
2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder as Chief Technology Officer, and the Founder shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.  
2.2 The Founder shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as are normally associated with such role, and as may be lawfully assigned from time to time by the Board of Directors of the Company.  

**3. Term**  
3.1 The employment under this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Clause 15.  

**4. Remuneration**  
4.1 The Company shall pay the Founder a base salary of £98,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
4.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme operated by the Company, at the discretion of the Board, targeted at 25% of base salary.  

**5. Working Hours and Place of Work**  
5.1 The Founder shall work such hours as are necessary to perform the duties of the role, including outside standard business hours where required.  
5.2 The principal place of work shall be the Company’s registered office at 8 Old Broad Street, London, but the Founder may be required to travel as necessary for business purposes.  

**6. Benefits**  
6.1 The Founder shall be entitled to 28 days’ paid holiday per calendar year, inclusive of statutory holidays.  
6.2 The Founder shall be entitled to participate in the Company’s private medical insurance scheme and pension plan in accordance with their respective terms.  

**7. Confidentiality**  
7.1 The Founder shall not at any time during or after employment disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the Company, its affiliates, clients (including but not limited to Pemberton Energy plc, company number 10675846), suppliers or business operations.  
7.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.  

**8. Intellectual Property**  
8.1 All intellectual property created by the Founder in the course of employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.  

**9. Notices**  
9.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the recipient’s last known address or email address as recorded in the Company’s files.  
9.2 Notices shall be deemed served 24 hours after sending by email, or 48 hours after posting by first-class post.  

**10. Entire Agreement**  
10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes any prior discussions or agreements relating to its subject matter.  

**11. Variation**  
11.1 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.  

**12. Assignment**  
12.1 The Company may assign its rights under this Agreement to any successor entity. The Founder may not assign or transfer any rights or obligations under this Agreement.  

**13. Severability**  
13.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.  

**18. Non-Compete**  
18.1 The Founder undertakes that during the period of 18 months following the Termination Date, he shall not, whether directly or indirectly, engage or be concerned in any business which competes with the Business of the Company as carried on at the Termination Date, within the United Kingdom.  
18.2 This restriction applies to any role involving ownership, management, consultancy, employment or investment in a competing entity.  

**19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
