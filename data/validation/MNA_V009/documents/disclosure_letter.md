2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)

2.1 The Company's core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Juniper Logistics plc. The development work was carried out pursuant to an informal understanding between the Company and Juniper Logistics plc, and no written contract or statement of work was executed between the parties in relation to such development.

2.2 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Juniper Logistics plc at the time of development or at any time thereafter. As a consequence, the Company does not hold documented proof of assignment of all intellectual property rights in the core platform software from Juniper Logistics plc.

2.3 The Seller acknowledges that the absence of a written assignment may give rise to uncertainty as to the full and exclusive ownership by the Company of all intellectual property rights in the core platform software. The Buyer is accordingly notified that the representation and warranty contained in Warranty 8.2 of the SPA in relation to intellectual property ownership is qualified by this disclosure.

2.4 The Company has, since 2023, used the core platform software in the ordinary course of its business without challenge from Juniper Logistics plc or any third party in respect of ownership or infringement of intellectual property rights.

2.5 The Seller has not received any notice from Juniper Logistics plc asserting any retained intellectual property rights in the core platform software or seeking compensation, licence fees or other remedies in connection with such software.

2.6 The Seller confirms that it has made all disclosures known to it in relation to the development and ownership of the core platform software and that this constitutes full and fair disclosure in response to Warranty 8.2.
