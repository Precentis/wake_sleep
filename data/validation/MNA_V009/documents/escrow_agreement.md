ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] December 2026 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Oakhurst Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12001680, whose registered office is at 79 Moorgate, London (the "Seller");

(2) Galloway Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13235247, whose registered office is at 118 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a financial institution acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] December 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13206760, whose registered office is at 104 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the consideration under the SPA, an amount (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited into an escrow account to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller;

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 22 January 2027.

"Claims" means any written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to the release date specified in Clause 4.1, asserting a claim for indemnification under the SPA.

"Escrow Account" means the account established by the Escrow Agent for the deposit and holding of the Escrow Amount.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount specified in the SPA to be held in escrow, as confirmed in writing by the Buyer and the Seller to the Escrow Agent.

"Marlow Energy plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10908241, whose registered office is at 100 King William Street, London, which is a material customer of the Company.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent hereby accepts such appointment, on the terms set forth herein.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall be deposited into the Escrow Account promptly following the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount except as expressly provided herein.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending Claims notified before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such Claim is finally resolved, waived, or withdrawn in writing by the Buyer.

5. Payment Mechanics

5.1 All payments from the Escrow Account shall be made by the Escrow Agent via wire transfer to such account(s) as may be designated in writing by the recipient Party.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile, or by overnight courier to the registered office of the Party.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information relating to the Escrow Amount and the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or legal process.

8. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

8.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one or more arbitrators appointed in accordance with such Rules, with the seat of arbitration in New York.

9. Miscellaneous

9.1 This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by all Parties.

9.2 This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
