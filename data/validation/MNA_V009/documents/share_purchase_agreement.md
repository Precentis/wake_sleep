**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **20 November 2026**.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Oakhurst Analytics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12001680**, whose registered office is at 79 Moorgate, London (the **"Seller"**); and

(2) **Galloway Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13235247**, whose registered office is at 118 Old Broad Street, London (the **"Buyer"**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Dunmore Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13206760**, whose registered office is at 104 Old Broad Street, London (the **"Company"**).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

---

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

> **"Closing Date"** means **22 January 2027**;

> **"Company"** means **Dunmore Robotics Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13206760**, and includes any successor in title to its business and assets;

> **"Material Customer"** means **Marlow Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10908241**, whose registered office is at 100 King William Street, London;

> **"Purchase Price"** means the aggregate sum of **$180,000,000** (one hundred and eighty million US dollars), payable in accordance with Clause 2;

> **"Transaction Documents"** means this Agreement and all documents entered into or delivered pursuant to or in connection with it.

**1.2** Unless otherwise defined, terms defined in the Companies Act 2006 have the same meaning in this Agreement.

**1.3** References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

**1.4** The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

---

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** On the terms and subject to the conditions contained in this Agreement, at the Closing, the Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, for the Purchase Price.

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** The obligation of the Buyer to pay the Purchase Price is unconditional and not subject to any set-off, counterclaim or deduction of any kind.

---

**3. CLOSING**

**3.1** The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.2** At the Closing, the Seller shall deliver or procure the delivery to the Buyer of:

  (a) duly executed stock transfer forms in respect of the Shares, accompanied by the certificates representing the Shares;

  (b) a certificate of non-foreign status under Section 1.897-2 of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, if required;

  (c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

  (d) all books, records, minute books and statutory registers of the Company;

  (e) such other documents and instruments as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares to the Buyer.
