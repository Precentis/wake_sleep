**3. Approval of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 14 November 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously to approve them as a true and accurate record.

**4. Update on Proposed Acquisition by Galloway Engineering Ltd**  
The board received an update regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Robotics Ltd by Galloway Engineering Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13235247, registered office 118 Old Broad Street, London) from Oakhurst Analytics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12001680, registered office 79 Moorgate, London).  

The transaction remains subject to shareholder approval and regulatory clearance under the UK City Code on Takeovers and Mergers. All due diligence materials have been provided to the Buyer’s legal and financial advisers. No material changes to the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 December 2026 have been received. The board reaffirmed its commitment to ensuring an orderly transition should the acquisition proceed.

**5. Outstanding Legal Matter – Yarrow Capital LLP Claim**  
The board noted a letter before action received on 8 December 2026 from Yarrow Capital LLP, legal advisers acting for a third party, threatening proceedings against Dunmore Robotics Ltd in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The claimed amount is approximately $450,000. The matter is understood to relate to deliveries made under a contract dated 15 March 2025 between Dunmore Robotics Ltd and an unnamed industrial client.  

Legal counsel has been instructed to review the correspondence and assess potential exposure. Management is to monitor the position closely and report any formal claim to the board within 48 hours of receipt. No admission of liability has been made.

**6. Material Customer Relationship – Marlow Energy plc**  
The board reviewed the ongoing commercial relationship with Marlow Energy plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10908241, registered office 100 King William Street, London), which continues to account for approximately 37% of the Company’s quarterly revenue. A scheduled service review is due on 20 December 2026. The management team confirmed that all contractual deliverables under the current agreement (term: 36 months, effective from 1 January 2025) are being met.
