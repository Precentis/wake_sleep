1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Company” means Glenrock Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10864907, with its registered office at 47 Bishopsgate, London.

“Underwriter” means Dunmore Medical Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14311505, with its registered office at 76 Moorgate, London.

“Whitlock Media Ltd” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13372214, with its registered office at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London, which is a material customer of the Company.

“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 each in the share capital of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus.

“Prospectus” means this offering document, dated 1 May 2026, relating to the initial public offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company.

“Related Party Transactions” means transactions between the Company and its directors, substantial shareholders, subsidiaries, or associated undertakings, as defined under the UK version of the Transparency Directive and applicable Listing Rules.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision.

2. Offer Details

2.1 The Company is making an initial public offering of 15,000,000 Ordinary Shares, representing 30 per cent of the enlarged issued share capital following admission to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange.

2.2 The offering is being conducted on a firm commitment underwritten basis, with Dunmore Medical Securities acting as sole bookrunner and underwriter.

2.3 The Underwriter has agreed to subscribe for and purchase all Ordinary Shares not taken up by investors in the offering, subject to the terms of the underwriting agreement dated on or about 1 May 2026.

3. Use of Proceeds

3.1 Net proceeds from the offering, after deducting underwriting commissions and offering expenses, are estimated at £42.8 million, based on an assumed issue price of £3.20 per Ordinary Share.

3.2 The proceeds shall be allocated as follows: 55 per cent for research and development activities, 25 per cent for expansion of commercial operations, 15 per cent for repayment of existing short-term borrowings, and 5 per cent for general corporate purposes.

4. Related Party Transactions

4.1 As of the date of this Prospectus, there are no related party transactions other than directors' service agreements entered into in the ordinary course of business and on arm’s length terms.

4.2 The directors' service agreements are filed as part of the Company’s public filings with the Registrar of Companies and are available for inspection at the registered office of the Company.

5. Payment and Settlement

5.1 Payment for the Ordinary Shares subscribed for by investors shall be made in cleared funds to an escrow account designated by the Underwriter not later than 11.59 p.m. on 12 April 2026.

5.2 Settlement of the shares will occur on 13 April 2026, or such later date as may be agreed between the Company and the Underwriter, acting reasonably, provided that such date shall not be later than 15 April 2026.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 47 Bishopsgate, London, marked for the attention of the Company Secretary.

6.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 76 Moorgate, London, marked for the attention of Legal and Compliance Department.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or a competent regulatory authority.

7.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive the listing of the Ordinary Shares for a period of three years from 1 May 2026.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Prospectus and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
