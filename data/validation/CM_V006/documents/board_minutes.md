**MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF GLENROCK TECHNOLOGIES LTD**

**Date:** 12 April 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 47 Bishopsgate, London, and by video conference  
**Present:**  
- Eleanor Vance (Chair)  
- Marcus Thackeray (Director)  
- Julian Pierce (Director)  
- Naomi Hart (Non-executive Director)  

**1. Confirmation of Quorum**  
The Chair confirmed that a quorum was present, being at least two directors including the Chair, as required under the Company’s Articles of Association. The meeting was duly convened and constituted.

**2. Apologies and Substitutions**  
No apologies were received. All directors were present.

**3. Approval of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 14 February 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously that the minutes be confirmed as a true and accurate record.

**4. Review of Financial Position**  
The Finance Director presented the unaudited financial statements for the quarter ending 31 March 2026. The Company remains in a strong financial position, with net assets of £48.7 million and positive operating cash flow. The board noted the continued growth in revenue from existing contracts, including the agreement with Whitlock Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13372214, registered office 141 Queen Victoria Street, London).

**5. Initial Public Offering – Update**  
The board received an update from external legal and financial advisers regarding the proposed initial public offering of ordinary shares, to be underwritten by Dunmore Medical Securities (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14311505, registered office 76 Moorgate, London). The prospectus draft has been circulated for board review, and the expected listing date remains targeted for late Q2 2026. The board reaffirmed its commitment to full compliance with the UK Prospectus Regulation Rules and Listing Rules of the Financial Conduct Authority.

**6. Director’s Remuneration Policy**  
The board reviewed the proposed remuneration policy for executive directors post-IPO, in line with corporate governance requirements. The policy will be subject to shareholder approval at the forthcoming general meeting. No changes to current remuneration arrangements were approved at this meeting.

**7. Approval of Director’s Loan**  
The board considered a request from Marcus Thackeray, a director of the Company, for a loan facility. It was resolved:  
(a) That the Company advance a loan in the amount of $6,250,000 (six million two hundred fifty thousand US dollars) to Marcus Thackeray;  
(b) That the loan shall carry zero interest;  
(c) That the loan shall be repayable in full on or before 30 June 2031;  
(d) That the loan constitutes a qualifying money lending transaction under section 197 of the Companies Act 2006, subject to shareholder approval at the next annual general meeting;  
(e) That the transaction be disclosed in the prospectus in accordance with FCA disclosure requirements.  
The Chair declared a conflict of interest and withdrew during the discussion and vote. The resolution was approved by the remaining three directors.

**8. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.

**9. Adjournment**  
The meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
