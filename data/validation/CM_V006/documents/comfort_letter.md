2. Scope of Procedures  
2.1 We have performed specified procedures in relation to certain financial and operational information included in the preliminary prospectus dated 10 April 2026 issued by the Company in connection with the Initial Public Offering.  
2.2 The procedures were conducted in accordance with the standards applicable to comfort letters issued in connection with public offerings under English accounting practice and relevant professional guidelines.  
2.3 The procedures did not include any form of audit, review or assurance engagement and accordingly, we do not express an opinion, conclusion or any form of assurance on the financial or operational information reviewed.  

3. Financial Information Reviewed  
3.1 We have reviewed selected unaudited financial summaries and key performance indicators prepared by the Company covering the three-year period ended 31 December 2025.  
3.2 The reviewed data includes revenue, cost of sales, operating profit, and net assets, as derived from the Company’s internal management accounts.  
3.3 We have compared the reviewed financial data against source documentation, including general ledger records, trial balances and supporting schedules prepared by the Company’s finance team.  
3.4 During the period under review, Whitlock Media Ltd accounted for 23.7% of the Company’s total revenue in the year ended 31 December 2024.  

4. Operational and Contractual Information  
4.1 We have reviewed summaries of material contracts entered into by the Company with third parties, including but not limited to supply agreements, customer contracts and service arrangements.  
4.2 The Company has represented that it maintains an active contractual relationship with Whitlock Media Ltd under a service agreement dated 15 June 2023, which remains in full force and effect as of the Cut-off Date.  
4.3 No event of default or material breach has been notified by either party under the said agreement as of the Cut-off Date.
