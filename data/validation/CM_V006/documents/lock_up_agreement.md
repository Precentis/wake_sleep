2. Lock-Up Undertaking

2.1 The Shareholder agrees that, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, it shall not directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, assign, hypothecate, transfer, or otherwise dispose of (whether by sale or otherwise), or enter into any transaction which is designed to, or might reasonably be expected to, result in the disposition of (regardless of whether any such transaction would be settled by delivery of Shares or cash), any Shares held by the Shareholder as of the date of the Prospectus, during the period commencing on the date of the Prospectus and ending 365 days after the date of the Prospectus.

2.2 The Shareholder acknowledges that the restrictions set out in Clause 2.1 are essential to the success of the initial public offering and that any breach of these restrictions may cause irreparable harm to the Company and the Underwriter.

2.3 For the avoidance of doubt, the prohibition in Clause 2.1 applies to any Shares beneficially owned by the Shareholder, whether held directly or indirectly, and includes any Shares acquired following the date of the Prospectus through any dividend, split, distribution or reclassification.
