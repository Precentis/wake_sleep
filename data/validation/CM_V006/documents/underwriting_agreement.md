# Underwriting Agreement  
**between Glenrock Technologies Ltd and Dunmore Medical Securities**  

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date set forth in the subscription documents, by and between **Glenrock Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10864907**, whose registered office is at 47 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), and **Dunmore Medical Securities**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **14311505**, whose registered office is at 76 Moorgate, London (the “Underwriter”).  

## 1. Definitions  
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Pricing Date” means the date on which the final offering price per Ordinary Share is determined and confirmed in writing by the Underwriter to the Company, which shall be **1 May 2026**;  
(b) “Offering” means the initial public offering of the Ordinary Shares of the Company underwritten by the Underwriter pursuant to this Agreement;  
(c) “Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, each with a nominal value of £0.01;  
(d) “Prospectus” means the final prospectus prepared and filed in connection with the Offering, including any amendments or supplements;  
(e) “Closing Date” means the date specified in the subscription documentation upon which settlement of the purchase and sale of the Ordinary Shares occurs, which shall not be later than 10 business days after the Pricing Date;  
(f) “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.  

## 2. Appointment and Undertaking  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter agrees to underwrite the sale of **25,500,000 Ordinary Shares** at the Pricing Date, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.  

## 3. Payment and Delivery  
On the Closing Date, the Underwriter shall pay to the Company the aggregate purchase price for the Ordinary Shares, calculated at the final offering price per share as determined on the Pricing Date, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Company. In consideration thereof, the Company shall deliver to the Underwriter certificates or electronic book-entry interests representing the Ordinary Shares, registered in such name or names and in such denominations as the Underwriter may request at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.  

## 4. Representations and Warranties  
Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Pricing Date that:  
(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
(b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into, execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder;  
(c) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization, moratorium or similar laws affecting creditors’ rights generally.  

## 5. Covenants  
The Company covenants that it shall:  
(a) prepare and file the Prospectus in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations;  
(b) not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, offer, sell, or agree to sell any Ordinary Shares or securities convertible into Ordinary Shares during the period beginning on the date of this Agreement and ending **180 days** after the Pricing Date (the “Lock-Up Period”).  

## 6. Notices  
Any notice, request, instruction or communication required or permitted hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly given when delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic transmission, or mailed by registered or certified mail, return receipt requested, to the following addresses:  
- If to the Company: 47 Bishopsgate, London, to the attention of the Company Secretary;  
- If to the Underwriter: 76 Moorgate, London, to the attention of the Head of Capital Markets.  

## 7. Conditions to Closing  
(b) The Underwriter’s obligation to purchase the Ordinary Shares shall be conditioned upon the delivery of a comfort letter from the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm, addressed to the Underwriter, dated no earlier than **3 business days before the pricing date**, confirming that such firm has performed certain procedures with respect to specified financial information included in the Prospectus.  

## 8. Confidentiality  
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation, or to professional advisors bound by confidentiality obligations.  

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
