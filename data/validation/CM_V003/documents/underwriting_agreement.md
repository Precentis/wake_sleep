UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] January 2026 (the “Execution Date”) between:

(1) Whitlock Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12344827, whose registered office is at 20 King William Street, London (the “Company”); and

(2) Marlow Energy Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13545748, whose registered office is at 58 Fleet Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

Recitals:

A. The Company proposes to offer and sell, and the Underwriter has agreed to underwrite, a number of newly issued ordinary shares in the Company (the “Shares”) in connection with an initial public offering (the “Offering”), subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

B. The Shares will be offered pursuant to a prospectus (the “Prospectus”) prepared in accordance with applicable securities laws and regulations.

C. The Company has 89,250,000 ordinary shares in issue.

Now, therefore, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London, England.

“Closing Date” means the date on which settlement of the sale of the Shares is to occur, as specified in the final terms of the Offering.

“Pricing Date” means 9 March 2026.

“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered in the Offering.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter to act as underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter accepts such appointment and agrees to purchase the Shares from the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein.

3. Offering and Sale of Shares

3.1 The Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, the Shares at a price to be determined by agreement between the parties on or prior to the Pricing Date.

4. Payment and Delivery

4.1 Payment for the Shares shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Company.

4.2 Delivery of the Shares shall be made through the facilities of the relevant securities depository system on the Closing Date.

5. Representations and Warranties of the Company

5.1 The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the Execution Date and as of the Closing Date that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate or partnership action;

(c) the Shares, when issued and sold, will be duly and validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable; and

(d) the information to be included in the Prospectus does not contain any untrue statement of a material fact or omit to state a material fact required to be stated therein or necessary to make the statements therein, in light of the circumstances under which they were made, not misleading.

6. Covenants of the Company

6.1 The Company shall use its reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Prospectus complies with all applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

7. Conditions to Obligations

7.1 The obligations of the Underwriter under this Agreement are subject to the receipt on or before the Closing Date of a comfort letter from the Company’s independent auditors in customary form.

7.2 The comfort letter shall be dated no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the address or email address set out in the preamble, or to such other address or email address as a party may notify in writing.

9. Jurisdiction and Governing Law

9.1 The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation or by any securities exchange on which the Shares may be listed.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
