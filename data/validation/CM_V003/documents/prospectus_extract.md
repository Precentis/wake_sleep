2. Details of the Issuer  
2.1 The Issuer is Whitlock Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12344827. Its registered office is located at 20 King William Street, London.  
2.2 The Company is engaged in the business of providing capital financing solutions to mid-tier infrastructure firms operating in the United Kingdom and select European markets.  
2.3 Northgate Engineering Ltd is a material customer of the Company and has maintained a commercial relationship with the Company since 2021. The Company derives approximately 18.4% of its annual revenue from contracts with Northgate Engineering Ltd.

3. Capitalisation  
3.1 As of the date of this Prospectus, the capital structure of the Company is as follows:  
(a) There are 88,000,000 Ordinary Shares in issue;  
(b) Each Ordinary Share carries the right to one vote at general meetings of the Company and is entitled to dividends as declared by the board of directors;  
(c) There are no treasury shares;  
(d) There are no convertible securities or outstanding options to subscribe for shares, except as disclosed in the full audited financial statements attached as Annex A.  
3.2 The initial public offering (the “Offering”) will involve the issue of new Ordinary Shares by the Company, with the Underwriter acting as sole bookrunner and underwriter of the Offering. The Offering is expected to occur in the second quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and market conditions.  
3.3 Upon completion of the Offering, the enlarged issued share capital of the Company will be disclosed in the final prospectus filed with the Financial Conduct Authority.

4. Underwriting Arrangements  
4.1 The Underwriter has entered into an underwriting agreement with the Company, pursuant to which the Underwriter has agreed to purchase any Securities not subscribed for by investors in the Offering, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the underwriting agreement dated 28 January 2026.  
4.2 The Underwriter is not entitled to any commission or fee in respect of Securities purchased by it under the underwriting agreement, except for a management fee of 1.25% of the gross proceeds of the Offering, payable by the Company upon settlement.  
4.3 The obligations of the Underwriter under the underwriting agreement are subject to customary conditions precedent, including the accuracy of representations and warranties made by the Company as of the date of the Prospectus and the delivery of legal opinions and comfort letters from auditors.

5. Payment Procedures  
5.1 All payments in connection with the purchase of Securities under this Offering shall be made in cleared funds to the designated escrow account established by the Company’s solicitors, no later than two business days following the allocation date.  
5.2 Settlement of the Securities will occur on the third business day after the pricing date, such settlement to be effected through the Clearing and Settlement Agency or such other means as may be permitted under UK market practice.
