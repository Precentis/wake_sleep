1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Agreement" means the Master Services Agreement dated on or about 23 April 2024 between Upton Bioscience plc (the Supplier) and Eskdale Capital LLP (the Customer);  
(b) "Business Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
(c) "Downtime" means any period during which the Managed IT Services are wholly or partially inaccessible or non-operational due to causes within the Supplier’s control;  
(d) "Monthly Availability" means the percentage of time in a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are available, calculated as (1 – (Total Downtime / Total Scheduled Service Time)) × 100;  
(e) "Total Scheduled Service Time" means the total number of minutes in a calendar month minus any scheduled maintenance windows agreed in writing by the parties at least five (5) Business Days in advance;  
(f) "Service Credit" means a credit applied to the Customer’s subsequent invoice as described in Paragraph 4.  

2. Service Availability  
2.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that Monthly Availability of the Managed IT Services is at least 99.5%.  
2.2 Scheduled maintenance may be conducted during non-Business Hours and will not count towards Downtime if prior notice of at least forty-eight (48) hours is provided to the Customer.  
2.3 Unscheduled Downtime arising from causes beyond the Supplier’s reasonable control, including but not limited to force majeure events, third-party network failures, or actions by the Customer or its agents, shall not count towards Downtime for the purpose of calculating Monthly Availability.  

3. Monitoring and Reporting  
3.1 The Supplier shall monitor system availability using industry-standard tools and provide the Customer with a monthly availability report within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month.  
3.2 The report shall include the Total Scheduled Service Time, Total Downtime, Monthly Availability percentage, and any scheduled or unscheduled maintenance events.  
3.3 The Customer may dispute the reported Monthly Availability by providing written notice to the Supplier within five (5) Business Days of receipt of the report, specifying the basis of the dispute with supporting data.  

4. Service Credits  
4.1 If the Monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the monthly fee shall apply.  
4.2 If the Monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the monthly fee shall apply.  
4.3 The monthly fee is $120,000.  
4.4 Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet the availability targets set out in this Schedule.  
4.5 Service Credits will be issued as a deduction from the Customer’s next monthly invoice or, if no further services are to be rendered, refunded within thirty (30) days of issuance.  

5. Payment  
5.1 All fees under this Agreement are exclusive of value-added tax and any applicable taxes, which shall be paid by the Customer.  
5.2 Payments shall be made in US dollars by electronic transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Supplier.  

6. Notices

6.2 Notices shall be sent by first-class pre-paid letter or by email with confirmation of receipt.  

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.  
7.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.  

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 This Service Level Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Schedule.
