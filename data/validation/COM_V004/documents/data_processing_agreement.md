**Data Processing Agreement**

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 12 March 2025, between:

(1) **Eskdale Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11880437, with registered office at 24 Ludgate Hill, London ("Customer"); and

(2) **Upton Bioscience plc**, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15322158, with registered office at 8 Cannon Street, London ("Supplier").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- *“Applicable Data Protection Law”* means all applicable legislation relating to data protection and privacy, including the Data Protection Act 2018 and any successor legislation, and regulations made under it, as applicable from time to time.
- *“Customer Personal Data”* means any personal data (as defined in the Applicable Data Protection Law) that the Supplier processes on behalf of the Customer in the course of providing the Services, which may include personal data relating to employees, contractors, or clients of the Customer, including those of its material customer, Oakhurst Energy plc.
- *“Material Customer”* means Oakhurst Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12463313, with registered office at 153 Cannon Street, London.
- *“Services”* means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer under the Master Services Agreement dated 7 January 2023.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

**2. Scope and Purpose**

2.1 This Agreement applies to the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier in connection with the provision of the Services.

2.2 The nature and purpose of the processing is the operation, maintenance, and support of the Customer’s IT infrastructure, including email systems, cloud storage, and network security.

2.3 The duration of the processing shall be co-terminous with the term of the Master Services Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

**3. Obligations of the Supplier**

3.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to a binding duty of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including encryption of personal data and regular testing of systems.

**4. Sub-processing**

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any third-party processor in respect of Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 Where authorised, the Supplier shall enter into a written agreement with the sub-processor containing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

**5. Data Subject Rights**

5.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction.

5.2 The Supplier shall respond to any such request promptly and in any event within 15 days of receipt.

**6. Compliance with Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

**7. Security Breach Notification**

7.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

7.2 The Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in investigating and mitigating the breach, at the Customer’s written request and expense.

**8. Confidentiality**

8.1 The Supplier shall maintain the confidentiality of all Customer Personal Data and shall not disclose it to any third party except as necessary to perform the Services or as required by law.

**9. Audit Rights**

9.1 The Customer may request, once per calendar year, a written report on the Supplier’s data protection policies and procedures.

**10. Return or Deletion of Data**

10.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, the Supplier shall, at the Customer’s election, either securely return or delete all Customer Personal Data.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
