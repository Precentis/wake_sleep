SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE  
(to the Master Services Agreement dated 15 January 2027 between Rothley Systems Ltd and Whitlock Industries Ltd)

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Agreement” means the Master Services Agreement dated 15 January 2027 between Rothley Systems Ltd (the Customer) and Whitlock Industries Ltd (the Supplier);  
(b) “Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are accessible and operational, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure events;  
(c) “Business Hours” means 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
(d) “Monthly Fee” means the sum of $120,000, payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services;  
(e) “Scheduled Maintenance” means planned maintenance activities notified by the Supplier to the Customer in writing at least 48 hours in advance and conducted outside Business Hours.  

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

2. Service Levels  
2.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Availability of the Managed IT Services is not less than 99.5% in any calendar month.  
2.2 The Supplier shall provide first-line support within two hours of a service request being logged during Business Hours.  
2.3 Critical incidents affecting core system functionality shall be acknowledged within 30 minutes and assigned to a qualified technician within one hour of reporting.

3. Monitoring and Reporting  
3.1 The Supplier shall monitor system performance continuously and provide the Customer with a monthly performance report by the fifth business day of the following month.  
3.2 The performance report shall include, at a minimum, detailed metrics on system Availability, incident response times, resolution durations, and any instances of Scheduled Maintenance.

4. Service Credits  
4.1 If the monthly Availability falls below 99.5% but is at least 98.0%, the Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee.  
4.2 If the monthly Availability is below 98.0%, the Customer shall be entitled to a service credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee.  
4.3 Service credits shall be calculated and applied automatically to the following month’s invoice unless otherwise agreed in writing.  
4.4 The maximum aggregate service credits payable in any calendar month shall not exceed 25% of the Monthly Fee.  
4.5 The remedies set out in this Clause 4 are the Customer’s sole and exclusive remedies for failure to meet the Availability targets.

5. Payment  
5.1 The Monthly Fee of $120,000 shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and paid within 30 days of invoice date.  
5.2 All payments shall be made in US Dollars to the bank account designated by the Supplier in writing.

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party:  
(a) For the Customer: 48 Fleet Street, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11246553;  
(b) For the Supplier: 112 Bishopsgate, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13389704.  
6.2 Notices may be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the official corporate address.

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public, sensitive information received from the other party pursuant to the Agreement, including performance data, incident reports, and financial terms.  
7.2 The obligations under this Clause 7 shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three years.

8. General  
8.1 This Schedule forms part of the Agreement and is governed by its terms.  
8.2 This Schedule is governed by English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
