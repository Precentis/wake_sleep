1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Agreement" means the master services agreement between Rothley Systems Ltd (the Customer) and Whitlock Industries Ltd (the Supplier), including all schedules, appendices, and amendments.

"Business Day" means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England, and on which banks are open for normal business in London.

"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written, electronic, or in any other form, disclosed by one party to the other, whether marked as confidential or not, that reasonably constitutes proprietary or confidential information of the disclosing party.

"Deliverables" means all materials, documentation, services, reports, software, or other output provided by the Supplier to the Customer under this Agreement.

"Effective Date" means the date of execution of this Agreement by both parties.

"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Any reference to a clause, schedule, or appendix is a reference to a clause of, or schedule or appendix to, this Agreement.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. APPOINTMENT AND PERFORMANCE

2.1 The Supplier is appointed by the Customer to provide the Services on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with due care, skill, and diligence, and in accordance with generally accepted industry standards for managed IT service providers in the United Kingdom.

2.3 The Supplier shall ensure that all personnel involved in the performance of the Services are suitably qualified and experienced.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 14 July 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other if:

(a) the other party commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of being notified in writing of the breach;

(b) the other party becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, or has a receiver or administrator appointed;

(c) circumstances arise which make it unlawful for either party to continue performing its obligations.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule 2, in pounds sterling, within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.2 All amounts stated are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base lending rate of the Bank of England from time to time, accruing daily and compounding monthly.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law or regulation.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the receiving party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received as follows:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, 48 hours after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

7. ASSIGNMENT

7.1 Clause 19: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. A change of control of a party is deemed an assignment for the purposes of this restriction.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
