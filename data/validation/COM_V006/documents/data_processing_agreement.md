**Data Processing Agreement**

This Data Processing Agreement (“**Agreement**”) is entered into on 5 September 2026, between Holloway Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11941336 and registered office at 136 Moorgate, London (“**Customer**”), and Arden Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12046388 and registered office at 53 Cannon Street, London (“**Supplier**”).

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- **“Applicable Data Protection Law”** means (a) Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation); (b) the Data Protection Act 2018; and (c) any applicable national laws made under or pursuant to (a) or (b), as amended, replaced or re-enacted from time to time.
- **“Customer Personal Data”** means any personal data of the Customer that is disclosed by or on behalf of the Customer to the Supplier, or to which the Supplier otherwise has access in the course of providing the Services, which constitutes or includes personal data as defined in Article 4(1) of the GDPR.
- **“GDPR”** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data.
- **“Services”** means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer under the Master Services Agreement dated 3 March 2023.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

**2. Scope and Purpose**

2.1 This Agreement applies to the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer in the course of providing the Services.

2.2 The nature and purpose of the processing is the provision of managed IT services, including system monitoring, data backup, network administration, and technical support.

2.3 The categories of data subjects include the Customer’s employees, contractors, suppliers, and clients.

2.4 The types of personal data processed may include names, contact details, job titles, network login credentials, device identifiers, and usage logs.

**3. Obligations of the Supplier**

3.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage.

**4. Sub-processing**

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any third-party processor in respect of Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 Any permitted sub-processor shall be bound by written obligations no less onerous than those set out in this Agreement.

**5. Data Subject Rights**

5.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise rights under the GDPR, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection.

5.2 The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer upon receiving any such request.

**6. Compliance with Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 The Supplier shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the Customer and marked or otherwise identified as confidential, including Customer Personal Data, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law.

**8. Payment**

8.1 The Customer shall pay all fees due under the Master Services Agreement in accordance with its terms. No separate fees are payable under this Agreement.

**9. Notices**

9.1 Any notice given under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the registered office of the recipient.

9.2 Notices shall be deemed received if delivered personally, on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two business days after posting; or if sent by email, one business day after transmission.

**10. Term and Termination**

10.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first above written and shall continue in force until termination of the Master Services Agreement.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days.

**11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

11.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
