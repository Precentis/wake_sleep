1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this master services agreement between Foxley Holdings Ltd (the Customer) and Delamere Industries Ltd (the Supplier);

“Business Day” means a day which is Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;

“Confidential Information” includes all information, whether oral, written or electronic, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement;

“Effective Date” means the date on which the last party executes this Agreement;

“Fees” means the fees payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Services, as set out in the applicable Service Schedule.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of the provisions.

2. Appointment and Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide managed IT services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any applicable Service Schedules.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in compliance with generally accepted industry standards and in a timely and professional manner.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 24 September 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by notice in writing to the other if the other party:

(a) commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is irremediable, or if remediable, is not remedied within 30 days of being notified in writing to do so;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, or has a receiver or administrator appointed.

4. Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees to the Supplier within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.2 All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

4.3 Late payment shall bear interest at the rate of 8% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing from the due date until payment in full is made.
