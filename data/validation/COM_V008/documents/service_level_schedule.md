1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Affiliate” means, in relation to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party;  
(b) “Business Hours” means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
(c) “Customer” means Foxley Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13845424, whose registered office is at 139 King William Street, London;  
(d) “Downtime” means any period during which the Managed IT Services are wholly or partially unavailable to the Customer due to causes within the Supplier’s reasonable control;  
(e) “Monthly Fee” means the fee of $40,000 payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services in any calendar month;  
(f) “Service Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are available, calculated as (1 – (Total Downtime / Total Time in Month)) × 100;  
(g) “Service Credit” means a credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice as a result of failure to meet the Service Levels;  
(h) “Supplier” means Delamere Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10371144, whose registered office is at 133 Queen Victoria Street, London.  
1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Service Availability  
2.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Service Availability for each calendar month is not less than 99.5%.  
2.2 Service Availability shall be measured using monitoring tools deployed and maintained by the Supplier, with logs retained for a minimum of twelve (12) months.

3. Reporting  
3.1 The Supplier shall provide the Customer with a monthly service performance report within ten (10) Business Days of the end of each calendar month.  
3.2 The report shall include, at minimum, the calculated Service Availability, total Downtime, and any incidents that contributed to Downtime.

4. Service Credits  
4.1 If the monthly Service Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee applies.  
4.2 If the monthly Service Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the Monthly Fee applies.  
4.3 No Service Credit shall apply if Service Availability is 99.5% or higher.  
4.4 Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet Service Availability targets under this Schedule.  
4.5 Service Credits shall be applied automatically to the invoice for the following month unless otherwise agreed in writing.

5. Payment  
5.1 The Customer shall pay the Monthly Fee of $40,000 in advance on the first day of each calendar month.  
5.2 All payments shall be made in US Dollars by bank transfer to the account designated in writing by the Supplier.  
5.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Clause 1.1(c) and (h).  
6.2 Notices shall be deemed received if delivered by hand: on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post: two (2) Business Days after posting; if sent by email: on the same Business Day, provided a delivery receipt is obtained.

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.  
7.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Master Services Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

8. General  
8.1 This Service Level Schedule forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 4 March 2027 between the Customer and the Supplier.  
8.2 This Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
