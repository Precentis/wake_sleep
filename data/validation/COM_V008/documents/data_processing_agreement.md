1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Data Processing Agreement (the “DPA”), unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms shall have the meanings set out in the Master Services Agreement dated 12 January 2025 (the “MSA”) between Foxley Holdings Ltd (the “Customer”) and Delamere Industries Ltd (the “Supplier”), and the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) “Applicable Data Protection Law” means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and any applicable national laws implementing or supplementing the same, as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

(b) “Customer Personal Data” means any personal data (as defined in the UK GDPR) that the Customer discloses to the Supplier for the purposes of the Supplier providing the Managed IT Services under the MSA.

(c) “Standard Contractual Clauses” means the standard contractual clauses for processors adopted by the European Commission or the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, as applicable, where required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

(d) “Sub-processor” means any processor engaged by the Supplier to process Customer Personal Data in connection with the Services.

2. Scope and Purpose

2.1 This DPA applies to the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer in the course of providing the Managed IT Services under the MSA.

2.2 The duration of the data processing shall be co-terminous with the term of the MSA unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall take appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including, where applicable, encryption of personal data.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Customer hereby authorises the Supplier to engage Sub-processors in connection with the provision of the Services. The Supplier shall enter into a written agreement with each Sub-processor containing data protection obligations consistent with those set out in the MSA and this DPA.

4.2 The Supplier shall remain fully liable to the Customer for the performance of the Sub-processor’s obligations.

5. International Transfers

5.1 Where the Supplier transfers Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom, it shall ensure such transfers are made in compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law, including by implementing the Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

6. Compliance with Data Protection Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. Audit and Cooperation

7.1 Upon reasonable notice, the Supplier shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for and contribute to audits or inspections conducted by the Customer or a third party designated by the Customer.

7.2 The Customer acknowledges that Galloway Systems Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15738302, registered office 5 Queen Victoria Street, London) may act as a designated auditor under this clause, provided such designation is made in writing.

8. Data Subject Rights and Breach Notification

8.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in fulfilling its obligations to respond to requests from data subjects exercising rights under Applicable Data Protection Law.

8.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, of any personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, including all available details relating to the nature of the breach.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Supplier shall keep confidential all information received from the Customer that is designated as confidential or that reasonably ought to be considered confidential, including Customer Personal Data, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law.

10. Notices

10.1 Any notices under this DPA shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the respective parties:

(a) For the Customer: 139 King William Street, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13845424.

(b) For the Supplier: 133 Queen Victoria Street, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10371144.

11. Miscellaneous

11.1 This DPA forms part of the MSA and shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

11.2 No variation of this DPA shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.
