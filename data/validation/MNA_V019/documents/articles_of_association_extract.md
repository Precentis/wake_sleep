# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF NETHERBY LOGISTICS PLC

**Interpretation**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:
   (a) "the Company" means Netherby Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11151133;
   (b) "the Buyer" means Larkspur Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13419768;
   (c) "the Seller" means Marlow Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10752543;
   (d) "the Material Customer" means Netherby Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10207738;
   (e) "the Consideration" means the total sum of £47,000,000 payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 23 April 2027;
   (f) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;
   (g) "Director" means a director of the Company;
   (h) "Ordinary Resolution" means a resolution passed by a simple majority of votes cast at a general meeting of the members of the Company;
   (i) "Written Notice" means notice in writing delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

**Share Capital and Shareholders**

2. The issued share capital of the Company shall be £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each. All issued shares have been fully paid.

3. The Company shall not issue new shares without prior written consent of the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed, for a period of 24 months following completion of the acquisition on 26 April 2027.

4. Transfer of shares in the Company shall be governed by applicable UK company law and the provisions of the Share Purchase Agreement, including any restrictions on disposal during the 18-month period ending 26 October 2028.

**Directors**

5. The number of Directors shall not exceed nine nor be fewer than two at any time.

6. Directors shall be appointed by Ordinary Resolution of the shareholders or by written resolution signed by shareholders holding not less than 75% of the voting rights.

7. A Director may be removed from office by Ordinary Resolution passed at a general meeting with at least 14 days’ Written Notice to the Company.

8. The Board may appoint committees consisting of two or more Directors to oversee operations, compliance, and financial reporting.

9. A Director shall cease to hold office if he or she becomes bankrupt, enters into insolvency proceedings, or is disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986.

**Board Meetings**

10. The Board shall meet at least four times in each calendar year at intervals not exceeding 120 days.

11. The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

12. Meetings may be convened by any Director on not less than five days’ Written Notice to all other Directors.

13. Resolutions may be passed at a meeting upon a majority vote of the Directors present, or by written resolution signed by all Directors entitled to vote.

14. A Director may participate in a meeting by video conference, telephone, or other electronic means, provided that all participants can hear and speak to one another simultaneously.

**Confidentiality**

15. All Directors, officers, and employees of the Company shall maintain confidentiality of all business information, including financial data, customer contracts, and operational procedures, for a period of 60 months from 26 April 2027.

16. Disclosure shall not be required under this Article if compelled by law or court order, provided that prior notice is given to the Board unless legally prohibited.

**Notices**

17. Any notice required under these Articles shall be sufficiently given if delivered to the registered office of the Company at 169 Cannon Street, London, or to the registered office of the Buyer or Seller as listed in Clause 1.

18. Notices shall be effective upon receipt if delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by first-class post, or upon confirmed delivery if sent by email.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

19. These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising under or in connection with these Articles.

20. Nothing in these Articles shall confer rights on any third party pursuant to the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.
