2. Disclosure Against Warranties

2.1 This Disclosure Letter is delivered pursuant to Clause 8 of the Agreement and qualifies the Warranties set out therein to the extent indicated below.

2.2 Disclosure of any matter shall not be construed as an admission that such matter is material, gives rise to a liability, or would result in a breach of any Warranty if not disclosed.

3. Disclosure Against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)

3.1 The Seller discloses that there is no pending, threatened or reasonably anticipated claim, suit, arbitration, investigation or proceeding of any nature, whether civil, criminal, regulatory or otherwise, before any court, tribunal, arbitral body or governmental authority, involving or affecting the Company or any of its assets, operations or business, whether as claimant, defendant or otherwise.

3.2 For the purposes of Warranty 7.1, the Seller confirms that there is no litigation, arbitration, investigation or proceeding of any kind disclosed or required to be disclosed under applicable law or the Agreement.

3.3 The disclosure against Warranty 7.1 is stated as: **None.**

4. Payment

4.1 Any adjustment to the consideration under the Agreement arising as a result of a claim for breach of Warranty shall be made in accordance with the terms of the Agreement, including any provisions relating to set-off, escrow or deferred consideration.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted to be given under this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Clause 1.1 above, or to such other address or email as a party may notify in writing to the other party in accordance with the notice provisions of the Agreement.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed served in accordance with the deemed service provisions set out in the Agreement.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 This Disclosure Letter and its contents shall be treated as confidential by the parties and their advisers, and shall not be disclosed to any third party except as permitted under the Agreement or required by law.

6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall survive termination, expiry or completion of the Agreement.

7.1 This Disclosure Letter shall be read and construed as part of the Agreement, and any reference to the Agreement shall include this Disclosure Letter.

7.2 This Disclosure Letter is addressed to and for the benefit of the Buyer and its successors and assigns, and shall not confer any rights on any other person pursuant to the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.
