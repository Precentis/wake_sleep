SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 11 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Marlow Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 10752543 and registered office at 49 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Larkspur Bioscience plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 13419768 and registered office at 127 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Netherby Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 11151133 and registered office at 169 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 19 July 2027;

   "Confidential Information" means all confidential or proprietary information concerning the business, operations, customers or suppliers of the Company, including but not limited to information relating to Netherby Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 10207738 and registered office at 73 Moorgate, London;

   "Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable under this Agreement for the Shares;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   "Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Clause 8.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $200,000,000 (two hundred million US dollars), which shall be paid in full in cleared funds to the Seller’s designated account on the Closing Date.

2.2 The purchase and sale of the Shares shall take place on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 3.

3. CLOSING

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of the Shares, properly endorsed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee;

   (b) share certificates representing the Shares (or a duly executed declaration of loss if unavailable);

   (c) a certificate of non-UK tax residency in respect of the Seller, if required under applicable tax legislation; and

   (d) such other documents and assurances as are necessary to vest legal and beneficial title to the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

3.2 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice given under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the recipient at its registered office as set out above, or to such other address as a Party may notify in writing.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received when delivered by hand, on the second Business Day after posting by first class pre-paid post, or on transmission by email with read receipt confirmed.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party, except:

   (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidentiality;

   (b) as required by law or any regulatory authority; or

   (c) with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of six years.

8. WARRANTIES

8.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that, as at the date of this Agreement and as at the Closing Date, each of the statements set out in Schedule 1 is true, accurate and not misleading.

9. INDEMNITIES AND CLAIMS

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of any Warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within 20 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the facts giving rise to such claim.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
