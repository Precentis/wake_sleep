ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 22 April 2027 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Larkspur Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13419768 whose registered office is at 127 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Marlow Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10752543 whose registered office is at 49 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered office at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ, acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Background

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 21 April 2027 (the "SPA") with the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Netherby Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11151133 whose registered office is at 169 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) Pursuant to the terms of the SPA, an amount equal to 15 per cent of the total purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Amount is to be held, administered and released by the Escrow Agent.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Claim Notice" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent stating the nature and basis of a claim, the amount claimed (in pounds sterling), and, where available, supporting documentation;

   "Effective Date" has the meaning given in the preamble;

   "Escrow Amount" has the meaning given in Recital B;

   "Indemnity Period" means the period ending on 25 April 2028;

   "SPA" has the meaning given in Recital A.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment, on the terms set out herein.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") at HSBC Bank plc in accordance with its standard escrow account terms.

3. Payment and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or before 25 April 2027.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account until released in accordance with Clause 5.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the recipient’s registered office or nominated email address.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to 127 Cheapside, London.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 49 Cheapside, London.

4.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to legal.notices@hsbc.co.uk with a copy to trusts.uk@hsbc.com.

5. Claims and Release

5.1 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Account only in accordance with a joint written instruction signed by the Buyer and the Seller or upon receipt of a final and binding court order issued by the courts of England and Wales.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 No claim may be made after the expiry of the Indemnity Period.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or disclosure to its professional advisers.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
