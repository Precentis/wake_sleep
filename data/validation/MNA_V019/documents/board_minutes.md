# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Netherby Logistics plc

**Date:** 24 April 2027  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.

## 3. Confirmation of Agenda

3.1. The agenda was reviewed and confirmed as accurate and complete.

## 4. Approval of Share Purchase Agreement

4.1. The board considered the proposed acquisition by Larkspur Bioscience plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13419768, of the entire issued share capital of Netherby Logistics plc (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11151133, from Marlow Capital LLP (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10752543.  

4.2. Legal counsel provided a summary of the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement dated 23 April 2027, including provisions relating to warranties, covenants, and post-completion obligations.  

4.3. The board discussed the strategic implications of the transaction, including the expected completion date of 26 April 2027, and confirmed that no shareholder approval was required under applicable rules.  

4.4. After deliberation, the board concluded that the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement is in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders.  

4.5. **RESOLVED** that the Company approve entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Larkspur Bioscience plc, such agreement to be executed by the Company in accordance with its constitutional documents and applicable law.

## 5. Material Litigation Risk

5.1. The board noted a letter before action dated 22 April 2027 from Lowther Software Ltd threatening legal proceedings against the Company for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply contract.  

5.2. Management reported that the matter is currently under internal review and that preliminary correspondence has been exchanged.  

5.3. The board instructed the Company’s legal advisors to assess the merits of the claim and recommended ongoing monitoring of the position.  

5.4. It was further noted that Netherby Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10207738, remains a material customer of the Company, and any disruption to that relationship could have operational implications.  

5.5. **RESOLVED** that the matter be referred to the Chief Legal Officer for ongoing monitoring and regular reporting to the board, with a view to determining whether formal response or settlement discussions are warranted.

## 6. Adjournment

6.1. There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
