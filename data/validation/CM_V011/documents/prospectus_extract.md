“Underwriter” means Merrion Systems Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10577841, with its registered office at 24 Cheapside, London;

“net proceeds” refers to the aggregate amount received by the Company from the Offering after deduction of underwriting commissions, fees, and offering-related expenses.  
1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Details of the Offering  
2.1 The Issuer of this Offering is Lowther Robotics Ltd.  
2.2 The date of this Prospectus is 10 March 2026.  
2.3 The Company is offering a total of 15,000,000 ordinary shares, each with a nominal value of £0.01, through an initial public offering conducted under English law.  
2.4 The Offering is being conducted on a firm commitment basis and is underwritten by Merrion Systems Securities.

3. Use of Proceeds  
3.1 The net proceeds of the Offering are estimated at $150,000,000.  
3.2 The Company intends to apply the net proceeds as follows:  
(a) $68,000,000 for the repayment of the outstanding balance under its existing revolving credit facility;  
(b) $45,000,000 to fund capital expenditure over the 24-month period following the closing of the Offering, including investment in research and development infrastructure and automation systems;  
(c) $42,000,000 for general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions, and operational expansion.  
3.3 The allocation of proceeds set out in Clause 3.2 represents management’s current intentions and may be adjusted within reasonable limits subject to business conditions, provided such adjustments are disclosed in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

4. Legal Proceedings  
4.1 As of the date of this Prospectus, the Company is not party to any legal proceedings which may have a significant effect on its financial position.  
4.2 The Company confirms that no material litigation, arbitration or administrative proceedings are pending or, to the best of its knowledge, threatened against it, its directors, or its material assets, which would reasonably be expected to impair the Company’s ability to proceed with the Offering or to operate in the ordinary course.

5. Payment and Settlement  
5.1 Payment for shares issued pursuant to the Offering shall be made in full in cleared funds, in US Dollars, to an account designated by the Company and notified to the Underwriter not less than five (5) business days prior to the closing date.  
5.2 Settlement of shares shall occur on or before the third business day following the date of allocation, in accordance with standard market practice for offerings on the London Stock Exchange.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to 179 Gresham Street, London, and notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to 24 Cheapside, London.  
6.3 A notice shall be deemed to have been received at 10:00 a.m. on the second business day following the day of posting if sent by pre-paid first-class post, or on the day of delivery if delivered by hand or electronic transmission with confirmed receipt.
