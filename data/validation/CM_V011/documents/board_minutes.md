1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. The quorum was confirmed to be present in accordance with the Articles of Association of the Company.

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 13 January 2026 were reviewed. A motion was made by J. Smith and seconded by R. Patel to approve the minutes as drafted. The motion was passed unanimously.

3. LEGAL CLAIM DISCLOSURE  
The board discussed a class action filed by Kingsmere Engineering Ltd seeking $20,000,000 in damages. Counsel has advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success. The board received a briefing from external legal advisors on the nature of the allegations, which relate to alleged misrepresentations in a prior product specification issued in Q3 2024. The Company is preparing its formal response, to be filed by 28 February 2026. The board directed management to ensure full cooperation with legal counsel and to evaluate potential disclosure obligations in connection with the pending initial public offering. A reserve has not been established in the financial statements as of 31 December 2025 in respect of this matter.

4. INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING UPDATE  
The board received an update from the executive team on the progress of the proposed initial public offering of ordinary shares, underwritten by Merrion Systems Securities (the Underwriter), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10577841, registered office 24 Cheapside, London. The offering is expected to consist of 15,000,000 ordinary shares, with pricing anticipated between £1.85 and £2.10 per share. The Company, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12125344, registered office 179 Gresham Street, London, intends to apply for listing on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market. The board reviewed draft prospectus materials and authorised further revisions to incorporate the disclosure required under Item 3 above. The roadshow is scheduled to commence on 15 February 2026.

5. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP  
The board considered the ongoing commercial relationship with Lowther Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10225903, registered office 63 Gresham Street, London, identified as a material customer. Revenue attributable to Lowther Capital LLP for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2025 accounted for 22% of total consolidated revenue. The current contract with Lowther Capital LLP expires on 30 June 2026. Management reported that negotiations for renewal are underway, with no indication of material change in terms at this stage.
