UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date of execution by the last party to sign (the "Execution Date"), by and between:

(1) Lowther Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12125344, whose registered office is at 179 Gresham Street, London ("the Company"); and

(2) Merrion Systems Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10577841, whose registered office is at 24 Cheapside, London ("the Underwriter").

The Company and the Underwriter are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

A. The Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter proposes to purchase and underwrite, 10,000,000 ordinary shares (the "Firm Shares") in the capital of the Company, in connection with an initial public offering (the "Offering").

B. The Parties have agreed upon the terms and conditions under which the Underwriter will underwrite the Offering and any additional shares that may be issued pursuant to the Over-allotment Option, as defined below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

"Over-allotment Option" means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter under Clause 4 to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares of the Company.

"Offering" means the initial public offering of the Firm Shares and, if applicable, any Over-allotment Shares, as described herein.

"Over-allotment Shares" means any shares purchased by the Underwriter pursuant to the exercise of the Over-allotment Option.

"Gross Proceeds" means the total aggregate proceeds received by the Company from the sale of the Firm Shares at a price of $22.00 per share, before deduction of any commissions, fees or expenses.

2. Appointment and Engagement

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as the exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby accepts such appointment and agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

3. Payment and Commission

The Company shall pay to the Underwriter a commission equal to 7.0% of the Gross Proceeds of the Offering. The Gross Proceeds are calculated based on the issuance of 10,000,000 shares at $22.00 per share, amounting to $220,000,000. The commission payable under this Clause 3 is therefore $17,710,000. Such commission shall be paid on the Closing Date (as defined below) by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Underwriter.

4. Over-allotment Option

The Company hereby grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares (the "Over-allotment Shares"), representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares. The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time on or prior to 30 days following the Closing Date, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company. The Over-allotment Shares shall be purchased at the same per-share price as the Firm Shares, and all other terms of this Agreement shall apply to such purchase.

5. Notices

Any notice, request, demand or communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly given if delivered in person, by email to the principal corporate email address of the recipient Party, or by pre-paid first-class post to the registered office of the relevant Party. Notices shall be deemed effective upon receipt if delivered in person or by email during business hours, or on the second business day following posting if sent by post.

6. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three years.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
