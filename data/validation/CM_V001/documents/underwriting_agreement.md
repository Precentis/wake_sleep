UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date of execution by the parties hereto, by and between:

(1) Merrion Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15859679, whose registered office is at 61 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”); and

(2) Whitlock Energy Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10757245, whose registered office is at 91 Cheapside, London (the “Underwriter”).

Recitals

A. The Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter proposes to purchase, 16,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company (the “Firm Shares”), in connection with an initial public offering (the “Offering”) of its ordinary shares.

B. The Firm Shares are to be offered to the public at a price per share to be determined in connection with the Offering (the “Public Offering Price”).

C. The Company has 46,450,000 ordinary shares in issue.

D. The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on a firm commitment basis, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

Now, therefore, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

“Additional Shares” means the ordinary shares issuable upon exercise of the Over-allotment Option pursuant to Clause 4.

“Closing Date” means the date on which settlement of the purchase and sale of the Firm Shares and, if applicable, the Additional Shares occurs, as agreed between the parties.

“Over-allotment Option” has the meaning set forth in Clause 4.

2. Appointment and Engagement

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to act in such capacity, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

3. Purchase and Sale

On the Closing Date, the Company shall issue, sell and deliver to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter shall purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at a price per share equal to the Public Offering Price less an underwriting discount to be agreed upon in writing prior to the Closing Date.

4. Over-allotment Option

The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the “Over-allotment Option”) exercisable at any time prior to the date falling 30 days after the Closing Date, to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares (the “Additional Shares”), representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares. The Additional Shares shall be purchased at the same price per share (net of underwriting discount) as the Firm Shares. The Underwriter may exercise the Over-allotment Option in whole or in part by written notice to the Company.

5. Payment

Payment for the Firm Shares and, if applicable, the Additional Shares shall be made by the Underwriter to the Company by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Company, on the Closing Date.

6. Notices

All notices and other communications required or permitted hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly given if delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile transmission, or sent by internationally recognised courier service to the addresses set forth on the first page of this Agreement, or to such other address as either party may designate in writing.

7. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to treat as confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as may be required by law or regulation or by any securities exchange on which securities of the disclosing party are listed.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
