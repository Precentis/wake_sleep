# PROSPECTUS EXTRACT  
**Merrion Logistics plc**  
(Incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15859679)  
Prospectus dated 3 June 2026  

---

**1. Definitions**  
In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“the Company” means Merrion Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15859679, with its registered office at 61 Fleet Street, London;  
“the Underwriter” means Whitlock Energy Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10757245, with its registered office at 91 Cheapside, London;  
“Calloway Bioscience plc” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13205902, with its registered office at 74 Cheapside, London;  
“Prospectus” means this offering document dated 3 June 2026, including all appendices and incorporated documents;  
“ordinary shares” means the ordinary shares of the Company of £0.01 each;  
“net proceeds” means the aggregate consideration received by the Company from the initial public offering after deduction of underwriting commissions, listing fees, and associated transaction expenses;  
“revolving facility” means the credit facility entered into between the Company and Allied Trust Bank on 15 March 2024, in the original principal amount of $80,000,000.  

---

**2. Use of Proceeds**  
The net proceeds of the offering are expected to amount to $120,000,000. The Company intends to apply the net proceeds as follows:  
(a) $54,000,000 to repay a portion of the outstanding balance under the revolving facility;  
(b) $36,000,000 to fund capital expenditure, including the acquisition of six new logistics hubs and associated fleet upgrades, to be deployed over the 18-month period following admission;  
(c) $35,000,000 for general corporate purposes, including working capital, debt servicing costs, and potential strategic acquisitions within the European logistics sector.  
The allocation of proceeds is subject to the continued accuracy of representations made to the Underwriter at the pricing of the offering, and no funds shall be redirected without prior written notice to the Underwriter.  

---

**3. Capitalisation**  
At the date of this Prospectus, the capital structure of the Company is as follows:  
(a) 45,200,000 ordinary shares are in issue;  
(b) no other classes of shares are outstanding;  
(c) each ordinary share carries the right to one vote and is entitled to dividends as declared by the board of directors.  
The offering will result in the issuance of additional ordinary shares, subject to the final allotment determined in accordance with the bookbuilding process. A detailed capitalisation table post-offering will be included in the final prospectus.  

---

**4. Payment and Settlement**  
All payments due from investors under the offering shall be made in US dollars via the clearing systems designated by the London Stock Exchange. Settlement of shares shall occur on the date that is T+5 business days after the publication of the final pricing announcement. The Company shall instruct its registrars to issue share certificates or electronic credit entries in accordance with instructions provided by the Underwriter.  

---

**5. Notices**  
Any notice required or permitted under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party. Notices to the Company shall be sent to 61 Fleet Street, London, marked for the attention of the Chief Financial Officer. Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to 91 Cheapside, London, marked for the attention of the Head of Equity Capital Markets. Notices shall be deemed effective on the business day after posting by first-class pre-paid post or on actual receipt if delivered by courier or email with read receipt.  

---

**6. Confidentiality**  
Each recipient of this Prospectus agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information contained herein, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or the rules of the Financial Conduct Authority or London Stock Exchange. This obligation shall survive for a period of two years from the date of this Prospectus.  

---

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Prospectus and any matters arising from it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Prospectus.
