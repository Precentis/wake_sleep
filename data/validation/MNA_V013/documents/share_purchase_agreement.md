SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 11 March 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Marlow Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10863041, whose registered office is at 102 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Calloway Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11184185, whose registered office is at 165 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15965563, whose registered office is at 162 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

"Closing Date" means 22 June 2026;

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied;

"FY2026" means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties set out in clauses relating to title, capacity, and tax;

"General Warranties" means all warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement other than the Fundamental Warranties;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase the Shares from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell the Shares to the Buyer, for a total consideration of $160,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000.

3. Earn-out Payment

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $6,500,000, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated and paid within 90 days following the end of FY2026, subject to independent audit.

8. Limitation of Liability

8.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with the General Warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $21,600,000. This limitation does not apply to claims arising under the Fundamental Warranties.

10. Survival and Notification

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive the Closing for 48 months. Any claim under this Agreement must be notified in writing to the party against whom the claim is made before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

12. Non-Competition

12.1 The Seller covenants to the Buyer that it shall not, and shall ensure that none of its subsidiaries or controlled affiliates shall, carry on, be engaged in, concerned with, or have any direct or indirect interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as conducted on the Closing Date, in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates, for a period of 5 years from the Closing Date.

13. Notices

13.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

14. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

14.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

14.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
