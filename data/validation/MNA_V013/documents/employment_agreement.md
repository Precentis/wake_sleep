1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business” means the business of manufacturing, distributing and supplying organic food products currently carried out by the Company and any business activities materially related thereto or in connection therewith;

“Company” means Corrib Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15965563, whose registered office is at 162 Bishopsgate, London;

“Confidential Information” includes, without limitation, trade secrets, customer lists, supplier agreements, pricing structures, operational methods, marketing plans, financial data, technical know-how, research and development projects, and any other proprietary or commercially sensitive information relating to the Company or the Buyer, whether or not marked as confidential;

“Effective Date” means the date on which this Agreement is executed by all parties;

“Founder” means Mr. Declan O’Shea, founder and former Chief Executive Officer of the Company;

“Group” means, collectively, the Company, the Buyer, and each of their respective subsidiaries and associated undertakings from time to time;

“Restricted Territory” means the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland;

“Trade Connections” means any person, firm, company or organisation which is, or was within the 12 months prior to termination of employment, a customer, supplier, agent or distributor of the Company or the Buyer in connection with the Business;

“Buyer” means Calloway Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11184185, whose registered office is at 165 Ludgate Hill, London.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to persons include corporations and unincorporated associations.

2. Employment

2.1 The Founder shall be employed by the Buyer pursuant to the terms of this Agreement, effective as of the Effective Date, in the role of Senior Strategic Advisor.

3. Payment and Benefits

3.1 In consideration for the Founder’s services, the Buyer shall pay a monthly retainer of £18,500, payable in arrears on the last business day of each calendar month.

3.2 The Founder shall be entitled to participate in such pension, bonus and benefit schemes as may be offered by the Buyer to senior executives from time to time, subject to the terms of those schemes.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Founder shall not, during the term of employment or at any time thereafter, directly or indirectly use, disclose or disseminate any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of duties under this Agreement or as required by law.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for any reason and shall continue indefinitely.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office of the respective party as set out above.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed served: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, 48 hours after posting; if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.

6. Entire Agreement

6.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations and understandings, whether oral or written, relating to the subject matter herein.

18. Non-Compete

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by reason of his position and access to Confidential Information, he holds a key role in the Business.

18.2 For a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether voluntary or involuntary, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage, invest in, own, operate, manage, control, participate in, or render services to any business that carries on or is preparing to carry on a business in competition with the Business within the Restricted Territory.

18.3 The Founder shall not, during the same 18-month period, solicit or endeavour to entice away any Trade Connections of the Company or the Buyer, nor shall he interfere with any relationship between the Company, the Buyer or any member of the Group and any such Trade Connections.

18.4 The parties acknowledge that these restrictions are reasonable and necessary for the protection of legitimate business interests, including goodwill, customer relationships and Confidential Information.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

19.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
