# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Quayside Capital LLP

**Date of Meeting:** 5 March 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 91 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7BU  
**Chair:** Ms. Elaine Travers, Managing Partner  
**Company Secretary:** Mr. Daniel Reed  

**In Attendance:**  
- Ms. Elaine Travers (Managing Partner)  
- Mr. Daniel Reed (Company Secretary)  
- Mr. Aaron Bell (Finance Partner)  
- Ms. Priya Nkosi (Operations Partner)  

**1. Call to Order**  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. A quorum was present. The agenda was adopted without amendment.

**2. Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 6 December 2025 were reviewed. Upon motion by Mr. Bell and seconded by Ms. Nkosi, the minutes were confirmed and signed as an accurate record.

**3. Financial Update**  
Mr. Bell presented the unaudited financial statements for the quarter ending 31 December 2025. Total revenue for the period was reported at £4.2 million. Gross profit margin remained at 38%. The board noted that cash reserves stood at £1.7 million as of 31 December 2025.

**4. Material Contracts**  
The board reviewed the status of key client agreements. The contract with Juniper Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15311630, registered office 1 Old Broad Street, London) was confirmed as active under its current terms. The agreement, originally executed on 14 February 2024, provides for services over a 36-month period and represents 27% of the Company’s total annual revenue.

**5. Legal and Compliance Matters**  
The board noted a letter before action dated 28 February 2026 from Kingsmere Engineering Ltd threatening proceedings for approximately $2,750,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The letter refers to deliveries made between April and August 2025 under a supply agreement dated 12 March 2024. Legal counsel has been instructed to respond. Management is to monitor developments and report to the board on a monthly basis until resolution. The board directed that appropriate reserves be assessed in consultation with the Company’s auditors for the next reporting period.

**6. Proposed Sale of the Company**  
The board reviewed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Capital LLP (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14040098, registered office 91 Ludgate Hill, London) by Upton Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15665556, registered office 3 Queen Victoria Street, London). The transaction is being conducted by Northgate Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10548491, registered office 3 Cannon Street, London), the sole shareholder of the Company.  

The board acknowledged the draft sale and purchase agreement and confirmed that due diligence documentation has been prepared in accordance with the seller’s obligations. Directors are to provide requisite representations and warranties in line with standard market practice under English law, with disputes to be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**7. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.

**8. Adjournment**  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
