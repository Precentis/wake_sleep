ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 8 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Upton Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15665556, whose registered office is at 3 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Northgate Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10548491, whose registered office is at 3 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent Name], a law firm or financial institution acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Effective Date (the "SPA"), pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller all of the issued share capital of Quayside Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14040098, whose registered office is at 91 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) As part of the SPA, an amount of £8,750,000 (eight million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited into an escrow account (the "Escrow Account") to secure certain indemnities and obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Escrow Agent shall hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller setting out in reasonable detail the nature, basis and quantum of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

"Effective Date" means 8 March 2026.

"Escrow Amount" means £8,750,000, being the amount deposited into the Escrow Account pursuant to the SPA.

"Material Customer" means Juniper Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15311630, whose registered office is at 1 Old Broad Street, London.

2. Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account with a recognised financial institution in England. The Escrow Account shall be held in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of the Buyer and the Seller as their interests may appear under this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely as stakeholder and not as principal, and shall not be subject to set-off, counterclaim or any other right of the Escrow Agent.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains undisputed or unreleased, shall be released from the Escrow Account to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date (the "Release Date"), unless prior to such date a Claim Notice has been served in accordance with Clause 5.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by signed courier, or sent by email to the addresses or email addresses specified in the SPA or as otherwise notified in writing.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to its registered office or as otherwise notified.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to its registered office or as otherwise notified.

4.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent in accordance with its instructions provided in writing.

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice at any time during the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending on the date falling 18 months thereafter.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall promptly notify the Seller and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount to which the claim relates until the earlier of: (a) the resolution of the claim by agreement between the Buyer and the Seller, (b) determination by binding arbitration or final non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, or (c) written direction from both the Buyer and the Seller.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold all information received under this Agreement in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
