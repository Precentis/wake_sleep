# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated **27 February 2026**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Upton Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **15665556**, whose registered office is at 3 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) **Northgate Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **10548491**, whose registered office is at 3 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Quayside Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number **14040098**, whose registered office is at 91 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means **4 April 2026**, or such later date as the Parties may agree in writing;

"Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties set out in Clause 7.1 (Title), Clause 7.2 (Capacity) and Clause 7.3 (Tax);

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Clause 7, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

"Juniper Logistics plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **15311630**, whose registered office is at 1 Old Broad Street, London;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

## 2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell the Shares to the Buyer, and the Buyer agrees to purchase the Shares from the Seller, for a total consideration of **$145,000,000** (the "Purchase Price"), payable in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full on the Closing Date, subject to satisfaction of the Conditions.

## 3. Completion

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver or procure the delivery of the share transfer instruments and such other documents as are reasonably required to vest the Shares in the Buyer free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

## 4. Conditions

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction of the following conditions:

(a) receipt of all necessary corporate and regulatory approvals;

(b) confirmation in writing from Juniper Logistics plc that its existing commercial arrangements with the Company shall continue uninterrupted post-Closing.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling **90 days** after the date of this Agreement (being **27 May 2026**), either Party may by written notice to the other terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights accrued prior to termination.

## 9. Warranty Claims

9.7 The Buyer shall notify the Seller of any claim arising under the warranties contained in Clause 7 within **20 days** of becoming aware of the relevant matter giving rise to the claim. Notification shall be in writing and specify in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the basis upon which it is made and, where practicable, an estimate of the loss claimed.

## 10. Survival and Time Limits

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months** from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months** from the Closing Date. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

## 11. Notices

11.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England.

12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
