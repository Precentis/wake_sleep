**ESCROW AGREEMENT**

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of 14 March 2027 (the “Effective Date”) by and among:

(1) Merrion Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14948217, whose registered office is at 47 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”);

(2) Northgate Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10193118, whose registered office is at 14 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 01785497, whose registered office is at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Larkspur Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11064711, whose registered office is at 115 Moorgate, London.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £1,250,000 (one million two hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement dated 14 March 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller.

“Escrow Period” means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending on 15 March 2027.

“Material Customer Claim” means any claim, demand or liability arising from or related to the termination, reduction in volume or non-renewal of any supply agreement between the Company and Eskdale Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12327320, whose registered office is at 110 Cannon Street, London.

**2. Deposit of Escrow Amount**

2.1 The Seller hereby deposits with the Escrow Agent, and the Escrow Agent acknowledges receipt of, the Escrow Amount, which shall be held in a segregated interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Account”) maintained in pounds sterling with HSBC Bank plc.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any lien, set-off or counterclaim.

**3. Release of Escrow Amount**

3.1 Upon the expiration of the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, to the Seller, unless prior thereto the Buyer has delivered to the Escrow Agent a written notice asserting a claim for indemnification under the Share Purchase Agreement (a “Claim Notice”).

3.2 If a Claim Notice is received prior to the expiration of the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall retain the portion of the Escrow Amount specified in the Claim Notice pending final resolution of the claim.

**4. Payment Instructions**

4.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made by same-day value wire transfer in immediately available funds to such account as may be designated in writing by the receiving Party.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the addresses specified in the preamble to this Agreement, or to such other address or email address as a Party may from time to time designate in writing.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or legal process.

**7. Escrow Agent Responsibilities**

7.1 The Escrow Agent acts as a stakeholder only and shall not be liable for any action or omission taken in good faith, except to the extent resulting from its gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**8. Termination**

8.1 This Agreement shall terminate upon the full release of the Escrow Amount and the closure of the Escrow Account.

**9. Costs and Expenses**

9.1 The Escrow Agent’s fees and expenses shall be shared equally between the Buyer and the Seller.

**12. Governing Law and Arbitration**

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce in accordance with its Rules, and such arbitration shall take place in New York. The language of the arbitration shall be English.
