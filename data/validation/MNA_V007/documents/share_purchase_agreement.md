**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 28 February 2027.

**PARTIES**

(1) Northgate Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10193118, whose registered office is at 14 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Merrion Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14948217, whose registered office is at 47 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Larkspur Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11064711, whose registered office is at 115 Moorgate, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, including the recitals, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Closing” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

“Closing Date” means 1 June 2027.

“Closing Working Capital” means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties given by the Seller in respect of title, capacity and tax.

“General Warranties” means all warranties given by the Seller other than the Fundamental Warranties.

“Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, subject to adjustment under Clause 2.3.

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

“Target Working Capital” means $5,000,000.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, the Shares for a purchase price of $190,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”).

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles and paid within ten (10) Business Days following agreement or determination of the Closing Working Capital.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or such other location as the parties may agree, at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date, unless another time, date or place is agreed in writing by the parties.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all documents and instruments of transfer required to vest good and marketable title to the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

**9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY**

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $950,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over such amount.

**10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England are to have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).

**16. NOTICES**

16.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by fax, or sent by first-class pre-paid post to the recipient at its registered office or such other address as it may designate by prior written notice.

**17. CONFIDENTIALITY**

17.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.
