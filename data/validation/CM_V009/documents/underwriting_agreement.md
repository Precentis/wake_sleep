UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT  
DATED AS OF [●] 2027  

BETWEEN:  

(1)	UPTON MEDIA LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 10844848), whose registered office is at 65 Cannon Street, London (the “Company”); and  

(2)	GALLOWAY FOODS SECURITIES, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 10127011), whose registered office is at 107 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Underwriter”).  

RECITALS:  

A.	The Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter proposes to purchase, an aggregate of 24,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company (the “Firm Shares”) in connection with the initial public offering (the “Offering”).  

B.	The Offering is being conducted pursuant to a prospectus to be filed with the relevant securities regulators in the United Kingdom and other jurisdictions as required.  

C.	The Company has 63,000,000 ordinary shares in issue.  

1.	Definitions  
For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them below:  
(a)	“Over-allotment Option” means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter under Clause 4 to purchase up to 4,800,000 additional Shares.  
(b)	“Pricing Date” means the date on which the final price per Share is determined, being 20 June 2027.  
(c)	“Shares” means the ordinary shares of the Company, nominal value of £0.01 each.  

2.	Purchase and Sale of Shares  
Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Company agrees to issue and sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at a price to be agreed at the Pricing Date.  

3.	Payment  
Settlement for the Firm Shares shall occur on a delivery-versus-payment basis on the closing date, which shall be no later than five business days after the Pricing Date, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Payment for the Firm Shares shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Company.  

4.	Over-allotment Option  
The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the “Over-allotment Option”) to purchase up to 4,800,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 24,000,000 Firm Shares. The Underwriter may exercise this option in whole or in part at any time prior to 30 days following the Pricing Date, by written notice to the Company. The additional Shares shall be purchased at the same per-share price as the Firm Shares.  

5.	Representations and Warranties  
Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Pricing Date that:  
(a)	it has the corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder;  
(b)	this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms; and  
(c) the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate any applicable law or any provision of its constitutional documents.  

6.	Notices  
Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the respective party as set out in the preamble. Notices shall be deemed received on the date of delivery if delivered by hand or email (with confirmation of transmission), or two business days after posting if sent by post.  

7.	Conditions Precedent  
(a)	The obligations of the Underwriter under this Agreement are subject to the receipt of a comfort letter from the Company’s independent auditors, addressed to the Underwriter, dated no earlier than the comfort letter cut-off date.  
(b)	The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date. The Pricing Date is 20 June 2027.  

8.	Confidentiality  
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation, or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of two years.  

9.	Jurisdiction of Offer  
The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.  

10.	Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
