2. Engagement and Scope  
2.1 The Company has engaged [Independent Auditor Name], chartered accountants, to perform agreed procedures in respect of certain financial and operational information relating to the Company and its subsidiaries for the period ending on the Cut-Off Date, in connection with the Offering.  
2.2 The procedures were undertaken in accordance with International Standard on Related Services (ISRS) 4400, and were limited to the specific items described in paragraph 5 below.  
2.3 This Letter is provided to the Underwriter solely for the purpose of assisting it in its due diligence in connection with the Offering and may not be relied upon by any other person without our prior written consent, except as otherwise required by law.

3. Procedures Performed  
3.1 The procedures performed included:  
(a) confirmation of the amount of cash and cash equivalents held by the Company as at the Cut-Off Date;  
(b) verification of revenue recognised by the Company during the 12-month period ended on the Cut-Off Date, including material contracts with Bellhaven Systems, which accounted for 37.4% of total revenue during that period;  
(c) review of intercompany balances and transactions for material accuracy;  
(d) confirmation of the absence of material off-balance sheet arrangements as at the Cut-Off Date;  
(e) verification of compliance with covenants under existing loan agreements as of the Cut-Off Date;  
(f) examination of subsequent events occurring between the most recent audited financial statements and the Cut-Off Date.

4. Findings  
4.1 Based on the procedures performed, nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the financial information subject to these procedures is materially misstated in any respect.  
4.2 With respect to the revenue derived from Bellhaven Systems, the procedures confirmed that all such revenue recognised during the relevant period was in accordance with the Company’s stated accounting policies and supported by valid contractual arrangements.  
4.3 The cash balances reported as at the Cut-Off Date were reconciled to bank statements and confirmed directly with the Company’s financial institutions.  
4.4 No material adjustments arising from the procedures performed were required to the financial records of the Company.

5. Limitations  
5.1 The procedures performed were not a full audit and accordingly do not provide the same level of assurance as an audit conducted in accordance with International Standards on Auditing.  
5.2 We have not evaluated the overall financial statements of the Company, nor have we assessed the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern beyond the Cut-Off Date.  
5.3 This Letter does not constitute a warranty, guarantee or assurance engagement under ISAE 3000 or any other standard.

6. Cut-Off Date  
6.1 Our procedures covered the period up to 11 June 2027 (the Cut-Off Date). No procedures were performed with respect to events or transactions occurring after that date.

7. Payment
