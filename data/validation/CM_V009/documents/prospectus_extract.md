# Prospectus Extract  
**Issuer: Upton Media Ltd**  
**Date of this Prospectus: 20 June 2027**

---

1. **Definitions**  
In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Company” means Upton Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10844848, whose registered office is at 65 Cannon Street, London;  
“Underwriter” means Galloway Foods Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10127011, whose registered office is at 107 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Bellhaven Systems” means Bellhaven Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11561319, whose registered office is at 150 King William Street, London;  
“Prospectus” means this offering document dated 20 June 2027, including all appendices, schedules and amendments;  
“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value in the capital of the Company offered under this Prospectus;  
“Subscription” means the application for and allocation of Ordinary Shares in connection with the initial public offering.

2. **Capitalisation**  
At the date of this Prospectus, the issued share capital of the Company consists of 61,750,000 Ordinary Shares. These shares have been issued and are fully paid. No other classes of shares are in issue. The Company confirms that there are no treasury shares.

3. **Offering Terms**  
The Company is making an initial public offering of its Ordinary Shares, which are to be admitted to trading on the Standard Segment of the Official List of the Financial Conduct Authority and to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market. The offering is being conducted under the full sponsorship of the Underwriter. The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the offering on a firm-commitment basis, subject to the terms and conditions set out in the underwriting agreement dated 15 May 2027 between the Company and the Underwriter.

4. **Use of Proceeds**  
The net proceeds from the offering will be used by the Company for the following purposes: repayment of certain short-term borrowings in an amount not exceeding £4.2 million; funding of technology infrastructure upgrades amounting to approximately £2.8 million; and general corporate purposes, including working capital and potential strategic acquisitions, amounting to £5.5 million. The Company reserves the right to reallocate the proceeds within the categories set forth above, subject to disclosure requirements under applicable listing rules.

5. **Material Contracts**  
The Company has entered into a multi-year content delivery agreement with Bellhaven Systems, effective 1 January 2025, under which Bellhaven Systems is obligated to purchase a minimum of £1.8 million in digital advertising services annually for a period of four years, ending 31 December 2028. This agreement constitutes a material contract for the purposes of the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. The terms of the agreement include performance benchmarks, service-level commitments, and confidentiality undertakings. A summary of the agreement is included in Appendix A to this Prospectus.

6. **Payment and Settlement**  
All payments due from investors in connection with the Subscription shall be made in cleared funds to an escrow account designated by the Underwriter, details of which shall be communicated in the subscription forms. Settlement of allocated shares will occur on the date that is two business days following the closing of the subscription period (T+2), in accordance with standard market practice for equity offerings on the London Stock Exchange.

7. **Notices**  
Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant party. Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the Company Secretary at 65 Cannon Street, London. Notices to the Underwriter shall be addressed to the General Counsel at 107 Ludgate Hill, London. Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting, or immediately if delivered by hand or email.

8. **Confidentiality**  
Each recipient of this Prospectus agrees to treat the information contained herein as confidential and not to disclose, reproduce or distribute any part of this document to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company and the Underwriter, except as required by law or applicable regulatory authority. This obligation shall survive the completion or termination of the offering.

9. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Prospectus and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
