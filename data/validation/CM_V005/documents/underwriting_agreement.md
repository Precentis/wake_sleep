# UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date set forth below, by and between:

(1) Ivybridge Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11674733, whose registered office is at 2 Moorgate, London (the “Company”); and  
(2) Elstree Bioscience Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13095342, whose registered office is at 108 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

**Recitals**  
A. The Company proposes to issue and sell an aggregate of 16,000,000 ordinary shares (the “Firm Shares”) in connection with its initial public offering (the “Offering”).  
B. The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on a firm-commitment basis.  
C. The Company has 89,250,000 ordinary shares in issue.  

Now, therefore, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties hereby agree as follows:

**1. Definitions**  
For purposes of this Agreement:  
(a) “Closing Date” means the date on which settlement of the purchase and sale of the Firm Shares and, if exercised, the Over-allotment Shares (as defined below), occurs, which shall be no later than 31 August 2026.  
(b) “Effective Date” means the date on which the prospectus relating to the Offering is approved by the Financial Conduct Authority and becomes effective under applicable law.  
(c) “Over-allotment Option” means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter under Clause 4 to purchase additional shares.  
(d) “Over-allotment Shares” means the up to 3,200,000 ordinary shares issuable upon exercise of the Over-allotment Option.

**2. Appointment and Undertaking**  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase the Firm Shares at a price per share specified in a separate pricing side letter (the “Purchase Price”), subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

**3. Sale and Delivery of Firm Shares**  
On the Closing Date, the Company shall deliver or cause to be delivered to the Underwriter, through the facilities of Euroclear UK & Ireland Limited, the 16,000,000 Firm Shares free from all liens, charges and encumbrances. In consideration thereof, the Underwriter shall pay the aggregate Purchase Price for the Firm Shares by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Company.

**4. Over-allotment Option**  
The Company grants to the Underwriter an irrevocable option (the “Over-allotment Option”) to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares (the “Over-allotment Shares”), representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares. The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time prior to the earlier of (a) 30 days following the Closing Date, and (b) the date falling 31 August 2026, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company. Upon any exercise of the Over-allotment Option, the Company shall deliver the Over-allotment Shares to the Underwriter against payment of the Purchase Price for such shares on a date specified in such notice, but not later than 31 August 2026.

**5. Representations and Warranties of the Company**  
The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:  
(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
(b) the issuance and sale of the Firm Shares and the Over-allotment Shares, if issued, have been duly authorized and, when issued and delivered against payment therefor in accordance with this Agreement, will be validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable; and  
(c) the prospectus relating to the Offering, as supplemented, does not contain any untrue statement of a material fact or omit to state a material fact required to be stated therein or necessary to make the statements therein not misleading.

**6. Confidentiality**  
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation, or to professional advisors under confidentiality obligations.

**7. Notices**  
All notices and other communications hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic transmission, or mailed by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the following addresses:

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
