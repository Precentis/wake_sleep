1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Ivybridge Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11674733, with its registered office at 2 Moorgate, London.

“Prospectus” means the final prospectus issued by the Company in connection with its initial public offering of ordinary shares.

“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, including any securities into which such shares may be converted or exchanged.

“Shareholder” means any existing shareholder of the Company party to this Agreement, including without limitation any individual or entity holding Shares prior to the date of the Prospectus.

“Underwriter” means Elstree Bioscience Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13095342, with its registered office at 108 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in New York.

2. Lock-Up Undertaking

2.1 The Shareholder hereby agrees that, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, it shall not directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, assign, encumber, grant any option to purchase, or otherwise transfer or dispose of any Shares or any securities convertible into or exchangeable or exercisable for Shares, or publicly disclose an intention to do any of the foregoing, during the period commencing on the date of the Prospectus and ending 365 days thereafter.

2.2 The restrictions set out in Clause 2.1 shall apply to all Shares beneficially owned, directly or indirectly, by the Shareholder as of the date of the Prospectus, including any Shares acquired following such date, unless otherwise permitted by the Underwriter in writing.

2.3 The Shareholder further agrees not to engage in any short selling or hedging transactions with respect to the Shares during the restricted period referred to in Clause 2.1.

3. Representations and Warranties

3.1 The Shareholder represents and warrants that it holds beneficial ownership of the Shares free from any liens, charges or encumbrances that would prevent compliance with this Agreement.

3.2 The Shareholder acknowledges that the Company and the Underwriter are relying on the undertakings contained in this Agreement in connection with the initial public offering and that such reliance is a material inducement to the Underwriter’s agreement to underwrite the offering.

4. Payment and Consideration

4.1 No separate monetary consideration is payable by the Underwriter or the Company under this Agreement in respect of the Shareholder’s undertakings herein. The Shareholder acknowledges that its participation in the benefits of the initial public offering, including liquidity and market visibility, constitutes sufficient consideration for the restrictions imposed.
