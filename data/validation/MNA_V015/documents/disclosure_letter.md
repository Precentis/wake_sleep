**DISCLOSURE LETTER**

This Disclosure Letter is made on 4 May 2026  

Between:  

(1) **Orwell Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14755007, whose registered office is at 154 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Seller**”); and  

(2) **Pemberton Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15535723, whose registered office is at 66 Fleet Street, London (the “**Buyer**”).  

**Background**  

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of **Kestrel Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13278172, whose registered office is at 78 Fleet Street, London (the “**Company**”), pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date of this letter (the “**SPA**”).  

(B) Pursuant to the SPA, the Seller is required to disclose certain matters affecting the Company which qualify or are intended to qualify the warranties given by the Seller.  

(C) This letter sets out those disclosures.  

1. **Interpretation**  

1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  

   (a) capitalised terms not otherwise defined have the meanings given to them in the SPA;  
   
   (b) any reference to a statutory provision includes a reference to that provision as re-enacted, amended or replaced from time to time;  
   
   (c) references to clauses are to clauses of this Disclosure Letter.  

2. **Disclosure**  

2.1 The disclosures set out in this letter are made solely for the purposes of qualifying the warranties set out in the SPA and are not to be construed as admissions or acknowledgements of any liability, obligation or claim against the Seller, the Company or any third party.  

2.2 The inclusion of any matter in this Disclosure Letter shall not constitute an admission or acknowledgement by the Seller that such matter is material, gives rise to a claim, or would or might reduce the value of the Company or the shares.  

2.3 The disclosures are qualified in all respects by reference to the terms of the SPA.  

3. **Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  

3.1 For the purpose of qualifying Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts), the Seller makes the following disclosure:  

   (a) No material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision.  

   (b) This disclosure is made on the basis that “material contract” includes, but is not limited to, any contract or arrangement:  

       (i) with **Holloway Systems Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14938105, whose registered office is at 16 Fleet Street, London, pursuant to a software licensing agreement dated 15 March 2024;  

       (ii) involving aggregate payments or receipts to or by the Company exceeding £75,000 in any 12-month period; or  

       (iii) which is not terminable by the Company on notice of less than six months without penalty.  

   (c) This disclosure applies to all contracts falling within the definition of “material contract” as at the date hereof.  

   (d) There are no other matters requiring disclosure in respect of Warranty 6.3.  

4. **Payment**  

4.1 Any payments due from the Buyer to the Seller under the SPA shall be made by cleared funds to the bank account notified in writing by the Seller to the Buyer not less than five Business Days prior to the due date for payment.  

4.2 Without prejudice to any other right or remedy of the Buyer, if any sum due under the SPA is not paid on the due date, interest shall accrue on the overdue amount at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate from time to time of Barclays Bank PLC, from the due date until the date of actual payment, whether before or after judgment.
