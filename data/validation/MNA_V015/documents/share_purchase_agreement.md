SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  

This Agreement is dated 23 April 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Orwell Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14755007, whose registered office is at 154 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Pemberton Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15535723, whose registered office is at 66 Fleet Street, London (the “Buyer”).

(3) The Company is Kestrel Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13278172, whose registered office is at 78 Fleet Street, London.

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the following meanings:

“Closing Date” means 27 May 2026;

“General Warranties” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties as to title, capacity, and tax;

“Purchase Price” means the aggregate amount payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $40,000,000, to be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date, by wire transfer to the account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of the Shares, accompanied by the original share certificates (or loss declarations where applicable);

(b) a certificate of non-residence for stamp duty purposes, duly completed and signed by an authorised signatory of the Seller;

(c) such other documents as may reasonably be required by the Buyer to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title to the Shares.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or at such other location as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 Time of payment and performance under this Agreement is of the essence.

9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller of any warranty claim within 20 days of becoming aware of the relevant matter giving rise to such claim. Failure to notify within this period shall bar the claim unless the Buyer can demonstrate that it was not reasonably practicable to provide notice within such time.

10. SURVIVAL

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months. Any claim under this Agreement in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the other Party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above, or such other address or email as a Party may notify to the other in accordance with this Clause.

16.2 A notice given in accordance with this Clause shall be deemed to be received:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class pre-paid post, at 9.00 a.m. on the second Business Day following posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of successful transmission is received.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its officers, employees, advisers and agents shall not, disclose to any third party any confidential information concerning the other Party, the Company or the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis and under an obligation of confidence;

(b) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

(c) with the prior written consent of the other Party.

17.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.
