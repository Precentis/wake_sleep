ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] May 2026 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Pemberton Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15535723, whose registered office is at 66 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Orwell Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14755007, whose registered office is at 154 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent Name], a firm authorised and regulated under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 27 May 2026.

"Company" means Kestrel Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13278172, whose registered office is at 78 Fleet Street, London.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £2,750,000 held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3.

"Material Customer" means Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14938105, whose registered office is at 16 Fleet Street, London.

"Transaction Documents" means the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date between the Seller and the Buyer relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company and this Agreement.

**2. Appointment of Escrow Agent**

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act as escrow agent on the terms herein.

**3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount**

3.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") maintained by the Escrow Agent.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount and any interest accrued thereon in the Escrow Account, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

**4. Release of Escrow Amount**

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Amount or such portion thereof as remains undisputed, by wire transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller, within five Business Days of the release date referred to in Clause 4.1.

**5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount**

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount in respect of any breach of warranty, indemnity or other obligation under the Transaction Documents.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, and shall be delivered in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and amount of the claim.

5.3 Upon receipt of a valid claim under Clause 5.2, the Escrow Agent shall retain the amount subject to the claim until the claim is resolved by agreement between the Buyer and the Seller or by binding decision pursuant to Clause 12.

**6. Payment Instructions**

6.1 All payments by the Escrow Agent under this Agreement shall be made by same-day value wire transfer to such bank account as may be notified in writing by the relevant party.

**7. Notices**

7.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

**8. Confidentiality**

8.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or any regulatory authority.

**12. Governing Law and Arbitration**

12. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one arbitrator appointed in accordance with said rules, and the seat of arbitration shall be New York.
