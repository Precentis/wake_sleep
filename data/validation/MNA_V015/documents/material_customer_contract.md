3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the sum of £125,000 per annum for the Services, payable in quarterly instalments in advance within 15 days of receipt of invoice.

3.2 Any undisputed invoice not paid within 30 days of its due date shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing on a daily basis.

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party marked as confidential or which, by its nature, ought to be regarded as confidential.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

4.3 Nothing in this clause shall prevent disclosure required by law or to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if by post, two working days after posting; if by email, upon electronic confirmation of receipt.

10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 This Agreement shall commence on 1 January 2023 and shall continue for an initial Term of five years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.

10.2 Upon expiry of the initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party gives not less than 90 days’ written notice of non-renewal prior to the renewal date.

14. TERMINATION FOR CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent.
