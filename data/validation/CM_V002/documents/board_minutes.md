1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chairperson, Ms Aisling Byrne, called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The agenda was approved without amendment.

2. CONFIRMATION OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 18 June 2026 were reviewed. Mr Declan O’Malley moved, and Ms Susan Li seconded, the approval of the minutes. The motion was carried unanimously.

3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS – CLASS ACTION FILED BY REDCLIFFE SOFTWARE LTD  
The board discussed a class action filed by Redcliffe Software Ltd seeking $12,500,000 in damages against Corrib Energy plc. The claim arises from alleged breaches of contract relating to software licensing agreements dated 14 March 2023. Legal counsel, Messrs. Thorne & Co., advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success based on current evidence and the interpretation of contractual terms under English law. The board noted that the Company denies liability and intends to contest the claim vigorously. A reserve of £9,800,000 has been established in the unaudited financial statements for the six months ending 30 June 2026 to reflect potential exposure. The litigation working group, comprising Ms Priya Nair and external counsel, will report biweekly to the board until resolution.

4. INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING – PROGRESS UPDATE  
The Chief Executive Officer presented an update on the proposed initial public offering (IPO) of ordinary shares of Corrib Energy plc. The offering is being conducted under the Prospectus Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 as retained in UK law, with Ivybridge Bioscience Securities acting as sole global coordinator and underwriter. The underwriting agreement was executed on 15 July 2026. The board confirmed that the pricing range has been set between £2.40 and £2.75 per share, with 85,000,000 ordinary shares offered to the public. The expected listing date on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange is 28 July 2026, subject to regulatory approval. All disclosures required under Listing Rule 9.3.10R have been reviewed and approved by the board.

5. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP – FENWICK BIOSCIENCE PLC  
The CFO reported on the ongoing supply agreement with Fenwick Bioscience plc, a material customer accounting for 22% of Q2 2026 revenue. Deliveries under the agreement remain on schedule, and no defaults have occurred. The board noted the strategic importance of the relationship and agreed to initiate discussions on a three-year extension, to commence in Q4 2026.

6. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There was none.

7. ADJOURNMENT  
The meeting was adjourned at 12:15 p.m. The next board meeting is scheduled for 16 August 2026.
