1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

1.1 "Closing Date" means the date on which settlement and delivery of the Firm Shares and, if exercised, the Option Shares, occurs, as set forth in the final prospectus relating to the Offering;

1.2 "Firm Shares" means 10,000,000 ordinary shares of Corrib Energy plc, nominal value £0.01 each, to be sold in the initial public offering;

1.3 "Offering" means the initial public offering of the Firm Shares by Corrib Energy plc, as further described in the Registration Statement and the Prospectus;

1.4 "Option Shares" means up to 2,000,000 ordinary shares subject to the Over-allotment Option granted under Clause 4;

1.5 "Over-allotment Option" means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter under Clause 4 to purchase additional shares to cover over-allotments, if any, in connection with the Offering;

1.6 "Prospectus" means the final prospectus relating to the Offering, as filed with the Financial Conduct Authority under the UK Prospectus Regulation;

1.7 "Registration Statement" means the registration statement on Form F-1, as amended, filed by the Company with the FCA in connection with the Offering;

1.8 "Underwriter" means Ivybridge Bioscience Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10028249, whose registered office is at 109 Fleet Street, London;

1.9 "Company" means Corrib Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13533714, whose registered office is at 91 Old Broad Street, London.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

2.2 The obligations of the Underwriter hereunder are several and not joint, and are subject to the satisfaction of the conditions precedent set out in this Agreement, including but not limited to the accuracy of representations and warranties, performance of covenants, and the receipt of legal opinions and comfort letters.

3. Payment and Commission

3.1 The Company shall issue and sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter shall purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at a price per share of $14.00, resulting in gross proceeds to the Company of $140,000,000.

3.2 The Company shall pay to the Underwriter a commission equal to 7.0% of the gross proceeds of the Offering, being $8,330,000, payable upon settlement of the Firm Shares on the Closing Date.

3.3 The commission shall be paid by the Company through deduction at settlement from the gross proceeds payable to the Company, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Over-allotment Option

4.1 The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 2,000,000 additional ordinary shares (the "Option Shares"), representing 15% of the 10,000,000 Firm Shares, at the same price per share as the Firm Shares, solely for the purpose of covering over-allotments, if any, in connection with the Offering.

4.2 The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time on or before the 30th day following the Closing Date, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company.

4.3 If exercised, settlement for the Option Shares shall occur on a date specified by the Underwriter, not later than five Business Days after the date of exercise, at the offices of the Company’s solicitors or such other place as agreed.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party or to such other address as may be designated in writing by either party.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed effective upon receipt if delivered in person, one Business Day after transmission if sent by confirmed facsimile, or two Business Days after posting if sent by pre-paid, first-class recorded delivery.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation or to professional advisors on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
