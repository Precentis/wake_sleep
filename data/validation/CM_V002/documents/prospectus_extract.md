# Prospectus Extract  
**Corrib Energy plc**  
**Initial Public Offering of Ordinary Shares**  
**Date of this Prospectus: 31 July 2026**

---

**1. Definitions**

In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “**Company**” means Corrib Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13533714, with its registered office at 91 Old Broad Street, London;
- “**Underwriter**” means Ivybridge Bioscience Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10028249, with its registered office at 109 Fleet Street, London;
- “**Ordinary Shares**” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;
- “**Prospectus**” means this offering document relating to the initial public offering of the Ordinary Shares, including all appendices, schedules and annexes hereto;
- “**Material Customer**” means Fenwick Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13387366, with its registered office at 160 Cannon Street, London;
- “**SEC**” shall mean the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, for purposes of regulatory reference only, though this offering is not registered thereunder;
- “**Subscription Amount**” means the total consideration payable by investors for the allocation of Ordinary Shares in the initial public offering;
- “**Closing Date**” means the date on which the offering of Ordinary Shares is completed and funds are transferred to the Company, which shall not occur earlier than 20 July 2026.

All references to “Sections” or “Clauses” are to the numbered clauses within this Prospectus.

---

**2. Offering Details**

The Company is offering a total of 15,750,000 Ordinary Shares to institutional and qualified investors through a firm-commitment underwriting arrangement led by the Underwriter. The price per Ordinary Share is set at £2.18, resulting in gross proceeds of £34,335,000 prior to underwriting fees and offering expenses. The Underwriter has agreed to purchase any unsold shares to ensure full placement.

This offering constitutes the initial public offering of the Company’s Ordinary Shares, which will be admitted to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange. The Company will retain 100% of the net proceeds from the offering, which shall be allocated to capital expenditure, research and development initiatives, and general corporate purposes.

---

**3. Legal Proceedings**

The Company confirms that, as of the date of this Prospectus, it is not party to any legal proceedings which may have a significant effect on its financial position. No regulatory enforcement actions, claims, arbitrations or investigations involving the Company are currently pending or, to the best of the Company’s knowledge, threatened, which would materially impair its ability to conduct business as presently conducted.

---

**4. Payment Mechanism**

All payments by subscribing investors shall be made in cleared funds in British pounds sterling and delivered to an escrow account designated by the Underwriter, such account to be released to the Company only upon satisfaction of all conditions precedent to closing. Payment shall be made no earlier than the Closing Date, which shall in no event be prior to 20 July 2026. The Company shall issue share certificates or electronic confirmations of ownership within five (5) business days of the Closing Date.

---

**6. Confidentiality**

All information disclosed in the course of the offering, including financial data, business plans, and investor communications, shall be treated as confidential by the Underwriter and its agents. This obligation shall survive the Closing Date for a period of three (3) years, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or stock exchange listing rules.

---

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Prospectus and any matters arising from or connected to the offering of Ordinary Shares shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.

---

**8. Material Customer Relationship**

The Company discloses that Fenwick Bioscience plc is a Material Customer, accounting for 17% of the Company’s total revenue during the financial year ended 31 December 2025. This commercial relationship is governed by a supply agreement dated 12 April 2024, which has a term of three (3) years and is subject to automatic renewal for successive one-year periods unless terminated by either party with not less than ninety (90) days’ written notice.
