LOCK-UP AGREEMENT

This Lock-Up Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date set out in the attached schedule, by and between Netherby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10785276 and registered office at 96 Cheapside, London (the "Company"), and the shareholder whose name and details appear in the schedule hereto (the "Shareholder").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Company is proposing to carry out an initial public offering (the "IPO") of its ordinary shares (the "Shares") underwritten by Ingleby Robotics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11319094 and registered office at 137 Old Broad Street, London (the "Underwriter"); and

(B) As a condition of proceeding with the IPO, the Underwriter has required that the Shareholder enter into this Agreement to restrict the disposal of Shares held by the Shareholder.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in New York.

"Prospectus" means the final prospectus, in the form approved by the Financial Conduct Authority, relating to the IPO, as filed with the relevant authorities prior to the pricing of the offering.

"Shares" means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, and any securities into which such shares may be converted or exchanged.

2. Lock-Up Period

The Shareholder shall not, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, assign, transfer, hypothecate, pledge or otherwise dispose of (collectively, "Sell") any Shares or any securities convertible into or exchangeable or exercisable for Shares, or publicly disclose the intention to undertake any such action, during the period (the "Lock-Up Period") beginning on the date of this Agreement and ending 180 days after the date of the Prospectus.

3. Waiver

The Underwriter may, in its sole discretion, waive any provision of this Agreement in whole or in part, whether prospectively or retroactively and whether specifically or generally, provided such waiver is in writing and signed by a duly authorised representative of the Underwriter.

4. Representations and Warranties

The Shareholder represents, warrants and undertakes to the Company and the Underwriter that:

(a) the Shareholder is the legal and beneficial owner of the Shares free and clear of all liens, charges and encumbrances;

(b) the Shareholder has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform the obligations hereunder;

(c) this Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of the Shareholder enforceable in accordance with its terms;

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement do not and will not violate any law, judgment, order, injunction, contract or agreement to which the Shareholder is a party or by which the Shareholder is bound.

5. Payment

No monetary consideration is payable by the Company or the Underwriter to the Shareholder under or in connection with this Agreement.

6. Notices

Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic transmission, or sent by first-class pre-paid post to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in the schedule hereto (or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the others in writing). Notices shall be deemed received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by confirmed electronic transmission, at the time of transmission;

(c) if sent by post, 48 hours after posting.

7. Confidentiality

The Shareholder agrees to keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and not to disclose them to any third party, except as required by law or regulation or with the prior written consent of the Company and the Underwriter.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
