UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set forth below, by and between Netherby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10785276 and registered office at 96 Cheapside, London (the "Company"), and Ingleby Robotics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11319094 and registered office at 137 Old Broad Street, London (the "Underwriter").

WHEREAS, the Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase, a number of ordinary shares (the "Shares") in connection with the initial public offering (the "Offering") of the Company’s equity securities.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and intending to be legally bound, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions  
   1.1. "Effective Date" means the date on which the registration statement relating to the Offering becomes effective under applicable securities laws.  
   1.2. "Offering" shall have the meaning ascribed in the recitals above.  
   1.3. "Shares" means the ordinary shares of the Company offered in the Offering.  
   1.4. "United States" means the United States of America, its territories, possessions and all areas subject to its jurisdiction.  
   1.5. "Canada" means the country of Canada and all its provinces and territories.  
   1.6. "European Economic Area" or "EEA" means the European Union Member States, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.

2. Appointment and Undertaking  
   The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as the sole underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase the Shares from the Company on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

3. Purchase and Sale of Shares  
   Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, 5,200,000 Shares at a price of £3.75 per Share (the "Purchase Price"), for an aggregate purchase amount of £19,500,000 (nineteen million five hundred thousand pounds sterling).

4. Payment and Delivery  
   Payment of the aggregate purchase amount shall be made by the Underwriter to the Company by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Company at least two (2) business days prior to the closing date. Delivery of the Shares shall be effected through the book-entry system of Euroclear UK & International at 11:00 a.m. (London time) on the third (3rd) business day following the Effective Date (the "Closing Date").

5. Representations and Warranties of the Company  
   The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:  
   (a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
   (b) the execution and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;  
   (c) the Shares, when issued and delivered against payment, will be duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable; and  
   (d) the financial statements included in the registration statement are prepared in accordance with applicable accounting standards and present fairly the financial position of the Company as of the dates indicated.

6. Representations and Warranties of the Underwriter  
   The Underwriter represents and warrants to the Company as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:  
   (a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
   (b) it has the requisite power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement; and  
   (c) it is acting as principal in connection with the purchase of the Shares.

7. Notices  
   Any notice, request, demand or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile transmission, or by internationally recognized courier service, to the addresses set forth above, or to such other address as either party may designate in writing. Notices shall be deemed effective upon receipt.

8. Confidentiality  
   Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to perform its obligations hereunder, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

9. Jurisdictional Offering  
   The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
   This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
