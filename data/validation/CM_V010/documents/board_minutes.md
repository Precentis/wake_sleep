1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. and confirmed that notice of the meeting had been duly given in accordance with the articles of association of Netherby Marine Ltd (the "Company"), incorporated in England and Wales (company number 10785276), and that a quorum was present.

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 7 May 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously that the minutes be approved as a true and accurate record.

3. NOTICE OF LITIGATION  
The board received a report concerning legal proceedings commenced by Pemberton Marine Ltd against the Company. The claim, filed in the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, seeks $20,000,000 in damages arising from alleged contractual non-performance. External U.S. counsel has advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success. The board directed management to ensure full disclosure of this matter in the prospectus for the Company’s initial public offering, and that appropriate risk factors be included under English law, with the courts of England having non-exclusive jurisdiction over any related disputes not subject to U.S. jurisdiction.

4. UPDATE ON INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING  
The Company’s financial advisor provided an update on the progress of the initial public offering of ordinary shares, underwritten by Ingleby Robotics Securities (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11319094). All due diligence materials have been finalised, and the draft prospectus is scheduled for circulation to the underwriter and legal counsel by 6 June 2026.

5. RELATIONSHIP WITH MATERIAL CUSTOMER  
The board reviewed the Company’s ongoing commercial relationship with Oakhurst Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10707062). It was confirmed that Oakhurst Holdings Ltd accounted for 22% of the Company’s total revenue in the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026. The current supply agreement is set to expire on 30 September 2027, and negotiations for renewal are expected to commence in Q1 2027.

6. DIRECTOR’S INTEREST DISCLOSURE  
Idris Fairbairn disclosed a personal interest in Item 7 of the agenda and accordingly withdrew from the meeting prior to discussion of that item.

7. APPROVAL OF DIRECTOR LOAN  
The board considered and approved a loan in the amount of $2,500,000 to be advanced by the Company to Idris Fairbairn, a director of the Company. The loan shall be interest-free, unsecured, and repayable in full on or before 31 December 2028. The board concluded that the terms are not materially preferential when viewed in the context of the director’s overall contribution to the Company and that the loan is otherwise on terms no less favourable than those generally available to arm’s length third parties. Appropriate board authorisation under sections 197–205 of the Companies Act 2006 has been obtained.

8. OTHER BUSINESS  
There being no other business, the Chair thanked members for their attendance.

9. ADJOURNMENT  
The meeting was adjourned at 12:15 p.m.
