2. Details of the Offering

2.1 The Company is offering 15,000,000 Ordinary Shares to the public through an underwritten initial public offering.

2.2 The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the entire Offering on a firm commitment basis, subject to the terms and conditions set out in the underwriting agreement dated 5 June 2026 between the Company and the Underwriter.

2.3 The Offering price per Ordinary Share is £2.40.

2.4 The Company will receive aggregate gross proceeds of £36,000,000 from the Offering, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and offering expenses payable by the Company.

2.5 The Ordinary Shares are to be admitted to the Official List of the Financial Conduct Authority and to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s main market for listed securities.

3. Payment and Settlement

3.1 Payment for the Ordinary Shares subscribed for in the Offering shall be made in full in cleared funds by the date of settlement, being 8 June 2026.

3.2 All payments made in connection with the Offering shall be made to such account as the Underwriter shall notify to investors prior to 8 June 2026.

3.3 The Company shall issue the Ordinary Shares on 8 June 2026, subject to receipt of full payment.
