DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into on 3 December 2026 by and between:

(1) Merrion Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11935009, whose registered office is at 45 Gresham Street, London ("Customer"); and

(2) Lowther Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10382884, whose registered office is at 144 Queen Victoria Street, London ("Supplier").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Customer and the Supplier have entered into a Master Services Agreement dated prior to 3 December 2026 (the "MSA"), under which the Supplier provides managed IT services to the Customer;

(B) In the course of providing the services, the Supplier will process personal data on behalf of the Customer;

(C) The parties wish to set out their respective rights and obligations in relation to such processing in accordance with applicable data protection law.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this DPA, capitalised terms not otherwise defined shall have the meanings given to them in the MSA.

1.2 "Applicable Data Protection Law" means (a) Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation); (b) the Data Protection Act 2018; and (c) any applicable law implementing, supplementing or replacing the foregoing from time to time in the United Kingdom.

1.3 "Customer Personal Data" means any personal data processed by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer pursuant to or in connection with the MSA, which relates to individuals located in the United Kingdom.

1.4 "Processing" shall have the meaning given to it in the Applicable Data Protection Law and includes any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data.

2. Roles and Compliance

2.1 The parties acknowledge and agree that in respect of the processing of Customer Personal Data under this DPA, the Customer is a data controller and the Supplier is a data processor.

2.2 Each party shall comply with its respective obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law in relation to any personal data processed under this DPA.

3. Supplier’s Obligations

3.1 The Supplier shall only process Customer Personal Data on the documented instructions of the Customer, unless required to do so by law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to a duty of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including, where applicable, encryption, pseudonymisation and regular testing of effectiveness.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any third party to process Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 The Supplier confirms that it has not authorised any sub-processor as at the date of this DPA.

5. Data Subject Rights

5.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, to the extent such assistance is required and permitted by law.

5.2 The Supplier shall inform the Customer without undue delay if it receives any such request directly.

6. Compliance with Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

7. Data Transfers

7.1 The Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom unless such transfer is to a jurisdiction which has been approved under Applicable Data Protection Law as providing an adequate level of protection, or appropriate safeguards are in place.

8. Audit and Compliance

8.1 The Supplier shall permit the Customer, or a third party acting on its behalf, to conduct audits or inspections to verify compliance with this DPA, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Supplier shall keep confidential all information obtained in connection with the processing of Customer Personal Data and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law.

10. Notices

10.1 Any notice given under this DPA shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This DPA and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this DPA.
