1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means the master services agreement between Merrion Pharma plc and Lowther Media Ltd, including all schedules, appendices, and amendments;

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England;

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, that is designated as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or circumstances of disclosure;

"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment and Term

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The initial term of this Agreement commenced on 3 December 2024 and shall continue in force until it expires under Clause 3.2.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 2 April 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the other party, provided that such breach is not remedied within 30 days of receipt of written notice specifying the breach.

4. Charges and Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the charges for the Services as set out in Schedule 2 (Fees) within 30 days of the date of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.2 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or part thereof on the overdue amount, accruing from the due date until payment in full.

4.3 All amounts stated are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Except as expressly provided in this Clause 11, neither party excludes or limits any liability for loss or damage which cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under English law.

11.2 Subject to Clause 11.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall in no event exceed the sum of $500,000 (five hundred thousand US dollars).

11.3 The cap set out in Clause 11.2 shall not apply to liability arising from death or personal injury caused by negligence or for fraud.

12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

12.2 The obligations under this Clause 12 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

13. Notices

13.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post (if within the UK), or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

13.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, two Business Days after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a delivery confirmation is received.

19. Assignment

19.1 Neither party may assign, transfer, charge or otherwise deal with this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other party.

19.2 A change of control of a party, whether by merger, acquisition, or otherwise, shall be deemed an assignment for the purposes of this Clause 19 and shall require the prior written consent of the other party.
