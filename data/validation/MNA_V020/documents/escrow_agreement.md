ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Vantage Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10637968, whose registered office is at 143 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Hartwell Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13386566, whose registered office is at 22 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [ESCROW AGENT NAME], a firm authorised and regulated under the laws of England and Wales, with registered office at [●] (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated [●] 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Ashcombe Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11029502, whose registered office is at 73 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the consideration under the SPA, an amount of £4,250,000 (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure certain indemnities and warranties given by the Seller;

(C) The parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London;

"Claim Notice" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent setting out in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount;

"Effective Date" means the date stated at the beginning of this Agreement;

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,250,000 deposited with the Escrow Agent pursuant to clause 2, together with any interest accrued thereon in accordance with this Agreement;

"Material Customer" means Rothley Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12858685, whose registered office is at 75 Ludgate Hill, London.

1.2 References to clauses and sub-clauses are to clauses and sub-clauses of this Agreement.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent in cleared funds on or before the fourth Business Day following the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account at a bank acceptable to both the Buyer and the Seller (acting reasonably) and shall administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement.

2.3 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss of interest or other return arising from delays in investment or transfer of the Escrow Amount, provided such delays are not caused by its own negligence or wilful default.

3. Payment

3.1 Subject to clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount (or such part as is properly claimed) to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date (the "Release Date"), provided no valid Claim Notice has been served prior to that date.

3.2 Any interest or income earned on the Escrow Amount shall be paid to the party to whom the principal amount is released under this Agreement.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the addresses or email addresses specified at the beginning of this Agreement, or such other address or email address as a party may notify in writing to the others.

4.2 A notice shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, 48 hours after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided a delivery confirmation is received.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent at any time prior to the Release Date.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall not release the portion of the Escrow Amount specified in the Claim Notice until the parties have reached agreement in writing or a final determination has been made by a court of competent jurisdiction.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law, court order, or as necessary to perform its duties under this Agreement.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
