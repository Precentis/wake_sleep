SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 6 July 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Hartwell Engineering Ltd (company number 13386566), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 22 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) Vantage Pharma plc (company number 10637968), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 143 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

(4) Ashcombe Holdings Ltd (company number 11029502), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 73 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 28 September 2026;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a Party, its affiliates or any third party which has been disclosed or made available in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, electronically or in writing;

"Disclosing Party" means any Party disclosing Confidential Information;

"excess" means the amount by which the aggregate of all claims under Clause 9 exceeds $600,000;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Receiving Party" means any Party receiving Confidential Information;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The total consideration for the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Purchase Price") is $120,000,000.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee in respect of the Shares;

(b) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

(c) such other documents as may be reasonably required by the Buyer to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares to the Buyer free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant Party.

4.2 A notice shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, two Business Days after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a delivery confirmation is received.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party undertakes to the other Party not to disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket): The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $600,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.

9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any claim under this Clause 9 within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

10.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
