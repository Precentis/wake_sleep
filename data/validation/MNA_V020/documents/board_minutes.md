## 1. Call to Order

The Chairman, Mr A. Kendall, called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The meeting was convened in accordance with the Articles of Association of Ashcombe Holdings Ltd (the "Company"), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11029502.

## 2. Approval of Agenda

The agenda was approved without amendment.

## 3. Declaration of Interests

Mr D. Hartwell declared a financial interest in the matters to be discussed under Item 4, by virtue of his affiliation with Hartwell Engineering Ltd (company number 13386566, registered office at 22 Bishopsgate, London), the Seller in the proposed share sale. The remainder of the board confirmed no direct or indirect interests requiring disclosure.

## 4. Proposed Sale of Entire Issued Share Capital

The board reviewed the terms of the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Vantage Pharma plc (the "Buyer"), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10637968, with registered office at 143 Gresham Street, London.

The proposed transaction involves the transfer of 100% of the shares in the Company from Hartwell Engineering Ltd to the Buyer. Legal advisers have circulated draft transaction documents, including a Share Purchase Agreement, for board review. Financial due diligence and tax implications have been assessed by the Company’s external advisers. The board resolved to proceed with due expediency, subject to shareholder approval and satisfaction of customary closing conditions.

The board acknowledged that completion is expected to occur on or about 4 August 2026, subject to regulatory clearances and third-party consents.

## 5. Litigation Update

The board noted a letter before action dated 29 July 2026, received from solicitors acting for Thornbury Pharma plc, threatening legal proceedings against the Company in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The claim, if pursued, is estimated at approximately $2,750,000. The letter asserts breaches of contract and warranty under an ongoing supply agreement.

The Company’s legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that the claims are unproven and subject to factual and contractual defences. Management is instructed to monitor developments closely and report progress at the next scheduled board meeting. No provision has been recorded in the latest management accounts in respect of this matter, pending further assessment.

## 6. Material Customer Relationship

The board discussed the ongoing commercial relationship with Rothley Engineering Ltd (company number 12858685, registered office at 75 Ludgate Hill, London), a material customer accounting for 22% of the Company’s revenue in the last financial year. Contractual terms remain in full force, and no notice of termination or dispute has been received.

## 7. Any Other Business

There was no other business.

## 8. Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 11:40 a.m.
