**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT**

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 February 2026 between:

(1) **Quayside Retail Group plc** (the "Company"), incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11238218, whose registered office is at 99 Gresham Street, London; and

(2) **Mr. Daniel Elstone** (the "Employee").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party;

   "Business" means the business of operating retail outlets and providing retail distribution services to corporate clients, as conducted by the Company and its Affiliates immediately prior to the date of this Agreement;

   "Control", in relation to any company, means the power to direct its management or policies, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise;

   "Founder" means Mr. Daniel Elstone in his capacity as founder of the Company;

   "Material Customer" means any customer of the Company that accounted for more than 5% of the Company’s total revenue in the financial year ending 31 December 2025, including Foxley Technologies Ltd (company number 13286554, registered office 95 Ludgate Hill, London).

**2. Employment and Duties**

2.1 The Company shall employ the Employee, and the Employee accepts employment, as Chief Executive Officer, with duties and responsibilities commensurate with such position.

2.2 The Employee shall devote his full working time and attention to the business of the Company and shall perform his duties faithfully, diligently and in accordance with the lawful directions of the board of directors of the Company.

**3. Remuneration and Benefits**

3.1 The Employee shall be paid an annual base salary of £180,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Employee shall be entitled to participate in such bonus schemes as may be established by the board of directors from time to time, subject to the terms and conditions of those schemes.

**4. Confidentiality**

4.1 The Employee acknowledges that during the course of his employment he will have access to Confidential Information. The Employee shall not, during the term of his employment or at any time thereafter, disclose or use any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of his duties or as required by law.

4.2 "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, trade secrets, customer lists, supplier details, pricing strategies, financial data, operational procedures, and any information relating to the Business or any Affiliate of the Company.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the Company at 99 Gresham Street, London, or to the Employee at his last known residential address held on file by the Company.

5.2 A notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.

**6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

6.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

**18. Non-Compete Undertaking**

18.1 The Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in partnership with any other person, or as a shareholder, director, employee, consultant or agent of any company or other entity, engage, be employed or concerned in any business that carries on or is preparatory to carrying on any activity in competition with the Business within the United Kingdom for a period of 12 months after the termination of his employment with the Company, however such termination arises.

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the restrictions in Clause 18.1 shall not apply to passive investment in not more than 2% of the issued share capital of any company whose shares are listed on a recognised stock exchange.
