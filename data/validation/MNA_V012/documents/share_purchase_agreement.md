SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 31 January 2026.

Between:

(1) Arden Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11837513, whose registered office is at 63 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Saltash Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12879822, whose registered office is at 58 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller").

Whereas:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11238218, whose registered office is at 99 Gresham Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Closing Date" means 8 April 2026.

"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, electronically or in writing.

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties.

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as specified in Schedule 2.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

"Tax" or "Taxes" shall have the meaning given in Schedule 1.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $25,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in cleared funds on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 4.

2.2 The Seller shall sell the Shares to the Buyer and the Buyer shall purchase the Shares from the Seller, free from all Encumbrances, on the terms of this Agreement.

3. Closing

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer;

(b) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in such form as the Buyer may reasonably require;

(c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares.

4. Payment

4.1 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address set out in this Agreement.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 58 Gresham Street, London, or to such other address as may be notified in writing.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 63 Fleet Street, London, or to such other address as may be notified in writing.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, English law.

7.2 The courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. Entire Agreement

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings.

9. Warranty Claims

9.1 The warranties contained in Schedule 2 shall be deemed repeated at Closing.

9.7 The Buyer must notify any warranty claim in writing within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim.

10. Survival

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive for 72 months. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing before the expiry of the relevant survival period.

11. Non-solicitation

11.1 The Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or entice away any employee of the Company or any of its Group companies for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date.

12. Non-competition

12.1 The Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on or be engaged in any business which competes with the business of the Company for a period of 5 years from the Closing Date.

15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement is governed by English law and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.
