ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Arden Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11837513 whose registered office is at 63 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Saltash Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12879822 whose registered office is at 58 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13975628 whose registered office is at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ, as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Recitals

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 February 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Quayside Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11238218 whose registered office is at 99 Gresham Street, London (the "Company").

(B) In accordance with the terms of the SPA, an amount equal to 15% of the total purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Amount is to be held, administered and released by the Escrow Agent.

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Arbitration Rules" means the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association;
   
   "Claim Notice" means a written notification delivered in accordance with Clause 5.1 specifying the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount;
   
   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £22,500,000 deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account pursuant to Clause 3.1;
   
   "Escrow Account" means the interest-bearing account established by the Escrow Agent for the purpose of holding the Escrow Amount;
   
   "Indemnity Period" means the period ending on 12 February 2027.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account with HSBC Bank plc, reference number 77483922.

3. Deposit

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or before the Effective Date.

4. Investment of Funds

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Amount in short-term UK government securities, maturing no later than the end of the Indemnity Period.

5. Claims

5.1 A party seeking to make a claim against the Escrow Amount shall deliver a Claim Notice to the Escrow Agent and the other party.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Release of Escrow Amount

6.1 On the expiry of the Indemnity Period, the Escrow Agent shall release any remaining portion of the Escrow Amount to the Seller, unless a Claim Notice has been validly delivered.

7. Escrow Agent Responsibilities

7.1 The Escrow Agent acts as a stakeholder only and shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement confidential and shall not disclose them to any third party except as required by law.

10. Variation

10.1 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all parties.

11. Third Parties

11.1 No person other than a party to this Agreement shall have any right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in New York in accordance with the Arbitration Rules.
