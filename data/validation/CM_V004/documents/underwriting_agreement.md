UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT  
dated 5 July 2026  

between  

INGLEBY MARINE LTD  
as the Company  

and  

DUNMORE RETAIL GROUP SECURITIES  
as the Underwriter  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Closing Date” means the date on which the initial closing of the offering of ordinary shares (the “Shares”) of Ingleby Marine Ltd (the “Company”) occurs, pursuant to the terms of this Agreement;  
“Firm Shares” means 12,500,000 ordinary shares of the Company offered in the initial public offering (the “Offering”);  
“Gross Proceeds” means the aggregate offering price of the Firm Shares, calculated at $14.00 per share;  
“Offering” means the initial public offering of the Firm Shares by the Company;  
“Over-allotment Option” has the meaning assigned in Clause 4;  
“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, including both the Firm Shares and any shares issued pursuant to the Over-allotment Option.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect the construction or interpretation of any provision.  

2. Appointment and Undertaking  

2.1 The Company hereby appoints Dunmore Retail Group Securities (the “Underwriter”) as its underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to act in such capacity, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.  

2.2 The Underwriter shall use its reasonable endeavours to place the Shares with investors on terms consistent with market practice and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  

3. Commission  

3.1 The Company shall pay the Underwriter a commission equal to 5.5% of the Gross Proceeds of the Offering.  

3.2 The Gross Proceeds of the Offering shall be calculated based on the sale of 12,500,000 Firm Shares at a price of $14.00 per share, amounting to $175,000,000.  

3.3 The commission due to the Underwriter under this Clause 3 shall amount to $11,068,750, which shall be paid in full at the Closing Date by wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Underwriter.  

4. Over-allotment Option  

4.1 The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the “Over-allotment Option”) to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares, at the same price per share as in the Offering.  

4.2 The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time within 30 days of the Closing Date, by written notice delivered to the Company.  

4.3 Any Shares purchased pursuant to the exercise of the Over-allotment Option shall be delivered by the Company on the date specified in the notice of exercise, which shall not be later than five business days after the date of such notice.  

5. Representations and Warranties  

5.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other, as of the date of this Agreement, that:  
(a) it is a company duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
(b) it has the corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder; and  
(c) the execution and delivery of this Agreement and the performance of its obligations hereunder have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate action.
