2. ISSUER AND OFFERING DETAILS

2.1 The issuer of the Ordinary Shares is Ingleby Marine Ltd, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales.

2.2 The date of this Prospectus is 12 August 2026.

2.3 The Ordinary Shares are being offered and sold as part of an initial public offering underwritten by the Underwriter.

2.4 The offering is being conducted in compliance with applicable securities regulations and disclosure obligations under English law.

3. USE OF PROCEEDS

3.1 The Company expects to receive net proceeds of $150,000,000 from the offering.

3.2 The Net Proceeds shall be applied as follows:

(a) $68,000,000 towards the repayment of the Company’s existing revolving facility;

(b) $45,000,000 towards capital expenditure, including the acquisition and upgrade of marine propulsion systems and digital monitoring infrastructure;

(c) $42,000,000 towards general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions and operational enhancements.

3.3 The allocation of Net Proceeds is at the discretion of the Company’s board of directors, subject to compliance with the stated allocation framework set out in this Prospectus. No portion of the Net Proceeds will be used for the repayment of shareholder loans or distributions to shareholders prior to the completion of the allocation as described.

4. PAYMENT MECHANICS

4.1 Payment for the Ordinary Shares shall be made in full in cleared funds to an account designated by the Company and notified to investors through the subscription process.

4.2 All payments shall be made in United States Dollars (USD), the currency in which the offering is denominated.

4.3 The settlement of shares shall occur on such date as determined by the Company in consultation with the Underwriter, in accordance with standard market practice for initial public offerings in London.

5. NOTICES

5.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 2 Cannon Street, London, to the attention of the Company Secretary.

5.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 81 Fleet Street, London, to the attention of the Head of Capital Markets.

5.4 Notices shall be deemed to have been received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if by post, 48 hours after posting; if by electronic transmission, when the transmission is confirmed as received.
