**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **25 May 2027**

Between:  
(1) **Ivybridge Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13268937**, whose registered office is at 37 Cheapside, London (the **"Seller"**); and  
(2) **Bellhaven Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12681968**, whose registered office is at 53 Fleet Street, London (the **"Buyer"**).

**Background**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Delamere Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13788024**, whose registered office is at 63 Fleet Street, London (the **"Company"**).  
(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.  
(C) The Closing is scheduled to take place on **14 August 2027** (the **"Closing Date"**).

**1. Interpretation**

**1.1** In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$20,000,000** (the **"Purchase Price"**), payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller, on the Closing Date.

**3. Earn-out**

**3.4** In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the **"Earn-out Payment"**) equal to **3 times** the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 (**"FY2026 EBITDA"**) exceeds **$4,000,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be calculated and paid in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 3. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at **$3,000,000**.

**4. Conditions to Completion**

**4.5** If the Conditions (as defined in Schedule 2) are not satisfied or waived in writing by the Buyer by the date falling **120 days** after the date of this Agreement (being **24 September 2027**), either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued prior to termination.

**5. Confidentiality**

Each party agrees that it shall keep confidential all information received from the other party or the Company in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

**6. Notices**

Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post, or email to the registered office of the recipient or such other address as notified in writing. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 37 Cheapside, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to 53 Fleet Street, London. Notices shall be deemed received on the next business day following transmission.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**10. Warranties and Liability**

**10.1** The General Warranties contained in Schedule 1 shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months**. Any claim under this clause must be notified in writing to the other party prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.
