1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this contract dated 12 June 2027 between Bellhaven Media Ltd (the “Customer”) and Delamere Media Ltd (the “Supplier”).

“Commencement Date” means 1 January 2024.

“Services” means the provision of digital content distribution, media placement, and associated reporting services as described in Schedule 1.

“Term” means the initial period of three (3) years from the Commencement Date, subject to extension or earlier termination in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment and Scope of Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and the specifications set out in Schedule 1.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with due care, skill, and diligence and in accordance with generally accepted industry standards.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 In consideration for the Services, the Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule 2 (the “Fees”), subject to the provisions of this Agreement.

3.2 The Fees shall be invoiced quarterly in arrears, and payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.

3.3 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling to such bank account as the Supplier may notify in writing.

3.4 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis until paid in full.

4. Term and Termination

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for the Term unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 4.

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. The counterparty is Bellhaven Media Ltd.

4.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement forthwith by notice in writing if the other party:

(a) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation (whether compulsory or voluntary), or makes any arrangement with its creditors;

(b) ceases or threatens to cease to carry on business; or

(c) commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not capable of remedy or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within thirty (30) days of receipt of notice specifying the breach.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 The obligations under Clause 5.1 shall not apply to information which:

(a) is or comes into the public domain through no fault of the receiving party;

(b) is independently developed by the receiving party without reference to the disclosing party’s information;

(c) is required to be disclosed by law or any regulatory authority.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and addressed to the other party at its registered office or such other address as may be notified in writing.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed to be served:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two (2) business days after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that confirmation of receipt is received.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings.

7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

7.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.

7.4 No failure or delay by a party to exercise any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of that right.

7.5 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

7.6 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
