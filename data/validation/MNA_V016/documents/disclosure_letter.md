**Between:**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Disclosed” means fully, truly and fairly disclosed in this letter, with sufficient detail to enable a reasonably prudent purchaser to understand the nature and scope of the matter disclosed;  
(b) “Warranties” means the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 3 to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the date hereof between the Seller and the Buyer (the “SPA”);  
(c) “Company” means Delamere Media Ltd (registered number 13788024), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 63 Fleet Street, London;  
(d) “Material Contracts” has the meaning given in Warranty 6.3 of the SPA;  
(e) any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced at any time prior to the date of this Disclosure Letter.  

1.2 The headings in this Disclosure Letter are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.  

**2. General Disclosure**  
2.1 Save as specifically disclosed in this Disclosure Letter, there are no matters to disclose in relation to any of the Warranties.  
2.2 The disclosures made in this Disclosure Letter qualify the Warranties only to the extent expressly indicated and shall not operate to broaden the scope of any Warranty unless the terms of such disclosure clearly and expressly require it.  

**3. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3, the Seller confirms that the Company is not a party to any Material Contract that contains a change of control provision.  
3.2 The term “Material Contract” in this context includes, without limitation, any agreement with Bellhaven Media Ltd (registered number 10512329), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 169 Old Broad Street, London, to which the Company is a party.  
3.3 The Company has, within the 12-month period ending on 12 June 2027, derived in excess of 15% of its total revenue from contracts with Bellhaven Media Ltd, and such contracts are accordingly deemed to be Material Contracts for the purposes of the SPA.  
3.4 Notwithstanding the foregoing, no Material Contract to which the Company is a party contains any provision that is triggered by reason of a change in the shareholding of the Company or any assignment of rights or obligations arising therefrom as a consequence of the transaction contemplated by the SPA.  

**4. Payment**

4.2 All amounts stated in the SPA and any related documentation are exclusive of any value added tax or other applicable sales tax, which shall be paid by the Buyer in addition to any consideration or indemnity amount where appropriate.
