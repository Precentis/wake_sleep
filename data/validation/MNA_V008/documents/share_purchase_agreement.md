**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **18 August 2026**

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Hartwell Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11006867**, whose registered office is at 110 Bishopsgate, London (the "**Seller**");

(2) **Eskdale Systems Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12992869**, whose registered office is at 74 Old Broad Street, London (the "**Buyer**").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Ingleby Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15838390**, whose registered office is at 143 Fleet Street, London (the "**Company**").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED as follows:**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"**Closing**" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"**Closing Date**" means **6 October 2026**;

"**Closing Working Capital**" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1;

"**English law**" means the law of England and Wales;

"**Purchase Price**" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as adjusted under Clause 2.3;

"**Shares**" means all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company;

"**Target Working Capital**" means **$5,000,000**;

"**Working Capital**" shall be calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied and using the same accounting policies and practices as adopted by the Company in preparing its unaudited management accounts for the quarter ending 30 June 2026.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$35,000,000** (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall have notified to the Buyer in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$5,000,000**. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, no upward adjustment shall be made to the Purchase Price.

**3. CLOSING**

**3.1** The Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date.

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) certified copies of resolutions of its board of directors authorising the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement;

(b) share transfer forms duly executed by the Seller in respect of the Shares;

(c) the share certificate(s) representing the Shares, together with such other documents as may be necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares to the Buyer free from all Encumbrances;

(d) a certificate of non-residence for UK land transaction tax purposes under section 71A of the Finance Act 2003, if applicable.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

**15.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with **English law** and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.

**15.2** Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).
