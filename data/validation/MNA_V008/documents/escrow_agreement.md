**ESCROW AGREEMENT**

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 25 August 2026 (the "Effective Date")  

**BETWEEN:**  
(1) Hartwell Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11006867 whose registered office is at 110 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller");  
(2) Eskdale Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12992869 whose registered office is at 74 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and  
(3) Ingleby Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15838390 whose registered office is at 143 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").  

**RECITALS:**  
(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company, pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated as of even date herewith (the "SPA").  
(B) As part of the transaction contemplated by the SPA, certain funds and documents are to be held in escrow to secure the obligations of the Seller under the SPA.  
(C) The parties have appointed [Name of Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, to act as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").  

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:  

**1. Definitions**  
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which clearing banks in London are generally open for business.  
"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim made under the SPA.  
"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,750,000 (one million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 8.75% of the total consideration payable under the SPA, to be held in escrow pursuant to this Agreement.  
"Escrow Period" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending at 5:00 p.m. on 31 August 2027.  
"Material Customer" means Upton Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14141699 whose registered office is at 106 Moorgate, London.  

**2. Deposit of Escrow Amount**  
Within two Business Days of the Effective Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") established by the Escrow Agent at a UK-authorised credit institution. Interest accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount.  

**3. Release of Escrow Amount**  
Subject to Clause 4, the Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains undisputed, shall be released to the Seller on the first Business Day following the expiry of the Escrow Period.  

**4. Claims and Disputes**  
(a) The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice on the Escrow Agent and the Seller at any time prior to the end of the Escrow Period.  
(b) Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall withhold from release the portion of the Escrow Amount specified in the Claim Notice (the "Disputed Amount") until the earlier of:  
(i) written agreement between the Buyer and the Seller as to the disposition of the Disputed Amount; or  
(ii) delivery to the Escrow Agent of a final, binding determination by arbitration in accordance with Clause 12.  
(c) Any undisputed portion of the Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller in accordance with Clause 3.  

**5. Payment**  
All payments under this Agreement shall be made in pounds sterling by same-day cleared funds to such account as the recipient may designate in writing.  

**6. Notices**  
Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the addresses or email addresses specified in the introductory provisions above, or as otherwise notified.  

**7. Confidentiality**  
The Escrow Agent and the parties shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or regulatory authority.  

**8. Costs and Expenses**  
The Buyer and the Seller shall each bear their own legal and advisory costs in connection with this Agreement. The fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by the Buyer and the Seller.  

**9. Entire Agreement**  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings.  

**10. Amendments**  
No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all parties.  

**11. Counterparts**  
This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.  

**12. Governing Law and Arbitration**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce in accordance with its Rules, and seated in New York. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language of the arbitration shall be English.
