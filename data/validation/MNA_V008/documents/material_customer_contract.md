1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this contract for services between the parties, including all schedules and appendices.

“Commencement Date” means 1 July 2024.

“Confidential Information” means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, relating to business operations, technical data, customer lists, pricing, software, or trade secrets.

“Company” means Ingleby Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15838390, whose registered office is at 143 Fleet Street, London.

“Customer” means Upton Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14141699, whose registered office is at 106 Moorgate, London.

“Effective Date” means the date of execution of this Agreement by both parties.

“Services” means the software development, integration and support services to be provided by the Company to the Customer as described in Schedule A.

“Term” means the initial period of three years commencing on the Commencement Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

2. Provision of Services

2.1 The Company shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the specifications, timelines and quality standards set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Company shall ensure that all personnel performing the Services are suitably qualified and experienced.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees specified in Schedule B, payable quarterly in advance within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

3.2 All fees are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the prevailing rate.

3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, calculated on a daily basis.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep the other party’s Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by first-class pre-paid post, email or personal delivery to the recipient’s registered office or such other address as notified.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting or immediately if sent by email, provided confirmation of receipt is obtained.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All pre-existing intellectual property rights shall remain the property of the originating party.

6.2 Any deliverables developed under this Agreement shall be owned by the Customer, subject to full payment of all fees due.

14. Termination

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon 60 days’ written notice for material breach by the other party, if uncured within 30 days of notice.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. The counterparty for the purposes of this clause is Upton Media Ltd.

15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

15.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

16. Miscellaneous

16.1 Entire Agreement: This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

16.2 Amendments: No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

16.3 No waiver of any breach shall constitute a waiver of subsequent breaches.

16.4 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
