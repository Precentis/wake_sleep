# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 31 December 2025.

**Parties**

(1) **Ivybridge Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12031299, whose registered office is at 13 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Vantage Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14991407, whose registered office is at 93 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are referred to in this Agreement individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

(4) The company whose shares are the subject of this Agreement is **Holloway Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14731434, whose registered office is at 106 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;
- "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;
- "Closing Date" means 25 February 2026;
- "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, determined in accordance with IFRS as consistently applied by the Company;
- "FY2026" means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026;
- "Earn-out Payment" has the meaning given in Clause 3.4;
- "IFRS" means International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the IASB;
- "Purchase Price" means the amount specified in Clause 2.1;
- "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

---

**2. Sale and Purchase**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$90,000,000** (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

---

**3. Consideration Mechanism**

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional deferred consideration (the "Earn-out Payment") calculated as follows:  
(a) The Earn-out Payment shall equal **3 times** the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds **$4,000,000**.  
(b) The Earn-out Payment shall be paid within 90 days of the final audited accounts for FY2026 being approved by the board of the Company.  
(c) The Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed **$10,000,000**.  

---

**9. Warranties and Indemnities**

9.5 **Limitation of Liability**  
(a) The aggregate liability of the Seller to the Buyer for all claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **10% of the Purchase Price**, being **$11,250,000**.  
(b) No claim shall be brought under the warranties unless the individual claim exceeds $100,000, and the aggregate value of all such claims exceeds $500,000, in which case liability shall attach to the full amount of each claim, not merely the excess.  
(c) This Clause 9.5 does not apply to any claims arising from fraud, wilful misconduct or intentional misrepresentation.

---

**10. General Provisions**

10.1 **Notices**  
Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing, addressed to the registered office of the relevant Party, and delivered by pre-paid first-class post or by hand. Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting or immediately if delivered by hand.

10.2 **Confidentiality**  
Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the other Party in connection with this transaction, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to professional advisers bound by confidentiality obligations.

10.3 **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

10.4 **Entire Agreement**  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings in relation to its subject matter.

10.5 **Counterparts**  
This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
