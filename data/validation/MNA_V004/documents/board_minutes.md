**3. Declaration of Interests**  
No interests to declare.

**4. Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 15 December 2025 were reviewed. Subject to minor typographical corrections, the minutes were confirmed as a true record and approved.

**5. Legal and Regulatory Matters**  
The board noted a letter before action dated 24 January 2026 received from legal representatives of Corrib Media Ltd, addressed to Holloway Media Ltd. The letter referred to alleged defective deliveries under a services agreement between the Company and Corrib Media Ltd and indicated an intention to commence proceedings for a sum of approximately $450,000. The board acknowledged the matter and directed management to monitor developments closely, maintain all relevant documentation, and provide a further update within 14 days. Legal counsel has been instructed to assess the merits of the claim and advise on appropriate responses, including potential settlement or defence options, as the matter progresses.

**6. Material Customer Relationship**  
The board reviewed the current commercial relationship with Foxley Engineering Ltd (company number 11536322), a significant customer of the Company. The latest contract with Foxley Engineering Ltd, valued at £1.2 million, commenced on 1 October 2025 and has a term of 18 months. Performance to date has been in line with expectations, with all deliverables met on schedule. The board noted the strategic importance of this account and reaffirmed the need for continued quality assurance and client engagement.

**7. Shareholding and Transaction Update**  
The board noted that discussions between Vantage Pharma plc (company number 14991407), incorporated in England and Wales, and Ivybridge Robotics Ltd (company number 12031299), also incorporated in England and Wales, regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Media Ltd (company number 14731434) were ongoing. The transaction remains subject to due diligence, final contractual terms, and regulatory compliance. The board instructed management to cooperate fully with the due diligence process while maintaining business continuity.

**8. Financial Performance**  
The interim management accounts for the six-month period ending 31 December 2025 were reviewed. Revenue was reported at £4.1 million, with a net profit margin of 12%. The board acknowledged solid performance in core divisions, though noted increased costs in logistics and digital infrastructure. A detailed variance analysis will be presented at the next board meeting.

**9. Any Other Business**  
There being no other business, the meeting concluded at 16:45.
