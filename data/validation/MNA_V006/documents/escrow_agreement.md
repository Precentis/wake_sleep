ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Ivybridge Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13992954, whose registered office is at 38 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller");

(2) Redcliffe Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12911928, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a bank or financial institution appointed as the Escrow Agent under this Agreement (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London for general business.

"Closing Date" means 17 June 2027.

"Company" means Saltash Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14357331, whose registered office is at 6 Fleet Street, London.

"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by or on behalf of any Party to this Agreement to the Escrow Agent or any other Party, including the existence and terms of this Agreement.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £2,500,000 (two million five hundred thousand pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total purchase price for the entire issued share capital of the Company, deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account maintained by the Escrow Agent.

"Material Customer" means Whitlock Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12785730, whose registered office is at 69 King William Street, London.

"Notice" shall have the meaning set out in Clause 8.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") into which the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount on or prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any set-off, counterclaim or other lien except as expressly provided herein.

3. Purpose of Escrow

3.1 The Escrow Amount is held as security against potential claims arising under the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller (the "SPA") relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall have no responsibility to determine the validity or merits of any claim made under the SPA, and shall act solely on the joint written instructions of the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise directed by a final, non-appealable court order.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

4.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount remaining after resolution of all notified claims shall be released to the Seller within five (5) Business Days of the final determination of such claims.

4.3 Any portion of the Escrow Amount attributable to valid claims notified in accordance with the SPA and not withdrawn or finally determined in favour of the Seller shall be released to the Buyer within five (5) Business Days of such determination.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party undertakes to the others that it shall not, and shall procure that its agents shall not, disclose any Confidential Information to any third party, except:

(a) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

(b) to professional advisers of any Party, provided such advisers are bound by confidentiality obligations; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.
