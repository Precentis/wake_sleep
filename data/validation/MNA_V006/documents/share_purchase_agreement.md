SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 10 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Ivybridge Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13992954, whose registered office is at 38 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Redcliffe Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12911928, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Saltash Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14357331, whose registered office is at 6 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Closing Date” means 17 June 2027.

“EBITDA” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied.

“FY2026” means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026.

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties set out in clauses relating to the Seller’s title to the Shares, capacity to enter into this Agreement, and tax compliance.

“General Warranties” means all representations and warranties given by the Seller in this Agreement other than the Fundamental Warranties.

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The total consideration for the sale and purchase of the Shares (the “Purchase Price”) is $190,000,000, payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cash by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date, such payment to be made on the Closing Date.

3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT

3.4 The Seller shall be entitled to an additional payment (the “Earn-out Payment”) calculated as follows: two (2) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $5,000,000; provided that in no event shall the Earn-out Payment exceed $3,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller within ninety (90) days of the final audited financial statements of the Company for FY2026 being approved by the board of directors of the Buyer, but in no event later than 31 March 2027.

10. SURVIVAL AND CLAIMS

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months. Any claim in respect of a Warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which such claim shall be extinguished and no proceedings may be brought in respect thereof. For the avoidance of doubt, claims under the General Warranties must be made within 18 months and claims under the Fundamental Warranties must be made within 72 months, both periods running from the Closing Date.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party agrees that it shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

11.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall not apply to disclosures made to professional advisers, financing sources, or regulatory authorities, provided such recipients are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

12. NOTICES

12.1 Any notice or other communication required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

12.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 38 Ludgate Hill, London, email: legal@ivybridgepharma.co.uk.

12.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 140 Moorgate, London, email: corporate@redcliffemedical.co.uk.

12.4 A notice shall be deemed to have been received: (a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; (b) if sent by post, at the expiry of 48 hours from the time of posting; and (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure is received.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
