SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 10 May 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Ashcombe Medical Ltd (company number 14117523), whose registered office is at 17 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Galloway Capital LLP (company number 12529821), whose registered office is at 54 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the registered and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Foxley Engineering Ltd (company number 12700671), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 3 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

(C) Completion of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement is scheduled to take place on 13 August 2027 (the "Closing Date").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

1.4 Unless otherwise stated, references to a statutory provision are to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $95,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. COMPLETION

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:  
(a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of all shares in the Company;  
(b) the share certificates in respect of the shares in the Company; and  
(c) such other documents as are reasonably required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title to the shares in the Company.

4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

4.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date, each of the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 1 is true and accurate.

4.2 The warranties in this Agreement are given as a whole and not in isolation, and no reliance is placed by the Buyer on any information not expressly included in this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $9,120,000. The Purchase Price is $95,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

9. BASKET AND THRESHOLDS

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable for any individual claim or aggregate claims under this Agreement in respect of warranties unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $950,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which such aggregate claims exceed $950,000, and only for the excess over that threshold.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out at the beginning of this Agreement, or such other address as a party may notify in writing.

10.2 A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed received at the time of delivery. A notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed received two (2) Business Days after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed received on the next Business Day following transmission.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

11.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

12.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any claim or matter arising under this Agreement.
