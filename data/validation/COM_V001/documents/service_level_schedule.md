**Service Level Schedule**  
**to the Master Services Agreement between Kestrel Media Ltd and Foxley Systems Ltd**

1. **Interpretation**  
   1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) "Agreement" means the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 9 April 2026 between Kestrel Media Ltd (the Customer) and Foxley Systems Ltd (the Supplier);  
   (b) "Business Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
   (c) "Monthly Fee" means the sum of $65,000 payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the managed IT services under the Agreement;  
   (d) "Service Credits" means the credits payable by the Supplier to the Customer in accordance with Clause 4;  
   (e) "Service Hours" means 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, 365 days per year;  
   (f) "Service Level Target" means the performance standard specified for each service metric in this Schedule.

2. **Service Availability**  
   2.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that the managed IT services are available at all times during the Service Hours.  
   2.2 Availability shall be measured monthly as the percentage of minutes during the Service Hours in which the services are accessible and operational, excluding scheduled maintenance and circumstances beyond the Supplier's reasonable control.  
   2.3 Scheduled maintenance may be carried out during Business Hours, provided that the Supplier gives the Customer at least 72 hours’ prior written notice. Such maintenance shall not exceed four (4) hours in any calendar month.

3. **Reporting and Monitoring**  
   3.1 The Supplier shall monitor the availability of the services continuously and shall provide the Customer with a report within ten (10) Business Days following the end of each calendar month.  
   3.2 The report shall include:  
   (a) the total minutes of unavailability during the month;  
   (b) the calculated availability percentage;  
   (c) any incidents that contributed to unavailability; and  
   (d) any scheduled maintenance activities carried out.  
   3.3 The Customer may review the accuracy of the report upon reasonable notice, and the Supplier shall provide supporting data.

4. **Service Credits for Unavailability**  
   4.1 If the monthly availability falls below 99.5% but is at least 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee.  
   4.2 If the monthly availability falls below 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee.  
   4.3 Service Credits shall not exceed, in any single month, 25% of the Monthly Fee, and shall not be cumulative across months.  
   4.4 The Supplier shall apply the Service Credit to the Customer’s next monthly invoice within thirty (30) days of the report being finalised.  
   4.5 The Customer’s sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the Service Level Target for availability is the receipt of the applicable Service Credit as set out in this Clause 4.

5. **Payment**  
   5.1 The Monthly Fee of $65,000 shall be invoiced by the Supplier in advance on the first day of each month and shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.  
   5.2 All payments shall be made in US dollars to the bank account nominated in writing by the Supplier.  
   5.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank Plc from time to time, accruing daily from the due date until payment in full.

6. **Confidentiality**  
   6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party marked as confidential or which ought reasonably to be considered confidential.  
   6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. **Notices**  
   7.1 Any notice to be given under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party:  
   (a) Kestrel Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14832030, 21 Bishopsgate, London; or  
   (b) Foxley Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13296367, 144 Old Broad Street, London.  
   7.2 Notices may be delivered by hand, by pre-paid first-class post, or by email with confirmed receipt.

8. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
   8.1 This Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
   8.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Schedule.
