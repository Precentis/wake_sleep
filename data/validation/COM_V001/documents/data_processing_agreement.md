# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Kestrel Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14832030, with its registered office at 21 Bishopsgate, London ("Customer"), and Foxley Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13296367, with its registered office at 144 Old Broad Street, London ("Supplier"), collectively referred to as the "Parties".

The Agreement forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 9 April 2026 between the Parties and governs the processing of personal data by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- *Applicable Data Protection Law* means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended from time to time.
- *Customer Personal Data* means any personal data of the Customer that the Supplier processes on behalf of the Customer under the Master Services Agreement, including but not limited to data relating to employees, contractors, and clients of the Customer.
- *Processing* has the meaning given in the Applicable Data Protection Law.
- *Sub-processor* means any third party engaged by the Supplier to process Customer Personal Data.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Services Agreement.

**2. Subject Matter and Duration**

2.1 This Agreement applies to the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier in connection with the provision of managed IT services under the Master Services Agreement.

2.2 This Agreement shall commence on the date of execution and shall continue in force until the termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

**3. Obligations of the Supplier**

3.1 The Supplier shall act only on the documented instructions of the Customer in relation to the processing of Customer Personal Data, unless required to do otherwise by Applicable Data Protection Law, in which case the Supplier shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

**4. Sub-processing**

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any Sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 The Supplier has obtained the Customer’s prior written authorisation to engage Bellhaven Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12734637, with registered office at 2 Moorgate, London, as a Sub-processor.

**5. Security Measures**

5.1 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access.

5.2 These measures shall include, but are not limited to, encryption of data in transit, access controls, and regular system audits.

**6. Compliance with Data Protection Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 The Supplier shall maintain the confidentiality of all Customer Personal Data and shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under this Agreement or Applicable Data Protection Law.

7.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of 3 years.

**8. Audit Rights**

8.1 The Customer may, once per calendar year, request in writing that the Supplier provide evidence of compliance with this Agreement, including through inspection of relevant policies and records.

8.2 Where an audit is requested, the Supplier shall cooperate fully and provide all necessary documentation within 30 days of receipt of the request.

**9. Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation**

9.1 Where processing under this Agreement is likely to result in a high risk to data subjects, the Supplier shall, at the Customer’s request, assist the Customer in fulfilling its obligation to carry out a data protection impact assessment and, where applicable, to consult with the Information Commissioner.

**10. Notices**

10.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective Party.

**11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
