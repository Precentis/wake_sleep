# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 23 February 2027.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Fenwick Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15873137, whose registered office is at 21 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

(2) **Penrith Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13076364, whose registered office is at 47 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are together referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Thornbury Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14302737, whose registered office is at 117 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1** In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

## 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1** The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$185,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$2,000,000**.

## 3. CLOSING

The completion of the sale and purchase of the shares (the "Closing") shall take place on **2 April 2027** (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

## 4. PAYMENT

The Purchase Price, subject to any adjustments under Section 2.3, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

## 9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

**9.3** (Limitation of Liability) The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims under the warranties given by the Seller in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **25% of the Purchase Price**, being **$57,812,500**. The Purchase Price is $185,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

**9.7** The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any warranty claim within **20 days** of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim. Any claim not notified within such period shall be time-barred and unenforceable.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

## 11. NOTICES

Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or internationally recognised courier to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as a Party may notify in writing.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**15.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with **English law**. The courts of **England** shall have **exclusive jurisdiction** to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
