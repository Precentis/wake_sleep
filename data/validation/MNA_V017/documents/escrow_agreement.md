1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement"), unless the context otherwise requires:

"Buyer" means Fenwick Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15873137, with its registered office at 21 Cheapside, London;

"Company" means Thornbury Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14302737, with its registered office at 117 Moorgate, London;

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £750,000 (seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer into an escrow account maintained with Barclays Bank PLC, London, Account No. 92748361, Sort Code 20-22-54, in accordance with Clause 4;

"Escrow Agent" means Barclays Bank PLC, a company authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority, with its principal office at 1 Churchill Place, London E14 5HP;

"Seller" means Penrith Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13076364, with its registered office at 47 Queen Victoria Street, London;

"Transaction" means the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 28 February 2027.

1.2 Capitalised terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Transaction documents.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to perform its duties hereunder in accordance with the terms set out herein.

4. Deposit of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the designated escrow account within two (2) Business Days following the completion of the Transaction.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount in writing to both the Buyer and the Seller within one (1) Business Day of such deposit.

5. Claims and Disbursements

5.1 The Escrow Amount shall be held as security for the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the Share Purchase Agreement, and may be disbursed only in accordance with the written joint instructions of the Buyer and the Seller or pursuant to an arbitral award under Clause 12.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it. Such notification shall be in writing, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim, the amount claimed, and supporting documentation.

6. Payment Mechanics

6.1 All disbursements from the Escrow Amount shall be made by the Escrow Agent via same-day cleared funds to such account or accounts as may be designated in writing by the recipient.

6.2 The Escrow Agent shall have no obligation to disburse any amount in the absence of joint written instructions or a final and binding arbitral award.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party.

7.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 21 Cheapside, London, with a copy to legal@fenwickmarine.co.uk.

7.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 47 Queen Victoria Street, London, with a copy to compliance@penrithmedical.co.uk.

7.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: Escrow Services, Barclays Bank PLC, 1 Churchill Place, London E14 5HP, with a copy to escrow.london@barclays.com.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party undertakes to keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement, except as required by law or regulation, or with the prior written consent of the other parties.

8.2 The Escrow Agent may disclose information to its legal or financial advisors or as required by regulatory authorities, provided such disclosures are made under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in New York under the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, and the seat of arbitration shall be New York.
