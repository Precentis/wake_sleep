# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 31 March 2026.

**BETWEEN:**  
(1) Ingleby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14705484 whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and  
(2) Oakhurst Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15511854 whose registered office is at 39 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer").

**RECITALS:**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12702280 whose registered office is at 64 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Closing Date" means 27 May 2026;  
"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies consistently applied by the Company as at 31 December 2025;  
"FY2026" means the financial year of the Company commencing on 1 January 2026 and ending on 31 December 2026;  
"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties set out in clauses 8.1 (Title), 8.2 (Capacity) and 8.5 (Tax);  
"General Warranties" means all warranties given by the Seller in clause 8 except the Fundamental Warranties.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $145,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

**3. PAYMENT AND ADJUSTMENTS**

**3.1** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds by the Buyer to an account designated in writing by the Seller no later than the Closing Date.

**3.2** The Buyer shall procure that the Company, within 90 days of the Closing Date, prepares and delivers to the Seller a draft calculation of the Company’s EBITDA for FY2026.

**3.3** If the Seller disputes the Buyer’s calculation, it may deliver a notice to that effect to the Buyer within 30 days of receipt of the draft calculation, specifying the grounds for such dispute.

**3.4** (Earn-out): The Buyer shall make an additional payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to 3 times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be paid within 30 days of the final determination of FY2026 EBITDA. The total Earn-out Payment under this clause shall be capped at $5,000,000.

**4. CLOSING**

**4.1** Completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer's legal advisers or such other place as the parties may agree.

**4.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms and such other documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares free from all Encumbrances.

**10. WARRANTIES AND CLAIMS**

**10.1** (Survival): The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months. Any claim for breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period, specifying in reasonable detail the nature of the claim and the amount claimed.

**11. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**11.1** Each party agrees that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates and advisers shall not, disclose any information acquired in connection with this transaction to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

**11.2** The obligations under this clause shall continue for a period of 3 years from the Closing Date.

**12. NOTICES**

**12.1** Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by first-class pre-paid post or recorded delivery to the registered office of the relevant party set out in the preamble.

**12.2** Notices shall be deemed received at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, or 48 hours after posting.

**13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

**13.1** This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

**13.2** The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
