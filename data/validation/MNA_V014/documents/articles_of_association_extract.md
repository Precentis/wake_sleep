# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF GLENROCK BIOSCIENCE PLC

**1. Interpretation**

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms have the meanings set out in this Article 1.

1.2 “Articles” means these articles of association of the Company, as amended from time to time.

1.3 “Board” means the board of directors of the Company for the time being.

1.4 “Buyer” means Oakhurst Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15511854, whose registered office is at 39 Queen Victoria Street, London.

1.5 “Company” means Glenrock Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12702280, whose registered office is at 64 Cannon Street, London.

1.6 “Seller” means Ingleby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14705484, whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London.

1.7 “Writing” includes any mode of reproducing words in visible form and shall include electronic communication, provided that such communication is capable of being accessed and reproduced in a tangible form.

**2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights**

2.1 The share capital of the Company shall be such amount as determined by ordinary resolution passed by the shareholders from time to time.

2.2 The rights attaching to any class of shares may be varied or abrogated with the consent in writing of the holders of not less than three-quarters of the issued shares of that class or by special resolution of the holders of that class.

**3. Directors**

3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all the powers of the Company, subject to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006, these Articles, and any directions that may be given by ordinary or special resolution of the members.

3.2 The number of directors shall not be less than one nor more than ten, as determined from time to time by ordinary resolution.

3.3 A director may be appointed by ordinary resolution of the members or, between general meetings, by a resolution of the Board.

3.4 Any director may be removed from office before the expiration of their term by ordinary resolution of the members, notwithstanding anything in any agreement between the Company and such director.

**11. Board Meetings**

11.1 The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

11.2 A director may appoint another director as their proxy to attend and vote at any meeting of the Board, provided that such appointment is in writing and delivered to the Company not less than 24 hours before the scheduled time of the meeting.

11.3 Meetings of the Board may be convened by the chairman of the Board or any two directors, and at least 48 hours' written notice shall be given of any such meeting unless waived in writing by all directors entitled to attend.

11.4 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes of the directors present and voting on the question. In the case of an equality of votes, the chairman of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.

**12. Payment and Financial Provisions**

12.1 All payments made by or to the Company shall be made in sterling and through a designated bank account held in the name of the Company.

12.2 Any contract or transaction involving an amount exceeding £500,000 shall require prior written approval by a resolution of the Board.

**13. Notices**

13.1 Any notice to be given to a director, shareholder or the Company shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class post, or electronic means to the relevant registered office or last known address.

13.2 A notice addressed to a director or shareholder at their registered address or last known address shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting or transmission.

**14. Confidentiality**

14.1 Every director, officer, and employee of the Company shall maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information relating to the Company, its business, or its customers, including but not limited to Lowther Robotics Ltd (company number 15102369, registered office 112 Moorgate, London), and shall not disclose such information to any third party unless required by law or expressly authorised in writing by the Board.

14.2 The obligations under this Article 14 shall continue indefinitely after the termination of any individual’s office or employment with the Company.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15.1 These Articles and all matters arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
