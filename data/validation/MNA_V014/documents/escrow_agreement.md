# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] April 2026 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Ingleby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14705484, whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Oakhurst Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15511854, whose registered office is at 39 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a professional trustee firm acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.
- "Closing Date" means 27 May 2026.
- "Escrow Amount" means the amount of £15,000,000 (fifteen million pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date concerning the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Bioscience plc.
- "Escrow Account" means the interest-bearing account established by the Escrow Agent into which the Escrow Amount has been paid, maintained under account number [●] at [●] Bank plc.
- "Glenrock Bioscience plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12702280, whose registered office is at 64 Cannon Street, London.
- "Lowther Robotics Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15102369, whose registered office is at 112 Moorgate, London, being a material customer of Glenrock Bioscience plc.
- "Parties" means collectively the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent, and "Party" means any one of them.
- "Pending Claim" means any valid claim made by the Buyer under the Share Purchase Agreement which has been notified in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to the release date under Clause 4.1 and remains unresolved as of that date.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

**2. Appointment of Escrow Agent**

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount upon the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to perform its duties herein in accordance with the terms set forth.

**3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount**

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account, and shall not release any part of it except in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3.3 Any interest accruing on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be held and distributed under the same terms.

**4. Release of Escrow Amount**

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. For the avoidance of doubt, any amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such claim is finally resolved by agreement or determination, whereupon it shall be released either to the Seller or the Buyer as appropriate.

4.2 The release under this Clause 4 shall occur on the next Business Day if the 24-month anniversary of the Closing Date is not a Business Day.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as notified in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 17 King William Street, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 39 Queen Victoria Street, London.  
Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: [●].

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or to professional advisors under a duty of confidentiality.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
