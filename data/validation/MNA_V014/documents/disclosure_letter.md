2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)

2.1 For the purposes of disclosure against Warranty 8.2, which relates to the ownership and validity of intellectual property rights used by the Company in its business, the Seller discloses as follows:

2.1.1 The Company’s core platform software, known internally as “Project Helix v1.0”, was developed in 2023 by Kestrel Bioscience plc, an independent contractor not affiliated with the Company, the Seller, or the Buyer. The development work was undertaken pursuant to an informal arrangement governed by email correspondence and periodic project updates. No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into.

2.1.2 As a consequence, legal title to any intellectual property developed by Kestrel Bioscience plc in the course of performing the development work has not been formally assigned to the Company. The Company currently uses and relies upon the software in its ordinary course of business and has done so since January 2024.

2.1.3 The Company has made no payments to Kestrel Bioscience plc for the assignment of intellectual property rights, as no such assignment was requested or executed. However, the Company paid Kestrel Bioscience plc a total of £187,500 in three instalments: £62,500 on 15 March 2023, £62,500 on 20 June 2023, and £62,500 on 10 September 2023, for services rendered in the development of the software.

2.1.4 Kestrel Bioscience plc is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13402125. Its registered office is at 8 Finsbury Circus, London.

2.1.5 The absence of any written agreement containing a provision for intellectual property assignment means that **No written assignment** of rights was executed between the Company and Kestrel Bioscience plc, and accordingly, no formal transfer of ownership of the underlying source code, algorithms, or related intellectual property has occurred.
