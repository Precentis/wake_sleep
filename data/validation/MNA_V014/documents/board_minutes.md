MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF GLENROCK BIOSCIENCE PLC

Date: 10 April 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 64 Cannon Street, London  
Present:  
- Dr. Eleanor Hartley, Director  
- Mr. Simon Travers, Director  

Apologies for Absence:  
- Ms. Clara Nwosu, Director  
- Mr. Rajiv Mehta, Director  
- Ms. Felicity Byrne, Director  

1. **Call to Order**  
The meeting was called to order at 10:00 a.m. by Dr. Eleanor Hartley, who assumed the chair in accordance with the Company’s Articles of Association.  

2. **Quorum**  
It was confirmed that 2 directors were present. The Company’s Articles of Association require a quorum of 2 directors. It was acknowledged that the meeting was quorate.  

3. **Approval of Agenda**  
RESOLVED that the agenda for the meeting be, and hereby is, approved as circulated.  

4. **Review of Material Contracts and Customers**  
The Board reviewed the status of material contracts, including the supply agreement dated 15 March 2023 with Lowther Robotics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15102369, registered office 112 Moorgate, London), which accounts for 37% of the Company’s total revenue for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2025. The agreement has an unexpired term of 18 months as of the date of this meeting.  

5. **Share Purchase Agreement**  
The Board considered the proposed transaction whereby Ingleby Bioscience plc (the Seller, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14705484, registered office 17 King William Street, London) will sell the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Bioscience plc (the Company, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12702280, registered office 64 Cannon Street, London) to Oakhurst Pharma plc (the Buyer, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15511854, registered office 39 Queen Victoria Street, London).  

The directors reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement, which provides for the sale of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company. The transaction is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

6. **Board Resolutions**  
Upon discussion, the Board concluded that the interests of the Company and its shareholders would be best served by supporting the proposed transaction, having regard to the strategic rationale, financial terms, and due diligence undertaken.  

RESOLVED:  
6.1 That the Company hereby approves entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Oakhurst Pharma plc as Buyer and Ingleby Bioscience plc as Seller, pursuant to which the entire issued share capital of the Company will be sold.  

6.2 That Dr. Eleanor Hartley and Mr. Simon Travers, or either of them, be and are hereby authorised to execute the Share Purchase Agreement and to take all such steps and execute all such documents as may be necessary or desirable to give effect to the transaction.  

7. **Adjournment**  
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
