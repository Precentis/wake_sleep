ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [insert date] (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Eskdale Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15357821, whose registered office is at 116 King William Street, London ("Buyer");

(2) Holloway Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15068651, whose registered office is at 63 Gresham Street, London ("Seller"); and

(3) [Insert Name of Escrow Agent], a law firm or financial institution acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

The Buyer, Seller and Escrow Agent are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about [insert date] (the "SPA") for the acquisition from the Seller of the entire issued share capital of Merrion Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14709579, whose registered office is at 31 Moorgate, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the consideration under the SPA, an amount of £1,250,000 (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited into an escrow account (the "Escrow Account") maintained with the Escrow Agent, to secure certain indemnities and warranties given by the Seller;

(C) The Parties have agreed upon the terms under which the Escrow Amount may be released, claimed or distributed.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which commercial banks are open for general business in London, England.

"Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer for indemnification pursuant to the SPA, arising from a breach of warranty or indemnity by the Seller.

"Escrow Account" means the designated account held with the Escrow Agent in which the Escrow Amount is held.

"Redcliffe Retail Group plc" means the customer of the Company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14041860, whose registered office is at 105 Fleet Street, London.

"Release Date" means the date falling 18 months after the date of completion of the acquisition of the Company under the SPA.

2. Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account, separate from its own funds and in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held in an interest-bearing account, and any interest accrued shall form part of the Escrow Amount.

3. Payment and Release

3.1 On the Release Date, subject to any unresolved Claims, the Escrow Agent shall release 100% of the Escrow Amount to the Seller, unless notice of a Claim has been validly made in accordance with Clause 5.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Account only upon receipt of joint written instructions from the Buyer and Seller or a final determination by arbitration or a court of competent jurisdiction.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify to the others.

4.2 A notice given in accordance with this Clause 4 shall be deemed served on the next Business Day if delivered by email or hand, or on the second Business Day after posting if sent by post.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount by delivering written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, setting out in reasonable detail the nature, basis and quantum of the Claim.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
