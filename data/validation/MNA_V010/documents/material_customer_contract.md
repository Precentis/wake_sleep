**MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT EXTRACT**

This agreement is made on 12 March 2021 between **Redcliffe Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14041860 whose registered office is at 105 Fleet Street, London ("Customer"), and **Merrion Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14709579 whose registered office is at 31 Moorgate, London ("Supplier").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
    "Agreement" means this contract as amended, supplemented or novated from time to time;  
    "Change of Control" means the acquisition by a person or group of persons acting in concert of direct or indirect control over more than 50% of the issued share capital of a party or the power to direct its management or affairs;  
    "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 6;  
    "Effective Date" means 12 March 2021;  
    "Notice" has the meaning given in Clause 12;  
    "Term" means the period specified in Clause 13.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

**2. Supply of Services**

2.1 The Supplier shall provide energy management and grid-balancing services (the "Services") to the Customer in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with due care, skill and diligence and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

**3. Payment Terms**

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule B for the Services rendered, payable monthly in arrears within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

3.2 All amounts stated are exclusive of value-added tax, which shall be added where applicable.

3.3 If payment is not made within the due date, the Supplier may suspend performance of the Services until all outstanding amounts are settled.

**4. Term and Termination**

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of five years (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 4.

4.2 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives not less than 90 days' written notice prior to the end of the then-current Term.

4.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party:  
    (a) commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of being notified in writing;  
    (b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, or has a receiver or administrator appointed; or  
    (c) suspends or ceases to carry on all or substantially all of its business.

**14. Miscellaneous**

14.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as notified in writing. Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after transmission if sent by first-class post or on receipt if delivered by hand or email.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the avoidance of doubt, such consent shall not be required if the change of control results from the acquisition of the Supplier’s entire issued share capital by **Eskdale Robotics Ltd**, company number 15357821.

14.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, representations or agreements relating to its subject matter.

14.4 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party marked as confidential or which, by its nature, ought reasonably to be treated as confidential ("Confidential Information").

6.2 The obligations in Clause 6.1 shall not apply to information which:  
    (a) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving party;  
    (b) was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure; or  
    (c) is required to be disclosed by law or a regulatory authority.

6.3 These confidentiality obligations shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
