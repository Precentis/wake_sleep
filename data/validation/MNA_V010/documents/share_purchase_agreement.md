SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 3 July 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Holloway Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15068651, whose registered office is at 63 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Eskdale Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15357821, whose registered office is at 116 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

BACKGROUND:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Merrion Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14709579, whose registered office is at 31 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions contained in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, including the recitals, the following terms have the following meanings:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 29 August 2026;

   "EBITDA" means, in respect of the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026"), the earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation of the Company, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied;

   "Earn-out Payment" has the meaning given in Clause 3.4;

   "FY2026" means the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

   "Purchase Price" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

   "Redcliffe Retail Group plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14041860, whose registered office is at 105 Fleet Street, London;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a purchase price of $45,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Seller or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.3 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller at Closing all necessary instruments of transfer and such other documents as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares.

3.4 In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $6,500,000, such Earn-out Payment to be calculated following the audit of the Company’s financial statements for FY2026 and paid within sixty (60) days of such audit. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $3,000,000.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with any warranty contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $6,750,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $45,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1.

9. GENERAL

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim, failing which such claim shall be time-barred. The requirement to notify within 30 days is a condition precedent to the Buyer’s right to bring any claim for breach of warranty. The Buyer acknowledges that it has 30 days from the date it becomes aware of any matter giving rise to a claim to provide notice in accordance with this Clause 9.7, and no claim shall be actionable or enforceable unless validly notified within such 30 days.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any related information, except as may be required by law or regulation or by any regulatory authority.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
