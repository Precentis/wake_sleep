# PROSPECTUS EXTRACT  
**Issuer: Kingsmere Energy plc**  
**Date of this Prospectus: 23 April 2027**

## 1. Definitions  
In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“the Company” means Kingsmere Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12201470, with registered office at 48 Old Broad Street, London;  
“the Underwriter” means Oakhurst Bioscience Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11233398, with registered office at 124 Gresham Street, London;  
“Glenrock Energy plc” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13017135, with registered office at 17 Moorgate, London;  
“the Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company underwritten by the Underwriter;  
“ordinary shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value in the Company;  
“net proceeds” means the total proceeds from the Offering after deducting underwriting commissions and associated offering expenses;  
all references to currency are to United States dollars (USD) unless otherwise stated.

## 2. Capitalisation  
As at the date of this Prospectus, 61,750,000 ordinary shares are in issue. The share capital of the Company comprises:  
- 61,750,000 ordinary shares of £0.01 each;  
- no other classes of share capital.  
The Company has no treasury shares.

## 3. Use of Proceeds  
The net proceeds of the Offering are expected to amount to $120,000,000. These funds will be applied by the Company strictly in accordance with the following allocation:  
- $54,000,000 to the repayment of the outstanding balance under the revolving credit facility;  
- $36,000,000 to capital expenditure projects, including the development and modernisation of energy infrastructure assets;  
- $35,000,000 to general corporate purposes, including working capital, operational enhancements, and potential strategic acquisitions.  
The allocation of proceeds is based on current plans and expectations and may be adjusted in accordance with market conditions and operational requirements, subject to compliance with regulatory disclosure obligations.

## 4. Legal Proceedings  
The Company confirms that, as at the date of this Prospectus, it is not party to any legal or arbitration proceedings, whether civil or criminal, pending or threatened, which, individually or in aggregate, may have a significant effect on its financial position, results of operations or prospects.

## 5. Related Party Transactions  
Save for the directors’ service agreements entered into in the ordinary course of business, there are no related party transactions to which the Company or any of its subsidiaries is a party, nor are there any such transactions proposed, save as may be disclosed in the financial statements or elsewhere in this Prospectus. All directors’ service agreements have been approved by the board of directors and are consistent with market terms for comparable companies.

## 6. Payment and Settlement  
All payments arising under or in connection with the Offering shall be made in cleared funds to such account as the Company or the Underwriter may designate in writing. Settlement of shares issued pursuant to the Offering shall occur through the Clearstream and Euroclear systems, with delivery of shares against payment on the date specified in the final terms of the Offering, which shall not be later than ten (10) business days following the pricing date.

## 7. Notices  
Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Prospectus or in connection with the Offering shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to:  
(a) in the case of the Company: the Company Secretary, Kingsmere Energy plc, 48 Old Broad Street, London;  
(b) in the case of the Underwriter: Compliance Department, Oakhurst Bioscience Securities, 124 Gresham Street, London.  
Notices sent by post shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting; notices by email shall be deemed received at the time of transmission, provided confirmation of receipt is obtained.

## 8. Confidentiality  
Each party shall treat as confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation or any competent regulatory authority. This obligation shall survive termination of any arrangements between the parties for a period of five (5) years.

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Prospectus and any non-contractual obligations arising in connection with the Offering shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
