**1. Call to Order**  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The agenda was adopted without amendment.

**2. Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 31 January 2027 were reviewed. Upon motion by Ms. Reed, seconded by Mr. Vane, the minutes were confirmed as a true and accurate record and approved.

**3. Update on Litigation – Elstree Robotics Ltd Claim**  
The board received a report from external legal counsel, Messrs. Selby & Co., regarding the class action initiated by Elstree Robotics Ltd against Kingsmere Energy plc. The claim, filed on 14 February 2027, seeks damages in the amount of $8,000,000. The allegations relate to alleged contractual misrepresentations made in connection with a technology supply agreement entered into in Q3 2025. Legal counsel advised that, based on the current evidence and applicable English law, the claim has reasonable prospects of success. The board directed management to prepare a preliminary risk assessment for the next board meeting and instructed counsel to initiate settlement discussions while preserving all defences. Mr. Montague declared a non-interest due to no material connection with the claimant.

**4. IPO Progress Report**  
Mr. Vane provided an update on the ongoing initial public offering, underwritten by Oakhurst Bioscience Securities (company number 11233398, registered office 124 Gresham Street, London). The prospectus draft had been circulated to the board for review, with final submissions scheduled for 2 April 2027. The board confirmed the target listing date on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market as 5 April 2027, subject to regulatory approval. The Underwriter had confirmed readiness to proceed, pending final board sign-off on disclosure items.

**5. Customer Contract – Glenrock Energy plc**  
Ms. Nair reviewed the long-term supply agreement with Glenrock Energy plc (company number 13017135, registered office 17 Moorgate, London), a material customer. The contract, effective for a period of seven (7) years from 1 January 2026, accounted for 34% of the Company’s 2026 revenue. Performance indicators remained within agreed parameters. No material breaches had been reported.

**6. Financial Projections Review**  
The board examined unaudited financial projections for FY2027–2029. Revenue forecasts indicated a compound annual growth rate of 9.2%. The projections accounted for the IPO proceeds, operating expenditures, and anticipated capital investments. The board accepted the projections subject to revision following the IPO closing.

**7. Director Loan – Idris Montague**  
The board considered a proposal to advance a loan in the amount of $4,000,000 to Mr. Idris Montague, a non-executive director, on interest-free terms. The purpose of the loan was stated as personal financial restructuring unrelated to Company operations. After full disclosure and consideration of fiduciary duties under English law, the board approved the loan. Ms. Reed and Mr. Vane declared no interest. The transaction will be disclosed in the next board report and in compliance with Listing Rule 9.8.1.

**8. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.

**9. Adjournment**  
The meeting was adjourned at 12:15 p.m.
