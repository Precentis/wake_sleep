UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set out below, by and between:

(1) Kingsmere Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12201470, whose registered office is at 48 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Oakhurst Bioscience Securities, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11233398, whose registered office is at 124 Gresham Street, London (the "Underwriter").

Recitals:

A. The Company proposes to issue and sell, pursuant to an initial public offering (the "Offering"), a total of 37,000,000 new ordinary shares of £0.01 each (the "Offer Shares"), representing approximately 37% of the total enlarged issued share capital of the Company following completion of the Offering.

B. The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite, on a firm commitment basis, the entire amount of the Offer Shares, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

C. The Company currently has 63,000,000 ordinary shares in issue.

D. The Offering will be conducted in accordance with the prospectus prepared by the Company (the "Prospectus"), which will be approved by the Financial Conduct Authority in the United Kingdom.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations and warranties contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Offer Shares under this Agreement;

"Closing Date" means 5 April 2027, or such other date as the parties may agree in writing;

"Effective Date" means the date on which the Prospectus is approved by the Financial Conduct Authority;

"Offering" has the meaning set out in Recital A.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter to act as underwriter of the Offer Shares, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, all 37,000,000 Offer Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

2.2 The purchase price per Offer Share shall be £1.25 (one pound twenty-five pence), payable in full at Closing.

3. Payment and Delivery

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Underwriter shall pay the aggregate purchase price of £46,250,000 (thirty-seven million times £1.25) to the Company by irrevocable wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Company at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Simultaneously with payment, the Company shall deliver to the Underwriter electronic confirmation of the issuance and registration of the Offer Shares into the Underwriter’s nominee account at Euroclear UK & Ireland.

4. Representations and Warranties

4.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof that:

(a) it is a company duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder;

(c) this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the respective party.

5.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 48 Old Broad Street, London, Attn: Company Secretary, email: legal@kingsmere-energy.co.uk.

5.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 124 Gresham Street, London, Attn: Head of Capital Markets, email: notices@obsec.co.uk.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, except to the extent disclosure is required by law, regulation, or stock exchange rule, or with the prior written consent of the other party.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

7.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
