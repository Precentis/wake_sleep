# PROSPECTUS EXTRACT

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

- “the Company” means Galloway Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13333879, whose registered office is at 168 Old Broad Street, London;
- “the Underwriter” means Rothley Foods Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12686710, whose registered office is at 11 Old Broad Street, London;
- “Foxley Engineering” means Foxley Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13678647, whose registered office is at 88 Moorgate, London;
- “Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of the Company offered hereby;
- “Capitalisation” means the total number of Ordinary Shares in issue as at the date of this Prospectus;
- “this Prospectus” means the present offering document dated 5 June 2026.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation. References to clauses are to clauses of this Prospectus unless otherwise stated.

**2. Issuer and Offering Details**

2.1 The issuer of the Ordinary Shares offered in this offering is Galloway Marine Ltd. The date of this Prospectus is 5 June 2026.

2.2 The Ordinary Shares are being offered to the public for the first time pursuant to an initial public offering (IPO) underwritten by the Underwriter. The Underwriter has entered into an underwriting agreement with the Company pursuant to which it has committed to purchase any unsold Ordinary Shares at the final offer price.

2.3 At the date of this Prospectus, the Capitalisation of the Company consists of 61,750,000 Ordinary Shares in issue. These shares represent the entire issued share capital of the Company as of 5 June 2026.

**3. Underwriting Arrangements**

3.1 The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the offering on a firm-commitment basis, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the underwriting agreement dated on or about 4 May 2026 between the Company and the Underwriter.

3.2 The Underwriter shall be entitled to receive a commission equal to 3.5% of the gross proceeds received by the Company from the sale of the Ordinary Shares in this offering. Such commission shall be paid out of the proceeds of the offering at settlement.

3.3 The Company acknowledges that the Underwriter may engage in stabilising transactions in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements, including but not limited to short sales, purchases, and syndicate covering transactions, for a period not exceeding 30 days from the date of pricing of the offering.

**4. Payment and Settlement**

4.1 Payment for Ordinary Shares subscribed for in this offering shall be made in full in cleared funds prior to the admission of the shares to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market.

4.2 Settlement shall occur on or about the third Business Day following the pricing date (T+3), in accordance with standard market practice for equity offerings under English law.

4.3 All payments made under this Prospectus shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) and shall be non-refundable except as required by applicable law or regulation, or as otherwise agreed in writing between the Company and the Underwriter.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party:

(a) in the case of the Company, to: 168 Old Broad Street, London;  
(b) in the case of the Underwriter, to: 11 Old Broad Street, London.

5.2 A notice shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, two Business Days after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is generated.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information disclosed in connection with this offering, including financial data, customer contracts, and commercial terms, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

6.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive the completion or termination of the offering for a period of five years from the date of this Prospectus.

**7. General Provisions**

7.1 This Prospectus and any related agreements shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.

7.3 Nothing in this Prospectus constitutes or shall be deemed to constitute an admission of liability, a waiver of rights, or a variation of terms except as expressly stated herein.

7.4 The Company confirms that Foxley Engineering is a material customer, accounting for 22.4% of the Company’s total revenue for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.
