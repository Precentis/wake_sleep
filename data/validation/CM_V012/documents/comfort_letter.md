1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Comfort Letter (the “Letter”), the following terms shall have the meanings set out below unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) “Company” means Galloway Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13333879, whose registered office is at 168 Old Broad Street, London;

(b) “Underwriter” means Rothley Foods Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12686710, whose registered office is at 11 Old Broad Street, London;

(c) “Foxley Engineering Ltd” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13678647, whose registered office is at 88 Moorgate, London, being a material customer of the Company;

(d) “IPO” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company underwritten by the Underwriter;

(e) “Cut-off Date” means 24 May 2026, being the date up to which the procedures referred to in this Letter were performed.

1.2 Any reference to a party includes its successors and permitted assigns. References to “writing” or “written” include communication by electronic means where such means are legally recognised.

2. Purpose and Scope of Procedures

2.1 This Letter is delivered by the Company to the Underwriter solely for the purpose of enabling the Underwriter to carry out its due diligence in connection with the IPO.

2.2 The procedures undertaken by the Company’s financial and legal advisers were limited in scope and consisted of a review of the audited financial statements of the Company for the financial years ended 31 December 2023 and 31 December 2024, the unaudited financial statements for the period ending on the Cut-off Date, and certain internal management accounts and commercial contracts relevant to the financial and operational performance of the Company, including but not limited to the supply agreement dated 15 March 2022 between the Company and Foxley Engineering Ltd.

2.3 The Company confirms that, to the best of its knowledge, information and belief after making due and careful enquiry, the financial data provided to the Underwriter, including revenue figures, cost structures, and profit margins for the relevant periods, are fairly stated in all material respects.

3. Representations and Undertakings

3.1 The Company represents and undertakes to the Underwriter that:

(a) all financial and operational information furnished in connection with the IPO, including but not limited to revenue derived from Foxley Engineering Ltd, which constituted 37.5% of the Company’s total revenue during the 12-month period ending on the Cut-off Date, has been prepared in accordance with applicable accounting standards and fairly reflects the financial position of the Company as of the respective dates and for the respective periods to which they relate;

(b) no material adverse change has occurred in the business, financial condition, or prospects of the Company since 31 December 2024, except as disclosed in writing to the Underwriter prior to the date hereof;

(c) the Company remains contractually bound to Foxley Engineering Ltd under the existing supply agreement, with no notice of termination or material amendment having been received or served by either party as of the Cut-off Date.

4. Payment and Indemnification

4.1 The Underwriter shall have no obligation to make any payment to the Company under or in connection with this Letter.

4.2 The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Underwriter, its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against any and all claims, losses or liabilities arising out of or in connection with any material inaccuracy in the information provided by the Company to the Underwriter for the purposes of the IPO, except to the extent such inaccuracy is directly attributable to the Underwriter’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Letter shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Clause 1.1.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or upon electronic confirmation of receipt if sent by email.

6. Limitations and Disclaimer

6.1 The procedures conducted by the Company in support of this Letter were limited to the information available as of the Cut-off Date and did not include any forensic audit or independent third-party verification beyond the scope of standard due diligence.

6.2 Our procedures covered the period up to 24 May 2026 (the cut-off date).

6.3 This Letter is addressed to and solely for the benefit of the Underwriter and may not be relied upon by any other person, nor may it be disclosed, distributed or published in whole or in part to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company, except as required by law or regulation.

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party under or in connection with this Letter, except to the extent that such information is already in the public domain (other than by breach of this Clause) or is required to be disclosed by law, regulation or any competent authority.
