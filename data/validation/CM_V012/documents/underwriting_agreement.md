UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●], 2026, between:

(1) Galloway Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13333879, whose registered office is at 168 Old Broad Street, London (the “Company”); and

(2) Rothley Foods Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12686710, whose registered office is at 11 Old Broad Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

Recitals:

A. The Company intends to make an initial public offering (the “Offering”) of its ordinary shares (the “Shares”) pursuant to a prospectus (the “Prospectus”) to be prepared in connection with the admission of the Shares to the standard listing segment of the Official List of the UK Financial Conduct Authority and to trading on the Main Market of the London Stock Exchange.

B. The Company has authorised for issue and will issue 16,000,000 ordinary shares (the “Firm Shares”) in connection with the Offering.

C. The Company has 63,000,000 ordinary shares in issue.

D. The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Firm Shares and certain additional Shares on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

The parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London.

“Closing Date” means the date specified in the pricing agreement for the settlement of the Offering, which shall be no later than 10 Business Days after the Pricing Date.

“Pricing Date” means 5 June 2026.

“Over-allotment Option” has the meaning given in Clause 4.

“Person” includes any individual, company, corporation, partnership, association, joint venture, trust, unincorporated organisation or other entity.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to underwrite the purchase of the Firm Shares from the Company, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

3. Sale and Purchase of Firm Shares

The Company shall issue and sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter shall purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at the public offering price less an underwriting discount as agreed in the pricing agreement. Payment for the Firm Shares shall be made in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Company.

4. Over-allotment Option

The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the “Over-allotment Option”) to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares, at the same public offering price less underwriting discount. The Underwriter may exercise the Over-allotment Option in whole or in part, at any time prior to 30 days after the Closing Date, by written notice to the Company. Any Shares issued on exercise of the Over-allotment Option shall be deemed to form part of the Offering.

5. Representations and Warranties

Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:

(a) it has the full power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement;

(b) this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms; and

(c) the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate any applicable law or any agreement to which it is a party.

6. Conditions to Obligations

The obligations of the Underwriter hereunder are subject to the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the Company on the Closing Date, compliance by the Company with its covenants herein, and the receipt by the Underwriter of a comfort letter from the Company's auditors, addressed to the Underwriter, in customary form and substance.

7. Comfort Letter

(b) The comfort letter referred to in Clause 6 shall be dated no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date.

8. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or courier, or sent by email to the address or email address set out in the preamble. Notices shall be deemed received when delivered, if delivered personally; 48 hours after posting, if sent by post; and when confirmed by electronic receipt, if sent by email.

9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, unless disclosure is required by law, regulation or stock exchange rule, or the information is in or enters the public domain through no fault of the receiving party.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
