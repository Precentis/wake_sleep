# LOCK-UP AGREEMENT EXTRACT

**This Lock-Up Agreement** (the "Agreement") is made on [●] May 2026 between:

1. **Galloway Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13333879 whose registered office is at 168 Old Broad Street, London ("the Company"); and  
2. **Rothley Foods Securities**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12686710 whose registered office is at 11 Old Broad Street, London ("the Underwriter").

**Recitals**  
A. The Company intends to proceed with an initial public offering (the "Offering") of its ordinary shares (the "Shares") underwritten by the Underwriter.  
B. As a condition to its obligations under the underwriting agreement in connection with the Offering, the Underwriter has required the Company and certain shareholders to enter into lock-up agreements restricting the sale, transfer or disposal of Shares during a specified period.  
C. This Agreement sets out the terms under which certain restrictions apply to the disposal of Shares by relevant parties, including the Company as a shareholder of its own Shares for the purposes of this Agreement.

**For and in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:**

**1. Definitions**

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in New York;  
"Prospectus" means the final prospectus dated 4 May 2026 relating to the Offering;  
"Shareholder" means any person or entity holding Shares in the capital of the Company, including the Company itself in respect of treasury shares or unissued Shares held prior to the Offering;  
"Shares" means ordinary shares in the Company, nominal value £0.01 each.

**2. Lock-Up Restriction**

The Shareholder shall not, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, grant any option to purchase, make any short sale, pledge or otherwise dispose of or transfer any Shares, or enter into any hedging or similar transaction with the same economic effect, during the period commencing on the date of the Prospectus and ending 365 days thereafter (the "Lock-Up Period"). This restriction applies to all Shares beneficially owned, whether now held or hereafter acquired, and includes any Shares acquired upon the exercise of options, warrants or other rights, or upon conversion or exchange of other securities.

**3. Payment**

No consideration is payable by either party under this Agreement solely by virtue of the execution of this Agreement. Any obligations arising hereunder are in support of the Offering and are not subject to separate monetary settlement.

**4. Notices**

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the Company or the registered office of the Underwriter, as applicable. Notices shall be deemed received:  
(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, two Business Days after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, upon transmission confirmation, provided that no automated error message is returned.  
For the Company: 168 Old Broad Street, London.  
For the Underwriter: 11 Old Broad Street, London.

**5. Confidentiality**

Each party shall treat as confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the Offering, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under confidentiality obligations; (b) as required by law, regulation or competent authority; or (c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

**6. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings relating thereto.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
