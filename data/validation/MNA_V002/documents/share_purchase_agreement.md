SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 8 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1)	Rothley Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13160682 whose registered office is at 43 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2)	Foxley Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15434788 whose registered office is at 154 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

RECITALS:

(A)	The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Larkspur Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11651361 whose registered office is at 54 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B)	The Buyer wishes to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1.	INTERPRETATION

1.1	In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event, change or effect which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company taken as a whole, excluding any effect to the extent resulting from: (a) changes in general economic, business or political conditions; (b) changes affecting the food production industry generally; or (c) pandemics, provided that such exclusions shall not apply to the extent that the Company is disproportionately affected relative to other participants in its industry.

2.	PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1	The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to acquire, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $175,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3.	PAYMENT

3.1	The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4.	CLOSING AND CONDITIONS

4.1	The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on 28 May 2027 (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, unless another date or location is agreed in writing by the parties.

4.2	On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms and such other documents as are reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the entire issued share capital of the Company free from all Encumbrances.

4.3	The obligations of the parties to complete the transactions contemplated by this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or lawful waiver of the following conditions (the "Conditions") by the Closing Date:

(a)	all necessary corporate and regulatory approvals shall have been obtained;

(b)	there shall have been no Material Adverse Change since the date of this Agreement;

(c)	the representations and warranties of each party herein shall be true and accurate in all material respects as of the Closing Date.

4.4	If any Condition is not satisfied or waived by the Closing Date, the parties shall use all reasonable endeavours to agree in writing an extension, provided that such extension shall not exceed the long stop date set out in Clause 4.5.

4.5	If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 7 July 2027), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, in which event neither party shall have any further liability under this Agreement, except for obligations under Clauses 10 and 11 which shall survive termination.

10.	SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1	The General Warranties given by the Seller in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the defaulting party before the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which the right to make such claim shall be extinguished.

11.	CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1	Each party undertakes that it shall not, and shall procure that its officers, employees and advisers shall not, disclose to any person any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except:

(a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

(b) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidentiality; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

12.	NOTICES

12.1	Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

13.	GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1	This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2	Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
