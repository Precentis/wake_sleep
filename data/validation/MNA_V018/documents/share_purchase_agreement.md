SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 14 June 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Pemberton Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10871447, whose registered office is at 98 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Juniper Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12500752, whose registered office is at 98 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Seller agrees to sell and the Buyer agrees to purchase all of the issued share capital in Glenrock Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14173362, whose registered office is at 13 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the term "Closing Date" means 12 August 2026.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be $220,000,000 (two hundred and twenty million US dollars), to be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in accordance with the payment mechanism set out in Section 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The closing of the transactions contemplated under this Agreement (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s legal advisers, or at such other location as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer; (b) a certified copy of the board resolution of the Company approving the change of ownership; and (c) such other documents as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares.

4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

4.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that as at the date of this Agreement and as at the Closing Date: (a) it is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances; (b) it has full capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder; and (c) the execution of this Agreement does not breach any agreement to which the Seller or the Company is a party.

10. SURVIVAL AND NOTIFICATION

10.1 The General Warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under this Agreement based on a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under or in connection with any breach of warranty under this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $33,000,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $220,000,000, as set out in Section 2.1.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

16.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 98 Moorgate, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 98 Old Broad Street, London.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from or about the other party or the Company in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.
