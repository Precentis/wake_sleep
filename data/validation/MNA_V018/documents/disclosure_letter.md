2.1 This Disclosure Letter is delivered by the Seller pursuant to the Sale Agreement and serves to disclose matters which qualify or are exceptions to the Warranties given by the Seller.  

2.2 The disclosures set out in this Disclosure Letter are made solely for the purposes of qualifying the Warranties in the Sale Agreement and do not create any independent right or obligation outside that agreement.  

**3. Scope of Disclosure**  
3.1 The disclosures contained in this letter are specific and are not to be construed as admitting that the matters disclosed are material for the purposes of the Sale Agreement or that they would give rise to a claim in the absence of disclosure.  

3.2 References to sections of the Sale Agreement or Warranties are to the corresponding provisions in Schedule 3 of the Sale Agreement.  

3.3 The disclosures herein are qualified in their entirety by the terms of the Sale Agreement and are subject to all applicable contractual limitations, including time limits and financial caps on liability.  

**4. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
4.1 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3 concerning Material Contracts, the Seller confirms that, as of the date hereof, no material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision.  

4.2 The Seller has not entered into any agreement or understanding with Pemberton Robotics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15120874, registered office at 177 Gresham Street, London) that would require notification, consent or approval in connection with the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

4.3 The Seller confirms that the Company’s material contracts include, but are not limited to, the service agreement dated 15 April 2023 with Pemberton Robotics Ltd for the supply of marine propulsion subsystems (the “Subsystems Agreement”), which remains in full force and effect and contains no provision permitting termination or amendment upon a change of control of the Company.  

**5. General Qualifications**  
5.1 The disclosures in this letter are made on a without-prejudice basis and are not intended to, and shall not, enlarge the scope of any Warranty or the obligations of the Seller under the Sale Agreement.  

5.2 Disclosure of any matter in one section of this letter shall not be deemed to constitute disclosure of any other matter unless it is clearly and expressly linked to that other matter.  

**6. Payment**  
6.1 Any liability arising under or in connection with this Disclosure Letter shall be subject to the payment mechanisms, caps and procedures set out in the Sale Agreement.  

**7. Notices**  
7.1 Notices under this Disclosure Letter shall be delivered in accordance with the notice provisions set out in Clause 14 of the Sale Agreement, addressed to the parties at their respective registered offices.

8.1 The contents of this Disclosure Letter and any information disclosed herein shall be treated as confidential by the Buyer and may only be disclosed to its professional advisers, officers or employees on a need-to-know basis, in accordance with Clause 12 of the Sale Agreement.

9.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Disclosure Letter.
