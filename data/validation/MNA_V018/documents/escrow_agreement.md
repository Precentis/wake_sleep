ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 30 June 2026 (the “Effective Date”) among:

(1) Juniper Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12500752, whose registered office is at 98 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Pemberton Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10871447, whose registered office is at 98 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a company duly authorised to act as escrow agent under the laws of England and Wales (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Effective Date (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14173362, whose registered office is at 13 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”).

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase consideration (the “Escrow Amount”) shall be deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the Seller’s indemnification obligations under the SPA.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

   “Closing Date” means 12 August 2026.

   “Escrow Amount” means the aggregate sum of £7,500,000 (seven million five hundred thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account established by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement.

   “Indemnification Claim” means any claim for indemnification made by the Buyer against the Seller pursuant to the terms of the SPA.

2. Establishment of Escrow

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within two (2) Business Days following the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Account”) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be held by the Escrow Agent until released in accordance with Clause 4.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending Indemnification Claims notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to that date. For the avoidance of doubt, any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Indemnification Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such claim is finally resolved by mutual agreement of the Buyer and the Seller or by a final, non-appealable decision of a competent authority.

5. Payment Instructions

5.1 All payments to be made by the Escrow Agent under this Agreement shall be made by same-day or next-day electronic funds transfer to such account as may be designated in writing by the recipient Party.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile, or by overnight courier to the address of the relevant Party set out in this Agreement, or to such other address as a Party may designate by prior written notice.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose any such information to any third party, except as required by law or legal process.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce, seated in New York, New York, United States. The number of arbitrators shall be one, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
