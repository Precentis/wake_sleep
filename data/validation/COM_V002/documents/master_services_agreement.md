2. Appointment and Scope of Services

2.1 The Customer hereby appoints the Supplier to provide the Services, and the Supplier accepts such appointment, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services in accordance with applicable industry standards and in a professional and timely manner.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and continue in full force and effect until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 8 October 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by giving written notice to the other Party if the other Party commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of receiving written notice requiring remedy.

4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule B (“Fees”), in advance on a quarterly basis.

4.2 All fees are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing from the due date until payment is made in full.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the receiving Party or to such other address as notified in writing.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received: (a) if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; (b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two Business Days after posting; and (c) if sent by email, on the next Business Day following transmission.

11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Each party's total liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be capped at $1,000,000 in aggregate for any and all claims during any calendar year.

11.2 The cap set out in Clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or to liability arising from fraud.
