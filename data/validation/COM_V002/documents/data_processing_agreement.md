1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Data Processing Agreement ("DPA"), the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

(a) "Applicable Data Protection Law" means (i) the UK General Data Protection Regulation ((EU) 2016/679) as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, and (ii) the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended from time to time;

(b) "Customer Personal Data" means any personal data (as defined under Applicable Data Protection Law) that is disclosed by Dunmore Medical Ltd to Delamere Analytics Ltd or otherwise processed by Delamere Analytics Ltd on behalf of Dunmore Medical Ltd in the course of providing the Services;

(c) "Services" means the managed IT services provided by Delamere Analytics Ltd to Dunmore Medical Ltd under the Master Services Agreement dated 15 March 2023, as amended.

1.2 Capitalised terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Services Agreement between the Parties.

2. Role of the Parties

2.1 Dunmore Medical Ltd acts as the data controller in respect of the Customer Personal Data, and Delamere Analytics Ltd acts as a data processor.

2.2 Delamere Analytics Ltd shall only process Customer Personal Data on documented instructions from Dunmore Medical Ltd, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 Delamere Analytics Ltd shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction or damage.

3.2 The technical and organisational measures include, but are not limited to, encryption of data at rest and in transit, access controls based on role-based permissions, regular system audits, and staff training on data protection principles. These measures shall be reviewed and updated at least annually.

3.3 Delamere Analytics Ltd shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Supplier may engage sub-processors only with the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 Delamere Analytics Ltd has appointed Juniper Marine Ltd (company number 14975018, registered office 89 King William Street, London) as a sub-processor for data backup and disaster recovery services.

4.3 The Supplier shall remain fully liable for all acts and omissions of any sub-processor as if they were its own.

5. Data Subject Rights

5.1 The Supplier shall, taking into account the nature of the processing, provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and data portability.

5.2 Any such request received by the Supplier shall be promptly notified to the Customer, and the Supplier shall not respond to the request without the Customer’s prior written instructions.

6. Compliance with Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. Data Transfers

7.1 The Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area unless such transfer is in compliance with the transfer mechanisms permitted under Applicable Data Protection Law, including the use of the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

8. Security Breach Notification

8.1 In the event of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, the Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay and in any event no later than 48 hours after becoming aware of the breach.

8.2 The notification shall include, where possible, the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Supplier shall maintain the confidentiality of all Customer Personal Data and shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under this DPA.

10. Term and Termination

10.1 This DPA shall commence on 7 April 2027 and shall continue in force until the termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement.

10.2 Upon termination, the Supplier shall, at the Customer’s election, either securely delete or return all Customer Personal Data.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice to be given under this DPA shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party as set out in the Canonical Party Details.

12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 This DPA and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this DPA.
