**Service Level Schedule**  

1. **Interpretation**  
   1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) "Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, a body corporate or other legal entity which Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party;  
   (b) "Business Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
   (c) "Change Request" means any request by the Customer to vary the Services;  
   (d) "Downtime" means any period during which the core IT infrastructure managed by the Supplier is wholly or partially inaccessible to authorised users of the Customer for reasons within the Supplier’s reasonable control;  
   (e) "Incident" means any unplanned interruption or reduction in the quality of the Services;  
   (f) "Monthly Availability" means the percentage of time in a calendar month that the Services are available, calculated as (1 – (Total Downtime in minutes / Total minutes in the month)) × 100;  
   (g) "Service Credit" means a credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice as compensation for failure to meet a Service Level;  
   (h) "Service Level" means a performance standard specified in this Schedule;  
   (i) "Services" means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer under the Master Services Agreement.  

2. **Service Levels**  
   2.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure Monthly Availability of the Services is no less than 99.9%.  
   2.2 Monthly Availability shall be measured at the network level at the primary data centre serving the Customer.  
   2.3 Measurement shall be based on monitoring data recorded in the Supplier’s central system logs, which shall be deemed accurate unless proven otherwise by clear and verifiable evidence provided by the Customer within ten (10) Business Days of receipt of the monthly performance report.  

3. **Service Credits**  
   3.1 Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet a Service Level.  
   3.2 Service Credits shall not exceed, in aggregate, 25% of the monthly fee for any single month.  
   3.3 A Service Credit shall only apply if the Customer submits a written claim, including supporting data, within fifteen (15) Business Days of the end of the month to which the claim relates.  
   3.4 The Supplier shall process approved Service Credit claims within thirty (30) days of validation and apply the credit to the next invoice.  

4. **Monthly Availability Service Credit**  
   4.1 If the Monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the monthly fee applies.  
   4.2 If the Monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the monthly fee applies.  
   4.3 The monthly fee is $120,000.  
   4.4 No Service Credit shall apply if Downtime results from: (a) force majeure; (b) the Customer’s or its users’ equipment, software, or network; (c) scheduled maintenance with at least 48 hours’ notice; or (d) unauthorised actions by third parties not engaged by the Supplier.  

5. **Payment**  
   5.1 The Customer shall pay the monthly fee in advance on the first day of each calendar month, unless otherwise agreed in writing.  
   5.2 All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.  
   5.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at 2% per annum above the base rate of the Bank of England, calculated daily.  

6. **Notices**  
   6.1 Any notice under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.  
   6.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: Dunmore Medical Ltd, 87 Cheapside, London.  
   6.3 Notices to the Supplier shall be sent to: Delamere Analytics Ltd, 21 Moorgate, London.  

7. **Confidentiality**  
   7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with prior written consent.  
   7.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Services for a period of three (3) years.  

8. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
   8.1 This Service Level Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
   8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
