**ORWELL CAPITAL LLP**

**Initial Public Offering of Ordinary Shares**  
**Date of this Prospectus: 26 May 2026**

---

**1. Definitions**

In this Prospectus and any related offering documents, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Company” means Orwell Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15223157, with its registered office at 123 Bishopsgate, London;  
“Underwriter” means Corrib Industries Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12558225, with its registered office at 139 Gresham Street, London;  
“Netherby Bioscience plc” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10848431, with its registered office at 14 Cheapside, London;  
“Prospectus” means this document, including all appendices, schedules and incorporated information by reference, dated 26 May 2026;  
“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;  
“Net Proceeds” means the aggregate amount received by the Company from the offering of the Ordinary Shares, after deduction of underwriting discounts, commissions, and offering expenses payable by the Company.

---

**2. Offering Summary**

The Company is offering its Ordinary Shares to the public for the first time. The offering is being conducted through a firm commitment underwriting arrangement with the Underwriter. The Underwriter has agreed to purchase the shares from the Company and resell them to the public. The shares will be listed on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market for listed securities.

---

**3. Use of Proceeds**

The net proceeds from the offering are expected to amount to $150,000,000. The Company intends to apply these net proceeds as follows:  
(a) $68,000,000 to repay in full the outstanding balance under its revolving credit facility;  
(b) $45,000,000 to fund capital expenditure, including the construction and fit-out of new operational facilities and the acquisition of technology infrastructure;  
(c) $45,000,000 for general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions, and investment in research and development initiatives.  

The allocation of proceeds is based on current plans and expectations. Actual allocation may vary depending on market conditions and the Company’s evolving needs, provided such variations remain consistent with the overall purpose described herein.

---

**4. Payment**

All payments to be made by the Underwriter to the Company in connection with the purchase of the Ordinary Shares shall be made in immediately available funds via wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Company no later than two business days following the closing date of the offering. The closing date shall be on or about 21 April 2026, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.

**6. Confidentiality**

Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the offering, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except: (a) to its affiliates, officers, directors, employees, legal counsel, auditors, or financial advisors who have a need to know such information and are bound by confidentiality obligations; (b) as required by law, regulation, or legal process; or (c) to the extent such information is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party.
