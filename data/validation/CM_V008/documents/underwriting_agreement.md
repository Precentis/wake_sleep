UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on the date set forth in the records of the parties hereto by and between Orwell Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated under the laws of England and Wales, company number 15223157, with its registered office at 123 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company"), and Corrib Industries Securities, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales, company number 12558225, with its registered office at 139 Gresham Street, London (the "Underwriter").

WHEREAS, the Company proposes to issue and sell an aggregate of 12,500,000 ordinary shares (the "Shares") in connection with its initial public offering (the "Offering") on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions  
For purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings ascribed below:  
(a) "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in London, England.  
(b) "Closing Date" means the date on which settlement of the sale of the Shares occurs, as specified in the final prospectus relating to the Offering.  
(c) "Gross Proceeds" means the aggregate price received by the Company for the Shares sold in the Offering, calculated as 12,500,000 shares at $14.00 per share, amounting to $175,000,000.  
(d) "Offering" means the initial public offering of the Shares underwritten pursuant to this Agreement.  
(e) "Pricing Date" means 26 May 2026, the date on which the final pricing of the Shares is determined.

2. Appointment and Engagement  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter accepts such appointment, subject to the terms and conditions herein.

3. Payment and Commission  
The Company shall pay the Underwriter a commission equal to 7.0% of the Gross Proceeds of the Offering. Such commission shall amount to $10,412,500, calculated on 12,500,000 Shares at $14.00 per Share. Payment of the commission shall be made on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Underwriter.

4. Representations and Warranties  
Each party represents and warrants to the other that:  
(a) it has the full power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement;  
(b) this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid, and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms; and  
(c) the execution and delivery of this Agreement do not violate any law, regulation, or contractual obligation to which it is subject.

5. Notices  
All notices, requests and other communications hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, by electronic transmission with confirmation of receipt, or by internationally recognized overnight courier to the respective addresses set forth above or to such other address as a party may designate by notice.

6. Confidentiality  
Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under confidentiality obligations.

7. Conditions to Closing  
(b) The Underwriter shall have received from the Company’s independent accountants a comfort letter, dated no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date, confirming certain financial data and supporting the prospectus. The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the pricing date. The pricing date is 26 May 2026.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England for the determination of any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

9. Selling Restrictions and Permitted Jurisdictions  
The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

10. Entire Agreement  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether written or oral.
