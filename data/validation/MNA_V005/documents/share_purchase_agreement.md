SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 17 May 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Galloway Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10301510, whose registered office is at 46 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Dunmore Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15959491, whose registered office is at 69 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12974753, whose registered office is at 48 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 3 August 2027;

   "Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles and methodology set out in Schedule 1;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   "Target Working Capital" means $2,000,000.

   "Working Capital" means current assets minus current liabilities, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies consistently applied by the Company as of the date of the latest audited financial statements.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. PURCHASE PRICE AND ADJUSTMENT

2.1 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller a purchase price of $35,000,000 (the "Purchase Price") for the Shares, payable in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be adjusted in accordance with Clause 2.3.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, the Purchase Price shall not be increased.

3. CLOSING

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, duly executed by the Seller;

   (b) all share certificates representing the Shares, accompanied by such documents as may be required to effect legal transfer under the laws of England and Wales; and

   (c) a certificate of non-residence for stamp duty purposes, if applicable.

3.2 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller on the Closing Date the adjusted Purchase Price, after giving effect to any adjustment under Clause 2.3.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 46 Bishopsgate, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

4.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 69 Fleet Street, London, and to the attention of the Chief Financial Officer.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

6.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
