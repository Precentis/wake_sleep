MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OAKHURST MEDICAL LTD  
HELD AT THE REGISTERED OFFICE ON 22 MAY 2027

1. **Attendance and Quorum**  
   1.1. The following directors were present:  
        (a) Mr A. Thorley  
        (b) Dr L. Mendoza  
   1.2. It was confirmed that the Company has five (5) appointed directors in total and that 2 directors were present.  
   1.3. The Chair noted that 2 directors constitute a quorum under the Company’s Articles of Association, and the meeting was quorate.

2. **Chair and Secretary**  
   2.1. Mr A. Thorley was appointed Chair of the meeting.  
   2.2. The Company Secretary, Ms R. Carter, was present in an advisory capacity and will retain custody of these minutes.

3. **Declaration of Interests**  
   3.1. The Chair invited any director to declare a personal interest in any matter to be discussed.  
   3.2. Mr A. Thorley declared a direct pecuniary interest in the proposed transaction with Galloway Bioscience plc due to a consultancy agreement between himself and Galloway Bioscience plc dated 14 June 2023.  
   3.3. Dr L. Mendoza confirmed she had no interests requiring disclosure.

4. **Review of Proposed Transaction**  
   4.1. The Chair presented a copy of the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 22 May 2027 between Galloway Bioscience plc (the Seller) and Dunmore Industries Ltd (the Buyer) for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Medical Ltd (the Company).  
   4.2. The Company Secretary confirmed that the Seller holds 100% of the issued shares in the Company.  
   4.3. The Board reviewed the terms of the transaction, including the proposed transfer of all shares in the Company to the Buyer.  
   4.4. The Board received legal advice from external counsel confirming that the transaction is structured as a share sale and will result in a change of control of the Company.

5. **Consideration of Material Customer Relationship**  
   5.1. The Board discussed the Company’s commercial relationship with Brightwater Bioscience plc, a material customer incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15315144, registered office 26 Moorgate, London.  
   5.2. It was noted that a supply agreement with Brightwater Bioscience plc, dated 3 January 2025, includes a clause requiring notification of change of control, but does not permit termination solely on account of such change.

6. **Resolution**  
   6.1. RESOLVED THAT the Board approves the entry by Galloway Bioscience plc into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 22 May 2027 for the sale of all issued shares in Oakhurst Medical Ltd to Dunmore Industries Ltd.  
   6.2. RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Company Secretary be authorised to provide such documentation and certifications as may be reasonably required by the Buyer or Seller to give effect to the transaction, subject to compliance with English law and the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

7. **Adjournment**  
   7.1. Having concluded the business, the meeting adjourned at 16:42.
