ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF OAKHURST MEDICAL LTD

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Dunmore Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15959491, with its registered office at 69 Fleet Street, London;  
“Company” means Oakhurst Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12974753, with its registered office at 48 Bishopsgate, London;  
“Material Customer” means Brightwater Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15315144, with its registered office at 26 Moorgate, London;  
“Seller” means Galloway Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10301510, with its registered office at 46 Bishopsgate, London;  
“Shareholders” means the holders of the issued shares in the Company from time to time;  
“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company.  
1.2 Unless otherwise stated, any reference to a statutory provision includes any statutory modification, re-enactment, amendment or replacement thereof.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The Company may from time to time, by ordinary resolution, increase its share capital by such sum and in such manner as the Shareholders think fit.  
2.3 The rights attached to any class of shares may only be varied with the consent in writing of the holders of not less than 75% of the issued shares of that class.

3. Transfer of Shares  
3.1 Subject to these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of their Shares by instrument in writing in the usual or common form or in any other form approved by the Board.  
3.2 The instrument of transfer shall be executed by or on behalf of the transferor and, where required by the Board, shall be executed by the transferee.  
3.3 No transfer of a Share shall be registered if the Board, at its absolute discretion, determines that the transfer would result in a breach of any agreement to which the Company is a party, including but not limited to the agreement dated 14 November 2026 between the Company and the Material Customer.

4. Directors  
4.1 The number of directors shall not be less than one nor more than ten.  
4.2 Directors may be appointed by ordinary resolution of the Shareholders.  
4.3 A director may be removed from office before the expiration of their term by ordinary resolution, subject to any contractual rights to compensation.  
4.4 The Board may appoint any person to be a director, provided that such appointment is ratified by the Shareholders at the next general meeting.

5. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
5.1 The Board shall meet as often as the business of the Company requires, and at least once every calendar quarter.  
5.2 Notice of a meeting of the Board shall be given to every director at least 48 hours prior to the meeting, unless all directors present waive such notice.  
5.3 A resolution in writing, signed by all the directors entitled to receive notice of a Board meeting, shall be as valid as if passed at a meeting of the Board.  
5.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the Board unless the quorum is present at the commencement of the meeting. If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the appointed time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other date, time and place as the Board may determine.

6. Payment  
6.1 Any payment due under an instrument governed by these Articles shall be made in sterling by cleared funds to such bank account as the recipient may designate in writing.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given to the Company shall be in writing and delivered to, or sent by first-class post or recognised overnight courier to, the registered office of the Company.  
7.2 Any notice to a member shall be sent to their registered address as recorded in the register of members.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Every director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information acquired by them in the course of their duties, unless disclosure is required by law or permitted in writing by the Board.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
