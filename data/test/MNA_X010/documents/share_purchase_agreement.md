# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 5 June 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Upton Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14168126, whose registered office is at 79 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) **Brightwater Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13420366, whose registered office is at 105 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Bellhaven Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14322399, whose registered office is at 160 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- "Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;
- "Closing Date" means 19 August 2026;
- "Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4.1;
- "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to Clause 2.1;
- "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company, being 100% of the ordinary shares in the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Purchase Price is $70,000,000, which shall be paid in full to the Seller by the Buyer on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall acquire the Shares free from all liens, charges, equities and third-party rights.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer in respect of all the Shares;

(b) such deeds of resignation and board minutes as may be necessary to effect the removal of any directors or secretaries not retained by the Buyer;

(c) original constitutional documents of the Company.

**4. CONDITIONS**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon:

(a) the Buyer having completed satisfactory legal and financial due diligence on the Company;

(b) no material adverse change having occurred in the business, financial condition or prospects of the Company since 31 March 2026;

(c) all necessary consents and approvals having been obtained from third parties, including without limitation any required from **Brightwater Robotics Ltd** (company number 11388228).

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement, being 24 October 2026, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

**8. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES**

8.4 **Limitation of Liability**: The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $8,400,000. The Purchase Price is $70,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

**10. CONFIDENTIALITY**

10.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its representatives shall not, disclose any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby to any third party, except as required by law or regulation.

**11. NOTICES**

11.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

**12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
