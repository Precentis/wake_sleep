1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Agreement" means this contract for the supply and purchase of robotic automation systems between the parties;

"Buyer" means Brightwater Robotics Ltd, company number 11388228, a company incorporated in England and Wales, with its registered office at 17 King William Street, London;

"Seller" means Bellhaven Engineering Ltd, company number 14322399, a company incorporated in England and Wales, with its registered office at 160 Cheapside, London;

"Effective Date" means 15 April 2020;

"Services" means the design, manufacture, testing and delivery of custom robotic automation cells as specified in Schedule A;

"Confidential Information" means all technical, commercial or operational information disclosed directly or indirectly by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, in writing or by inspection of materials.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction or interpretation.

2. Supply of Services

2.1 The Seller shall provide the Services to the Buyer in accordance with the specifications, timelines and quality standards set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Seller shall ensure that all personnel engaged in the performance of the Services are suitably qualified and experienced.

3. Payment Terms

3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Seller the total contract sum of £3,250,000 in four instalments as follows:

(a) 20% upon execution of this Agreement;

(b) 30% upon completion of design and engineering sign-off;

(c) 40% upon successful factory acceptance testing;

(d) 10% upon successful site commissioning and handover.

3.2 All invoices shall be submitted by the Seller to the Buyer’s designated accounts payable department and paid within 30 days of receipt, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated representative of the relevant party.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 17 King William Street, London, and to the attention of the Procurement Director.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 160 Cheapside, London, and to the attention of the Contracts Manager.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five years.

14. Termination

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated:

(a) by mutual written agreement of the parties;

(b) by either party if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice;

(c) by either party if the other enters into liquidation, administration or any form of insolvency proceeding.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this clause, "change of control" includes the acquisition by any person or entity of more than 50% of the issued share capital or voting rights of the relevant party. The counterparty is Brightwater Robotics Ltd.

15. General Provisions

15.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, representations or agreements relating to its subject matter.

15.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.

15.3 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
