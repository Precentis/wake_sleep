3. DIRECTORS’ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST

4. UPDATE ON PROPOSED SALE OF THE ENTIRE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL  
The board reviewed the status of the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Bellhaven Engineering Ltd (the Company) by Upton Retail Group plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14168126, registered office 79 Old Broad Street, London. The Seller in the transaction is Brightwater Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13420366, registered office 105 Cannon Street, London. The Company is incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14322399, with registered office at 160 Cheapside, London.  

The board noted that the draft share purchase agreement was under review by legal counsel and that completion is expected to occur on or around 30 June 2026, subject to customary conditions precedent.  

5. LITIGATION RISK UPDATE  
The board noted a letter before action dated 10 June 2026 issued by Foxley Robotics Ltd, addressed to the Company, threatening proceedings for approximately $2,750,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply contract dated 15 March 2025. The claim relates to components supplied to Brightwater Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11388228, registered office 17 King William Street, London.  

Legal counsel has confirmed that the letter complies with pre-action protocol requirements under English civil procedure rules. The board resolved that management shall continue to monitor developments in relation to this matter, maintain all relevant documentation, and report to the board biweekly until resolution or formal withdrawal of the claim. No provision has been recognised in the unaudited financial statements as of 31 March 2026, pending further assessment.  

6. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW  
The interim management accounts for the period ending 31 May 2026 were presented. Revenue for the period was £8.2 million, with a net profit margin of 6.4%. The board noted continued performance pressures in the automation components division, offset by growth in after-sales service contracts.
