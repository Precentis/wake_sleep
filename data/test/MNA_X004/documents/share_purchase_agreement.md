SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 17 June 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Bellhaven Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12182607 whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Upton Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13898570 whose registered office is at 173 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Galloway Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10509082 whose registered office is at 140 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 5 October 2026;

"Company" has the meaning given in the preamble;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties set out in paragraphs (a) to (c) of Clause 7.1 (Title, Capacity, Tax);

"General Warranties" means the warranties contained in Clause 7.2 to Clause 7.8 inclusive, excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Seller" has the meaning given in the preamble;

"Tax" means any tax, duty, levy, impost, rate, assessment, withholding or other fiscal charge of any kind imposed by any governmental or public authority;

"Year End" means the financial year end of the Company, being 31 March in each calendar year.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $60,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller by bank transfer in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to such bank account as the Seller shall have notified to the Buyer in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Buyer shall bear all bank charges and transfer fees arising from the payment of the Purchase Price.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the General Warranties and Fundamental Warranties (other than fraud) shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $9,000,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $60,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1.

10. SURVIVAL AND NOTICE OF CLAIMS

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity and Tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under any warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which the relevant warranty claim shall be extinguished.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party undertakes to the other not to disclose any confidential information received in connection with this Agreement to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

11.2 This obligation shall continue after the Closing or termination of this Agreement.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in the preamble.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), misrepresentation or otherwise, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
