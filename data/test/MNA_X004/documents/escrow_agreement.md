1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Upton Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13898570, whose registered office is at 173 Fleet Street, London.

“Company” means Galloway Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10509082, whose registered office is at 140 Cannon Street, London.

“Escrow Agent” means the third-party financial institution appointed by mutual agreement of the Seller and the Buyer to hold the Escrow Funds in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

“Escrow Funds” means the sum of £2,150,000 (two million one hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement dated 12 June 2026, deposited by the Buyer into an escrow account for the benefit of the Seller, subject to release in accordance with Clause 5.

“Seller” means Bellhaven Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12182607, whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London.

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company entered into between the Seller and the Buyer dated 12 June 2026.

“Quayside Energy plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15692338, whose registered office is at 39 Ludgate Hill, London, and which constitutes a material customer of the Company.

2. Escrow Arrangement

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Funds with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days of the Completion Date as defined in the Share Purchase Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a designated interest-bearing account at a branch of a Schedule 1 bank in London, and any interest accrued shall form part of the Escrow Funds.

3. Release of Escrow Funds

3.1 The Escrow Funds shall be released in two tranches:

(a) 50% of the Escrow Funds shall be released to the Seller on the date that is twelve (12) months after the Completion Date, provided no valid indemnity claim is outstanding under Clause 8.1 of the Share Purchase Agreement; and

(b) the remaining 50% of the Escrow Funds shall be released to the Seller on the date that is twenty-four (24) months after the Completion Date, subject to the same condition.

3.2 In the event of a bona fide indemnity claim under the Share Purchase Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall release funds only upon receipt of a written joint instruction signed by both the Buyer and the Seller or a final, binding determination pursuant to Clause 12.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, on the second Business Day after posting.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Escrow Agent shall not disclose any information relating to the Escrow Funds, the Parties or the transactions contemplated hereby to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This escrow agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York.
