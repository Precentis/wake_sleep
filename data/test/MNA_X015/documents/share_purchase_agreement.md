SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 21 April 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Larkspur Technologies Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 11558638, registered office at 17 King William Street, London) (the “Seller”).

(2) Oakhurst Systems Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 11699268, registered office at 83 Queen Victoria Street, London) (the “Buyer”).

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Engineering Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 15006133, registered office at 96 Old Broad Street, London) (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

(C) The Buyer and the Seller intend that completion of the sale and purchase shall occur on the Closing Date.

OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “Closing Date” means 3 June 2027;

   “General Warranties” means the representations and warranties set out in Clause 8.2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

   “Fundamental Warranties” means the representations and warranties relating to the Seller’s title to the Shares, capacity to enter into this Agreement, and tax matters;

   “Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1;

   “Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares is $100,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), to be paid in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5.00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall deliver the Purchase Price by wire transfer to the Seller’s account specified in a written notice delivered at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The completion of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the “Closing”) shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Seller or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of the Shares;

   (b) share certificates representing the Shares, endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed instruments of transfer;

   (c) a certificate of good standing for the Company issued by Companies House within five (5) Business Days of the Closing Date;

   (d) such other documents as may be reasonably required by the Buyer to evidence title and transfer of the Shares.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket): Notwithstanding any other provision in this Agreement, the Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer in respect of any claim under the Warranties unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $1,000,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over $1,000,000.

10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for 36 months. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which such claim shall be time-barred and of no effect.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement (including non-contractual disputes or claims).

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email, to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as it may notify in writing to the other party.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

17.2 The obligations in Clause 17.1 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Clause.
