ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on 31 May 2027 (the "Effective Date") by and among:

(1) Larkspur Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11558638, whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Oakhurst Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11699268, whose registered office is at 83 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 31 May 2027 (the "SPA") for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15006133, whose registered office is at 96 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the SPA, a portion of the purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA;

(C) The Parties and the Escrow Agent now wish to set out the terms upon which the Escrow Amount shall be held, administered and released.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 3 June 2027.

"Claim Notice" means written notice from the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, delivered in accordance with Clause 8, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and amount of any claim under the SPA.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £3,750,000 (three million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), credited to the escrow account held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

"Material Customer" means Penrith Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11755994, whose registered office is at 149 Cannon Street, London.

2. Establishment of Escrow

2.1 The Buyer shall transfer the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent within one Business Day of the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") and shall administer such funds strictly in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Investment of Funds

3.1 The Escrow Agent may, at its discretion, invest the Escrow Amount in short-term, highly liquid instruments denominated in sterling, provided such investments are limited to obligations of, or guaranteed by, the UK government or any UK-regulated clearing bank.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains undisputed, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent that a Claim Notice has been delivered to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to that date in respect of which the matter remains unresolved. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending claim shall remain in escrow until such claim is resolved by agreement of the Parties or final determination pursuant to the SPA, whereupon such portion shall be released to the Party entitled thereto.

5. Acknowledgement of Escrow Agent

5.1 The Escrow Agent acts as a stakeholder and disburser only and shall have no duty to inquire into the existence or validity of any Claim Notice or dispute between the Parties.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or legal process, or as reasonably necessary to perform its duties hereunder.

7. Resignation and Successor

7.1 The Escrow Agent may resign upon providing not less than 30 days’ prior written notice to the Parties, provided that a successor escrow agent is appointed by mutual agreement of the Parties.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

9. Entire Agreement

9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, with the seat of arbitration in New York, United States of America.
