1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this contract dated on or about 10 May 2027 between Penrith Systems Ltd and Holloway Engineering Ltd;

"Company" means Holloway Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15006133, whose registered office is at 96 Old Broad Street, London;

"Confidential Information" means all proprietary, commercial or technical information disclosed by either party, whether orally, visually or in writing, including but not limited to pricing, technical specifications, business practices, and customer data;

"Customer" means Penrith Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11755994, whose registered office is at 149 Cannon Street, London;

"Effective Date" means the date of execution of this Agreement;

"Services" means the engineering and systems integration services to be provided by the Company to the Customer as specified in Schedule A.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment and Scope

2.1 The Customer hereby engages the Company to provide the Services, and the Company accepts such engagement, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The detailed scope, deliverables and timelines for the Services shall be as set out in Schedule A, which forms an integral part of this Agreement.

3. Payment Terms

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees for the Services as specified in Schedule B, payable in arrears monthly upon receipt of a valid invoice.

3.2 All sums payable under this Agreement shall be exclusive of VAT, which shall be added at the prevailing rate if applicable.

3.3 Any undisputed invoice not paid within 30 days of receipt shall bear interest at the rate of 8% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank Plc, accruing on a daily basis.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as may be notified in writing.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed served if delivered personally, on the date of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, 48 hours after posting; or if sent by email, on the date of successful transmission with read receipt confirmation.

6. Term and Termination

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice specifying the breach.

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations and understandings, whether oral or written.

14.2 Either party may terminate this agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the avoidance of doubt, the counterparty is Penrith Systems Ltd.

14.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

14.4 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

14.5 Nothing in this Agreement shall create a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.
