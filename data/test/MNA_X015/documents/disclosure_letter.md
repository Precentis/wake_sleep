Dear Sirs,  

In connection with the share purchase agreement dated on or about 10 May 2027 (the “SPA”) between Larkspur Technologies Ltd (the “Seller”), Oakhurst Systems Ltd (the “Buyer”) and Holloway Engineering Ltd (the “Company”), under which the Buyer has agreed to acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller, this letter constitutes the disclosure by the Seller pursuant to Clause 7 of the SPA (Disclosure against Warranties).  

1. Definitions  
In this letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Buyer” means Oakhurst Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11699268, whose registered office is at 83 Queen Victoria Street, London.  
“Company” means Holloway Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15006133, whose registered office is at 96 Old Broad Street, London.  
“Seller” means Larkspur Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11558638, whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London.  
“SPA” means the share purchase agreement entered into between the Buyer, the Seller and the Company on or about 10 May 2027 relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
“Warranties” means the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 3 of the SPA.  

2. General Disclosure  
The Seller confirms that it has disclosed all matters, facts and circumstances which are required or permitted to be disclosed under the SPA in order to limit or extinguish liability under any of the Warranties. This disclosure is made on a full, fair and accurate basis and contains sufficient detail to enable the Buyer to understand the nature and implications of each disclosed matter.  

3. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)  
3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3, the Seller confirms that the list of material contracts set out in Schedule A to this letter is complete and accurate as at the date hereof.  
3.2 The Seller further confirms that no material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision.  
3.3 The material contracts include, but are not limited to, the contract dated 12 March 2022 between the Company and Penrith Systems Ltd (company number 11755994), whose registered office is at 149 Cannon Street, London, under which the Company provides engineering services for a term of 48 months, subject to automatic renewal unless terminated by either party on not less than 180 days’ notice.  

4. Payment Provisions  
All payments due under any contract disclosed herein shall continue to be made in accordance with their existing terms, and no amendment, waiver or variation has been agreed or is contemplated that would alter the payment obligations of any party, including the Company, as at the date of this letter.  

5. Notices  
Any notice to be given under or in connection with any disclosed contract may be served at the registered office of the relevant party as set out above or as otherwise notified in writing in accordance with the terms of the applicable agreement.  

6. Confidentiality  
All information contained in this disclosure letter, including the existence and terms of the disclosed contracts, is confidential and may not be disclosed to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party. This obligation of confidentiality shall survive the completion of the transaction contemplated under the SPA.  

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This disclosure letter, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.  

8. Entire Disclosure  
This letter, together with any schedules hereto, constitutes the entire disclosure by the Seller in relation to the Warranties under the SPA and supersedes any prior disclosures, representations or undertakings, whether written or oral.
