SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE  
DATED PRIOR TO 22 JUNE 2026  
between  
JUNIPER INDUSTRIES LTD (the Customer)  
and  
SALTASH SYSTEMS LTD (the Supplier)  

1. Interpretation  

1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services were accessible and operational as measured by the Supplier’s monitoring systems, excluding scheduled maintenance periods and Force Majeure events.  
“Business Hours” means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England.  
“Monthly Fee” means the sum of $40,000 (forty thousand US dollars), payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services.  
“Service Credit” means a credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice in the event that specified service levels are not met.  

1.2 Capitalised terms not otherwise defined in this Schedule shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 22 June 2026 between the parties.  

2. Service Availability  

2.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Availability of the Managed IT Services is not less than 99.5% in any calendar month.  

2.2 Availability shall be measured continuously each calendar month using the Supplier’s network monitoring tools, with data collected at five-minute intervals. Downtime is recorded when the core services are inaccessible or non-functional for more than ten consecutive minutes.  

2.3 Scheduled maintenance windows shall be limited to a maximum of eight (8) hours per calendar month and must be communicated to the Customer at least five (5) Business Days in advance. Downtime occurring during approved and notified maintenance windows shall not count toward Availability calculations.  

4. Service Credits for Failure to Meet Availability  

4.1 If the monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.2 If the monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.3 The Monthly Fee is $40,000.  
4.4 Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet the Availability targets set out in this Schedule and shall be applied automatically to the following month’s invoice unless otherwise agreed in writing.  
4.5 The aggregate liability of the Supplier under this Schedule for Service Credits in any calendar month shall not exceed 25% of the Monthly Fee.  

5. Payment  

5.1 The Monthly Fee of $40,000 shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.  
5.2 All payments shall be made in US dollars by bank transfer to the account specified on the Supplier’s invoice.  
5.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid in full.  

6. Notices  

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  
6.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: 159 Bishopsgate, London, and to the attention of the Head of Procurement, with a copy by email to procurement@juniperindustries.co.uk.  
6.3 Notices to the Supplier shall be sent to: 109 King William Street, London, and to the attention of the Service Delivery Manager, with a copy by email to slasupport@saltashsystems.co.uk.  

7. Confidentiality  

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party marked as confidential or which by its nature ought reasonably to be treated as confidential.  
7.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination of this Schedule for a period of three (3) years.  

8. General  

8.1 This Schedule is governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Schedule.  
8.3 This Schedule forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Juniper Industries Ltd and Saltash Systems Ltd.
