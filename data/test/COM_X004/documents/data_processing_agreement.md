**DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT**

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 15 April 2024, between:

(1) Juniper Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10828878 whose registered office is at 159 Bishopsgate, London ("Customer"); and

(2) Saltash Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11328176 whose registered office is at 109 King William Street, London ("Supplier").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- “Applicable Data Protection Law” means all applicable legislation relating to data protection and privacy, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the Data Protection Act 2018, and any successor legislation.
- “Customer Personal Data” means any personal data (as defined in the Applicable Data Protection Law) that is provided by or on behalf of the Customer to the Supplier, or otherwise processed by the Supplier on the Customer’s behalf in connection with the provision of the Services under the Master Services Agreement dated 12 March 2023.
- “Services” means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer under the Master Services Agreement.
- “Sub-processor” means any third party engaged by the Supplier to process Customer Personal Data.

**2. Subject Matter and Purpose**

2.1 This Agreement governs the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer in connection with the provision of the Services.

2.2 The nature and purpose of the processing are as set out in the Master Services Agreement, including system monitoring, data backup, incident response, and technical support.

**3. Term**

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date of signature and shall continue in force until termination of the Master Services Agreement or until otherwise terminated in accordance with its terms.

**4. Obligations of the Supplier**

4.1 The Supplier shall only process Customer Personal Data in accordance with the documented instructions of the Customer, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

4.2 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including measures for ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of processing systems and services.

4.3 The Supplier shall not engage any Sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Customer. Where such authorisation is granted, the Supplier shall ensure the Sub-processor is bound by equivalent data protection obligations.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Supplier shall ensure that any personnel who have access to Customer Personal Data are subject to a duty of confidentiality, whether under a contract or statutory obligation.

5.2 The Supplier shall not disclose Customer Personal Data to any third party except as necessary for the performance of the Services or as required by law.

**6. Compliance with Data Protection Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

**7. Audit Rights**

7.1 The Customer shall have the right, or shall procure that a third-party auditor has the right, to conduct audits or inspections of the Supplier’s data processing activities, including reviewing the Supplier’s security policies and procedures, up to once per calendar year, upon not less than 30 days’ prior written notice.

7.2 The Supplier shall provide reasonable assistance and cooperation in connection with any such audit.

**8. Data Subject Rights**

8.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction.

8.2 The Supplier shall promptly notify the Customer upon receiving any such request.

**9. Data Breach Notification**

9.1 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

9.2 The Supplier shall provide the Customer with all reasonable assistance and information necessary to enable the Customer to comply with its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law in respect of the breach.

**10. Return or Deletion of Data**

10.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Master Services Agreement, the Supplier shall, at the Customer’s written election, either securely delete or return all Customer Personal Data.

10.2 The Supplier shall certify in writing the completion of such deletion or return.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.

**12. Payment**

12.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier for any reasonable costs incurred in fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement, including audit assistance and data breach response, where such activities exceed standard service inclusions under the Master Services Agreement.

**13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
