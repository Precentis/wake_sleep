# Master Services Agreement – Extract

This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made on 15 April 2021 between:

(1) **Juniper Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10828878, whose registered office is at 159 Bishopsgate, London (“Customer”); and  
(2) **Saltash Systems Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11328176, whose registered office is at 109 King William Street, London (“Supplier”).

The Customer and the Supplier may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Confidential Information” means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, electronically, visually or in writing, including technical data, business practices, pricing, customer information, and service specifications.  
“Services” means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer as specified in the applicable Statement of Work.  
“Term” means the period during which this Agreement remains in force, as set out in Clause 3.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

---

**2. Provision of Services**

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services in accordance with the specifications, service levels, and timelines set out in the applicable Statement of Work.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with due care, skill, and diligence, in compliance with generally accepted industry standards.

---

**3. Term**

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

3.2 Following the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

---

**4. Fees and Payment**

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services as set out in the applicable Statement of Work (“Fees”).

4.2 Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears and are payable within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice. Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per month above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing daily.

4.3 All Fees are exclusive of taxes, which shall be borne by the Customer.

---

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each Party shall hold the other’s Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party, except as required by law.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

---

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class registered post, or email (with confirmation of receipt) to the registered office of the recipient Party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if sent by post, on the second business day after posting; if sent by email, on the same day if sent during business hours, otherwise the next business day.

---

**11. Limitation of Liability**

11.1 Each Party’s aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall be limited to **$500,000**.

11.2 The limitation in Clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability arising from: (a) death or personal injury caused by the negligence of a Party; or (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

---

**19. Assignment**

19.1 Neither Party may assign, transfer, charge, subcontract or otherwise dispose of this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party.

19.2 A change of control of a Party, including but not limited to an acquisition of more than 50% of its voting shares or a merger involving such Party, shall be deemed an assignment for the purposes of this Clause 19 and shall require the prior written consent of the other Party.

---

**20. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

20.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

20.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
