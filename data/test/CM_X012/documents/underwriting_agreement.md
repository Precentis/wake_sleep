UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 June 2027 between:

(1) Ingleby Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12377809, whose registered office is at 97 Cannon Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Fenwick Medical Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12980422, whose registered office is at 15 Moorgate, London (the "Underwriter").

Each of the Company and the Underwriter may be referred to in this Agreement as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS the Company proposes to undertake an initial public offering (the "Offering") of its ordinary shares (the "Shares") which shall be underwritten by the Underwriter on a firm commitment basis.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London;

"Closing Date" means the date specified in the final pricing agreement for settlement of the purchase and sale of the Shares;

"EEA" means the European Economic Area;

"Pricing Date" means 5 July 2027;

"Shares" means the ordinary shares of the Company offered in the Offering.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter to act as sole underwriter in connection with the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to underwrite the Shares on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

3. Subscription and Payment

On the Closing Date, the Underwriter shall subscribe for and pay for the Shares in cleared funds by wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Company. Payment shall be made against delivery of the Shares in book-entry form through the clearing systems of Euroclear and Clearstream, as applicable.

4. Representations and Warranties

Each Party represents and warrants to the other that, as of the date hereof and as of the Pricing Date:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement;

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate action; and

(d) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

5. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may designate in writing. Notices shall be deemed received when delivered, if delivered personally; two Business Days after posting, if sent by post; or when confirmed by automated email receipt, if sent by email.

6. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with the Offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation or stock exchange rule, or is already in the public domain through no fault of the receiving Party.

7. Comfort Letter

(a) The Company shall cause its auditors to issue a comfort letter in form and substance reasonably acceptable to the Underwriter.

(b) The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the pricing date. The pricing date is 5 July 2027.

8. Expenses

Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with the negotiation, preparation and execution of this Agreement.

9. Offers to Investors

The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
