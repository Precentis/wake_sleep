1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Ingleby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12377809 and registered office at 97 Cannon Street, London;

“Underwriter” means Fenwick Medical Securities, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12980422 and registered office at 15 Moorgate, London;

“Dunmore Holdings Ltd” means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11704520 and registered office at 66 Ludgate Hill, London;

“Prospectus” means this offering document dated 5 July 2027, including all appendices, schedules and amendments thereto;

“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of nominal value £0.10 each in the share capital of the Company being offered pursuant to this Prospectus;

“Offering” means the initial public offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company underwritten by the Underwriter.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. References to clauses are to clauses of this Prospectus unless otherwise stated.

2. Offering Terms

2.1 The Company is offering 15,000,000 Ordinary Shares to the public for the first time, representing 30% of its enlarged issued share capital upon completion of the Offering.

2.2 The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on a firm commitment basis, subject to the terms set out in the underwriting agreement dated on or about the date of this Prospectus.

2.3 The price per Ordinary Share will be determined during the bookbuilding process and will be announced in due course in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

3. Use of Proceeds

3.1 The net proceeds from the Offering will be used by the Company for the following purposes:

(a) 55% for the development and expansion of renewable energy infrastructure;

(b) 25% for debt reduction;

(c) 20% for general corporate purposes, including working capital and research and development.

3.2 The Company reserves the right to reallocate the use of proceeds in a manner consistent with its business strategy, provided such reallocation is disclosed to the Financial Conduct Authority and made public promptly.

4. Related Party Transactions

4.1 The Company confirms that, as at the date of this Prospectus, there are no related party transactions other than directors’ service agreements entered into in the ordinary course of business and on usual commercial terms.

4.2 The directors’ service agreements have been disclosed in the Directors’ Report included in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2026.

5. Payment and Settlement

5.1 Payment for the Ordinary Shares subscribed for under the Offering must be made in full by electronic transfer in cleared funds to the account designated by the Company or its agents prior to the settlement date.

5.2 Settlement of the Ordinary Shares will occur on such date as notified by the Company, which shall not be later than ten (10) business days after the closing of the subscription period.
