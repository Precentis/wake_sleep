2. Lock-Up Undertaking

2.1 During the period commencing on the date of the Prospectus and ending 180 days thereafter (the “Lock-Up Period”), the Shareholder shall not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, directly or indirectly:

(a) offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, assign, grant any option to purchase, make any short sale or otherwise transfer or dispose of any Shares; or

(b) enter into any swap, hedge or other arrangement that transfers to another, in whole or in part, any economic consequence of ownership of any Shares.

2.2 The restrictions set out in Clause 2.1 shall apply to all Shares beneficially owned by the Shareholder as of the date of the Prospectus and any Shares thereafter acquired, whether by purchase, gift, dividend or otherwise.
