# Minutes of the Board Meeting of Ingleby Energy plc

**Date:** 10 June 2027  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 97 Cannon Street, London, EC4N 5BT  
**Chairperson:** Mr. Julian Hartley, Chairman  

**Present:**  
- Mr. Julian Hartley (Chairman)  
- Ms. Priya Nair (Non-Executive Director)  
- Mr. Marcus Daley (Chief Executive Officer)  
- Ms. Leila Bhattacharya (Director)  
- Mr. Thomas Reed (Company Secretary)  

**1. Call to Order**  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m.

**2. Confirmation of Agenda**  
The agenda was confirmed as circulated. No additions or deletions were proposed.

**3. Approval of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 12 May 2027 were reviewed and approved as an accurate record.

**4. CEO Report on IPO Progress**  
The Chief Executive Officer reported that discussions with Fenwick Medical Securities, as the proposed Underwriter, are progressing in accordance with the timeline for the initial public offering of ordinary shares. All due diligence materials have been provided, and the draft prospectus is under final review. The offering is expected to launch in early July 2027, subject to regulatory clearance.

**5. Financial Update**  
The Company Secretary presented unaudited financial statements for the quarter ending 31 March 2027. The board noted revenue growth of 14.3% compared to the same period in the prior year. Operating expenses remain within forecast, and cash reserves are sufficient to support pre-IPO obligations.

**6. Material Customer Agreement**  
The board reviewed the existing supply agreement with Dunmore Holdings Ltd, a significant customer. The agreement, originally executed on 1 March 2023, remains in full force and governs the supply of energy services over a five-year term. No amendments were proposed at this time. The board affirmed the strategic importance of the relationship to the Company’s revenue stability.

**7. Approval of Director Loan**  
The board considered a request from Ms. Leila Bhattacharya, a director of the Company, for financial assistance. After full deliberation and in accordance with the Company’s internal lending policy, the board resolved to approve an interest-free loan in the amount of $2,500,000 from the Company to Ms. Leila Bhattacharya. The loan shall be repayable in full no later than 30 June 2030. No interest shall accrue on the principal sum at any time. Security for the loan shall not be required. The Company Secretary shall prepare the loan agreement for execution, consistent with English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**8. Conflict of Interest Disclosure**  
Ms. Leila Bhattacharya declared a personal interest in Agenda Item 7 and withdrew from the meeting during discussion and voting. The remaining directors confirmed that the transaction was fair and in the Company’s best interests, having considered alternative financing options and potential impacts on liquidity.

**9. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.

**10. Adjournment**  
The meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
