2. Purpose of Escrow  

2.1 The parties acknowledge that the Buyer is acquiring the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated 25 September 2026 (the “SPA”).  

2.2 The Escrow Amount is held by the Escrow Agent as security for potential indemnification claims under the SPA.  

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount  

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent into an interest-bearing account designated by the Escrow Agent no later than two Business Days following the date of this Agreement.  

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not release any portion of it except as expressly authorised herein.  

3.3 All interest accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be subject to the same terms and conditions.  

4. Release of Escrow Amount  

4.1 On the date which is 18 months from the date of this Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall release 50% of the Escrow Amount to the Seller, provided no Claim Notice is outstanding.  

4.2 The remaining 50% of the Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller 27 months from the date of this Agreement, unless a Claim Notice is validly delivered and remains unresolved.  

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount  

5.1 A claim against the Escrow Amount shall be made by delivery of a Claim Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, with a copy to the Buyer, specifying in reasonable detail the nature, basis and quantum of the claim.  

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.  

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall notify the Seller promptly and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount that is the subject of such claim until the claim is settled, withdrawn or finally determined.  

6. Notices  

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post or email to the addresses specified in Clause 1.  

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if sent by post, two Business Days after posting; if by email, on the day of transmission, provided a confirmation of receipt is received.  

7. Confidentiality  

7.1 Each Relevant Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from any other Relevant Party, and shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under this Clause.  

7.2 “Confidential Information” means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Recipient, whether in oral, written or electronic form, relating to the transactions contemplated by the SPA or this Agreement.  

7.3 A Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided it gives prior notice to the Disclosing Party where practicable.  

8. General  

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  

8.2 The courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.  

8.3 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
