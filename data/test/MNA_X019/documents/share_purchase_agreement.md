SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 19 September 2026 and made between:

(1) Dunmore Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11741935 whose registered office is at 116 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Ashcombe Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11287524 whose registered office is at 98 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

The Seller and the Buyer are together referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Calloway Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12258118 whose registered office is at 35 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Basket" means the threshold amount of $2,100,000 (1.0% of the Purchase Price) set out in Clause 9.4;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 1 November 2026;

   "Confidential Information" means all non-public information concerning the Company, the Buyer, the Seller or their respective businesses, assets or financial affairs;

   "excess" means the amount by which the aggregate of all valid claims exceeds the Basket;

   "Lowther Engineering Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13330624 whose registered office is at 7 Gresham Street, London;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as specified in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $210,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

3. Payment and Completion

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Completion shall take place remotely via exchange of electronic signatures and documents at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify to the other in accordance with this Clause.

4.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been received at the time it would have been delivered in the ordinary course of post or email transmission.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not at any time disclose to any person any Confidential Information without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law or a regulatory authority.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three years.

9. Warranties and Indemnities

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket): The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $2,100,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price, the "Basket"), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over the Basket.

9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any claim for breach of warranty within 30 days of becoming aware of the facts giving rise to such claim, failing which the claim shall be time-barred. For the avoidance of doubt, any claim notified under this Clause 9.7 must relate to a loss that forms part of the excess over the Basket in order to give rise to liability.

10. General

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
