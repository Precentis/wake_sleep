Service Level Schedule  
to the Master Services Agreement  
between Lowther Industries Ltd and Juniper Industries Ltd  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Customer” means Lowther Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12531984, with its registered office at 57 Bishopsgate, London.  
(b) “Supplier” means Juniper Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12132385, with its registered office at 35 Fleet Street, London.  
(c) “Agreement” means the master services agreement entered into between the Customer and the Supplier, together with this Service Level Schedule.  
(d) “Monthly Fee” means the sum of $65,000, payable by the Customer to the Supplier in respect of each calendar month for the provision of managed IT services.  
(e) “Service Credit” means a credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice as compensation for failure by the Supplier to meet agreed service levels.  
(f) “Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the managed IT services are accessible and operational, excluding scheduled maintenance and circumstances beyond the Supplier’s reasonable control.  

2. Service Availability  
2.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Availability of the managed IT services is not less than 99.5% in any calendar month.  
2.2 Scheduled maintenance may be conducted during pre-notified maintenance windows, not exceeding four (4) hours in total per calendar month, provided that the Supplier gives the Customer at least seventy-two (72) hours’ prior written notice.  

3. Performance Measurement  
3.1 Availability shall be measured by the Supplier using automated monitoring tools approved by the Customer.  
3.2 Reports detailing the measured Availability for each calendar month shall be delivered to the Customer within ten (10) Business Days of the end of the relevant calendar month.  

4. Service Credits  
4.1 If the monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.2 If the monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.3 Service Credits shall not exceed in aggregate 25% of the Monthly Fee for any single calendar month.  
4.4 Service Credits shall be applied as a deduction against the Customer’s next due invoice for services under the Agreement.  
4.5 The remedies set out in this Clause 4 are the Customer’s sole and exclusive remedies for failure to meet the Availability targets.  

5. Payment  
5.1 The Monthly Fee of $65,000 shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and shall be due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.  
5.2 All payments shall be made in US Dollars to a bank account nominated in writing by the Supplier.  
5.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month, calculated on a daily basis from the due date until the date of full payment.  

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice to be given under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  
6.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: 57 Bishopsgate, London, and to the attention of the Chief Information Officer.  
6.3 Notices to the Supplier shall be sent to: 35 Fleet Street, London, and to the attention of the Head of Client Services.  

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or circumstances of disclosure.  
7.2 The obligations under this Clause 7 shall not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.  

8. General  
8.1 This Schedule is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Schedule.  
8.3 No variation of this Schedule shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.
