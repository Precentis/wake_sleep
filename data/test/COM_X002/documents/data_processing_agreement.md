DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 3 March 2023 by and between:

(1) Lowther Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12531984, whose registered office is at 57 Bishopsgate, London ("Customer"); and

(2) Juniper Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12132385, whose registered office is at 35 Fleet Street, London ("Supplier").

The Customer and the Supplier are each referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS the Parties are party to a Master Services Agreement dated 15 May 2021 (the "MSA"), under which the Supplier provides managed IT services to the Customer;

WHEREAS in the course of providing the Services, the Supplier processes personal data on behalf of the Customer;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means (a) Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation), (b) the Data Protection Act 2018, (c) any applicable implementing or supplementary legislation in force from time to time in the United Kingdom, and (d) to the extent applicable, the EU Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive) and any replacement or re-enactment thereof.

"Customer Personal Data" means any personal data relating to the Customer’s employees, contractors, or service users that is disclosed by the Customer to the Supplier or that the Supplier processes on the Customer’s behalf in the course of providing the Services.

"Services" has the meaning given in the MSA.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings given in the MSA.

2. Scope and Purpose

2.1 This Agreement applies solely to the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier in connection with the provision of the Services.

2.2 The Supplier shall only process Customer Personal Data for the purposes and on the instructions of the Customer, as documented in the MSA and this Agreement.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 The Supplier shall ensure that any person it authorises to process Customer Personal Data is subject to obligations of confidentiality.

3.2 The Supplier shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 The Supplier shall remain fully liable for all acts and omissions of any sub-processor.

5. Data Subject Rights

5.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability.

5.2 The Supplier shall respond to any such request directed to it without delay and shall immediately inform the Customer.

6. Compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. Audits and Information

7.1 Upon reasonable notice, the Supplier shall permit the Customer, or a third party appointed by the Customer, to conduct audits or inspections to verify the Supplier’s compliance with this Agreement.

7.2 The Supplier shall provide all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with its obligations under this Agreement.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

9. Notices

9.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective Party.

10. Term and Termination

10.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue in force until termination of the MSA.

10.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of notice.

11. Miscellaneous

11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements and understandings.

11.2 This Agreement is governed by English law and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under it.
