1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Affiliate” means, in relation to a party, a body corporate which is from time to time directly or indirectly controlled by, under common control with, or controlling that party;

“Business Day” means a day on which banks are open for general business in London, being any day from Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;

“Confidential Information” means all information of a proprietary or confidential nature disclosed by either party to the other, whether orally, visually, in writing or by any other means, including technical data, business practices, pricing, customer details and operational methods;

“Effective Date” means the date on which the last party executes this Agreement;

“Services” means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as set out in Schedule A and any associated service schedules or work orders issued under this Agreement.

2. Provision of Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and the specifications set out in the applicable schedules.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in compliance with generally accepted industry standards, and in a timely and professional manner.

2.3 The Customer shall cooperate with the Supplier as reasonably required to enable the proper and timely delivery of the Services, including by providing access to systems, personnel and documentation as necessary.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 21 August 2026 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by giving written notice to the other if the other party:

(a) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation, administration or any form of composition with creditors;

(b) materially breaches any term of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of receipt of written notice specifying the breach.

4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule B (Pricing and Payment Terms), payable in advance on a monthly basis unless otherwise specified.

4.2 All fees are exclusive of VAT and any applicable taxes, which shall be payable by the Customer at the prevailing rate.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount, accruing from the due date until payment is made in full.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except:

(a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

(b) to its employees, advisors or Affiliates on a need-to-know basis, provided they are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

6. Notices
