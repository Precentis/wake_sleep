SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 3 January 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Orwell Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12861730, whose registered office is at 133 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Ingleby Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15136362, whose registered office is at 106 Bishopsgate, London (the "Buyer");

and

(3) Kingsmere Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10013230, whose registered office is at 140 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 4;

"Closing Date" means 7 April 2027;

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies set out in the Company’s audited financial statements for the financial year ended 31 December 2026 ("FY2026");

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties given by the Seller in Clauses 7.1 (Title), 7.2 (Capacity and Authority) and 7.6 (Tax);

"General Warranties" means the warranties given by the Seller in Clauses 7.3 to 7.5 and 7.7 to 7.10, inclusive;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the entire issued share capital of the Company, as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $45,000,000, to be satisfied in cash on the Closing Date, subject to adjustment in accordance with Clause 2.3.

3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional consideration (the "Earn-out Payment") calculated as two times (2x) the amount by which the FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $5,000,000 in aggregate. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within 60 days of the final audited FY2026 financial statements being approved by the Buyer’s board, provided such approval is not unreasonably withheld or delayed.

4. CLOSING

4.1 The Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and payments on the Closing Date.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the General Warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $5,625,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $45,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1. This limitation shall not apply to claims arising under the Fundamental Warranties, which shall have no monetary cap.

10. SURVIVAL AND NOTIFICATION OF CLAIMS

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months. Any claim for breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which the right to claim shall be extinguished.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the terms of this Agreement, the Company, or the transactions contemplated herein.

12. NOTICES

12.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
