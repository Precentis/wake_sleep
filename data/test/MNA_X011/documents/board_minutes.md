**5. Legal Correspondence – Yarrow Analytics Ltd**  
The board noted a letter before action dated 18 January 2027 from Yarrow Analytics Ltd, addressed to Kingsmere Media Ltd. The letter asserts claims in connection with alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement entered into on 12 June 2025. Yarrow Analytics Ltd is seeking approximately $450,000 in damages. The board acknowledged the seriousness of the matter and instructed management to monitor the position closely, including maintaining records of all communications and preserving relevant documentation. Legal counsel has been engaged to assess the merits of the claim and to prepare a formal response in accordance with English procedural rules. The board resolved that no admission of liability shall be made without prior board approval.  

**6. Update on Proposed Acquisition by Ingleby Analytics Ltd**  
The board received a report on the status of the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Kingsmere Media Ltd by Ingleby Analytics Ltd (Buyer), a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15136362, with registered office at 106 Bishopsgate, London. The Seller in the transaction is Orwell Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12861730, with registered office at 133 Old Broad Street, London.  

The draft Share Purchase Agreement has been circulated to the board for review. Due diligence inquiries are ongoing, with particular focus on intellectual property assignments and customer contracts.  

**7. Material Customer Relationship – Saltash Pharma plc**  
The board reviewed the status of the commercial relationship with Saltash Pharma plc, a material customer. Saltash Pharma plc is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14512766, with registered office at 52 Cannon Street, London.  

Revenue derived from Saltash Pharma plc in the 12-month period ending 31 December 2026 accounted for 38% of total group turnover. The current contract with Saltash Pharma plc is due for renewal on 30 June 2027. Management has initiated discussions regarding terms for renewal, including service scope and pricing adjustments.
