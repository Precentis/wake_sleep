**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT – EXTRACT**

This Employment Agreement ("the Agreement") is made on 5 July 2024 between:

(1) **Juniper Medical Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 12569142, registered office at 26 Ludgate Hill, London) (the "Employer"); and  
(2) **Eleanor M. Hart** (the "Employee").

The parties agree as follows:

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Affiliate" means, in relation to any person, a person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with, that person;  
(b) "Business" means the business of developing, manufacturing and supplying medical diagnostic devices, as conducted by the Employer or any of its Subsidiaries at any time during the Employee’s employment;  
(c) "Control" (and its derivatives) means the legal or beneficial ownership of more than 50 per cent of the issued share capital or voting rights of a company, or the right to appoint or remove a majority of its board of directors;  
(d) "Group" means the Employer and its Affiliates, including Oakhurst Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13641194, registered office 176 King William Street, London);  
(e) "Material Customer" means any customer of the Group with whom the Employee had material contact during the 12 months prior to termination of employment and which, during that period, accounted for at least 5 per cent of the Group’s annual revenue;  
(f) "Subsidiary" has the meaning given in section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006.

**2. Commencement and Term**

2.1 The Employee’s employment shall commence on 1 September 2024 and shall continue until terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

**3. Position and Duties**

3.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Scientific Officer and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer.  
3.2 The Employee shall perform the duties and responsibilities reasonably assigned to her from time to time by the Employer, commensurate with her role.

**4. Remuneration**

4.1 The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £110,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
4.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme operated by the Group, at the sole discretion of the Board of Directors of the Employer.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Employee shall not at any time during or after the termination of her employment, whether directly or indirectly, disclose or use for any purpose any Confidential Information (as defined below) except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as authorised in writing by the Employer.  
5.2 "Confidential Information" includes all non-public information relating to the business, operations, clients (including Upton Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15505680, registered office 87 Old Broad Street, London), suppliers, technology or finances of the Employer or any member of the Group.

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the Employer or to the Employee’s last known address.  
6.2 Notices to the Employer shall be addressed to the Company Secretary at 26 Ludgate Hill, London.

**18. Non-Compete Obligation**

18.1 During the period of 12 months immediately following the termination of the Employee’s employment for any reason, the Employee shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with others, engage or be concerned in any capacity in any business which competes with the Business anywhere in the United Kingdom.  
18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this clause shall apply regardless of whether the termination is with or without cause, and irrespective of who initiates the termination.  
18.3 The Employee acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable in scope, duration and geography to protect the legitimate business interests of the Employer and the Group.

**19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
