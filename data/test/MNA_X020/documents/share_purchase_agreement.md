**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 7 September 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Dunmore Technologies Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13522103, whose registered office is at 87 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) **Oakhurst Holdings Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13641194, whose registered office is at 176 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Juniper Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12569142, whose registered office is at 26 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"**Agreement**" means this share purchase agreement, including the Recitals and any schedules or annexes;

"**Buyer**" has the meaning given in the preamble;

"**Closing**" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares;

"**Closing Date**" means 9 December 2026;

"**Company**" has the meaning given in the Recitals;

"**Conditions**" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

"**Fundamental Warranties**" means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax;

"**General Warranties**" means all warranties in Schedule 1 other than the Fundamental Warranties;

"**Purchase Price**" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

"**Seller**" has the meaning given in the preamble;

"**Shares**" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Clause 1 have the meanings given in the Recitals or elsewhere in this Agreement.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$135,000,000** (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

**3. PAYMENT**

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in full, in cleared funds, on the Closing Date, by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall have notified to the Buyer in accordance with Clause 11.

**4. CONDITIONS**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or valid waiver of each of the Conditions.

4.2 The Conditions include, but are not limited to: (a) the absence of any law or order restraining or prohibiting completion; (b) the accuracy, as of Closing, of the representations and warranties of each party, subject to materiality thresholds; and (c) the delivery of all documents required under this Agreement.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 17 February 2027), then either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

**10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months** from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months** from the Closing Date. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the party giving the warranty before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**11. NOTICES**

11.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the address or email of the relevant party set out in the preamble or as otherwise notified.

11.2 A notice is deemed to be received at the time of delivery if delivered personally, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or 1 hour after transmission if sent by email, unless sent after 6:00 p.m. local time, in which case it is deemed received at 9:00 a.m. on the next Business Day.

**12. POST-COMPLETION COVENANTS**

12.1 The Seller covenants that it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, concerned with or interested in any business which competes with the business of the Company in England and Wales for a period of **4 years** after the Closing Date.

**13. CONFIDENTIALITY**

13.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

**14. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

14.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

14.2 The courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
