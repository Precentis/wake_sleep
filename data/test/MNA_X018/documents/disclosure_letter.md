**2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**

The Seller discloses pursuant to clause 9.1 of the Agreement that:

2.1 The Company’s core platform software, known internally as “Project Helix”, was developed in 2023 by Dunmore Media Ltd, an independent contractor engaged by the Company on a consultancy basis.

2.2 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Dunmore Media Ltd.

2.3 The engagement of Dunmore Media Ltd was conducted on a verbal basis, with payment made via invoice in December 2023. No written contract, licence, or transfer documentation exists evidencing the transfer or assignment of ownership of any intellectual property rights in the software to the Company.

2.4 The Company currently asserts ownership of the source code and associated documentation for the core platform software and has incorporated such software into its commercial operations without challenge to date.

2.5 The Seller acknowledges that this absence of written assignment may affect the enforceability of the Company’s rights in the software under English law, particularly in the event of a claim by Dunmore Media Ltd or any third party asserting residual rights.

2.6 The Buyer is hereby notified that reliance on Warranty 8.2 in relation to the ownership, title, and enforceability of the intellectual property in the core platform software is subject to this full and specific disclosure.
