1. MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF UPTON CAPITAL LLP  
   (Company Number: 14777323)  
   Held at the registered office: 132 Moorgate, London  
   On 10 October 2026 at 10:00 a.m.

2. Attendance  
   2.1. Present:  
        (a) Mr. Julian Hartwell (Director)  
        (b) Ms. Elena M. Price (Director)  
   2.2. Absent:  
        (a) Mr. Thomas Wrenn  
        (b) Ms. Sarah Lin  
        (c) Mr. Daniel Cade  
   2.3. The Chair confirmed that 2 directors were present and that the meeting was quorate in accordance with the special quorum provisions previously adopted by the Board.

3. Agenda Item 1: Confirmation of Preamble and Attendance  
   3.1. The Chair confirmed the accuracy of the attendance list and reiterated that 2 directors constituted a quorate meeting under the resolution dated 3 January 2025, which reduced the quorum to 2 directors for all matters relating to the proposed sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
   3.2. RESOLVED unanimously that the minutes of the previous board meeting held on 18 September 2026 be and are hereby approved as a true record.

4. Agenda Item 2: Review of Proposed Share Sale Transaction  
   4.1. The Chair presented a summary of the proposed transaction whereby Hartwell Engineering Ltd (Seller), the sole shareholder of Upton Capital LLP (Company), would sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to Hartwell Medical Ltd (Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales (company number 12234276), registered office at 143 Ludgate Hill, London.  
   4.2. Counsel had confirmed that the proposed Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) was drafted under English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  
   4.3. The Board reviewed the key commercial terms, including:  
        (a) The Buyer’s intention to integrate the Company’s operations into its existing medical technology division.  
        (b) The existing contract with Orwell Analytics Ltd (company number 15600374), a material customer, which remains in full force and effect until 30 June 2027.  
        (c) No material amendments to the Company’s financial covenants were expected post-completion.

5. Agenda Item 3: Directors’ Considerations and Fiduciary Duties  
   5.1. The Board acknowledged its fiduciary duties under the Companies Act 2006, particularly in relation to the interests of the Company’s members and employees.  
   5.2. The Directors considered the implications of the transaction on staff, ongoing contracts, and the Company’s strategic direction.  
   5.3. Independent legal advice had been obtained confirming that the terms of the SPA were fair and reasonable from the perspective of the Company.

6. Agenda Item 4: Resolution on Transaction Approval  
   6.1. After discussion, it was  
        RESOLVED that the Board of Upton Capital LLP hereby approves the entry by Hartwell Engineering Ltd into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 14 October 2026 for the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company to Hartwell Medical Ltd, and further authorises the Company to provide all necessary cooperation and documentation required under the terms of the SPA, noting that the meeting was attended by 2 directors and was quorate.

7. Adjournment  
   7.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
