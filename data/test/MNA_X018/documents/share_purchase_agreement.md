**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 26 September 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Hartwell Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12081060, whose registered office is at 56 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Seller**”);

(2) **Hartwell Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12234276, whose registered office is at 143 Ludgate Hill, London (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Upton Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14777323, whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer has agreed to acquire and the Seller has agreed to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including the Recitals and Schedules;

“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“**Closing Date**” means 30 November 2026;

“**General Warranties**” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2 (Warranties), excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

“**Fundamental Warranties**” means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as set out in Part A of Schedule 2 (Warranties);

“**Purchase Price**” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of **$255,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in cash on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 3.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver or procure the delivery of:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, in favour of the Buyer or its nominee;

(b) such consents and authorisations as are necessary to effect the transfer of the Shares;

(c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in such form as the Buyer may reasonably require.

3.2 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer in immediately available funds to such account as the Seller shall have notified to the Buyer in writing at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

9.7 The Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of any claim under a warranty within **30 days** of becoming aware of the facts giving rise to such claim.

**10. SURVIVAL AND NOTICE OF CLAIMS**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing until **18 months** after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing until **48 months** after the Closing Date. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the relevant survival period.

**11. CONFIDENTIALITY**

11.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it will not, and will procure that its representatives will not, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the other Party, the Company or the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

**12. NOTICES**

12.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter or sent by email to the address or email address set out in the preamble or such other address as a Party may notify in writing.

12.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to be served at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by first-class post, or upon receipt of a delivery confirmation if sent by email.

**13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
