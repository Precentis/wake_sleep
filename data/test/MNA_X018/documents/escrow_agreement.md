ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] October 2026 (the “Effective Date”)  

BETWEEN:  

(1) Hartwell Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12081060 whose registered office is at 56 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”);  

(2) Hartwell Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12234276 whose registered office is at 143 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”); and  

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm authorised and regulated under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, acting in its capacity as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).  

The parties agree as follows:  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   “Claim Notice” means a written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 10 specifying the nature of a claim, the basis for such claim, and the amount claimed from the Escrow Amount;  
   “Closing Date” means 14 October 2026;  
   “Confidential Information” means any non-public information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, including financial, operational, and commercial data;  
   “Disclosure Letter” means the disclosure letter dated on or about the Closing Date delivered by the Seller to the Buyer in connection with the Share Purchase Agreement;  
   “Escrow Amount” means an amount equal to 15% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement, being held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement;  
   “Material Adverse Effect” means any event, change or circumstance that, individually or in the aggregate, has had or would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of Upton Capital LLP, including any material loss of a customer that accounts for more than 20% of the Company’s revenue in any trailing twelve-month period;  
   “Orwell Analytics” means Orwell Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15600374 whose registered office is at 158 Bishopsgate, London;  
   “Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement dated on or about the Closing Date pursuant to which the Buyer agrees to acquire the entire issued share capital of Upton Capital LLP from the Seller;  
   “Upton Capital LLP” or the “Company” means Upton Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14777323 whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London.  

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.  

2. Escrow Arrangement  

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date.  
2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Account”) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  
2.3 The Escrow Amount may only be released in accordance with Clauses 5 and 6.  

3. Payment and Release  

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, or such portion as remains undisputed and unpaid, to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date (the “Release Date”).  
3.2 Any interest earned on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be released to the same recipient as the principal sum to which it relates.  

4. Confidentiality  

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other parties and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except:  
   (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent obligations of confidentiality; or  
   (b) as required by law or regulatory authority.  

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount  

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by delivering a Claim Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.  
5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.  
5.3 The Seller may, within 30 days of receipt of a Claim Notice, serve a written response disputing all or part of the claim.  
5.4 If the parties fail to resolve the dispute within 30 days of the Seller’s response, either party may refer the matter to the courts of England and Wales.  

6. Notices  

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.  

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  

7.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
