**ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF UPTON CAPITAL LLP**  
*(Extract)*

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) "the Company" means Upton Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14777323;  
   (b) "the Buyer" means Hartwell Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12234276;  
   (c) "the Seller" means Hartwell Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12081060;  
   (d) "the Material Customer" means Orwell Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15600374;  
   (e) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
   (f) "Shareholder" means any person for the time being entered in the register of members of the Company as the holder of one or more shares in the capital of the Company;  
   (g) any reference to a statute or statutory provision includes a reference to it as amended, re-enacted or extended from time to time and to any subordinate legislation made under it.

2. The Company’s registered office is situated at 132 Moorgate, London.

3. The Company shall have the unrestricted power to carry on or undertake any business, trade, profession or occupation, and to do all such other things as are calculated to advance the success of the Company’s business for the benefit of its members as a whole, subject always to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles.

4. The Company’s share capital shall consist of ordinary shares of £1.00 each. The rights attached to the ordinary shares include the right to receive dividends as declared from time to time, the right to vote at general meetings of the Company, and the right to a distribution of surplus assets upon winding-up.

5. Dividends may be declared by ordinary resolution of the Shareholders and shall be paid in accordance with the respective entitlements of the holders of shares in the Company. No dividend shall be paid unless it is properly authorised and funded from the Company’s distributable profits.

6. The Board shall have the authority to appoint such officers and employees as it considers necessary for the proper conduct of the Company’s business, and to determine their terms and conditions of service.

7. The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors. If within one hour from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other time and place as the directors may determine. At the adjourned meeting, two directors present shall constitute a quorum.

8. Any resolution in writing signed by all the directors for the time being entitled to receive notice of and to attend and vote at a meeting of the Board shall be as valid and effective as if it had been passed at a meeting of the Board duly convened and held.

9. Any notice required to be given to a Shareholder, director or officer of the Company under these Articles may be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by electronic means to the address or electronic contact point last notified to the Company. Notices sent by post shall be deemed to be served 48 hours after posting.

10. All information acquired by any director, officer or employee of the Company in the course of their duties, including financial data, customer relationships, and strategic plans, shall be treated as confidential both during and after the termination of their office or employment, unless disclosure is required by law or authorised in writing by the Board.

11. The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

12. The Company shall maintain full and accurate accounting records in accordance with the requirements of the Companies Act 2006. Such records shall be kept at the registered office or such other place as the Board may determine.

13. The financial year of the Company shall end on 31 December in each calendar year.

14. These Articles and any related matters shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with them.
