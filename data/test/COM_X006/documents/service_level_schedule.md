**SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE**  
This Service Level Schedule ("Schedule") forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 20 November 2026 (the "Agreement") between Redcliffe Technologies Ltd ("Customer") and Eskdale Robotics Ltd ("Supplier"). Capitalised terms used but not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.

**1. Definitions**  
1.1. "Availability" means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are accessible and operational to authorised users at the Customer’s primary site, excluding Scheduled Maintenance Windows and Force Majeure Events.  
1.2. "Business Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England.  
1.3. "Scheduled Maintenance Window" means pre-approved periods of system unavailability notified by the Supplier to the Customer at least 72 hours in advance, not exceeding four (4) hours in any calendar month.  
1.4. "Service Credit" means a financial credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice as specified in Paragraph 4.  
1.5. "Monthly Fee" means the fixed fee payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services, amounting to $120,000 per calendar month.

**2. Service Availability Commitment**  
2.1. The Supplier undertakes that the Availability of the Managed IT Services shall be at least 99.5% in each calendar month.  
2.2. Availability shall be measured by automated monitoring systems operated by the Supplier and verified through bi-weekly reports submitted to the Customer.  
2.3. The Customer may request an independent audit of Availability data once per calendar quarter upon no less than ten (10) Business Days’ written notice.

**3. Reporting and Monitoring**  
3.1. The Supplier shall deliver a Service Performance Report to the Customer by the fifth (5th) Business Day of each month, detailing Availability, incident response times, and resolution rates for the prior calendar month.  
3.2. Reports shall include a breakdown of all service interruptions, including start time, duration, root cause classification, and remedial actions taken.  
3.3. All data used in reporting shall be retained by the Supplier for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of generation.

**4. Service Credits**  
4.1. If the measured Availability for any calendar month is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee.  
4.2. If the measured Availability for any calendar month is below 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee.  
4.3. Service Credits shall be calculated and applied automatically unless disputed in writing by the Customer within ten (10) Business Days of receipt of the Service Performance Report.  
4.4. The total aggregate liability of the Supplier under this Paragraph 4 in any calendar quarter shall not exceed 50% of the total Monthly Fees payable for that quarter.

**5. Payment**  
5.1. The Monthly Fee of $120,000 shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and shall be payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.  
5.2. Invoices shall include any applicable Service Credits from the prior month, clearly itemised.  
5.3. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by English law, whichever is lower.

**6. Notices**  
6.1. All notices under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by recorded delivery, or transmitted by email to the registered office or nominated representative of the relevant party.  
6.2. Notices to the Customer shall be addressed to:  
Redcliffe Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13541816, registered office 163 King William Street, London.  
6.3. Notices to the Supplier shall be addressed to:  
Eskdale Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15069411, registered office 148 King William Street, London.

**7. Confidentiality**  
7.1. Each party shall treat all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Services as confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with prior written consent.  
7.2. This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**8. General**  
8.1. This Schedule is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  
8.2. No variation of this Schedule shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.
