1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day on which banks are open for general business in London, being Monday to Friday inclusive, excluding public holidays in England;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a party (the "Disclosing Party") disclosed to or accessed by the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement, whether in writing, orally, electronically or otherwise, including but not limited to technical, commercial, operational and financial information, and whether or not marked as confidential;

"Commencement Date" means the date on which this Agreement is executed by both parties;

"Services" means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule A (Scope of Services).

2. Agreement for Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and the specifications set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable care and skill and in accordance with generally accepted industry standards.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 7 May 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by notice to the other if the other commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not capable of remedy, or if it becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or has a receiver appointed.

4. Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule B (Fees and Charges) within 30 days of the date of a valid invoice issued by the Supplier.

4.2 All fees are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis and compounding quarterly.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party undertakes to the other not to disclose any Confidential Information of the other party to any third party, nor to use such Confidential Information for any purpose other than in connection with the performance of this Agreement.

5.2 The obligations under Clause 5.1 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available otherwise than through a breach of this Agreement, or which is lawfully received from a third party without restriction on use or disclosure.

5.3 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.
