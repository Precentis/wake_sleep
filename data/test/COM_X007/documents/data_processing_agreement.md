**DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT**

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 12 March 2024 ("Effective Date") between:

(1) Glenrock Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11140619, whose registered office is at 39 Fleet Street, London (the "Customer"); and

(2) Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10781802, whose registered office is at 50 Fleet Street, London (the "Supplier").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means (a) the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR); (b) the Data Protection Act 2018; and (c) any applicable national implementing or supplementary laws, as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

"Customer Personal Data" means any personal data of the Customer that is processed by the Supplier under or in connection with the Master Services Agreement, including but not limited to personal data relating to employees, contractors, clients and suppliers of the Customer, such as names, contact details, job titles and identification numbers.

"Processing" has the meaning given to it in the UK GDPR and includes any operation or set of operations performed upon personal data, whether or not by automated means.

"Special Category Data" means personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data, data concerning a person’s sex life or sexual orientation, or data concerning criminal convictions and offences.

**2. Subject Matter, Nature and Purpose of Processing**

2.1 The subject matter of this Agreement is the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier on behalf of the Customer in connection with the provision of managed IT services under the Master Services Agreement.

2.2 The nature and purpose of the processing is the operation, maintenance, monitoring, support and security of the Customer’s IT systems, networks and data, including the storage, access, backup, recovery and technical administration of Customer Personal Data.

2.3 The duration of the processing corresponds to the term of the Master Services Agreement, unless earlier terminated in accordance with its terms.

**3. Data Protection Obligations of the Supplier**

3.1 The Supplier shall act only on the documented instructions of the Customer in relation to the processing of Customer Personal Data, unless required to do otherwise by Applicable Data Protection Law, in which case the Supplier shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process the Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

**4. Security Measures**

4.1 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including, as appropriate, encryption, access controls and regular testing of security measures.

**5. Sub-processing**

5.1 The Supplier shall not engage any sub-processor without the prior written authorisation of the Customer. Authorised sub-processors as of the Effective Date include Vantage Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14522149, whose registered office is at 104 Fleet Street, London.

**6. Compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law**

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 The Supplier shall keep confidential all information obtained in connection with the processing of Customer Personal Data and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Customer.

**8. Payment**

8.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees for processing services as set out in the Master Services Agreement, which amount includes 87.5% of the total annual service charge allocated specifically to data processing activities.

**9. Notices**

9.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered to the registered office of the respective party as set out in the preamble.

**10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

10.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
