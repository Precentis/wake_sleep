MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made on the date set out in the Schedule and is between:

(1) Glenrock Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11140619, whose registered office is at 39 Fleet Street, London ("Customer"); and

(2) Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10781802, whose registered office is at 50 Fleet Street, London ("Supplier").

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England;  
"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including technical data, business processes, pricing, customer information and service specifications;  
"Effective Date" means the date upon which this Agreement is executed by both parties;  
"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as further described in the applicable Statement of Work.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Supply of Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any applicable Statement of Work.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable care and skill, in a timely manner, and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in force until the expiry date set out in Clause 3.2.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 26 August 2026 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by giving written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice specifying the breach.

11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Subject to Clause 11.2, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the sum of $1,000,000.

11.2 The cap in Clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability arising from: (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence; or (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

11.3 Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, neither party excludes or limits any liability for any implied terms, representations or warranties to the fullest extent permitted by law.

19. Assignment

19.1 Neither party may assign, transfer, charge or otherwise deal with all or any part of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

19.2 A change of control of a party, including but not limited to the acquisition of more than 50% of its shares or voting rights by any person or entity, is deemed an assignment and shall require the prior written consent of the other party.

20. Confidentiality

20.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not use such information except as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

20.2 The obligations under this Clause shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving party prior to disclosure; or (c) is independently developed by the receiving party without reference to the disclosing party’s Confidential Information.

20.3 These obligations shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

21. Notices

21.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and addressed to the recipient at its registered office or such other address as notified in writing.

21.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received: (a) if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; (b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two Business Days after posting; or (c) if sent by email, on the day of transmission if sent on a Business Day before 5:00 p.m., otherwise on the next Business Day.

22. General

22.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations and understandings.

22.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
