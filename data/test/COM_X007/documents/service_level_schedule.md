SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This Service Level Schedule ("Schedule") forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 14 April 2026 (the "Agreement") between Glenrock Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11140619, whose registered office is at 39 Fleet Street, London ("Customer"), and Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10781802, whose registered office is at 50 Fleet Street, London ("Supplier").

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Schedule, the following terms have the meanings set out below unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, a body corporate which is directly or indirectly controlled by, under common control with, or controlling that party.

"Agreement" has the meaning given in the preamble.

"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written, or in electronic form, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the services, including but not limited to technical data, business practices, pricing, and customer information.

"Monthly Fee" means the amount of $40,000 payable by Customer to Supplier each calendar month for the provision of managed IT services.

"Service Credits" means the financial credits payable by Supplier to Customer as set out in Clause 4.

"Service Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England.

2. Payment

2.1 The Customer shall pay the Monthly Fee in advance on the first day of each calendar month, by electronic transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Supplier.

2.2 No deductions or set-offs shall be made by the Customer from the Monthly Fee unless expressly permitted under the Agreement.

3. Notices

3.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Schedule shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the recipient.

3.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: 39 Fleet Street, London, or such other address or email as notified in writing.

3.3 Notices to the Supplier shall be sent to: 50 Fleet Street, London, or such other address or email as notified in writing.

3.4 A notice shall be deemed received on the next business day if sent by email during Service Hours, or on the second business day following posting if sent by first-class post.

4. Service Level Commitments – System Availability

4.1 The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the managed IT services are available 99.5% of each calendar month during Service Hours ("Monthly Availability").

4.2 If Monthly Availability falls below 99.5% but is at least 98.0%, the Customer shall be entitled to a Service Credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee.

4.3 If Monthly Availability falls below 98.0%, the Customer shall be entitled to a Service Credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee.

4.4 Service Credits shall be calculated and payable within thirty (30) days of the end of the relevant calendar month, upon written request by the Customer supported by documented performance reports.

4.5 The maximum aggregate Service Credits payable in any calendar month shall not exceed 25% of the Monthly Fee.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

6. General

6.1 This Schedule is governed by English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

6.2 This Schedule may only be amended by written agreement signed by both parties.

6.3 No failure or delay by a party in exercising any right under this Schedule shall operate as a waiver of that right.

6.4 The rights and remedies provided under this Schedule are cumulative and not exclusive of any rights or remedies provided by law.
