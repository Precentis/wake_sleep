SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 25 February 2027.

Parties:

(1) Dunmore Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10990318, whose registered office is at 44 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

(2) Lowther Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11462386, whose registered office is at 18 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

Background:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15205481, whose registered office is at 28 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 6 April 2027;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

"General Warranties" means the warranties given by the Seller in Schedule 1, clauses 1 to 8;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties given by the Seller in Schedule 1, clauses 9 (title), 10 (capacity), and 11 (tax);

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The Purchase Price is $30,000,000, which shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller by no later than 5.00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall procure that the payment referred to in Clause 2.1 is made by electronic transfer to the Seller’s nominated account.

3. Completion

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates for the Shares;

(c) such other documents and assurances as are reasonably required to vest the Shares in the Buyer free from all Encumbrances.

4. Conditions

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon satisfaction of the Conditions, which the parties shall use reasonable endeavours to procure.

4.2 The Conditions include, but are not limited to:

(a) the Buyer having received all necessary corporate and third-party consents;

(b) no material adverse change having occurred in the business or financial condition of the Company;

(c) confirmation from Penrith Foods Ltd (company number 10777622) of continued supply arrangements with the Company.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 26 May 2027), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving notice in writing to the other, whereupon no party shall have any further liability under this Agreement except for accrued rights or obligations.

10. Post-Completion Provisions

10.1 Survival: The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under a Warranty must be notified in writing to the party giving the Warranty before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as notified in writing.

12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all related information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
