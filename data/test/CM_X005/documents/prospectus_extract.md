# Prospectus Extract  
**Yarrow Technologies Ltd**  
(Incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11073020)  
Registered office: 88 Cheapside, London  

**Date of this Prospectus:** 18 October 2026  

---

**1. Definitions**  
In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“the Company” means Yarrow Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11073020;  
“the Underwriter” means Ashcombe Marine Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14032950;  
“Penrith Energy plc” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15017132;  
“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value each in the capital of the Company;  
“this Prospectus” means the prospectus dated 18 October 2026 relating to the initial public offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company;  
“the Offering” means the initial public offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company underwritten by the Underwriter;  
“the Registration Statement” means the registration statement on Form F-1 (File No. 333-274190) filed by the Company with the Financial Conduct Authority pursuant to the UK Prospectus Regulation Rules.  

**2. Issuer and Offering Details**  
The issuer of the securities described herein is Yarrow Technologies Ltd. The date of this Prospectus is 18 October 2026. The Company is offering to the public for the first time its Ordinary Shares pursuant to the Offering. The Offering is being conducted by means of an underwritten public offering, with Ashcombe Marine Securities acting as sole underwriter.  

**3. Capitalisation**  
At the date of this Prospectus, the Company’s issued share capital consists of 61,750,000 Ordinary Shares in issue. All Ordinary Shares carry equal rights to dividends, voting and capital distribution. The Company’s share capital has been structured to support the scalability of operations and future growth initiatives, including strategic partnerships and technology development.  

**4. Use of Proceeds**  
Net proceeds from the Offering are expected to be used for general corporate purposes, including research and development, expansion of production facilities, repayment of certain short-term liabilities, and investment in key customer relationships. A portion of the proceeds may be allocated to strengthen the Company’s strategic alliance with Penrith Energy plc, a material customer, to enhance integration of the Company’s proprietary monitoring systems into its offshore energy infrastructure.  

**5. Underwriting**  
The Offering is being undertaken on a firm commitment basis by Ashcombe Marine Securities, which has agreed to purchase and place the Ordinary Shares in accordance with the terms of an Underwriting Agreement dated 12 September 2026. The Underwriter has severally, and not jointly, undertaken to subscribe for the entire issue of shares should public demand be insufficient. The Underwriter may engage in stabilising and short-position covering transactions in accordance with the UK Market Abuse Regulation, provided such activities comply with applicable disclosure obligations.  

**6. Payment Terms**  
Payment for shares subscribed under the Offering shall be made in full, without deduction or set-off, within five (5) business days following the date of allotment. All payments shall be made by irrevocable bank transfer to an account designated by the Company, details of which will be provided in the subscription confirmation.  

**7. Notices**  
Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the recipient. Notices to the Company shall be sent to 88 Cheapside, London. Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to 154 Cannon Street, London. Notices to Penrith Energy plc shall be sent to 103 King William Street, London. Such notice shall be deemed effective upon receipt where delivered by hand or email (with confirmation of transmission), or forty-eight (48) hours after posting where sent by post.  

**8. Confidentiality**  
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or competent authority. Confidential information includes, but is not limited to, financial projections, customer contracts, and technical specifications. This obligation shall survive the completion or termination of the Offering for a period of three (3) years.  

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Prospectus and any matters arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Prospectus.
