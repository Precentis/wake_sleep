UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set forth in the records of the parties, by and between Yarrow Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11073020, with its registered office at 88 Cheapside, London (the "Company"), and Ashcombe Marine Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14032950, with its registered office at 154 Cannon Street, London (the "Underwriter").

Recitals

A. The Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter proposes to purchase, in an initial public offering (the "Offering"), 10,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company (the "Firm Shares"), on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

B. The Company and the Underwriter intend that the Offering shall be conducted pursuant to a prospectus complying with the UK Prospectus Regulation and the relevant rules of the Financial Conduct Authority.

C. The Company has 62,250,000 ordinary shares in issue.

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London, England.

"Closing Date" means the date specified in the offering documents for settlement of the purchase of the Firm Shares, which shall be no later than 5 Business Days after the Pricing Date.

"Pricing Date" means 18 October 2026.

"Prospectus" means the base prospectus, any supplement to the prospectus and any final prospectus filed with the Financial Conduct Authority in connection with the Offering.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company on the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

3. Purchase and Sale of Firm Shares

3.1 The Company agrees to issue and sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at a price per share as agreed in a separate pricing side letter, payable in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

4. Over-allotment Option

4.1 The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 2,000,000 additional ordinary shares of the Company (the "Additional Shares"), representing 15% of the 10,000,000 Firm Shares, at the same purchase price per share as the Firm Shares.

4.2 The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part, at any time, and from time to time, within 30 days of the Closing Date, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company.

5. Representations and Warranties

5.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has the corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement; and

(c) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganisation and similar laws affecting creditors’ rights generally.

6. Covenants

6.1 The Company shall use its reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Prospectus is approved by the Financial Conduct Authority and that the Listing Rules are complied with in all material respects.

7. Conditions to Obligations

7. (a) The obligations of the Underwriter hereunder are subject to the delivery of a comfort letter from the Company's auditors in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to the Underwriter.

7. (b) The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the pricing date. The pricing date is 18 October 2026.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by fax, or sent by email to the addresses set forth above, or to such other address as a party may designate by notice given in accordance with this clause.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Underwriter shall keep confidential all non-public information provided to it by or on behalf of the Company in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

10. General

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
