1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business” means the business of manufacturing, distribution and sale of ambient food products carried out by Whitlock Industries Ltd or any member of the Oakhurst Foods group as conducted at the date of this Agreement.

“Confidential Information” means all information (whether of a technical, commercial or personal nature) relating to the Buyer, the Company, the Seller or any of their respective affiliates, clients, suppliers or employees, which is either marked as confidential or by its nature ought reasonably to be treated as confidential.

“Effective Date” means 1 April 2027.

“Founder” means Mr. Julian Whitlock, director and founder of Whitlock Industries Ltd.

“Territory” means the United Kingdom.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder and the Founder agrees to be employed by the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, with effect from the Effective Date.

3. Duties

3.1 The Founder shall hold the office of Chief Innovation Officer and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of Oakhurst Foods Ltd.

3.2 The Founder shall use his best endeavours to promote the interests of the Company and to perform his duties faithfully and diligently.

4. Remuneration

4.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £120,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in the annual bonus scheme operated by Oakhurst Foods Ltd, subject to the terms of that scheme, with a target bonus of 35% of base salary.

5. Expenses

5.1 The Company shall reimburse the Founder for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses properly incurred by him in the performance of his duties, provided that such expenses are supported by valid receipts and submitted in accordance with the Company’s expense policy.

6. Working Time and Place

6.1 The Founder’s normal place of work shall be the registered office of the Company at 137 Ludgate Hill, London.

6.2 The Founder shall work such hours as are necessary to fulfil his duties, which may include periods outside standard business hours.

7. Leave

7.1 The Founder shall be entitled to 28 days of paid holiday per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays.

7.2 Holiday shall be taken at such times as agreed with the Company.

8. Sickness and Incapacity

8.1 The Founder shall be entitled to full salary during periods of certified sickness absence in accordance with the Company’s applicable sickness policy.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Founder shall not at any time during or after the termination of his employment disclose to any person, or use for his own or any other person’s benefit, any Confidential Information.

9.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment, however occasioned.

10. Intellectual Property

10.1 All intellectual property created by the Founder in the course of his employment shall belong to the Company absolutely.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the respective party.

11.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to 137 Ludgate Hill, London, marked for the attention of the Chief Executive Officer.

12. Entire Agreement

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, arrangements and agreements relating to its subject matter.

13. Variation

13.1 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.

14. Assignment

14.1 The Company may assign its rights under this Agreement to any member of the Oakhurst Foods group.

15. Severance

15.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

16. Waiver

16.1 Failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that provision.

17. Duration and Termination

17.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party giving six months’ written notice.

18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, engage or be concerned in any business which competes with the Business within the Territory for a period of 12 months after termination of employment, however occasioned.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
