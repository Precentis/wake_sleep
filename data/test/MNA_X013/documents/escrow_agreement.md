1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement"), the following terms shall have the meanings set out below unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) "Buyer" means Oakhurst Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15252203, whose registered office is at 40 Ludgate Hill, London.

(b) "Seller" means Netherby Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15612881, whose registered office is at 95 Moorgate, London.

(c) "Company" means Whitlock Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14353796, whose registered office is at 137 Ludgate Hill, London.

(d) "Closing Date" means 10 June 2027.

(e) "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £15,000,000 (fifteen million pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total purchase price paid by the Buyer to the Seller for the entire issued share capital of the Company, deposited into an escrow account pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 April 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller.

(f) "Escrow Agent" means Barclays Bank PLC, or such other financial institution appointed in writing by the Buyer and Seller, which has agreed in writing to act as escrow agent under this Agreement.

(g) "Lowther Capital LLP" means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14489442, whose registered office is at 164 Bishopsgate, London, being the material customer of the Company as of the Closing Date.

(h) "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London.

(i) "Claim" means any bona fide notification by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller, prior to the release of the Escrow Amount, asserting liability under the warranties, indemnities or covenants contained in the Share Purchase Agreement.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") with Barclays Bank PLC, London, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer on or before the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely for the purposes set out in this Agreement, and shall not be subject to any lien, set-off or counterclaim by any party, including the Buyer or the Seller, except as expressly provided herein.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released by the Escrow Agent to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, being 10 June 2028, save to the extent that one or more Claims have been validly notified to the Escrow Agent and the Seller on or prior to that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to pending Claims shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such Claims in accordance with the dispute resolution mechanism set out in the Share Purchase Agreement.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall act upon written joint instructions from the Buyer and the Seller regarding the release or retention of any portion of the Escrow Amount. In the absence of such joint instructions, the Escrow Agent shall retain the disputed amount until receipt of a final determination by arbitration or written agreement between the parties.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered in accordance with the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association. The seat of arbitration shall be New York. The language of the arbitration shall be English.
