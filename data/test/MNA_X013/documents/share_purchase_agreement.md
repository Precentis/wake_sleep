SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 4 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Netherby Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15612881, whose registered office is at 95 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”); and

(2) Oakhurst Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15252203, whose registered office is at 40 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Whitlock Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14353796, whose registered office is at 137 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

   “Closing Date” means 10 June 2027.

   “Confidential Information” means any information of a confidential or proprietary nature disclosed by one Party to another pursuant to or in connection with this Agreement.

   “General Warranties” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, excluding the Fundamental Warranties.

   “Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $130,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), to be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3.00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $11,700,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $130,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. SURVIVAL AND NOTIFICATION OF CLAIMS

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

12. NON-COMPETE OBLIGATIONS

12.1 The Seller covenants and agrees that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates shall not, directly or indirectly engage in or carry on any business that is competitive with the business of the Company for a period of 5 years following the Closing Date.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).

NOTICES

Any notice or other communication required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or email address last known to the sender. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 95 Moorgate, London, and to the Buyer to 40 Ludgate Hill, London.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall keep confidential and not (except as required by law or regulation) disclose to any third party any Confidential Information received from the other Party pursuant to this Agreement, except where such information is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party. This obligation shall survive the termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years. The terms of this Agreement are confidential and shall be governed by English law.
