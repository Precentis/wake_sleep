ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] June 2025 between:

(1) Pemberton Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10877783, whose registered office is at 123 Old Broad Street, London ("Buyer");

(2) Redcliffe Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12749720, whose registered office is at 173 Cannon Street, London ("Seller"); and

(3) [●], in its capacity as escrow agent ("Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Buyer, Seller and Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 31 May 2026;

"Company" means Northgate Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13389634, whose registered office is at 109 Fleet Street, London;

"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party to another in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, electronically or in writing, including without limitation financial data, customer lists, and details of contractual arrangements with third parties;

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,850,000 (one million eight hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) representing 5.0% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] June 2025 between the Buyer and the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Juniper Pharma plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15224907, whose registered office is at 122 Cannon Street, London, being a material customer of the Company;

"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement dated [●] June 2025 between the Buyer and the Seller pursuant to which the Buyer acquires from the Seller the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Share Purchase Agreement.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to perform its duties hereunder subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deliver the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent, which shall hold the same in a segregated interest-bearing account maintained with a recognised financial institution in London.

3.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely for the purposes of securing the Buyer’s right to make claims arising under the Share Purchase Agreement.

4. Payments from Escrow

4.1 No disbursement shall be made from the Escrow Amount except in accordance with written instructions jointly signed by the Buyer and the Seller or as otherwise directed by a final judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.2 Upon receipt of joint written instructions or a court order, the Escrow Agent shall release the relevant portion of the Escrow Amount to the Buyer or return it to the Seller, as applicable.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may submit a claim against the Escrow Amount by delivering written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, two business days after posting; if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is generated.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party undertakes to the others that it shall not, and shall procure that its representatives shall not, disclose any Confidential Information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

7.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
