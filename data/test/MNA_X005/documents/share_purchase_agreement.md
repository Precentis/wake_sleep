# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated **23 May 2026**.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Redcliffe Logistics plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12749720**, whose registered office is at **173 Cannon Street, London** (the **Seller**); and

(2) **Pemberton Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10877783**, whose registered office is at **123 Old Broad Street, London** (the **Buyer**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Northgate Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13389634**, whose registered office is at **109 Fleet Street, London** (the **Company**).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

---

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

**"Material Adverse Change"** means any event, change or effect which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, but excluding any change arising from (i) general economic or political conditions in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates, (ii) changes affecting the industry in which the Company operates generally, or (iii) pandemics, epidemic diseases or public health emergencies declared by the World Health Organization.

**"Target Working Capital"** means **$3,500,000**.

**"Closing Working Capital"** means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1.

**"Warranties"** means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Clause 7.

---

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$110,000,000** (the **Purchase Price**).

**2.2** The completion of the sale and purchase (the **Closing**) shall take place at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree, at 10.00 a.m. on **23 June 2026** (the **Closing Date**), or such other date and time as the parties may agree in writing.

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced, on a dollar-for-dollar basis, by the amount by which the Closing Working Capital is less than the Target Working Capital. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, no upward adjustment shall be made.

---

**3. PAYMENT**

**3.1** The Purchase Price, as adjusted pursuant to Clause 2.3, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller by bank transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3.2** Any adjustment to the Purchase Price under Clause 2.3 shall be settled within ten (10) Business Days following delivery of the Closing Working Capital statement by the Buyer to the Seller.

---

**5. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**5.1** Each party undertakes to the other to keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

---

**9. INDEMNITIES AND WARRANTIES**

**9.7** Any claim by the Buyer for breach of the Warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller within **20 days** of the date on which the Buyer first becomes aware (or reasonably ought to have become aware) of the facts giving rise to the claim, failing which the claim shall be time-barred.

---

**10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

**10.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

**10.2** The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
