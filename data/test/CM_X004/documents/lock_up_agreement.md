**LOCK-UP AGREEMENT**

This Lock-Up Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] 2026 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Glenrock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12005405 and registered office at 180 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”);

(2) Delamere Logistics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10554270 and registered office at 96 Cannon Street, London (the “Underwriter”); and

(3) The Shareholder, whose identity is set out in Schedule 1 hereto.

**1. Definitions**

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in New York;

“Prospectus” means the final prospectus dated 27 May 2026 relating to the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company underwritten by the Underwriter;

“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company held by the Shareholder as of the date hereof, and any securities into which such shares may be converted or exchanged;

“Shareholder” means the person identified in Schedule 1 who holds Shares in the Company.

**2. Lock-Up Undertaking**

The Shareholder hereby agrees that, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, it shall not, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, grant any option to purchase, pledge, assign, hypothecate, transfer, or otherwise dispose of, or enter into any transaction which is designed to, or might reasonably be expected to, result in the disposition of (whether or not for value, and whether or not such disposition would constitute a sale by the Shareholder for United States federal income tax purposes) any Shares, during the period (the “Lock-Up Period”) ending 180 days after the date of the Prospectus.

**3. Exceptions**

The restrictions set out in Clause 2 shall not apply to:

(a) transfers of Shares as a bona fide gift;

(b) transfers of Shares by will or the laws of intestacy;

(c) transfers of Shares to a trust established for the direct or indirect benefit of the Shareholder or any of their immediate family members;

(d) distributions of Shares to partners, members or shareholders of the Shareholder, provided that in the case of any transfer pursuant to (a) to (d), each transferee agrees in writing, in form satisfactory to the Underwriter, to be bound by the terms of this Agreement; or

(e) transfers pursuant to an order of a court or regulatory authority;

provided that in each case, no filing by any party under Section 16(a) of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, or any public announcement shall be required or shall be made voluntarily in connection with such transfer.

**4. Payment and Consideration**

No separate payment or consideration shall be provided by the Underwriter or the Company to the Shareholder under this Agreement in respect of the obligations undertaken herein.

**5. Notices**

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class post (pre-paid, in the case of post), by recorded delivery, or by electronic means (including email, provided that confirmation of receipt is obtained) to the address or email address set out in Schedule 1 or such other address or email address as a party may have notified to the others in accordance with this clause.

**6. Confidentiality**

The Shareholder agrees that the terms of this Agreement, and the fact of its existence, are confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party other than (a) to its legal or financial advisers, (b) as required by law or regulation, or (c) with the prior written consent of the Underwriter.

**7. General**

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and arrangements, whether written or oral, between the parties in respect of such subject matter. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law and the parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
