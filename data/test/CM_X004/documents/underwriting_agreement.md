**UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT**  
between  
GLENROCK MEDIA LTD  
and  
DELA MERE LOGISTICS SECURITIES  

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date of execution by the parties hereto, by and between Glenrock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12005405, whose registered office is at 180 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), and Delamere Logistics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10554270, whose registered office is at 96 Cannon Street, London (the “Underwriter”).  

**1. Definitions**  
In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:  
(a) “Pricing Date” means 16 June 2026, being the date on which the final price per Ordinary Share is determined in connection with the initial public offering.  
(b) “Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered in the initial public offering.  
(c) “Initial Public Offering” means the offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company to the public, as underwritten by the Underwriter pursuant to this Agreement.  
(d) “Closing Date” means the date on which settlement of the offering occurs, as specified in the final prospectus, and in any event no later than five business days after the Pricing Date.  
(e) “Prospectus” means the final prospectus relating to the Initial Public Offering, as approved by the Financial Conduct Authority and published in accordance with applicable law.  
(f) “Material Customer” means Lowther Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14354358, whose registered office is at 90 Cannon Street, London.  

**2. Appointment and Undertaking**  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter in connection with the Initial Public Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to underwrite the offering of Ordinary Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.  

**3. Offering Size and Price**  
The Company shall issue and sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter shall purchase from the Company, a number of Ordinary Shares representing 100% of the shares offered in the Initial Public Offering, at a price per share to be determined on the Pricing Date. The aggregate purchase price shall be paid by the Underwriter in cleared funds to the Company’s designated account on the Closing Date.  

**4. Representations and Warranties of the Company**  
The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter that:  
(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
(b) the execution and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate action;  
(c) the Ordinary Shares have been, or will be prior to the Closing Date, duly authorised and, when issued and delivered against payment, will be validly issued, fully paid and free from pre-emption rights;  
(d) there are no material misstatements or omissions in the Prospectus, and all statements therein are true and accurate in all material respects;  
(e) the Material Customer, Lowther Retail Group plc, accounts for 42% of the Company’s annual revenue for the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026.  

**5. Covenants**  
The Company covenants to:  
(a) prepare and file the Prospectus in a timely manner;  
(b) cooperate fully with the Underwriter in all aspects of the Initial Public Offering;  
(c) not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, enter into any material transaction or commitment outside the ordinary course of business prior to the Closing Date.  

**6. Payment and Delivery**  
On the Closing Date, the Underwriter shall remit the aggregate purchase price for the Ordinary Shares by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the Company at least three business days prior to the Closing Date. The Company shall deliver to the Underwriter share certificates (or electronic delivery via CREST) representing the Ordinary Shares, registered in such name or names and in such denominations as the Underwriter may request.  

**7. Comfort Letter**  
(a) The Company shall deliver to the Underwriter, on or prior to the date specified in this clause, a letter from its auditors in customary form providing comfort with respect to the financial statements included in the Prospectus.  
(b) The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the pricing date. The pricing date is 16 June 2026.  

**8. Notices**  
All notices and other communications hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile, or sent by overnight courier to the respective addresses set forth above, or to such other address as a party may designate in writing.  

**9. Confidentiality**  
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Initial Public Offering, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary in connection with regulatory filings.  

**10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
