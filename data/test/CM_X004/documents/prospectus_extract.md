1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Glenrock Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12005405, with its registered office at 180 Bishopsgate, London;

“Underwriter” means Delamere Logistics Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10554270, with its registered office at 96 Cannon Street, London;

“Lowther Retail Group plc” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14354358, with its registered office at 90 Cannon Street, London;

“Prospectus” means this offering document, dated 16 June 2026, relating to the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company;

“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;

“Initial Public Offering” or “IPO” means the first public offering of Ordinary Shares by the Company, underwritten by the Underwriter;

“Directors” means the directors of the Company for the time being, including executive and non-executive directors.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision.

1.3 References to “£” are to Pound Sterling.

2. Details of the Offering

2.1 The Company is making an initial public offering of 15,000,000 Ordinary Shares at an offer price of £1.25 per share.

2.2 The Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on a firm commitment basis, subject to the terms and conditions set out in the Underwriting Agreement dated 10 May 2026.

2.3 The net proceeds from the Offering, after deduction of underwriting commissions and offering expenses, are estimated to amount to £17,125,000.

2.4 The underwriting commission payable to the Underwriter shall be 4.5% of the gross proceeds raised from the Offering.

2.5 The Ordinary Shares will be admitted to the Official List of the UK Financial Conduct Authority and to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market for listed securities.

3. Use of Proceeds

3.1 The Company intends to apply approximately 60% of the net proceeds to expand digital content production capacity, including investment in studio infrastructure and talent acquisition.

3.2 Approximately 25% of the net proceeds will be allocated to the repayment of existing short-term debt facilities.

3.3 The remaining 15% of the net proceeds shall be reserved for general corporate purposes, including marketing initiatives and working capital.

4. Related Party Transactions

4.1 The Company confirms that there are no related party transactions other than directors' service agreements entered into in the ordinary course of business.

4.2 All directors’ service agreements have been approved by the Audit Committee and disclosed in the Directors’ Report contained in the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025.

5. Material Customer

5.1 The Company derives approximately 38% of its annual revenue from contracts with Lowther Retail Group plc.

5.2 The principal agreement with Lowther Retail Group plc is a multi-year digital advertising services contract, effective from 1 March 2023 to 28 February 2027, subject to automatic renewal for successive one-year terms unless terminated by either party on 180 days’ written notice.

5.3 The loss of Lowther Retail Group plc as a customer could have a material adverse effect on the Company’s revenue and profitability.

6. Payment and Settlement

6.1 Application monies for the Ordinary Shares must be paid in full at the time of application.

6.2 Settlement of the Ordinary Shares issued under the Offering will take place on T+5, where T is the date of pricing of the shares.

6.3 All payments under this Prospectus shall be made in cleared funds to such account as may be designated by the Company or the Underwriter in writing.
