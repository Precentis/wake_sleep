(f) any reference to a statutory provision includes any amendment, re-enactment or replacement thereof for the time being in force; and

**2. Scope of Procedures**  
2.1 The Company has engaged the undersigned to perform agreed-upon procedures in connection with certain financial and operational information included in the registration statement and prospectus related to the IPO.  
2.2 The procedures performed were limited to those specified by the Underwriter and were conducted in accordance with International Standard on Related Services (ISRS) 4400 (UK), as applicable.

**3. Period Covered**  
3.1 The procedures covered the period up to **4 June 2026 (the cut-off date)**.

**4. Financial Information**  
4.1 We have examined the unaudited interim financial statements of the Company for the six-month period ended 31 December 2025, the audited annual financial statements for the year ended 30 June 2025, and certain selected financial data derived therefrom.  
4.2 With respect to the financial information referenced in clause 4.1, our procedures consisted of:  
(a) comparing the financial data to the accounting records of the Company;  
(b) making inquiries of appropriate personnel regarding accounting policies and financial reporting practices; and  
(c) performing analytical procedures on significant balances and fluctuations.  
4.3 Nothing came to our attention as a result of the procedures performed that caused us to believe that the financial information was not prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework.

**5. Material Customer Relationship**  
5.1 During the 12 months preceding the cut-off date, Lowther Retail Group plc accounted for **35%** of the Company’s total revenue.  
5.2 The Company has represented that the commercial relationship with Lowther Retail Group plc is ongoing and not subject to any known termination provisions or material renegotiations as of the cut-off date.

**6. Cut-Off Date**  
6.1 Our procedures covered the period up to **4 June 2026 (the cut-off date)**.

**7. Limitation of Comfort**  
7.1 This letter is addressed solely to the Underwriter and is for the purpose of assisting in connection with the IPO.  
7.2 No part of this letter may be quoted or referred to in any public document, nor may any third party rely on this letter, without our prior written consent.

**8. Payment**  
8.1 The Underwriter shall pay the fees for the services rendered pursuant to a separate engagement letter dated 12 March 2025.  
8.2 All payments shall be made in sterling and are non-refundable, save as otherwise agreed in writing.

**9. Notices**  
9.1 Any notice required or permitted under this letter shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as may be notified in writing.

**10. Confidentiality**  
10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information obtained in connection with this engagement and shall not disclose it to any third party except:  
(a) as required by law or regulation;  
(b) to professional advisers; or  
(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

**11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this letter.
