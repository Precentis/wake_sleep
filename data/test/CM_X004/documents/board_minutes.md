MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF GLENROCK MEDIA LTD  
HELD AT THE REGISTERED OFFICE ON 25 MAY 2026  

1. Call to Order  
The Chairperson, Mr. Elias Thorne, called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m.  

2. Attendance  
Present: Mr. Elias Thorne (Chair), Ms. Aoife Abernethy, Mr. Julian Pike, Dr. Sarah Nkosi, and Ms. Freya Booth.  
Ms. Abernethy declared a personal interest in Item 7 below and withdrew from the meeting during discussion and voting on that item.  

3. Apologies  
Mr. Daniel Hartley offered his apologies for absence due to prior professional commitments.  

4. Confirmation of Agenda  
The agenda was confirmed as circulated, with no additions.  

5. Review of Draft Minutes of Previous Meeting  
The draft minutes of the board meeting held on 18 March 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously to approve them as a true and accurate record.  

6. Company Performance Update  
The Chief Financial Officer presented unaudited financial results for the quarter ending 31 March 2026. Revenue increased by 14.7% compared to the prior quarter. EBITDA margins remained stable at 21.3%. The Chair noted the performance supported investor confidence ahead of the planned initial public offering.  

7. Approval of Director Loan  
The board considered a proposed loan from the Company to Aoife Abernethy, a director, in the amount of $4,000,000. The terms of the loan are interest-free and do not specify a repayment date. After due deliberation, and with Ms. Abernethy having declared her interest and excused herself from the meeting for this item, the board resolved to approve the loan. The transaction is structured as a director’s advance and will be disclosed in accordance with applicable provisions of the Companies Act 2006. Legal counsel confirmed the arrangement is compliant with English law and does not trigger adverse tax consequences for the Company.  

8. Initial Public Offering Update  
The board received a progress report on the proposed initial public offering of ordinary shares, currently underwritten by Delamere Logistics Securities (company number 10554270), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 96 Cannon Street, London. The preliminary prospectus has been circulated to the board for review. The offering is expected to raise gross proceeds of £32,500,000, valuing the Company at £180,000,000 post-money.  

9. Material Customer Contract  
The board reviewed the existing commercial agreement with Lowther Retail Group plc (company number 14354358), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 90 Cannon Street, London. That customer accounts for 38% of the Company's annual revenue under a contract due for renewal on 30 September 2026. Engagement with the customer’s procurement team is ongoing, and management anticipates a renewal on substantially similar terms.  

10. Next Meeting  
The next board meeting is scheduled for 24 June 2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the registered office of the Company, 180 Bishopsgate, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12005405.  

11. Adjournment  
The meeting was adjourned at 12:15 p.m.
