DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 15 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Quayside Medical Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with company number 10407615 and registered office at 14 Gresham Street, London (the "Customer"); and

(2) Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with company number 10612431 and registered office at 91 Cannon Street, London (the "Supplier").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Customer and the Supplier are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated 1 December 2025 (the "MSA"), under which the Supplier provides managed IT services to the Customer;

(B) In the course of providing the services, the Supplier will process certain personal data on behalf of the Customer;

(C) The parties wish to set out their respective rights and obligations in relation to such processing in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and any other applicable legislation relating to the processing of personal data as from time to time in force in the United Kingdom.

"Customer Personal Data" means any personal data of the Customer or its end users that is processed by the Supplier pursuant to or in connection with the MSA, including but not limited to names, contact details, job titles, and system access logs.

"Processing" shall have the meaning given to it in the Applicable Data Protection Law and includes any operation or set of operations performed upon personal data.

"Special Categories of Personal Data" means personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data for identifying a natural person, data concerning health, or data concerning a person’s sex life or sexual orientation.

2. Subject Matter, Nature and Purpose

2.1 This Agreement governs the processing of Customer Personal Data by the Supplier when providing the managed IT services under the MSA.

2.2 The duration of the data processing is co-terminous with the term of the MSA, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 The Supplier shall only process Customer Personal Data on the documented instructions of the Customer, unless required to do so by Applicable Data Protection Law.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to a duty of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any third-party processor (a "Sub-processor") in connection with the processing of Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 The Supplier has appointed Rothley Software Ltd (company number 12880440, registered office 18 Old Broad Street, London) as a Sub-processor for software monitoring services.

5. Security of Processing

5.1 The Supplier shall maintain a documented information security policy and shall review it annually.

5.2 The Supplier shall report any personal data breach to the Customer without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

6. Compliance with Data Protection Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. International Transfers

7.1 The Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom unless such transfer is permitted under Applicable Data Protection Law and the Customer has been notified in advance.

8. Audits and Assistance

8.1 The Supplier shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

8.2 The Supplier shall allow for and contribute to audits or inspections conducted by the Customer or its designated third party.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 Both parties shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

10. Notices

10.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party.

11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

11.2 This Agreement forms part of the MSA and shall have effect as if set out in full therein.
