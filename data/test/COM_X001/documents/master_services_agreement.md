1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Agreement" means this master services agreement between the Customer and the Supplier, including all schedules and appendices, as amended from time to time in accordance with its terms.

"Business Day" means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, which is identified as confidential or which, by its nature, ought reasonably to be considered confidential, including but not limited to technical data, pricing, business plans, and customer lists.

"Effective Date" means the date on which the Agreement was originally executed by both parties.

"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer in accordance with the Service Specifications, including system monitoring, maintenance, and technical support.

"Service Specifications" means the detailed description of the Services as agreed in writing between the parties from time to time.

"Term" has the meaning given in Clause 3.2.

2. Provision of Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the Service Specifications and the standards of skill, care and diligence expected of a competent IT service provider operating in the United Kingdom.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services using personnel of appropriate skill and experience, and shall ensure that all equipment and software used in the provision of the Services comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Term and Termination

3.2 This Agreement expires on 24 July 2026 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

4. Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services as set out in the applicable invoice issued by the Supplier in accordance with the payment schedule specified in the Service Specifications.

4.2 Invoices shall be payable within 30 days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Lloyds Bank plc, accruing daily from the due date until the date of actual payment, whether before or after judgment.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, during the Term and for a period of five years thereafter, disclose any Confidential Information to any third party, nor use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

5.2 The obligations under Clause 5.1 shall not apply to information which:

(a) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving party;  
(b) was known to the receiving party prior to disclosure under this Agreement;  
(c) is independently developed by the receiving party without reference to the disclosing party’s Confidential Information; or  
(d) is required to be disclosed by law or by a regulatory authority.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the receiving party.

6.2 Notices to the Customer shall be sent to: 14 Gresham Street, London, and to the Supplier at: 91 Cannon Street, London.

6.3 A notice shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, two Business Days after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no automatic error message is generated.

11. Liability

11.1 Subject to Clause 11.2, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall be limited to $500,000.

11.2 The cap on liability in Clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability for:  
(a) death or personal injury caused by the party’s negligence; or  
(b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.
