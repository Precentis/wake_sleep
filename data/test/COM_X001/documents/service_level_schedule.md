SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

   "Availability" means the percentage of time in a calendar month during which the Managed IT Services are accessible and functional to the Customer, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure events, as measured by the Supplier’s monitoring systems.

   "Business Hours" means 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England.

   "Customer" means Quayside Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10407615, whose registered office is at 14 Gresham Street, London.

   "Force Majeure" has the meaning given in the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 16 January 2026 between the Customer and the Supplier.

   "Monthly Fee" means the sum of $65,000, payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services in each calendar month.

   "Scheduled Maintenance" means planned maintenance activities notified by the Supplier to the Customer in advance and conducted outside Business Hours, provided such activities do not exceed four (4) hours per calendar month.

   "Service Credits" means the credits payable by the Supplier to the Customer in accordance with Paragraph 4, calculated as a percentage of the Monthly Fee.

   "Services" means the managed IT services provided by the Supplier under the Master Services Agreement.

   "Supplier" means Yarrow Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10612431, whose registered office is at 91 Cannon Street, London.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Service Level Schedule. The headings in this Schedule are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Service Levels

2.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that Availability of the Services meets or exceeds 99.5% in each calendar month.

2.2 Availability shall be measured continuously using automated monitoring tools located at the primary service delivery point. Measurement data shall be retained by the Supplier for a period of twelve (12) months and made available to the Customer upon written request within ten (10) Business Days.

2.3 The Supplier shall provide a monthly performance report within five (5) Business Days of the end of each calendar month, detailing Availability, incidents resolved, and any Scheduled Maintenance undertaken.

3. Payment

3.1 The Monthly Fee shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.2 No deductions shall be made from the Monthly Fee except as permitted under this Schedule through the application of Service Credits.

3.3 Service Credits, if applicable, shall be applied as a deduction against the next due Monthly Fee or, at the Customer’s election, refunded within thirty (30) days of the end of the relevant calendar month.

4. Service Credits

4.1 If the monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee applies.

4.2 If the monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the Monthly Fee applies.

4.3 Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet the Availability targets set out in this Schedule.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Paragraph 1.1.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two (2) Business Days after posting; if sent by electronic mail, on the day transmitted, provided confirmation of receipt is obtained.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party marked as confidential or which by its nature is confidential, including performance data, pricing, and technical specifications.

6.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination of the Master Services Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Service Level Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
