**PROSPECTUS EXTRACT**  
**Dated 27 February 2027**

**1. Definitions**  
In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Company” means Penrith Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14501968, with its registered office at 110 Fleet Street, London;  
“Underwriter” means Saltash Systems Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11537997, with its registered office at 138 Fleet Street, London;  
“Redcliffe Analytics” means Redcliffe Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10614272, with its registered office at 26 Fleet Street, London;  
“Prospectus” means this prospectus dated 27 February 2027;  
“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;  
“Closing Date” means the date on which the initial public offering of the Ordinary Shares is completed;  
“Proceeds” means the net proceeds received by the Company from the offering of the Ordinary Shares.  

All references to “$” are to United States dollars.

**2. Date of Prospectus**  
2.1. The date of this Prospectus is 27 February 2027.  
2.2. This Prospectus is issued in connection with the initial public offering of the Ordinary Shares by the Company and the underwriting arrangement entered into with the Underwriter.

**3. Offering and Underwriting**  
3.1. The Company has entered into an underwriting agreement with the Underwriter pursuant to which the Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the initial public offering of the Ordinary Shares on a firm commitment basis.  
3.2. The Underwriter has undertaken to purchase, and the Company has agreed to sell, the total number of Ordinary Shares subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the underwriting agreement, subject to customary closing conditions.  
3.3. The offering is being conducted in accordance with the applicable securities laws and regulations of the relevant jurisdictions in which the shares are offered.  
3.4. The Company and the Underwriter reserve the right to suspend, postpone or terminate the offering at any time prior to the Closing Date.

**4. Use of Proceeds**  
4.1. The net proceeds from the offering are expected to amount to $80,000,000.  
4.2. The Company intends to apply the net proceeds as follows:  
(a) $36,000,000 for the repayment of the outstanding balance under the Company’s revolving credit facility;  
(b) $24,000,000 for capital expenditure, including investment in research and development infrastructure and software platform enhancements;  
(c) $25,000,000 for general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions, and expansion into new markets.  
4.3. The allocation of proceeds is based on the Company’s current intentions and may be adjusted if necessary, subject to disclosure requirements and regulatory obligations.  
4.4. Pending application, the proceeds will be held in interest-bearing accounts in accordance with the Company’s treasury policies.

**5. Material Customer Relationship**  
5.1. The Company has entered into a multi-year service agreement with Redcliffe Analytics, pursuant to which Redcliffe Analytics accounts for 18% of the Company’s total revenue for the financial year ending 31 December 2026.  
5.2. The agreement with Redcliffe Analytics is scheduled to expire on 30 June 2028, unless extended or renewed in accordance with its terms.

**6. Payment**  
6.1. Payment for the Ordinary Shares shall be made in full by the Underwriter to the Company on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Company.  
6.2. The Company shall deliver share certificates or electronic confirmations representing the Ordinary Shares to the Underwriter or its designee in accordance with applicable settlement procedures.

**7. Notices**  
7.1. Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered personally, by pre-paid first-class post (or airmail if sent outside the United Kingdom), or by electronic mail to the registered office of the relevant party.  
7.2. Notices sent to the Company shall be addressed to: 110 Fleet Street, London.  
7.3. Notices sent to the Underwriter shall be addressed to: 138 Fleet Street, London.  
7.4. A notice shall be deemed served at the time of personal delivery, 48 hours after posting, or upon confirmed receipt if sent by electronic mail.

**8. Confidentiality**  
8.1. Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the offering, except as required by law or regulation.  
8.2. The obligations under this clause shall survive the completion or termination of the offering for a period of three years.

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
9.1. This Prospectus and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
