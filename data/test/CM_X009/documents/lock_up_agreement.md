LOCK-UP AGREEMENT

This Lock-Up Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] February 2027 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Penrith Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14501968 whose registered office is at 110 Fleet Street, London ("the Company"); and

(2) Saltash Systems Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11537997 whose registered office is at 138 Fleet Street, London ("the Underwriter").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Company proposes to carry out an initial public offering (the "IPO") of its ordinary shares (the "Shares") underwritten by the Underwriter.

(B) In connection with the IPO, the Shareholder (as defined below) holds certain Shares and has agreed not to dispose of any such Shares during a specified period.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in New York;

"Prospectus" means the final prospectus dated 8 February 2027 relating to the IPO;

"Shareholder" means any person or entity holding Shares in the Company immediately prior to the closing of the IPO, as listed in Schedule A hereto;

"Shares" means the ordinary shares of the Company, nominal value £0.01 each.

2. Lock-Up Restriction

The Shareholder shall not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, assign, hypothecate, transfer, or otherwise dispose of (collectively, "Transfer"), or enter into any transaction which is designed to, or which reasonably could be expected to, result in a Transfer of, any Shares beneficially owned by the Shareholder as of the date hereof (including, without limitation, Shares issuable upon exercise of options, warrants or other rights) during the period ending 365 days after the date of the Prospectus.

3. Payment

No consideration shall be payable by the Underwriter or the Company to the Shareholder under this Agreement for the covenants contained herein. The Shareholder acknowledges that the obligations under this Agreement are given in connection with and as an inducement to the Underwriter to proceed with the IPO.

4. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, recorded delivery, or by email to the address or email address set out in Schedule A, or to such other address or email address as the relevant party may have notified to the other parties in writing. Notices sent by post shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting. Notices sent by email shall be deemed received on the next Business Day following transmission.

5. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, except to the extent disclosure is required by law, regulation, or any listing authority with jurisdiction over the disclosing party, or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under a duty of confidentiality.

6. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, negotiations and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
