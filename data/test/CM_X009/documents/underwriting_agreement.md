UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 5 February 2027, between:

(1) Penrith Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14501968, whose registered office is at 110 Fleet Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Saltash Systems Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11537997, whose registered office is at 138 Fleet Street, London (the "Underwriter").

WHEREAS the Company proposes to issue and sell ordinary shares (the "Shares") in connection with its initial public offering (the "Offering"), and the Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Effective Date" means the date on which the registration statement relating to the Offering becomes effective under applicable securities legislation;

"Firm Shares" means 24,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company offered in the Offering;

"Over-allotment Option" has the meaning given in Clause 4;

"Shares" means the Firm Shares and, if exercised, the additional shares issuable pursuant to the Over-allotment Option.

**2. Appointment and Undertaking**

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter accepts such appointment and agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The obligation of the Underwriter to purchase the Firm Shares shall be subject to the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the Company contained herein as of the Effective Date and the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions precedent set out in Clause 7.

**3. Purchase and Sale of Firm Shares**

3.1 Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, the Firm Shares at a price of £2.25 per share (the "Purchase Price").

3.2 Payment for the Firm Shares shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by the Company at least two business days prior to the closing date (the "Closing Date"), which shall occur on the third business day following the Effective Date.

**4. Over-allotment Option**

4.1 The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 4,800,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 24,000,000 Firm Shares, at the Purchase Price.

4.2 The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time prior to 30 days after the Closing Date, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company.

4.3 Any Shares issued pursuant to the exercise of the Over-allotment Option shall be deemed to be Shares for all purposes under this Agreement.

**5. Representations and Warranties**

5.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Effective Date that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;

(c) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms;

(d) the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate any applicable law or any agreement to which it is a party.

**6. Covenants**

6.1 The Company covenants that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, offer, sell, or otherwise dispose of any securities similar to the Shares during a period of 90 days following the Closing Date.

**7. Conditions to Closing**

7.1 The obligations of the Underwriter hereunder are subject to the delivery of customary closing documents, including legal opinions, officer’s certificates, and evidence of listing approval.

**8. Notices**

8.1 All notices and communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party, or to such other address as a party may designate by written notice.

**9. Offerings in Specific Jurisdictions**

9.1 The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

**10. Confidentiality**

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulatory authority.

**11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
