ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 28 March 2026 among:

(1) Corrib Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14855800, with its registered office at 146 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Juniper Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10037944, with its registered office at 52 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) Lloyds Bank Corporate Markets plc, with its principal office at 25 Gresham Street, London EC2V 7HN, as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

Recitals

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 28 March 2026 (the “SPA”) to acquire the entire issued share capital of Merrion Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11008774, with its registered office at 42 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”), from the Seller.

(B) As part of the SPA, a portion of the total consideration is to be held in escrow to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount (as defined below) on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

“Closing Date” means 1 May 2026.

“Claim Notice” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to the Release Date, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim under the SPA, together with an estimate of the amount claimed.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £1,750,000 (one million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total purchase price under the SPA, which has been deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date.

“Release Date” means the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date.

“SPA” has the meaning given in Recital (A).

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, administer, and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Funds

3.1 The Escrow Amount has been transferred by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date and shall be held by the Escrow Agent in an interest-bearing account at Lloyds Bank Corporate Markets plc under account number 77482910 (the “Escrow Account”).

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall not invest the Escrow Amount or any interest accrued thereon unless otherwise directed in writing by the Buyer and the Seller jointly.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be released by the Escrow Agent to the Seller on the Release Date, save to the extent that a Claim Notice has been validly delivered before the Release Date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount to which a pending claim relates shall be retained by the Escrow Agent until such claim is finally resolved by mutual agreement of the Buyer and the Seller or by final determination of a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.2 Upon the resolution of any pending claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed balance of the Escrow Amount to the Seller within five (5) Business Days of receiving written joint instructions from the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
