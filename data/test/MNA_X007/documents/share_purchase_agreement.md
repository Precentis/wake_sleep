SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 April 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Corrib Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14855800, whose registered office is at 146 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Juniper Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10037944, whose registered office is at 52 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Merrion Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11008774, whose registered office is at 42 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the following meanings:

   "Closing Date" means 1 May 2026;

   "Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration for the sale and purchase of the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1;

   "Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $20,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be determined in accordance with the accounting principles and procedures set out in Schedule 2.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 Payment under Clause 2.2 shall be made without set-off, deduction or withholding, except as required by law.

4. CONDITIONS

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or written waiver by the Buyer of the following conditions (the "Conditions"):

   (a) all representations, warranties and undertakings given by the other party shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; and

   (b) no order, injunction or decree issued by any court or governmental authority shall be in effect which restrains or prohibits the Closing.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (being 1 April 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided such termination shall not affect any accrued rights or liabilities.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be addressed to: 146 Ludgate Hill, London, with a copy by email to legal@corribengineering.co.uk.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be addressed to: 52 Moorgate, London, with a copy by email to legal@juniperengineering.co.uk.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information received from the other party in connection with the transaction contemplated herein, except as required by law or regulation.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.3 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $1,920,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $20,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1.
