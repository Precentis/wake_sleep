**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this contract between the parties, including all schedules and appendices;

“Company” means Juniper Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13666107, with registered office at 137 Bishopsgate, London;

“Buyer” means Orwell Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13211845, with registered office at 133 Old Broad Street, London;

“change of control” means, in relation to a corporate entity, the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting in concert of direct or indirect control of more than 50 per cent of the voting rights or of the ability to appoint or remove a majority of the board of directors;

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public technical, commercial, financial or operational information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, and whether marked as confidential or not;

“Customer” means Saltash Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15558548, with registered office at 163 Moorgate, London;

“Effective Date” means the date of execution of this Agreement by both parties;

“Seller” means Yarrow Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15106901, with registered office at 80 Gresham Street, London.

1.2 Any reference to a clause, schedule or annex refers to a clause, schedule or annex to this Agreement.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

**2. Supply of Services**

2.1 The Company shall provide energy supply and related technical services (the “Services”) to the Customer in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A, attached hereto and incorporated by reference.

2.2 The Services shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of five (5) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein.

**3. Payment Terms**

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees for the Services as detailed in Schedule B, payable quarterly in arrears by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Company.

3.2 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4 per cent per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing from the due date until actual payment.

**4. Confidentiality**

4.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other’s Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

4.2 This obligation shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if by post, two (2) business days after posting; if by email, on the day of transmission, provided a confirmation of receipt is obtained.

**14. Termination**

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon ninety (90) days’ written notice if the other party commits a material breach of its obligations and fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving notice requiring remediation.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days’ written notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the Customer. For the purposes of this clause, the counterparty is Saltash Logistics plc.

**15. General**

15.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations or understandings, whether written or oral.

15.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by duly authorised representatives of both parties.

15.3 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
