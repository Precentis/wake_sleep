SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  

This Agreement is dated 7 May 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Orwell Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13211845 whose registered office is at 133 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(2) Yarrow Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15106901 whose registered office is at 80 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Juniper Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13666107 whose registered office is at 137 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the Shares on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Closing Date" means 26 June 2026.

"Confidential Information" means all information of a proprietary or confidential nature relating to the business, operations, customers, or suppliers of the Company or the Buyer, whether in written, electronic or oral form.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

"Warranties" means the statements, representations and warranties set out in Schedule 1.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $20,000,000 (twenty million US dollars) (the "Purchase Price").

3. Completion

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing. At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all instruments of transfer, share certificates and such other documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges, equities and third-party rights.

4. Payment

4.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller by bank transfer to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the address or email address set out opposite the party’s name in this Agreement or such other address or email as a party may notify to the other in writing.

5.2 A notice sent by pre-paid post shall be deemed to be served 48 hours after posting.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not at any time after the date of this Agreement disclose to any person any Confidential Information, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

8. Limitation of Liability

8.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for claims arising under or in connection with the Warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $2,400,000. This limit applies cumulatively in respect of all such claims.

12. Non-Compete

12.1 The Seller covenants that for a period of 5 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, whether directly or indirectly, carry on, be concerned with or be engaged in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

13. General

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the whole agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes any prior discussions, correspondence or agreements.

13.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
