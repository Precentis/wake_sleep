EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 25 May 2026 between:

(1) Juniper Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13666107, whose registered office is at 137 Bishopsgate, London ("Company");

(2) Orwell Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13211845, whose registered office is at 133 Old Broad Street, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) Mr. Elias Thorne, of 42 Fernleigh Gardens, London, EC1V 9DS ("Founder").

Recitals

(A) The Company is engaged in the business of developing and operating renewable energy infrastructure across the United Kingdom (the "Business").

(B) The Buyer has entered into an agreement to acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from Yarrow Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15106901, whose registered office is at 80 Gresham Street, London ("Seller"), with completion to occur on or before 2 June 2026.

(C) The Founder is a key technical officer of the Company and has been instrumental in the development of the Company’s intellectual property portfolio, including proprietary energy storage systems.

(D) In connection with the acquisition, the parties wish to formalize the continued employment of the Founder under the terms set out herein.

Now it is hereby agreed as follows:

1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, any subsidiary or holding company of that person, or any subsidiary of such a holding company, from time to time, as defined in section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006.

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information, technical data, trade secrets, customer lists, pricing strategies, financial information, research, product plans, or business operations concerning the Company, the Buyer, or any of their respective Affiliates.

"Effective Date" means the date of completion of the share acquisition by the Buyer from the Seller, being 2 June 2026.

2. Employment

The Company shall continue to employ the Founder, and the Founder agrees to remain in the employ of the Company, on the terms of this Agreement from the Effective Date.

4. Remuneration

The Founder shall receive an annual base salary of £98,500, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each calendar month, subject to statutory deductions.

5. Bonus

The Founder shall be eligible to receive an annual discretionary bonus of up to 45% of base salary, payable at the sole discretion of the Buyer’s board of directors following the end of each financial year.

8. Confidentiality

The Founder undertakes that he shall not, at any time during or after the termination of his employment, disclose or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the proper performance of his duties, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Buyer.

10. Notices

Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the Company or to the Founder’s last known address as recorded in the Company’s personnel files. Notices shall be deemed served 24 hours after email transmission or 48 hours after posting.

12. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six months’ written notice to the other party.

18. Non-Competition

The Founder shall not, during the period of 12 months immediately following the termination of his employment with the Company for any reason, whether directly or indirectly, whether on his own account or in conjunction with or on behalf of any other person, firm, or company, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the Business of the Company or any Affiliate of the Company, including but not limited to businesses supplying energy storage solutions to material customers such as Saltash Logistics plc (company number 15558548, registered office 163 Moorgate, London). For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction shall last for a period of 12 months and shall apply within the United Kingdom.

20. Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
