EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT

This extract forms part of the Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") entered into on 15 June 2024 between Kestrel Software Ltd (the "Company"), a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 15577361) with its registered office at 35 Moorgate, London, and Mr. Elias Thorne (the "Employee").

1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Buyer" means Kestrel Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales (company number 14777801) with its registered office at 166 Old Broad Street, London;

"Business" means the development, licensing, and support of enterprise software solutions for media and biotechnology sectors, as conducted by the Company or any Group company at any time during the Employee’s employment;

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information, technical data, trade secrets, customer lists (including but not limited to Holloway Foods Ltd, company number 15162973), software code, algorithms, business strategies, pricing models, and financial information disclosed to or developed by the Employee during employment;

"Effective Date" means 15 June 2024;

"Founder" means Mr. Elias Thorne in his capacity as founder and Chief Technology Officer of the Company;

"Group" means the Company and any subsidiary or holding company from time to time, including the Buyer following completion of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Seller;

"Seller" means Orwell Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales (company number 13771253) with its registered office at 32 Queen Victoria Street, London;

"Termination Date" means the date on which the Employee’s employment with the Company is terminated, whether by notice, resignation, or otherwise.

2. Employment and Duties

2.1 The Company shall employ the Employee, and the Employee shall serve the Company, as Chief Technology Officer, with duties and responsibilities as determined by the Board from time to time.

2.2 The Employee shall devote his full working time, attention, and skill to the performance of his duties and shall act in the best interests of the Company and the Group at all times.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Employee shall receive an annual salary of £95,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme as may be established by the Board at its discretion, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Employee shall not, during or after the Termination Date, use or disclose any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of his duties or as compelled by law, provided that in the latter case, the Employee shall give the Company prior written notice of such disclosure.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out above, or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting by first-class mail, or immediately if delivered by hand or email.

18. Non-Compete

18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as principal, partner, shareholder, director, consultant, employee, or otherwise, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business which competes with the Business within the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company or any Group company operates, for a period of 18 months after termination of employment (the "Restricted Period").

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies regardless of whether the termination is voluntary, involuntary, or by expiry of notice.

18.3 The parties acknowledge that the duration of 18 months is reasonable having regard to the nature of the Business, the Employee’s role, and the legitimate interests of the Company and the Buyer in protecting goodwill and Confidential Information.

18.4 This clause shall be read and construed independently in respect of each geographical area and type of activity and, if any part is held to be unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

19.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
