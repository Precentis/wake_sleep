**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 12 March 2024  

**BETWEEN:**  

(1) Holloway Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15162973 whose registered office is at 130 King William Street, London (the "Customer"); and  

(2) Kestrel Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15577361 whose registered office is at 35 Moorgate, London (the "Supplier").  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:  
(a) "Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party;  
(b) "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure;  
(c) "Effective Date" means 12 March 2024;  
(d) "Services" means the software development and integration services to be provided by the Supplier under Schedule 1.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.  

**2. Provision of Services**  

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule 1 and in a professional and workmanlike manner.  

2.2 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to complete the Services within the timeframe specified in Schedule 1.  

**3. Payment**  

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule 2 within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.  

3.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling to the bank account notified by the Supplier in writing.  

3.3 Without limiting any other right or remedy of the Supplier, the Customer shall pay interest on overdue amounts at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis and being compounded quarterly.  

**4. Confidentiality**  

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not use such information except for the purposes of performing its obligations under this Agreement.  

4.2 The obligations under Clause 4.1 shall not apply to information that:  
(a) is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement; or  
(b) is independently developed by the receiving party without reference to the disclosing party’s Confidential Information.  

**5. Notices**  

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first class post, or sent by email to the address or email address set out above, or such other address as may be notified in writing.  

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received:  
(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first class post, 48 hours after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of receipt is received.  

**14. Termination**  

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party on 60 days’ written notice to the other party in the event of a material breach which is not remedied within the notice period.  

14.2 Either party may terminate this agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent.  

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  

15.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter.
