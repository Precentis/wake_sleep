**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **8 June 2026**

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Orwell Bioscience plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13771253**, whose registered office is at **32 Queen Victoria Street, London** (the **Seller**);

(2) **Kestrel Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **14777801**, whose registered office is at **166 Old Broad Street, London** (the **Buyer**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Kestrel Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15577361**, whose registered office is at **35 Moorgate, London** (the **Company**).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED as follows:**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Schedule 3;

“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“**Closing Date**” means **29 August 2026**, or such other date as the parties may agree in writing;

“**Parties**” means the Seller and the Buyer, and “**Party**” means either of them;

“**Purchase Price**” means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as provided in Clause 2.1;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of **$20,000,000** (the **Purchase Price**), to be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than **5:00 p.m. (London time)** on the Closing Date.

**3. CLOSING**

**3.1** On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates representing the Shares, endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed stock transfer forms;

(c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

(d) such corporate authorisations and consents as the Buyer may reasonably require to confirm the validity of the sale.

**3.2** On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT**

**4.1** The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or, where permitted, waiver in writing by both Parties, of the Conditions.

**4.2** The Parties shall use all reasonable endeavours to procure the fulfilment of the Conditions as soon as reasonably practicable.

**4.5** If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling **150 days after the date of this Agreement** (being **3 December 2026**), either Party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or remedies that may have accrued prior to termination.

**5. NOTICES**

**5.1** Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify to the other.

**5.2** Notices to the Seller shall be addressed to: 32 Queen Victoria Street, London.

**5.3** Notices to the Buyer shall be addressed to: 166 Old Broad Street, London.

**12. NON-COMPETITION**

**12.1** The Seller undertakes that, for a period of **5 years** from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on or be engaged or concerned in any business which materially competes with the business of the Company as conducted at the date of this Agreement, including but not limited to any supply of software solutions to Holloway Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15162973.

**13. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**13.1** Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

**14. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

**14.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

**14.2** The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
