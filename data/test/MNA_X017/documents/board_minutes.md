1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chairperson called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The board resolved to proceed with the agenda.

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 10 December 2025 were reviewed. The board approved the minutes as a true and accurate record.

3. DIRECTORS’ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST  
Each director confirmed that they had no new disclosable interests to declare under the Articles of Association and the Companies Act 2006. The board noted the absence of any conflicts of interest in respect of the matters under discussion.

4. REVIEW OF FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE  
The board received the unaudited management accounts for the quarter ending 31 December 2025. Revenue from operations during the period totalled £3.8 million, with a gross profit margin of 22%. Net profit for the quarter was £412,000. The board noted that performance remained in line with forecast, supported in part by continued supply to Oakhurst Technologies Ltd, which accounted for 37% of total revenue.

5. LEGAL MATTER – LETTER BEFORE ACTION FROM LOWTHER ENGINEERING LTD  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 11 January 2026 from Lowther Engineering Ltd, claiming approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 15 March 2024. The letter asserts that certain components delivered between June and September 2024 failed to meet agreed specifications, resulting in production delays at Lowther Engineering Ltd’s facility in Derby.  
Legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that liability remains unproven and that the claim is disputed on both factual and contractual grounds. The board instructed management to monitor developments closely and to provide a further update within 14 days. No provision has been recorded in the unaudited accounts in relation to this matter as of 31 December 2025.

6. PROPOSED SALE OF THE ENTIRE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL  
The board received a briefing on the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Medical Ltd by Brightwater Logistics plc, from Fenwick Holdings Ltd, the sole shareholder. The transaction is structured as a share purchase and is subject to completion on or about 15 January 2026.  
The board acknowledged the terms of the proposed transaction and confirmed that all necessary corporate consents have been or will be obtained in accordance with the Articles of Association and applicable law. The board further noted that no directors intend to resign immediately post-completion, and continuity of management is expected.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There being no other business, the Chairperson closed the meeting at 11:25 a.m.
