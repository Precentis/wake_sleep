# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**This Agreement is made on 2 January 2026**

**Between:**

(1) **Fenwick Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12438959**, whose registered office is at 92 Ludgate Hill, London (the **"Seller"**); and

(2) **Brightwater Logistics plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13132124**, whose registered office is at 72 Fleet Street, London (the **"Buyer"**).

**Recitals**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Medical Ltd (the **"Company"**), a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12026699**, whose registered office is at 79 Cannon Street, London.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**Operative Provisions**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

**"Material Adverse Change"** means any event, change or development that, individually or in the aggregate with any other events, changes or developments, has had a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company; provided that none of the following shall be deemed to constitute, and none of the following shall be taken into account in determining whether there has been, a Material Adverse Change: (a) changes in general economic or political conditions or financial markets in general; (b) changes that affect the industry in which the Company operates generally; or (c) pandemics.

**"Purchase Price"** means the amount specified in Section 2.1.

**"Conditions"** means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4 of this Agreement.

## 2. Purchase and Sale

**2.1** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$105,000,000** (the **"Purchase Price"**), payable in accordance with Section 2.2.

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

## 3. Closing

**3.1** The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the **"Closing"**) shall take place on **25 March 2026** (the **"Closing Date"**), or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all such instruments of transfer, share certificates and other documents as may be reasonably required to vest in the Buyer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances.

## 4. Conditions Precedent

**4.5** If the Conditions have not been satisfied or validly waived in writing by both parties by the date falling **120 days** after the date of this Agreement (being **1 February 2026** extended to **2 April 2026**), then either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or remedies which may have accrued prior to such termination.

## 5. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party. Notices to the Seller shall be addressed to 92 Ludgate Hill, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be addressed to 72 Fleet Street, London.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates, officers, employees and advisers shall not, disclose to any third party any information relating to the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

## 7. General

**7.1** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and arrangements.

**7.2** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
