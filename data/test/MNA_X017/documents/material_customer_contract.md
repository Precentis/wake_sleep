**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 12 January 2024  

**Between:**  

(1) Oakhurst Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12026699, whose registered office is at 79 Cannon Street, London (the “Supplier”); and  

(2) Oakhurst Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13110768, whose registered office is at 67 Moorgate, London (the “Customer”).  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  

“Affiliate” means, in relation to a party, a company which is from time to time a subsidiary or holding company of that party, or a subsidiary of that party’s holding company, in each case as defined in the Companies Act 2006;  

“Change of Control” means, in relation to a company, that such company: (a) sells, leases, disposes of, or otherwise transfers all or substantially all of its assets; or (b) undergoes any corporate transaction or series of transactions as a result of which more than 50% of the voting rights in the company are held by a person or group of persons different from those who held such rights immediately prior to such transaction(s);  

“Confidential Information” means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including business plans, pricing, financial information, technical data and customer details;  

“Effective Date” means 12 January 2024.  

**2. Supply of Services**  

2.1 The Supplier shall provide to the Customer the services described in Schedule A (the “Services”) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable care and skill, in a timely manner, and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.  

**3. Payment**  

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule B (the “Fees”) for the Services rendered under this Agreement.  

3.2 Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears and are payable within 30 days of the date of invoice.  

3.3 Without limiting any other right or remedy of the Supplier, the Customer shall pay interest on overdue amounts at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis from the due date until the date of actual payment, whether before or after judgment.  

**4. Term and Termination**  

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.  

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice to the other if the other party:  
(a) commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not capable of remedy; or  
(b) fails to remedy a material breach within 30 days of receiving written notice requiring remedy.  

**14. Miscellaneous**  

14.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as notified in writing from time to time.  

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this clause, Oakhurst Technologies Ltd is the counterparty.  

14.3 This Agreement contains the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements and understandings relating to its subject matter.  

14.4 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.  

**5. Confidentiality**  

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information disclosed by the other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party or use it for any purpose other than as permitted under this Agreement.  

5.2 The obligations under this clause shall not apply to information which: (a) is or comes into the public domain otherwise than through breach of this Agreement; or (b) is required to be disclosed by law or a regulatory authority.  

**6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.  

6.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).
