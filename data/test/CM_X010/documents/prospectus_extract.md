1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Prospectus:

"Company" means Whitlock Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11702320, whose registered office is at 38 Fleet Street, London.

"Underwriter" means Yarrow Holdings Securities, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14310235, whose registered office is at 147 Old Broad Street, London.

"Prospectus" means this offering document dated 1 November 2026 relating to the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company.

"Ordinary Shares" means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value in the capital of the Company.

"Material Customer" means Kestrel Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11327393, whose registered office is at 88 Cheapside, London.

"Revolving Facility" refers to the existing credit facility entered into by the Company with a syndicate of lenders, dated 14 March 2022, under which a maximum principal sum of $75,000,000 is available for borrowing.

"Closing Date" means the date on which settlement of the initial public offering occurs, being the second business day following the date of pricing of the offering.

2. Issuer and Offering

2.1 The issuer of the securities described in this Prospectus is Whitlock Technologies Ltd.

2.2 The date of this Prospectus is 1 November 2026.

2.3 The Company is offering to the public for the first time its Ordinary Shares in an initial public offering (the "Offering"), which will be underwritten by the Underwriter on a firm-commitment basis.

3. Capitalisation

3.1 As at the date of this Prospectus, the issued share capital of the Company comprises 45,200,000 Ordinary Shares.

3.2 Following completion of the Offering, the enlarged issued share capital of the Company will be disclosed in the final prospectus supplement, to be filed with the Financial Conduct Authority.

4. Use of Proceeds

4.1 The net proceeds from the Offering are estimated at $120,000,000, after deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and estimated offering expenses payable by the Company.

4.2 The Company intends to apply the net proceeds as follows:

(a) $54,000,000 to the repayment in full of the outstanding balance under the Revolving Facility;

(b) $36,000,000 to capital expenditure, including investment in research and development infrastructure and expansion of manufacturing capacity;

(c) $38,000,000 to general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions, and administrative costs associated with being a publicly listed company.

4.3 The allocation of proceeds set out above represents management’s current intentions and may be adjusted with prior notice to the Underwriter, provided such adjustments do not materially alter the overall allocation without regulatory approval.

5. Payment and Settlement

5.1 Payment for the Ordinary Shares shall be made in US dollars to the Company through the Underwriter’s designated clearing account on the Closing Date.

5.2 All payments shall be made by irrevocable wire transfer in immediately available funds.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile, or by prepaid first-class post to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to: General Counsel, Whitlock Technologies Ltd, 38 Fleet Street, London.

6.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be addressed to: Head of Capital Markets, Yarrow Holdings Securities, 147 Old Broad Street, London.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or any regulatory authority.

7.2 This obligation shall survive the Closing Date for a period of three years.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Prospectus and any matter arising from or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
