UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT  
between  
WHITLOCK TECHNOLOGIES LTD  
and  
YARROW HOLDINGS SECURITIES  

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated as of 12 October 2026 and made between:

(1) Whitlock Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11702320, whose registered office is at 38 Fleet Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Yarrow Holdings Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14310235, whose registered office is at 147 Old Broad Street, London (the "Underwriter").

Recitals:

A. The Company intends to issue and sell, and the Underwriter has agreed to underwrite, an initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company (the "Shares").

B. The Company proposes to list the Shares on a recognised stock exchange pursuant to the listing rules applicable thereto.

C. The Company has 45,700,000 ordinary shares in issue.

D. The Underwriter has agreed to purchase, and the Company has agreed to sell, 24,000,000 Shares (the "Firm Shares"), subject to the terms of this Agreement.

Clause 1 – Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"Closing Date" means the date on which settlement of the purchase of the Firm Shares and, if applicable, the exercise of the Over-allotment Option, takes place, being no later than three business days following the date of this Agreement;

"EEA" means the European Economic Area;

"Ordinary Shares" means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value in the capital of the Company;

"Over-allotment Option" has the meaning given in Clause 4;

"Shares" means the Firm Shares and, if applicable, any additional Shares issued pursuant to the exercise of the Over-allotment Option.

Clause 2 – Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company appoints the Underwriter to act as sole underwriter in relation to the offer and sale of the Shares.

2.2 The Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company at a price of £1.42 per share, subject to adjustment in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

Clause 3 – Closing

3.1 Payment for the Firm Shares shall be made by the Underwriter to the Company by irrevocable wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Company at least two business days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Delivery of the Firm Shares shall be effected through the clearing and settlement mechanisms of Euroclear and Clearstream, or such other system as may be agreed by the parties.

Clause 4 – Over-allotment Option

4.1 The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 4,800,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 24,000,000 Firm Shares, at the same price per share as the Firm Shares.

4.2 The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time prior to 30 days following the Closing Date, by written notice from the Underwriter to the Company.

Clause 5 – Representations and Warranties

5.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other that it has the corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.

Clause 6 – Notices

6.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the recipient.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to: 38 Fleet Street, London.

6.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be addressed to: 147 Old Broad Street, London.

Clause 7 – Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, except to the extent disclosure is required by law or by any regulatory authority.

Clause 8 – Entire Agreement

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings.

Clause 9 – Offerings

9.1 The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.

Clause 10 – Governing Law and Jurisdiction

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
