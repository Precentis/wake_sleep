# Underwriting Agreement (Extract)

**This Underwriting Agreement** (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set forth in the final executed counterpart, by and among:

**(1)** Lowther Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12698117, whose registered office is at 21 Cheapside, London (the "Company"); and

**(2)** Thornbury Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159869, whose registered office is at 151 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Underwriter").

Each of the Company and the Underwriter may be referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- **"Firm Shares"** means the 24,000,000 ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered by the Underwriter in connection with the initial public offering.
- **"Over-allotment Option"** has the meaning given in Clause 4.
- **"Pricing Date"** means 16 April 2027.
- **"Prospectus"** means the final prospectus prepared by the Company in connection with the offering, as approved by the Financial Conduct Authority.
- **"Shares"** means the ordinary shares of the Company, including both the Firm Shares and any shares issued pursuant to the exercise of the Over-allotment Option.

## 2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter to act as sole underwriter in connection with the initial public offering of the Firm Shares. The Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, the Firm Shares from the Company at the offering price determined in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

## 3. Payment and Settlement

The purchase price for the Firm Shares shall be paid to the Company by the Underwriter in cleared funds on the closing date, which shall be five business days following the Pricing Date, unless otherwise agreed in writing. The closing shall take place through the facilities of the UK Clearing and Settlement System. The Company shall deliver to the Underwriter, or its nominee, a share certificate or electronic confirmation evidencing ownership of the Firm Shares registered in the name of the Underwriter or its designee.

## 4. Over-allotment Option

The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 4,800,000 additional ordinary shares (the "Additional Shares"), representing 15% of the 24,000,000 Firm Shares. The Over-allotment Option may be exercised in whole or in part at any time on or before the earlier of (a) 30 calendar days following the Pricing Date and (b) the date on which the Underwriter notifies the Company in writing of its election not to exercise the Over-allotment Option. The purchase price for any Additional Shares shall be the same as the offering price per Firm Share. The Company shall, upon exercise of the Over-allotment Option, issue and deliver the Additional Shares in the same manner as the Firm Shares.

## 5. Representations and Warranties

Each Party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof that:

(a) It is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) It has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder;

(c) The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate or partnership action, as applicable;

(d) This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable insolvency, bankruptcy and similar laws affecting creditors’ rights generally.

## 6. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by registered post (first class, post free), or by electronic mail to the registered office of the relevant Party. Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered personally, on the day of delivery; if sent by post, on the second business day following posting; if sent by electronic mail, on the next business day following transmission, provided that confirmation of receipt is obtained.

## 7. Conditions to Closing

(b) As a condition to the obligations of the Underwriter hereunder, the Company shall deliver to the Underwriter a comfort letter from its independent auditors, dated no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date, confirming certain financial and accounting matters in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to the Underwriter.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and not to disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or regulation or as permitted by written consent of the disclosing Party.

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
