**LOCK-UP AGREEMENT**

This Lock-Up Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] 2026 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Lowther Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12698117, whose registered office is at 21 Cheapside, London (the “Company”);

(2) Thornbury Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159869, whose registered office is at 151 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Underwriter”); and

(3) the shareholder of the Company whose name and details are set out in Schedule A hereto (the “Shareholder”).

**1. Definitions**

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in New York.  
“Prospectus” means the final prospectus dated 15 March 2027, as approved by the Financial Conduct Authority, relating to the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the Company.  
“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company held by the Shareholder as at the date hereof, including any securities convertible into or exchangeable for such shares acquired during the Lock-Up Period (as defined below).

**2. Lock-Up Undertaking**

The Shareholder undertakes that it shall not, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, assign, hypothecate, transfer or otherwise dispose of (collectively, “Transfer”), or enter into any swap, hedge or other arrangement that transfers, in whole or in part, any of the economic consequences of ownership of, any Shares, during the period (the “Lock-Up Period”) commencing on the date of this Agreement and ending 365 days after the date of the Prospectus.

**3. Permitted Dispositions**

The restrictions in Clause 2 shall not apply to:

(a) transfers of Shares by gift to a family member or charitable organisation, provided that each transferee agrees in writing to be bound by the terms of this Agreement to the same extent as the Shareholder;

(b) transfers required by operation of law, including but not limited to a court order or inheritance;

(c) transfers to a wholly owned subsidiary or affiliated entity of the Shareholder, provided that such entity agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement; or

(d) transfers approved in writing by the Underwriter in its sole discretion.

Any transfer permitted under this Clause 3 shall be made only if the transferee delivers to the Company and the Underwriter a written agreement confirming acceptance of the lock-up obligations under this Agreement.

**4. Payment and Consideration**

No consideration shall be paid by the Company or the Underwriter to the Shareholder under this Agreement. The Shareholder acknowledges that the obligations under this Agreement are given in consideration of the Underwriter’s agreement to underwrite the initial public offering of the Company’s shares.
