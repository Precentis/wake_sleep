1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Comfort Letter (the “Letter”), unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Company” means Lowther Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12698117, with its registered office at 21 Cheapside, London;  
(b) “Underwriter” means Thornbury Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159869, with its registered office at 151 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
(c) “Arden Logistics plc” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10212919, with its registered office at 113 Cheapside, London;  
(d) “IPO” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, underwritten by the Underwriter;  
(e) “Cut-Off Date” means 4 April 2027.  
1.2 The headings in this Letter are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction. References to clauses are to clauses of this Letter.  

2. Scope of Procedures  
2.1 The Company has engaged [Independent Auditor], chartered accountants, to perform certain agreed-upon procedures in relation to specified financial information of the Company for the purpose of assisting the Underwriter in connection with the IPO.  
2.2 The procedures performed were limited to those agreed between the Company and the Underwriter and were conducted in accordance with International Standard on Related Services (ISRS) 4400, “Agreed-Upon Procedures Engagements.”  
2.3 The financial information subject to review included unaudited interim financial statements for the six-month period ended 30 September 2026, and unaudited monthly financial data for the period from 1 October 2026 to the Cut-Off Date.  

3. Limitation of Comfort  
3.1 This Letter is addressed solely to the Underwriter and is provided in connection with the IPO. It may not be reproduced, distributed or quoted in whole or in part to any other party without the prior written consent of the Company, except as required by law or regulation.  
3.2 The comfort provided herein is strictly limited to the procedures performed and is not a compilation, review or audit engagement. Accordingly, no opinion or assurance is expressed on the financial statements or any part thereof.  
3.3 Nothing in this Letter shall create any responsibility to update the information provided beyond the Cut-Off Date or to notify the recipient of any subsequent events.  

4. Payment  
4.1 The Company shall reimburse the Underwriter for all documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the IPO, including legal, printing, listing and regulatory fees, up to a maximum aggregate amount of £385,000.  
4.2 All payments due under this Letter shall be made in pounds sterling to a bank account designated in writing by the Underwriter within ten (10) business days of receipt of an invoice.  

5. Notices  
5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Letter shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class post (pre-paid), by courier or by email to the registered office of the respective party.  
5.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 21 Cheapside, London, Attn: Company Secretary.  
5.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 151 Queen Victoria Street, London, Attn: Head of Capital Markets.  
5.4 Notices sent by email shall be deemed received on the first business day following transmission, provided confirmation of delivery is obtained.  

6. Procedures and Cut-Off Date  
Our procedures covered the period up to 4 April 2027 (the Cut-Off Date). The procedures included verification of revenue recognition patterns, accounts receivable confirmations with key customers (including Arden Logistics plc), and analysis of material contracts for compliance with stated terms. Revenue attributable to Arden Logistics plc accounted for 37% of total revenue during the six-month period ended 30 September 2026. No adjustments arising from the procedures performed were found to materially affect the financial information reviewed.  

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received from the other party in connection with the IPO, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation or stock exchange rule.  
7.2 Confidential information shall not include information that is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Letter.  
7.3 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination or completion of the IPO for a period of three (3) years.  

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 This Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Letter.
