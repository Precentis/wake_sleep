1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Lowther Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12698117, with its registered office at 21 Cheapside, London;

“Underwriter” means Thornbury Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159869, with its registered office at 151 Queen Victoria Street, London;

“Arden Logistics plc” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10212919, with its registered office at 113 Cheapside, London;

“Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, as underwritten by the Underwriter;

“Prospectus” means this offering document, dated 16 April 2027, including all appendices, schedules and annexes hereto;

“Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value each in the share capital of the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;

“Subscription Amount” means the aggregate consideration payable by investors for the Ordinary Shares subscribed for under the Offering;

“Effective Date” shall be deemed to be the date on which the UK Financial Conduct Authority confirms that this Prospectus complies with the UK Prospectus Regulation;

“Confidential Information” means all non-public financial, operational, commercial and technical information disclosed by either party in connection with the Offering.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Offering Terms

2.1 The Company is offering 5,250,000 Ordinary Shares to the public, representing 37.5% of the Company’s issued share capital on a fully diluted basis immediately following completion of the Offering.

2.2 The Underwriter has entered into an underwriting agreement dated 10 March 2027 with the Company pursuant to which it has committed to purchase, on a firm commitment basis, any unsubscribed Ordinary Shares, up to the total Offering size.

2.3 The Subscription Amount shall be calculated at a price per Ordinary Share of £2.75, resulting in gross proceeds to the Company of £14,437,500 before deducting expenses.

2.4 The Company intends to apply for admission of the Ordinary Shares to the Standard Listing Segment of the Official List of the UK Listing Authority and to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market.

3. Use of Proceeds

3.1 The net proceeds of the Offering, after underwriting fees and estimated transaction expenses of £892,000, are expected to amount to £13,545,500.

3.2 The Company intends to allocate 63% of the net proceeds to fund the acquisition of two new cargo vessels, 22% to expand its dry-dock maintenance facilities at its operational base in Southampton, and 15% to general corporate purposes, including debt reduction and working capital.

4. Material Customer Relationship

4.1 The Company has a long-term service contract with Arden Logistics plc, under which it provides maritime freight transport services. During the financial year ended 31 December 2026, revenues from Arden Logistics plc amounted to £6.8 million, representing 41% of the Company’s total revenue.

4.2 The contract with Arden Logistics plc has a remaining term of 2.5 years and is subject to automatic renewal for successive 12-month periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days prior to expiry.

5. Payment Mechanics

5.1 Subscription monies must be paid in cleared funds to the designated escrow account established by the Underwriter at Barclays Bank plc, Account Number 77321904, Sort Code 20-22-30, by no later than 5.00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date, defined as 14 May 2027.

5.2 No subscription will be valid unless accompanied by the fully completed and signed Application Form, delivered in accordance with Section 7 (Notices).

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations;

(b) as required by law, regulation or competent authority;

(c) to the extent the information is already in the public domain through no fault of the recipient.

6.2 This obligation shall survive for a period of five years from the date of this Prospectus.

7.1 Any notice required under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Section 1.1.
