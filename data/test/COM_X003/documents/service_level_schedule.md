SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE  
to the Master Services Agreement between Galloway Industries Ltd and Eskdale Energy plc  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Service Level Schedule, the following terms have the meanings set out below:  
(a) “Availability” means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the Managed IT Services are accessible and operational, as measured by the Supplier’s monitoring systems, excluding Scheduled Maintenance and Force Majeure Events;  
(b) “Business Hours” means 08:00 to 18:00 London time, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England;  
(c) “Monthly Fee” means the sum of $40,000 payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of the Managed IT Services, due in advance on the first day of each calendar month;  
(d) “Scheduled Maintenance” means planned maintenance activities notified to the Customer in accordance with Clause 3, which may occur only between 18:00 and 06:00 Business Hours, and not exceeding four (4) hours in any calendar month;  
(e) “Service Credit” means a credit applied against future payments due under the Agreement, calculated as a percentage of the Monthly Fee, as specified in Clause 4.  

2. Payment  
2.1 The Customer shall pay the Monthly Fee of $40,000 in cleared funds to the bank account designated by the Supplier, without set-off or deduction of any kind, within five (5) Business Days of receipt of invoice.  
2.2 Invoices shall be issued on or before the first day of each calendar month and shall specify the period to which the services relate.  
2.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, from the due date until paid in full.  

3. Scheduled Maintenance and Downtime  
3.1 The Supplier may perform Scheduled Maintenance only upon no less than five (5) Business Days’ prior written notice to the Customer.  
3.2 No Scheduled Maintenance shall reduce Availability for more than four (4) hours in any given calendar month. Any unscheduled or unplanned downtime outside Scheduled Maintenance periods shall be included in the calculation of Availability.  

4. Service Levels and Credits  
4.1 The Supplier shall ensure that the Managed IT Services achieve a monthly Availability of at least 99.5%.  
4.2 If monthly Availability is below 99.5% but at least 98.0%, a Service Credit of 10% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.3 If monthly Availability is below 98.0%, a Service Credit of 25% of the Monthly Fee shall apply.  
4.4 Service Credits shall be claimed by written notice from the Customer to the Supplier within thirty (30) days of the end of the relevant calendar month.  
4.5 Service Credits shall be applied as a deduction against the next due invoice or, if the Agreement has terminated, refunded within sixty (60) days of receipt of a valid claim.  
4.6 The Service Credits specified in this Clause 4 are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet the Availability targets.  

5. Notices  
5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the recipient as set out below:  
(a) To Galloway Industries Ltd: 82 Cheapside, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10226454;  
(b) To Eskdale Energy plc: 43 Cannon Street, London, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15596237.  
5.2 Notices sent by email shall be effective upon transmission, provided confirmation of receipt is received.  

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Services, including system performance data, service levels, and financial terms.  
6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall not apply to information that is publicly available, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law or regulatory authority.  

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
7.1 This Service Level Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Schedule.
