EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 5 February 2026 between:

(1) Redcliffe Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12776184, whose registered office is at 103 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company");

(2) Saltash Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15953681, whose registered office is at 95 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Mr. Julian Hartwell (the "Founder").

RECITALS

A. The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about 5 February 2026 to acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from Kestrel Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11474363, whose registered office is at 104 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller").

B. In connection with the acquisition, the Founder is to continue in employment with the Company, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

"Confidential Information" means any information, in whatever form, relating to the business, finances, products, services, customers, suppliers or affairs of the Company, the Buyer, the Seller or any member of their respective groups, including but not limited to technical data, trade secrets, customer lists, pricing policies, operational methods, marketing plans and software.

"Effective Date" means 6 February 2026.

"Termination Date" means the date on which the Founder’s employment with the Company terminates for any reason.

2. Position and Duties

The Founder shall serve as Chief Innovation Officer of the Company and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are consistent with such role, as may be assigned from time to time by the board of directors of the Company.

3. Remuneration

The Founder shall receive an annual base salary of £142,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month, subject to statutory deductions.

4. Payment

All payments due under this Agreement shall be made by electronic transfer to the bank account nominated in writing by the Founder.

5. Notices

Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class post or email to the registered office of the respective party or such other address as may be notified in writing. Notices to the Founder shall be sent to the address most recently recorded in the Company’s personnel files.

6. Confidentiality

The Founder shall not, at any time during or after the Termination Date, disclose or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the proper performance of his duties, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the board of the Company.

7. Return of Property

Upon termination of employment, the Founder shall promptly return all documents, records, equipment and other property belonging to the Company or the Buyer.

8. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property created by the Founder in the course of his employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

18. Non-Compete

The Founder acknowledges that in the course of his employment he will have access to Confidential Information and will develop substantial goodwill in connection with the business of the Company. Accordingly, the Founder agrees that for a period of 18 months following the Termination Date, he shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any person, firm or company, engage, be employed, concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the Termination Date within the United Kingdom or any other territory in which the Company carries on business and in respect of which the Founder had material involvement during the 12 months prior to termination, including but not limited to supplying goods or services to Merrion Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15180858, whose registered office is at 7 Cannon Street, London.
