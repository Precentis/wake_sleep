ESCROW AGREEMENT  
(Extract)

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Saltash Technologies Ltd (company number 15953681), incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 95 Fleet Street, London.

“Company” means Redcliffe Industries Ltd (company number 12776184), incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 103 Ludgate Hill, London.

“Completion” means the completion of the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 31 January 2026.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £1,850,000, representing 8.5% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement, deposited by the Buyer into an escrow account upon Completion.

“Escrow Agent” means Lloyds Bank plc, acting through its Corporate Escrow Services division, appointed to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

“Seller” means Kestrel Energy plc (company number 11474363), incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 104 Cannon Street, London.

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement dated 31 January 2026 between the Seller and the Buyer for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

“Material Customer” means Merrion Retail Group plc (company number 15180858), incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 7 Cannon Street, London.

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time, and includes any subordinate legislation made under it.

2. Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain a dedicated interest-bearing escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) with Lloyds Bank plc under account number GB27LOYD12345678901234) into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer on or before Completion.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any charge, lien or set-off by the Escrow Agent or any third party.

3. Payment

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released by the Escrow Agent only upon receipt of joint written instructions signed by the Buyer and the Seller, or in accordance with a final determination by the courts of England and Wales as provided in Clause 6.

3.2 In the absence of joint instructions or a court determination, the Escrow Amount shall be released in full to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after Completion, unless a claim has been validly notified under Clause 5.2.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid recorded delivery, or sent by email to the most recent known address or email address of the relevant party.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: General Counsel, Saltash Technologies Ltd, 95 Fleet Street, London, EC4M 7FN, with a copy by email to legal@saltashtech.co.uk.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: Company Secretary, Kestrel Energy plc, 104 Cannon Street, London, EC4N 6DT, with a copy by email to escrow.notifications@kestrel-energy.co.uk.

4.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: Escrow Services Manager, Lloyds Bank plc, 25 Gresham Street, London, EC2V 7HN, with a copy by email to corporate.escrow@lloydsbank.co.uk.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer shall have the right to make a claim against the Escrow Amount for any breach of warranty, indemnity or other obligation under the Share Purchase Agreement which is not remedied by other means.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, and such notification shall specify in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the amount claimed (which shall not exceed the balance of the Escrow Amount), and the basis under the Share Purchase Agreement for such claim.

6. Dispute Resolution

6.1 In the event of a dispute between the Buyer and the Seller arising out of or in connection with a claim under this Agreement, the matter shall be referred to the courts of England and Wales, which shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party undertakes to the others that it shall not at any time hereafter disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the terms of this Agreement, the Escrow Amount, or the Share Purchase Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other parties.

7.2 This obligation shall survive the release of the Escrow Amount and the termination of this Agreement.

8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
