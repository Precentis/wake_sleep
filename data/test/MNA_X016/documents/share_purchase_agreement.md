**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 20 January 2026.

**Parties**  
(1) Kestrel Energy plc (company number 11474363), whose registered office is at 104 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller").  
(2) Saltash Technologies Ltd (company number 15953681), whose registered office is at 95 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").  
(3) Redcliffe Industries Ltd (company number 12776184), whose registered office is at 103 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

**Recitals**  
(A) The Seller owns the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**Operative Provisions**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement dated 20 January 2026;  
"Buyer" means Saltash Technologies Ltd;  
"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares;  
"Closing Date" means 31 March 2026;  
"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;  
"excess" means the amount by which the aggregate of all claims exceeds the Basket;  
"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares;  
"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;  
"Tax" includes any tax, duty, levy, impost, deduction, assessment, withholding or other governmental charge of any nature, and any fine, penalty, interest or addition thereto;  
"Warranties" means the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 2.

**2. Purchase and Sale**  
2.1 The Purchase Price is $65,000,000, to be satisfied in accordance with Clause 3.  
2.2 The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, all of the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, with full title guarantee, on the terms of this Agreement.

**3. Payment**  
3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 4.00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

**4. Conditions**  
4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions.  
4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement, being 21 May 2026, either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement with immediate effect.

**9. Limitations on Liability**  
9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of Warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $650,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.  
9.7 The Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of any claim under the Warranties within 30 days of becoming aware of the facts giving rise to such claim.

**10. Survival and Claims**  
10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity, and Tax) shall survive the Closing for 72 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**12. Post-Completion Undertakings**  
12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date, within the United Kingdom.

**13. General**  
13.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  
13.2 This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.  
13.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter.
