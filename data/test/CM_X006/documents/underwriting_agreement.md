UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set out below by and between:

(1) Penrith Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14811302 whose registered office is at 131 Fleet Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Glenrock Analytics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12151839 whose registered office is at 150 Bishopsgate, London (the "Underwriter").

Each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties".

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"Closing Date" means the date on which settlement of the offered shares occurs, as specified in the final prospectus.

"Offered Shares" means the ordinary shares of the Company offered in connection with the initial public offering described herein.

"Pricing Date" means 20 June 2027.

"Prospectus" means the base and final prospectus relating to the offering of the Offered Shares, as amended or supplemented from time to time in accordance with applicable law.

"Securities" means the Offered Shares and any other securities issued by the Company in connection with the offering.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as the sole underwriter for the initial public offering of the Offered Shares, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to act in such capacity, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

2.2 The Underwriter undertakes to use reasonable endeavours to place the Offered Shares with investors on the terms set out in the Prospectus and this Agreement.

3. Payment and Settlement

3.1 The purchase price for the Offered Shares shall be calculated at the price per share determined by agreement between the Company and the Underwriter on or prior to the Pricing Date.

3.2 Payment for the Offered Shares shall be made by the Underwriter to the Company on the Closing Date by irrevocable wire transfer in immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Company at least two business days prior to the Closing Date.

4. Representations and Warranties

4.1 Each Party represents and warrants to the other that, as of the date hereof and as of the Pricing Date:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement; and

(c) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms.

5. Undertakings

5.1 The Company undertakes to provide to the Underwriter all documents, financial statements and disclosures required for the preparation and filing of the Prospectus on a timely basis.

5.2 The Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, make any amendment or supplement to the Prospectus after the date of this Agreement that results in a material change to the offering terms.

7. Conditions Precedent

7.1 The obligations of the Underwriter under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following conditions:

(a) the Company shall have delivered a comfort letter from its auditors in form and substance satisfactory to the Underwriter;

(b) the comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date. The pricing date is 20 June 2027.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to perform its obligations hereunder.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
