# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Penrith Software Ltd

**Date of Meeting:** 25 May 2027  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 131 Fleet Street, London, EC4A 2BU  
**Chair:** Mr. Alistair Thornewell, Chief Executive Officer  
**Company Secretary:** Ms. Elena Mistry  

Present:  
- Mr. Alistair Thornewell (CEO)  
- Ms. Elena Mistry (Company Secretary)  
- Mr. Julian Hart (Non-Executive Director)  
- Ms. Priya Nair (Non-Executive Director)  
- Mr. Ben Carter (Finance Director)  

**1. Call to Order**  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. The quorum was confirmed as met under the Articles of Association of Penrith Software Ltd (the “Company”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14811302.

**2. Approval of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 18 April 2027 were circulated in advance. Upon motion by Mr. Hart and seconded by Ms. Nair, the minutes were approved as a true and accurate record.

**3. Director’s Declaration of Interest**  
Mr. Alistair Thornewell declared a personal interest in matters relating to Item 7 of the agenda. He confirmed that he would abstain from voting on the matter but would remain present to answer questions.

**4. Review of Draft Prospectus for IPO**  
The draft prospectus for the initial public offering of the Company’s ordinary shares was reviewed. The offering is being underwritten by Glenrock Analytics Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12151839. Legal counsel provided an update on compliance with the Disclosure and Transparency Rules and Prospectus Regulation. The board authorised the draft to proceed to final review by external advisers.

**5. Material Customer Contract Update**  
The board received a report on the ongoing commercial relationship with Foxley Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11856163. The existing service agreement, renewed on 1 March 2027, accounts for 32% of the Company’s annual revenue. The Finance Director confirmed that no material changes in credit risk or performance obligations have arisen.

**6. Financial Projections and Underwriting Agreement**  
The CFO presented unaudited financial projections for the fiscal year ending 31 December 2028. These projections have been shared with Glenrock Analytics Securities in connection with the underwriting agreement. The board affirmed the assumptions underpinning revenue growth of 18% year-on-year and EBITDA margins of 23%. The underwriting terms, including the issue price range and lock-up provisions, were noted for finalisation by 2 June 2027.

**7. Approval of Director Loan**  
The board considered a request from Ms. Mira Abernethy, a director of the Company, for a loan facility. Upon consideration, the board approved a loan of $4,000,000 from the Company to Mira Abernethy. The loan shall be interest-free, unsecured, and repayable on demand. Ms. Mira Abernethy confirmed she has no conflict of interest beyond her directorship. The Company Secretary was instructed to prepare the loan agreement in accordance with English law and file it at the registered office, 131 Fleet Street, London.

**8. Any Other Business**  
The Chair invited any other matters. There were none.

**9. Adjournment**  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.

**Signed:**  
Alistair Thornewell  
Chair of the Board  
Penrith Software Ltd  

**Date:** 27 May 2027
