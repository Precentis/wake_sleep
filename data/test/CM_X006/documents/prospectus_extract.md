# PROSPECTUS EXTRACT

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “Board” means the board of directors of Penrith Software Ltd;
- “Company” means Penrith Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14811302, with its registered office at 131 Fleet Street, London;
- “Directors’ Service Agreements” means the employment and service contracts entered into between the Company and its executive directors;
- “Foxley Logistics plc” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11856163, with its registered office at 62 Bishopsgate, London;
- “Glenrock Analytics Securities” or the “Underwriter” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12151839, with its registered office at 150 Bishopsgate, London;
- “IPO” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company;
- “Ordinary Shares” means the ordinary shares of £0.01 nominal value in the Company offered pursuant to this Prospectus;
- “Prospectus” means this prospectus dated 20 June 2027, including all appendices, schedules and annexes hereto.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of its provisions.

**2. Details of the Offer**

2.1 The Company is making an initial public offering of 15,000,000 Ordinary Shares, representing 22.7% of its issued share capital following admission to trading on the London Stock Exchange’s Main Market.

2.2 The offering is being conducted on a firm commitment underwritten basis, with Glenrock Analytics Securities acting as sole bookrunner and underwriter.

2.3 The price per Ordinary Share will be determined following the bookbuilding process, which shall conclude no later than 1 July 2027.

2.4 Admission to trading is expected to occur on or about 5 July 2027, subject to satisfaction of all regulatory conditions.

**3. Use of Proceeds**

3.1 Net proceeds from the IPO are estimated at £38.5 million after underwriting fees and offering expenses.

3.2 The Company intends to allocate the proceeds as follows: 45% for investment in research and development, 30% for expansion into continental European markets, 15% for repayment of existing short-term debt, and 10% for general corporate purposes.

**4. Related Party Transactions**

4.1 The Company confirms that there are no related party transactions requiring disclosure under applicable listing rules and the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules of the Financial Conduct Authority, other than the Directors’ Service Agreements.

4.2 Each of the Directors’ Service Agreements has been entered into on arm’s length terms and has been reviewed and approved by the Audit Committee of the Board.

**5. Material Customer Relationship**

5.1 As of the date of this Prospectus, Foxley Logistics plc accounts for 37.4% of the Company’s total revenue over the financial year ending 31 March 2027.

5.2 The commercial agreement with Foxley Logistics plc has an initial term of three years, commencing on 1 April 2025, and renews annually thereafter unless terminated by either party on not less than 180 days’ written notice.

5.3 The Board has assessed the concentration risk associated with this customer and has implemented a strategic diversification plan to reduce reliance on any single customer to below 25% of annual revenue by 31 March 2029.

**6. Payment and Settlement**

6.1 Payment for shares allocated under the IPO shall be made in cleared funds to the designated escrow account established by Glenrock Analytics Securities no later than two business days following allocation confirmation.

6.2 Settlement of transactions in Ordinary Shares will occur through Euroclear UK & International and will follow standard market practice on a T+2 basis.

**7. Notices**

7.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or transmitted by email to the registered office of the recipient or to such other address or electronic contact as may be notified in writing.

7.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to: 131 Fleet Street, London, Attn: Company Secretary.

7.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be addressed to: 150 Bishopsgate, London, Attn: Head of Equity Capital Markets.

**8. Confidentiality**

8.1 All parties receiving confidential information in connection with the IPO undertake to keep such information confidential and not to disclose it to any third party except as required by law, regulation, or stock exchange rule.

8.2 This obligation shall survive the closing of the offering for a period of three years.

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

9.1 This Prospectus and any disputes arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Prospectus.

**10. Date of Prospectus**

10.1 The date of this Prospectus is 20 June 2027.
