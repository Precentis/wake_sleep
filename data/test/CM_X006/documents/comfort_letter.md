1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Comfort Letter (the "Letter"), unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) "Company" means Penrith Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14811302 and registered office at 131 Fleet Street, London;

(b) "Underwriter" means Glenrock Analytics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12151839 and registered office at 150 Bishopsgate, London;

(c) "Offering" means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company underwritten by the Underwriter;

(d) "Cut-Off Date" means 8 June 2027;

(e) "Foxley Logistics plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11856163 and registered office at 62 Bishopsgate, London;

(f) All references to statutory provisions shall be construed as references to those provisions as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

2. Engagement and Scope

2.1 The Company has engaged the Underwriter in connection with the Offering. The Underwriter has requested this Letter in support of its due diligence inquiries.

2.2 The management of the Company has carried out procedures to provide comfort to the Underwriter regarding certain financial and operational matters of the Company.

3. Management Procedures and Findings

3.1 The Company confirms that it has conducted a review of its financial records, contracts, and material relationships in preparation for the Offering.

3.2 The Company acknowledges that the Underwriter has relied upon the accuracy and completeness of the information provided in connection with such review.

3.3 The Company confirms that, to the best of its knowledge and belief after due inquiry, the financial information provided to the Underwriter, including revenue, expenses, and material contracts, fairly presents the financial position of the Company as at the dates indicated and for the periods presented.

4. Material Contracts

4.1 The Company is party to a software licensing agreement with Foxley Logistics plc, entered into on 15 March 2025, pursuant to which Foxley Logistics plc accounts for 38.4% of the Company’s total revenue for the financial year ended 31 December 2026.

4.2 The Company confirms that, as of the Cut-Off Date, the agreement with Foxley Logistics plc remains in full force and effect and there are no ongoing disputes, notices of termination, or material breaches known to the Company.

4.3 The Company has not entered into any side agreements or oral understandings with Foxley Logistics plc that modify the terms of the written agreement in any material respect.

5. Financial Information

5.1 The unaudited interim financial statements for the six-month period ended 30 June 2026 were prepared on a basis consistent with the Company’s audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025.

5.2 There have been no material adverse changes in the financial position or results of operations of the Company from 30 June 2026 up to and including the Cut-Off Date, except as disclosed in writing to the Underwriter.

6. Cut-Off Date

6.1 Our procedures covered the period up to 8 June 2027 (the Cut-Off Date).

7. Payment and Expenses

7.1 The Company shall reimburse the Underwriter for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the Offering, including legal, accounting, and printing fees, up to a maximum aggregate amount of £750,000.

7.2 Any such reimbursement shall be made within 30 days of receipt of an invoice from the Underwriter.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Letter shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

8.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 131 Fleet Street, London.

8.3 Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 150 Bishopsgate, London.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Letter, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or a competent regulatory authority.

9.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination of this Letter for a period of five years.

10. General

10.1 This Letter is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Letter.
