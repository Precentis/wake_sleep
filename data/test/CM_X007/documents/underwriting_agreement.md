UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date hereof by and between:

(1) Calloway Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15492992, with its registered office at 88 Gresham Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Arden Technologies Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14303028, with its registered office at 96 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Underwriter").

Recitals

A. The Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter proposes to purchase, 16,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company (the "Firm Shares"), in connection with the initial public offering of the Company's ordinary shares.

B. The Company and the Underwriter have entered into this Agreement to set forth the terms and conditions governing the sale of the Firm Shares and any additional shares that may be purchased pursuant to the Over-allotment Option, as defined below.

C. The Company has 46,450,000 ordinary shares in issue.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations and warranties contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Over-allotment Option" means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter pursuant to Clause 4 to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares of the Company.

"Pricing Date" means 13 July 2027.

"Shares" means the Firm Shares and, if applicable, the additional shares purchased pursuant to the Over-allotment Option.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the offering of the Shares, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase the Firm Shares from the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

3. Purchase and Sale of Firm Shares

Subject to the terms and conditions herein, the Company agrees to issue, sell and deliver to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares, at a price per share to be agreed by the parties prior to the Pricing Date.

4. Over-allotment Option

The Company grants to the Underwriter an option (the "Over-allotment Option") to purchase up to 3,200,000 additional ordinary shares (the "Additional Shares"), representing 15% of the 16,000,000 Firm Shares. The Underwriter may exercise the Over-allotment Option in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time during the period beginning on the Pricing Date and ending 30 days thereafter, by giving written notice to the Company.

5. Closing

The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place at the offices of the Company's solicitors, or at such other location as the parties may agree, on the second business day following the Pricing Date, or at such other time and date as the parties may determine.

6. Representations and Warranties

Each party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof and as of the Closing that it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder, and that this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

7. Conditions Precedent

(a) The obligations of the parties hereunder are subject to the delivery by the Company’s auditors of a comfort letter in form and substance satisfactory to the Underwriter.

(b) The comfort letter cut-off date shall be no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date. The Pricing Date is 13 July 2027.

8. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile or email, or sent by registered post, first class, to the addresses set out above.

9. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except as required by law or regulation.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
