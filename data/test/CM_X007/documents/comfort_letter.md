1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Comfort Letter, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Company” means Calloway Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15492992, whose registered office is at 88 Gresham Street, London.

“Underwriter” means Arden Technologies Securities, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14303028, whose registered office is at 96 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Hartwell Software Ltd” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15712438, whose registered office is at 138 Cheapside, London.

“Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, underwritten by the Underwriter.

“Cut-off Date” means 1 July 2027.

“Procedures” means the review procedures conducted by the undersigned with respect to the financial information of the Company provided in connection with the Offering.

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or extended from time to time, and shall include any subordinate legislation made under it.

1.3 The headings in this Comfort Letter are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Scope of Procedures

2.1 The undersigned has performed limited review procedures with respect to the unaudited financial statements and related disclosures of the Company for the periods ending on or prior to the Cut-off Date, in connection with the Underwriter’s reliance thereon in relation to the Offering.

2.2 The Procedures were conducted in accordance with professional standards applicable to comfort letters issued in connection with securities offerings under English law and were limited in scope to matters relevant to the financial condition and performance of the Company.

2.3 The Procedures did not include a full audit, nor were they designed to form a basis for any assurance on the financial statements of the Company. Accordingly, no opinion or assurance is expressed or implied herein as to the financial statements or the financial position of the Company.

3. Findings

3.1 Based on the Procedures performed, nothing has come to the attention of the undersigned that causes it to believe that:

(a) the financial information of the Company included in the Offering documents contains any material misstatement;

(b) the Company has not maintained records in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements; or

(c) there has been any significant change in the financial condition or results of operations of the Company as at the Cut-off Date that has not been disclosed in the Offering documents.

3.2 The Company is contractually bound to Hartwell Software Ltd under a software licensing agreement dated 14 March 2026, whereby Hartwell Software Ltd provides critical operational software to the Company. The annual fee under this agreement is £375,000, payable quarterly in arrears, and the term extends for a period of three years with an automatic renewal provision unless terminated with 120 days’ written notice by either party.

4. Payment

4.1 All payments due from the Company to third parties in connection with the Offering, including fees payable to the Underwriter, are to be settled in accordance with the terms of the underwriting agreement dated 18 May 2027, which provides for a commission of 4.2% of the gross proceeds raised.

4.2 The Company confirms that no payment in connection with the Offering is contingent upon any matter that has not been fully disclosed to the Underwriter.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Comfort Letter shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the Company or the Underwriter, respectively.

5.2 Notices sent by post shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting. Notices sent by email shall be deemed received at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure is reported.

6. Limitation of Procedures

Our procedures covered the period up to 1 July 2027 (the Cut-off Date). No procedures were performed subsequent to that date, and accordingly this letter does not reflect any events or transactions occurring after the Cut-off Date.

7.2 The contents of this Comfort Letter shall remain confidential except to the extent required by law or regulatory authority.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Comfort Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Comfort Letter.
