ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Ingleby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11818106, whose registered office is at 63 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller");

(2) Brightwater Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10128007, whose registered office is at 74 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a licensed financial institution, as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

   "Closing Date" means 25 December 2026.

   "Company" means Yarrow Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14698329, whose registered office is at 124 Cannon Street, London.

   "Confidential Information" means all information, whether written or oral, disclosed by one Party to another in connection with this transaction, including financial, operational, commercial and technical data.

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £12,750,000 (twelve million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] 2026.

   "Material Customer" means Penrith Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11123883, whose registered office is at 3 Moorgate, London.

   "Pending Claim" means any claim arising under the Share Purchase Agreement that has been validly notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller prior to the release date specified in Clause 4.1, and which remains unresolved as of that date.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated account (the "Escrow Account") on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the instructions of the Parties as set forth herein.

3. Payment and Deposit

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account and shall not release any portion thereof except in accordance with Clause 4 or upon receipt of joint written instructions from the Buyer and the Seller.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain held in escrow until such claim is finally resolved by agreement or determination, at which point the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed portion to the Seller and, upon resolution, disburse the balance in accordance with the outcome of such claim.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall have no obligation to investigate or adjudicate any claim and shall act solely on the basis of written instructions from the Parties or a final determination.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as notified in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 63 Bishopsgate, London.  
    Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 74 Gresham Street, London.  
    Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: [●].

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party undertakes to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information and not to disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive the release of the Escrow Amount and shall continue for a period of five years from the Effective Date.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
