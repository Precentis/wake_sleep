SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 19 October 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Ingleby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11818106, whose registered office is at 63 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) Brightwater Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10128007, whose registered office is at 74 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Yarrow Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14698329, whose registered office is at 124 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Closing Date" means 25 December 2026.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $220,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller in full, in cleared funds, by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller may notify to the Buyer in writing at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date, on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS TO COMPLETION

4.5 If the Conditions (as defined in the Disclosure Letter dated the date of this Agreement) are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 17 January 2027), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided that neither party has materially breached its obligations under this Agreement prior to such date.

5. CLOSING

5.1 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Seller’s solicitors, or such other place as the parties may agree in writing, at 10.00 a.m. on the Closing Date.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing, addressed to the relevant party at its registered office or such other address as it may notify in writing, and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email (with confirmation of receipt), and shall be deemed to have been received at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, 24 hours after transmission if sent by email, or two (2) Business Days after posting if sent by first-class pre-paid letter.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

8. TAX COVENANT

8.1 The Seller hereby covenants to the Buyer that it shall indemnify the Buyer and the Company against any Tax Liability arising from or in connection with any act, omission, or transaction of the Company on or before the Closing Date.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for indemnification under this Agreement unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $1,100,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which such aggregate claims exceed $1,100,000, and then only for the excess over $1,100,000.

10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1 The General Warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title to shares, capacity to enter into this Agreement, and Tax matters) shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim for breach of a warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
