1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Data Processing Agreement (the “DPA”), the following terms shall have the meanings set out below unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) “Applicable Data Protection Law” means the Data Protection Act 2018, the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), and any other applicable legislation relating to the processing of personal data in the United Kingdom from time to time in force;

(b) “Customer Personal Data” means any personal data disclosed by Northgate Holdings Ltd to Elstree Systems Ltd for the purposes of providing the Services under the Master Services Agreement, including but not limited to names, contact details, job titles, and system access logs of employees and contractors of Northgate Holdings Ltd and its client Delamere Energy plc;

(c) “Processing” shall have the meaning given in Article 4(2) of the UK GDPR and includes any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means;

(d) “Services” means the managed IT services provided by Elstree Systems Ltd to Northgate Holdings Ltd under the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 13 January 2027.

1.2 The headings in this DPA are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment and Scope

2.1 Northgate Holdings Ltd hereby appoints Elstree Systems Ltd as a data processor in respect of Customer Personal Data for the sole purpose of providing the Services in accordance with the terms of the Master Services Agreement and this DPA.

2.2 Elstree Systems Ltd shall not process Customer Personal Data other than on the documented instructions of Northgate Holdings Ltd, unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Law, in which case Elstree Systems Ltd shall inform Northgate Holdings Ltd of such legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 Elstree Systems Ltd shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including but not limited to encryption, access controls, and regular testing of security measures.

3.2 Elstree Systems Ltd shall only permit authorised personnel to process Customer Personal Data and shall ensure such personnel are subject to a duty of confidentiality.

3.3 Elstree Systems Ltd shall assist Northgate Holdings Ltd in responding to data subject rights requests and in fulfilling its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law, taking into account the nature of the processing.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 Northgate Holdings Ltd hereby grants prior general written authorisation for Elstree Systems Ltd to engage sub-processors in connection with the provision of the Services.

4.2 Elstree Systems Ltd shall enter into a written agreement with each sub-processor containing data protection obligations no less stringent than those set out in this DPA, and shall remain fully liable to Northgate Holdings Ltd for the performance of the sub-processor’s obligations.

5. Data Subject Rights and Cooperation

5.1 Elstree Systems Ltd shall promptly notify Northgate Holdings Ltd of any request from a data subject to exercise their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law.

5.2 Elstree Systems Ltd shall provide reasonable assistance to Northgate Holdings Ltd in responding to such requests.

6. Compliance with Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with the Services and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

8. Notices

8.1 Any notice under this DPA shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the receiving party as set out below:

(a) Northgate Holdings Ltd: 46 King William Street, London, company number 13330697;

(b) Elstree Systems Ltd: 79 Fleet Street, London, company number 12132209.

8.2 Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after transmission if sent by first-class pre-paid post.

9. Miscellaneous

9.1 This DPA forms part of the Master Services Agreement between Northgate Holdings Ltd and Elstree Systems Ltd and may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

9.2 This DPA is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
