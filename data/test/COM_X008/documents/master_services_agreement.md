MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 12 January 2027

Between:

(1) Northgate Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13330697 whose registered office is at 46 King William Street, London (the "Customer"); and

(2) Elstree Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12132209 whose registered office is at 79 Fleet Street, London (the "Supplier").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England and is a day on which banks are open for business in London;

"Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one Party to the other, either directly or indirectly, in connection with this Agreement;

"Deliverable" means any report, software, documentation or service output provided by the Supplier under this Agreement;

"Services" means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule A.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Provision of Services

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and with reasonable skill and care.

2.2 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to meet all performance deadlines for the Deliverables, as set out in the applicable project plan.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first above written and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 3 June 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of notice being given.

4. Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services in accordance with the payment schedule set out in Schedule B.

4.2 All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party shall keep the other Party’s Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three years.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address set out in this Agreement.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received at the time of delivery if delivered personally or by email (with acknowledgment of receipt), or 48 hours after posting if sent by post.

11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 Subject to Clause 11.2, each party's total liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall be capped at an aggregate of $1,000,000.

11.2 The cap in Clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or to liability for fraud.

19. Assignment

19.1 Neither Party may assign, transfer, charge or otherwise deal with this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the other Party.

19.2 A change of control of a Party, whether by way of merger, acquisition, or otherwise, shall be deemed an assignment for the purposes of this Clause 19 and shall require the prior written consent of the other Party.

Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
