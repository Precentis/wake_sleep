# Service Level Schedule

This Service Level Schedule ("Schedule") is entered into pursuant to the Master Services Agreement dated prior to 13 January 2027 (the "Agreement") between Northgate Holdings Ltd ("Customer") and Elstree Systems Ltd ("Supplier"). Capitalised terms used but not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.

**1. Definitions**

1.1. "Monthly Fee" means the recurring fee of $40,000 payable by the Customer to the Supplier for the provision of managed IT services, as specified in the Agreement.

1.2. "Service Credit" means a credit applied to the Customer’s next invoice in the event the Supplier fails to meet the service levels specified in this Schedule.

1.3. "Service Hours" means 24:00 hours per day, 7 days per week, 365 days per year, excluding scheduled maintenance windows previously notified by the Supplier in accordance with Clause 3.

1.4. "Availability" means the percentage of time during a calendar month that the managed IT services are accessible and functional to the Customer and its authorised users, excluding any downtime caused by Force Majeure events or actions or omissions of the Customer or third parties not under the Supplier’s control.

**2. Service Commitments**

2.1. The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Availability of the managed IT services is not less than 99.5% in any calendar month.

2.2. Availability shall be measured using automated monitoring tools operated by the Supplier, with logs retained for a minimum of twelve (12) months and made available to the Customer upon request.

**3. Scheduled Maintenance**

3.1. The Supplier may perform scheduled maintenance during which the services may be temporarily unavailable, provided that such maintenance is carried out outside critical business hours for the Customer where reasonably practicable.

3.2. The Supplier shall provide written notice of any scheduled maintenance requiring more than two (2) hours of downtime at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance, unless exigent circumstances require shorter notice.

**4. Service Credits**

4.1. If the Availability of the services in a calendar month falls below 99.5% but is at least 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 10% of the Monthly Fee.

4.2. If the Availability in a calendar month falls below 98.0%, the Supplier shall issue a Service Credit equal to 25% of the Monthly Fee.

4.3. Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy available to the Customer for failure to meet the Availability commitments in this Schedule, and shall be applied automatically to the following month’s invoice unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4.4. No Service Credit shall accrue if the failure to meet Availability results from factors excluded under Clause 1.4.

**5. Payment**

5.1. The Monthly Fee shall be invoiced in advance on the first day of each calendar month and shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

5.2. All payments shall be made in US dollars to the bank account designated by the Supplier in writing.

**6. Notices**

6.1. Any notice required under this Schedule shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as set out below:

- Northgate Holdings Ltd: 46 King William Street, London, company number 13330697;
- Elstree Systems Ltd: 79 Fleet Street, London, company number 12132209.

6.2. Notices may be delivered by first-class post, courier, or email, and shall be deemed received on the second business day after posting, on the next business day if sent by courier, or immediately if sent by email with confirmed receipt.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1. Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received from the other party in connection with this Schedule, including service performance data, and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law.

7.2. This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1. This Schedule shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Schedule.
