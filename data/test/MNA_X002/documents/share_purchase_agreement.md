**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **13 February 2027**

Between:  
(1) **Northgate Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11697322**, whose registered office is at **14 King William Street, London** (the “**Seller**”); and  
(2) **Calloway Logistics plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **14546196**, whose registered office is at **118 Cannon Street, London** (the “**Buyer**”).

**Background**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Oakhurst Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11710719**, whose registered office is at **168 Gresham Street, London** (the “**Company**”).  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.  

**Operative Provisions**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;  
“**Closing Date**” means **22 April 2027**;  
“**Confidential Information**” means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement;  
“**Parties**” means the Seller and the Buyer;  
“**Purchase Price**” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;  
“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Sale and Purchase**  
2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$225,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in immediately available funds to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Closing**  
3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at 10:00 a.m. (London time) at the offices of the Buyer, or at such other time and place as the Parties may agree in writing.  
3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares; (b) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer; and (c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to effect the transfer of the Shares.  
3.3 At Closing, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

**4. Notices**  
4.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the address or email address set out for the relevant Party above, or such other address as a Party may notify in writing.  
4.2 A notice is deemed effective when delivered if by hand, two (2) Business Days after posting if by post, or upon receipt of a read receipt if by email.

**5. Confidentiality**  
5.1 Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all Confidential Information and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.  
5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years.

**9. Limitation of Liability**  
9.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any and all claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **10% of the Purchase Price**, being **$27,000,000**. The Purchase Price is **$225,000,000** (as set out in Section 2.1).

**12. Non-Compete**  
12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of **4 years** from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**13. General**  
13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions.  
13.2 This Agreement is governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales. The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  
13.3 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
