1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Calloway Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14546196, whose registered office is at 118 Cannon Street, London.

“Company” means Oakhurst Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11710719, whose registered office is at 168 Gresham Street, London.

“Seller” means Northgate Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11697322, whose registered office is at 14 King William Street, London.

“Founder” means the individual appointed as Founder and Chief Technical Officer of the Company prior to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer from the Seller.

“Yarrow Engineering Ltd” means the customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12549892, whose registered office is at 63 Old Broad Street, London, and which accounts for 38% of the Company’s annual revenue as at 28 February 2027.

2. Employment

2.1 The Founder shall be employed by the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, commencing on 1 March 2027.

2.2 The Founder’s principal place of work shall be the Company’s registered office, 168 Gresham Street, London, although the Founder may be required to travel as reasonably necessary in connection with the performance of duties.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £147,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme operated by the Company or the Buyer, subject to the terms of such scheme and the discretion of the Board.

4. Working Hours and Leave

4.1 The Founder’s normal working hours shall be 37.5 per week, worked in accordance with the Company’s standard working patterns.

4.2 The Founder shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to statutory holidays, to be taken at times agreed with the Board.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 During the term of employment and thereafter, the Founder shall not, without prior written consent of the Board, disclose to any person, or use for personal benefit or the benefit of any third party, any Confidential Information.

5.2 For the purposes of this clause, “Confidential Information” includes all non-public information relating to the Company, the Buyer, the Seller, Yarrow Engineering Ltd, or any of their respective businesses, operations, clients, suppliers, financial affairs, or technical data.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All intellectual property created or developed by the Founder in the course of employment shall be owned exclusively by the Company.

6.2 The Founder shall execute all documents and take all steps reasonably required by the Company to perfect such ownership.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the recipient’s registered office or last known address.

7.2 Notices to the Founder shall be sent to the address recorded in the Company’s personnel file.

18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that the goodwill of the Company includes its relationships with key customers, including Yarrow Engineering Ltd, and that protection of such relationships is legitimate and reasonable.

18.2 Accordingly, the Founder undertakes that during the period of 12 months immediately following the termination of employment for any reason, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with or on behalf of any other person, engage or be concerned in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of termination, within the United Kingdom.

18.3 This restriction applies specifically to any business activity serving or seeking to serve any customer of the Company with whom the Founder had material contact during the 12 months prior to termination, including Yarrow Engineering Ltd.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
