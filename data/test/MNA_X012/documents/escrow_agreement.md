ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Pemberton Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13361573 whose registered office is at 112 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Pemberton Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10543168 whose registered office is at 95 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [●], as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties to this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 4 May 2027.

"Company" means Quayside Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15213572 whose registered office is at 135 King William Street, London.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £3,750,000 (three million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) representing a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

"Material Customer" means Saltash Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11159549 whose registered office is at 131 Old Broad Street, London.

"Parties" means the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent, and "Party" means any one of them.

"Pending Claim" means any claim for breach of warranty, indemnity or other obligation notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to the Release Date, which claim has not been fully resolved, withdrawn or settled by such date.

"Release Date" means the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date.

"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement dated on or about the Execution Date between the Buyer and the Seller for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") with a recognised financial institution in London, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer within three (3) Business Days of the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any lien, set-off or counterclaim by any Party or third party.

3. Payment and Interest

3.1 Any interest earned on the Escrow Amount shall accrue for the benefit of the Seller and shall be released together with the Escrow Amount (or the portion thereof) in accordance with Clause 4.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall remit any interest to the Seller at the same time as releasing the relevant portion of the Escrow Amount.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount (less any amount attributable to unresolved Pending Claims) to the Seller by wire transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than three (3) Business Days following the Release Date.

4.3 In the event that a Pending Claim exists on the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall retain in the Escrow Account an amount equal to the full value of such claim (or such portion thereof as remains unresolved) until such time as the claim is finally resolved, withdrawn or settled, at which time the Escrow Agent shall release the unclaimed balance (if any) to the Seller.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to 112 Gresham Street, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 95 Queen Victoria Street, London. Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address specified in the signature block below.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received or obtained pursuant to this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Parties, except as required by law or regulation.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
