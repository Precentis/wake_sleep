SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 22 February 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Pemberton Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10543168, whose registered office is at 95 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Seller”); and

(2) Pemberton Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13361573, whose registered office is at 112 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”).

Each a “Party” and together the “Parties”).

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15213572, whose registered office is at 135 King William Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing Date” means 4 May 2027;

“Conditions” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“Material Adverse Change” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $105,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), which shall be satisfied in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in full and in cleared funds on the Closing Date, by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the fulfilment, or valid waiver by the Buyer, of each of the Conditions.

4.2 The Conditions include, but are not limited to, the accuracy of the representations and warranties set out in this Agreement, the delivery of all necessary corporate and shareholder approvals, and the absence of any Material Adverse Change.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 24 May 2027), either Party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued prior to termination.

5. CLOSING

5.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer, or at such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

5.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms, and such other documents as are necessary to effect the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

12. RESTRICTIONS ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that for a period of five (5) years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, operate, manage, control, engage in or be concerned with any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement. This restriction applies for the duration of 5 years and shall be enforceable by the Buyer as the acquirer of the Company.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party shall treat as confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law or by any regulatory authority.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
