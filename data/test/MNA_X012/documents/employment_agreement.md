1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"the Business" means the business of research, development, and commercialisation of biopharmaceutical technologies currently carried out by Quayside Bioscience plc;

"the Company" means Quayside Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15213572, whose registered office is at 135 King William Street, London;

"the Effective Date" means 15 March 2027;

"Founder" means Dr. Eleanor Pemberton, employed in the capacity of Chief Scientific Officer;

"the Parties" means collectively the Company and the Founder;

"the Restricted Period" means the period of 12 months immediately following the termination of the Founder’s employment with the Company, regardless of the reason for such termination;

"Territory" means the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company agrees to employ the Founder, and the Founder accepts employment, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, with effect from the Effective Date.

3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 The Founder shall serve as Chief Scientific Officer and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position, and as may be lawfully assigned by the Board of Directors of the Company from time to time.

4. Remuneration

4.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £115,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme as may be established by the Board, subject to the terms and conditions thereof, with a target bonus of 45% of base salary.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Founder shall not, at any time during or after the termination of employment, directly or indirectly disclose or use for her own benefit or the benefit of any third party any Confidential Information.

5.2 For the purposes of this clause, "Confidential Information" includes all technical, commercial, financial and operational information relating to the Company, its affiliates, customers (including but not limited to Saltash Industries Ltd, company number 11159549), suppliers, research partners, and business strategies, whether recorded in writing or otherwise.

5.3 This obligation shall survive the termination of employment for a period of five years.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All intellectual property created by the Founder during the course of her employment, whether solely or jointly with others, shall be the absolute property of the Company.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by first-class pre-paid post to the registered office of the Company at 135 King William Street, London, or to such other address as may be notified in writing by either Party.

8. Entire Agreement

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and arrangements relating to the subject matter hereof.

18. Non-Compete

18.1 During the Restricted Period, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in partnership with any person, or as a shareholder, director, employee, consultant or agent of any company or other entity, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business within the Territory.

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies irrespective of whether the termination is with or without cause, and includes any entity which provides biopharmaceutical research and development services to customers such as Saltash Industries Ltd.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
