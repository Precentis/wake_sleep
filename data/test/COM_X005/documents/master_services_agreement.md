**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- **"Agreement"** means this master services agreement between Hartwell Energy plc and Eskdale Engineering Ltd, including all schedules, appendices, and amendments thereto.
- **"Business Day"** means any day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England, and on which banks are open for general business in London.
- **"Confidential Information"** means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one party to the other, whether before, on or after the effective date of this Agreement, that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.
- **"Services"** means the managed IT services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule A.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

**2. Parties**

This Agreement is entered into on a date prior to 10 November 2026 between:

(a) Hartwell Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14199323, whose registered office is at 180 Ludgate Hill, London (the **Customer**); and

(b) Eskdale Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11008735, whose registered office is at 103 King William Street, London (the **Supplier**).

**3. Term and Termination**

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.2 This Agreement expires on 21 April 2027 and renews automatically for successive 2-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before expiry. Notices are effective only on Business Days (Monday to Friday).

3.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by notice in writing to the other if the other party:

(a) commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of being notified in writing of such breach;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or has a receiver or administrator appointed; or

(c) ceases to carry on business.

**11. Limitation of Liability**

11.1 Subject to clause 11.2, each party's aggregate liability to the other under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall be limited to $500,000.

11.2 The limitations in clause 11.1 shall not apply to liability arising from:

(a) death or personal injury caused by the negligence of a party;

(b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; or

(c) any other liability which cannot be limited under applicable law.

**19. Assignment**

19.1 Neither party may assign, transfer, sub-license or otherwise deal with all or any part of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

19.2 A change of control of a party (within the meaning of the Companies Act 2006) is deemed an assignment and shall require the prior written consent of the other party.

19.3 Any attempted assignment, transfer or delegation in breach of this clause is deemed an assignment without consent and shall be void.

**20. Confidentiality**

20.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except:

(a) to its employees, officers, advisors or contractors who need to know such information for the purposes of performing its obligations under this Agreement and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations;

(b) as required by law, regulation or any competent authority; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

20.2 The obligations in this clause shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**21. Notices**

21.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the receiving party.

21.2 Notices shall be deemed received:

(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class post, 48 hours after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of receipt is obtained.

21.3 For the purposes of notices:

(a) the Customer’s address is 180 Ludgate Hill, London;

(b) the Supplier’s address is 103 King William Street, London.

**22. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

22.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

22.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
