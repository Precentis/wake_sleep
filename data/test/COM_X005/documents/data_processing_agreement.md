1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Data Processing Agreement (the “Agreement”), the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) “Applicable Data Protection Law” means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended, extended or re-enacted from time to time;

(b) “Customer Personal Data” means any personal data of Hartwell Energy plc (the “Customer”) that is disclosed to, or processed by, Eskdale Engineering Ltd (the “Supplier”) pursuant to or in connection with the Master Services Agreement between the parties dated on or prior to 10 November 2026;

(c) “Processing” shall have the meaning given to it in the UK GDPR and includes any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means;

(d) “Security Breach” means any unauthorised or accidental access, loss, destruction, alteration, disclosure or acquisition of Customer Personal Data.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment and Scope

2.1 The Customer hereby appoints the Supplier to process Customer Personal Data solely for the purposes of delivering managed IT services under the Master Services Agreement entered into between the parties prior to 10 November 2026.

2.2 The nature and purpose of the Processing are the provision, monitoring, maintenance and support of managed IT infrastructure and services as specified in the Master Services Agreement.

2.3 The duration of the Processing shall correspond to the term of the Master Services Agreement and any applicable extension periods, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

3. Obligations of the Supplier

3.1 The Supplier shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against a Security Breach, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, as well as the risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

3.2 The Supplier shall ensure that any person it authorises to process Customer Personal Data is subject to a duty of confidentiality.

3.3 The Supplier shall, at the Customer’s written request, provide information enabling the Customer to verify compliance with its obligations under this Agreement.

4. Sub-processing

4.1 The Supplier shall not engage any sub-processor in relation to Customer Personal Data without the prior written authorisation of the Customer.

4.2 Notwithstanding any prior authorisation, the Supplier shall remain fully liable for the acts and omissions of any sub-processor.

5. Data Subject Rights and Assistance

5.1 The Supplier shall assist the Customer in fulfilling its obligations to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Law, to the extent such requests relate to Customer Personal Data processed by the Supplier.

5.2 The Supplier shall respond to any such requests only as directed by the Customer and shall not communicate directly with data subjects unless legally required to do so.

6. Compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law

6.1 The Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

6.2 In particular, the Supplier shall ensure that all Processing is lawful, fair and transparent, and that it maintains records of Processing activities as required under Article 30 of the UK GDPR.

7. Security of Processing

7.1 The Supplier shall implement and maintain technical and organisational security measures including, but not limited to, encryption of data in transit, access controls, regular testing of systems, and staff training programmes, designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

7.2 In the event of a Security Breach, the Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay and in any event no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the incident.

8. International Transfers

8.1 The Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom unless such transfer is made in compliance with the transfer mechanisms approved under Applicable Data Protection Law.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 Each party shall treat all information received from the other party as confidential and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
