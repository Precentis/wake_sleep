SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 10 April 2026.

Parties:

(1) Galloway Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13179048, whose registered office is at 18 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Hartwell Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14434666, whose registered office is at 126 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”).

Background:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Delamere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12077880, whose registered office is at 175 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

“Closing” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

“Closing Date” means 12 July 2026;

“Closing Working Capital” means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1;

“EBITDA” means earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies consistently applied by the Company as at the date of the latest audited accounts;

“FY2026” means the financial year of the Company ending 31 December 2026;

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties set out in Clause 7.1 (Title), Clause 7.2 (Capacity) and Clause 7.10 (Tax);

“General Warranties” means the warranties set out in Clauses 7.3 to 7.9 inclusive, excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

“Target Working Capital” means $3,500,000.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $65,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”).

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash at Closing by wire transfer to an account(s) designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, the Buyer shall pay the amount of such excess to the Seller within five Business Days of the determination of Closing Working Capital.

3. Earn-out

3.4 The Buyer shall, within 90 days of the end of FY2026, calculate the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026. If such EBITDA exceeds $5,000,000, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the “Earn-out Payment”) equal to two times the amount by which such EBITDA exceeds $5,000,000; provided that the Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed $3,000,000.

9. Limitations on Liability

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer in respect of any claim under this Agreement unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $325,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which such aggregate claims exceed $325,000.

10. Survival and Notification

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months, and the Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by courier or transmitted by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above (or as otherwise notified).

12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

13. General

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
