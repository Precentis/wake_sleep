2. ISSUER AND OFFERING DETAILS

2.1 The issuer of the securities described herein is Ivybridge Technologies Ltd. The date of this Prospectus is 21 December 2026.

2.2 The Company is undertaking an initial public offering (the “Offering”) of its ordinary shares, which are being offered to the public for the first time and will be underwritten by the Underwriter on a firm-commitment basis.

3. USE OF PROCEEDS

3.1 The net proceeds from the Offering are estimated to be $120,000,000. The Company intends to apply the net proceeds as follows:

(a) $54,000,000 to the repayment of the outstanding balance under the Company’s revolving credit facility;

(b) $36,000,000 to fund capital expenditure, including investment in research and development infrastructure, software platform enhancements, and expansion of data processing capacity;

(c) $38,000,000 for general corporate purposes, including working capital, potential strategic acquisitions, and investment in customer acquisition initiatives.

3.2 The allocation of proceeds set forth above represents the Company’s current intentions based on its business plan as of the date of this Prospectus. The Company retains discretion to reallocate amounts among the stated purposes if circumstances warrant, provided that any material deviation shall be disclosed in a prospectus supplement or periodic filing as required by applicable securities laws.

4. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP

4.1 The Company has a material commercial relationship with Netherby Logistics plc (company number 11853980), which accounted for approximately 22% of the Company’s total revenue during the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026. The relationship is governed by a software services agreement dated 15 June 2023, which has an initial term of three years and automatically renews for successive one-year periods unless terminated by either party upon 180 days’ written notice.

4.2 The loss of Netherby Logistics plc as a customer could have a material adverse effect on the Company’s revenue and operating results. However, the Company continues to diversify its customer base and does not anticipate termination of this arrangement in the near term.
