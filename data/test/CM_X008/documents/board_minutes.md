## 3. Update on Litigation: Kestrel Systems Ltd Claim

3.1 The board received a report from external legal counsel regarding a class action initiated by Kestrel Systems Ltd against Ivybridge Technologies Ltd.

3.2 The claim seeks damages in the amount of $12,500,000, allegedly arising from performance issues in software delivered under a contract dated 14 March 2024.

3.3 Counsel advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success and recommended that the Company prepare a robust defence while evaluating potential settlement avenues to mitigate reputational and financial exposure.

3.4 The board directed management to coordinate with legal advisors to assess the financial provisioning required in the upcoming financial statements, in accordance with IAS 37.

3.5 Mr. Cartwright was appointed to lead a subcommittee to oversee legal strategy and reporting on this matter ahead of the next board meeting.

## 4. Initial Public Offering and Underwriting Arrangements

4.1 The board reviewed the status of the proposed initial public offering (IPO) of ordinary shares of the Company.

4.2 Netherby Software Securities (Company number: 14813235, registered office: 49 Cannon Street, London) has been appointed as the sole underwriter for the offering.

4.3 A preliminary prospectus has been circulated to the Financial Conduct Authority for review, with amendments being finalised in consultation with legal and financial advisors.

4.4 The board confirmed that all material contracts, including those with key customers, have been disclosed in the draft registration statement.

4.5 Ms. Mehta reported ongoing engagement with Netherby Logistics plc (Company number: 11853980, registered office: 70 Bishopsgate, London), a material customer, to ensure continuity of service and transparency regarding the transition to public status.
