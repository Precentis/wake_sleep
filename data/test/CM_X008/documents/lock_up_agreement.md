LOCK-UP AGREEMENT

This Lock-Up Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] November 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Ivybridge Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10943680 whose registered office is at 53 Gresham Street, London (the "Company");

(2) Netherby Software Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14813235 whose registered office is at 49 Cannon Street, London (the "Underwriter"); and

(3) the shareholder of the Company whose name and signature appear on the execution page hereof (the "Shareholder").

WHEREAS the Company proposes to undertake an initial public offering (the "IPO") of its ordinary shares (the "Shares") pursuant to a prospectus dated 23 November 2026 (the "Prospectus"), with Netherby Software Securities acting as the underwriter.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

(a) "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in New York;

(b) "Prospectus" means the final prospectus dated 23 November 2026 relating to the IPO, as filed with the relevant regulatory authority;

(c) "Shares" means the ordinary shares of the Company, nominal value £0.01 each.

2. Lock-Up Restriction

The Shareholder hereby agrees that, during the period (the "Lock-Up Period") commencing on the date of the Prospectus and ending 180 days thereafter, the Shareholder shall not, directly or indirectly, offer, sell, contract to sell, pledge, grant any option to purchase, make any short sale or otherwise dispose of any Shares or any securities convertible into or exchangeable or exercisable for or linked to Shares, whether beneficially owned or hereafter acquired, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the restrictions set out in this Clause 2 shall not apply to:

(a) transfers made by will or by the laws of intestacy;

(b) transfers to a trust established for the direct or indirect benefit of the Shareholder or members of the Shareholder’s immediate family;

(c) transfers made pursuant to a court order, provided that the transferee agrees in writing to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.

Any permitted transferee shall hold the Shares subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Payment

No consideration shall be payable by the Company or the Underwriter to the Shareholder under or in connection with this Agreement.

4. Notices

Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, recorded delivery, or by electronic mail to the address or email address set out in the records of the Company. Notices shall be deemed received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two Business Days after posting;

(c) if sent by electronic mail, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

5. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and shall not disclose them to any third party except:

(a) as required by law or regulation;

(b) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidentiality;

(c) with the prior written consent of the other parties.

6. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements or understandings, whether written or oral.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
