**UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT**  
**EXTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 20 November 2026  

**Between:**  
(1) Ivybridge Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10943680, whose registered office is at 53 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”); and  
(2) Netherby Software Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14813235, whose registered office is at 49 Cannon Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

**1. Definitions**  
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Agreement” means this underwriting agreement, including its recitals and schedules;  
“Shares” means the ordinary shares in the capital of the Company offered pursuant to the registration statement and prospectus forming part of the initial public offering;  
“Offering” means the initial public offering of the Shares underwritten by the Underwriter;  
“Registration Statement” means the registration statement on Form F-1 (Registration No. 333-275102) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in respect of the Shares;  
“Prospectus” means the final prospectus relating to the Offering, as supplemented or amended;  
“United States” means the United States of America, its territories, possessions and all areas subject to its jurisdiction;  
“Canada” means the country of Canada and its provinces and territories; and  
“European Economic Area” or “EEA” means the European Economic Area as defined in the Agreement on the European Economic Area.  

**2. Appointment and Undertaking**  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, 5,250,000 Shares at a price of £2.40 per Share, subject to the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.  

**3. Purchase and Payment**  
The obligation of the Underwriter to purchase the Shares shall be subject to the accuracy of the representations and warranties contained in this Agreement, the performance by the Company of all covenants and agreements on its part to be performed, and the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. Payment for the Shares shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Company at least three (3) business days prior to the closing date. Settlement shall occur on 23 November 2026 (“Closing Date”).  

**4. Representations and Warranties of the Company**  
The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:  
(a) it is a company duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;  
(b) the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorised by all necessary corporate action;  
(c) the Shares to be issued are duly authorised and, when issued and delivered against payment, will be validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable;  
(d) the Registration Statement and the Prospectus, as of their respective dates, comply in all material respects with the applicable requirements of the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended, and the rules and regulations thereunder.  

**5. Covenants**  
The Company shall use its reasonable endeavours to ensure that the Registration Statement remains effective and that the Prospectus is accurate and not misleading. The Company shall promptly notify the Underwriter of any material amendment or supplement to the Registration Statement or the Prospectus.  

**6. Notices**  
Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile transmission, or sent by courier or certified mail, postage prepaid, to the address of the respective party set out in the preamble. Notices shall be deemed effective upon receipt.  

**7. Confidentiality**  
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law, regulation or legal process, or as permitted by written consent. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years.  

**8. Entire Agreement**  
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations and agreements.  

**9. Offerings in Selected Jurisdictions**  
The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.  

**10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
