UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date set forth below, by and between Whitlock Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13767367 and registered office at 122 Moorgate, London (the "Company"), and Elstree Logistics Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12098149 and registered office at 125 Cheapside, London (the "Underwriter").

WHEREAS, the Company proposes to offer and sell 10,000,000 ordinary shares (the "Shares") in an initial public offering (the "Offering") pursuant to a registration statement filed with the relevant regulatory authority; and

WHEREAS, the Underwriter has agreed to underwrite the Offering on the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

(a) "Closing Date" means the date on which the Shares are delivered to the Underwriter against payment therefor, as specified in the final prospectus relating to the Offering;

(b) "Effective Date" means the date on which the registration statement relating to the Offering becomes effective under applicable securities laws;

(c) "Gross Proceeds" means the total amount received by the Company from the sale of the Shares in the Offering at the public offering price of $22.00 per Share, without deduction for any expenses or commissions;

(d) "Offering" means the initial public offering of the Shares as described in the registration statement and prospectus.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as the exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, the Shares at a price per share equal to $22.00, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

3. Payment and Commission

The Company shall pay the Underwriter a commission equal to 6.0% of the gross proceeds of the Offering. The gross proceeds of the Offering amount to $220,000,000 (10,000,000 Shares at $22.00 per Share). The commission payable to the Underwriter is therefore $15,180,000. Such commission shall be paid on the Closing Date by deduction from the gross proceeds remitted to the Company.

4. Representations and Warranties of the Company

The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) the Shares have been duly authorized and, when issued and delivered against payment in accordance with this Agreement, will be validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable;

(d) the financial statements included in the registration statement fairly present the financial condition of the Company as of the dates indicated and the results of operations for the periods specified;

(e) there are no material legal proceedings pending or, to the Company’s knowledge, threatened against it, other than as disclosed in the registration statement.

5. Covenants of the Company

The Company covenants that:

(a) it shall use its reasonable efforts to cause the registration statement to become effective and remain continuously effective during the Offering;

(b) it shall comply with all applicable securities laws and regulatory requirements in connection with the Offering;

(c) it shall not, without the prior written consent of the Underwriter, offer, sell or contract to sell any equity securities of the Company or securities convertible or exchangeable into equity securities during the period of 90 days following the Effective Date (the "Lock-Up Period"), except as permitted in the registration statement.

6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation, or to professional advisors under a duty of confidentiality.

7. Notices

Any notice, request, instruction or other document to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by certified mail (return receipt requested), or by a recognized overnight courier service to the addresses set forth above, or to such other address as either party may designate in writing.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
