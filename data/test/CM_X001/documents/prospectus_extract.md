# Prospectus Extract  
**Whitlock Holdings Ltd**  
**Registered Office: 122 Moorgate, London**  
**Company Number: 13767367**  
**Incorporated in England and Wales**  
**Date of this Prospectus: 25 April 2027**

---

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“the Company” means Whitlock Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13767367, whose registered office is at 122 Moorgate, London;  
“the Underwriter” means Elstree Logistics Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12098149, whose registered office is at 125 Cheapside, London;  
“Thornbury Holdings Ltd” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15370501, whose registered office is at 123 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“this Prospectus” means the prospectus dated 25 April 2027 issued by the Company in connection with its initial public offering of ordinary shares;  
“ordinary shares” means the class of equity securities being offered by the Company pursuant to this Prospectus;  
“Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares by the Company underwritten by the Underwriter.  

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or extended from time to time.

1.3 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Prospectus.

---

**2. Offering Structure**

2.1 The Company is offering ordinary shares to the public for the first time through an underwritten initial public offering.

2.2 The Underwriter has entered into an underwriting agreement with the Company pursuant to which the Underwriter has agreed to purchase and resell the ordinary shares on the terms set out in this Prospectus.

2.3 The Offering is subject to regulatory approval and compliance with applicable securities laws in the United Kingdom.

---

**3. Legal Proceedings**

3.1 As at the date of this Prospectus, the Company is not party to any legal proceedings which may have a significant effect on its financial position.

3.2 The Company confirms that there are no pending or threatened claims, investigations or disputes involving the Company that could reasonably be expected to result in material liability or adversely affect the Company’s ability to proceed with the Offering.

---

**4. Related Party Transactions**

4.1 The Company confirms that there are no related party transactions other than directors’ service agreements entered into in the ordinary course of business.

4.2 All directors’ service agreements have been disclosed to the Underwriter and are on arm’s length terms consistent with market practice.

4.3 There are no financial arrangements, supply agreements, loan agreements or other material contracts between the Company and any of its directors, substantial shareholders, or their connected persons, except as disclosed in this Prospectus.

---

**5. Payment Terms**

5.1 Payment for ordinary shares subscribed under the Offering shall be made in cleared funds to an account designated by the Company and notified to subscribers in writing.

5.2 All payments received from subscribers shall be held in a segregated escrow account pending completion of the Offering.

5.3 The Company reserves the right to reject any subscription or allot any lesser number of ordinary shares than applied for, at its absolute discretion.

---

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted to be given under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, recorded delivery, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 122 Moorgate, London.  
Notices to the Underwriter shall be sent to: 125 Cheapside, London.

6.3 A notice shall be deemed served 48 hours after being dispatched by recorded delivery, or immediately if delivered by hand or confirmed by email with read receipt.

---

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each party shall treat as confidential all non-public information received from the other party or its representatives in connection with the Offering.

7.2 The obligations under this clause shall not apply to information which: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was known to the receiving party on a non-confidential basis prior to disclosure; or (c) is required to be disclosed by law or regulatory authority.

7.3 This confidentiality obligation shall survive the completion or termination of the Offering for a period of five years.

---

**8. General**

8.1 This Prospectus and any subscription for ordinary shares shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.

8.3 The Company reserves the right to amend or supplement this Prospectus at any time prior to the closing of the Offering, provided that such amendments are not materially adverse to subscribers.
