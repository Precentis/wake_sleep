MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF WHITLOCK HOLDINGS LTD  
HELD AT 122 MOORGATE, LONDON  
ON 10 APRIL 2027  

Present:  
- A. J. Whitlock (Chairman)  
- M. Rosenthal (Director)  
- E. T. Vance (Director)  
- S. Patel (Company Secretary)  

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The meeting was called to order at 10:00 a.m. by Mr. A. J. Whitlock, Chairman of the Board, at the registered office of Whitlock Holdings Ltd, 122 Moorgate, London. Quorum was confirmed to be present.  

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 12 March 2027 were reviewed and approved as a true and accurate record.  

3. LITIGATION UPDATE  
The Board received a report from external legal counsel regarding a class action commenced by Yarrow Holdings Ltd, seeking damages in the amount of $8,000,000. The claim arises from alleged contractual non-performance in connection with a distribution agreement entered into in Q3 2025. Counsel advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success and recommended preparation of a robust defence. The Board resolved to instruct counsel to proceed with the defence and to keep the matter under continuous review. No provision was made in the draft financial statements for the year ending 31 December 2026 in respect of this claim.  

4. INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING PROGRESS  
The Underwriter, Elstree Logistics Securities (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12098149, registered office 125 Cheapside, London), provided an update on the progress of the proposed initial public offering of ordinary shares. The draft prospectus has been circulated to the Board for review, with feedback due by 15 April 2027. The offering is expected to launch on 1 June 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. The Board reaffirmed its commitment to full compliance with the UK Listing Rules and Prospectus Regulation Rules.  

5. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP  
The Board reviewed the ongoing commercial relationship with Thornbury Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15370501, registered office 123 Ludgate Hill, London), which accounts for 37% of the Company’s annual revenue. A new three-year supply agreement is under negotiation and is expected to be finalised prior to 30 June 2027.  

6. FINANCIAL OUTLOOK  
The CFO presented a forecast for the fiscal year 2027, projecting a 12% increase in revenue and a 7% rise in net profit before taxation. Capital expenditure for the year is anticipated to remain within the approved budget of £1.8 million.  

7. DIRECTOR LOAN AGREEMENT  
The Board considered and approved a loan agreement between the Company and Marcus Rosenthal, a director of Whitlock Holdings Ltd. The loan in the amount of $2,500,000 shall be advanced on interest-free terms, with no repayment schedule currently established. The transaction was reviewed for compliance with Section 197 of the Companies Act 2006, and the Board determined that shareholder approval is required. A circular to shareholders will be issued by 18 April 2027, with a general meeting scheduled for 3 May 2027 to seek ratification.  

8. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
No other matters were raised.  

9. ADJOURNMENT  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
