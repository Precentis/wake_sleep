5. Legal and Compliance Update  

5.1 The Board noted a letter before action from Kestrel Industries Ltd dated 8 January 2027, threatening proceedings for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries.  
5.2 Legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that no immediate action is required at this stage.  
5.3 The Board instructed management to monitor developments in relation to the matter and provide a further update within 21 days.  
5.4 No other material litigation or regulatory proceedings were reported.  

6. Share Purchase Agreement — Approval  

6.1 The Board reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 January 2027, pursuant to which Hartwell Marine Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10461032, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of Yarrow Analytics Ltd (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11938514, to Penrith Capital LLP (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11767967.  
6.2 The Board considered the terms of the transaction, including representations, warranties, covenants, and conditions precedent.  
6.3 Independent legal advice was obtained, and financial implications were assessed.  
6.4 The Board concluded that the transaction is in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders.  

RESOLVED, that the Board of Yarrow Analytics Ltd hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 January 2027, by and among Hartwell Marine Ltd, Penrith Capital LLP, and Yarrow Analytics Ltd, and authorises the Chair to execute the Agreement and any ancillary documents on behalf of the Company, such execution to be in accordance with English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.
