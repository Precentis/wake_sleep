**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) "Company" means Yarrow Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11938514, whose registered office is at 61 Fleet Street, London;

   (c) "Transaction Documents" means the share purchase agreement dated on or about 14 January 2027 between the Seller and the Buyer for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company, and any documents supplemental thereto;  
   (d) "Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in the Transaction Documents.  

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.  

**2. Purpose**  
2.1 This Disclosure Letter is provided pursuant to the terms of the Transaction Documents and contains full and specific disclosure by the Seller in relation to the Warranties.  

2.2 The disclosures made herein qualify the Warranties to the extent expressly stated and only to the extent required by the terms of the Transaction Documents.  

**3. General Disclosure**  
3.1 The disclosures set out in this letter are made on a pro tanto basis and are not to be construed as admissions of any liability or obligation beyond the context of the Warranties.  

3.2 The disclosure of any matter shall not be construed as an admission that such matter is material for the purposes of the Transaction Documents, nor shall it constitute disclosure in relation to any Warranty other than the one to which it is expressly linked.  

**4. Disclosure Against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**
