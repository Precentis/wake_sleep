SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 23 December 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Hartwell Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10461032, whose registered office is at 165 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Penrith Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11767967, whose registered office is at 125 Old Broad Street, London (the “Buyer”).

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are together referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

BACKGROUND

(A) The Company is Yarrow Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11938514, whose registered office is at 61 Fleet Street, London.

(B) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(C) The Buyer has agreed to acquire and the Seller has agreed to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

(D) Closing is scheduled to occur on 26 March 2027 (the “Closing Date”).

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, “Material Adverse Change” means any event or circumstance which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, but excluding any effect to the extent arising from: (a) changes in general economic, political, regulatory or financial market conditions affecting the United Kingdom or global economy; (b) changes or conditions generally affecting the industry in which the Company operates; or (c) pandemics or public health emergencies, regardless of duration or geographic scope.

1.2 The term “Company” means Yarrow Analytics Ltd, company number 11938514.

1.3 The term “EBITDA” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with UK GAAP consistently applied, and specifically for FY2026, as audited or otherwise verified in accordance with Clause 3.4.

1.4 The term “Earn-out Payment” shall have the meaning assigned in Clause 3.4.

1.5 The term “FY2026” means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The total consideration for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company (the “Purchase Price”) shall be US$210,000,000 (two hundred and ten million US dollars).

2.2 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares at Closing.

3. PAYMENT MECHANICS AND ADJUSTMENTS

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by irrevocable wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller at Closing a statement setting out the calculation of the final net working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, subject to post-closing adjustment pursuant to a separate mechanism to be agreed in the Disclosure Letter.

3.4 Earn-out Payment: If the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds US$4,000,000, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the “Earn-out Payment”) equal to two (2) times the amount by which such EBITDA exceeds US$4,000,000; provided that in no event shall the Earn-out Payment exceed US$5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within ninety (90) days following the final audited financial statements for FY2026, subject to verification by mutual agreement or, failing such agreement, by an independent accountant appointed jointly by the Parties.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing, addressed to the registered office of the relevant Party, and delivered by hand, registered post or email with read receipt.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed effective upon receipt if delivered by hand or email, or on the second Business Day following posting if sent by registered post.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Parties shall keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information disclosed in connection with the Transaction, except as required by law or regulation, or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

6.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
