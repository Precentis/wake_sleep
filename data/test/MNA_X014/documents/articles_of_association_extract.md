ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF YARROW ANALYTICS LTD

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Buyer" means Penrith Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11767967, with registered office at 125 Old Broad Street, London;  
(b) "Seller" means Hartwell Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10461032, with registered office at 165 Cannon Street, London;  
(c) "Company" means Yarrow Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11938514, with registered office at 61 Fleet Street, London;  
(d) "Kingsmere" means Kingsmere Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10232718, with registered office at 55 Gresham Street, London;  
(e) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
(f) "Director" means a director of the Company for the time being;  
(g) "Shareholder" means a person for the time being entered in the register of members of the Company as the holder of one or more shares in the Company.

2. Share Capital and Transfer of Shares  
2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 No Shareholder may transfer any shares in the Company without the prior written consent of the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, provided that transfers to wholly owned subsidiaries of the Buyer shall be permitted without consent.  
2.3 All applications for transfer of shares shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company.

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The Board shall consist of no fewer than four and no more than seven Directors, appointed in accordance with these Articles.  
3.2 The Board may appoint any individual to fill a casual vacancy, provided that the total number of Directors does not exceed the maximum permitted.  
3.3 A Director may be removed by ordinary resolution passed by the Shareholders, subject to compliance with applicable law.

4. Meetings and Proceedings  
4.1 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter.  
4.2 Written notice of at least five business days shall be given for any Board meeting, unless all Directors waive such notice in writing.  
4.3 Resolutions in writing signed by all Directors entitled to vote on the matter shall be as effective as if passed at a properly convened meeting.  
4.4 No business shall be transacted at any meeting unless a quorum is present.

5. Quorum  
5.1 Article 11: The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors. If within half an hour from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, unless otherwise agreed by the Directors. Attendance at any meeting shall constitute a waiver of notice by those present unless a person attends for the express purpose of objecting to the transaction of any business on the grounds that the meeting is not properly constituted.

6. Payment and Financial Provisions  
6.1 All payments due to the Seller under any share purchase agreement relating to the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be made in cleared funds to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller.  
6.2 The Company shall maintain audited financial records in accordance with the Companies Act 2006 and prepare annual accounts for each financial year ending on 31 December.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email as may be notified in writing.  
7.2 Notices to the Company shall be deemed effectively given if delivered to or sent to its registered office.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Each Director and Shareholder shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, operations, and financial affairs of the Company and its material customers, including but not limited to Kingsmere, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.  
8.2 The obligation under this Article shall survive the termination of any directorship or shareholding.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
