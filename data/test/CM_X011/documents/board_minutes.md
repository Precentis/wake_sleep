**MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF BELLHAVEN ENERGY PLC**

Held at 17 Bishopsgate, London, on Monday, 5 December 2026

**1. ATTENDANCE**

1.1 The following directors were present for the entire meeting:  
- Mr. A. N. Thorpe (Chair)  
- Ms. L. R. Davenport  
- Mr. Felix Yardley  
- Mr. J. C. Meldrum  

1.2 The Company Secretary, Ms. E. T. Carr, was also in attendance and recorded these minutes.

**2. CONFLICT OF INTEREST**

2.1 Mr. Felix Yardley declared a personal interest in Agenda Item 7 and accordingly withdrew from the meeting prior to the discussion and vote on that matter. He was not present during the deliberations or resolution relating to that item.

**3. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS – CLASS ACTION BY WHITLOCK SYSTEMS LTD**

3.1 The board reviewed a legal update from external counsel regarding a class action initiated by Whitlock Systems Ltd against Bellhaven Energy plc, claiming damages in the amount of $8,000,000. The claim arises from alleged contractual defaults in connection with a supply agreement dated 14 March 2025.

3.2 Counsel advised that the claim has reasonable prospects of success and recommended that the Company initiate settlement discussions while simultaneously preparing a robust defence.

3.3 The board resolved to instruct legal counsel to enter into preliminary negotiations with the claimant’s representatives and to report back within 30 days on the status of those discussions.

3.4 The board further authorised the allocation of up to £500,000 from the legal reserves to cover immediate litigation costs associated with the matter.

**4. PROGRESS ON IPO PREPARATIONS**

4.1 The Chief Financial Officer provided an update on the ongoing initial public offering of ordinary shares, underwritten by Bellhaven Pharma Securities (company number 11175667, registered office 36 Old Broad Street, London).

4.2 All required filings with the Financial Conduct Authority have been submitted, and the prospectus is under final review. Trading is expected to commence on the London Stock Exchange on or around 15 January 2027, subject to regulatory approval.

**5. CUSTOMER CONTRACT UPDATE – MARLOW ENGINEERING LTD**

5.1 The board received confirmation of the continued performance of the supply agreement with Marlow Engineering Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15828163, registered office 68 Cannon Street, London).

5.2 The contract, valued at £12.4 million over a 36-month term, remains on schedule, with no material defaults or disputes reported to date.

**6. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW**

6.1 The board reviewed unaudited financial statements for the nine months ended 30 September 2026. Revenue was reported at £89.7 million, with EBITDA of £21.3 million.

6.2 The board noted the continued alignment of financial performance with projections submitted to the underwriter.

**7. DIRECTOR LOAN TO FELIX YARDLEY**

7.1 The board considered a request from Mr. Felix Yardley for financial assistance in connection with personal liquidity requirements arising from relocation and property acquisition.

7.2 Subject to the declaration of conflict and Mr. Felix Yardley’s withdrawal from the meeting, the board approved the provision of an interest-free loan in the amount of $6,250,000 to Mr. Felix Yardley.

7.3 The loan is repayable in full on or before 30 November 2029. No security has been provided in connection with the loan.

7.4 The board concluded that the loan is in the best interests of the Company, having regard to Mr. Felix Yardley’s contribution and expected future service, and confirmed that the terms comply with applicable provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and the Company’s internal governance policies.

**8. NEXT MEETING**

8.1 The next board meeting is scheduled for 9 January 2027 at 17 Bishopsgate, London.

**9. ADJOURNMENT**

9.1 The meeting concluded at 16:42.
