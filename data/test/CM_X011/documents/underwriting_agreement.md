UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date set forth below by and between Bellhaven Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12808152 and registered office at 17 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), and Bellhaven Pharma Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11175667 and registered office at 36 Old Broad Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

WHEREAS, the Company proposes to issue and sell ordinary shares (the “Shares”) in connection with its initial public offering (the “Offering”), and the Underwriter desires to act as the sole underwriter for the Offering, subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions  
For the purposes of this Agreement:  
(a) “Firm Shares” means the 10,000,000 ordinary shares of the Company to be sold in the Offering;  
(b) “Over-allotment Option” means the option granted by the Company to the Underwriter under Clause 4 to purchase up to 2,000,000 additional Shares;  
(c) “Effective Date” means the date on which the offering circular relating to the Offering becomes effective under applicable law;  
(d) “Closing” means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares under this Agreement, to take place on the date specified in writing by the Underwriter, provided that such date shall be no later than 7 December 2026.

2. Appointment and Undertaking  
The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive underwriter for the Offering, and the Underwriter hereby agrees to use its reasonable endeavours to solicit offers to purchase the Firm Shares and any Shares issued pursuant to the Over-allotment Option, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Purchase and Sale of Firm Shares  
On the terms and conditions set forth herein, the Company agrees to sell to the Underwriter, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase from the Company, the Firm Shares at a price per share to be agreed in writing prior to the Effective Date (the “Purchase Price”). Payment for the Firm Shares shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Company, against delivery of the Shares in book-entry form on the Closing.

4. Over-allotment Option  
The Company hereby grants to the Underwriter an option (the “Over-allotment Option”) to purchase up to 2,000,000 additional Shares, representing 15% of the 10,000,000 Firm Shares, solely to cover over-allotments, if any. The Underwriter may exercise the Over-allotment Option in whole or in part at any time on or prior to the date falling 30 days after the Closing, by written notice to the Company. If exercised, the purchase price for such additional Shares shall be the same as the Purchase Price for the Firm Shares. Closing of any purchase pursuant to the Over-allotment Option shall occur on a date designated by the Underwriter, not later than 7 December 2026.

5. Representations and Warranties of the Company  
The Company represents and warrants to the Underwriter as of the date hereof and as of the Closing that: (a) it is a company duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales; (b) the Shares to be issued have been duly authorised and, when issued and delivered against payment, will be validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable; and (c) the offering circular, at the time of the Effective Date, will not contain any untrue statement of a material fact or omit to state a material fact necessary to make the statements therein, in light of the circumstances under which they were made, not misleading.

6. Covenants  
The Company covenants that it shall: (a) use its reasonable endeavours to ensure the effectiveness of the offering circular; (b) provide the Underwriter with all necessary corporate authorisations and documents; and (c) cooperate with the Underwriter in connection with the Offering.

7. Notices  
All notices, requests and other communications hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered personally, sent by confirmed facsimile or email, or sent by registered mail to the parties at their respective registered offices as set forth above, or to such other address as either party may designate in writing.

8. Confidentiality  
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except as required by law or regulation, or as necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

9. Offers to Investors  
The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. The Company acknowledges that the Underwriter shall determine the manner and timing of such offers in its sole discretion, without any selling restrictions or exemption conditions being specified for any jurisdiction.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
