(a) “Company” means Ashcombe Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10287054, whose registered office is at 33 Bishopsgate, London.

(d) “Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, underwritten by the Underwriter.

(e) “Cut-Off Date” means 13 February 2026, being the date up to which the procedures referenced herein were conducted.

(f) All references to “£” or “pounds” are to the lawful currency of the United Kingdom.

1.2 The headings in this Letter are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision.

2. Scope of Procedures

2.1 This Letter is issued in connection with the Offering and is addressed to the Underwriter and for the benefit of the representatives of the several underwriters involved in the Offering.

2.2 The procedures undertaken by the undersigned independent accountants were limited in nature and were conducted in accordance with applicable professional standards relevant to comfort letters in connection with securities offerings. Such procedures included inquiries of certain personnel of the Company responsible for financial and accounting matters and a review of the unaudited interim financial statements of the Company for the nine-month period ended 30 November 2025 and the related selected financial data derived from such financial statements.

2.3 The procedures did not include a complete audit, and accordingly, no opinion or assurance is expressed as to the financial statements or the financial position of the Company.

3. Comfort Provided

3.1 Based solely upon such limited procedures conducted up to the Cut-Off Date, nothing came to our attention that caused us to believe that:

(a) The unaudited interim financial statements referred to in Section 2.2 do not conform in all material respects to the accounting records of the Company;

(b) There has been any material adverse change in the financial condition or results of operations of the Company between 30 November 2025 and the Cut-Off Date;

(c) The revenue recognized by the Company from Orwell Holdings Ltd under the Orwell Holdings Agreement during the six-month period ending 31 January 2026 constituted 22.4% of the Company’s total revenue for such period.
