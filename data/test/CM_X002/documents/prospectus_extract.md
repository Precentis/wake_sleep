1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Prospectus, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Company” means Ashcombe Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10287054, with its registered office at 33 Bishopsgate, London;

“Delamere” means Delamere Bioscience Securities, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11608005, with its registered office at 17 Old Broad Street, London;

“Orwell Holdings” means Orwell Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15890349, with its registered office at 61 Cheapside, London;

“Offering” means the initial public offering of ordinary shares in the capital of the Company as described in this Prospectus;

“Prospectus” means this prospectus dated 25 February 2026, including all appendices, schedules, and incorporated documents.

1.2 The headings in this Prospectus are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of its terms.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Prospectus unless otherwise stated.

2. Offered Securities

2.1 The Company is offering 15,750,000 ordinary shares (the “Offered Shares”) to the public for the first time, representing 37.5% of the enlarged issued share capital of the Company upon completion of the Offering.

2.2 Each Offered Share carries equal rights to dividends, voting, and capital distribution, subject to the Articles of Association of the Company.

2.3 The Offered Shares will rank pari passu in all respects with the existing ordinary shares of the Company.

3. Underwriting Arrangements

3.1 The Offering is being conducted on a firm-commitment underwritten basis by Delamere, acting as sole underwriter.

3.2 Delamere has irrevocably agreed to purchase any Offered Shares that remain unsubscribed by the public at the closing of the Offering, at the final offer price determined in accordance with applicable pricing procedures.

3.3 The underwriting fee payable to Delamere shall be 4.2% of the gross proceeds raised from the Offering, calculated on the total aggregate price paid for all Offered Shares issued, whether to the public or to Delamere.

3.4 The obligations of Delamere under the underwriting agreement shall terminate on 10 February 2026 unless the Offering is completed on or before that date.

4. Use of Proceeds

4.1 The Company expects net proceeds from the Offering to be approximately £21,100,000 after deduction of underwriting fees and other offering expenses.

4.2 The net proceeds shall be allocated as follows: £12,500,000 for expansion of production facilities in East Anglia; £5,000,000 for repayment of existing short-term debt; and £3,600,000 for general working capital purposes.

5. Material Contracts

5.1 The Company has entered into a supply agreement with Orwell Holdings dated 14 August 2024, under which Orwell Holdings has committed to purchase a minimum of 48% of the Company’s annual output of plant-based protein products for a period of five years, commencing 1 January 2025.

5.2 The supply agreement may be terminated early only in the event of material breach, insolvency, or force majeure affecting performance for more than 120 consecutive days.

6. Payment Mechanics

6.1 Applications for Offered Shares must be submitted, and full payment remitted, no later than 5.00 p.m. (London time) on 8 February 2026.

6.2 All payments shall be made in sterling by cleared funds to the designated escrow account established by the Company’s legal advisors.

6.3 Shares will be credited to investors’ nominee accounts on 12 February 2026, subject to allotment and clearing of funds.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Prospectus shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

7.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to: Company Secretary, Ashcombe Foods Ltd, 33 Bishopsgate, London.

7.3 Notices to Delamere shall be addressed to: Head of Capital Markets, Delamere Bioscience Securities, 17 Old Broad Street, London.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or stock exchange rule.

8.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive the completion or termination of the Offering for a period of three years.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 This Prospectus and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Prospectus.
