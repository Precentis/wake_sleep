UNDERWRITING AGREEMENT

This Underwriting Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the date set forth below by and between Ashcombe Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10287054 and registered office at 33 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), and Delamere Bioscience Securities, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11608005 and registered office at 17 Old Broad Street, London (the “Underwriter”).

WHEREAS, the Company proposes to issue and sell, and the Underwriter agrees to purchase, certain ordinary shares of the Company (the “Shares”) in connection with an initial public offering (the “Offering”).

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

(a) “Offering” shall mean the initial public offering of the Shares by the Company through the Underwriter as principal.

(b) “Pricing Date” shall mean 25 February 2026, the date on which the price per Share and the total number of Shares to be sold in the Offering shall be agreed upon by the parties.

(c) “Closing Date” shall mean the date on which settlement of the purchase and sale of the Shares occurs, which shall be the second business day following the Pricing Date, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

(d) “Gross Proceeds” shall mean the total amount received by the Company from the sale of the Shares in the Offering, calculated as the number of Shares offered multiplied by the price per Share.

2. Appointment and Undertaking

The Company hereby appoints the Underwriter as its exclusive agent for the purposes of underwriting the Offering on the terms and conditions set forth herein. The Underwriter agrees to purchase the Shares from the Company at a price equal to the gross proceeds less the commission payable under Clause 3.

3. Payment and Commission

The Company shall pay the Underwriter a commission equal to 7.0% of the gross proceeds of the Offering. The gross proceeds are calculated based on the sale of 20,000,000 Shares at $18.50 per share, resulting in total gross proceeds of $310,000,000. Therefore, the commission due to the Underwriter shall be $22,015,000. Such commission shall be paid on the Closing Date by deduction from the gross proceeds remitted to the Company.

4. Representations and Warranties

Each party represents and warrants to the other that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales;

(b) it has full power and authority to enter into, execute and deliver this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder; and

(c) this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

5. Notices

Any notice, request, demand or communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly given if delivered personally, sent by internationally recognised overnight courier, or transmitted by confirmed facsimile or email to the respective addresses, facsimile numbers or email addresses set out below:

6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information received from the other party in connection with the Offering and not to disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or regulatory authority.

7. Conditions to Closing

(a) The obligations of the Underwriter hereunder are subject to the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the Company as of the Pricing Date and the Closing Date.

(b) The comfort letter to be delivered by the Company’s auditors shall be dated no earlier than 3 business days before the Pricing Date. The pricing date is 25 February 2026.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

9. Offerings and Distribution

The Underwriter may offer the Shares to investors in the United States, Canada and the European Economic Area. No selling restrictions or exemption conditions are specified for any jurisdiction.
