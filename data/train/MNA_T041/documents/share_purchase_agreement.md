**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **4 April 2026**.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Holloway Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10211124**, whose registered office is at **120 Bishopsgate, London** (the “**Seller**”);

(2) **Juniper Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10296357**, whose registered office is at **90 Queen Victoria Street, London** (the “**Buyer**”);

(each a “**Party**” and together the “**Parties**”)

**RECITALS:**

(A) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of **Corrib Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under number **14924621**, whose registered office is at **33 Cannon Street, London** (the “**Company**”), on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

(B) The acquisition will be completed on the Closing Date, as defined below.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and schedules;

“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

“**Closing Date**” means **11 July 2026**, or such other date as the Parties may agree in writing;

“**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“**EBITDA**” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies applied by the Company in the preparation of its audited financial statements for the financial year ending 31 December 2025;

“**FY2026 EBITDA**” means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

“**Parties**” and “Party” have the meanings given in the preamble;

“**Purchase Price**” means the amount specified in Clause 2.1, payable in accordance with Clause 2;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

---

**2. Sale and Purchase**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$70,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in cash on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

---

**3. Earn-out**

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the “**Earn-out Payment**”) equal to **two (2) times** the amount by which **FY2026 EBITDA** exceeds **$5,000,000**, provided that such Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed **$3,000,000** in aggregate. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated based on the audited financial statements of the Company for the year ending 31 December 2026 and paid within ninety (90) days of the date of such audit.

---

**4. Conditions**

4.5 If the Conditions have not been satisfied or duly waived in writing by the Parties on or before the date falling **150 days after the date of this Agreement** (being **24 September 2026**), either Party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement with immediate effect, without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued prior to such termination.

---

**9. Limitations on Liability**

9.4 **De Minimis and Basket**. The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty or indemnity under this Agreement unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds **$700,000** (being **1.0% of the Purchase Price**). If such threshold is exceeded, the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over **$700,000**, subject to any other limitations set forth in this Agreement.

---

**10. Notices**

10.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or electronic transmission (with confirmation of receipt) to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in the preamble, or such other address as may be notified by a Party in writing.

10.2 Notices delivered by hand or courier shall be deemed received on the date of delivery. Notices sent by electronic transmission shall be deemed received on the first Business Day after transmission.

---

**11. Confidentiality**

11.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

---

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **England**.

12.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **courts of England** in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
