# Minutes of the Board Meeting of Vantage Capital LLP

**Date:** 8 November 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 71 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7NY  
**Chair:** Mr. A. Thompson  
**Company Secretary:** Ms. R. Patel  

## 1. Call to Order

The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:03 a.m. The meeting was convened pursuant to notice given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Vantage Capital LLP (the “Company”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13084389.  

## 2. Attendance and Quorum

2 directors were present:  
- Mr. A. Thompson  
- Ms. E. Clarke  

The Company has a total board of 5 directors. The meeting was duly quorate as per the Articles of Association.  

## 3. Conflict of Interest

The directors confirmed that no conflicts of interest arose in connection with the matters to be considered, save that the transaction discussed in Agenda Item 5 directly involves the Company as a party to the proposed arrangements. The directors affirmed their duty to act in the best interests of the Company and confirmed that their consideration of the matter was conducted independently and in good faith.  

## 4. Review of Draft Resolutions

The draft resolutions to be considered were circulated in advance by the Company Secretary. The directors reviewed the wording and confirmed their readiness to proceed.  

## 5. Approval of Share Purchase Agreement

The directors considered the proposed Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) between Northgate Pharma plc (the “Seller”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10989626, and Dunmore Analytics Ltd (the “Buyer”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12435686, pursuant to which the Seller will sell, and the Buyer will acquire, the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

The directors noted that the Company is a party to material contracts with Penrith Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15952474, which remains in full force and effect.  

The directors received a briefing from external legal counsel regarding the implications of the proposed transaction, including ongoing obligations, transition arrangements, and the impact on employees and material contracts.  

The directors further noted that the SPA is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.  

After full deliberation, the board concluded that the transaction is in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders.  

**RESOLVED as follows:**  

**5.1** That the directors of Vantage Capital LLP hereby approve the entry by the Seller and the Buyer into the Share Purchase Agreement in relation to the entire issued share capital of the Company, and all ancillary documentation connected thereto, and authorise the Company Secretary to take all necessary and appropriate steps to facilitate the transaction, including the provision of requisite consents and confirmations.  

## 6. Adjournment

There being no further business, the Chair adjourned the meeting at 11:15 a.m.
