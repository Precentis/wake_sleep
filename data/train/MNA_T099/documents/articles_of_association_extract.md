ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF VANTAGE CAPITAL LLP  
(Company Number: 13084389)  
Registered Office: 71 Ludgate Hill, London  

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Articles” means these Articles of Association of Vantage Capital LLP as amended from time to time;  
(b) “Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
(c) “Buyer” means Dunmore Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12435686, with registered office at 97 Cheapside, London;  
(d) “Company” means Vantage Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13084389, with registered office at 71 Ludgate Hill, London;  
(e) “Seller” means Northgate Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10989626, with registered office at 32 King William Street, London;  
(f) “Penrith Bioscience plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15952474, with registered office at 26 Moorgate, London, being a material customer of the Company;  
(g) “Share Transfer” means the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller.  

2. Share Capital and Transfer  
2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall consist entirely of ordinary shares.  
2.2 The Seller shall retain full legal and beneficial ownership of all issued shares in the Company until such time as the Share Transfer is completed in accordance with the terms of the acquisition agreement dated prior to 10 November 2026.  
2.3 Upon completion of the Share Transfer, the Buyer shall become the sole shareholder of the Company and shall assume all rights and obligations attaching to the shares.  

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the authority to make all decisions concerning the management and administration of the Company’s affairs.  
3.2 The Board shall consist of such number of directors as may be determined from time to time by ordinary resolution, subject to any provision in the Company’s shareholders’ agreement.  
3.3 Directors may exercise all the powers of the Company to borrow money and mortgage or charge its undertaking, property and uncalled capital, and to issue debentures and other securities.  

11. Quorum for Board Meetings  
11.1 The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.  
11.2 If within thirty minutes from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other day, time and place as the directors may determine.  

12. Notices  
12.1 Any notice or communication required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or transmitted by email to the registered office of the relevant party.  
12.2 A notice sent by post to the registered office of the Company or any director or shareholder shall be deemed to have been received 48 hours after posting.  

13. Confidentiality  
13.1 All directors, officers and employees of the Company shall maintain strict confidentiality in respect of all commercial, financial and operational information relating to the Company, including but not limited to information concerning the relationship with Penrith Bioscience plc.  
13.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of any directorship or employment and shall continue indefinitely.  

14. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
14.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
14.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
