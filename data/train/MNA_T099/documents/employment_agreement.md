18.2 Accordingly, the Founder agrees that, for a period of 12 months after the Termination Date, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person, firm, company or organisation:

(a) carry on, be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business in the United Kingdom;
