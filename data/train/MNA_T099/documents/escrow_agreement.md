2. Appointment and Duties of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under the terms of this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or before the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account, which shall be an interest-bearing account with a financial institution acceptable to the Parties. Any interest earned on the Escrow Amount shall accrue to the benefit of the Seller and shall be released together with the Escrow Amount.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

4.2 If, on the date referred to in Clause 4.1, there are one or more pending Claims, the Escrow Agent shall withhold from release an amount equal to the aggregate of all such pending Claims (the “Withheld Amount”) and shall release only the balance of the Escrow Amount to the Seller.

4.3 The Withheld Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent until final resolution of the pending Claims in accordance with Clause 6, whereupon it shall be released to the Seller or the Buyer, as determined by mutual written instruction or by final adjudication.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by signed delivery service, or sent by email to the most recently provided email address of the recipient Party.

5.2 Notices shall be sent to the registered office of the relevant Party unless otherwise notified.

6. Confidentiality and Claims

6.1 Any Claim must be notified in writing to the Escrow Agent and to the Seller by no later than the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date.

6.2 The Escrow Agent shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except as required by law or as necessary to perform its duties under this Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
