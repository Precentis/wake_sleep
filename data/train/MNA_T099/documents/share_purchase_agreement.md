SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 3 November 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Northgate Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10989626 whose registered office is at 32 King William Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Dunmore Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12435686 whose registered office is at 97 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

BACKGROUND:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Vantage Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under number 13084389 whose registered office is at 71 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “Closing Date” means 29 January 2027;

   “Company” means Vantage Capital LLP, company number 13084389;

   “Parties” means the Buyer and the Seller;

   “Penrith Bioscience plc” means Penrith Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15952474, with registered office at 26 Moorgate, London.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be $125,000,000 (one hundred and twenty-five million US dollars) (the “Purchase Price”), payable in full in cash at Closing.

2.2 The Buyer shall procure payment of the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 4:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other location as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   (b) such consents, resignations and documentation as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of beneficial and legal ownership of the Shares to the Buyer;

   (c) a certificate of non-foreign status under Section 1445 of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, if applicable.

4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

4.1 Each Party represents and warrants to the other as of the date hereof that:

   (a) it has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder;

   (b) this Agreement constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or emailed to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email as a Party may designate in writing.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, two Business Days after posting, or 24 hours after transmission by email, provided confirmation of receipt is obtained.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement and all information received in connection with the Transaction confidential, except as required by law or regulation, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Party.

12. RESTRICTION ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that for a period of 4 years from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on or be engaged in any business which is competitive with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date, within the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company operated at such time.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
