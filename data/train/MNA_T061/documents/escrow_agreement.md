2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold, administer, and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to perform its duties hereunder solely in accordance with the provisions set forth in this Agreement.

3. Deposit of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within three Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account maintained with a UK-authorised deposit-taker, and any interest accruing on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. For the avoidance of doubt, any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a bona fide claim properly notified in writing to the Escrow Agent by the Buyer prior to such date shall remain held until final resolution of such claim.

4.2 Upon the expiry of the 24-month period referred to in Clause 4.1, the Escrow Agent shall, within five Business Days, release to the Seller such portion of the Escrow Amount not subject to any outstanding claims, unless otherwise directed jointly in writing by the Buyer and the Seller.
