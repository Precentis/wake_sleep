SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 28 February 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Quayside Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14396634, whose registered office is at 25 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Orwell Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15516634, whose registered office is at 134 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11646179, whose registered office is at 138 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell the same, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions have the following meanings:

   "Closing Date" means 20 May 2026;

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration for the sale and purchase of the Shares, as determined in accordance with Clause 2;

   "Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares (the "Purchase Price") is $85,000,000.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to the account(s) designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer no later than noon on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 2 and shall be final and binding unless objected to in writing within 30 days of delivery of the Closing Net Working Capital Statement. The Buyer shall have the right to set off any amount by which the Closing Working Capital falls short of the Target Working Capital against the Purchase Price, and any such set-off shall result in a corresponding reduction of the amount payable at Closing, with the excess, if any, to be settled in accordance with Clause 2.3.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all documents and instruments of transfer required to vest the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances, and the Buyer shall pay the adjusted Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or, where permitted, waiver in writing of the following Conditions:

   (a) the absence of any material adverse change in the financial or trading position of the Company;

   (b) the receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals;

   (c) the provision of written confirmation from Saltash Retail Group plc, company number 13195979, registered office 121 Cannon Street, London, confirming continuation of its existing supply agreement with the Company.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may by written notice to the other terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights accrued prior to termination.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim under this Agreement unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds $425,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which such aggregate claims exceed $425,000. For the avoidance of doubt, no claim shall give rise to liability unless and until the threshold of $425,000 is crossed, and liability shall arise solely in respect of the excess.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information acquired in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post or transmitted by email to the address or email address set out in this Agreement or as otherwise notified.

12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
