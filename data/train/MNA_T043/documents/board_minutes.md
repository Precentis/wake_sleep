## 2. Declaration of Interest

2.1 No director present had any disclosable interest in the matters to be considered at this meeting.  

## 3. Review of Proposed Transaction

3.1 The Board reviewed the proposed transaction whereby Arden Marine Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10139993, would sell the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Industries Ltd to Netherby Pharma plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13305811, with registered office at 43 Fleet Street, London.  

3.2 The Board received a summary of the draft Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) dated 2 August 2026, which provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued shares in the Company.  

3.3 The Company Secretary confirmed that the Buyer’s registered office is correctly stated and that all party details in the SPA match the canonical records held by Companies House.  

3.4 The Board considered the implications of the transaction on the Company’s operations, including its commercial relationship with Upton Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13881489, registered office 9 Queen Victoria Street, London), a material customer.  

3.5 Legal counsel advised that the SPA is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

## 4. Resolutions

4.1 **RESOLVED** that the Board hereby approves the entry by the Company into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 2 August 2026 between Arden Marine Ltd (Seller) and Netherby Pharma plc (Buyer) for the sale of the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Industries Ltd.  

4.2 **RESOLVED** that the Company Secretary is authorised to take all necessary steps to execute the SPA on behalf of the Company, including obtaining any required consents and filing all statutory notifications.  

4.3 **RESOLVED** that the Company Secretary is authorised to communicate this resolution to the Buyer and Seller and to confirm compliance with all pre-completion undertakings.  

## 5. Adjournment

5.1 The meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
