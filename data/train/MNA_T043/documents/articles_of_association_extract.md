ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF IVYBRIDGE INDUSTRIES LTD

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Act" means the Companies Act 2006;  
(b) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
(c) "Buyer" means Netherby Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13305811, with its registered office at 43 Fleet Street, London;  
(d) "Company" means Ivybridge Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14223923, with its registered office at 96 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
(e) "Material Customer" means Upton Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13881489, with its registered office at 9 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
(f) "Seller" means Arden Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10139993, with its registered office at 116 Moorgate, London;  
(g) "Secretary" means the company secretary of the Company or any person appointed to perform the functions of the secretary;  
(h) "Winding-up" means winding-up under the Act or any corresponding future enactments.  

2. Share Capital and Allotment  
2.1 The Company's issued share capital consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1 each.  
2.2 The Company may from time to time, subject to the Act and these Articles, issue shares with such rights, restrictions or conditions as the Board determines.  

3. Transfer of Shares  
3.1 No transfer of shares shall be registered unless accompanied by the instrument of transfer and share certificate, properly stamped and executed.  
3.2 The Board may, at its discretion, decline to register any transfer of shares without assigning a reason.  

4. Board Meetings  
4.1 The Board shall meet as frequently as necessary for the management of the Company’s affairs, but not less than once every calendar quarter.  
4.2 Notice of a board meeting shall be given at least five (5) clear days prior to the meeting, unless all directors waive such notice in writing.  
4.3 Resolutions in writing signed by all directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting shall be as valid as if passed at a duly convened meeting.  

5. Quorum and Proceedings  
5.1 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.  
5.2 No business shall be transacted at any board meeting unless a quorum is present at the commencement of the meeting.  
5.3 If within half an hour of the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, unless otherwise agreed by the directors.  

6. Payment Provisions  
6.1 Any payment due under an agreement involving the Company shall be made in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the recipient.  
6.2 Payments shall be deemed received only when the funds are irrevocably available to the recipient without set-off or deduction, save as required by law.  

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice to be given to the Company shall be in writing and delivered to its registered office.  
7.2 Any notice to a director or shareholder shall be sent to their last known address recorded in the Company’s register.  
7.3 Notices sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.  

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Directors, officers and employees of the Company shall not, without prior written authorisation, disclose any information concerning the Company’s business, clients (including the Material Customer), financial affairs or strategic plans to any third party.  
8.2 The obligation under this Article 8 shall survive termination of office or employment for a period of five (5) years.  

9. General  
9.1 These Articles shall be interpreted and governed by English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.  
9.3 Nothing in these Articles shall override the provisions of the Act.
