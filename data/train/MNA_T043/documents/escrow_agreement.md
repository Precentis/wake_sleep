ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 25 July 2026 (the "Execution Date") among:

(1) Arden Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10139993, whose registered office is at 116 Moorgate, London (the "Seller");

(2) Netherby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13305811, whose registered office is at 43 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Travers & Co., a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, whose principal office is at 150 Chancery Lane, London (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14223923, whose registered office is at 96 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the consideration under the SPA, a portion of the purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") is to be held in an escrow account by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement for the purposes of satisfying any claims that may be made by the Buyer in respect of indemnities or warranties under the SPA;

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London;

"Closing Date" means 1 October 2026;

"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller in accordance with Clause 5, specifying the nature, basis and quantum of a claim under the SPA for which payment from the Escrow Amount is sought;

"Escrow Amount" means the amount of £4,200,000 (four million, two hundred thousand pounds sterling) deposited into the escrow account established by the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3;

"SPA" has the meaning given in Recital (A).

1.2 References to clauses and recitals are to clauses and recitals of this Agreement.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall, on or prior to the Closing Date, deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent to be held in a designated interest-bearing client account (the "Escrow Account").

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account on trust for the Buyer and the Seller, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2.3 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss of interest or income arising from the investment or non-investment of the Escrow Amount unless such loss results from the Escrow Agent’s wilful misconduct or negligence.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 4.1, the Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be released by the Escrow Agent to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, provided that there are no outstanding Claim Notices.

4. Withholding and Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. Pending claims shall remain secured in the Escrow Account until final determination, after which the Escrow Agent shall release any unclaimed portion to the Seller.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify in writing to the others.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 116 Moorgate, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 43 Fleet Street, London.  
Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: 150 Chancery Lane, London.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any information received from another Party pursuant to this Agreement, except as required by law or disclosure to professional advisers bound by confidentiality.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
