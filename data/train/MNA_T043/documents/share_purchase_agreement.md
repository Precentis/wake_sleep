**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **10 July 2026**

**PARTIES**

(1) **Arden Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10139993**, whose registered office is at 116 Moorgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Netherby Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13305811**, whose registered office is at 43 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Ivybridge Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **14223923**, whose registered office is at 96 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following definitions and interpretations apply:

"**Closing Date**" means **1 October 2026**;

"**Closing Working Capital**" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with Schedule 1;

"**EBITDA**" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026"), calculated in accordance with the accounting policies set out in Schedule 2;

"**Earn-out Payment**" means the sum calculated in accordance with Section 3.4;

"**FY2026**" means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026;

"**Purchase Price**" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the sale and purchase of the Shares, as set out in Section 2.1;

"**Target Working Capital**" means **$5,000,000**;

"**Shares**" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase the Shares from the Seller for a total consideration of **$95,000,000** (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by bank transfer to an account or accounts designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$5,000,000**. If Closing Working Capital exceeds Target Working Capital, no upward adjustment shall be made.

**3. EARN-OUT**

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to **2 times** the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds **$4,000,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at **$10,000,000**. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated and paid within ninety (90) days following the end of FY2026, based on the audited financial statements of the Company for FY2026.

**4. CLOSING**

4.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

4.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all necessary instruments of transfer, share certificates and such other documents as may be required to vest good and marketable title to the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

**5. NOTICES**

5.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email, to the address or email address of the relevant party set out at the beginning of this Agreement, or such other address as a party may notify in writing.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

**7. GENERAL**

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes any prior agreement, understanding or arrangement.

7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
