1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this letter, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Affiliate" means, in relation to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such person;
- "Company" has the meaning given in recital (A);
- "Disclosed" means fairly and accurately disclosed in this letter with sufficient detail to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed;
- "SPA" has the meaning given in recital (A);
- "Warranty" means any warranty, representation or statement given by the Seller in the SPA.

1.2 The headings in this letter are included for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this letter.

2. DISCLOSURE

2.1 The Seller hereby discloses pursuant to Clause 8 of the SPA all matters, facts, circumstances or events which are Disclosed in this letter, and which qualify or otherwise affect the accuracy of the Warranties.

2.2 Disclosure in this letter shall be deemed to be disclosure:

(a) under all relevant Warranties to which it reasonably relates; and

(b) against any qualification or limitation in the SPA relating to materiality, knowledge, significance or similar standards.
