ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 March 2027 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Vantage Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12855691, whose registered office is at 19 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Holloway Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10253897, whose registered office is at 94 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Norton Escrow Services plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered office at 52 Cornhill, London, acting as escrow agent hereunder (the "Escrow Agent").

Background

A. The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 9 March 2027 (the "SPA") to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Orwell Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12337103, whose registered office is at 100 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

B. As part of the SPA, the Buyer has agreed to deposit an amount equal to 15% of the total purchase price into an escrow account to secure the Seller’s indemnification obligations as set out therein.

C. The parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the escrow funds on the terms set out in this Agreement.

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"Agreement" means this Escrow Agreement, as amended from time to time.

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

"Claim" means any written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller pursuant to the SPA asserting a liability arising under the indemnification provisions of the SPA.

"Escrow Funds" means the sum of 15% of the total purchase price paid under the SPA, transferred by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent on the date of completion of the share purchase, together with any interest earned thereon.

"Material Customer" means Redcliffe Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15027754, whose registered office is at 14 Ludgate Hill, London.

"SPA" means the Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 March 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the Company.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

The parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to perform its duties herein.

3. Deposit of Escrow Funds

The Buyer shall transfer the Escrow Funds to the Escrow Agent within one Business Day of the completion of the purchase under the SPA. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated interest-bearing account at a designated financial institution in London.

4. Release of Escrow Funds

The Escrow Funds, or any portion thereof, shall be released:

(a) to the Seller, in accordance with a joint written instruction signed by the Buyer and the Seller; or

(b) to the Buyer or the Seller, as determined by final award of arbitration pursuant to Clause 12; or

(c) upon the expiry of 24 months from the Effective Date, the unclaimed portion shall be released to the Seller, unless a Claim is pending.

5. Duties of Escrow Agent

The Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with instructions received from the Buyer and Seller, or as required by binding legal order. The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken in good faith reliance on such instructions.

6. Notices

All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the respective addresses set out above, by pre-paid first-class post or email, and shall be deemed served 24 hours after email transmission or two Business Days after posting.

7. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep the terms of this Agreement and any related disclosures confidential, except as required by law or to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

8. Costs and Fees

The fees of the Escrow Agent shall be shared equally by the Buyer and the Seller.

9. Assignment

No party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties.

10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter herein.

11. Amendments

No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all parties.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York.
