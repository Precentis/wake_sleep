ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ORWELL ENERGY PLC

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Buyer” means Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10253897, whose registered office is at 94 Fleet Street, London;  
“Company” means Orwell Energy plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12337103, whose registered office is at 100 Fleet Street, London;  
“Seller” means Vantage Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12855691, whose registered office is at 19 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
“Redcliffe Retail Group plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15027754, whose registered office is at 14 Ludgate Hill, London, currently a material customer of the Company;  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Director” means a director of the Company;  
“Secretary” means the company secretary of the Company, if any, and where no secretary is appointed, references to the Secretary shall be disregarded.  

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as from time to time amended, re-enacted or replaced, and includes any subordinate legislation made under it.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1 each.  
2.2 The rights attached to the ordinary shares include the right to receive dividends as declared, to vote at general meetings, and to share in the surplus assets of the Company upon winding up.

3. Payment for Shares  
3.1 All payments for shares in the Company shall be made in sterling by cleared funds to such bank account as the Board may designate in writing.  
3.2 No share certificate shall be issued until full payment has been received in respect of the shares to which it relates.

4. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
4.1 The Board may meet to conduct business at such times and places as it sees fit, provided that reasonable notice is given to all Directors.  
4.2 A Director may participate in a meeting of the Board by means of a conference call or other communication equipment allowing all participants to hear each other, and such participation shall constitute attendance in person.  
4.3 Resolutions in writing signed by all Directors entitled to receive notice of a Board meeting shall be valid and effective as if passed at a duly convened and held meeting of the Board.

5. Quorum for Board Meetings  
5.1 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice required to be given to a member, Director or the Company shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post to the registered address of the recipient, or sent by email to the last known email address on record.  
6.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed served on the date of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Every Director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, affairs, customers (including Redcliffe Retail Group plc), suppliers and financial matters of the Company, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.  
7.2 The obligations under this Article 7 shall continue to apply after a Director or officer ceases to hold office.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 These Articles shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
