The meeting was convened pursuant to the Articles of Association of Orwell Energy plc (the “Company”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12337103. With 2 directors (of 5) present, the meeting was quorate.

**Chairman of the Meeting:** Mr A. Kendall

**1. Call to Order**  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m.

**2. Confirmation of Agenda**  
The following agenda was adopted:  
1. Call to Order  
2. Confirmation of Agenda  
3. Review of Ongoing Material Contracts  
4. Update on Material Customer Relationships  
5. Legal and Regulatory Matters  
6. Proposed Transaction with Holloway Systems Ltd  
7. Any Other Business  
8. Adjournment

**3. Review of Ongoing Material Contracts**  
The board reviewed the status of key supply and service agreements. All contracts currently in force were confirmed to be performing in accordance with their terms. No material defaults or renegotiations were reported.

**4. Update on Material Customer Relationships**  
The board received a report on the relationship with Redcliffe Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15027754, registered office 14 Ludgate Hill, London), a material customer of the Company. Revenue from Redcliffe Retail Group plc accounted for 37% of total group revenue in the financial year ended 31 December 2026. The account remains active and in good standing.

**5. Legal and Regulatory Matters**  
The board noted a letter before action from Marlow Holdings Ltd threatening proceedings for approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. Legal counsel has been instructed to assess the claim. Management to monitor.

**6. Proposed Transaction with Holloway Systems Ltd**  
The board considered the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Holloway Systems Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10253897, registered office 94 Fleet Street, London), acting through its wholly owned subsidiary Vantage Engineering Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12855691, registered office 19 Queen Victoria Street, London.  

A copy of the draft Share Purchase Agreement was circulated and reviewed. The board acknowledged the strategic rationale for the transaction and noted that the terms had been subject to extensive negotiation and due diligence.  

**RESOLVED** that the Company approve entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Holloway Systems Ltd and Vantage Engineering Ltd on the terms presented, such agreement to be governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**7. Any Other Business**  
There was no other business.

**8. Adjournment**  
The meeting adjourned at 11:25 a.m.
