SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 February 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Vantage Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12855691 whose registered office is at 19 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10253897 whose registered office is at 94 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Orwell Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12337103 whose registered office is at 100 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

   "Buyer" means Holloway Systems Ltd;

   "Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Sale Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 18 May 2027;

   "Company" means Orwell Energy plc;

   "English law" means the law of England and Wales;

   "Parties" means the Buyer and the Seller, and "Party" means either of them;

   "Purchase Price" means the aggregate amount payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Sale Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

   "Sale Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are inserted for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Sale Shares for a total consideration of $165,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Sale Shares, accompanied by the share certificates for the Sale Shares;

   (b) a certificate of good standing or equivalent document in relation to the Company issued by the Registrar of Companies for England and Wales not more than ten Business Days prior to the Closing Date;

   (c) resignations, effective as of the Closing Date, of any directors and secretaries of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably request.

3.2 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

   (b) In the case of the Buyer:

4.2 A notice given in accordance with this Clause 4 shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting, or upon email confirmation of receipt, unless received on a non-Business Day, in which case it shall be deemed received on the next Business Day.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party undertakes that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the other Party, disclose to any person any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or regulation or as necessary to its professional advisers.
