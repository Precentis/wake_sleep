SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 15 April 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Fenwick Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12096752, whose registered office is at 89 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

(2) Redcliffe Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12910222, whose registered office is at 162 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The subject matter of this Agreement is the entire issued share capital of Eskdale Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13874685, whose registered office is at 160 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions have the following meanings:

   "Closing Date" means 20 July 2027;

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as determined under Clause 2.1.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $220,000,000. The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CONDITIONS

3.1 The obligation of the Buyer to complete the purchase of the Shares is subject to satisfaction or waiver, in writing, by the Buyer, of each of the Conditions, including but not limited to the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the Seller and the delivery of all required documents.

4. CLOSING AND TERMINATION

4.1 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer or such other location as the parties may agree, on the Closing Date.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (signed 15 April 2027), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued prior to termination.

5. PAYMENT AND ADJUSTMENTS

5.1 The Purchase Price shall be adjusted, if applicable, in accordance with the terms of this Agreement for any working capital or net debt adjustments, provided such adjustments are agreed or determined in accordance with the procedures set out in Schedule 2.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing, addressed to the relevant party at its registered office or such other address as it may notify, and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office email address. Notices shall be deemed received when delivered, if by hand; 48 hours after posting, if by post; or upon transmission confirmation, if by email.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party or the Company in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

8. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

8.2 Limitation of Liability: The aggregate liability of the Seller for any and all claims arising under the warranties given in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 20% of the Purchase Price, being $39,600,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $220,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

9.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

9.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
