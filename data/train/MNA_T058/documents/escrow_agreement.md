1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Agreement” means this escrow agreement dated on or about the date falling 6 May 2027, entered into among the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent.

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general banking business in London, England.

“Closing Date” means 20 July 2027.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £8,500,000 (eight million five hundred thousand pounds sterling), representing a portion of the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement, deposited into the Escrow Account by the Buyer on the Closing Date.

“Escrow Account” means the interest-bearing account established and maintained by the Escrow Agent at its branch in London, England, designated for the deposit and holding of the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

“Escrow Agent” means Lloyds Bank plc, acting through its corporate escrow services division, appointed by the Buyer and the Seller to hold and administer the Escrow Amount under the terms of this Agreement.

“Parties” means, collectively, the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent.

“Pending Claim” means any written claim for indemnification properly notified by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement prior to the Release Date, which remains unresolved as of such date.

“Release Date” means the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date.

“Seller” means Fenwick Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12096752, whose registered office is at 89 Cheapside, London.

“Buyer” means Redcliffe Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12910222, whose registered office is at 162 Moorgate, London.

“Company” means Eskdale Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13874685, whose registered office is at 160 Moorgate, London.

“Material Customer” means Fenwick Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12006679, whose registered office is at 110 Queen Victoria Street, London.

1.2 The singular includes the plural and vice versa, and a reference to one gender includes all genders. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account in London, England, with effect from the Closing Date, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer on or before the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

3. Payment and Interest

3.1 All funds deposited into the Escrow Account shall remain the property of the Buyer until released in accordance with Clause 4.1.

3.2 Any interest accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be held and released under the same terms.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains undisputed, shall be released to the Seller on the Release Date, which shall be the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any Pending Claim notified by the Buyer to the Seller in writing prior to the Release Date. The Seller acknowledges and agrees that any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claim pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 89 Cheapside, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 162 Moorgate, London.  
Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to such address or email as notified in writing by the Escrow Agent to the Parties.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with the Escrow Account and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

7.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
