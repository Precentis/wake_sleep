SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 14 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Calloway Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10072278, whose registered office is at 127 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Netherby Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10817656, whose registered office is at 45 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Northgate Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12743171, whose registered office is at 60 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and schedules;

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 6 June 2027;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

"Parties" means the Seller, the Buyer and the Company collectively, and "Party" means any one of them;

"Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $140,000,000, to be paid in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller such instruments of transfer as may be necessary to effect the transfer of the Shares to the Buyer, duly executed in blank or in favour of the Buyer.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place remotely via electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates representing the Shares, together with duly executed instruments of transfer.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.1 The obligations of the Parties to complete the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or, where permitted by law, waiver in writing by both Parties of each of the Conditions.

4.2 The Conditions include, without limitation: (a) the absence of any order, injunction or decree prohibiting the transaction; (b) receipt of all third-party consents and regulatory approvals necessary to complete the transaction; and (c) the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the other Party as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date.

4.3 The Parties shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions as soon as reasonably practicable.

4.4 If any Condition is not capable of being satisfied, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to determine whether the condition may be waived.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

12. NON-COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller undertakes that, for a period of 4 years following the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the business of the Company as conducted at the date of this Agreement.

12.2 The Seller may hold as a passive investment up to 5% of the issued shares of any competing company listed on a recognised stock exchange.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant Party.

14.2 Notices sent by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
