1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Confidential Information" includes all trade secrets, technical data, customer lists, supplier information, pricing strategies, financial records, and any other proprietary information disclosed to or accessible by the Employee during employment.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company agrees to employ the Employee, and the Employee agrees to serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Employee’s initial position is [Job Title], reporting to the Chief Executive Officer.

3. Term

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with Clause 9.

4. Remuneration

4.1 The Company shall pay to the Employee a base salary of £[●] per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme as determined by the Board of Directors in its sole discretion.

5.1 The Employee shall work such hours as are reasonably necessary to perform the duties of the role, normally based at the Company’s registered office or such other location as the Company may designate.

6. Duties

6.1 The Employee shall perform the duties of the role diligently and to the best of their ability and shall comply with all lawful and reasonable directions of the Company.

7.1 During employment and after termination, the Employee shall not use, disclose or permit the misuse of any Confidential Information.

8.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of employment shall be owned exclusively by the Company.

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving [●] months’ written notice to the other.

10.1 Upon termination, the Employee shall be entitled to accrued but unpaid salary and benefits up to the termination date.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company or the last known address of the Employee.

11.2 Notices shall be deemed received two working days after posting by first-class post.

12. Data Protection

12.1 The Employee consents to the processing of personal data for employment-related purposes in accordance with applicable data protection law.

13. Entire Agreement

13.1 This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

18. Non-Competition

18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage, be employed, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business for a period of 18 months after termination of employment.

18.2 This restriction applies to any enterprise operating in the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company or its group operates, including but not limited to dealings with material customers such as Redcliffe Technologies Ltd (company number 13206788).
