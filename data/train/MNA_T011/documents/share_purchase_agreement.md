# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 6 April 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Kingsmere Foods Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 11732451, registered office at 96 Cheapside, London) (the "Seller").

(2) **Quayside Logistics plc** (registered in England and Wales under company number 15277710, registered office at 133 Gresham Street, London) (the "Buyer").

(3) The Seller and the Buyer are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Galloway Medical Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 10867778, registered office at 18 Old Broad Street, London) (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, including the recitals, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

- **"Closing"** means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.
- **"Closing Date"** means 8 July 2026.
- **"General Warranties"** means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, excluding the Fundamental Warranties.
- **"Fundamental Warranties"** means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as set out in Schedule 2.
- **"Purchase Price"** means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1.
- **"Shares"** means all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company.
- **"Survival Period"** means the period during which the Warranties remain in full force and effect, as specified in Clause 10.1.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The total consideration for the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Purchase Price") is **$55,000,000** (fifty-five million US dollars), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

2.2 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell the Shares to the Buyer, and the Buyer agrees to purchase the Shares from the Seller, with effect from the Closing.

**3. PAYMENT**

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such bank account as the Seller may notify to the Buyer in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Payment of the Purchase Price shall constitute full and final settlement of all obligations of the Buyer under this Agreement in respect of the purchase of the Shares.

**9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY**

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of Warranty unless the aggregate of all claims for which the Seller might otherwise be liable exceeds **$275,000** (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price). If such threshold is exceeded, the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over **$275,000**.

**10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months**. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable Survival Period.

**12. POST-CLOSING COVENANTS**

12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of **4 years** from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, concerned with or have any financial interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**13. CONFIDENTIALITY**

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information disclosed under or in connection with this Agreement and shall not, without the prior written consent of the other Party, disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or regulation.

**14. NOTICES**

14.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by electronic means (with confirmation of receipt) to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in this Agreement.

14.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, two Business Days after posting, or upon confirmed transmission if sent electronically.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

15.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
