SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 December 2025.

BETWEEN:

(1) Arden Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10368339 whose registered office is at 57 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Glenrock Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14700670 whose registered office is at 127 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Calloway Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11061754 whose registered office is at 42 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 21 February 2026;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

"Target Working Capital" means $2,000,000.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares for a total consideration of $255,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be payable in full in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles set out in Schedule 2.

3. CONDITIONS

3.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or (where permitted) waiver of the following conditions (the "Conditions") on or before the Closing Date:

(a) all necessary corporate and shareholder approvals having been obtained by the parties;

(b) no injunction, restraint or prohibition having been issued by any court or regulatory authority which prevents or delays completion of the transaction contemplated hereby;

(c) the Company having delivered to the Buyer unaudited management accounts as of the Last Balance Sheet Date.

4. TERMINATION

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 17 May 2026), either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights which may have accrued prior to such termination.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address, as applicable, of the relevant party set out above.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery if delivered personally, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or upon transmission if sent by email, provided that no error message is returned.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis;

(b) as required by law or any regulatory authority; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five years.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
