ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 9 January 2026 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Arden Systems Ltd (the "Seller"), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10368339, whose registered office is at 57 Old Broad Street, London;

(2) Glenrock Systems Ltd (the "Buyer"), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14700670, whose registered office is at 127 Old Broad Street, London;

(3) Calloway Pharma plc (the "Company"), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11061754, whose registered office is at 42 Moorgate, London; and

(4) [Name of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent"), a firm authorised to hold client funds under the Solicitors Regulation Authority.

The Seller, the Buyer, the Company and the Escrow Agent are hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the date hereof (the "SPA").

(B) In connection with the SPA, a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller is to be held in escrow pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.

(C) The Parties have appointed the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount (as defined below) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

   "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

   "Closing Date" means 21 February 2026.

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,250,000, representing 12.5% of the total consideration under the SPA, deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account established by the Escrow Agent.

   "Escrow Account" means the client account designated by the Escrow Agent for the holding of the Escrow Amount.

   "Kestrel Medical Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14800179, whose registered office is at 24 Moorgate, London, being a material customer of the Company.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

3. Deposit

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or prior to the Closing Date.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

5. Payment

5.1 Any disbursement from the Escrow Account shall be made by the Escrow Agent via bank transfer to such account or accounts as may be designated in writing by the recipient Party.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in this Agreement or as otherwise notified.

6.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 127 Old Broad Street, London.

6.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 57 Old Broad Street, London.

6.4 Notices to the Company shall be sent to: 42 Moorgate, London.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all information received from any other Party in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

8.2 The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
