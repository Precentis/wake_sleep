SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 March 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Glenrock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11107890, whose registered office is at 9 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”); and

(2) Lowther Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 1428670, whose registered office is at 34 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Delamere Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13794172, whose registered office is at 167 Old Broad Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Material Adverse Change” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $235,000,000 (two hundred and thirty-five million US dollars).

3. Closing

3.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the “Closing”) shall take place on 11 June 2026 (the “Closing Date”) at the offices of the Seller or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer such instruments of transfer, share certificates and corporate authorisations as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

3.3 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated by the Seller in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

9. Warranties and Limitations

9.5 Limitation of Liability: The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties set out in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $44,062,500. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $235,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein, except where disclosure is required by law, regulation or any competent authority, or where such information is already in the public domain (other than as a result of a breach of this clause).

10.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall survive the Closing or any termination of this Agreement for a period of three years.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post (if within the United Kingdom) or by internationally recognised courier (if outside the United Kingdom) to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

11.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 9 Old Broad Street, London, and notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 34 Gresham Street, London.

12. General

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements and understandings between them.

12.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
