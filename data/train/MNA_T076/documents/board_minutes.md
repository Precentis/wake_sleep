**1. Call to Order**  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present.  

**2. Approval of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 10 February 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved that the minutes be approved as an accurate record and signed by the Chairman.  

**3. Matters Arising from Previous Minutes**  
No matters arose from the previous minutes.  

**4. Update on Proposed Sale of Entire Issued Share Capital**  
The board received an update regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Delamere Foods Ltd (the Company) by Lowther Holdings Ltd (the Buyer), from Glenrock Media Ltd (the Seller). The Buyer is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14282670, with its registered office at 34 Gresham Street, London. The Seller is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11107890, with its registered office at 9 Old Broad Street, London. The Company is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13794172, with its registered office at 167 Old Broad Street, London.  
The board noted that the transaction remains on track for completion, subject to standard conditions precedent. Legal documentation is being finalised under English law and will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

**5. Outstanding Legal Matter – Claim by Saltash Software Ltd**  
The board noted a letter before action received from Saltash Software Ltd, dated 8 April 2026, threatening legal proceedings against the Company for approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a software supply agreement. The claim relates to deliveries purportedly made between 15 October 2024 and 14 January 2025. The board acknowledged the seriousness of the claim and resolved that management shall monitor the matter closely, maintain full records of all communications, and ensure that the Company’s legal advisors are retained to assess the merits of the claim and prepare a formal response in accordance with English law. No admission of liability was made or authorised.  

**6. Material Customer Contract Renewal**  
The board discussed the status of the supply agreement with Thornbury Retail Group plc, a material customer incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10355278, with its registered office at 101 Cannon Street, London. The current agreement is due for renewal on 30 June 2026. Management reported ongoing negotiations and expressed cautious optimism regarding renewal on broadly similar terms. The board instructed the Commercial Director to report formally on progress by 24 April 2026.  

**7. Financial Performance Review**  
The CFO presented unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Revenue was reported at £8.7 million, with a gross profit margin of 22.4%. The board noted the results were in line with forecast.  

**8. Any Other Business**  
There were no other matters to discuss.  

**9. Adjournment**  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
