SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 18 March 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15919403, whose registered office is at 92 Bishopsgate, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Pemberton Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10809814, whose registered office is at 173 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Company is Ingleby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12044225, whose registered office is at 49 Moorgate, London.

RECITALS

A. The Seller holds the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 17 April 2026.

   "Company" means Ingleby Marine Ltd, company number 12044225.

   "Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax.

   "General Warranties" means all warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement, excluding the Fundamental Warranties.

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the sale and purchase of the Shares, as set out in Section 2.1.

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall acquire, the Shares for a total consideration of $100,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer in writing at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. CLOSING

4.1 Completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

4.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer;

   (b) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company, in such form as the Buyer may reasonably require;

   (c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to effect the transfer of the Shares and control of the Company.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or other communication required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given if delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post (in the case of the United Kingdom) or by internationally recognised courier service, addressed to the recipient at its registered office.

5.2 Notices shall be effective upon receipt if delivered by hand, two (2) Business Days after posting if sent by post, and one (1) Business Day after dispatch if sent by courier.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information acquired in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.3 The aggregate liability of the Seller under this Agreement for all claims arising out of or in connection with any breach of the warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 25% of the Purchase Price, being $31,250,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $100,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months. Any claim in respect of a warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
