# ESCROW AGREEMENT EXTRACT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 20 March 2026 (the “Execution Date”) between:

(1) Pemberton Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10809814, whose registered office is at 173 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15919403, whose registered office is at 92 Bishopsgate, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) HSBC Escrow Services Ltd, a company acting as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London for general business.

“Closing Date” means 17 April 2026.

“Company” means Ingleby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12044225, whose registered office is at 49 Moorgate, London.

“Confidential Information” means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by or on behalf of either the Buyer or the Seller to the Escrow Agent or otherwise arising in connection with this Agreement, including the terms of the share purchase agreement dated 20 March 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £2,500,000 held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement as security for potential indemnification claims under the share purchase agreement.

“Material Customer” means Yarrow Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13765373, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London.

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

**4. Release of Escrow Amount**

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains unclaimed, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent that, prior to such date, the Buyer has delivered to the Escrow Agent and the Seller a written notice specifying one or more claims for which indemnification is sought under the share purchase agreement (a “Claim Notice”). In the event a Claim Notice is validly served before the expiry of the 12-month period, the Escrow Agent shall retain the portion of the Escrow Amount corresponding to such claims (the “Held Amount”) and disburse it only upon the earliest of: (i) written joint instruction by the Buyer and the Seller; or (ii) receipt of a final and binding decision from arbitration conducted in accordance with Clause 12 directing the disbursement of such Held Amount.

**7. Payment Mechanics**

7.1 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in a designated interest-bearing account at HSBC Bank plc, London, in accordance with the instructions provided by the Buyer and the Seller at the Execution Date. Any interest accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and be subject to the same terms of release.

**9. Notices**

9.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as may be notified in writing.

9.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 173 Ludgate Hill, London, Attn: Company Secretary.

9.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 92 Bishopsgate, London, Attn: Legal Director.

**10. Confidentiality**

10.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except: (a) as required by law or regulatory authority; (b) to its professional advisers; or (c) to the extent necessary to enforce its rights under this Agreement.

**12. Governing Law and Arbitration**

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, and judgment on the award rendered by the arbitrator(s) may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof. The place of arbitration shall be New York, New York.
