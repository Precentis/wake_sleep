**Date:** 6 March 2027  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 135 Cannon Street, London, EC4A 6BZ  

**Chairman:** Mr Alistair Trent  
**Company Secretary:** Ms Clara Madsen  

## 1. Attendance

1.1. The following directors were present for the duration of the meeting:  
- Mr Alistair Trent  
- Ms Eleanor Voss  

1.2. The following directors were absent:  
- Mr Julian Parfitt  
- Ms Rachel Heng  
- Mr Samuel Boyd  

1.3. The Chairman confirmed that 2 directors were present, constituting a quorate meeting under the company’s Articles of Association, which require a minimum of two directors for a valid meeting.  

## 2. Apologies and Declarations of Interest

2.1. No apologies for absence were received.  

2.2. Ms Eleanor Voss declared a personal interest in Netherby Holdings Ltd but confirmed she had no financial or material interest in the transaction under discussion that conflicted with the interests of Quayside Logistics plc.  

## 3. Agenda Item 1: Consideration of the Proposed Share Sale

3.1. The Chairman introduced the matter concerning the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Logistics plc by Netherby Holdings Ltd from Ingleby Retail Group plc.  

3.2. The Company Secretary confirmed that the transaction was governed by a draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 March 2027, a copy of which had been circulated to all directors.  

3.3. The Chairman outlined the key terms of the transaction:  
- The Seller, Ingleby Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15735132), proposes to transfer 100% of the issued share capital of Quayside Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13292017) to the Buyer, Netherby Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13239288).  
- The transaction is conditional upon certain pre-completion undertakings, including the maintenance of contracts with material customers.  
- Foxley Marine Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11631842) is a material customer under the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement.  
- The Share Purchase Agreement is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

## 4. Discussion

4.1. Mr Alistair Trent noted that the Company had conducted due diligence on the Buyer and considered the transaction to be in the best interests of Quayside Logistics plc and its stakeholders.  

4.2. Ms Eleanor Voss confirmed that no board resolution or shareholder approval was required from Quayside Logistics plc for the transaction, as the shares are held at shareholder level by Ingleby Retail Group plc.  

4.3. The directors discussed the operational continuity of the Company post-completion and acknowledged the importance of maintaining existing commercial relationships, particularly with Foxley Marine Ltd.  

## 5. RESOLVED

5.1. **RESOLVED** that the board of Quayside Logistics plc hereby approves, for information and noting purposes, the entry by Ingleby Retail Group plc into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 March 2027 with Netherby Holdings Ltd regarding the sale of 100% of the issued share capital of Quayside Logistics plc.  

5.2. **RESOLVED** that the Company Secretary be authorised to provide such assistance as may be required to facilitate the completion of the transaction, including the provision of corporate records and confirmations as to the status of the Company.  

## 6. Adjournment

6.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
