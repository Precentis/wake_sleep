ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 5 March 2027 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Netherby Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13239288 and registered office at 12 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”);

(2) Ingleby Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15735132 and registered office at 77 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors appointed as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

The Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are each referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.

RECITALS

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 4 March 2027 (the “Purchase Agreement”) with the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13292017 and registered office at 135 Cannon Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) In connection with the Purchase Agreement, the Parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase consideration, amounting to 18% of the total consideration, shall be deposited into an escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) to secure the Seller’s indemnification obligations under the Purchase Agreement.

(C) The Escrow Agent has agreed to hold and administer the escrow funds (the “Escrow Amount”) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Claim” means a written notice by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller asserting a liability for which the Seller is indemnifiable under the Purchase Agreement and seeking release of all or part of the Escrow Amount.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of 18% of the total purchase consideration paid under the Purchase Agreement, deposited into the Escrow Account on the Effective Date.

“Purchase Agreement” has the meaning set out in Recital (A).

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. DEPOSIT OF ESCROW AMOUNT

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account maintained with the Escrow Agent on the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement.

3. PAYMENT AND RELEASE

3.1 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Account only upon receipt of joint written instructions from the Buyer and the Seller, or pursuant to a final arbitration award or court order.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the recipient’s registered office or such other address as notified in writing.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed delivered when sent by confirmed email or on the second business day after posting by first-class pre-paid mail.

5. CLAIM PROCEDURE

5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim by delivering a written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both Parties.

7. ESCROW AGENT DUTIES

7.1 The Escrow Agent acts as a stakeholder and owes no duty to either Party beyond the terms of this Agreement.

7.2 The Escrow Agent may rely on instructions believed in good faith to be genuine.

8. FEES AND EXPENSES

8.1 The Buyer and the Seller shall each pay 50% of the Escrow Agent’s fees and expenses.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 The Escrow Account shall be terminated upon the release of the entire Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION

12.1 This escrow agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce.
