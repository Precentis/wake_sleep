SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 20 February 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Ingleby Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 15735132, whose registered office is at 77 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Netherby Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 13239288, whose registered office is at 12 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 13292017, whose registered office is at 135 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"Closing" means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 15 April 2027;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event, circumstance or condition which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company taken as a whole, excluding any such effect arising from (i) changes in general economic, political, regulatory or market conditions affecting the United Kingdom or global economy, (ii) changes affecting the logistics and transportation industry generally, or (iii) pandemics, epidemics or outbreaks of disease, whether or not declared as such by any governmental or supranational authority;

"Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $180,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT AND CLOSING

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales to settle any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant party.

16.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 77 Moorgate, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 12 King William Street, London.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and not to disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent obligations of confidentiality.
