ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF QUAYSIDE LOGISTICS PLC

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
"Buyer" means Netherby Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13239288, whose registered office is at 12 King William Street, London;  
"Company" means Quayside Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company officer number 13292017, whose registered office is at 135 Cannon Street, London;  
"Foxley Marine Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11631842, whose registered office is at 18 King William Street, London;  
"Meeting" means a meeting of the Board or of shareholders, as the context requires;  
"Seller" means Ingleby Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15735132, whose registered office is at 77 Moorgate, London;  
"these Articles" means the articles of association for the time being in force as the same may be amended in accordance with the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles.

1.2 The headings in these Articles are inserted for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of these Articles.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The Company's share capital shall be such amount as may be determined from time to time by ordinary resolution of the shareholders.  
2.2 The rights attaching to any class of shares may be varied only with the consent in writing of the holders of not less than seventy-five per cent (75%) of the issued shares of that class, or with the approval of a special resolution passed at a general meeting of the holders of that class.

3. Payment for Shares  
3.1 All monies payable for shares in the Company shall be paid in sterling to the Company’s nominated bank account as notified in writing by the Board from time to time.  
3.2 Any failure to make payment within fourteen (14) days of invoice constitutes an immediate default, entitling the Company to cancel the allotment of such shares unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Board.

4. Board Meetings and Resolutions  
4.1 The Board shall meet as required for the proper management of the Company’s affairs, and at least four (4) times per calendar year.  
4.2 Notice of a Board meeting shall be given not less than forty-eight (48) hours prior to such meeting, unless all directors waive such notice in writing.  
4.3 A resolution in writing signed by all the directors entitled to receive notice of a Board meeting shall be as valid as if passed at a duly convened and quorate meeting.  
4.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. No business shall be transacted at any Board meeting unless a quorum is present at the time when the relevant resolution is proposed.

5. Notices  
5.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given to a director, shareholder or the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or transmitted by email to the address or email address last known to the Company.  
5.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed to be given forty-eight (48) hours after being posted to the addressee’s last registered address.  
5.3 A notice transmitted by email shall be deemed to be given at the time of transmission if delivered before 5:00 p.m. on a business day, and otherwise at 9:00 a.m. on the next business day.

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 Directors, officers and employees of the Company shall not, without the prior written consent of the Board, disclose any information concerning the Company’s business, operations, customers (including Foxley Marine Ltd), financial condition or strategic plans to any third party, unless required by law.  
6.2 The obligations under this Article 6 shall survive termination of office or employment for a period of five (5) years.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
7.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them or their subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles or their subject matter or formation.
