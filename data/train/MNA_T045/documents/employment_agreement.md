1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“the Business” means the business of manufacturing, distribution and sale of ambient food products conducted by Ashcombe Foods Ltd and its subsidiary undertakings, including Ivybridge Holdings Ltd following completion of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer from the Seller.

“the Company” means Ivybridge Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10114141, whose registered office is at 67 Ludgate Hill, London.

“the Buyer” means Ashcombe Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13456470, whose registered office is at 179 Fleet Street, London.

“the Seller” means Pemberton Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10273442, whose registered office is at 133 Ludgate Hill, London.

“Confidential Information” includes all trade secrets, technical data, customer lists, supplier arrangements, pricing strategies, financial information, and any other proprietary information disclosed by the Company, the Buyer, or any member of the Ashcombe Foods group, whether prior to or after the date of this Agreement, and whether in written, oral or electronic form.

“Founder” means Mr. Julian Hartley, who founded the original operating entity that evolved into Ivybridge Holdings Ltd.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The employment of the Founder commenced on 1 May 2015 and continues on an at-will basis subject to the notice provisions contained herein.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Founder shall receive an annual base salary of £87,500, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 In addition to the base salary, the Founder shall be eligible to receive a performance-related bonus of up to 22% of annual salary, subject to the achievement of individual and corporate performance targets as determined by the Board of Directors of the Buyer.

4. Working Time and Leave

4.1 The Founder shall work such hours as are necessary to perform his duties, typically between 09:00 and 17:30, Monday to Friday, at the Company’s registered office or such other location as required.

4.2 The Founder is entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of statutory holidays.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Founder shall not, during the term of employment or at any time thereafter, disclose or make use of any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of his duties or as compelled by law.

5.2 Upon termination of employment, the Founder shall immediately return all documents, electronic files and other materials containing Confidential Information.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to 67 Ludgate Hill, London, and to the Buyer at 179 Fleet Street, London.

7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six months’ written notice to the other.

7.2 The Company may terminate the employment summarily for gross misconduct without notice or payment in lieu.

18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by reason of his role and access to sensitive information, it is reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Buyer and the Company that he be subject to post-termination restrictions.

18.2 Accordingly, the Founder shall not, for a period of 12 months after termination of employment, whether by resignation, dismissal or otherwise, directly or indirectly carry on, be employed by, engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the Business anywhere in England and Wales.

18.3 For the avoidance of doubt, a competing business includes any entity supplying ambient food products to Ingleby Analytics Ltd (company number 10293798, registered office 9 Ludgate Hill, London) or any of its subsidiary undertakings.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
