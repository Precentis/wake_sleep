# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Ivybridge Holdings Ltd

**Date of Meeting:** 27 June 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** Registered Office, 67 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7HH  
**Chairman:** Mr. Julian Hartley  
**Company Secretary:** Ms. Eleanor Pike  

## 1. Attendance and Quorum

1.1 The following directors were present:  
- Mr. Julian Hartley  
- Ms. Caroline Naylor  

1.2 The Company has five (5) appointed directors. 2 directors were present, and the presence of 2 directors constitutes a quorum under the Articles of Association. The meeting was duly quorate.

1.3 The Company Secretary was in attendance to record proceedings.

## 2. Confirmation of Agenda

2.1 The Chairman proposed the following agenda, which was approved unanimously:

- (a) Confirmation of quorum and attendance  
- (b) Review of proposed transaction with Ashcombe Foods Ltd  
- (c) Approval of entry into Share Purchase Agreement  
- (d) Any other business  
- (e) Adjournment

## 3. Review of Proposed Transaction

3.1 The Chairman presented details of a proposed transaction pursuant to which Pemberton Industries Ltd (the Seller), company number 10273442, incorporated in England and Wales, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Holdings Ltd (the Company), company number 10114141, incorporated in England and Wales, to Ashcombe Foods Ltd (the Buyer), company number 13456470, incorporated in England and Wales.

3.2 The proposed acquisition involves the transfer of 100% of the shares in the Company, held by the Seller, to the Buyer under a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 28 June 2026.

3.3 The Board reviewed the material terms of the transaction, including the consideration, representations, warranties, and covenants to be entered into by the parties, as disclosed in the draft Share Purchase Agreement.

3.4 The Board noted that the Company’s principal customer, Ingleby Analytics Ltd (company number 10293798, incorporated in England and Wales), has been consulted regarding the proposed change of control, and no material objections or termination rights have been triggered under existing commercial contracts as of the date hereof.

## 4. Approval of Entry into Share Purchase Agreement

4.1 RESOLVED that, having considered the strategic, financial, and operational implications of the transaction, and having received legal and financial advice, the Board hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement between Pemberton Industries Ltd and Ashcombe Foods Ltd in relation to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Holdings Ltd.

4.2 RESOLVED FURTHER that Mr. Julian Hartley and Ms. Caroline Naylor are authorised to take all necessary and appropriate steps to execute, deliver, and perform the Share Purchase Agreement on behalf of the Company, including providing consents, confirmations, and ancillary documentation required under the terms of the transaction.

4.3 RESOLVED FURTHER that the Company Secretary is authorised to file all relevant documents with Companies House and notify stakeholders in accordance with applicable law and regulatory requirements.

## 5. Any Other Business

5.1 There was no other business to be discussed.

## 6. Adjournment

6.1 The meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
