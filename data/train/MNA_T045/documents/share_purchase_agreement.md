**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **5 June 2026**

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Pemberton Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10273442**, whose registered office is at 133 Ludgate Hill, London (the **"Seller"**);

(2) **Ashcombe Foods Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13456470**, whose registered office is at 179 Fleet Street, London (the **"Buyer"**); and

(3) **Ivybridge Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10114141**, whose registered office is at 67 Ludgate Hill, London (the **"Company"**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"**Material Adverse Change**" means any event or circumstance which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, excluding (a) changes in general economic or political conditions in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates, (b) changes affecting the industry in which the Company operates generally, and (c) pandemics.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$145,000,000** (the "**Purchase Price**").

**3. PAYMENT**

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Time for payment shall be of the essence.

**4. CLOSING**

4.1 Closing shall take place on **28 September 2026** (the "**Closing Date**"), at 10:00 a.m. (London time), or such other time or date as the parties may agree in writing.

4.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer such instruments of transfer, board resolutions and other documents as may be necessary to vest legal and beneficial title to the entire issued share capital of the Company in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

**5. NOTICES**

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as a party may notify in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 179 Fleet Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 133 Ludgate Hill, London.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES**

**10.1** The General Warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **72 months**. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the relevant party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**12. NON-COMPETE**

**12.1** The Seller covenants that for a period of **4 years** from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

13.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
