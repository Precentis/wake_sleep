ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF IVYBRIDGE HOLDINGS LTD  
(Extract)  

1. Interpretation  

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Ashcombe Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 13456470, whose registered office is at 179 Fleet Street, London;  
“Company” means Ivybridge Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10114141, whose registered office is at 67 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Seller” means Pemberton Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10273442, whose registered office is at 133 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Material Customer” means Ingleby Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10293798, whose registered office is at 9 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Ordinary Share” means an ordinary share in the capital of the Company;  
“Secretary” means the company secretary of the Company, if any; and  
“Writing” includes any method of reproducing words legibly, including by electronic means.  

1.2 The singular includes the plural and vice versa, and reference to any gender includes all genders.  

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  

2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 Ordinary Shares of £1.00 each.  

2.2 The rights attached to the Ordinary Shares include the right to receive dividends as declared, the right to vote at general meetings, and the right to a share in the surplus assets of the Company upon liquidation.  

2.3 The Company may, with the approval of a special resolution of the shareholders, increase its share capital, consolidate or divide shares, or cancel unissued shares.  

3. Transfer of Shares  

3.1 Subject to these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of their shares by instrument in Writing under the form prescribed in the Companies Act 2006 or such other form as the Board may approve.  

3.2 Every transfer of shares shall be completed only upon registration of the transferee’s name in the register of members, which shall be within 28 days of receipt of the instrument of transfer and any required documentation.  

4. Board of Directors  

4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all powers of the Company except those reserved for shareholders by law or these Articles.  

4.2 The Board may appoint such officers and agents as it deems necessary to conduct the business of the Company.  

4.3 The Board may meet at any time and place as determined by the directors, and meetings may be convened by any director or the Secretary.  

4.4 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes. In the case of equality, the chair of the meeting shall not have a second or casting vote.  

5. Quorum for Board Meetings  

5.1 Article 11: The quorum for any meeting of the Board shall be three directors. If at any time during a meeting the number of directors present falls below the quorum, no further business shall be transacted unless otherwise resolved by unanimous consent of the directors then present.  

6. Payment and Financial Provisions  

6.1 All payments made by or to the Company shall be recorded in sterling unless otherwise approved by the Board for specific international transactions.  

6.2 The Company shall maintain audited financial statements in accordance with the requirements of the Companies Act 2006 and applicable accounting standards.  

7. Notices  

7.1 Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in Writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class post, or transmitted by electronic means to the registered office of the relevant party.  

7.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. A notice sent electronically shall be deemed served upon receipt of confirmation of delivery.  

8. Confidentiality  

8.1 Directors, officers and employees of the Company shall not disclose any confidential information concerning the Company’s operations, clients (including but not limited to the Material Customer), financial position, or strategic plans to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.  

8.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall continue indefinitely after cessation of office or employment.  

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  

9.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  

9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.
