SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 September 2026.

Parties:

(1) Netherby Technologies Ltd (company number 13483114), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 69 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Vantage Robotics Ltd (company number 11978147), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 68 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”).

Background:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Fenwick Engineering Ltd (company number 11607786), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 165 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer has agreed to purchase, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Closing” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

“Closing Date” means 4 January 2027;

“Closing Working Capital” means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1;

“EBITDA” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 (“FY2026”), calculated in accordance with the accounting policies consistently applied by the Company;

“Earn-out Payment” means the payment calculated in accordance with Clause 3.4;

“Material Adverse Change” means any event, change or effect that, individually or in the aggregate with any other events, changes or effects, has had a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, excluding changes in general economic or political conditions, changes affecting the industry in which the Company operates generally, and pandemics;

“Purchase Price” means the aggregate sum payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1;

“Target Working Capital” means $3,500,000.

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase the Shares from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell the Shares to the Buyer, for a total consideration of $20,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in accordance with Clauses 2.2 and 2.3.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cash on the Closing Date by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such adjustment shall be determined in good faith by the Buyer following delivery of the Closing Working Capital Statement and shall be final and binding on the Parties unless the Seller gives written notice of dispute within 20 Business Days. The amount of any such reduction shall constitute a direct reduction to the Purchase Price.

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional earn-out consideration (the “Earn-out Payment”) equal to three (3) times the amount by which the FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $6,500,000, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated and paid within 90 days following the end of FY2026, based on the audited financial statements of the Company for that year.

9.4 The Seller shall have no liability in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $200,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller shall not be liable for any claim unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds the $200,000 threshold, and liability, if triggered, shall be limited to the excess above such threshold.

9.7 The Buyer shall not have any right to make a claim under this Agreement for breach of warranty unless it gives written notice to the Seller specifying in reasonable detail the nature of the claim within 30 days of becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the claim.

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

10.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter.
