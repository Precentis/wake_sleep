**ESCROW AGREEMENT**

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 October 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and among:

(1) **Netherby Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13483114 whose registered office is at 69 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) **Vantage Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11978147 whose registered office is at 68 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) **Fenwick Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11607786 whose registered office is at 165 Gresham Street, London (the "Company").

The Buyer, Seller and Company are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

(4) **Cleartrust Escrow Services Ltd**, a company duly authorised to provide escrow services, whose registered office is at 10 Fenchurch Avenue, London, EC3M 5AA (the "Escrow Agent").

**WHEREAS**:

(A) The Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of the Company pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 October 2026 (the "Purchase Agreement").

(B) In connection with the Purchase Agreement, the Buyer and Seller have agreed that a portion of the consideration shall be held in escrow to secure certain indemnities and claims.

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount (as defined below) under the terms of this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- **"Claim Notice"** means a written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 10, specifying in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the factual basis, and the amount claimed from the Escrow Amount.
- **"Closing Date"** means 9 October 2026.
- **"Escrow Amount"** means £1,250,000 (one million two hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 12.5% of the total consideration under the Purchase Agreement, deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account with the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date.
- **"Escrow Period"** means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending at 5:00 p.m. on 9 October 2027.
- **"Indemnity Obligations"** means the Seller’s indemnity obligations under Clause 8 of the Purchase Agreement, including but not limited to liabilities arising from breach of warranties, tax liabilities, and pre-completion obligations.
- **"Material Customer"** means Oakhurst Medical Ltd, company number 15416040, registered office 36 Gresham Street, London.

**2. Escrow Deposit**

2.1 The Buyer shall, on or before the Closing Date, deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") established with the Escrow Agent under account number ETX-7742-FE.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Amount in strict accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

**3. Release of Escrow Amount**

3.1 Upon the expiry of the Escrow Period, any portion of the Escrow Amount not subject to a pending Claim Notice shall be released to the Seller within three (3) Business Days.

3.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim Notice at the expiry of the Escrow Period shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claim.

**4. Payment Mechanism**

4.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made in pounds sterling by same-day cleared funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient.

**5. Claims**

5.1 A Party seeking to make a claim against the Escrow Amount (the "Claiming Party") shall deliver a Claim Notice to the Escrow Agent and the other Party.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

**6. Escrow Agent’s Role**

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with instructions signed by both the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise directed by final order of a court of competent jurisdiction.

6.2 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith reliance upon instructions believed to be genuine.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each Party agrees to keep the terms of this Agreement and all matters related to the Escrow Account confidential, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

**8. Notices**

8.1 Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by prepaid first-class post, or by email to the addresses set out in the preamble.

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

**10. Miscellaneous**

10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding among the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

10.2 No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by all Parties.
