ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Kestrel Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10202293, whose registered office is at 139 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Orwell Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13418160, whose registered office is at 40 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a firm authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, whose registered office is [Address of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about 10 June 2026 (the "Purchase Agreement") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Merrion Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14055295 and whose registered office is at 135 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the consideration under the Purchase Agreement, an amount equal to 15% of the total purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the Purchase Agreement;

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London;

"Claim" means any notification by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a right to indemnification under Clause 8 of the Purchase Agreement;

"Purchase Agreement" has the meaning given in the recitals.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") separate from its own funds and in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall cause the Escrow Amount to be paid to the Escrow Agent within three Business Days of the Effective Date.

3.2 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, or any portion thereof, to the Seller on 15 June 2026, unless prior to such date a Claim has been validly notified.

4. Payment Mechanics

4.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made by same-day value sterling wire transfer to the account designated in writing by the relevant Party.

5. Claims

5.1 A Claim shall be validly made only if delivered in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent at their respective registered offices, or such other address as may be notified pursuant to Clause 7.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from another Party in connection with this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or a regulatory authority.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant Party.

8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

8.3 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
