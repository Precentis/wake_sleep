# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 8 June 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Orwell Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13418160, whose registered office is at 40 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) **Kestrel Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10202293, whose registered office is at 139 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Merrion Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14055295, whose registered office is at 135 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, a person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that person.

"Control" (and its cognates) means, in relation to a company, the power to secure, whether through the ownership of voting shares, by contract or otherwise, that a person's affairs are conducted in accordance with the wishes of that person.

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 4.

"Closing Date" means 11 September 2026.

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied, and for the purposes of Clause 3.4, specifically excluding any one-off restructuring costs incurred prior to the Closing Date.

"FY2026" means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is **$50,000,000** (fifty million US dollars), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

## 3. Payment Mechanism

3.1 The Buyer shall pay $40,000,000 of the Purchase Price by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3.00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

3.2 The remaining $10,000,000 of the Purchase Price shall be held in escrow pursuant to the terms of a separate escrow agreement to be entered into at Closing, to secure the Seller’s indemnification obligations under this Agreement.

3.4 **Earn-out Payment**: The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to three times (3x) the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds **$4,000,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be calculated based on the audited financial statements of the Company for FY2026. The Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed **$10,000,000** in aggregate. Payment shall be made within 60 days of the finalisation of the FY2026 audited accounts.

## 4. Completion

4.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree.

4.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all instruments of transfer, board resolutions and such other documents as may be necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares free from all charges, liens and encumbrances.

## 9. Warranties and Indemnities

9.7 Any claim for breach of warranty must be notified by the Buyer to the Seller in writing, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim, within **30 days** of the date on which the Buyer becomes aware of the relevant breach. Failure to comply with this requirement shall bar the claim.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party, disclose to any third party any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby.

10.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of two years.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

11.3 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the respective party.

11.4 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, correspondence and agreements.
