2. Supply of Goods  
2.1 The Company shall supply, and the Customer shall purchase, the marine components specified in Schedule A (the “Goods”) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Payment  
3.1 The Customer shall pay the invoice price for the Goods within forty-five (45) days of the date of the Company’s invoice.  
3.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling to the bank account nominated in writing by the Company from time to time, without any set-off, counterclaim or deduction.

4. Delivery  
4.1 The Company shall deliver the Goods to the location specified in Schedule B (the “Delivery Location”) on or before the delivery date specified in the applicable purchase order.

5. Title and Risk  
5.1 Risk in the Goods shall pass to the Customer upon delivery at the Delivery Location.  
5.2 Title to the Goods shall pass to the Customer upon receipt of full payment for the Goods.

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.  
6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  
7.2 Notices shall be deemed received:  
(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, two (2) business days after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

8. Term and Termination  
8.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date of execution and shall continue for an initial term of five (5) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.
