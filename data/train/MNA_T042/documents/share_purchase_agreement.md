SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 18 February 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Whitlock Industries Ltd (company number 15706206), a company incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 37 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Brightwater Energy plc (company number 14865458), a company incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 140 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Foxley Robotics Ltd (company number 15132369), a company incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 28 Gresham Street, London (the "Company").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 29 April 2026;

   "Purchase Price" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means all the issued shares in the capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares at the Closing for a total consideration of $55,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in cash on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 3.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree.

3.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer in respect of the Shares;

   (b) the share certificates in respect of the Shares;

   (c) such other documents and assurances as may be reasonably required to vest legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares in the Buyer free from all Encumbrances.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction of the following Conditions:

   (a) the Buyer having obtained all necessary corporate approvals;

   (b) the Seller having provided audited financial statements of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2025;

   (c) no Material Adverse Change having occurred in the business, assets or financial condition of the Company.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 19 May 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any and all claims arising under or in connection with the Warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $4,950,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $55,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1.

10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under any Warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information acquired in connection with the transaction contemplated by this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

12. NOTICES

12.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class post or by email to the registered office of the relevant party.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

13.2 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
