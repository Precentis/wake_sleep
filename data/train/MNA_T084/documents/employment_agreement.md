Employment Agreement – Extract

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 May 2026 between:

(1) Pemberton Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10939197, whose registered office is at 54 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Employer"); and

(2) [Full Name of Founder], of [Address] (the "Founder").

Clause 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to any person, a company which is from time to time a subsidiary or holding company of that person, or a subsidiary of a holding company of that person;

"Business" means the business carried on by the Employer as at the date of this Agreement, including but not limited to the design, development, and supply of proprietary software solutions for enterprise clients;

"Confidential Information" includes all trade secrets, technical data, know-how, customer lists, pricing information, software code, and business strategies disclosed to or otherwise known by the Founder during the course of employment;

"Effective Date" means 12 May 2026.

Clause 2. Commencement and Term

2.1 The Founder's employment under this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with its terms.

Clause 3. Position and Duties

3.1 The Founder shall serve as Chief Technology Officer of the Employer.

3.2 The Founder shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as are customary for such position and as may be lawfully assigned by the board of directors of the Employer from time to time.

Clause 4. Remuneration

4.1 The Employer shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £95,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrear.

4.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme determined by the board of directors, subject to achievement of performance criteria.

Clause 5. Expenses

5.1 The Employer shall reimburse the Founder for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses properly incurred in the performance of duties under this Agreement, upon submission of receipts.

Clause 6. Working Time and Leave

6.1 The normal working hours shall be 35 hours per week, Monday to Friday.

6.2 The Founder shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays.

Clause 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Founder shall not, during or after employment, without prior written consent of the Employer, disclose or make use of any Confidential Information.

7.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.

Clause 8. Notices

8.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the Employer or the last known address of the Founder.

8.2 Notices to the Employer shall be addressed to the Company Secretary at 54 Queen Victoria Street, London.

Clause 18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by reason of the nature of the business and the position held, it is reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Employer that the Founder be subject to post-termination restrictions.

18.2 Accordingly, the Founder covenants that, for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether voluntary or otherwise, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage, be employed, concerned or interested in any capacity in any business which is in competition with the Business of the Employer as carried on at the date of termination.

18.3 This restriction applies within a radius of 25 miles from the registered office of the Employer and to any client with whom the Employer had a material business relationship during the 12 months preceding termination, including but not limited to Galloway Foods Ltd, company number 13183394.

Clause 19. General

19.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
