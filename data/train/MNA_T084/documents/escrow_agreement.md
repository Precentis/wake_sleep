ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] May 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) CORRIB CAPITAL LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12681640 and having its registered office at 49 Bishopsgate, London (the “Buyer”);

(2) MARLOW ROBOTICS LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14659354 and having its registered office at 75 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”); and

(3) [ESCROW AGENT NAME], a firm authorised to act as escrow agent under English law (the “Escrow Agent”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about 12 May 2026 (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10939197 and having its registered office at 54 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) As part of the purchase price under the SPA, an amount equal to 15% of the total consideration has been deposited into an escrow account (the “Escrow Amount”) to secure certain indemnities and warranties provided by the Seller.

(C) The parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are generally open for business in London.

“Claim” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent asserting a liability of the Seller under the SPA, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of such liability and the amount claimed.

“Effective Date” means the date on which this Agreement is executed by all parties.

“Galloway Foods Ltd” means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13183394 and having its registered office at 122 Cannon Street, London, which is a material customer of the Company.

“Escrow Account” means the segregated account maintained by the Escrow Agent at a designated financial institution in which the Escrow Amount is held.

“SPA” has the meaning given in the Recitals.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Escrow Arrangement

2.1 The Seller shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account within five Business Days of the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely as agent for the Buyer and the Seller, without ownership interest, and shall be released in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Payment and Release

3.1 On the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date (the “Release Date”), the Escrow Agent shall release to the Seller the balance of the Escrow Amount, less any amounts subject to a pending Claim.

3.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount not subject to a Claim on the Release Date shall be released to the Seller without further consent.

4. Claims

4.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim on the Escrow Agent only with the prior written consent of the Seller or pursuant to a final determination by a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5. Procedures

5.1 The Buyer shall deliver any Claim to the Escrow Agent and the Seller by registered post or email to the addresses specified in Clause 7.

5.2 A Claim shall only be valid if it complies with Clause 4.2 and includes supporting documentation reasonably sufficient to assess the basis of the claim.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both parties.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.

8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by English law.

8.2 The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
