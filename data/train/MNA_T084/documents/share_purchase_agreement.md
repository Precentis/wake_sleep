SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 May 2026.

Parties:

(1) Marlow Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14659354, whose registered office is at 75 Moorgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) Corrib Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12681640, whose registered office is at 49 Bishopsgate, London (the "Buyer").

Background:

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10939197, whose registered office is at 54 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

B. The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

Now this Agreement witnesses:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, including the recitals, the following definitions apply:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 29 June 2026;

   "Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4;

   "Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties set out in Clause 7.1 (Title), Clause 7.2 (Capacity) and Clause 7.3 (Tax);

   "General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Clauses 7.4 to 7.12 inclusive;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the "Purchase Price") is $200,000,000, payable in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

3. Closing

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

4. Conditions

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or (where applicable) waiver of the Conditions.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

7. Warranties

7.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that it has good legal and beneficial title to the Shares, free from all Encumbrances.

7.2 The Seller has the full capacity and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement.

7.3 The Company has duly filed all tax returns and paid all taxes due under applicable law, and there are no material disputes with any tax authority.

9. Warranty Claims

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified to the Seller in writing within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the claim.

10. Survival

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

12. Non-Competition

12.1 The Seller undertakes to the Buyer that, for a period of 5 years from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial or other interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at Closing, including but not limited to any business supplying technology solutions to Galloway Foods Ltd (company number 13183394).

13. Notices

13.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by first-class pre-paid letter or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above (or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other).

14. General

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings.

14.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
