4. Agenda Item 1 – Approval of Share Purchase Agreement  
The directors considered the draft Share Purchase Agreement (the "SPA") dated 5 February 2026, pursuant to which Kingsmere Systems Ltd ("Seller"), incorporated in England and Wales (company number 12213592), registered office at 122 Gresham Street, London, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to Yarrow Retail Group plc ("Buyer"), incorporated in England and Wales (company number 10176904), registered office at 68 King William Street, London.

The directors noted that the Company is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15147811, with registered office at 176 Cannon Street, London.

The directors reviewed the terms of the SPA, including but not limited to the obligations of the Company post-completion, the treatment of existing contracts, and the status of material customers, specifically Arden Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales (company number 15698138), registered office at 23 Cannon Street, London.

The directors received legal and financial advice from external counsel and financial advisers, confirming that the transaction was in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders.

After full discussion, it was:  

RESOLVED THAT the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement by Kingsmere Systems Ltd and Yarrow Retail Group plc, pursuant to which the Seller shall sell to the Buyer the entire issued share capital of the Company, being 100% of the shares in the Company, be and is hereby approved by the board of the Company for the purposes of Sections 170 and 171 of the Companies Act 2006, subject to the transaction being governed by English law and the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
