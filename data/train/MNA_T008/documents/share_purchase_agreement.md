SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 February 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Kingsmere Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12213592, whose registered office is at 122 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Yarrow Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10176904, whose registered office is at 68 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Foxley Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15147811, whose registered office is at 176 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, "Closing Date" means 28 February 2026.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the entire issued share capital of the Company (the "Purchase Price") is $125,000,000, to be paid in immediately available funds on the Closing Date in accordance with clause 3.

3. PAYMENT MECHANISM

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer no later than five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Payment in accordance with clause 3.1 shall be deemed full satisfaction of the Buyer's obligations under clause 2.1, and the Seller shall have no further claim against the Buyer in respect of the Purchase Price.

4. CONDITIONS AND CLOSING

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or, where permitted, waiver of the following conditions (the "Conditions"):

(a) receipt of all necessary third-party consents, including but not limited to those required under any agreement to which the Company is a party;

(b) no injunction or restraining order having been issued by any court of competent jurisdiction which, in the reasonable opinion of either party, would prevent the Closing;

(c) the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the other party, in all material respects, as of the Closing Date, except where such inaccuracy arises from circumstances disclosed in writing prior to the date of this Agreement.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 1 February 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided that such right to terminate shall not be available to any party that has materially failed to perform or comply with its obligations under this Agreement.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class recorded delivery, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 122 Gresham Street, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary, with a copy by email to legal@kingsmere-systems.co.uk.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 68 King William Street, London, and to the attention of the General Counsel, with a copy by email to manda.legal@yarrowretail.com.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket) The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $625,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller shall have no liability for any amount up to and including $625,000, and liability shall arise solely in respect of the excess.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received pursuant to or in connection with this Agreement, unless disclosure is required by law or regulation or by a regulatory authority.

10.2 The obligations under clause 10.1 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available other than by breach of this clause.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
