ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] 2026 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Kingsmere Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12213592, whose registered office is at 122 Gresham Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Yarrow Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10176904, whose registered office is at 68 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number [●], whose registered office is at [●] (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof (the “SPA”) with the Seller pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Foxley Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15147811 whose registered office is at 176 Cannon Street, London (the “Company”), on the terms and subject to the conditions set out therein.

(B) In accordance with the terms of the SPA, an amount (the “Escrow Amount”) has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to be held in escrow and released in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

“Closing Date” means 28 February 2026.

“Claim” means any claim made by the Buyer against the Seller under the SPA for breach of warranty, indemnity or otherwise, to the extent such claim is validly notified in accordance with this Agreement.

“Escrow Account” means the account designated by the Escrow Agent into which the Escrow Amount is deposited.

“Parties” means collectively the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent.

2. Escrow Arrangement

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account within two (2) Business Days of the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and applicable law.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 4, the Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as may remain after any deductions under valid Claims, shall be released from the Escrow Account to the Seller.

4. Conditions to Release

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending Claims notified before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such Claim is finally resolved by agreement or determination under the SPA, whereupon such portion shall be released to the Party entitled thereto.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify in writing.

5.2 A notice shall be deemed served on the day of delivery if delivered by hand, two (2) Business Days after posting if sent by pre-paid post, or on the same day if sent by email (provided that electronic confirmation of receipt is obtained).

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
