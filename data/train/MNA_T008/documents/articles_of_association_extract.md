# EXTRACT OF ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF FOXLEY MARINE LTD

**Interpretation and Definitions**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) “Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
   (b) “Buyer” means Yarrow Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10176904 and registered office at 68 King William Street, London;  
   (c) “Company” means Foxley Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15147811 and registered office at 176 Cannon Street, London;  
   (d) “Seller” means Kingsmere Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12213592 and registered office at 122 Gresham Street, London;  
   (e) “Arden Bioscience plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15698138 and registered office at 23 Cannon Street, London, being a material customer of the Company;  
   (f) any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time; and  
   (g) words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

**Board of Directors**

2. The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the authority to exercise all such powers and do all such acts and things as may be exercised or done by the Company, subject to the Companies Act 2006, these Articles and any directions given by special resolution.

3. The Board may from time to time delegate any of its powers to any committee consisting of one or more directors, and may revoke or vary any such delegation.

4. The Board shall meet at least once in every calendar quarter unless all directors waive such requirement in writing.

**Quorum and Proceedings**

11. The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors. If within one hour from the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other day, time and place as the Board may determine by notice in writing.

12. A director may participate in a meeting of the Board or of any committee of the Board by means of conference telephone or other communications equipment permitting all persons participating in the meeting to hear each other, and participation in such a manner shall constitute presence and participation in person at the meeting.

**Share Capital and Transfers**

15. The Company’s share capital shall consist of ordinary shares and such other classes of shares as may from time to time be created in accordance with these Articles and the Companies Act 2006.

16. Subject to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, the Board may at any time and from time to time allot, grant options over, or subdivide, consolidate, redeem or purchase its shares.

**Payment Mechanism**

18. Any payment required to be made by the Buyer to the Seller in connection with the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be made by electronic transfer to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the date of payment.

**Confidentiality**

19. Each party shall, except as required by law or any regulatory authority, treat as confidential and not disclose to any third party any information received from the other party in connection with the affairs of the Company or the transaction involving the transfer of the entire issued share capital to the Buyer, including but not limited to financial statements, customer contracts, and operational procedures.

20. The obligations under Clause 19 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available otherwise than through breach of this Clause, or which is independently developed without reference to confidential information.

**Notices**

21. Any notice required or permitted to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as may be notified in writing for such purpose.

22. A notice so sent by post shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting, and if sent by email shall be deemed served upon acknowledgment of receipt.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

23. These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.
