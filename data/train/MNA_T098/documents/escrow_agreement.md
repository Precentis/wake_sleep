ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 12 March 2027 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Elstere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13460187 whose registered office is at 75 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Whitlock Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14040002 whose registered office is at 134 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered office at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ, as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

RECITALS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 10 March 2027 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13838636 whose registered office is at 138 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Parties have agreed to establish an escrow arrangement pursuant to which a portion of the purchase price shall be held by the Escrow Agent to secure the Buyer's rights to make claims for indemnities and warranties under the SPA.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim, the amount claimed, and the relevant provision of the SPA under which the claim arises.

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,250,000 (four million two hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 15% of the total purchase price under the SPA, which has been deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the date of completion of the acquisition.

   "Escrow Period" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending at 5:00 p.m. on 15 March 2028.

1.2 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the SPA.

2. Escrow Deposit

2.1 The Buyer shall, on the date of completion of the acquisition, deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by way of irrevocable wire transfer to the escrow account designated by the Escrow Agent.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount (including any accrued interest) to the Seller on the first Business Day following the expiration of the Escrow Period, unless a Claim Notice has been validly served prior to such date.

4. Payment Mechanics

4.1 All payments made by the Escrow Agent under this Agreement shall be made by same-day or next-day cleared funds via wire transfer to the bank account(s) designated in writing by the recipient.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice at any time during the Escrow Period.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from any other Party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under a duty of confidentiality.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

8.2 The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
