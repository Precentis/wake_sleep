**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **6 March 2027**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Whitlock Analytics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number **14040002**, whose registered office is at 134 Bishopsgate, London (the “**Seller**”).

(2) **Elstree Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number **13460187**, whose registered office is at 75 Cheapside, London (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Pemberton Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number **13838636**, whose registered office is at 138 Moorgate, London (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“**Material Adverse Change**” means any event, act, omission or circumstance which has, or is reasonably likely to have, a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company taken as a whole, **excluding** any change or effect arising from: (i) general economic or political conditions; (ii) changes affecting the pharmaceutical or life sciences industry generally; or (iii) pandemics or public health emergencies.

“**FY2026 EBITDA**” means the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, as reported in its audited financial statements prepared in accordance with applicable accounting standards.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$120,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 3.

**3. PAYMENT MECHANICS**

**3.1** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3.2** The Closing shall take place on **7 May 2027** (the “**Closing Date**”), or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.4** In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out amount (the “**Earn-out Payment**”) within ninety (90) days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026 being approved by the Board of the Company. The Earn-out Payment shall be equal to **2 times** the amount by which **FY2026 EBITDA** exceeds **$5,000,000**, provided that the total Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed **$5,000,000**.

**4. NOTICES**

Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class mail, or email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 134 Bishopsgate, London, and to the Buyer to 75 Cheapside, London.

**9. REPRESENTATIONS AND INDEMNITIES**

**9.7** Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within **30 days** of the Buyer becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the claim.

**10. SURVIVAL AND LIMITATIONS**

**10.1** The General Warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title to shares, corporate capacity and authority, and tax matters) shall survive the Closing for **36 months**. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**11. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the transaction, except as required by law or disclosure to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

**12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
