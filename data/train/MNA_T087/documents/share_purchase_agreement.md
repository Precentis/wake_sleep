SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 14 January 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) Ivybridge Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14746840, whose registered office is at 61 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Galloway Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11271057, whose registered office is at 139 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Northgate Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12583840, whose registered office is at 120 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $180,000,000.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000.

**3. Payment**

The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price (as adjusted pursuant to Section 2.3) to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such bank account as the Seller may notify to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**4. Closing**

4.1 Closing shall take place on 10 April 2026 (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Buyer, or remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures, as the parties may agree.

4.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all necessary instruments of transfer, duly executed, and such corporate authorisations and consents as may be required to effect the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

**8. Warranties and Liability**

8.4 (Limitation of Liability) The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $14,400,000. The Purchase Price is $180,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above, or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other party in writing.

11.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been received at the time it would be delivered in the normal course of post if so posted, or if by email, at the time of transmission if transmitted during business hours on a Business Day, or otherwise at 9:00 a.m. on the next Business Day.

**12. Confidentiality**

12.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

12.2 This obligation shall survive the Closing or any termination of this Agreement.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15.1 This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
