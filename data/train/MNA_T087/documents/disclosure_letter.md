3.1 Warranty 8.2 of the Share Purchase Agreement states that the Company owns or has valid rights to use all intellectual property necessary for the operation of its business, free from any third-party claim, and that no material infringement of third-party intellectual property rights is occurring or has been alleged.

3.2 The Seller discloses that the core platform software used by the Company in its principal trading activities was developed in 2023 by Arden Pharma plc, an independent contractor engaged by the Company for software development services.

3.3 The development of the core platform software was conducted on a fee-for-service basis. No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between the Company and Arden Pharma plc in connection with the development.

3.4 The absence of any written agreement assigning intellectual property rights means that legal ownership of the source code, design documents, and underlying intellectual property in the core platform software may not have automatically transferred to the Company.

3.5 The Company continues to use the core platform software in its operations without interruption and has not received any claim, demand, or notice from Arden Pharma plc or any other third party asserting ownership of, or entitlement to, any intellectual property rights in the software.

3.6 The Seller confirms that no written assignment of intellectual property was entered into and that no subsequent assignment or licence agreement has been executed to remedy this.
