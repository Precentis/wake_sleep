ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 10 February 2026 (the “Effective Date”)

BETWEEN:

(1) Ivybridge Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14746840 whose registered office is at 61 Gresham Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Galloway Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11271057 whose registered office is at 139 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) The London Trust Corporation Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 02947033, whose registered office is at 50 Fenchurch Street, London, EC3M 3BY, acting as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Effective Date (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Northgate Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12583840 whose registered office is at 120 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”).

(B) The parties to the SPA have agreed that certain funds shall be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “Claim Notice” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim for indemnification under the SPA, together with an estimate of the amount claimed.

   “Escrow Amount” means the sum of £8,500,000 (eight million five hundred thousand pounds sterling), representing 15% of the total purchase price under the SPA, deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the Effective Date.

   “Escrow Period” means the period commencing on the Effective Date and expiring at 5:00 p.m. on 11 February 2027.

2. Deposit and Holding of Funds

2.1 The Escrow Agent hereby acknowledges receipt of the Escrow Amount, which shall be held by it in a designated interest-bearing account at a UK branch of a Tier 1 bank, segregated from its own funds.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Disbursements

3.1 Upon receipt of a joint written instruction signed by both the Buyer and the Seller, the Escrow Agent shall disburse all or part of the Escrow Amount to the Seller or the Buyer, as specified.

3.2 If, during the Escrow Period, the Buyer serves a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall retain the amount specified in the Claim Notice (or the full Escrow Amount if greater) pending resolution of the claim.

4. Return of Funds

4.1 If no Claim Notice has been received by the Escrow Agent prior to the expiry of the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the entire remaining Escrow Amount to the Seller within five Business Days of such expiry.

5. Payment Mechanics

5.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made in pounds sterling by same-day cleared funds to such account as the recipient may designate in writing.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the addresses or email addresses set out in the preamble, or as otherwise notified.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or legal process.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in New York in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, and the seat of arbitration shall be New York.

13. Miscellaneous

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter.

13.2 This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by all parties.

13.3 The rights and obligations under this Agreement shall bind and benefit the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
