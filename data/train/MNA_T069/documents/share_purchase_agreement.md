**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 12 January 2026.

**Parties:**

(1) **Juniper Foods Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 12944307, registered office at 159 Cannon Street, London) (the “Seller”).

(2) **Bellhaven Analytics Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 10278039, registered office at 100 Ludgate Hill, London) (the “Buyer”).

**Background:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Brightwater Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11739162, whose registered office is at 47 Moorgate, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**Agreed terms:**

**1. Definitions and interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing Date” means 23 April 2026;

“Material Adverse Change” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

“Target Working Capital” means $3,500,000.

**2. Purchase price and payment**

2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the “Purchase Price”) is **$30,000,000**.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to the Seller’s designated bank account no later than 5:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. For the purposes of this adjustment, “Closing Working Capital” shall be determined in accordance with the working capital statement prepared by the Buyer’s accountants and delivered to the Seller within ten (10) Business Days following the Closing Date, subject to review and dispute resolution under Section 2.4.

2.4 If the Seller disputes the calculation of Closing Working Capital, it may notify the Buyer in writing within ten (10) Business Days of receipt of the working capital statement, specifying the nature of the dispute. The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to resolve the dispute within ten (10) Business Days of such notice. If no resolution is reached, the matter shall be referred to an independent auditor mutually agreed upon by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding.

**3. Completion**

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed share transfer forms in respect of the entire issued share capital of the Company;

(b) the share certificates of the Company;

(c) a certificate of good standing in respect of the Company;

(d) such other documents as may reasonably be required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title in the Shares to the Buyer.

**4. Notices**

4.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above.

4.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to be received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by first-class post, two (2) Business Days after posting; if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

**15. Governing law and jurisdiction**

15.1 This Agreement is governed by English law and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement and that any proceedings in respect of such dispute shall be brought in such courts. The parties further agree that service of process in any such proceedings may be effected by delivery in accordance with Section 4. The foregoing shall not prejudice the right of any party to commence proceedings in any other jurisdiction.
