MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF BRIGHTWATER TECHNOLOGIES LTD

Date: 31 January 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 47 Moorgate, London, EC2R 6DB  
Present:  
- Ms. Eleanor Vance (Chair)  
- Mr. Thomas Reed  
- Ms. Priya Mehta  
- Mr. Daniel Finch  

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chair, Ms. Eleanor Vance, called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The agenda was approved without amendment.

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 15 December 2025 were reviewed. Upon motion by Mr. Thomas Reed and seconded by Ms. Priya Mehta, the minutes were approved as a true and accurate record.

3. DIRECTORS’ DECLARATIONS OF INTEREST  
Each director confirmed that they had no new disclosable interests requiring registration under the Companies Act 2006. The Chair reminded all directors of their ongoing duty to declare any personal interests in matters arising.

4. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE AND OPERATING REVIEW  
The CFO, Mr. Daniel Finch, presented the unaudited financial statements for the quarter ending 31 December 2025. Revenue for the period was reported at £5.2 million, with a net profit margin of 14.3%. The board noted strong performance in the data analytics division, which contributed 68% of total revenue. The contract with Merrion Industries Ltd (company number 11007484, registered office 171 Gresham Street, London) remained on track, with deliverables progressing according to the agreed schedule under the agreement dated 1 March 2024.

5. LEGAL MATTERS  
The board noted a letter before action dated 20 January 2026 received from legal representatives of Calloway Marine Ltd, threatening proceedings for approximately $3,400,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 15 June 2023. The board was informed that Brightwater Technologies Ltd denies liability and considers the claims to be substantially overstated. The General Counsel advised that the matter is being reviewed by external legal counsel. The board resolved that management shall monitor the situation closely and report developments at the next scheduled board meeting. No provision has been made in the financial statements as of 31 December 2025 in respect of this matter, pending further assessment.

6. PROPOSED SALE OF ENTIRE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL  
The board received an update on the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Brightwater Technologies Ltd (company number 11739162, registered office 47 Moorgate, London) by Bellhaven Analytics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10278039, registered office 100 Ludgate Hill, London) from Juniper Foods Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12944307, registered office 159 Cannon Street, London). The transaction remains subject to due diligence and definitive documentation. The board confirmed that all directors are supportive of the transaction in principle, subject to final terms and shareholder approval.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There was no other business.  

8. ADJOURNMENT  
Upon motion by Ms. Priya Mehta and seconded by Mr. Thomas Reed, the meeting was adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
