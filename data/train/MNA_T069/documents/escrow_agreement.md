ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Juniper Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12944307 whose registered office is at 159 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Bellhaven Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10278039 whose registered office is at 100 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties to this Agreement.

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has acquired from the Seller all of the issued share capital of Brightwater Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11739162 whose registered office is at 47 Moorgate, London (the "Company"), pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated as of the date hereof (the "Purchase Agreement").

(B) It is a condition of the Purchase Agreement that certain funds be placed in escrow to secure the Seller’s obligations under the warranties and indemnities contained therein.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds (as defined below) on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 23 April 2026.

"Dispute" has the meaning given in Clause 12.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,500,000 (four million five hundred thousand pounds sterling) representing a portion of the total consideration under the Purchase Agreement.

"Escrow Funds" means the Escrow Amount, together with any interest or income earned thereon while held in escrow, less any amounts released or withdrawn in accordance with this Agreement.

"Purchase Agreement" has the meaning given in the recitals.

"Released Amount" means any portion of the Escrow Amount released to the Seller in accordance with Clause 4.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") at a bank in London in its name for the purpose of holding the Escrow Funds.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, safeguard and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Payment

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account within two Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 No party shall have any right, title or interest in the Escrow Account other than as expressly provided in this Agreement.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains after satisfaction of any pending claims, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any claims notified by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in writing before that date under the terms of the Purchase Agreement (each, a "Pending Claim"). Any amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claim in accordance with the Purchase Agreement.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice given under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the address or email set out in this Agreement or as otherwise notified.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement unless disclosure is required by law or by a regulatory authority.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including regarding its existence, validity or termination (a "Dispute"), shall be finally resolved by arbitration in New York in accordance with the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce then in force, which Rules are deemed to be incorporated by reference into this clause.

12.3 The seat of arbitration shall be New York, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.
