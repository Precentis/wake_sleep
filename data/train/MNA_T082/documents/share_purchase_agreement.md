# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 6 January 2027.

**Parties**

(1) Upton Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12300388, whose registered office is at 134 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Juniper Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12921361, whose registered office is at 103 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

**Background**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14379226, whose registered office is at 59 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**Operative Provisions**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Closing Date" means 29 March 2027.

"Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 8.

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 1, other than the Fundamental Warranties.

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax.

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

## 2. Sale and Purchase

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of $165,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account or accounts as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** Upon receipt of the Purchase Price, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer in respect of the Shares, together with such other documents as may be necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares to the Buyer.

## 3. Completion

**3.1** Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.2** At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) certified copies of all necessary corporate authorisations approving the execution and performance of this Agreement by the Seller;

(b) the share certificates representing the Shares, if any;

(c) resignations of the current directors of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date; and

(d) such other documents and instruments as are reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares and the transition of control.

## 4. Notices

**4.1** Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by email to the addresses or email addresses set out in the preamble, or as otherwise notified in writing by a party from time to time.

**4.2** A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed received on the day of delivery; a notice sent by post shall be deemed received two Business Days after posting; a notice sent by email shall be deemed received on the day it is sent, provided that no delivery failure notification is generated.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1** Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement (the "Confidential Information") and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidentiality;

(b) as required by law or any regulatory authority; or

(c) to the extent such information is already in the public domain other than by breach of this Clause.

## 10. Survival and Claims

**10.1** The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.
