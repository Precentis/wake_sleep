ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF PEMBERTON MARINE LTD  
(Extract Relating to Corporate Governance and Shareholder Provisions)

Article 1: Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Buyer" means Upton Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 12300388, with registered office at 134 Fleet Street, London;  
"Company" means Pemberton Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 14379226, with registered office at 59 Fleet Street, London;  
"Seller" means Juniper Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under number 12921361, with registered office at 103 Cheapside, London;  
"Yarrow Industries" means Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 15505076, with registered office at 126 Gresham Street, London;  
"board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
"director" means a director of the Company;  
"notice" means written notice delivered personally, by first-class post, or by email to the last known address or email of the recipient.

1.2 References to legislation are to Acts of Parliament of the United Kingdom and include any re-enactment, amendment or replacement thereof.

Article 2: Share Capital and Transfer of Shares

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1 each, all of which are currently held by the Seller.

2.2 No transfer of shares in the Company shall be registered unless accompanied by a duly executed instrument of transfer and such evidence of authority to transfer as the board may reasonably require.

2.3 The board may, at its discretion, refuse registration of a transfer of shares if it has reasonable grounds to believe the transfer would breach any contractual obligation, including but not limited to confidentiality or non-compete agreements entered into between the Company and third parties, including Yarrow Industries.

Article 3: Board of Directors

3.1 The board shall consist of not fewer than two and not more than six directors, appointed in accordance with these Articles.

3.2 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution passed at a general meeting, provided that not less than 28 days’ written notice of the intention to propose the resolution has been given to the Company.

3.3 The board may appoint any person to fill a casual vacancy, but any such appointment shall be subject to ratification by the shareholders at the next annual general meeting.

Article 4: Board Meetings and Proceedings

4.1 The board shall meet as frequently as necessary to manage the business of the Company, but not less than four times in each calendar year.

4.2 At least five days’ written notice of a board meeting shall be given to every director, unless the directors unanimously agree to shorter notice.

4.3 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, at which a quorum shall be two directors.

4.4 Decisions at board meetings shall be made by a simple majority of those directors present and voting. In the case of an equality of votes, the chairperson shall not have a casting vote.

Article 5: Payment and Financial Matters

5.1 All payments due under any agreement to which the Company is a party, including payments to Yarrow Industries, shall be made in sterling into the designated bank account specified in the relevant agreement.

5.2 The board shall ensure that all invoices from material customers or suppliers are settled within 30 days of receipt unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Article 6: Notices

6.1 Any notice required or permitted under these Articles shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 A notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.

Article 7: Confidentiality

7.1 Every director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, operations, and financial condition of the Company, except where disclosure is required by law or with the prior written consent of the board.

7.2 This obligation shall continue to apply for a period of six years after the date on which a person ceases to be a director or officer.

Article 8: Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising under or in connection with these Articles.
