Date: 24 January 2027

Location: 59 Fleet Street, London, EC4A 2HS  
Chairman: Mr. A. T. Pemberton  
Secretary: Ms. L. R. Hartley  

Present:  
1. Mr. A. T. Pemberton

Total Directors: 5  
Present: 2 directors

The meeting was duly convened and constituted, and a quorum was present throughout.  

1. **Calling to Order**  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. and confirmed that notice of the meeting had been duly given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Pemberton Marine Ltd (the “Company”).  

2. **Confirmation of Quorum**  
It was confirmed that a quorum was present. The Company has five directors, and 2 directors were present. The Articles of Association of the Company provide for a quorum of two directors. It was therefore confirmed that the meeting was quorate.  

3. **Approval of Share Purchase Agreement**  
The Chairman presented to the Board a draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 25 January 2027 (the “Agreement”) between Juniper Capital LLP (the “Seller”), Upton Medical Ltd (the “Buyer”), and the Company, pursuant to which the Buyer will acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller.  

The Agreement provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued shares in the Company, representing all of the outstanding equity interests. The Board reviewed the terms of the Agreement, including, but not limited to, representations, warranties, covenants, and closing conditions.  

The Board noted that the Buyer, Upton Medical Ltd, is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12300388 and has its registered office at 134 Fleet Street, London. The Seller, Juniper Capital LLP, is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12921361 and has its registered office at 103 Cheapside, London. The Company, Pemberton Marine Ltd, is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14379226 and has its registered office at 59 Fleet Street, London.  

Further, the Board acknowledged that Yarrow Industries Ltd, a material customer of the Company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15505076 with registered office at 126 Gresham Street, London, may be affected by the transaction and that appropriate commercial considerations had been taken into account.  

After deliberation, the Board concluded that the terms of the Agreement are fair and reasonable and in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders.  

RESOLVED THAT the Board of Directors of Pemberton Marine Ltd hereby approves the entry by the parties into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 25 January 2027, pursuant to which Upton Medical Ltd will acquire from Juniper Capital LLP the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

4. **Adjournment**  
There being no further business, the Chairman adjourned the meeting at 10:45 a.m.
