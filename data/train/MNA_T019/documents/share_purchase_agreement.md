**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 7 May 2026.

**Parties**  
(1) **Eskdale Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11093855, whose registered office is at 119 Old Broad Street, London (the “Buyer”).  
(2) **Dunmore Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11766087, whose registered office is at 104 Cheapside, London (the “Seller”).  

**Background**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Brightwater Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11207902, whose registered office is at 26 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”).  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including all schedules and appendices;  
“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;  
“**Closing Date**” means 13 August 2026;  
“**Company**” means Brightwater Pharma plc, company number 11207902;  
“**Parties**” means the Buyer and the Seller collectively, and “Party” means either of them;  
“**Purchase Price**” has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;  
“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Purchase Price and Payment**  
2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the “Purchase Price”) is **$255,000,000**.  
2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by bank transfer to such account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Completion**  
3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or at such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.  
3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:  
(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;  
(b) the share certificates representing the Shares;  
(c) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;  
(d) such corporate authorisations and consents as may be required to effect the transfer of the Shares.  
3.3 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller on the Closing Date the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**8. Limitation of Liability**  
8.3 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under or in connection with any warranty given under this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **25% of the Purchase Price**, being **$57,375,000**. The Purchase Price is $255,000,000 (see Clause 2.1).

**9. Indemnities and Claims**  
9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within **30 days** of the date on which the Buyer becomes aware, or ought reasonably to have become aware, of the matter giving rise to the claim.

**10. Confidentiality**  
10.1 Each Party agrees that it shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of any other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein.  
10.2 The obligations under this Clause 10 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available otherwise than as a result of a breach of this Clause.

**11. Notices**  
11.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the registered office or primary email address of the relevant Party.  
11.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 119 Old Broad Street, London.  
11.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 104 Cheapside, London.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
