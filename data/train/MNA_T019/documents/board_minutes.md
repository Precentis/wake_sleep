**4. UPDATE ON PROPOSED SALE OF THE ENTIRE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL**

4.1 The board received an update from the Chair regarding ongoing negotiations for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Brightwater Pharma plc by Eskdale Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11093855, registered office 119 Old Broad Street, London) from Dunmore Software Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11766087, registered office 104 Cheapside, London).  

4.2 The transaction remains subject to due diligence, shareholder approval, and regulatory clearance. Completion is expected to occur on or shortly after 5 June 2026, subject to fulfilment of all conditions precedent.  

4.3 The board confirmed its continued support for the transaction, noting that the proposed terms are in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders.

**5. LEGAL CORRESPONDENCE – PENRITH RETAIL GROUP PLC**

5.1 The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 28 May 2026 from Penrith Retail Group plc, asserting a claim of approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement.  

5.2 Legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that the claim is currently unproven and subject to further investigation.  

5.3 The board directed management to monitor developments closely, preserve all relevant documentation, and provide a further update at the next scheduled board meeting. No provision has been made in the interim financial statements in respect of this matter.

**6. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP – OAKHURST MEDIA LTD**

6.1 The board reviewed the status of the commercial arrangement with Oakhurst Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14006828, registered office 60 Queen Victoria Street, London), a material customer of the Company.  

6.2 Management confirmed that all contractual deliverables have been met on time and in accordance with agreed specifications. The relationship remains stable and productive.

7.1 There was no other business.
