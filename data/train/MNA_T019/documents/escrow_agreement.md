This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] June 2026 (the “Effective Date”)

BETWEEN:

(1) Dunmore Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11766087, whose registered office is at 104 Cheapside, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Eskdale Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11093855, whose registered office is at 119 Old Broad Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [●], a professional firm appointed as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

RECITALS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated [●] June 2026 (the “SPA”) for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Brightwater Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11207902, whose registered office is at 26 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”), from the Seller.

(B) Pursuant to the SPA, an amount of £3,750,000 (the “Escrow Amount”) has been deposited into an escrow account held with the Escrow Agent for the purpose of securing certain indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The parties wish to establish the terms under which the Escrow Amount may be released, claimed or otherwise disposed of.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Claim Notice” means a written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 8 specifying the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount, including reasonable supporting documentation.

“Indemnity Obligations” has the meaning ascribed to it in the SPA.

“Material Customer” means Oakhurst Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14006828, whose registered office is at 60 Queen Victoria Street, London.

2. Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Account”) at [●], a UK-authorised deposit-taker.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held solely for the purposes set forth in this Agreement and shall not be subject to any claim or lien by any third party, including the creditors of any party to this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be released in full to the Seller on the date that is 18 months after the Effective Date (the “Release Date”), unless a valid Claim Notice has been served prior to such date.

4. Payment

4.1 All payments made pursuant to this Agreement shall be made by same-day cleared funds to the account specified in writing by the receiving party.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by serving a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Following receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount until the earlier of (a) the resolution of the claim by written agreement between the Buyer and the Seller, or (b) determination by the courts of England and Wales.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or any regulatory authority.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement.
