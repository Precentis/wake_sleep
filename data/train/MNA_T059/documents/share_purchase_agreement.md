SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 26 March 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Bellhaven Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15936119, whose registered office is at 171 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Netherby Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10510190, whose registered office is at 77 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11157768, whose registered office is at 10 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“Closing Date” means 22 June 2027;

“Parties” means the Seller and the Buyer, and “Party” means either of them;

“Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares (the “Purchase Price”) shall be $190,000,000, to be paid in full on the Closing Date in cleared funds by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place remotely via electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, accompanied by the share certificates for the Shares;

(b) a certificate of good standing or equivalent document in respect of the Company, issued not more than ten Business Days prior to the Closing Date;

(c) such corporate authorisations and resignations of directors and officers of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably require.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Buyer shall make the payment of the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1, failing which the Seller may, without prejudice to any other rights or remedies, treat the Agreement as terminated upon written notice to the Buyer.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the address or email address set out below:

To the Seller:

5.2 Notices sent by email shall be deemed served on the next Business Day following transmission.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party, disclose to any person any information acquired in connection with this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three years.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under or in connection with any warranty given under this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $23,750,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $190,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

10.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
