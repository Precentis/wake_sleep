MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF QUAYSIDESTATE ENGINEERING LTD  
HELD AT 10 BISHOPSGATE, LONDON, ON 8 APRIL 2027

Present:
1. Mr T. Granger (Chairman)
2. Ms R. Selwyn (Director)
3. Dr P. Naylor (Director)
4. Mr J. Hartley (Company Secretary)

Agenda:

1. Call to Order

1.1 The meeting was convened pursuant to notice given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Quayside Engineering Ltd (the “Company”). The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m.

2. Confirmation of Quorum

2.1 A quorum was confirmed as being present. All directors were present in person or by video conference.

3. Approval of Previous Minutes

3.1 The minutes of the board meeting held on 11 January 2027 were reviewed and approved as a correct record.

4. Financial and Operational Update

4.1 The Company Secretary provided a financial summary for the quarter ending 31 March 2027. Revenues were reported at £4.7 million, with gross profit margin at 28%. Expenditure remained within budgeted parameters.

4.2 Dr Naylor reported on ongoing projects under contract with Upton Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14756377, registered office 109 Cannon Street, London). Deliveries under Contract No. UL/2026/045 are on schedule. No delays or material variances have been recorded.

4.3 Ms Selwyn confirmed that all outstanding regulatory filings with Companies House have been completed, including the annual return for the year ended 31 December 2026.

5. Legal and Compliance Matters

5.1 The board noted a letter before action dated 3 April 2027 from Arden Foods Ltd threatening proceedings for approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under Purchase Order Ref: AF/QE/2025/11 across four consignments delivered between 17 June 2025 and 9 September 2025. The Company has engaged external counsel, Ashwood Legal LLP, to assess the merits of the claim and respond accordingly.

5.2 The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely, preserve all relevant documentation, and report biweekly to the board until resolution. No provision has been recorded in the unaudited financial statements for the period ending 31 March 2027 in respect of this matter.

6. Share Transfer and Acquisition Update

6.1 The Company Secretary advised that Netherby Capital LLP (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10510190, registered office 77 Moorgate, London) had entered into an agreement to acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from Bellhaven Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15936119, registered office 171 Cannon Street, London).

6.2 Completion remains conditional upon standard regulatory clearances and due diligence undertakings. Expected completion date is 15 May 2027, subject to no material adverse change.

6.3 Directors were reminded of their duties under Chapter 2, Part 10 of the Companies Act 2006 in relation to the proposed transaction.

7. Any Other Business

7.1 There being no other business, the Chairman adjourned the meeting at 11:45 a.m.
