1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms have the meanings set out in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 20 July 2026 (the "SPA") between Kingsmere Pharma plc (the "Seller") and Thornbury Logistics plc (the "Buyer") concerning the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Bioscience plc (the "Company").

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Disclosure Letter unless otherwise stated.

1.3 The headings in this Disclosure Letter are inserted for convenience only and shall not affect the construction of this Disclosure Letter.

1.4 References to a party include that party’s successors and permitted assigns.

1.5 The terms "including" and "include" mean "including without limitation".

2. Purpose of Disclosure

2.1 This Disclosure Letter is delivered by the Seller pursuant to the SPA and qualifies the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Schedule 3 (Warranties) to the SPA.

2.2 Disclosure in this Letter is made on a pro tanto basis and shall not imply any admission or confirmation of liability, wrongdoing or materiality in respect of the matters disclosed.

3. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)

3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3, the Seller confirms that none of the material contracts to which the Company is a party contains any provision that is triggered by a change of control of the Company.

3.2 Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the Seller confirms that the supply agreement dated 14 March 2024 between the Company and Arden Industries Ltd (company number 13601695, registered office 37 Cannon Street, London) does not contain a change of control clause or any condition permitting termination or renegotiation upon the transfer of the shares of the Company under the SPA.

3.3 The Seller makes no other disclosures under Warranty 6.3.

4. Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)

4.1 For the purposes of Warranty 7.1, the Seller confirms that, as at the date of this Disclosure Letter, there are no pending, threatened or reasonably anticipated claims, proceedings, investigations or disputes involving the Company before any court, tribunal or regulatory authority.

4.2 There are no outstanding injunctions, settlements, awards or judgments applicable to the Company.

4.3 The Seller makes no disclosures under Warranty 7.1.

5. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)

5.1 For the purposes of Warranty 8.2, the Seller discloses that the core platform software used by the Company in its principal operations was developed in 2023 by Pemberton Retail Group plc, an independent third-party contractor.

5.2 The development work was carried out under an oral understanding between the Company and Pemberton Retail Group plc, and no written contract was executed between the parties in relation to the software development.

5.3 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Pemberton Retail Group plc.

5.4 The Company has, since 2023, used, maintained and modified the core platform software in the ordinary course of business and holds itself out as the owner of such software.

5.5 The Seller acknowledges that the absence of a written assignment may affect the Company’s ability to assert full legal ownership of the intellectual property in the core platform software against third parties, including Pemberton Retail Group plc.

5.6 The Seller makes no other disclosures under Warranty 8.2.

6. General Disclosure Provisions

6.1 The disclosures in this Letter are made solely for the purposes of limiting the liability of the Seller under the SPA and do not constitute disclosures to any other party or for any other purpose.

6.2 Nothing in this Letter shall be construed as a representation or warranty by the Seller beyond those expressly set out in the SPA.
