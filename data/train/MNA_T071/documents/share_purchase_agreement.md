SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 5 July 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Kingsmere Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11265024 whose registered office is at 110 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Thornbury Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15294076 whose registered office is at 116 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15791464 whose registered office is at 73 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1.  Definitions and Interpretation

1.1  In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

   "Closing Date" means 25 August 2026;

   "Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles applied in the Company’s most recently prepared audited financial statements;

   "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with UK GAAP consistently applied;

   "FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

   "Earn-out Payment" has the meaning given in Clause 3.4;

   "FY2026" means the financial year of the Company ending 31 December 2026;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   "Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000.

1.2  Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2.  Purchase and Sale

2.1  Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell and the Buyer shall acquire the Shares for a purchase price of $205,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2  The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3  The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such reduction shall be calculated and paid in accordance with the procedures set out in Schedule 1.

3.  Earn-out

3.1  Within 90 days following the end of FY2026, the Buyer shall prepare and deliver to the Seller a certificate setting out the FY2026 EBITDA and calculating the Earn-out Payment.

3.2  The Seller shall have the right to review the Buyer’s calculation and to object in writing within 30 days of receipt of the certificate.

3.3  If the parties cannot agree on the Earn-out Payment, the dispute shall be referred to an independent accountant appointed by agreement between the parties or, failing agreement, by the President for the time being of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales.

3.4  The Earn-out Payment shall equal three (3) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000, such payment to be capped at $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller within 15 Business Days of final determination.

4.  Notices

4.1  Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the recipient.

4.2  Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 110 Queen Victoria Street, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

4.3  Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 116 Old Broad Street, London, and to the attention of the General Counsel.

5.  Confidentiality

5.1  Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and shall not disclose them to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.  Governing Law and Jurisdiction

6.1  This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

6.2  The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
