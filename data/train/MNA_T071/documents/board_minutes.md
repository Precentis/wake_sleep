**Minutes of a Board Meeting of Corrib Bioscience plc**

**Date:** 15 July 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 73 Cheapside, London, EC2V 6AA (Company’s registered office)  

**Chair:** Mr. D. F. Callaghan, Chairman  
**Attendees:**  
- Ms. R. N. Mistry, Chief Executive Officer  
- Mr. J. T. P. Bell, Chief Financial Officer  
- Mrs. L. A. Greaves, Company Secretary  
- Mr. E. H. Vance, Non-Executive Director  

**Apologies:**  
- Dr. S. K. Patel (Non-Executive Director)  

**1.** The Chairman opened the meeting and welcomed those present. He confirmed that a quorum was present and that notice of the meeting had been duly given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Corrib Bioscience plc (the “Company”).

**2.** The Chairman noted the ongoing transaction whereby Kingsmere Pharma plc (the “Seller”), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11265024, registered office 110 Queen Victoria Street, London, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to Thornbury Logistics plc (the “Buyer”), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15294076, registered office 116 Old Broad Street, London. The board acknowledged that the proposed acquisition remains subject to due diligence, contractual documentation, and regulatory clearances. The transaction is currently expected to complete on or about 20 July 2026.

**3.** The Chief Executive Officer provided an update on operational performance for the quarter ending 30 June 2026. She reported that revenues were broadly in line with internal forecasts, with a notable increase in demand from Arden Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13601695, registered office 37 Cannon Street, London, which now accounts for 38% of the Company’s total quarterly sales. The relationship with Arden Industries Ltd remains stable, and no material compliance or performance concerns were reported.

**4.** The Chief Financial Officer reviewed the latest unaudited financial statements and confirmed that the Company remains solvent and able to meet its liabilities as they fall due. He highlighted that working capital levels remain within target parameters and that no dividend would be recommended for the current financial year, in light of the pending acquisition.

**5.** The board noted a letter before action dated 10 July 2026, issued by solicitors acting for Hartwell Bioscience plc, threatening legal proceedings against the Company in respect of allegedly defective product deliveries. The letter claims damages of approximately $1,200,000. Legal counsel has been instructed to respond. The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely and report any developments at the next scheduled board meeting. No provision has been made in the unaudited financials for the potential liability, given the preliminary nature of the claim.

**6.** The Company Secretary confirmed that all filings due at Companies House have been made in a timely manner and that there are no outstanding regulatory requirements. She reminded directors of their continuing obligations under the Companies Act 2006 during the pendency of the proposed acquisition.

**7.** There being no further business, the Chairman adjourned the meeting at 11:45 a.m.
