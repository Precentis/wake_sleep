**AGREEMENT FOR THE SUPPLY OF PHARMACEUTICAL LOGISTICS SERVICES**

This Agreement is made on 15 June 2024

Between:

(1) Corrib Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15791464, whose registered office is at 73 Cheapside, London ("Supplier"); and

(2) Arden Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13601695, whose registered office is at 37 Cannon Street, London ("Customer").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, a body corporate which is from time to time directly or indirectly controlled by, under common control with, or controlling that party;

"Change of Control" means the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting in concert of direct or indirect control over more than 50% of the voting rights attaching to the issued share capital of a party, or the disposal of all or substantially all of the assets of a party;

"Confidential Information" means all information (whether of a technical, commercial or financial nature) disclosed by one party to the other, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, in connection with this Agreement;

"Services" means the pharmaceutical logistics and cold-chain delivery services provided by the Supplier to the Customer as detailed in Schedule A.

**2. Supply of Services**

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the specifications, standards and timelines set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with due care, skill and diligence and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

**3. Payment**

3.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Services ("Fees") as set out in Schedule B, within 30 days of the date of receipt of a valid invoice issued by the Supplier.

3.2 All amounts are stated in pounds sterling (£) and are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

**4. Confidentiality**

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential the other party’s Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the receiving party.

5.2 A notice delivered personally shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery; if sent by post, 48 hours after posting; and if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is returned.

**6. Term and Termination**

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first above written and shall continue for an initial term of three years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 6.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement forthwith by written notice if the other party:

(a) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation, administration or receivership; or

(b) materially breaches any term of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of receiving notice requiring remediation.

**14. Miscellaneous**

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, arrangements and understandings relating to its subject matter.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this provision, the counterparty is Arden Industries Ltd.

14.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of each of the parties.

14.4 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
