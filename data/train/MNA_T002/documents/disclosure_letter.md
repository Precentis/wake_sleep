DISCLOSURE LETTER

dated 24 February 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Merrion Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11352809, whose registered office is at 43 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Kestrel Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12130911, whose registered office is at 180 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14905358, whose registered office is at 122 Moorgate, London (the "Company"), pursuant to the terms of a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date of this letter (the "Agreement").

(B) Pursuant to the Agreement, the Seller is required to make certain disclosures in relation to the warranties given by it concerning the Company.

(C) This letter sets out the full and complete disclosures made by the Seller pursuant to the Agreement.

1. Interpretation

1.1 In this letter, terms defined in the Agreement shall have the same meaning unless otherwise defined herein.

1.2 The headings in this letter are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)

2.1 The Seller discloses that there are no actual, pending or, to the best of the Seller’s knowledge, threatened claims, actions, proceedings or investigations involving the Company before any court, tribunal or governmental authority.

2.2 There are no circumstances known to the Seller which may give rise to any such claim, action, proceeding or investigation.

3. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)

3.1 The Company's core platform software, known as "Glenrock Core v1.0", was developed in 2023 by Dunmore Retail Group plc (company number 16245123), an independent contractor engaged by the Company for software development services.

3.2 The development work undertaken by Dunmore Retail Group plc was performed pursuant to an informal engagement arrangement, and no written agreement was entered into between the Company and Dunmore Retail Group plc governing the terms of such engagement.

3.3 In particular, no written assignment of intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software was executed between the Company and Dunmore Retail Group plc.

3.4 The phrase "No written assignment" is expressly included in this disclosure to confirm the absence of any documented transfer of intellectual property rights from Dunmore Retail Group plc to the Company in relation to the core platform software.

3.5 The Company has made payments to Dunmore Retail Group plc in the aggregate amount of £87,500 for the development work, all of which were invoiced and settled in full by 15 December 2023.

3.6 To the best of the Seller’s knowledge, Dunmore Retail Group plc has not asserted any ownership or proprietary interest in the core platform software since its delivery to the Company.

3.7 The Company currently uses the core platform software in its ordinary course of business, including in its principal service offering to Rothley Energy plc (company number 15381632), a material customer.

4. Payment

4.1 Any claim for payment arising under or in connection with the disclosures contained in this letter shall be subject to the terms of the Agreement, including but not limited to the provisions relating to warranty claims, notification procedures, and financial caps.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this letter shall be in writing and delivered to the address of the relevant party set out above, or such other address as may be notified in writing by either party to the other, and shall be deemed to have been validly served if delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the registered email address of the recipient.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 This letter and its contents shall be treated as confidential by the Buyer and shall only be disclosed to its professional advisers, lenders or insurers on a need-to-know basis and under an obligation of confidence.

6.2 The confidentiality obligations in this clause shall not apply to disclosures required by law, regulation or any order of a competent court or regulatory authority.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This letter shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

7.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in relation to any dispute arising out of or in connection with this letter.
