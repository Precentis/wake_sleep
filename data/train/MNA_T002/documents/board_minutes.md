4. PROPOSED SALE OF SHARES TO KESTREL SOFTWARE LTD  
The board reviewed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Technologies Ltd by Kestrel Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12130911, with registered office at 180 Gresham Street, London. The transaction is being conducted by and between Glenrock Technologies Ltd and Merrion Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11352809, registered office 43 Ludgate Hill, London. Legal advisors have confirmed that due diligence is substantially complete, and all necessary corporate approvals have been or will be obtained. The board resolved to proceed with execution of the share purchase agreement, subject to no material adverse change occurring prior to completion, which is currently anticipated on or about 28 February 2026, governed by English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

5. LEGAL CORRESPONDENCE FROM THORNBURY CAPITAL LLP  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 15 February 2026 from Thornbury Capital LLP, threatening legal proceedings for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 14 June 2024. The matter pertains to a batch of firmware modules supplied in November 2025, which the counterparty claims failed performance benchmarks during integration testing. Legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that the claim appears contestable on grounds of contractual limitation and compliance with agreed specifications. Mr K. P. Naylor confirmed that a technical audit of the affected deliveries has been completed and supports the Company’s position. The board instructed management to monitor developments closely and report at the next scheduled meeting. No provision has been made in the financial statements as of 31 January 2026 in respect of this matter, consistent with current accounting guidance.
