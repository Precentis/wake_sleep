SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 29 January 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Merrion Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11352809, whose registered office is at 43 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller");

(2) Kestrel Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12130911, whose registered office is at 180 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

in respect of the entire issued share capital of Glenrock Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14905358, whose registered office is at 122 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Sale Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 22 May 2026, or such other date as the Parties may agree in writing;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Sale Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

   "Sale Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Sale Shares for a total consideration of $150,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other location as the Parties may agree.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) stock transfer forms in respect of the Sale Shares, duly executed and with all necessary endorsements;

   (b) resignations of the current directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

   (c) such other documents and instruments as may be reasonably required to vest full legal and beneficial ownership of the Sale Shares in the Buyer free from all Encumbrances.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office or email address of the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 43 Ludgate Hill, London, or to such other address or email as the Seller may notify in writing.

4.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 180 Gresham Street, London, or to such other address or email as the Buyer may notify in writing.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive the termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under or in connection with any warranty given pursuant to this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $18,000,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $150,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

10.2 Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
