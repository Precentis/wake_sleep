SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 11 January 2027

PARTIES

(1) Juniper Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15995964, whose registered office is at 44 Cannon Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Lowther Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11415358, whose registered office is at 93 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Ashcombe Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14063279, whose registered office is at 146 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 19 March 2027;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential or proprietary nature disclosed by one Party to another in connection with this Agreement;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $115,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 4.

2.2 The sale and purchase of the Shares shall take place at the Closing on the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer, or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At the Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates representing the Shares, if any;

(c) resignations of any directors or secretaries of the Company appointed by the Seller; and

(d) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to effect the transfer of the Shares.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller on the Closing Date by wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4.2 Time for payment shall be of the essence.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above or such other address as such Party may notify in writing.

5.2 Any notice delivered personally shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery. Any notice sent by post shall be deemed to have been served two (2) Business Days after posting. Any notice sent by email shall be deemed to have been served upon receipt of confirmation of successful transmission.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each Party shall treat as confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law or any regulatory authority.

6.2 This obligation shall survive the termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
