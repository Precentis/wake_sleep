# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Ashcombe Media Ltd

**Date:** 20 January 2027  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 146 Cannon Street, London, EC4A 1BP  
**Chair:** Mr. Edwin Parry, Director  

Present:  
- Mr. Edwin Parry (Director)  
- Ms. Lena Choi (Director)  
- Mr. Daniel Frost (Director)  

1. **Call to Order**  
   The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The Directors resolved that the meeting proceed in accordance with the Articles of Association of Ashcombe Media Ltd (company number 14063279, registered office at 146 Cannon Street, London).

2. **Approval of Agenda**  
   The agenda was approved without amendment.

3. **Review of Previous Minutes**  
   The minutes of the board meeting held on 18 December 2026 were reviewed and confirmed as a true and accurate record. A motion to approve the minutes was passed unanimously.

4. **Update on Proposed Share Sale**  
   The board received an update regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Ashcombe Media Ltd by Juniper Bioscience plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15995964, registered office at 44 Cannon Street, London) from Lowther Technologies Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11415358, registered office at 93 Fleet Street, London). Legal due diligence was ongoing, and draft transaction documents were under review by both parties’ counsel. The board noted that the transaction remains conditional upon completion of satisfactory due diligence and receipt of all necessary consents.

5. **Outstanding Legal Matter**  
   The board noted a letter before action from Eskdale Engineering Ltd threatening proceedings for approximately $2,750,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The letter, dated 15 January 2027, relates to deliveries purportedly made under a supply agreement dated 3 March 2024. The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely, liaise with external legal counsel, and report any developments at the next scheduled board meeting. No provision has been recorded in the financial statements as of the date hereof, pending further assessment.

6. **Customer Contract Renewal**  
   The board reviewed the status of material customer contracts, including the agreement with Ingleby Technologies Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14679625, registered office at 116 King William Street, London). Discussions regarding renewal terms are ongoing, with a target execution date of 28 February 2027. The board affirmed the commercial importance of maintaining the relationship.

7. **Any Other Business**  
   There being no other business, the meeting concluded at 11:20 a.m.

8. **Adjournment**  
   The Chair adjourned the meeting.
