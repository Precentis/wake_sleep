**Disclosure Letter dated 24 January 2027**  
**From: Lowther Technologies Ltd (Seller)**  
**To: Juniper Bioscience plc (Buyer)**  

This Disclosure Letter is made pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 25 January 2027 (the “SPA”) under which Juniper Bioscience plc (the “Buyer”) has agreed to acquire from Lowther Technologies Ltd (the “Seller”) the entire issued share capital of Ashcombe Media Ltd (the “Company”). Capitalised terms used but not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the SPA.

---

**1. Definitions**

In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “Warranty” means any representation, warranty, undertaking or statement made or given by the Seller in the SPA;
- “Disclosed” means adequately and fully disclosed in this letter, with sufficient detail to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed;
- “Material Adverse Effect” has the meaning given in Clause 3.1 of the SPA;
- “IP Rights” means patents, registered and unregistered trade marks, design rights, copyright, database rights, know-how and all other intellectual property rights arising under the law of any jurisdiction, whether registered or unregistered and including all applications and rights to apply for registration of such rights.

---

**2. General Disclosure**

2.1 The Seller discloses pursuant to Clause 9 of the SPA the matters set out in this letter. This Disclosure Letter is intended to qualify and limit the Warranties given by the Seller in the SPA solely to the extent expressly set out herein.

2.2 The disclosure in any section of this letter shall qualify the corresponding Warranty or Warranties in the SPA to which it clearly relates. Where relevant, such disclosures may also constitute general disclosures qualifying the Warranties in their entirety to the extent such disclosures are clearly capable of doing so.

2.3 The inclusion of any matter in this Disclosure Letter shall not be construed as an admission by the Seller that such matter gives rise to a liability, nor shall it be admissible in any dispute under the SPA as evidence of the existence of any fact not otherwise arising from the express terms of the disclosure.

---

**3. Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**

3.1 There is no pending, threatened or, to the best of the Seller’s knowledge, likely claim, action, arbitration, prosecution, investigation or proceeding by or before any court or governmental or regulatory authority involving the Company.

3.2 For the purposes of Warranty 7.1, the Seller confirms that there are no circumstances which may give rise to any such proceedings.

3.3 The disclosure against Warranty 7.1 is: **None**.

---

**4. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (IP)**

4.1 The Company does not own any registered patents, trade marks or designs.

4.2 The Company’s core platform software, known internally as “Project Helix”, was developed in 2023 by Eskdale Robotics Ltd, a third-party contractor based in Penrith, Cumbria.

4.3 The development work undertaken by Eskdale Robotics Ltd was conducted pursuant to a verbal understanding and no written contract or statement of work was executed between the Company and Eskdale Robotics Ltd.

4.4 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Eskdale Robotics Ltd.

4.5 The Company continues to use the core platform software in its ordinary course of business and has not received any claim or notice asserting ownership or infringement rights in relation to the software.

4.6 The disclosure against Warranty 8.2 is limited to the facts set out in Clauses 4.2 to 4.5.

---
