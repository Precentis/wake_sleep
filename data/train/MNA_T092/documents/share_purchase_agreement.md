# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 November 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Thornbury Software Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13159015**, whose registered office is at 109 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Whitlock Pharma plc**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13769379**, whose registered office is at 30 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Kingsmere Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10254942**, whose registered office is at 85 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"**Material Adverse Change**" means any event, circumstance or condition which has had or would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition, results of operations or prospects of the Company, taken as a whole, but excluding any such effect arising from or attributable to:

(a) changes in general economic or political conditions in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates;

(b) changes affecting the software or healthcare technology industries generally;

(c) the outbreak or escalation of hostilities, war, acts of terrorism or pandemics;

(d) the announcement or performance of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby.

"**Closing**" means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2.

"**Closing Date**" means 19 February 2027, or such later date as the Parties may agree in writing.

"**Conditions**" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.

"**Parties**" and "Party" shall have the meaning ascribed in the preamble.

"**Purchase Price**" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1.

"**Shares**" means all of the issued and outstanding ordinary shares in the capital of the Company, being 100% of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

---

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, on the Closing Date, the Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares free from all Encumbrances, for a total aggregate consideration of **$60,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee;

(b) resignations of any directors or officers of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably request;

(c) such other documents and instruments as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares and control of the Company to the Buyer.

---

**3. NOTICES**

Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant Party.

---

**4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT**

**4.5** If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived in writing by the Buyer on or before the date falling **90 days** after the date of this Agreement (being 30 January 2027), either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party, whereupon no Party shall have any further obligations under this Agreement, except for those provisions which by their terms survive termination. For the avoidance of doubt, the right to terminate under this Clause 4.5 shall arise automatically upon the expiry of the **90 days** period without satisfaction of the Conditions, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Parties.

---

**5. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each Party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party. This obligation shall survive any termination of this Agreement.

---

**6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
