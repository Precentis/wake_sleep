ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF KINGSMEERE SOFTWARE LTD  
(As amended and restated with effect from 20 November 2026)

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Whitlock Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13769379, with registered office at 30 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Company” means Kingsmere Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10254942, with registered office at 85 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Seller” means Thornbury Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159015, with registered office at 109 Old Broad Street, London;  
“Calloway Medical Ltd” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12799985, with registered office at 100 King William Street, London;  
“Ordinary Share” means an ordinary share in the capital of the Company; and  
“Transfer” means the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Seller to the Buyer pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 22 November 2026.

1.2 The headings in these Articles are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The Company’s share capital consists of 10,000 Ordinary Shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The rights attaching to the Ordinary Shares may be altered only with the prior written consent of the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

3. Transfer of Shares  
3.1 The Seller shall, on the Completion Date (as defined in the Share Purchase Agreement), transfer to the Buyer all of its right, title and interest in and to 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  
3.2 The Buyer shall pay the purchase consideration of £32,750,000 to the Seller via same-day cleared funds to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 4:00 p.m. on the Completion Date.

4. Board of Directors  
4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the power to bind the Company in all matters relating to its operations.  
4.2 The Board may from time to time delegate any of its powers to any committee consisting of one or more directors or other persons as it sees fit.  
4.3 Any director may, by notice in writing to the Company, resign from office at any time.  
4.4 The Company may, by ordinary resolution of the shareholders, remove any director before the expiration of their term.

5. Board Meetings  
5.1 The quorum necessary for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.  
5.2 A meeting of the Board may be convened by any director on not less than 48 hours’ notice to each director, unless waived in writing by all directors entitled to attend.  
5.3 Meetings may be held physically or by video or teleconference, provided that all participants can hear one another in real time.

6. Article 11  
6.1 The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party by hand, post or email.  
7.2 Notices sent by first-class pre-paid post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. Notices sent by email shall be deemed served upon transmission, provided a delivery receipt is obtained.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 The parties agree to keep confidential all terms of the Transfer and any information acquired in connection with the Company’s operations, except where disclosure is required by law or to professional advisers bound by confidentiality obligations.  
8.2 This obligation shall survive termination of any agreement relating to the Transfer and shall continue for a period of seven years from 25 November 2026.

9. Material Customer Contract  
9.1 The Company shall maintain in full force and effect the software services agreement with Calloway Medical Ltd (company number 12799985), without amendment to its core terms, for a minimum period of 36 months from the Completion Date.  
9.2 Any termination or material modification of such agreement must be approved in advance by the Buyer in writing.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
10.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising in connection with these Articles.
