MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF KINGSMERE SOFTWARE LTD  
HELD AT 85 LUDGATE HILL, LONDON, EC4M 7AN  
ON 20 NOVEMBER 2026  

1. ATTENDANCE AND QUORUM  
1.1. The following directors were present for the duration of the meeting:  
       (a) Mr. Elliot Grayson  
       (b) Mrs. Helena Price  

1.2. The Chairman of the Board, Mr. Grayson, opened the meeting and confirmed that notice of this meeting had been duly given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Kingsmere Software Ltd (the “Company”).  

1.3. The Chairman confirmed that 2 directors were in attendance, and that such attendance constituted a quorum under the Company’s Articles of Association, having regard to the total board comprising five directors.  

2. CONFLICT OF INTEREST  
2.1. Mrs. Price declared an indirect interest in the matters to be discussed under agenda item 3 by virtue of her sister’s employment as a senior legal counsel at Thornbury Software Ltd (the “Seller”), the vendor in the proposed transaction.  

2.2. Having considered the nature of the interest, the Chairman confirmed that Mrs. Price would remain in attendance for the discussion and voting, as the interest was not direct and did not constitute a disqualifying conflict under the Company’s internal governance guidelines or applicable law.  

3. PROPOSED SALE OF ENTIRE ISSUED SHARE CAPITAL  
3.1. The directors reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Seller proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to Whitlock Pharma plc (the “Buyer”).  

3.2. The Buyer is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13769379, with its registered office at 30 Ludgate Hill, London.  

3.3. The Seller is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13159015, with its registered office at 109 Old Broad Street, London.  

3.4. The Company is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10254942, with its registered office at 85 Ludgate Hill, London.  

3.5. The directors noted that the SPA contains provisions relating to representations, warranties, and post-completion undertakings, all of which have been reviewed by external legal counsel.  

3.6. The directors discussed the potential implications of the transaction on the Company’s ongoing commercial relationship with Calloway Medical Ltd, a material customer incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12799985, with its registered office at 100 King William Street, London.  

3.7. The Board received assurances that no consent or notification obligation arises under the existing commercial agreement with Calloway Medical Ltd as a result of the change in ownership structure.  

4. RESOLUTIONS  
4.1. IT WAS RESOLVED THAT:  
       (a) The Board approves the entry by the Seller into the Share Purchase Agreement in relation to the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer.  
       (b) The terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, in the form presented, be and are hereby approved.  
       (c) The transaction shall be governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

4.2. The resolution was put to a vote and carried unanimously by the 2 directors present.  

5. CLOSING  
5.1. The Chairman declared the meeting closed at 11:45 a.m.
