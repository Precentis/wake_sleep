**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 15 January 2022  

**Between:**  
(1) **Eskdale Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 13579672 whose registered office is at 139 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Customer”); and  
(2) **Kingsmere Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 12166763 whose registered office is at 20 Cheapside, London (the “Supplier”).

**Recitals**  
(A) The Supplier is engaged in the business of providing precision engineering services and equipment maintenance.  
(B) The Customer wishes to procure certain engineering support services from the Supplier, and the Supplier agrees to provide such services, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

**Operative Provisions**  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Affiliate” means, in relation to a party, a person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party;  
“Control” means the power to direct the management or policies of a person, whether through ownership of shares, entitlement to voting rights, by contract or otherwise;  
“Effective Date” means 15 January 2022;  
“Services” means the engineering maintenance and technical support services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule 1.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

**2. Appointment and Scope**  
2.1 The Customer hereby appoints the Supplier to provide the Services, and the Supplier accepts such appointment, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**3. Term**  
3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of five (5) years, unless earlier terminated under Clause 14.

**4. Payment**  
4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule 2, in pounds sterling, within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice issued by the Supplier.  
4.2 All sums payable under this Agreement are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be added where applicable.

**5. Performance of Services**  
5.1 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care, and diligence, and in accordance with industry standards.

**6. Confidentiality**  
6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.  
6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**7. Notices**  
7.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office of the recipient or such other address as notified in writing.  
7.2 Notices shall be deemed received if delivered by hand, on signature of delivery; if sent by post, two (2) business days after posting; if sent by email, on the next business day after transmission.

**8. Assignment and Subcontracting**  
8.1 Neither party may assign, transfer, or otherwise deal with any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
9.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
9.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

**14. Termination**  
14.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:  
(a) fails to remedy a material breach of this Agreement within thirty (30) days of receiving notice requiring remedy; or  
(b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation, administration, or analogous proceedings.  

14.2 Either party may terminate this agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this clause, “change of control” includes the acquisition of more than 50 per cent of the issued share capital of the relevant party by a person or group of persons acting in concert. The counterparty is Eskdale Energy plc.
