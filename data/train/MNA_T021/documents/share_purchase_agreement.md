# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 12 February 2026.

**Parties:**

(1) **Pemberton Marine Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14064560, whose registered office is at 29 King William Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Ivybridge Foods Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10460424, whose registered office is at 79 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Seller and the Buyer are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**Background:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Kingsmere Engineering Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12166763, whose registered office is at 20 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

Now, therefore, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;
- "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;
- "Closing Date" means 23 March 2026;
- "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 7.1;
- "Effective Date" means the date of this Agreement, being 12 February 2026;
- "Governmental Authority" means any domestic or foreign federal, state, local, municipal or other governmental, regulatory or administrative authority, department, court or agency;
- "Kingsmere Engineering Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12166763;
- "Law" includes any statute, statutory instrument, by-law, rule, regulation, order, judgment, decree, directive, treaty or code of any Governmental Authority;
- "Notice" has the meaning given in Clause 8.1;
- "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

---

**2. Purchase and Sale of Shares**

2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the "Purchase Price") is **$120,000,000**, to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in immediately available funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

---

**3. Completion**

3.1 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree, at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, in favour of the Buyer or its nominee(s);

(b) share certificates representing the Shares (if any), or evidence of loss in form satisfactory to the Buyer;

(c) such corporate authorisations and resolutions as the Buyer may reasonably require to demonstrate the Seller’s authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;

(d) a certificate of non-UK tax residency for the Seller under the UK Corporation Tax Act 2010, if applicable.

---

**4. Conditions**

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions.

4.2 The Buyer and the Seller shall use all reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions as soon as reasonably practicable.

4.3 The Conditions include, but are not limited to:

(a) receipt of all necessary third-party consents and approvals, including any required under agreements with Eskdale Energy plc (company number 13579672);

(b) no Governmental Authority having enacted, issued, promulgated, enforced or entered any Law or order which prohibits the Closing.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling **120 days after the date of this Agreement**, being 12 February 2026, then either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving Notice to the other Party, whereupon this Agreement shall cease to have effect, except for Clauses 7 (Confidentiality) and 8 (Notices), which shall survive.

---

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not at any time after the date of this Agreement disclose to any person any Confidential Information, except:

(a) as required by Law or any Governmental Authority;

(b) with the prior written consent of the other Party;

(c) to its professional advisers under an obligation of confidentiality.

7.2 "Confidential Information" means all information (of any nature and in any form) relating to the business, finances, customers (including Eskdale Energy plc), operations or prospects of the Company, the Seller or the Buyer, which is disclosed before or after the date of this Agreement.

---
