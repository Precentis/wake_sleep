SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 19 April 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Corrib Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11602557, whose registered office is at 154 Fleet Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Holloway Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15445207, whose registered office is at 56 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

and

Kingsmere Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11501611, whose registered office is at 164 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”).

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the Shares on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“Closing Date” means 4 July 2027;

“Confidential Information” means all information of a Party or the Company, whether financial, commercial, technical or otherwise, which is disclosed or made available before or after the date of this Agreement;

“EBITDA” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United Kingdom;

“excess” means the amount by which any value or claim exceeds a specified threshold under this Agreement;

“FY2026” means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026;

“Parties” means the Seller and the Buyer;

“Purchase Price” means the amount payable under Clause 2.1;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $135,000,000 (one hundred and thirty-five million US dollars), payable in cash at Closing, subject to adjustment in accordance with this Agreement.

3. PAYMENT AND CLOSING

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.4 Earn-out: If the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $6,500,000 (six million five hundred thousand US dollars), the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the “Earn-out Payment”) calculated as 2 (two) times the amount by which such EBITDA exceeds $6,500,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be paid within 90 days of the final audited accounts for FY2026 being delivered to the Buyer. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $3,000,000 (three million US dollars).

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

9.2 Limitation of Liability: The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims under this Agreement, including warranty claims, shall not exceed 25% of the Purchase Price, being $27,000,000 (twenty-seven million US dollars). The Purchase Price is $135,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

9.4 De Minimis and Basket: The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer for any claim under this Agreement unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $675,000 (six hundred and seventy-five thousand US dollars), being 0.5% of the Purchase Price. If such threshold is exceeded, the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which the aggregate claims exceed $675,000, and such liability shall be limited to the excess over that threshold.

9.7 Notification of Claims: Any claim by the Buyer under this Agreement must be notified to the Seller in writing within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the claim. Failure to notify within such period shall bar the claim. The notice shall specify the nature of the claim and, where reasonably practicable, an estimate of the amount of the claim and the basis for such estimate, including any relevant calculation of the excess, if applicable.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not at any time disclose to any person any Confidential Information, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant Party.

11.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 154 Fleet Street, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 56 Cheapside, London.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
