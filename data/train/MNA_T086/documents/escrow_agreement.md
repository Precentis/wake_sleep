ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 May 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Holloway Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15445207, whose registered office is at 56 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Corrib Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11602557, whose registered office is at 154 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) Kingsmere Trust Corporation Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 07890123, whose registered office is at 22 Moorgate, London, acting in its capacity as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Recitals

A. The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Kingsmere Robotics Ltd (the "Company"), a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11501611 and whose registered office is at 164 Ludgate Hill, London.

B. In connection with the SPA, the parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") shall be deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure, among other things, the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

C. The parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent specifying in reasonable detail the nature of a claim against the Escrow Amount, the amount claimed, and the basis of the claim under the SPA.

"Effective Date" means the date on which completion of the acquisition under the SPA occurs, being 4 May 2027.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £15,000,000 (fifteen million pounds sterling), representing 18.5% of the total consideration payable under the SPA, which has been deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 2.

"SPA" has the meaning given in Recital A.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

2. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1 On or prior to the Effective Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") maintained with a designated financial institution in London, in accordance with standard escrow practices.

2.3 The Escrow Agent shall not invest the Escrow Amount unless otherwise instructed in writing by the Buyer and the Seller, acting jointly.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be released by the Escrow Agent:

(a) ninety per cent (90%) of the Escrow Amount to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date, being 4 November 2028; and

(b) the remaining ten per cent (10%) of the Escrow Amount to the Seller on the date falling 36 months after the Effective Date, being 4 May 2030;

provided, in each case, that no Claim Notice has been validly served in respect of such portion.

4. Claims Against Escrow Amount

4.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent at any time prior to the release of the relevant portion of the Escrow Amount.

5. Notification and Resolution of Claims

5.1 Any claim against the Escrow Amount must be initiated in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the SPA.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the respective party.

6.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 56 Cheapside, London, attn: General Counsel.

6.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 154 Fleet Street, London, attn: Company Secretary.

6.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: 22 Moorgate, London, attn: Escrow Department.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and any information received pursuant to it confidential and shall not disclose such information to any third party other than its professional advisers, unless required by law.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
