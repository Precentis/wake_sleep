Present:

Apologies:

1. **Call to Order**  
The Chair confirmed that a quorum was present and called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m.

2. **Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 4 April 2027 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously that the minutes be confirmed as a true and accurate record.

3. **Update on Acquisition by Holloway Bioscience plc**  
The board noted that Holloway Bioscience plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15445207, registered office 56 Cheapside, London) has entered into an agreement to acquire the entire issued share capital of Kingsmere Robotics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11501611, registered office 164 Ludgate Hill, London) from Corrib Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11602557, registered office 154 Fleet Street, London). The transaction remains subject to standard regulatory approvals and is expected to complete in accordance with the agreed timetable under English law and the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

4. **Material Customer Contracts**  
The board received a report on material contracts. It was confirmed that Ashcombe Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11083242, registered office 98 Bishopsgate, London) remains a key customer under an existing supply agreement. No changes to the terms or duration of the engagement were reported.

5. **Outstanding Legal Matter**  
The board noted a letter before action received from Foxley Energy plc threatening proceedings for approximately $3,400,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. Legal counsel has been instructed to assess the merits of the claim. Management is to monitor developments closely and report to the board bi-weekly. No admission of liability has been made, and the matter is being treated as contested under English law.

6. **Financial Outlook**  
The CFO presented unaudited financial projections for the six-month period ending 30 September 2027. Revenue forecasts remain in line with internal targets, though margin pressures were noted due to rising component costs. The board endorsed the current cost-control measures in place.

7. **Other Matters**  
The Chair invited any other business. There were no additional items.

8. **Adjournment**  
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:28 a.m.
