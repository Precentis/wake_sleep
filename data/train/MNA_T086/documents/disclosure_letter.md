**2. Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**

**3. General Disclosures**  
3.1 Save as expressly disclosed in this letter, the Seller confirms that it is not aware of any matter, event or circumstance which renders any warranty contained in the SPA untrue or misleading.  
3.2 The disclosures made in this letter are made on a without-prejudice basis and without any admission that the matters disclosed constitute a material fact or give rise to a claim under the SPA.  
3.3 The disclosures are qualified by the knowledge and information available to the Seller as of the date of this letter.  

**4. Known Customer Relationship**  
4.1 The Company is party to a commercial supply agreement with Ashcombe Retail Group plc, entered into on 12 March 2025, under which the Company supplies robotic automation components for use in retail logistics systems.  
4.2 As at 31 December 2026, revenue derived from Ashcombe Retail Group plc constituted 38.4% of the Company’s total annual revenue.  
4.3 The current term of the agreement with Ashcombe Retail Group plc expires on 30 June 2029, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.  
4.4 No notice of termination or material dispute has been served by Ashcombe Retail Group plc in relation to the agreement as at the date of this letter.  

**5. Financial Matters**  
5.1 The unaudited management accounts of the Company as at 31 December 2026 were prepared on a going concern basis and reflect a profit before tax of £1,720,410 for the financial year then ended.  
5.2 There are no contingent liabilities, undisclosed borrowings, or off-balance sheet arrangements other than as disclosed in the financial statements provided to the Buyer pursuant to clause 4.3 of the SPA.  

**6. Payment and Consideration**  
6.1 The total consideration for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company is to be paid in accordance with clause 2.1 of the SPA, comprising £28,500,000 in cash, subject to adjustment for net debt and working capital in accordance with the SPA.  
6.2 The payment is to be made on Completion, as defined in the SPA, by electronic transfer to the bank account nominated in writing by the Seller prior to Completion.
