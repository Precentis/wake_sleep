ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF OAKHURST INDUSTRIES LTD

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
"Buyer" means Corrib Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13441571, whose registered office is at 65 Ludgate Hill, London;  
"Company" means Oakhurst Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14818919, whose registered office is at 33 Ludgate Hill, London;  
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of the Company, the Seller, the Buyer, or any of their respective affiliates, whether in writing, orally, or by observation, including but not limited to financial data, customer lists, trade secrets, and commercial arrangements;  
"Effective Date" means 11 September 2026;  
"Material Customer" means Upton Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13318431, whose registered office is at 65 Moorgate, London;  
"Seller" means Glenrock Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11833947, whose registered office is at 120 Ludgate Hill, London.

1.2 The headings in these Articles are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Share Capital and Transfer of Shares  
2.1 The Company's issued share capital consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 Any transfer of shares in the Company shall be subject to prior written consent from the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The Board shall consist of no fewer than four and no more than six directors.  
3.2 Directors may be appointed or removed by ordinary resolution of the shareholders.  
3.3 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter.

4. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
4.1 Notice of a board meeting shall be given to all directors at least five business days prior to the meeting, unless all directors waive such notice in writing.  
4.2 Meetings may be conducted in person, by video conference, or by other electronic means allowing all participants to communicate effectively.  
4.3 Resolutions in writing signed by all directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting shall be as valid as if passed at a duly convened meeting.  
4.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. No business shall be transacted at any meeting unless a quorum is present at the time the decision is taken.

5. Payment Provisions  
5.1 Any payment due under these Articles shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) to such bank account as the recipient may designate in writing.  
5.2 All payments shall be made no later than ten business days following the date of an invoice issued in accordance with these Articles.

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice required or permitted under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant party.  
6.2 A notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed served on the next business day following transmission.

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Each party shall keep Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party, except:  
(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations;  
(b) as required by law or any regulatory authority; or  
(c) with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.  
7.2 The obligations under this Article 7 shall survive the termination or completion of any transaction involving the Company for a period of five years.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
