       (a) Mr. Edwin Price

2.1. Mr. Edwin Price declared a personal interest in the matters to be discussed under Agenda Item 3, by virtue of his prior advisory engagement with Corrib Logistics plc.

3. REVIEW OF PROPOSED TRANSACTION  
3.1. The Chair provided the board with a summary of the proposed transaction whereby Glenrock Capital LLP (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11833947, registered office 120 Ludgate Hill, London, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Industries Ltd (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14818919, registered office 33 Ludgate Hill, London, to Corrib Logistics plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13441571, registered office 65 Ludgate Hill, London.  
3.2. The transaction is to be effected by way of a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 11 September 2026.  
3.3. The board reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement, noting it provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  
3.4. The directors discussed the implications of the transaction for the Company, including ongoing contracts, notably the supply agreement with Upton Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13318431, registered office 65 Moorgate, London, which remains in full force and effect.  
3.5. The board acknowledged the financial and operational due diligence conducted by the Seller’s advisers, and noted that no material adverse changes had been reported prior to the date of these minutes.

4. RESOLUTIONS  
4.1. After discussion, and having considered all relevant information, IT WAS:  
       RESOLVED to approve entry into the Share Purchase Agreement.

5. AUTHORISATIONS  
5.1. IT WAS FURTHER RESOLVED that any director of the Company be and is hereby authorised to take all such actions, deliver all such notices, and execute all such documents as may be required to give full effect to the resolutions passed at this meeting, including but not limited to the confirmation of corporate status and solvency under English law and the courts of England.
