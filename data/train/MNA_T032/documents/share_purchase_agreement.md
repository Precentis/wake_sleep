SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 6 September 2026.

Between:

(1) Glenrock Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (company number 11833947), whose registered office is at 120 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Seller”); and

(2) Corrib Logistics plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 13441571), whose registered office is at 65 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”).

Recitals

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Industries Ltd, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 14818919), whose registered office is at 33 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

(C) The Buyer and the Seller intend that completion of the sale and purchase shall take place on the Closing Date.

Now this Agreement witnesses:

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 9 December 2026;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, as determined in accordance with applicable accounting principles consistently applied.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

2.1 The total consideration for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company (the “Purchase Price”) is $250,000,000, which shall be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cash on the Closing Date via wire transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Completion and Earn-out**

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms and such other documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

3.3 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller such instruments of transfer, board resolutions and other documents as may be required to effect the transfer of the shares in the Company.

3.4 Earn-out Payment

The Buyer shall make an additional payment (the “Earn-out Payment”) to the Seller following the end of the financial year ending 31 December 2026. The Earn-out Payment equals 3 times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000, such payment being capped at $10,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within 90 days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026 being approved by the board of the Company.

**9. Limitations on Liability**

9.4 De Minimis and Basket

The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty or indemnity under this Agreement unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds $1,250,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller’s liability arises only in respect of the excess above $1,250,000 and not for any amount up to or equal to such threshold.

**10. Confidentiality**

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party or otherwise obtained in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation or with the prior written consent of the other party.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the address or email address set out at the beginning of this Agreement, or such other address as a party may notify in writing.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
