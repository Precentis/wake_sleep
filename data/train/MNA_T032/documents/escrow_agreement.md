ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] 2026, between:

(1) Glenrock Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11833947 and registered office at 120 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Corrib Logistics plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13441571 and registered office at 65 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm authorised and regulated by the [Relevant Authority], having an office at [●] (the “Escrow Agent”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14818919 and registered office at 33 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Company”), pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] 2026 (the “SPA”).

(B) The parties have agreed to establish an escrow account for certain funds in accordance with the terms of the SPA and this Agreement.

(C) The Escrow Agent has agreed to hold and administer the Escrow Amount (as defined below) on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

“Closing Date” means 9 December 2026.

“Confidential Information” means all information of a confidential or proprietary nature, whether oral, written or electronic, disclosed by or on behalf of any party to this Agreement to the Escrow Agent or otherwise arising from the performance of this Agreement.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £4,800,000 (four million eight hundred thousand pounds sterling), representing a portion of the consideration payable under the SPA, deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account established by the Escrow Agent.

“Material Customer” means Upton Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13318431 and registered office at 65 Moorgate, London.

“SPA” has the meaning given in the recitals.

“Working Day” shall have the same meaning as Business Day.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain a dedicated interest-bearing escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) at a recognised financial institution in England, into which the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount on or prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and the SPA, and shall not be released except as expressly provided herein.

3. Payment

3.1 The Buyer shall cause the Escrow Amount to be deposited into the Escrow Account by no later than 5:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount in writing to the Buyer and the Seller within two Business Days of such receipt.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof not subject to any pending claims notified in accordance with the SPA, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any claims notified by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller under the SPA prior to such date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to such pending claims shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claims in accordance with the SPA.

4.2 Upon the determination of any such pending claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed portion of the Escrow Amount to the Seller and any disputed portion as directed by joint written instructions of the Buyer and the Seller or, in the absence of such instructions, by final adjudication under the SPA.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the addresses or email addresses specified in this Agreement or as otherwise notified in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 65 Ludgate Hill, London, Attn: Company Secretary.

5.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 120 Ludgate Hill, London, Attn: Managing Partner.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep all Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law, regulation, or the lawful order of a court of competent jurisdiction.

6.2 This obligation shall survive the release of the Escrow Amount and the termination of this Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
