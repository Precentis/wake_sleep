# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF YARROW INDUSTRIES LTD

**Interpretation**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:
   (a) "the Company" means Yarrow Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under registered number 12308387;
   (b) "the Buyer" means Northgate Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under registered number 10641161;
   (c) "the Seller" means Northgate Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under registered number 12340467;
   (d) "Lowther Marine Ltd" means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under registered number 11156238;
   (e) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;
   (f) "Ordinary Resolution" means a resolution passed by a simple majority of votes cast at a general meeting of the Company;
   (g) "Written Notice" means communication delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or electronic transmission to the relevant registered office.

**Share Capital and Dividends**

2. The issued share capital of the Company consists of 1,000,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each. No further shares shall be issued without prior written consent from the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld following the completion of any acquisition involving the Company.

3. Subject to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, dividends may be declared and paid at such times and in such amounts as the Board may determine, provided that no dividend shall be declared or paid unless certified as payable by the Company’s auditors in accordance with the latest approved financial statements.

**Directors**

4. The Board shall consist of no fewer than two and no more than six directors. Directors shall be appointed by Ordinary Resolution of the shareholders.

5. A director may be removed from office by Ordinary Resolution passed at a general meeting, provided that at least 28 days’ Written Notice of the intention to propose such resolution has been given to the Company.

6. The Board may delegate any of its powers to committees as it sees fit, provided that such delegation is recorded in writing and retained in the Company’s minute books.

7. The Board shall meet at least quarterly. Minutes of all meetings shall be prepared and signed by the chairman of the meeting.

**Quorum and Proceedings**

8. For the purposes of board meetings, business may be conducted whether or not all directors are present.

9. A director shall disclose any personal interest in a transaction before it is approved by the Board. Failure to disclose shall disqualify the director from voting on the matter.

10. Any decision of the Board may be made either at a duly convened meeting or by written resolution signed by all directors entitled to receive notice of board meetings.

**Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. If within half an hour of the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, and if at the adjourned meeting a quorum is not present within half an hour, the directors present (provided there are at least two) shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.**

**Payment and Financial Provisions**

12. All payments made by the Company exceeding £50,000 in a single transaction must be approved in advance by two directors, one of whom must be the Finance Director.

13. The Company shall maintain bank accounts in accordance with policies approved by the Board. All signatories must be directors or duly authorised officers.

**Confidentiality**

14. Every director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information acquired in the course of their duties, except where disclosure is required by law or authorised by the Board.

15. The restrictions in Clause 14 shall survive termination of office or employment and shall apply for a period of five years from the date of such termination.

**Notices**

16. Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be sufficiently given if delivered to the registered office of the Company or the relevant party at its registered office as set out in Clause 1.

17. Notices sent by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. Notices sent electronically shall be deemed served upon confirmation of receipt.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

18. These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with these Articles.

19. The Company confirms that Lowther Marine Ltd, registered number 11156238, is a material customer whose annual contract value represents 37% of the Company’s total revenue for the financial year ending 31 December 2025.
