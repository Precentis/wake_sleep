**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 28 June 2026.

**Parties**

(1) Northgate Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12340467, whose registered office is at 6 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller").

(2) Northgate Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10641161, whose registered office is at 59 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

**Background**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Yarrow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12308387 and registered office at 151 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms have the following meanings:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 6 September 2026;

"Confidential Information" means any information, whether written or oral, relating to the business, products, finances, operations or affairs of the Company or the Seller, including technical, commercial or strategic information disclosed under or in connection with this Agreement;

"Claims" means any claims for breach of warranty or indemnity arising under this Agreement;

"De Minimis Threshold" means $1,200,000;

"Liability Cap" means 20% of the Purchase Price, being $24,000,000;

"Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

1.3 References to Clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

2.1 The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of $120,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Completion**

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer's solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates (if any) in respect of the Shares, duly endorsed or accompanied by stock transfer forms;

(c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

(d) such other documents as may reasonably be required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares free from all Encumbrances.

**8. Limitation of Liability**

8.1 Except in respect of fraud or wilful misrepresentation, the liability of the Seller under this Agreement shall be limited as set out in this Clause 8.

8.2 The representations and warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement shall not survive beyond 18 months from the Closing Date.

8.3 The aggregate liability of the Seller for warranty claims shall not exceed an amount equal to 20% of the Purchase Price, being $30,000,000. The Purchase Price is $120,000,000 (see Section 2.1).

**9. Financial Limitations on Claims**

9.1 No Claim shall be brought unless it exceeds $100,000 in value individually.

9.2 All Claims of less than $100,000 may be aggregated for the purpose of determining whether the threshold in Clause 9.1 is met.

9.3 The rights and remedies of the Buyer under this Agreement are subject to the De Minimis Threshold.

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable unless the aggregate of all Claims exceeds $1,200,000 (1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.

**10. Confidentiality**

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party, except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis and under an obligation of confidence;

(b) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

10.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

11.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered (if by hand), 48 hours after posting (if by post), or upon confirmed receipt (if by email).

**12. General**

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

12.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under this Agreement.
