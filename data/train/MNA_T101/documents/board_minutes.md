MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF YARROW INDUSTRIES LTD

Date: 10 July 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 151 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JW  
Present:  
- Mr A. Patel (Director)  
- Ms L. Clarke (Director)  

1. **Call to Order**  
The meeting was convened at 10:00 a.m. on 10 July 2026 at the registered office of Yarrow Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12308387, located at 151 Ludgate Hill, London. The meeting was duly called in accordance with the company’s Articles of Association.

2. **Quorum**  
It was confirmed that the quorum required for a board meeting under the company’s Articles of Association is two directors. There being 2 directors present, the Chairman declared the meeting quorate.

3. **Declaration of Interests**  
No director present had any disclosable interest in the matters to be considered under the following agenda items. No conflicts of interest were identified.

4. **Review of Proposed Transaction**  
The directors reviewed the proposed transaction whereby Northgate Holdings Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12340467 and having its registered office at 6 Ludgate Hill, London, intends to sell the entire issued share capital of Yarrow Industries Ltd to Northgate Bioscience plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10641161 and having its registered office at 59 Fleet Street, London.

5. **Consideration of Share Purchase Agreement**  
The draft Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) was reviewed. The SPA provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company. The directors noted that the Buyer is a related party to the Seller by way of common control. The terms of the SPA, including all warranties, covenants, and post-completion obligations, were examined in detail.

6. **Material Customer Relationship**  
The board discussed the Company’s ongoing contractual relationship with Lowther Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11156238 and having its registered office at 62 Queen Victoria Street, London. It was confirmed that Lowther Marine Ltd accounts for 37% of the Company’s total revenue over the past 12 months. The impact of the proposed transaction on this customer relationship was assessed, and it was determined that no material adverse effect is anticipated.

7. **Resolution on Approval of Share Purchase Agreement**  
Upon motion duly made,  
**RESOLVED** that the board of Yarrow Industries Ltd hereby approves the entry by the Seller into the Share Purchase Agreement for the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, and authorises any director of the Company to take such steps and execute such documents as may be necessary to facilitate completion of the transaction in accordance with the terms of the SPA.

8. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
It was noted that the Share Purchase Agreement is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

9. **Adjournment**  
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
