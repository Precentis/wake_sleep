1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Agreement" means this contract dated on or about 16 February 2026 between Fenwick Holdings Ltd and Rothley Retail Group plc;

"Commencement Date" means the date on which this Agreement becomes effective, being 1 January 2023;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a proprietary or confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, including but not limited to pricing structures, customer lists, operational procedures and technical data;

"Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected party, including but not limited to strikes, lock-outs, industrial action, acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemics, or government regulation;

"Initial Term" means the initial period of three (3) years commencing on the Commencement Date;

"Renewal Term" means any subsequent period following the Initial Term, each of one (1) year duration, automatically renewed unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to expiry of the then-current term;

"Services" means the supply of logistics, warehousing and inventory management services as described in Schedule A.

2. SUPPLY OF SERVICES

2.1 Rothley Retail Group plc shall provide the Services to Fenwick Holdings Ltd in accordance with the specifications, service levels and performance standards set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Services shall be performed with due care, skill and diligence and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 Fenwick Holdings Ltd shall pay Rothley Retail Group plc the fees as specified in Schedule B, payable in arrears on the last business day of each calendar month.

3.2 All amounts stated are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be added at the prevailing rate.

3.3 Any undisputed invoice not paid within thirty (30) days of receipt shall bear interest at the rate of 4.5% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, accruing on a daily basis.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the receiving party.

4.2 Notices to Fenwick Holdings Ltd shall be sent to: 66 Fleet Street, London, or such other address as notified in writing.

4.3 Notices to Rothley Retail Group plc shall be sent to: 64 Old Broad Street, London, or such other address as notified in writing.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information disclosed by the other and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and continue for the Initial Term, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

6.2 Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Clause 1.1.

14. TERMINATION FOR CHANGE OF CONTROL

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this clause, "change of control" shall include the acquisition of more than 51% of the issued share capital of Rothley Retail Group plc by Merrion Logistics plc or any other entity. The counterparty for the purposes of this Agreement is Fenwick Holdings Ltd.
