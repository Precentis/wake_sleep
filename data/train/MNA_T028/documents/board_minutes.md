MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF ROTHLEY RETAIL GROUP PLC

Date: 14 February 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Place: 64 Old Broad Street, London, EC2N 1HS  

1. Attendance  

1.1. Present:  
- Mr A. T. Greaves (Director)  
- Ms L. J. Chen (Director)  

1.2. Absent:  
- Mr R. P. Whitlock (Director)  
- Ms S. K. Adeyemi (Director)  
- Mr D. M. Ellis (Director)  

1.3. The Chairman confirmed that 2 directors were present, constituting a quorum pursuant to Article 12 of the Company’s Articles of Association. The meeting was accordingly quorate.  

1.4. The Chairman opened the meeting and invited the Company Secretary to confirm the agenda.  

2. Consideration of the proposed acquisition by Merrion Logistics plc  

2.1. The Company Secretary informed the Board that Merrion Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14588136, registered office 72 Moorgate, London) had made an offer to acquire the entire issued share capital of Rothley Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14397122, registered office 64 Old Broad Street, London) from Whitlock Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12045337, registered office 25 Cheapside, London).  

2.2. The Company Secretary outlined the terms of the proposed Share Purchase Agreement, including the obligation on the Buyer to purchase 100% of the issued shares in the Company from the Seller, and confirmed that the agreement was governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

2.3. The Board reviewed a copy of the draft Share Purchase Agreement provided by the Buyer’s legal advisers. The Board acknowledged that the transaction, if completed, would result in a change of control of the Company.  

2.4. The Directors considered the implications of the transaction for the Company, including its employees, commercial relationships, and ongoing operations. The Board noted that Fenwick Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11150981, registered office 66 Fleet Street, London) remained a material customer under existing supply terms.  

3. Resolutions  

3.1. It was proposed by Mr A. T. Greaves and seconded by Ms L. J. Chen:  

RESOLVED THAT the Board of Rothley Retail Group plc hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement between Merrion Logistics plc and Whitlock Medical Ltd relating to the acquisition of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Company Secretary be authorised to take all steps necessary to execute, deliver and file the Share Purchase Agreement and any related documents on behalf of the Company, including obtaining notarisation or legalisation where required.  

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Directors of the Company be and are hereby released from any liability arising from the implementation of this transaction, to the fullest extent permitted by law.  

4. Adjournment  

4.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:52 a.m.
