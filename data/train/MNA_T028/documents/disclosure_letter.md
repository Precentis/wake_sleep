3. Disclosure Against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)

3.1 For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller confirms that no material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision. This disclosure is made in response to Warranty 6.3 of the Agreement, which requires disclosure of any material contract containing provisions triggered by a change of control.

3.2 The Company is party to certain contracts with Fenwick Holdings Ltd (company number 11150981, registered office 66 Fleet Street, London), which are considered material contracts for the purposes of the Agreement. However, none of those contracts contains any provision that would be activated, modified or terminated by reason of the transaction contemplated under the Agreement, including any change of control of the Company arising from the sale of its entire issued share capital to the Buyer.

3.3 Without limiting the generality of clause 3.1, the Seller has not withheld any information relating to change of control rights, consents or obligations under any material contract to which the Company is bound.
