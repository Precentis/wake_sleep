SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 20 January 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Merrion Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14588136 whose registered office is at 72 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Whitlock Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12045337 whose registered office is at 25 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) Rothley Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14397122 whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its schedules and annexes;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 15 May 2026;

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3.2;

   "Parties" means the Buyer, the Seller and the Company, and "Party" means any one of them;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as specified in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $155,000,000. The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller may designate in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall transfer to the Buyer full legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, and the Buyer shall accept such transfer and pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 The obligations of the Parties to complete the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or, where permissible, waiver in writing by both Parties of the following conditions (the "Conditions") on or before the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement:

   (a) all necessary corporate and shareholder approvals in connection with the transaction contemplated by this Agreement shall have been obtained;

   (b) no order, injunction or other legal restraint preventing the Closing shall have been issued by any court or governmental authority of competent jurisdiction; and

   (c) the Seller shall have delivered to the Buyer all documents required under this Agreement, including but not limited to share transfer forms, resignations of directors (if agreed), and evidence of board approvals.

4. TERMINATION

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided that such right shall only be exercisable if the notifying Party is not in material breach of any of its obligations under this Agreement.

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

9.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date that the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 2 are true and accurate in all respects.

9.7 The Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of any claim under this Clause 9 within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim, failing which the claim shall be barred.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from any other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by prepaid first-class post or by email to the address or email address set out at the beginning of this Agreement, or such other address or email address as a Party may notify in writing.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
