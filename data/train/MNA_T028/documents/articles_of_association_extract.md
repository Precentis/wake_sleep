ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ROTHLEY RETAIL GROUP PLC  
(Extract)  

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Merrion Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14588136, with registered office at 72 Moorgate, London;  
“Company” means Rothley Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14397122, with registered office at 64 Old Broad Street, London;  
“Seller” means Whitlock Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12045337, with registered office at 25 Cheapside, London;  
“Customer” means Fenwick Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11150981, with registered office at 66 Fleet Street, London;  
“ordinary share” means an ordinary share in the capital of the Company;  
“person” includes any individual, company, partnership, trust, association or other body of persons, corporate or unincorporate; and  
“writing” includes any form of electronic communication capable of being reproduced in hard copy.  

2. Share Capital and Shares  
2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1 each.  
2.2 The Company may allot, grant options over or otherwise dispose of shares in its capital with such voting, dividend, return of capital and other rights as the Board may determine.  
2.3 The rights attached to any class of shares may be varied only with the consent in writing of the holders of not less than 75% of the issued shares of that class.  

3. Transfer of Shares  
3.1 Subject to these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of the member’s shares by instrument in writing in the usual or common form or in any other form approved by the Board.  
3.2 The instrument of transfer must be signed by or on behalf of the transferor and, unless the Board otherwise determines, by the transferee.  
3.3 No transfer of a share shall be registered if it would result in more than one holder being registered as owner of a single share.  

4. Board of Directors  
4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, who may exercise all the powers of the Company unless by these Articles or by law those powers are required to be exercised by the members.  
4.2 The Board may delegate any of its powers to committees or individuals as it sees fit, provided that any such delegation is recorded in writing and filed with the minute book of the Company.  
4.3 Directors may be appointed or removed by resolution of the Board, subject to any agreement in writing between the Company and any director.  

5. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
5.1 The Board shall meet as often as the management of the Company’s affairs requires, and at least once every calendar quarter.  
5.2 A director may participate in a meeting of the Board by means of such telephone, electronic or other communication equipment as permits all persons participating in the meeting to hear each other.  
5.3 Any decision of the Board may be made by a resolution in writing signed by all directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting.  
5.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.  

6. Payment and Financial Provisions  
6.1 All payments made by or to the Company in connection with the acquisition of shares or assets shall be made in sterling unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.  
6.2 Any payment due under an agreement involving the Company and the Buyer or Seller shall be made no later than 15 February 2026 unless expressly deferred in writing by the receiving party.  

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and served by delivering it personally, sending it by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the last known address or email address of the recipient.  
7.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed served at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.  

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Each director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, customers, suppliers and financial affairs of the Company, the Buyer and the Seller, including but not limited to information relating to the Customer.  
8.2 The obligations under this clause shall continue after the termination of office or employment and shall not be discharged by any disclosure required by law or regulation.  

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles and all matters arising from or connected with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
