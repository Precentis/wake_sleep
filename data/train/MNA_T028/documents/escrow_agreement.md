**ESCROW AGREEMENT**

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on 15 February 2026 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Merrion Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14588136, whose registered office is at 72 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Whitlock Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12045337, whose registered office is at 25 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, whose principal office is at [Address] (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Effective Date (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Rothley Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14397122, whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company");

(B) As part of the SPA, a portion of the purchase price is to be held in escrow in accordance with the terms of this Agreement to secure the obligations of the Seller under the SPA;

(C) The parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount (as defined below) on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. **Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London;

"Claim" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent setting out in reasonable detail the nature and basis of any claim arising under or in connection with the SPA;

"Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 8;

"Disclosing Party" has the meaning given in Clause 8;

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,250,000 held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3;

"Party" means a party to this Agreement and "Parties" means all such parties;

"Recipient" has the meaning given in Clause 8.

2. **Deposit of Escrow Amount**

2.1 The Seller hereby confirms that the Escrow Amount has been delivered to the Escrow Agent for safekeeping in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent acknowledges receipt of the Escrow Amount and agrees to hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. **Holding of Funds**

3.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated client account at a bank acceptable to the Buyer and the Seller.

3.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent as agent for the Buyer and the Seller, without assuming ownership or beneficial interest in the funds.

4. **Payment and Disbursement**

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or any portion thereof, shall be released only upon receipt by the Escrow Agent of:

(a) a joint written instruction signed by both the Buyer and the Seller; or

(b) a final and binding determination by a court of competent jurisdiction requiring release.

5. **Claims**

5.1 The Buyer may submit a Claim to the Seller and the Escrow Agent at any time during the period ending 18 months from the Effective Date (the "Claim Period").

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim, the Escrow Agent shall notify the Seller and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount in respect of such Claim unless instructed jointly or by final court order.

6. **Notices**

6.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above or as otherwise notified.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received at the time of delivery if delivered personally or by email (with confirmation of receipt), or 48 hours after posting if sent by first-class post.

7. **Confidentiality**

7.1 Each Party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all information received from another Party (the "Disclosing Party") in connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information").

7.2 The Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

7.3 The obligations under this Clause 7 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

8. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
