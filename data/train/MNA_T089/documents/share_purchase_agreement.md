SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Share Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 17 June 2026 (the "Execution Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Northgate Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15948369, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Vantage Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12520788, whose registered office is at 178 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Delamere Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10163457, whose registered office is at 2 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the Shares (as defined below) on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

(C) The acquisition is scheduled to complete on 12 August 2026 (the "Closing Date").

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this Share Purchase Agreement, including its recitals, schedules and any documents incorporated by reference.

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.

"Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 11.

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax.

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 2 (Representations and Warranties), excluding the Fundamental Warranties.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company, consisting of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

"Survival Period" means the period during which the applicable warranties survive Closing as set out in Clause 10.1.

"Tax" means any tax, duty, levy, impost, assessment, governmental charge or withholding of any nature, including any related fine, penalty, interest or addition to tax.

"Warranty Claim" means a claim by the Buyer for breach of any warranty given by the Seller under this Agreement.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $90,000,000 (ninety million United States dollars) (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

2.2 Subject to the satisfaction or waiver (where permitted) of the Conditions, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares on the Closing Date.

3. Payment Mechanism

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by wire transfer to the account(s) designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Payment shall be made in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

4. Conditions Precedent

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the prior satisfaction or waiver (where permitted) of the following Conditions:

(a) all necessary corporate and regulatory approvals having been obtained; and

(b) no order or injunction being issued by any court or authority restraining or prohibiting the transaction.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 16 September 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

9. Indemnities and Claims

9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any Warranty Claim within 30 days of becoming aware of the facts giving rise to such claim.

10. Survival

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive Closing for a period of 12 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the relevant Survival Period.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed in connection with this transaction ("Confidential Information") and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

11.2 The obligations under this Clause 11 shall continue to apply for a period of three years after termination or completion of this Agreement.

15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement is governed by English law and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with English law.
