MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF DELAMERE RETAIL GROUP PLC

Date: 24 June 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 2 Queen Victoria Street, London, EC4N 4SA  
Present:  
- Mr. Jonathan Pike (Director)  
- Ms. Eleanor Grant (Director)  

The meeting was convened in accordance with the Articles of Association of Delamere Retail Group plc. Notice of the meeting was duly given.  

1. **Call to Order**  
The Chair, Mr. Jonathan Pike, called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m.  

2. **Quorum**  
It was confirmed that 2 directors were present. The Articles of Association provide that a quorum for a board meeting is two directors. The Chair declared that a quorum was present and the meeting was quorate.  

3. **Declaration of Interests**  
Both directors confirmed that they had no personal interests in the matters to be discussed requiring disclosure under the Companies Act 2006.  

4. **Review of Proposed Share Purchase Agreement**  
The directors reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 23 June 2026, pursuant to which Northgate Software Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15948369, registered office 140 Moorgate, London, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of Delamere Retail Group plc (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10163457, registered office 2 Queen Victoria Street, London, to Vantage Industries Ltd (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12520788, registered office 178 Old Broad Street, London.  

The directors noted that the Company is a party to a commercial supply agreement with Marlow Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12917628, registered office 77 Bishopsgate, London, which constitutes a material customer relationship.  

5. **Approval of Entry into Share Purchase Agreement**  
After discussion, the directors considered the terms of the proposed transaction, including the implications for the Company’s operations, personnel, and ongoing contractual obligations.  

RESOLVED, as a special resolution of the board of directors of Delamere Retail Group plc:  

(a) That the Company hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 23 June 2026 between Northgate Software Ltd and Vantage Industries Ltd;  

(b) That the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, including all schedules, warranties, and conditions precedent, be and are hereby approved in full;  

(c) That Mr. Jonathan Pike and Ms. Eleanor Grant are jointly authorised to take all necessary steps to execute the Share Purchase Agreement on behalf of the Company, including the signing of counterparts and delivery of notices;  

(d) That the execution of the Share Purchase Agreement and all related documents shall be governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

6. **Next Steps**  
The Chair confirmed that the transaction is expected to complete on 30 June 2026, subject to satisfaction of conditions precedent. The directors instructed the Company Secretary to ensure all corporate filings are updated post-completion.  

7. **Adjournment**  
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
