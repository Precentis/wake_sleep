ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF DELAMERE RETAIL GROUP PLC

1. Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Board" means the board of directors of the Company;

"Buyer" means Vantage Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12520788, whose registered office is at 178 Old Broad Street, London;

"Company" means Delamere Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10163457, whose registered office is at 2 Queen Victoria Street, London;

"Company Secretary" means the person for the time being appointed to perform the functions of the secretary of the Company;

"Seller" means Northgate Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15948369, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London;

"Marlow Energy plc" means the customer incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12917628, whose registered office is at 77 Bishopsgate, London.

1.2 References to a "director" include any person called by any other name who performs the functions of a director.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall be divided into ordinary shares, each with a nominal value of £1.

2.2 The Company may from time to time by ordinary resolution determine that the share capital shall be increased by the creation of new shares of such nominal amount and with such rights, privileges, limitations or restrictions as the resolution may specify.

3. Payment for Shares

3.1 Any new shares issued by the Company shall be paid for in full in cash at the time of allotment, unless otherwise determined by a special resolution of the members.

4. Transfer of Shares

4.1 Subject to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of their shares by instrument in writing in the usual form or in any other form approved by the Board.

4.2 The instrument of transfer shall be delivered to the registered office of the Company, or such other place as the Board may determine, together with the relevant share certificate and such evidence of title and identity as the Board may reasonably require.

5. Meetings of Shareholders

5.1 The Company shall hold a general meeting as its annual general meeting in accordance with the Companies Act 2006.

5.2 The Board may call such other general meetings as it considers necessary. Such meetings shall be called by notice in writing given to each member entitled to attend.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given to any member, director or the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and may be served by leaving it at, or sending it by post or electronic means to, the address of that person last known to the Company.

6.2 A notice sent through the post shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Every director and officer of the Company shall preserve the confidentiality of all information relating to the business, affairs, customers (including Marlow Energy plc), suppliers and financial status of the Company, and shall not, without prior written consent of the Board, disclose any such information to any third party except as required by law.

7.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive the cessation of office of any director or officer.

8. Board of Directors

8.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the power to bind the Company in all matters incidental to or consequential upon its objects.

8.2 The Board may delegate any of its powers to committees consisting of such persons as it deems appropriate.

9. Board Meetings

9.1 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes of the directors present.

9.2 A director may, and the Chair shall where requested by two or more directors, convene a meeting of the Board upon not less than 24 hours' notice.

10. Quorum for Board Meetings

10.1 The quorum required for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.

12. Dividends and Distributions

12.1 The Company may, by ordinary resolution, declare dividends in respect of the ordinary shares, provided that no dividend shall be declared unless permitted by the Companies Act 2006 and the Company’s available profits.

13. Governing Law

13.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.
