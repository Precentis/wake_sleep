ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 20 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Vantage Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12520788, whose registered office is at 178 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Northgate Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15948369, whose registered office is at 140 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 02796993, whose registered office is at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ, as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Background

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 26 June 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Delamere Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10163457, whose registered office is at 2 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) In connection with the SPA, the parties have agreed that an amount equal to 25% of the total consideration (the "Escrow Amount") shall be deposited into an escrow account to secure certain indemnities and obligations of the Seller.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London and New York.

"Claim Notice" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Escrow Agent and the Seller specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

"Escrow Account" means the account designated by the Escrow Agent into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

2.1 On the Completion Date under the SPA, the Buyer shall cause 25% of the total consideration to be paid to the Escrow Agent for deposit into the Escrow Account.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Payment of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller in full on the date which is 18 months after the Completion Date (the "Release Date").

3.2 If a Claim Notice is validly served prior to the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall retain the portion of the Escrow Amount subject to such claim until final resolution.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim.

6. Escrow Agent Responsibilities

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with instructions signed by both the Buyer and the Seller, or as required by final award of arbitration under Clause 12.

6.2 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the recipient’s registered office.

7.2 Notices shall be deemed received 24 hours after transmission by email or 48 hours after being sent by pre-paid first-class post.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and not disclose them to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one or more arbitrators appointed in accordance with the said rules, and the place of arbitration shall be New York.
