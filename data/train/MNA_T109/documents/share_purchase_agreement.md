SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 30 January 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Calloway Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11836982, whose registered office is at 113 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) Corrib Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12921649, whose registered office is at 48 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

(4) Upton Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11822158, whose registered office is at 23 Ludgate Hill, London, is referred to as the "Company".

RECITALS

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

   "Closing Date" means 22 March 2027.

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.

   "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with UK GAAP consistently applied.

   "Earn-out Payment" has the meaning given in Clause 3.4.

   "FY2026" means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026.

   "Purchase Price" means the amount specified in Clause 2.1.

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $140,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

3. Payment Terms

3.1 Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to Closing.

3.2 The obligation to pay the Purchase Price shall be unconditional upon Closing, save as expressly provided herein.

3.3 The Buyer shall provide written confirmation of the bank transfer promptly after execution.

3.4 Earn-out: In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to three (3) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $6,500,000, provided that the Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within ninety (90) days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026 being approved by the board of the Company. For the avoidance of doubt, no Earn-out Payment shall be due unless EBITDA exceeds $6,500,000, and the excess shall be multiplied by three to determine the Earn-out Payment, subject to the $5,000,000 cap.

4. Conditions Precedent

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the following Conditions:

   (a) receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals;

   (b) accuracy of the representations and warranties as of the Closing Date, subject to materiality qualifications;

   (c) performance in all material respects of each Party’s covenants.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 30 May 2027), either Party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without liability, save for obligations under Clauses 8 and 9 which shall survive termination.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by prepaid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 113 Bishopsgate, London.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 48 Ludgate Hill, London.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

7. Entire Agreement

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

9. Limitations on Liability

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable for any claim unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $1,400,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For these purposes, the excess shall mean the aggregate amount of all valid claims less $1,400,000.
