**AGREEMENT FOR THE SUPPLY OF ENERGY SERVICES**  
DATED 15 January 2023  

BETWEEN:  

(1) KESTREL HOLDINGS LTD (the "Supplier"), incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15047904 and registered office at 8 Ludgate Hill, London; and  

(2) MERRION ENERGY PLC (the "Customer"), incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13901119 and registered office at 160 Gresham Street, London.  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Agreement" means this agreement for the supply of energy services, including all schedules and appendices;  
"change of control" means the acquisition, whether directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management or policies of a party, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise;  
"Commencement Date" means 1 February 2023;  
"Services" means the energy supply, monitoring, and reporting services to be provided by the Supplier to the Customer as described in Schedule A.  

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.  

**2. Supply of Services**  
2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A.  
2.2 The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to perform the Services with due care, skill, and diligence.  

**3. Term**  
3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.  
3.2 After the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party gives not less than ninety (90) days’ written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term.  

**4. Payment**  
4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees specified in Schedule B for the Services, payable in monthly arrears.  
4.2 All invoices shall be submitted by the Supplier on or before the fifth (5th) business day of each month and shall be paid by the Customer within thirty (30) days of receipt.  
4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of the Bank of England from time to time, accruing daily from the due date until the date of actual payment.  

**5. Confidentiality**  
5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, usage data, and technical specifications.  
5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall not apply to information which is publicly available or required to be disclosed by law.  

**6. Notices**  
6.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the receiving party.  
6.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, on the day of delivery; if by post, on the second business day following posting; if by email, on the day of transmission provided delivery confirmation is obtained.  

**7. Warranties**  
7.1 The Supplier warrants that the Services will be provided in accordance with generally accepted industry standards.  
7.2 The Customer warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations hereunder.  

**8. Termination**  
8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:  
(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice; or  
(b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation.  

**14. Miscellaneous**  
14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.  
14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days’ written notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the counterparty. For the purposes of this clause, the counterparty is Merrion Energy plc.  

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
15.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
