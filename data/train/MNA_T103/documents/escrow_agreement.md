ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 28 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Fenwick Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11287438, with its registered office at 146 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Whitlock Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13472089, with its registered office at 20 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 27 February 2026 pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Kestrel Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15047904, with its registered office at 8 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company"), on the terms and subject to the conditions set out therein (the "SPA").

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Buyer has agreed to deposit an amount equal to 10% of the total purchase price into an escrow account to secure, inter alia, the indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the escrow funds on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent stating in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim under the SPA, the amount claimed, and the provisions of the SPA under which the claim arises.

"Escrow Amount" means the aggregate amount of £12,500,000 deposited by the Buyer into the Escrow Account, representing 10% of the total purchase price under the SPA.

"Escrow Account" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of the Parties, account number GB29HBUK20169876543210.

"Material Customer Contract" means the supply agreement dated 15 January 2023 between the Company and Merrion Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13901119, with its registered office at 160 Gresham Street, London, as amended on 3 April 2024.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account with HSBC Bank plc in London.

2.2 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account within two (2) Business Days of the completion of the acquisition under the SPA.

3. Disbursement of Escrow Funds

3.1 Disbursements from the Escrow Account shall be made only upon receipt by the Escrow Agent of joint written instructions signed by the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise ordered by a final and binding arbitration award under Clause 12.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the addresses or email addresses set out in the SPA or as otherwise notified in writing.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce in accordance with its Rules of Arbitration, and such arbitration shall take place in New York, New York.
