# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 22 February 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Whitlock Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13472089, whose registered office is at 20 Moorgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Fenwick Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11287438, whose registered office is at 146 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Kestrel Holdings Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15047904, whose registered office is at 8 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and any schedules or annexes;

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 22 April 2026;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with Schedule 1;

"English law" means the law of England and Wales;

"excess" means, in relation to claims under Clause 9.4, the amount by which the aggregate of all claims exceeds the Basket;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000;

"Working Capital" shall have the meaning set out in Schedule 1.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

2.1 The Purchase Price is $70,000,000, payable in cash on the Closing Date in accordance with Clause 3.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be made in accordance with Clause 4.

**3. Completion**

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all such documents and instruments of transfer as are necessary to transfer to the Buyer full legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances.

**4. Working Capital Adjustment**

4.1 Within 90 days following the Closing Date, the Buyer shall prepare and deliver to the Seller a statement of the Closing Working Capital, together with reasonable supporting documentation.

4.2 The Seller shall have 30 days to review and respond to such statement. Any dispute shall be resolved in accordance with the independent accountant mechanism set out in Schedule 2.

**8. Warranties and Indemnities**

8.3 **Limitation of Liability**: The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $10,500,000. The Purchase Price is $70,000,000 (as defined in Section 2.1).

**9. Limitations on Claims**

9.4 **De Minimis and Basket**: The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer for any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate of all such claims exceeds $700,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

**16. Notices**

16.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the addresses set out above or such other address or email address as a party may notify in writing.

**17. Miscellaneous**

17.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings relating to its subject matter.

17.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this Agreement as a deed on the date first above written.
