# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] May 2026 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Whitlock Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15025980 whose registered office is at 34 King William Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Eskdale Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13485886 whose registered office is at 143 Fleet Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a licensed financial institution authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, acting in its capacity as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

“Closing Date” means 10 July 2026.

“Company” means Foxley Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14646479 whose registered office is at 119 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Confidential Information” means all information of a confidential or proprietary nature disclosed by or on behalf of any party in connection with this transaction, including financial, operational, commercial and technical data.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £1,450,000 (one million four hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), being the amount deposited by the Buyer into the Escrow Account pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] May 2026.

“Escrow Account” means the designated account held with the Escrow Agent into which the Escrow Amount has been deposited.

“Foxley Retail Group plc” means the material customer of the Company, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15644972 whose registered office is at 178 Gresham Street, London.

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company dated [●] May 2026 between the Seller and the Buyer.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

**2. Establishment of Escrow Account**

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account for the sole purpose of holding the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent as stakeholder and not as agent of either the Buyer or the Seller.

**3. Payment and Investment**

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account within two Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Amount in a UK-regulated interest-bearing account consistent with the Escrow Agent’s standard practices for funds held in escrow.

**4. Release of Escrow Amount**

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

4.2 For the avoidance of doubt, any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a valid claim properly notified by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller in accordance with the Share Purchase Agreement prior to the expiry of the 12-month period shall not be released until such claim is finally resolved or withdrawn.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 34 King William Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 143 Fleet Street, London.

5.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address or email specified in its appointment letter.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 Each party undertakes to the others that it shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

**7. General**

7.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
