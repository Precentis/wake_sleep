# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 11 April 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) Whitlock Marine Ltd (company number 15025980), incorporated in England and Wales and having its registered office at 34 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Eskdale Engineering Ltd (company number 13485886), incorporated in England and Wales and having its registered office at 143 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Foxley Software Ltd (company number 14646479), incorporated in England and Wales and having its registered office at 119 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the Shares on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

"Closing Date" means 10 July 2026.

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with UK Generally Accepted Accounting Principles consistently applied.

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

## 2. SALE AND PURCHASE

**2.1** The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $215,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

## 3. PAYMENT

**3.1** The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash at Closing by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3.2** Subject to Clause 3.4, the Purchase Price shall be adjusted at Closing for net debt and working capital in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1.

**3.4 (Earn-out)**  
The Buyer shall pay to the Seller, within ninety (90) days following the final audited financial statements for the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, an additional payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed $3,000,000.

## 4. CLOSING

**4.1** Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s legal advisers, or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

**4.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all documents and instruments necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

## 5. NOTICES

**5.1** Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, overnight courier, or email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in the preamble, or to such other address or email as a party may designate in writing.

**5.2** A notice delivered by hand or courier shall be deemed received on the day of delivery. A notice sent by email shall be deemed received on the next Business Day after transmission.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1** Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

## 9. INDEMNITIES

**9.4 (De Minimis and Basket)**  
The Seller shall not be liable for any individual claim or the aggregate of all claims unless the total amount of all such claims exceeds $1,075,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the amount by which such aggregate claims exceed $1,075,000 (the "excess").

## 12. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**12.1** The Seller covenants that, for a period of 5 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company as conducted on the Closing Date, within the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company operates.

## 13. GENERAL

**13.1** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings.

**13.2** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
