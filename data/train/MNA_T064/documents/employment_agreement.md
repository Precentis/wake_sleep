2. Employment

2.1 The Company hereby agrees to employ the Founder, and the Founder accepts employment, in the role of Chief Technology Officer, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 The Founder shall perform such duties as are commensurate with the position and as lawfully directed by the board of directors of the Company.

4. Remuneration

4.1 The Founder shall receive a base salary of £95,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme as determined by the board of the Company, subject to performance criteria.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Founder shall not at any time during or after the termination of employment, directly or indirectly, disclose, disseminate, or use any Confidential Information except as required in the course of employment or as authorised in writing by the Company or the Buyer.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company or, in the case of the Founder, to the last known address on file with the Company’s payroll department.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed served 48 hours after being sent by first-class post.

18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder acknowledges the legitimate business interest of the Company and the Buyer in protecting goodwill, customer relationships, and Confidential Information.

18.2 During the period of 12 months immediately following the Termination Date, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any person, firm, or company, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the Company or the Buyer in the United Kingdom.

18.3 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction shall include, but not be limited to, providing services to or soliciting orders from the Material Customer, Foxley Retail Group plc, or any entity controlled by it.

18.4 The parties agree that the duration, geographic scope, and nature of this restriction are reasonable and necessary for the protection of the Company’s and Buyer’s legitimate interests.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
