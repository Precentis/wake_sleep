**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT**  
**(ROTHLEY MEDICAL LTD ACQUISITION OF CORRIB TECHNOLOGIES LTD)**  

This employment agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on the 25th day of August 2026, between:  

(1) **Corrib Technologies Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 15224863, registered office at 12 Old Broad Street, London), the Company ("Employer"); and  
(2) **Dr. Elinor Grant**, of 32 Whitfield Street, London, W1T 2JH ("Employee").  

The Employer is a wholly owned subsidiary of **Rothley Medical Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 13939826, registered office at 47 Cheapside, London), which acquired the entire issued share capital of the Company from **Thornbury Retail Group plc** (registered in England and Wales under company number 10564833, registered office at 130 Fleet Street, London) effective as of 1 September 2026.  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Affiliate” means, in relation to any person, a subsidiary, holding company or subsidiary of a holding company of that person;  
“Business” means the research, development, manufacture and supply of diagnostic imaging software and associated digital health platforms;  
“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information, whether written, oral or electronic, relating to the Business, clients, suppliers, technology, processes, financial affairs or strategy of the Employer or any of its Affiliates;  
“Effective Date” means 1 September 2026;  
“Founder” refers to the Employee in her capacity as a founding director and key technical officer of the Employer;  
“Material Customer” means any customer who accounted for at least 5% of the Employer’s turnover in the 12 months preceding the termination of the Employee’s employment, including **Calloway Retail Group plc** (registered in England and Wales under company number 10324512, registered office at 174 Fleet Street, London).  

**2. Position and Duties**  
2.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Technology Officer of the Employer and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are reasonably assigned by the board of directors of Rothley Medical Ltd.  
2.2 The Employee shall devote her full working time and attention to the business of the Employer and shall not engage in any other employment or business activity without prior written consent.  

**3. Remuneration and Benefits**  
3.1 The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £125,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Rothley Medical Ltd Annual Incentive Plan, with a target bonus of 30% of annual base salary, subject to performance conditions determined by the board.  
3.3 The Employee shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays in England.  

**4. Termination**  
4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six months’ written notice.  
4.2 The Employer may terminate the Employee’s employment summarily for gross misconduct.  
4.3 Upon termination, the Employee shall be entitled to all accrued but unpaid salary, bonus (if declared), and unused holiday entitlement.  

**5. Confidentiality**  
5.1 The Employee shall not, at any time during or after her employment, disclose or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than in the proper performance of her duties.  
5.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive the termination of employment for a period of five years.  

**6. Intellectual Property**  
6.1 All intellectual property created or developed by the Employee during the course of her employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Employer.  
6.2 The Employee irrevocably assigns to the Employer all rights, title and interest in any such intellectual property.  

**7. Notices**  
7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Employer or to the Employee’s last known address.  
7.2 Notices shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting by first-class pre-paid post.  

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

**18. Non-Competition**  
18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as employee, consultant, director, shareholder, partner or otherwise, engage, be employed, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business anywhere in the United Kingdom for a period of 18 months after termination of employment.  
18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies regardless of whether the termination is with or without cause and includes any business providing diagnostic imaging software or digital health platforms to hospitals, clinics or Material Customers, including Calloway Retail Group plc.
