**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 22 August 2026  

**BETWEEN**  
(1) **Thornbury Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10564833 whose registered office is at 130 Fleet Street, London (the “**Seller**”); and  
(2) **Rothley Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13939826 whose registered office is at 47 Cheapside, London (the “**Buyer**”).  

**BACKGROUND**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Corrib Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15224863 whose registered office is at 12 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Company**”).  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.  

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including the Recitals;  
“**Closing**” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;  
“**Closing Date**” means 24 September 2026;  
“**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.1;  
“**Confidential Information**” has the meaning given in Clause 9.1;  
“**General Warranties**” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;  
“**Fundamental Warranties**” means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as set out in Schedule 2;  
“**Purchase Price**” means the aggregate amount payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;  
“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

**2. Purchase and Sale**  
2.1 The Purchase Price is **$210,000,000** (two hundred and ten million US dollars), payable in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3.00 p.m. on the Closing Date.  

**3. Closing**  
3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.  
3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:  
(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;  
(b) the share certificates for the Shares;  
(c) resignations of the current directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of Closing; and  
(d) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares.  

**4. Conditions**  
4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or, where permitted, waiver in writing by the Buyer of the following Conditions:  
(a) no order, injunction or decree issued by any court or governmental authority shall be in effect which prohibits the Closing;  
(b) all necessary consents from third parties, including material customers, shall have been obtained;  
(c) the representations and warranties of each party contained herein shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date.  
4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 21 November 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.  

**10. Warranties and Claims**  
10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months**. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months**. Any claim under this Clause must be notified in writing to the party in breach prior to the expiry of the relevant survival period, failing which such claim shall be extinguished.  

**12. Non-Compete**  
12.1 The Seller undertakes that for a period of **4 years** following the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the business of the Company as conducted on the Closing Date.  

**9. Confidentiality**  
9.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it will keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement (“**Confidential Information**”) and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.  

**13. General**  
13.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.  
13.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
