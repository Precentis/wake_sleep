## 1. Call to Order

## 3. Confirmation of Previous Minutes  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 14 July 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously that the minutes be confirmed as a true and accurate record.

## 4. Update on Proposed Acquisition by Rothley Medical Ltd  
The board received an update on the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Technologies Ltd by Rothley Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13939826, registered office 47 Cheapside, London) from Thornbury Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10564833, registered office 130 Fleet Street, London). Legal and financial due diligence was substantially complete. The board noted that the draft share purchase agreement was under review by external legal counsel. All directors were instructed to ensure timely responses to any outstanding information requests. The transaction remains conditional upon shareholder approval and compliance with regulatory requirements under English law. The board expressed confidence that completion would occur in accordance with the current timeline.

## 5. Litigation Risk – Northgate Robotics Ltd  
The board noted a letter before action received from Northgate Robotics Ltd, dated 25 August 2026, threatening proceedings for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under contract reference NRT-7742-2025. The letter asserts that certain shipments delivered between October 2025 and January 2026 failed to meet agreed specifications. Legal counsel has been engaged to assess the merits of the claim. The board directed management to monitor developments closely, preserve all relevant documentation, and report biweekly on the status of correspondence and potential exposure. No provision has been recorded in the unaudited financial statements as of 30 June 2026 in respect of this matter.

## 6. Material Customer Relationship – Calloway Retail Group plc  
The board reviewed the current trading relationship with Calloway Retail Group plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10324512, registered office 174 Fleet Street, London), which accounted for 32% of the Company’s total revenue in the 12-month period ended 30 June 2026. A quarterly performance review was scheduled for 5 September 2026. The board emphasised the importance of maintaining service levels and compliance with contractual delivery timelines.

## 7. Any Other Business  
No other matters were raised.
