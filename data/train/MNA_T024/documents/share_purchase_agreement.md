SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 24 April 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Kestrel Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13138687, whose registered office is at 27 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Juniper Software Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10349503, whose registered office is at 22 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

in respect of the entire issued share capital of:

Lowther Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11213711, whose registered office is at 55 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”).

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Closing Date” means 31 July 2026;

“Closing Working Capital” means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles and methodologies set out in Schedule 1;

“Conditions” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company, as set out in Clause 2.1;

“Target Working Capital” means $3,500,000.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $255,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and applied in accordance with Schedule 1 and paid by the Buyer to the Seller or by the Seller to the Buyer, as applicable, within 15 Business Days following the final determination of Closing Working Capital.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the “Closing”) shall take place remotely via electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of all shares in the Company; (b) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in forms acceptable to the Buyer; and (c) such corporate authorisations and consents as the Buyer may reasonably require.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or, where permitted, waiver in writing by both Parties, of the following Conditions:

(a) receipt of all necessary regulatory and third-party consents, including but not limited to any required under competition law;

(b) no injunction or restraining order having been issued by any court of competent jurisdiction which prohibits the Closing;

(c) the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the other Party in all material respects as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (being 23 August 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other Party.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it will not, and will procure that its affiliates, officers, employees and advisers will not, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the other Party, the Company or the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

6.2 Notices to the Seller shall be addressed to: 27 Moorgate, London, or to such other address or email as the Seller may notify to the Buyer in accordance with this Clause.

6.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be addressed to: 22 Cheapside, London, or to such other address or email as the Buyer may notify to the Seller in accordance with this Clause.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

7.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
