ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [date prior to 14 May 2026] between:

(1) Kestrel Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13138687, whose registered office is at 27 Moorgate, London ("Seller");

(2) Juniper Software Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10349503, whose registered office is at 22 Cheapside, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Background

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated [prior to 14 May 2026] (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Lowther Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11213711, whose registered office is at 55 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) As part of the SPA, the parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") shall be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent for the purposes of satisfying any valid claims made by the Buyer under the SPA in respect of breaches of warranties, indemnities or other obligations.

(C) The parties now wish to enter into this Agreement to regulate the holding and release of the Escrow Amount.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 31 July 2026.

"Claim" means any bona fide claim notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of the SPA, asserting a right to compensation or indemnity from the Seller under the SPA.

"Confidential Information" means all information of a proprietary or confidential nature disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to financial, commercial, operational and technical information.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of [specified amount] held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act as escrow agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days following the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in a designated interest-bearing client account at a UK-regulated financial institution.

3.3 The Escrow Agent shall not be responsible for any loss of value arising from market fluctuations or default by the financial institution, provided that it has used reasonable care in selecting the institution.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such part thereof as has not been the subject of a pending Claim notified in accordance with the SPA prior to the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, shall be released to the Seller on that date.

4.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim as at the expiry of the 12-month period referred to in Clause 4.1 shall remain held by the Escrow Agent until final resolution of such Claim, at which point it shall be released in accordance with the outcome of such resolution.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 22 Cheapside, London.

5.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 27 Moorgate, London.

5.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to its principal office as notified to the parties.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the others and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
