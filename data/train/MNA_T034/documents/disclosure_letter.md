**3. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**

The Company’s core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Holloway Energy plc. At the time of development, the Company engaged Holloway Energy plc on a time-and-materials basis to design, code and deliver the foundational version of the software platform used in the Company’s principal operations.

No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Holloway Energy plc. The development work was carried out pursuant to an informal email agreement and verbal understanding, and no formal contract or intellectual property clause was executed. As a result, no documentation exists transferring ownership of the source code, underlying algorithms, or related intellectual property rights from Holloway Energy plc to the Company.

The Company has continued to maintain, update, and develop the software internally since 2023, and all subsequent versions and derivative works have been created by employees of the Company. However, the initial foundational codebase remains attributable to Holloway Energy plc, and no formal title transfer has been obtained.

This disclosure is made in relation to Warranty 8.2 of the SPA concerning the ownership, validity and non-infringement of intellectual property rights of the Company.
