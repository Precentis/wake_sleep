# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  

This Agreement is made on **16 July 2026**  

**BETWEEN**  

(1) **Juniper Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15562359**, whose registered office is at 162 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and  

(2) **Northgate Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15194060**, whose registered office is at 35 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").  

**BACKGROUND**  

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Thornbury Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10683119**, whose registered office is at 120 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").  

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company upon the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.  

**AGREED TERMS**  

## 1. INTERPRETATION  

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  

"**Closing Date**" means **11 September 2026**;  

"**Material Adverse Change**" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;  

"**Target Working Capital**" means **$3,500,000**;  

and capitalised terms not otherwise defined in this Clause 1 shall have the meanings given elsewhere in this Agreement.  

## 2. SALE AND PURCHASE  

**2.1** The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the Closing Date, for a total consideration of **$25,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").  

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be satisfied in cash by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.  

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$3,500,000**. Any such reduction shall be calculated and verified in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1.  

## 3. CLOSING  

**3.1** Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date.  

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:  
(a) share certificates in respect of the entire issued share capital of the Company, duly endorsed in favour of the Buyer;  
(b) a copy of a board resolution of the Company approving the transfer of shares and updating its register of members; and  
(c) such other documents as are reasonably required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title to the Shares.  

## 9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES  

**9.7** The Buyer must notify the Seller of any claim under the warranties contained in this Agreement in writing within **20 days** of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim. Any claim not so notified shall be time-barred and unenforceable.  

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY  

**10.1** Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, at any time after the date of this Agreement, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the terms of this transaction, the Company, or its business, except:  
(a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;  
(b) to its professional advisers on a confidential basis; or  
(c) with the prior written consent of the other Party.  

## 11. NOTICES  

**11.1** Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.  

**11.2** Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 162 Ludgate Hill, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 35 Ludgate Hill, London.  

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION  

**12.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.  

**12.2** The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
