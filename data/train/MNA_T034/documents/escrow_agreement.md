1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”), unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Northgate Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15194060, whose registered office is at 35 Ludgate Hill, London;

“Closing Date” means 11 September 2026;

“Company” means Thornbury Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10683119, whose registered office is at 120 Fleet Street, London;

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £3,250,000 (three million two hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing a portion of the total purchase price for the entire issued share capital of the Company, to be held in escrow pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 31 July 2026 between the Seller and the Buyer;

“Escrow Agent” means the third-party financial institution appointed by mutual agreement of the Buyer and the Seller to hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement;

“Seller” means Juniper Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15562359, whose registered office is at 162 Ludgate Hill, London;

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company entered into between the Seller and the Buyer.

1.2 Any reference to a statute, statutory provision or subordinate legislation shall be construed as a reference to the same as amended or re-enacted from time to time.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent within two Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account with a recognised financial institution operating in England and any interest accruing thereon shall form part of the Escrow Amount.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion as remains after deduction of any claims properly notified and outstanding, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, being 11 September 2027, save to the extent that a claim has been notified in accordance with Clause 5.2 prior to that date. For the purposes of this Agreement, “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London.

4.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending claim as of the release date under Clause 4.1 shall be retained by the Escrow Agent until such claim is resolved by written agreement of the Buyer and the Seller or by final judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount in respect of any breach of warranty, indemnity or other obligation under the Share Purchase Agreement for which the Seller is liable.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

5.3 Any claim notice must specify in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the amount claimed, and the basis under the Share Purchase Agreement for such claim.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Clause 1.1, or to such other address as may be notified in writing by that party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed served if delivered personally on the next Business Day, or if sent by pre-paid first-class post, 72 hours after posting.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or court order.

7.2 This obligation shall not apply to information which is in or enters the public domain (other than by breach of this clause).

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
