1. **Call to Order**  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m.

2. **Apologies and Absences**  
Apologies were received from Mr. J. Hartley, who was unable to attend due to pre-existing professional commitments.

3. **Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 4 November 2026 were reviewed. It was resolved unanimously to confirm and approve the minutes as a true and accurate record.

4. **Update on Share Sale Transaction**  
The board received an update regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Media Ltd by Galloway Robotics Ltd from Corrib Foods Ltd. The transaction remains on track for completion by the deliverable date context of 7 December 2026. All due diligence materials have been provided to the buyer’s legal advisors. The board confirmed that the company number 13363178 is correct and that the registered office at 83 Fleet Street, London, remains the official address for service of notices.

5. **Outstanding Legal Matter**  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action from Bellhaven Pharma plc, dated 28 November 2026, threatening legal proceedings against Ingleby Media Ltd for approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The claim relates to a contract for digital media services provided between April 2025 and September 2025. Management was directed to monitor developments closely and report any formal notice of proceedings to the board within 48 hours. The board affirmed that no admission of liability has been made and instructed legal counsel to prepare a preliminary assessment of defences under English law.

6. **Material Customer Relationship**  
The board reviewed the ongoing commercial relationship with Northgate Technologies Ltd (company number 12909256), a material customer of the company. The current service agreement, executed on 15 March 2025, remains in full force and continues to generate approximately 28% of the company’s monthly revenue. The registered office of Northgate Technologies Ltd at 22 Gresham Street, London, was confirmed as the correct service address. No contractual disputes are currently outstanding.

7. **Financial Position and Liquidity**  
The CFO presented unaudited financial statements for the month ending 30 November 2026. The company remains within its forecasted EBITDA range. Cash reserves are sufficient to meet all known obligations, including the potential claim referenced in Item 5, without requiring external financing prior to completion of the acquisition.

8. **Any Other Business**  
There was no other business to discuss.

9. **Adjournment**  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:25 a.m.
