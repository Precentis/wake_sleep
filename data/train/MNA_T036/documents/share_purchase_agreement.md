# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**This Agreement** is dated 29 November 2026

**PARTIES**

(1) **Corrib Foods Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14838258, whose registered office is at 68 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Galloway Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14115615, whose registered office is at 6 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The "Company" means **Ingleby Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13363178, whose registered office is at 83 Fleet Street, London.

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1** In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

**1.2** Terms defined in the recitals have the same meaning unless otherwise defined in this clause.

**1.3** Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as including any statutory modification or re-enactment of it, whether before or after the date of this Agreement.

## 2. SALE AND PURCHASE

**2.1** The Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$85,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be satisfied in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

## 3. CLOSING

**3.1** Completion of the sale and purchase referred to in Clause 2 shall take place on **8 February 2027** (the "Closing Date"), or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:
(a) share certificates representing all shares in the Company;
(b) stock transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer;
(c) resignations of directors and secretaries of the Company, if required;
(d) such other documents as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial ownership of the shares.

**3.3** At Closing, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

## 4. CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

**4.1** The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction of the following conditions precedent:
(a) no Material Adverse Change having occurred in relation to the Company;
(b) receipt of all necessary regulatory and third-party consents;
(c) confirmation that all representations and warranties contained in this Agreement are true and accurate.

**4.5** If the Conditions referred to in Clause 4.1 are not satisfied or waived by the date falling **150 days after the date of this Agreement** (being 29 November 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, without liability to the terminating party, provided that such right to terminate shall not be available to any party in breach of its obligations hereunder.

## 9. WARRANTIES AND CLAIMS

**9.1** The Seller warrants to the Buyer that, as at the date of this Agreement, the Company:
(a) is not in breach of any material contract;
(b) has not received notice of termination from any material customer, including **Northgate Technologies Ltd** (company number 12909256);
(c) has no undisclosed liabilities.

**9.7** Any claim by the Buyer arising from a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within **30 days** of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim, specifying in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the warranty breached and, where practicable, the amount claimed.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

**10.1** Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction, and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

**10.2** This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

## 11. NOTICES

**11.1** Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

**11.2** Notices shall be deemed effective when delivered or, if posted, 48 hours after posting.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**12.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with **English law**.

**12.2** The courts of **England** shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
