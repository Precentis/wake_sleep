ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 5 December 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Galloway Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14115615, whose registered office is at 6 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Corrib Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14838258, whose registered office is at 68 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a limited liability company registered in England and Wales, whose registered office is at [Address of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

A. The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 4 December 2026 (the "SPA") with the Seller pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13363178, whose registered office is at 83 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

B. As part of the consideration under the SPA, an amount of £1,250,000 (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure certain indemnities and warranties given by the Seller.

C. The Parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Amount shall be held, administered and released by the Escrow Agent.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1. In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

   "Claim" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a liability of the Seller under the SPA in respect of which payment from the Escrow Amount is sought.

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,250,000 deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3.

   "Indemnity Period" means the period ending on 31 March 2027.

   "Northgate Claim" means any third-party claim arising from or in connection with the agreement between the Company and Northgate Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12909256, whose registered office is at 22 Gresham Street, London, dated 15 June 2025.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1. The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated account (the "Escrow Account") in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit

3.1. The Buyer shall cause the Escrow Amount to be deposited into the Escrow Account within two (2) Business Days of the Effective Date.

3.2. The Escrow Agent shall confirm in writing to the Parties the receipt of the Escrow Amount within one (1) Business Day of such receipt.

4. Payment and Release

4.1. The Escrow Amount, or any part thereof, shall be released only in accordance with the written joint instructions of the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise directed by a final judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.2. In the absence of joint instructions, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Amount, or the relevant portion, within five (5) Business Days of receipt of a final determination by the Parties or a court.

5. Claims

5.1. The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount only during the Indemnity Period.

5.2. A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3. Any Claim shall be made by delivering a written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the Claim and the amount claimed, which shall not exceed 75% of the Escrow Amount.

6. Notices

6.1. Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

7. Confidentiality

7.1. The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement unless disclosure is required by law or by a regulatory authority.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1. This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
