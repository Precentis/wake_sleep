SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 26 May 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Oakhurst Holdings Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 12335758), whose registered office is at 66 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Saltash Software Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 13684667), whose registered office is at 143 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Ashcombe Technologies Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 14966851), whose registered office is at 30 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all the issued shares in the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 4 August 2026;  
"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles applied in the preparation of the latest audited accounts of the Company;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;  
"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under Clause 2.1;  
"Shares" means all the issued shares in the capital of the Company;  
"Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000.

1.2 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Clause 1 have the meanings given to them elsewhere in this Agreement.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $100,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such adjustment shall be determined in good faith by the Buyer and notified to the Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, and shall be final and binding unless the Seller provides written notice of dispute within thirty (30) days of such notification.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion of the sale and purchase shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee;

(b) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

(c) certified copies of all corporate authorisations and resolutions of the Seller authorising the execution and performance of this Agreement;

(d) such other documents as may be reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares free from all Encumbrances.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party unless compelled by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

6.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
