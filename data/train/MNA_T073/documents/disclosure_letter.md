**DISCLOSURE LETTER**

1. **Interpretation**

1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires, capitalised terms have the meanings given to them in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 22 June 2026 (the "SPA") between Oakhurst Holdings Ltd (the "Seller"), Saltash Software Ltd (the "Buyer") and Ashcombe Technologies Ltd (the "Company").

1.2 The following terms have the meanings set out below:

- "Warranty 8.2 (IP)" means the warranty contained in clause 8.2 of the SPA relating to the intellectual property rights of the Company.

2. **Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (IP)**

2.1 The Company’s core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Calloway Robotics Ltd. The development work was carried out on a time-and-materials basis, without a written agreement providing for the assignment of intellectual property rights from Calloway Robotics Ltd to the Company. No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into. The Company has used the software in the ordinary course of business since its completion and has asserted ownership of the software in commercial contracts with third parties, including Holloway Energy plc, which has been licensed to use the software under a non-exclusive, perpetual licence granted on 15 April 2024.

2.2 The software in question forms the technical foundation of the Company’s service offering and is deployed in live environments for key customers. While the Company has treated the software as its own proprietary asset, legal title may remain with Calloway Robotics Ltd in the absence of an express assignment.
