# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT** is made on 4 June 2027  

**BETWEEN:**  

(1) **Kingsmere Retail Group plc**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15420110, whose registered office is at 160 Cheapside, London (the “**Seller**”); and  

(2) **Redcliffe Media Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12901211, whose registered office is at 97 Cannon Street, London (the “**Buyer**”).  

**WHEREAS** the Buyer has agreed to acquire and the Seller has agreed to sell the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15624637, whose registered office is at 75 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Company**”), on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.  

**NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED** as follows:  

---

**1. INTERPRETATION**  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;  
“**Buyer**” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares;  
“**Closing Date**” means 12 July 2027;  
“**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3;  
“**Company**” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“**English law**” means the law of England and Wales;  
“**Purchase Price**” means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;  
“**Seller**” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“**Shares**” means all of the issued and outstanding shares in the capital of the Company, representing 100% of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

1.2 Capitalised terms not otherwise defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the recitals or schedules.  

---

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**  

2.1 The Purchase Price is **$75,000,000** (seventy-five million US dollars). The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.  

2.2 Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions, the sale and purchase of the Shares shall take place on the Closing Date, at which time:  
(a) the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer such documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares to the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances; and  
(b) the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.  

---

**3. CONDITIONS**  

3.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the fulfilment of each of the following conditions (the “**Conditions**”) on or before the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement:  
(a) the receipt of all necessary corporate, regulatory and third-party consents and approvals required in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement;  
(b) the accuracy of the representations and warranties of the other party set out in this Agreement as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date, in all material respects; and  
(c) the performance in all material respects by the other party of its obligations under this Agreement required to be performed prior to Closing.  

---

**4. TERMINATION**  

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived in writing by both parties by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (which is 23 October 2027), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other party. Upon such termination, no party shall have any further rights or obligations under this Agreement, except for provisions which expressly survive termination.  

---

---

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**  

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential and shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose to any third party any information concerning the terms of this Agreement, the negotiations leading to it, or any business, financial or technical information of the other party or the Company, unless:  
(a) such disclosure is required by law or regulation; or  
(b) the disclosing party has obtained the prior written consent of the other party.  

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three years.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**  

15.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with **English law**. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
