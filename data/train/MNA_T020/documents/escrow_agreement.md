# ESCROW AGREEMENT  
*(Extract)*

This Escrow Agreement (the “**Agreement**”) is made on 9 June 2027 (the “**Effective Date**”) between:

(1) **Redcliffe Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12901211, whose registered office is at 97 Cannon Street, London (the “**Buyer**”);

(2) **Kingsmere Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15420110, whose registered office is at 160 Cheapside, London (the “**Seller**”); and

(3) **HSBC Escrow Services Limited**, a company authorised to provide escrow services, acting as escrow agent (the “**Escrow Agent**”).

**RECITALS**

A. The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 9 June 2027 (the “**SPA**”) pursuant to which the Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to acquire, the entire issued share capital of **Ingleby Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15624637, whose registered office is at 75 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Company**”).

B. In connection with the SPA, the parties have agreed to deposit certain funds into escrow to secure the obligations of the Seller under clause 8.2 of the SPA, which provides for the indemnification of the Buyer in respect of any breach of warranty or liability arising from the Company’s commercial relationship with **Eskdale Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10911332, whose registered office is at 96 King William Street, London.

C. The parties have instructed the Escrow Agent to hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount (as defined below) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Business Day**” means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London and New York;

“**Claim Notice**” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller, setting out in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount, supported by documentation evidencing the claim;

“**Disbursement Date**” means the date on which the Escrow Agent disburses any portion of the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement;

“**Escrow Amount**” means the sum of £1,750,000 (one million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 7.5% of the total purchase price under the SPA, deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the Effective Date;

“**Escrow Period**” means the period of 18 months commencing on the Completion Date (as defined in the SPA), which commenced on 10 June 2027.

**2. Deposit and Holding of Funds**

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent on the Effective Date. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the “**Escrow Account**”) at a branch of HSBC Bank plc in New York, United States, designated for such purpose.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall not invest the Escrow Amount unless otherwise instructed in writing by both the Buyer and the Seller, acting jointly.

**3. Disbursement Mechanism**

3.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be disbursed by the Escrow Agent in accordance with written joint instructions from the Buyer and the Seller, or as directed by a final and binding arbitration award under clause 12.

3.2 In the event a Claim Notice is served during the Escrow Period, the Escrow Agent shall notify the Seller and, unless the Seller disputes the claim within 15 Business Days, disburse the amount specified in the Claim Notice.

**4. Notices**

4.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class mail, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both parties.

**12. Governing Law and Arbitration**

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one or more arbitrators appointed in accordance with said rules, and such arbitration shall take place in New York, United States.
