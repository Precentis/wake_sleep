**Disclosure Letter dated 8 June 2027**  
**From: Kingsmere Retail Group plc**  
**To: Redcliffe Media Ltd**  
**Re: Disclosure in connection with the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Energy plc**

1. **Interpretation**  
1.1 In this letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Buyer” means Redcliffe Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12901211 and registered office at 97 Cannon Street, London;  
(b) “Seller” means Kingsmere Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15420110 and registered office at 160 Cheapside, London;  
(c) “Company” means Ingleby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15624637 and registered office at 75 Old Broad Street, London;  
(d) “Warranties” means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in clause 8 of the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 10 June 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller (the “SPA”);  
(e) “Disclosed Matter” means any matter, fact, circumstance or event disclosed in this letter;  
(f) capitalised terms not otherwise defined have the meanings ascribed in the SPA.

2. **Purpose and Scope**  
2.1 This letter is delivered pursuant to clause 8.7 of the SPA and serves as the full and only disclosure by the Seller against the Warranties.  
2.2 The existence of a Disclosed Matter in this letter qualifies the relevant Warranty(ies) only to the extent that such disclosure specifically identifies the Warranty(ies) affected and provides sufficient detail to enable the Buyer to assess the nature and scope of the matter.  
2.3 The disclosure of any matter in this letter does not constitute an admission by the Seller that such matter gives rise to a liability, nor that it is material for the purposes of the SPA.

3. **Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**  
3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 8.2 (which relates to the ownership and title to intellectual property of the Company), the Seller discloses that:  
(a) the Company’s core platform software, which forms a central component of its operational systems, was developed in 2023 by Lowther Foods Ltd, an independent contractor;  
(b) the development work undertaken by Lowther Foods Ltd was conducted without a written agreement assigning intellectual property rights in the software to the Company;  
(c) No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between the Company and Lowther Foods Ltd in connection with the development of the core platform software;  
(d) the Company has, since 2023, continued to operate, maintain and use the software in the ordinary course of its business on the basis of an implied licence, though formal title to the source code and underlying intellectual property rights has not been formally transferred in writing;  
(e) no claim has been made by Lowther Foods Ltd asserting ownership or demanding compensation in respect of the software, nor is the Seller aware of any intention by Lowther Foods Ltd to do so.  
3.2 The absence of a written assignment of intellectual property rights constitutes a full and specific disclosure against Warranty 8.2. The Buyer acknowledges receipt of this disclosure and accepts that the matter described herein falls within the scope of permitted disclosures under the SPA.  
3.3 The Seller confirms that the development of the core platform software by Lowther Foods Ltd, and the absence of any written assignment of intellectual property rights, is fully documented in the Company’s internal records and has been made available during the data room due diligence process.  
3.4 The Buyer is deemed to have accepted that the continued use of the software by the Company is not currently impeded by the lack of formal assignment, and No written assignment was ever executed between the parties.
