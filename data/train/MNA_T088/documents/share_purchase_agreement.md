SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 18 July 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Merrion Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10021209 whose registered office is at 108 Cheapside, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Vantage Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11994893 whose registered office is at 175 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”);

and

(3) Ingleby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12243348 whose registered office is at 94 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Company”).

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and any schedules or annexes;

“Closing” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“Closing Date” means 22 October 2026;

“Confidential Information” means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this transaction, whether orally, electronically or in writing;

“excess” means the amount by which any claim or aggregate of claims exceeds the Basket;

“General Warranties” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax;

“Liability Cap” means an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $15,600,000;

“Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $130,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office or a nominated email address of the relevant party.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed received at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or upon confirmation of receipt if sent by email.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party undertakes to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

6. CLOSING

6.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 The Seller hereby gives the warranties set out in Schedule 2 to the Buyer.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 The Seller’s liability in respect of any claim under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall be limited as set out in this Clause 8.

8.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims in respect of the General Warranties and any other warranty claims (other than fraud) shall not exceed the Liability Cap, being $15,600,000. The Purchase Price is $130,000,000 (see Clause 2.1).

9. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

9.1 A claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds $1,300,000 (1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.

10. SURVIVAL

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive for a period of 72 months. All claims must be notified in writing before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. NON-COMPETE

11.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on or be engaged in any business which competes with the business of the Company as conducted on the Closing Date.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
