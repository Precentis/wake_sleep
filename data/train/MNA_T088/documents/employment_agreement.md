1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Business” means the research, development, manufacture, marketing and sale of plant-based nutritional products and functional food ingredients using proprietary biotechnological processes, as conducted by the Company immediately prior to the date of this Agreement.

“Company” means Ingleby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12243348, whose registered office is at 94 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information, whether written or oral, relating to the business, products, trade secrets, know-how, customer relationships, suppliers, pricing policies, financial data, research projects, technical specifications or business strategies of the Company or any member of the Vantage Group.

“Effective Date” means 1 June 2026.

“Founder” means Dr. Eleanor Hart, employed by the Company in the role of Chief Scientific Officer.

“Group” means, in relation to a person, any subsidiary or holding company of that person, and any subsidiary of any holding company of that person, from time to time.

“Vantage Group” means Vantage Foods Ltd and each of its subsidiaries and associated undertakings, including the Company following completion of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer from the Seller.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, commencing on the Effective Date.

2.2 The Founder’s primary place of work shall be the Company’s registered office, 94 Queen Victoria Street, London, or such other location as may be agreed in writing by the parties.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £98,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrear on the last working day of each calendar month.

3.2 In addition to the base salary, the Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme established by the Board of the Company, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme as may be in effect from time to time.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Founder shall not, at any time during or after the term of employment, without the prior written consent of the Company, directly or indirectly use, disclose or permit the use or disclosure of any Confidential Information.

4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall continue indefinitely after termination of employment, save that they shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Founder.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or transmitted by email to the address or email address set out below:

(a) in the case of the Founder: the most recent personal address or email address held on file by the Company; and  
(b) in the case of the Company: 94 Queen Victoria Street, London, or such other address as the Company may notify to the Founder.

5.2 A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed to have been received at the time of delivery; if sent by post, 48 hours after posting; and if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

18. Non-competition

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by reason of her role, access to Confidential Information, and close involvement with the key customer relationships of the Company, including Bellhaven Medical Ltd (company number 15177437), the restriction contained in this Clause 18 is reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Company and the Vantage Group.

18.2 During the period of 18 months immediately following the termination of the Founder’s employment with the Company, for any reason, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person, firm or company, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business anywhere in the United Kingdom.

18.3 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies irrespective of whether the Founder is employed, self-employed, working as a consultant or providing services in any other capacity.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

19.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
