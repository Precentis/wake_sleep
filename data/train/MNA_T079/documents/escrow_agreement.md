ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 August 2026 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Upton Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13022058, whose registered office is at 21 Gresham Street, London ("Buyer");

(2) Whitlock Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13643219, whose registered office is at 81 Cannon Street, London ("Seller"); and

(3) Smithson Trust Corporation Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 04567812, whose registered office is at 50 Fenchurch Street, London ("Escrow Agent").

RECITALS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 10 August 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Oakhurst Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13262800, whose registered office is at 138 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) In accordance with the terms of the SPA, an amount equal to 25% of the Total Purchase Price (as defined in the SPA) (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure certain indemnities and warranties given by the Seller.

(C) The parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Claim Notice" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount;

   "Closing Date" means 17 August 2026;

   "Effective Date" has the meaning given in the preamble;

   "Escrow Amount" has the meaning given in Recital (B);

   "SPA" has the meaning given in Recital (A);

   "Total Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under the SPA.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") at a recognised financial institution in London.

4. Payment

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Account only upon receipt of:

   (a) joint written instructions from the Buyer and the Seller; or

   (b) a final determination by a court of competent jurisdiction directing such release.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by serving a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the address or email address set out below:

   To the Buyer:

   Email: legal@uptonholdings.co.uk  

   To the Seller:  
   At 81 Cannon Street, London  
   Email: compliance@whitlockindustries.co.uk  

   To the Escrow Agent:  
   At 50 Fenchurch Street, London  
   Email: escrow@smithsontrust.co.uk  

6.2 Notices shall be deemed served in accordance with standard rules of deemed service under English law.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from another party in connection with this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or by any regulatory authority.

8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
