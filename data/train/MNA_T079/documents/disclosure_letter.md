Disclosure Letter dated 15 August 2026  
Between:  
(1) Whitlock Industries Ltd (company number 13643219), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 81 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

**1. Interpretation**  
1.1 In this letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Company" means Oakhurst Analytics Ltd (company number 13262800), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 138 Cannon Street, London;  
(b) "Disclosed" means fairly and accurately disclosed in this letter with sufficient detail to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed;  
(c) "Warranty" means any warranty, representation or statement given by the Seller in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 15 August 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller (the "SPA");  
(d) "Orwell Analytics" means Orwell Analytics Ltd (company number 10563974), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 17 King William Street, London.  
1.2 Capitalised terms not otherwise defined herein have the meanings ascribed to them in the SPA.

**2. Purpose**  
2.1 This letter is delivered pursuant to clause 6.1 of the SPA and is intended to qualify the Warranties contained therein.  
2.2 Disclosure in this letter shall operate as a full disclosure against any claim arising under the Warranties to the extent that the matter disclosed is directly relevant and clearly described.

**3. Reliance**  
3.1 The Buyer acknowledges that it has not relied on any information or statements not expressly set out in this letter or the SPA in deciding to enter into the transaction.  
3.2 No disclosure in this letter shall be construed as an admission of liability, fault or breach of duty on the part of the Seller or the Company.

**4. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**  
4.1 For the purposes of Warranty 8.2 in the SPA, the Seller discloses that the Company's core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Whitlock Energy plc.  
4.2 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Whitlock Energy plc at the time of development.  
4.3 The Company has used the core platform software continuously since 2023 in the ordinary course of its business and has granted no third-party licences to such software, except to Orwell Analytics pursuant to a software usage agreement dated 12 March 2024.  
4.4 The Seller confirms that, to the best of its knowledge, no claim has been made by Whitlock Energy plc asserting ownership or any other right over the core platform software.

**5. Payments and Invoicing**  
5.1 All payments due under the SPA shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) to the bank account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least three (3) Business Days prior to the relevant payment date.  
5.2 Time for payment shall be of the essence in respect of all sums payable under the SPA.

**6. Notices**  
6.1 Any notice to be given under this letter shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in clause 1.  
6.2 A notice shall be deemed received:  
(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, at 9.00 a.m. on the second Business Day following posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at 9.00 a.m. on the next Business Day following transmission, provided confirmation of transmission is generated by the sender’s email system.  
6.3 Any party may change its contact details for notice by giving written notice to the other in accordance with this clause.

**7. Confidentiality**  
7.1 Each party shall keep confidential this letter and all information disclosed under it, except as required by law or any regulatory authority.  
7.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination or completion of the SPA for a period of five (5) years.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
8.1 This letter and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this letter.
