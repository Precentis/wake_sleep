SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 9 August 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Whitlock Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13643219, whose registered office is at 81 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Upton Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13022058, whose registered office is at 21 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

and

(3) Oakhurst Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13262800, whose registered office is at 138 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and any schedules;

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 7 October 2026;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with Schedule 1;

"Parties" means collectively the Buyer, the Seller and the Company, and "Party" means any one of them;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $2,000,000.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $150,000,000.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be satisfied by the Buyer paying to the Seller the amount in cleared funds on the Closing Date to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with Schedule 1.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all instruments of transfer, share certificates and such other documents as may be necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares to the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any claim under a warranty within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim, failing which the claim shall be barred.

12. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

12.1 The Seller covenants that for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date, within the United Kingdom.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party undertakes to the other Parties that it shall not at any time after the date of this Agreement disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify to the others.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
