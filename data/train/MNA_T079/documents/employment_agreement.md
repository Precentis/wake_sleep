**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 12 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Oakhurst Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13262800, whose registered office is at 138 Cannon Street, London ("the Company"); and

(2) Mr. Julian Hartley, of 45 Aldersgate Street, London, EC1A 1BB (the "Employee").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business" means the business of providing predictive data analytics services to financial institutions, as carried out by the Company on the date of this Agreement;

"Confidential Information" means all trade secrets, technical, commercial, operational, financial, or customer-related information disclosed to or otherwise known by the Employee during the course of employment;

"Founder" means Mr. Julian Hartley in his capacity as founder and Chief Technology Officer of the Company;

"Territory" means the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**2. Commencement and Term**

2.1 The Employee’s employment with the Company shall commence on 1 July 2026.

2.2 This is a permanent contract of employment, terminable by either party in accordance with the notice provisions herein.

**3. Position and Duties**

3.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Technology Officer of the Company.

3.2 The Employee shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as are normally associated with such position, and as may be lawfully and reasonably assigned by the Board of Directors of the Company from time to time.

**4. Remuneration**

4.1 The Employee shall receive a base salary of £115,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears.

4.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company’s annual bonus scheme, with a target bonus of 25% of annual salary, subject to achievement of individual and corporate performance objectives as determined by the Board.

**5. Expenses**

5.1 The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable and properly documented out-of-pocket expenses incurred by him in the performance of his duties, in accordance with the Company’s expense policy.

**6. Hours of Work and Holiday**

6.1 The normal working hours shall be 37.5 hours per week, excluding lunch breaks.

6.2 The Employee shall be entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 The Employee shall not at any time during or after the term of his employment, except as required in the proper performance of his duties, disclose or make use of any Confidential Information.

7.2 The obligations in Clause 7.1 shall continue indefinitely after termination of employment.

**8. Notices**

8.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the Company or the last known residential address of the Employee.

8.2 Notices shall be deemed received 24 hours after transmission by email, or 48 hours after posting.

**9. Termination**

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving twelve months’ written notice.

9.2 The Company reserves the right to make payment in lieu of notice, subject to contractual and statutory entitlements.

**18. Non-Competition**

18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in or be concerned with any business that competes with the Business within the Territory for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether as principal, agent, consultant, director or shareholder holding more than 5% of the issued share capital of any competing entity.

18.2 This restriction applies notwithstanding the reason for termination and is considered reasonable in scope, duration, and geography to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company, including its relationships with customers such as Orwell Analytics Ltd (company number 10563974).

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
