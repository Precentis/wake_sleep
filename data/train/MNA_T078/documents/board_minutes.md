MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF FOXLEY SOFTWARE LTD  
HELD AT 141 BISHOPSGATE, LONDON, ON 18 MAY 2026

Present:  
Mr. A. Patel (Chairman)  
Ms. E. Thompson (Chief Executive Officer)  
Mr. J. Carter (Chief Financial Officer)  
Ms. R. Naylor (Non-Executive Director)

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The meeting was called to order at 10:00 a.m. by Mr. A. Patel, who presided as Chairman of the Board of Foxley Software Ltd (the “Company”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13465627, with registered office at 141 Bishopsgate, London.

2. CONFIRMATION OF QUORUM  
A quorum was established with the attendance of all four board members. The Company’s Articles of Association require a minimum of two directors for a valid meeting; the present attendance satisfied this condition.

3. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 20 April 2026 were reviewed. A motion to approve the minutes was proposed by Ms. Naylor and seconded by Mr. Carter. The minutes were approved unanimously.

4. REVIEW OF ONGOING ACQUISITION PROPOSAL  
The board reviewed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Ivybridge Retail Group plc (the “Buyer”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15061795, with registered office at 79 Moorgate, London. The Buyer is acquiring the shares from Rothley Engineering Ltd (the “Seller”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11147208, with registered office at 13 Queen Victoria Street, London. Legal advisers have confirmed that due diligence is progressing, and the draft share purchase agreement is under review by both parties. The board confirmed its continued cooperation with the transaction process, noting that the expected completion remains subject to shareholder approval and regulatory clearances.

5. LEGAL MATTERS – JUNIPER PHARMA PLC CLAIM  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 12 May 2026 from Juniper Pharma plc, which asserts a claim against the Company for approximately $3,400,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 15 March 2024. The letter alleges that certain software modules delivered between June 2024 and January 2025 failed to meet performance specifications, resulting in operational disruption to Juniper Pharma plc’s distribution systems. Legal counsel has been instructed to assess the merits of the claim. Management is to monitor developments closely and report to the board biweekly. No provision has been recorded in the financial statements as of 31 March 2026, pending further evaluation.

6. CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP UPDATE – QUAYSIDE RETAIL GROUP PLC  
The board received a report on the ongoing service engagement with Quayside Retail Group plc, a key customer incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12783723, with registered office at 14 Ludgate Hill, London. The current support contract, valued at £1.2 million annually, is scheduled for renewal on 1 September 2026. Preliminary discussions have commenced, and management reports positive engagement from the customer’s procurement team.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There being no other business, the Chairman closed the meeting at 11:45 a.m.

8. ADJOURNMENT  
The meeting was adjourned.
