**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 1 May 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Rothley Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11147208, whose registered office is at 13 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “**Seller**”);

(2) **Ivybridge Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15061795, whose registered office is at 79 Moorgate, London (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Foxley Software Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13465627, whose registered office is at 141 Bishopsgate, London (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including any recitals, schedules, and appendices;

“**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“**Closing Date**” means 25 June 2026;

“**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“**General Warranties**” means the warranties given by the Seller in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

“**Fundamental Warranties**” means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax as set out in Schedule 2;

“**Purchase Price**” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$195,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be satisfied by the Buyer making a wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates representing the Shares, endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed stock transfer forms;

(c) resignations of any directors or officers of the Company appointed by the Seller;

(d) such corporate authorisations and consents as the Buyer may reasonably require.

3.3 The Buyer shall, on the Closing Date, pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or valid waiver of the Conditions.

4.2 The parties shall use all reasonable endeavours to procure the fulfilment of the Conditions as soon as reasonably practicable.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or validly waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 1 May 2026), either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights accrued prior to termination.

**5. NOTICES**

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 13 Queen Victoria Street, London, or such other address as notified in writing.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 79 Moorgate, London, or such other address as notified in writing.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated herein, except as required by law or any regulatory authority.

**10. SURVIVAL AND CLAIMS**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under it.
