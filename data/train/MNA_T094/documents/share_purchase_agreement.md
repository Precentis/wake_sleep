**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **31 May 2026**

Between:

(1) **Elstree Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (registered number **15344807**) whose registered office is at **7 Cheapside, London** (the **"Seller"**);

(2) **Quayside Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales (registered number **12494219**) whose registered office is at **110 Bishopsgate, London** (the **"Buyer"**).

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

**Background**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Holloway Systems Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales (registered number **11050762**) whose registered office is at **162 Fleet Street, London** (the **"Company"**).

(B) The Buyer has agreed to purchase, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED** as follows:

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "**Conditions**" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3;
- "**Closing**" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 5;
- "**Closing Date**" means **7 September 2026**, or such other date as the Parties may agree in writing;
- "**Confidential Information**" means all information (whether written, oral or in electronic form) disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement;
- "**English law**" means the law of England and Wales;
- "**Shares**" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;
- "**$**" denotes United States dollars.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

---

**2. Sale and Purchase**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$50,000,000** (the "**Purchase Price**").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller not later than 5:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

2.3 Title to, and all rights attaching to, the Shares shall pass to the Buyer at Closing.

---

**3. Conditions Precedent**

3.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or (where permissible) waiver in writing by both Parties of the following conditions (the "**Conditions**") on or before the Long Stop Date:

(a) all necessary corporate and shareholder approvals having been obtained by each Party;

(b) no order or injunction having been made by any court or regulatory authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated by this Agreement;

(c) the Buyer having received confirmation from **Juniper Analytics Ltd** (registered number **13009499**, registered office at **127 Cheapside, London**) that its existing contract with the Company remains in full force and effect.

---

**4. Termination**

4.1 This Agreement may be terminated prior to the Closing Date only in accordance with this Clause 4.

4.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other if the other Party materially breaches any of its obligations under this Agreement and such breach remains uncured for a period of 14 days following notice requiring performance.

4.3 Termination under this Clause 4 shall not affect any accrued rights or remedies of either Party.

4.4 The Conditions shall be deemed waived unless either Party gives written notice objecting to the satisfaction of any Condition within 15 Business Days of the relevant facts becoming known.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling **120 days after the date of this Agreement** (being **29 September 2026**), either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

---

**5. Closing**

5.1 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) resignations of all directors and secretary of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

(c) such corporate authority documents as the Buyer may reasonably require.

5.2 The Buyer shall make the payment referred to in Clause 2.2 at Closing.

---

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with **English law**. The courts of **England** shall have **exclusive jurisdiction** to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

---

**16. Notices**

16.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post (in the case of the UK) or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

16.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 7 Cheapside, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 110 Bishopsgate, London.

---

**17. Confidentiality**

17.1 Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other Party and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

17.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.
