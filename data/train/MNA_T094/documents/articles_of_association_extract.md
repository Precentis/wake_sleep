ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF HOLLOWAY SYSTEMS LTD  
(Extract Relating to Shareholder and Board Provisions)  

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Buyer” means Quayside Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12494219, whose registered office is at 110 Bishopsgate, London;  
“Company” means Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11050762, whose registered office is at 162 Fleet Street, London;  
“Seller” means Elstree Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15344807, whose registered office is at 7 Cheapside, London;  
“Juniper Analytics Ltd” means the customer of the Company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13009499, whose registered office is at 127 Cheapside, London;  
“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company, comprising ordinary shares;  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company from time to time.  

2. Share Transfer and Acquisition  
2.1 The Shares may be transferred in accordance with applicable law and these Articles.  
2.2 The transfer of 100% of the Shares from the Seller to the Buyer shall be effected in accordance with the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 15 June 2026.  
2.3 Following completion of the acquisition, the Buyer shall be the registered holder of all Shares in the capital of the Company.  

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the authority to exercise all powers of the Company, subject to the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles.  
3.2 The Board shall consist of no fewer than two and no more than five directors, appointed in accordance with these Articles.  
3.3 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution passed by shareholders holding not less than 75% of the voting rights at a general meeting of the Company.  

4. Board Meetings  
4.1 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter unless circumstances require more frequent meetings.  
4.2 Notice of a board meeting shall be given at least five business days in advance to each director, unless all directors agree to a shorter notice period.  
4.3 Resolutions may be passed in writing signed by all directors entitled to vote on the matter, and such resolutions shall have the same effect as a resolution passed at a duly convened meeting.  

5. Quorum and Voting  
5.1 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.  
5.2 If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the appointed time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, unless otherwise agreed by the directors.  
5.3 At any meeting of the Board, every question shall be decided by a majority of votes of the directors present and voting. In the event of an equality of votes, the chair of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.  

6. Payment and Financial Provisions  
6.1 All payments related to the transfer of Shares shall be made by electronic transfer to the bank account nominated in writing by the Seller.  
6.2 The purchase price for the Shares, as set out in the Share Purchase Agreement, shall be paid in full on the completion date, defined as 15 June 2026.  

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.  
7.2 Notices to the Company shall be validly served if delivered to 162 Fleet Street, London.  
7.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be validly served if delivered to 110 Bishopsgate, London.  
7.4 Notices to the Seller shall be validly served if delivered to 7 Cheapside, London.  
7.5 A notice shall be deemed received 48 hours after it is posted by first-class post or on actual delivery if delivered by hand or courier.  

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 The parties agree to keep confidential all information concerning the Company’s business, including but not limited to contracts with customers such as Juniper Analytics Ltd, unless disclosure is required by law.  
8.2 This obligation shall survive the transfer of the Shares and shall continue indefinitely.  

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
