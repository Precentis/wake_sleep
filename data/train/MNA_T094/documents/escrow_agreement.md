ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 14 June 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and among:

(1) Elstree Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15344807 and registered office at 7 Cheapside, London ("Seller");

(2) Quayside Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12494219 and registered office at 110 Bishopsgate, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a financial institution authorised and regulated in the United Kingdom, acting in its capacity as escrow agent under this Agreement (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated 14 June 2026 (the "SPA") with the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11050762 and registered office at 162 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) In accordance with the terms of the SPA, the Buyer has agreed to deposit into an escrow account a sum equal to 10.0% of the total consideration payable under the SPA (the "Escrow Amount") to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The Parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount under the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Effective Date" means 14 June 2026;

"Escrow Account" means the account established by the Escrow Agent for the purpose of holding the Escrow Amount, account number [to be inserted] with [Name of Bank];

"Indemnification Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer against the Seller under Clause 8 of the SPA for indemnification in respect of breaches of representations, warranties, or covenants;

"Material Customer" means Juniper Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13009499 and registered office at 127 Cheapside, London;

"Release Date" means 15 June 2027.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

2. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or before the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account and shall not release any portion of such funds except in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 On the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall release the entire Escrow Amount to the Seller, unless prior to such date the Escrow Agent has received from the Buyer a written notice asserting an Indemnification Claim.

3.2 If an Indemnification Claim is properly notified in writing to the Escrow Agent and the Seller before the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall retain an amount equal to the value of such claim (the "Retained Amount") and release the balance, if any, to the Seller. The Retained Amount shall be released only upon joint written instruction from the Parties or as determined in accordance with Clause 12.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 7 Cheapside, London, Attn: Managing Partner, with a copy to legal@elstreecapital.co.uk.

4.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 110 Bishopsgate, London, Attn: Chief Financial Officer, with a copy to legal@quaysideeng.co.uk.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the Escrow Amount, except as required by law or regulation or disclosure to professional advisors on a need-to-know basis.

6. Escrow Agent’s Role

6.1 The Escrow Agent acts solely as a stakeholder and shall not be liable for any actions taken in good faith reliance on documents or instructions believed to be genuine.

7. Governing Law and Arbitration

12. This Escrow Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York, to be conducted in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce. Judgment upon the award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction.
