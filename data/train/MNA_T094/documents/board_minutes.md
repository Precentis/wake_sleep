MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF HOLLOWAY SYSTEMS LTD

Date: 14 June 2026  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 162 Fleet Street, London EC4A 2DQ (held in person and by video conference)

1. Attendance and Quorum  
1.1. Present:  
- Mr A. Thorpe (Director)  
- Ms L. Patel (Director)  

1.2. Absent:  
- Mr J. Greene (Director)  
- Ms R. Singh (Director)  
- Mr D. Ford (Director)  

1.3. The Chair confirmed that 2 directors (of 5) were present. The meeting was quorate in accordance with the company’s articles of association.

2. Declaration of Interests  
2.1. Both directors present declared no material interests in the matters to be discussed, save for their general capacity as directors of the Company.

3. Review of Proposed Transaction  
3.1. The Board reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 14 June 2026 (the “Agreement”) between Elstree Capital LLP (company number 15344807), as seller, and Quayside Engineering Ltd (company number 12494219), as buyer, pursuant to which the Buyer will acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Holloway Systems Ltd (company number 11050762).

3.2. The Board was briefed by external legal counsel on the terms and effect of the Agreement, including the obligations of the Company post-completion and the implications of the change in control.

3.3. The Board considered the financial and operational impact of the transaction on Holloway Systems Ltd, including ongoing contractual relationships with material customers, particularly Juniper Analytics Ltd (company number 13009499).

3.4. The Board noted that the transaction represents a strategic opportunity for the Company given the Buyer’s stated intention to invest in the Company’s technology platform and expand its market reach.

4. Approval of Entry into Share Purchase Agreement  
4.1. Upon consideration of the terms of the Agreement and the strategic rationale for the transaction, the Board concluded that entry into the Share Purchase Agreement is in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders.

4.2. It was therefore RESOLVED to approve entry into the Share Purchase Agreement by and between Elstree Capital LLP and Quayside Engineering Ltd, pursuant to which the entire issued share capital of Holloway Systems Ltd shall be acquired by the Buyer.

5. Authority to Complete  
5.1. It was further RESOLVED that the directors of the Company are authorised to take all steps necessary to facilitate the completion of the transaction, including providing any corporate or administrative assistance reasonably required by the Buyer or the Seller under the Agreement.

6. Confidentiality and Announcements  
6.1. The Board agreed that no public announcement regarding the transaction shall be made by any director or officer of the Company without the prior written consent of the Buyer, except as required by law or any regulatory body.

7. Adjournment  
7.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:30 a.m.
