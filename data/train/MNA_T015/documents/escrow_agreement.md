ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Pemberton Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13237232 and registered office at 138 Cannon Street, London ("Seller");

(2) Foxley Capital LLP, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13923769 and registered office at 49 Gresham Street, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a law firm or financial institution acting as escrow agent under this Agreement (the "Escrow Agent").

RECITALS

A. The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated as of [●] 2026 (the "SPA"), pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14657056 and registered office at 157 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

B. As part of the consideration under the SPA, a portion of the purchase price is to be held in escrow to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

C. The parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount (as defined below) on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 13 November 2026.

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, whether in writing, orally or by inspection of physical objects.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount of £12,750,000, representing 15% of the total consideration payable under the SPA, deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to clause 2.

"Pending Claim" means a written notice of a claim for indemnification under the SPA delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with clause 5 prior to the Release Date, which has not been finally resolved or withdrawn.

"Release Date" means the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

2.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") maintained by the Escrow Agent.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Ownership of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall remain the property of the Buyer until released to the Seller or applied in satisfaction of any indemnification claim in accordance with this Agreement.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as is not subject to any Pending Claim, shall be released to the Seller on the Release Date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall be retained in the Escrow Account until such claim is finally resolved, at which time the undisputed balance shall be released to the Seller.

5. Notification of Claims

5.1 The Buyer may notify the Seller and the Escrow Agent of any claim for indemnification under the SPA by delivering a written notice to both parties prior to the Release Date.

5.2 All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set out above or such other address as a party may designate by prior written notice.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party, except as required by law.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be valid if in writing and delivered personally, sent by confirmed electronic transmission or sent by pre-paid first-class post to the registered office of the relevant party.

8. Fees and Expenses

8.1 The fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be shared equally between the Buyer and the Seller unless otherwise agreed in writing.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in New York in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, and the place of arbitration shall be New York.
