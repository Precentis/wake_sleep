SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 5 August 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Foxley Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (company number 13923769), whose registered office is at 49 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Pemberton Pharma plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 13237232), whose registered office is at 138 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) Ingleby Pharma plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 14657056), whose registered office is at 157 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 13 November 2026;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

and other capitalized terms have the meanings given to them elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

1.3 References to clauses and recitals are to clauses and recitals of this Agreement.

1.4 References to a person include any individual, company, partnership, joint venture, association, corporation, unincorporated body of persons or other entity.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a purchase price of $60,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 4.

2.2 The sale and purchase shall take place on the Closing Date.

3. EARN-OUT

3.4 The parties acknowledge and agree that, in addition to the Purchase Price, the Seller shall be entitled to an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") calculated as follows: the Earn-out Payment shall equal 2 times the amount by which the audited EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026 EBITDA") exceeds $4,000,000, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at $10,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within 90 days of the final audited accounts for FY2026 being approved by the Buyer’s board.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five business days prior to the Closing Date.

4.2 Time for payment is of the essence.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to its registered office at 49 Gresham Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to its registered office at 138 Cannon Street, London.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party undertakes to the other to keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement and not to disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

7. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior discussions, correspondence, representations or agreements.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.
