**Disclosure Letter**  
Dated 28 August 2026  

Between:  
(1) Pemberton Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13237232, whose registered office is at 138 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”); and  
(2) Foxley Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13923769, whose registered office is at 49 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”).

This letter (the “Disclosure Letter”) is delivered pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the date hereof between the Seller and the Buyer (the “SPA”). Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Letter have the meanings given to them in the SPA.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Disclosure Letter:  
  “Disclosed” means fairly and accurately disclosed in this Disclosure Letter with sufficient detail to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed;  
  “Warranties” means the representations and warranties given by the Seller to the Buyer under the SPA.  

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Disclosure Letter unless otherwise stated.  
1.3 The headings in this Disclosure Letter are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

**2. General Disclosure**  
2.1 Save as Disclosed in this Disclosure Letter, there is no matter, fact or circumstance that renders any Warranty untrue or inaccurate.  
2.2 The disclosure in this Disclosure Letter is limited to the Warranties to which it expressly relates and does not constitute disclosure for any other Warranty, whether by implication, overlap or otherwise.  
2.3 Disclosure of any matter in this Disclosure Letter shall not be construed as an admission that such matter is material for the purposes of the SPA or that it would give rise to a Claim.

**3. Disclosure Against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
3.1 Warranty 6.3 states that no Material Contract contains any provision that would be triggered by a change of control of the Company.  
3.2 The Seller confirms that no Material Contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision.  
3.3 For the avoidance of doubt, there is no further disclosure to be made under Warranty 6.3.  
3.4 The term “Material Contract” includes, without limitation, the supply agreement dated 14 May 2021 between Ingleby Pharma plc and Marlow Industries Ltd (company number 14479971, registered office at 45 Cheapside, London), pursuant to which Ingleby Pharma plc supplies pharmaceutical intermediates to Marlow Industries Ltd on terms requiring minimum annual purchases of £4.2 million over a five-year term ending on 13 May 2026. The Buyer acknowledges that this agreement expired on 13 May 2026 and has not been renewed. No consent or notice is required under any Material Contract as a result of the transaction contemplated by the SPA.

**4. Payment and Adjustments**  
4.1 All payments due under the SPA shall be made in pounds sterling by same-day cleared funds to such bank account as the relevant party may designate in writing.  
4.2 Any payment not made by the due date shall bear interest from the due date until the date of actual payment, both before and after judgment, at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate from time to time of Barclays Bank PLC, accruing on a daily basis.

**5. Notices**  
5.1 Any notice or other communication given under or in connection with this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in the SPA.  
5.2 A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed received when delivered. A notice sent by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed received at 9:00 a.m. on the second Business Day following posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed received at the time of transmission if a delivery receipt is obtained, otherwise at 9:00 a.m. on the next Business Day following transmission.

**6. Confidentiality**  
6.1 Each party shall keep confidential this Disclosure Letter and all information disclosed under it, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except:  
  (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidence;  
  (b) as required by law or any regulatory authority; or  
  (c) with the prior written consent of the other party.  
6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of the SPA.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
7.1 This Disclosure Letter and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Disclosure Letter.
