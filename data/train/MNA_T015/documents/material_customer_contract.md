**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 15 March 2024  

**Between:**  
(1) Marlow Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14479971 whose registered office is at 45 Cheapside, London (the "Customer"); and  
(2) Ingleby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14657056 whose registered office is at 157 Cheapside, London (the "Supplier").

**Recitals**  
A. The Customer and the Supplier have previously entered into a supply arrangement for the provision of pharmaceutical intermediates.  
B. The parties now wish to formalise the terms of supply under this Agreement.  

**Operative Provisions**  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that party;  
"change of control" means the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting in concert of the right to exercise, directly or indirectly, 50% or more of the voting rights attached to the issued shares of a party, or the acquisition of the right to appoint or remove a majority of its board of directors;  
"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, electronically or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably ought to be regarded as confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure;  
"Effective Date" means 15 March 2024.  

**2. Supply of Goods**  
2.1 The Supplier shall supply, and the Customer shall purchase, the goods described in Schedule 1 (the "Goods") in accordance with the specifications and delivery timelines set out therein.  

**3. Payment**  
3.1 The Customer shall pay the price for the Goods as specified in Schedule 1, within 60 days of the date of receipt of a valid invoice issued by the Supplier.  
3.2 All sums payable under this Agreement shall be paid in pounds sterling without any set-off, counterclaim or deduction of any kind.  

**4. Delivery**  
4.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Goods to the address specified in Schedule 1 (the "Delivery Address") on or before the delivery date specified in the applicable purchase order.  

**5. Invoicing**  
5.1 The Supplier shall issue invoices to the Customer at the Delivery Address, or such other address as the Customer may notify in accordance with Clause 13 (Notices).  

**6. Confidentiality**  
6.1 Each party shall treat all Confidential Information of the other party with the same degree of care as it treats its own confidential information and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as permitted under this Agreement.  
6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.  

**7. Term and Termination**  
7.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of three years unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.  

**13. Notices**  
13.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.  

**14. Termination**  
14.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach of any term of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of being notified to do so.  
14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the Customer. For the avoidance of doubt, this provision applies to any change of control affecting Ingleby Pharma plc, including any acquisition of its entire issued share capital by Foxley Capital LLP or any other third party.  

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.  
15.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
