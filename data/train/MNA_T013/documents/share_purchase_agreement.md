SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 10 February 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Vantage Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12116024, whose registered office is at 8 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Saltash Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10969231, whose registered office is at 135 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

and

(3) Hartwell Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12818907, whose registered office is at 7 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, "Closing Date" means 22 April 2027.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the Closing Date for a purchase price of $140,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000.

3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT

3.4 The Buyer shall make an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to three (3) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026 EBITDA") exceeds $6,500,000, provided that the Earn-out Payment shall not exceed $10,000,000 in aggregate.

9. WARRANTIES AND CLAIMS

9.7 If the Buyer becomes aware of any matter giving rise to a claim under any warranty, the Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of such claim within 20 days of becoming aware of the matter.

10. SURVIVAL AND LIMITATIONS

10.1 The General Warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 72 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under such warranties must be notified in writing to the other party prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

11.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 135 Moorgate, London.

11.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 8 Old Broad Street, London.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it will keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, including the existence and terms of this Agreement, save as required by law or any regulatory authority.

12.2 This obligation shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available otherwise than as a result of a breach of this clause.

13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement, together with the documents referred to herein, constitutes the whole agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior arrangement, understanding or agreement between them relating to the subject matter.

13.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
