ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on 20 February 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Vantage Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12116024, whose registered office is at 8 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Saltash Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10969231, whose registered office is at 135 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Hartwell Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12818907, whose registered office is at 7 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company"),

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company;

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Parties have agreed to establish an escrow arrangement for the secure holding of a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller (the "Escrow Amount"), to secure certain indemnities and warranties provided by the Seller;

(C) The Parties have appointed [Name of Escrow Agent], a professional escrow agent, to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms set out herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London;

   "Closing Date" means 22 April 2027;

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £15,000,000 (fifteen million pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account established under this Agreement;

   "Escrow Agent" means [Name of Escrow Agent], or any successor appointed in accordance with Clause 8;

   "SPA" has the meaning given in the Recitals.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Establishment of Escrow

2.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") maintained by the Escrow Agent at a recognised financial institution in London.

2.2 The Escrow Account shall be held in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of the Seller, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, to the Seller in accordance with Clause 4.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

4.2 For the avoidance of doubt, any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a validly notified claim under Clause 5 shall remain held in escrow until such claim is finally resolved or withdrawn.

5. Claims

5.1 A claim against the Escrow Amount may be made by the Buyer only in respect of a breach of representation, warranty or indemnity under the SPA.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, and shall be delivered in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim and the amount claimed (not to exceed the balance then held in the Escrow Account).

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party as set out in the Recitals.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting, or upon electronic confirmation of receipt if sent by email.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent and the Parties shall keep the terms of this Agreement and any claims notified hereunder confidential, except as required by law or to professional advisors bound by confidentiality.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under this Agreement.
