SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 30 May 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Kingsmere Technologies Ltd (company number 15703595), incorporated in England and Wales and having its registered office at 149 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Fenwick Holdings Ltd (company number 12747699), incorporated in England and Wales and having its registered office at 35 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 4 September 2026;

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

"Company" means Calloway Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10682870 and having its registered office at 92 Queen Victoria Street, London;

"Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 5.1;

"Purchase Price" means the consideration specified in Clause 2.1;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $225,000,000, which shall be satisfied by the Buyer to the Seller in full upon completion of the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2.2 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company, representing 100% of the voting and economic rights in the Company, free from all liens, charges, encumbrances, and third-party rights.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated in accordance with the working capital statement prepared by the Buyer within 90 days following the Closing Date, subject to review and dispute resolution under Clause 2.4.

2.4 The Closing Working Capital shall be determined based on the unaudited management accounts of the Company as of the Closing Date, prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) consistently applied. In the event of a dispute over the calculation, the parties shall appoint an independent accountant from a firm mutually acceptable to both parties to resolve the matter within 30 days.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures. At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms, board resolutions, and other documents necessary to transfer legal and beneficial title to the shares in the Company.

3.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, as adjusted under Clause 2.3, by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 11:59 a.m. London time on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

4.1 Each party’s obligation to complete is conditional upon the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions by the Closing Date.

4.2 The Conditions include, but are not limited to: (a) no Material Adverse Change having occurred in respect of the Company; (b) receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals; and (c) accuracy of representations and warranties as of the Closing Date.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 28 October 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NOTICES

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the transaction, except as required by law or regulation, or with the prior written consent of the other party.

5.2 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as notified in writing. Notices shall be deemed delivered on the second business day following transmission by certified courier or email with confirmation of receipt.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

6.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
