ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Kingsmere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15703595, whose registered office is at 149 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Fenwick Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12747699, whose registered office is at 35 King William Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, whose principal office is at [●] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the "Parties" and each a "Party".

1.  Definitions and Interpretation

1.1  In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London.

"Closing Date" means 4 September 2026.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £9,500,000 (Nine Million Five Hundred Thousand Pounds Sterling), representing part of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] 2026 in respect of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Calloway Medical Ltd.

"Material Customer" means Saltash Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15662991, whose registered office is at 123 Bishopsgate, London.

"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement dated [●] 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of Calloway Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10682870, whose registered office is at 92 Queen Victoria Street, London.

"Working Day" has the same meaning as Business Day.

1.2  The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2.  Establishment of Escrow

2.1  Upon execution of this Agreement, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent into an interest-bearing client account operated by the Escrow Agent in accordance with standard client money rules (the "Escrow Account").

2.2  The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3.  Payment of Escrow Amount

3.1  The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely for the purposes set out in this Agreement.

3.2  No withdrawal from the Escrow Account shall be made except in accordance with Clauses 4 or 5.

4.  Release of Escrow Amount to Seller

4.1  The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as remains undisputed, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any claims notified by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in writing before that date under the Share Purchase Agreement (each a "Pending Claim"). Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such claim is finally resolved, waived or withdrawn.

5.  Claims and Adjustments

5.1  Any claim under the Share Purchase Agreement which gives rise to a right to recover from the Escrow Amount must be notified in writing to both the Seller and the Escrow Agent before the date referred to in Clause 4.1.

5.2  Upon final determination of a Pending Claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed balance of the Escrow Amount to the Seller unless otherwise directed by joint written instruction of the Buyer and the Seller.

6.  Notices

6.1  Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify in writing.

7.  Confidentiality

7.1  The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement unless disclosure is required by law or by any regulatory authority.

8.  Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1  This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

8.2  The Parties agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
