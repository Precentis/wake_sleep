SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 15 September 2026

BETWEEN:

(1) Oakhurst Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10537719, whose registered office is at 35 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Brightwater Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12197564, whose registered office is at 15 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) Elstree Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10526697, whose registered office is at 22 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company upon the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 18 November 2026;

   "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

   "Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to Clause 2.1.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller hereby sells to the Buyer, and the Buyer hereby purchases from the Seller, the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all liens, charges, encumbrances and third-party rights, for a total consideration of $45,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The sale and purchase of the shares shall take place on the Closing Date, at which time legal and beneficial ownership of the shares shall transfer to the Buyer, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer no later than three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Any failure to make payment of the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 3.1 shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

4. CLOSING

4.1 The Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

4.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer in respect of all shares in the Company;

   (b) board resolutions of the Company approving the transfer of shares and the appointment of such directors as the Buyer may nominate;

   (c) such other documents and instruments as may reasonably be required to effect the transfer of the shares and confirm good title.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in the preamble.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received 48 hours after transmission if sent by email, or on the third Business Day following posting if sent by pre-paid first-class post.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the other, disclose to any third party any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated herein, except:

   (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under obligations of confidentiality;

   (b) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

   (c) in connection with the enforcement of rights under this Agreement.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
