ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ELSTREE INDUSTRIES LTD

The following are extracts from the Articles of Association of Elstree Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10526697, with registered office at 22 Cannon Street, London, in force as of a date prior to 30 September 2026.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

   (a) "Buyer" means Oakhurst Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10537719, with registered office at 35 Moorgate, London;

   (b) "Seller" means Brightwater Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12197564, with registered office at 15 Bishopsgate, London;

   (c) "Brightwater Marine Ltd" means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10687119, with registered office at 46 Bishopsgate, London;

   (d) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;

   (e) "Company" means Elstree Industries Ltd, the company to which these Articles apply;

   (f) "Directors" means the individuals for the time being members of the Board;

   (g) "Ordinary Resolution" means a resolution passed by a simple majority of votes cast by shareholders entitled to vote at a general meeting;

   (h) "Shareholder" means a person for the time being entered in the register of members as the holder of one or more shares in the Company.

2. Share Capital and Transfer of Shares

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall consist of 1,000,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

2.2 Subject to these Articles and applicable law, shares in the Company may be transferred in accordance with procedures established by the Board, provided that no transfer shall be registered unless accompanied by a properly executed instrument of transfer and such evidence as the Board may reasonably require.

2.3 The Board may, at its discretion, decline to register any transfer of shares without assigning any reason.

3. Directors

3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which shall have the authority to perform all acts and enter into all arrangements which it considers necessary or desirable in connection with the management of the Company’s business, subject always to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles.

3.2 The initial Directors shall be those persons appointed at incorporation or as subsequently notified to Companies House. Additional Directors may be appointed by Ordinary Resolution of the Shareholders.

3.3 A Director may be removed from office by Ordinary Resolution of the Shareholders, with or without cause, provided that not less than 21 days’ notice of any such resolution shall be given.

4. Board Meetings and Proceedings

4.1 The Board may meet to conduct business at such times and places as the Directors may determine.

4.2 Notice of a meeting of the Board shall be given to each Director at least 48 hours prior to such meeting, unless all Directors waive such notice in writing.

4.3 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of the votes of the Directors present and voting on the question. In the case of equality of votes, the Chair of the meeting shall not have a casting vote.

4.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

5. Payment and Financial Matters

5.1 All payments made by or to the Company shall be made in lawful currency of the United Kingdom unless otherwise agreed in writing between the Company and the relevant counterparty.

5.2 The Company shall maintain proper accounting records in accordance with the Companies Act 2006, including records of all sums received and expended, assets and liabilities, and sufficient information to enable the financial statements to be prepared.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office of the Company or to the last known address or email address of the recipient.

6.2 A notice sent by post to the registered office of the Company or of any Shareholder or Director shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Every Director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, operations, and financial affairs of the Company and its affiliates, including Brightwater Marine Ltd, and shall not use such information for any personal benefit or disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.

7.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall continue after the termination of a Director’s or officer’s office.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.
