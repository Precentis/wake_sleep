ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") among:

(1) Brightwater Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12197564 whose registered office is at 15 Bishopsgate, London ("Seller");

(2) Oakhurst Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10537719 whose registered office is at 35 Moorgate, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent Name], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, having an office at [●] ("Escrow Agent").

The parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 18 November 2026.

"Company" means Elstree Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10526697 whose registered office is at 22 Cannon Street, London.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,250,000 (one million two hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 10% of the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] 2026, held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement.

"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement dated [●] 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Any reference to a clause is to a clause of this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount in a designated client account (the "Escrow Account") on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to hold, administer and release the Escrow Amount in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall, on or before the Closing Date, pay the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account and shall not release any part of it except in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3.3 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent as agent for both the Buyer and the Seller, without any beneficial interest, and shall be kept separate from the Escrow Agent’s own funds.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending claims notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent before that date pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement. Any portion of the Escrow Amount not released due to pending claims shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claims, whereupon it shall be released in accordance with the outcome.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above or as otherwise notified in writing.

5.2 A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed served upon delivery. A notice sent by post shall be deemed served two Business Days after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed served on the date of transmission if sent on a Business Day before 5:00 p.m., or on the next Business Day if sent after that time.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement and the Escrow Account, except as required by law or professional obligation, or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, arrangements and agreements.

7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
