**4. Review of Proposed Transaction**  
4.1. The Chairperson presented a summary of the proposed transaction involving the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Elstree Industries Ltd by Oakhurst Marine Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10537719, registered office at 35 Moorgate, London) from Brightwater Robotics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12197564, registered office at 15 Bishopsgate, London).  
4.2. Counsel had provided a draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 14 September 2026, which was reviewed in summary form.  
4.3. The board discussed the strategic implications of the transaction, noting that Brightwater Marine Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10687119, registered office at 46 Bishopsgate, London) remains a material customer of Elstree Industries Ltd.  
4.4. The directors noted that the transaction is structured as a sale of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  

**5. Resolution**  
5.1. Upon motion duly made, it was:  
**RESOLVED** that Elstree Industries Ltd approve the entry by Brightwater Robotics Ltd into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 14 September 2026 for the sale of the entire issued share capital of Elstree Industries Ltd (company number 10526697) to Oakhurst Marine Ltd, such approval being granted in accordance with the requirements of English law and the constitutional documents of the Company.  

**6. Authority to Proceed**  
6.1. It was further agreed that the Secretary be authorised to take all necessary steps to file this resolution with Companies House as required under the Companies Act 2006.  
6.2. The Chairperson and any director present were authorised to sign all relevant transaction documents on behalf of the Company where necessary to give effect to the board’s approval.

7.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
