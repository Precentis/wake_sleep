The minutes of the board meeting held on 18 May 2027 were reviewed. It was moved by Mr. R. D. Patel and seconded by Ms. L. M. Grant that the minutes be approved as an accurate record. The motion was passed unanimously.

**5. Letter Before Action from Northgate Pharma plc**  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 20 June 2027 from Northgate Pharma plc, served via its legal representatives, alleging defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 15 March 2025. The letter claimed damages in the amount of approximately $2,750,000. The claim relates to batches of pharmaceutical intermediates delivered between October 2026 and January 2027, which Northgate Pharma plc asserts failed to meet specified purity thresholds. Legal counsel has been engaged to review the correspondence and assess potential exposure. The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely, preserve all relevant documentation, and report progress at the next scheduled meeting. No provision has been made in the unaudited financial statements as at 31 March 2027 in respect of this matter.

**6. Ongoing Acquisition by Yarrow Logistics plc**  
The board discussed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Vantage Bioscience plc by Yarrow Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14952716, registered office at 110 King William Street, London) from Merrion Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14550251, registered office at 84 King William Street, London). The transaction remains subject to shareholder approval and certain regulatory clearances. Due diligence has been completed by the Buyer, and no material objections were raised. The board reaffirmed its support for the transaction, noting that the strategic rationale and financial terms remain unchanged. Completion is expected on or around 30 June 2027, subject to all conditions precedent being satisfied.

**7. Material Customer Update – Foxley Analytics Ltd**  
The board reviewed the status of the commercial relationship with Foxley Analytics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13646755, registered office at 35 Bishopsgate, London), a material customer accounting for approximately 18% of the Company’s revenue in the financial year ended 31 March 2027. The current service agreement with Foxley Analytics Ltd is set to expire on 31 August 2027. Preliminary discussions regarding renewal have commenced, and management will provide a further update at the July board meeting.

**8. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.
