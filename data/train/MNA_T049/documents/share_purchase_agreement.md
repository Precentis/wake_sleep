SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 13 June 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Merrion Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14550251, whose registered office is at 84 King William Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Yarrow Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14952716, whose registered office is at 110 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Vantage Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14105211, whose registered office is at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 19 August 2027;

   "Purchase Price" means the amount payable by the Buyer to the Seller under Clause 2.1.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The purchase price for the entire issued share capital of the Company (the "Purchase Price") is $120,000,000, which shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by irrevocable transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties given in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 25% of the Purchase Price, being $37,500,000. The Purchase Price is $120,000,000 (as set out in Section 2.1). This limitation applies cumulatively to all warranty claims and shall not be increased or waived by any party.

12. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

12.1 The Seller covenants to the Buyer that, for a period of 4 years from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, manage, operate, join, control or be engaged or interested in any business that competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date, whether within the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company operates at such time. This restriction is limited to a period of 4 years and shall not extend beyond that term.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class recorded delivery, or international courier service, or sent by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

16.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 84 King William Street, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 110 King William Street, London. Either party may change its address for service by notice in writing to the other.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each party agrees that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the other party, disclose to any third party any information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law, regulation or any applicable stock exchange rule.

17.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall continue to apply after the Closing Date and shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement on the date first above written.
