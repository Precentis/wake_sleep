EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 15 April 2025 between:

(1) Vantage Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14105211, whose registered office is at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London ("Company"); and

(2) Dr. Eleanor Reed, of 22 Carlton Road, Cambridge, CB2 1JP ("Employee").

1. Definitions  
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Business" means the research, development, manufacture, and commercialisation of precision diagnostic platforms using biosensing technologies;  
"Effective Date" means 15 April 2025;  
"Founder" means Dr. Eleanor Reed in her capacity as Founder of the Company;  
"Group" means the Company and all its subsidiaries and associated undertakings from time to time;  
"Termination Date" means the date upon which the Employee’s employment with the Company is lawfully terminated, whether by notice, resignation, or dismissal.

2. Commencement and Position  
The Employee shall commence employment with the Company on the Effective Date and shall hold the position of Chief Scientific Officer and Founder, reporting to the Board of Directors.

3. Remuneration  
The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £115,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month. The salary shall be reviewed annually at the discretion of the Board.

4. Hours and Duties  
The Employee shall devote her full working time, attention, and abilities to the performance of her duties, acting at all times in the best interests of the Company and the Group.

5. Benefits  
The Employee shall be entitled to participate in all pension, health, and welfare benefit schemes made available by the Company to senior executives, subject to the terms of such schemes.

6. Leave  
The Employee shall be entitled to 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in England.

7. Expenses  
The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable and properly documented business expenses incurred in the performance of her duties, subject to the Company’s expense policy.

8. Payment Terms  
All payments due under this Agreement shall be made by electronic transfer to the Employee’s nominated bank account, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

9. Notices  
Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the Company at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London, or to the Employee’s last known address as recorded in the Company’s personnel files. Notices shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting or upon confirmed receipt if by email.

10. Confidentiality  
The Employee shall not, during or after her employment, disclose or use any Confidential Information, except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as required by law. "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, technical data, customer lists, supplier agreements, financial information, and business strategies.

11. Intellectual Property  
All intellectual property created by the Employee during her employment and arising from or relating to the Business shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

12. Data Protection  
The Employee consents to the processing of personal data by the Company for purposes connected with employment, in accordance with data protection legislation.

13. Termination  
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six months’ written notice. The Company may terminate summarily for gross misconduct or material breach.

14. Garden Leave  
The Company may place the Employee on garden leave during the notice period, during which she shall remain bound by the terms of this Agreement.

15. Return of Property  
On termination, the Employee shall promptly return all property belonging to the Company, including electronic devices, documents, and data.

16. Survival  
Clauses 10 (Confidentiality), 11 (Intellectual Property), and 18 (Non-Compete) shall continue to apply after termination.

17. Entire Agreement  
This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

18. Non-Compete  
The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage or be involved in any business that competes with the Business for a period of 18 months after the Termination Date, whether as employee, consultant, shareholder (except for holdings of less than 0.75% in publicly traded companies), or director. This restriction applies within the United Kingdom and any jurisdiction where the Company, including its customer Foxley Analytics Ltd (company number 13646755), operates.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
