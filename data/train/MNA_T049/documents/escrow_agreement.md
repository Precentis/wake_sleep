1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Buyer” means Yarrow Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14952716, whose registered office is at 110 King William Street, London.

“Closing Date” means 19 August 2027.

“Company” means Vantage Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14105211, whose registered office is at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Escrow Account” means the designated account at Barclays Bank PLC, 125 London Wall, London, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited.

“Escrow Amount” means an amount equal to 15% of the total Consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement, being the sum of £37,500,000, which shall be held in escrow in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

“Party” means any of the Buyer, the Seller or the Escrow Agent, and “Parties” means all of them collectively.

“Seller” means Merrion Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14550251, whose registered office is at 84 King William Street, London.

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement dated on or about the date of this Agreement between the Buyer and the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

“Tax” includes any tax, duty, levy, impost, deduction, assessment or withholding of a similar nature imposed by any governmental authority, including but not limited to income tax, corporation tax, value added tax and capital gains tax.

1.2 Any reference to a “clause” is a reference to a clause of this Agreement. The headings in this Agreement are inserted for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. Escrow Mechanism

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on the Closing Date, such deposit to be made in same-day cleared funds.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any charge, lien or encumbrance by any Party or third party.

3. Notices

3.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

3.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 110 King William Street, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

3.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 84 King William Street, London, and to the attention of the Director.

3.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address of the Escrow Account and to the attention of the Corporate Escrow Department.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof not then subject to any pending claims notified in accordance with Clause 4.2, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date. “Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London.

4.2 Any claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent on or prior to the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date. Such notification shall specify in reasonable detail the nature, basis and quantum of the claim.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

6. Governing Law and Arbitration

6.1 Clause 12: This escrow agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in New York in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce. The number of arbitrators shall be one, and the seat of arbitration shall be New York.
