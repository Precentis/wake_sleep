ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 5 January 2026 (the “Effective Date”)  

between:  

(1) Northgate Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10877821, whose registered office is at 157 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”);  

(2) Vantage Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14031600, whose registered office is at 118 Cheapside, London (the “Seller”); and  

(3) The London Trust Corporation Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 04567821, acting as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).  

The Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are hereinafter individually referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.  

WHEREAS:  

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 4 January 2026 (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Merrion Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10905440, whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the “Company”).  

(B) In accordance with the terms of the SPA, an amount equal to 15% of the total purchase price (the “Escrow Amount”) has been deposited into an escrow account to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.  

(C) The Parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:  

1. Definitions  
   1.1. In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
        “Claim Notice” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller specifying in reasonable detail the basis for a claim under the SPA and the amount being claimed from the Escrow Amount.  
        “Escrow Account” means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent for the purpose of holding the Escrow Amount.  
        “Indemnification Period” means the period ending on 31 December 2027.  

2. Establishment of Escrow Account  
   2.1. The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account in accordance with this Agreement.  
   2.2. The Escrow Amount shall be deposited into the Escrow Account on the date of completion under the SPA.  

3. Payment  
   3.1. The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds from the Escrow Account only in accordance with written joint instructions signed by the Buyer and the Seller or as otherwise required by a final arbitration award under Clause 12.  

4. Notices  
   4.1. Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as may be notified in writing.  

5. Confidentiality  
   5.1. The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.  

6. Duties of Escrow Agent  
   6.1. The Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall have no discretion to interpret or enforce the SPA.  

12. Governing Law and Arbitration  
   12.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.  
   12.2. Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce in New York, in accordance with its Rules. The seat of arbitration shall be New York. The language of the arbitration shall be English.
