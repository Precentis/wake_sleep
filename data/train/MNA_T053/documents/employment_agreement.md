EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 15 December 2025, between Merrion Medical Ltd (the "Company"), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10905440, whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London, and Dr. Eleanor Reed (the "Employee").

Clause 1 – Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Business” means the business of designing, manufacturing, and supplying medical diagnostic equipment and systems currently carried out by the Company, including but not limited to those supplied to Eskdale Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15322833, registered office 141 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
“Confidential Information” includes all trade secrets, technical data, customer lists, supplier agreements, software code, product development plans, pricing strategies, financial information, and any other proprietary information disclosed to or accessed by the Employee during her employment;  
“Effective Date” means 1 January 2026;  
“Founder” means Dr. Eleanor Reed, who is a founding director of the Company;  
“Territory” means England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, and any country in which the Company conducts business directly or through distribution partners as of the termination date of the Employee’s employment.

Clause 2 – Commencement and Term  
2.1 The Employee’s employment with the Company shall commence on the Effective Date.  
2.2 This Agreement shall continue in force until terminated in accordance with Clause 14.

Clause 3 – Position and Duties  
3.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Scientific Officer of the Company.  
3.2 The Employee shall perform her duties diligently and to the best of her ability, in accordance with the Company’s policies and procedures and in furtherance of the Company’s strategic objectives.

Clause 4 – Remuneration  
4.1 The Company shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of £115,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears.  
4.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme applicable to senior executives, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme as determined by the Board of Directors.

Clause 5 – Expenses  
5.1 The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable and properly documented travel, accommodation, and other business-related expenses incurred by her in the performance of her duties.

Clause 6 – Working Hours and Location  
6.1 The Employee shall work from the Company’s registered office at 132 Moorgate, London, unless otherwise agreed in writing.  
6.2 The normal working hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Monday to Friday, with a 30-minute lunch break.

Clause 7 – Leave Entitlement  
7.1 The Employee shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave in each calendar year, in addition to public holidays.

Clause 8 – Confidentiality  
8.1 The Employee shall not, during or at any time after the termination of her employment, disclose or make use of any Confidential Information, except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as authorised in writing by the Company.  
8.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.

Clause 9 – Intellectual Property  
9.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee during her employment in the course of her duties shall vest in the Company immediately upon creation.

Clause 10 – Notices  
10.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the Company or to the Employee’s last known address.

Clause 14 – Termination  
14.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving twelve months’ written notice.  
14.2 The Company may terminate the Agreement summarily for gross misconduct without notice or payment in lieu.

Clause 18 – Non-Compete  
18.1 The Founder shall not, either directly or indirectly, engage, be employed, concerned, or interested in any capacity as principal, partner, shareholder (except as a holder of not more than 3% of the shares in a publicly listed company), consultant, agent, or director in any business which competes with the Business within the Territory for a period of 12 months after termination of employment.
