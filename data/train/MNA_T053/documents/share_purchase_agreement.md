# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Share Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 17 December 2025 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Vantage Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14031600, with its registered office at 118 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Northgate Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10877821, with its registered office at 157 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

**Recitals**

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Merrion Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10905440, with its registered office at 132 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

C. The Closing is scheduled to occur on 14 March 2026 (the "Closing Date").

**Now therefore**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

**1.1** In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the medical technology industry generally, and pandemics.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

**2.1** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, all of the issued shares in the Company for a total purchase price of $100,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. The Closing Working Capital shall be determined in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1.

**3. Payment**

The Purchase Price, as adjusted pursuant to Section 2.3, shall be paid in full in immediately available funds by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 5:00 p.m. (London time) on the Closing Date.

**4. Completion**

Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing. At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms, and such other documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges, and encumbrances.

**5. Representations and Warranties**

Each party makes to the other the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 2, which shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months.

**6. Confidentiality**

Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

**12. Non-Competition**

**12.1** The Seller covenants and agrees that for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial or other interest in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

**15.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales for such purposes.

**16. Notices**

Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the recipient. Notices shall be deemed received 24 hours after transmission by email, or two business days after posting.

**17. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement, together with the Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, arrangements, and understandings.
