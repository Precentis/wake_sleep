**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 15 April 2023

Between:

(1) **Merrion Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10905440 whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the *Supplier*); and

(2) **Eskdale Systems Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15322833 whose registered office is at 141 Queen Victoria Street, London (the *Customer*).

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- *Agreement* means this contract, including all schedules and appendices as amended from time to time;
- *Commencement Date* means 15 April 2023;
- *Deliverables* means the products and services to be supplied by the Supplier to the Customer under this Agreement;
- *Effective Date* means the date of this Agreement;
- *Party* means either the Supplier or the Customer, and *Parties* means both of them.

1.2 Any reference to a statute or statutory provision includes any subordinate legislation made under it, and any amendment, consolidation, re-enactment or replacement of the same.

**2. Supply of Services**

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Deliverables in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Supplier shall perform all services under this Agreement with reasonable skill, care and diligence and in accordance with generally accepted professional standards.

**3. Payment Terms**

3.1 The Customer shall pay the fees for the Deliverables as set out in Schedule B.

3.2 Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears and are payable within 30 days of the date of invoice.

3.3 Without prejudice to any other rights or remedies, the Customer may withhold payment of any amount properly in dispute.

**4. Confidentiality**

4.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement, whether oral, written or electronic (the *Confidential Information*).

4.2 The obligations under Clause 4.1 shall not apply to information which:

(a) is or comes into the public domain otherwise than through breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure;

(c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information; or

(d) is required to be disclosed by law or any regulatory authority.

4.3 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three years.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the address or email address set out in this Agreement.

5.2 A notice shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class pre-paid post, 48 hours after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a delivery receipt is obtained.

**6. Termination**

6.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remediable; or

(b) fails to remedy a material breach within 30 days of receiving notice requiring remedy.

6.2 **Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days’ written notice to the other Party if the other Party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the terminating Party. For the purposes of this Clause 14.2, the counterparty is Eskdale Systems Ltd.**

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
