# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF IVYBRIDGE ENERGY PLC

**Interpretation**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:
   (a) the **"Company"** means Ivybridge Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14353855;
   (b) the **"Buyer"** means Elstree Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14978499;
   (c) the **"Seller"** means Calloway Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10188093;
   (d) **"material customer"** refers to Saltash Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14576618;
   (e) references to a "person" include any individual, company, corporation, partnership, firm, unincorporated body, or other legal entity.

**Share Capital and Variation of Rights**

2. The issued share capital of the Company shall consist of ordinary shares of £1.00 each. The rights attaching to the shares shall be as determined by ordinary resolution of the Company, subject to the Companies Act 2006.

3. The Company may by ordinary resolution reduce its share capital in any manner permitted by law.

**Transfer of Shares**

4. Subject to any restrictions imposed by the Company's constitutional documents or applicable law, any member may transfer all or any of its shares by instrument in writing in the usual or common form or such other form as the Board may approve.

5. The transfer of any share shall be effected by a written instrument of transfer signed by or on behalf of the transferor and the transferee.

**Directors**

6. The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board of Directors, who may exercise all the powers of the Company except such as are by the Companies Act 2006 or by these Articles required to be exercised by the members.

7. The Board may from time to time appoint any individual to serve as a director, subject to such terms and conditions as the Board may determine.

8. A director may be removed by ordinary resolution, notwithstanding anything in any service contract between the Company and such director.

9. The Board may appoint one of its members to serve as Chairman, who shall preside at all meetings of the Board.

10. The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter to consider the affairs of the Company.

11. The quorum for board meetings is three directors. No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the Board unless the quorum is present at the commencement of business.

**Dividends and Distributions**

12. Subject to the Companies Act 2006, the Company may by ordinary resolution declare dividends, provided that no dividend shall exceed the amount recommended by the Board.

13. The Company may make distributions to shareholders in accordance with applicable law and these Articles.

**Payment Provisions**

14. Any payment due under or in connection with any transaction involving the Company, including but not limited to the acquisition of shares or assets, shall be made in sterling to such account as designated in writing by the recipient.

**Notices**

15. Any notice required to be given to a member, director or other party under these Articles shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class post, or transmitted by email to the address or email address last known to the Company.

16. A notice shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting or, if sent by email, at the time of transmission.

**Confidentiality**

17. All directors, officers, and employees of the Company shall maintain the confidentiality of all commercial, financial, technical, and operational information disclosed to them in the course of their duties, except where disclosure is required by law or authorised in writing by the Board.

18. The obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination of office or employment and shall continue indefinitely.

**General Provisions**

19. These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.

20. The Company shall maintain its registered office at 159 Fleet Street, London, as required by the Companies Act 2006.
