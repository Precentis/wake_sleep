4.1. Review of proposed transaction with Elstree Marine Ltd  
4.2. Approval of entry into the Share Purchase Agreement  
4.3. Any other business  
4.4. Adjournment  

**5. Review of Proposed Transaction with Elstree Marine Ltd**  
The board reviewed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Elstree Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14978499, whose registered office is at 48 Old Broad Street, London. The transaction is to be executed by way of a Share Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) between Calloway Media Ltd, the Seller, and Elstree Marine Ltd, the Buyer.  

It was noted that Calloway Media Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10188093, with its registered office at 4 Fleet Street, London.  

The board received a summary from the Company’s legal advisers regarding the terms of the Agreement, including the transfer of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company, which is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14353855 and has its registered office at 159 Fleet Street, London.  

**6. Approval of Entry into the Share Purchase Agreement**  
The board considered whether to approve the Company’s cooperation in the transaction and the release of relevant corporate records and disclosures.  

After discussion, it was:  

**RESOLVED** that the Company approve, in accordance with its obligations as a subsidiary party to the transaction, the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement between Calloway Media Ltd and Elstree Marine Ltd, and that the Company’s directors be, and hereby are, authorised to take all necessary steps to facilitate completion, including the transfer of corporate documents, execution of ancillary disclosures, and cooperation with due diligence processes.  

It was further confirmed that the transaction does not require shareholder approval at this stage and that all necessary corporate consents from the Company’s side are being obtained in due course.  

**7. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.
