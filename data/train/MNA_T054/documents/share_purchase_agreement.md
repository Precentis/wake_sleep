# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 9 February 2027.

**PARTIES**

(1) Elstree Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14978499, whose registered office is at 48 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Calloway Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10188093, whose registered office is at 4 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of all the issued share capital of Ivybridge Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14353855 and registered office at 159 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1** In this Agreement, 'Material Adverse Change' means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

## 2. SALE AND PURCHASE

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to acquire, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total purchase price of $165,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), subject to the terms of this Agreement.

## 3. CLOSING

The obligations of the parties under this Agreement shall be performed at the closing (the "Closing"), which shall take place on 2 April 2027 (the "Closing Date"). At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all necessary instruments of transfer, share certificates and board resolutions required to effect the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

## 4. CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

**4.5** If the Conditions (as defined in Schedule 2) are not satisfied or validly waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 9 February 2027), either party may elect to terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other party, whereupon this Agreement shall terminate without liability, save for any accrued rights or obligations.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Seller hereby represents and warrants to the Buyer that, as at the date of this Agreement, the warranties set out in Schedule 3 are true, accurate and not misleading in all material respects.

## 6. INDEMNITIES

The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer and the Company against all losses, liabilities, costs and expenses arising out of or in connection with any breach of the warranties contained in Schedule 3.

## 7. PAYMENT MECHANISM

The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 2 April 2027 (the Closing Date).

## 8. NOTICES

Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other in writing from time to time. Notices shall be deemed received when delivered (if by hand), two business days after posting (if by post) or upon confirmation of receipt (if by email).

## 9. CLAIMS AND LIMITATIONS

**9.7** The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any claim under the warranties contained in Schedule 3 within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim. Failure to give such notice within this period shall bar the claim, save in cases of fraud.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, without prior written consent, disclose any information relating to the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby to any third party, except as required by law or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under obligations of confidentiality.

## 11. COSTS

Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with the negotiation, preparation and execution of this Agreement.

## 12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with the Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings in relation to its subject matter.

## 15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

**15.1** This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
