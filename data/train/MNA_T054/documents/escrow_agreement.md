ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 10 February 2027 (the “Effective Date”)  

between:  

(1) Elstree Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14978499, whose registered office is at 48 Old Broad Street, London (the “Buyer”);  

(2) Calloway Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10188093, whose registered office is at 4 Fleet Street, London (the “Seller”); and  

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 02657273, whose registered office is at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ (the “Escrow Agent”).  

WHEREAS:  

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 10 February 2027 (the “SPA”), pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Ivybridge Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14353855, whose registered office is at 159 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”).  

(B) In connection with the SPA, the parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase consideration shall be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.  

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  

“Claim Notice” means a written notification delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent setting out in reasonable detail the nature of a claim, the amount claimed, and the basis for such claim under the SPA.  

“Confidential Information” means any non-public information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, including financial, operational, or commercial information.  

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £4,250,000 held in an interest-bearing account by the Escrow Agent pursuant to clause 3.  

“SPA” has the meaning given in the recitals.  

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.  

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent  

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under the terms of this Agreement.  

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to hold, administer, and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms herein.  

3. Deposit  

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days of the Effective Date.  

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account at HSBC Bank plc. Any interest accrued shall form part of the Escrow Amount.  

4. Disbursements  

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Amount only upon receipt of joint written instructions from the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise required by a final award of arbitration under clause 12.  

5. Claims  

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by delivering a Claim Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.  

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.  

6. Acknowledgement by Escrow Agent  

6.1 The Escrow Agent acknowledges receipt of the Escrow Amount and agrees to comply with the terms of this Agreement.  

7. Confidentiality  

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from another party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.  

8. Notices  

8.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the addresses set out in the preamble.  

8.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, two Business Days after posting, or 24 hours after email transmission, provided no delivery failure is reported.  

9. Fees and Expenses  

9.1 The Escrow Agent’s fees and expenses in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by the Buyer and the Seller.  

10. Resignation and Removal  

10.1 The Escrow Agent may resign upon 30 days’ written notice.  

10.2 The Escrow Agent may be removed by joint written agreement of the Buyer and the Seller.  

11. Limitations of Liability  

11.1 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss arising from its good faith performance of its duties under this Agreement, except for gross negligence or wilful misconduct.  

12. Governing Law and Arbitration  

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.  

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in New York in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce.  

13. General  

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.  

13.2 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
