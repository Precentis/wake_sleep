SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 20 December 2025.

Parties:

(1) Arden Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14142805, whose registered office is at 80 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Foxley Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14540473, whose registered office is at 51 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

Recitals:

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Lowther Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11089899, whose registered office is at 5 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 18 March 2026.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

1.3 References to clauses and schedules are to clauses and schedules of this Agreement.

1.4 References to any person include any individual, company, corporation, partnership, association, joint venture, trust, unincorporated organisation, governmental or regulatory authority or agency, or any other entity, whether or not having separate legal personality.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances, for a total consideration of US dollars two hundred and forty million (US$240,000,000) (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller no later than 3:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

2.3 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors at 80 Ludgate Hill, London, on the Closing Date, or at such other place, date and time as the parties may agree in writing.

3. Completion

3.1 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver or procure the delivery of the following to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of all the shares in the capital of the Company, properly stamped where required;

   (b) share certificates representing the shares in the capital of the Company, or in default, an indemnity in respect of lost certificates in form reasonably acceptable to the Buyer;

   (c) a certificate of good standing in respect of the Company, issued not more than ten (10) Business Days prior to the Closing Date;

   (d) resignations, effective as of the Closing Date, of all directors and officers of the Company who are not required for ongoing operations, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

   (e) such other documents and instruments as the Buyer may reasonably require to give full effect to this Agreement.

9. Warranties and Indemnities

9.7 If the Buyer becomes aware of any matter, circumstance or condition giving rise to a claim under any warranty or indemnity contained in this Agreement, it shall notify the Seller in writing of such claim within 30 days of becoming aware of such matter, circumstance or condition, failing which the claim shall be forfeited.

10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby (the "Confidential Information"), and shall not disclose such Confidential Information to any third party except:

   (a) to its professional advisers, officers, directors or employees on a need-to-know basis, provided they are bound by obligations of confidentiality;

   (b) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

   (c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

10.2 The obligations under this Clause 10 shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party.

15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).
