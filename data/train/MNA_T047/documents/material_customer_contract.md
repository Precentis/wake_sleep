“Confidential Information” has the meaning given in Clause 10;  
“Effective Date” means 12 January 2026;  
“Price” means the price for the Components as specified in Schedule 2.  

2. Supply of Components  
2.1 The Company shall supply, and the Buyer shall purchase, the engineering components (“Components”) in the quantities, specifications and delivery schedule set out in Schedule 1.  
2.2 All deliveries shall be made to the Buyer’s premises at 177 Cannon Street, London, or to such other location as the Buyer may designate in writing.  

3. Payment  
3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Price for the Components within thirty (30) days of the date of the Company’s invoice.  
3.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) by electronic transfer to the bank account designated in writing by the Company.  
3.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 8% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis and being compounded monthly.  

4. Quality Assurance  
4.1 The Company warrants that the Components shall conform to the specifications set out in Schedule 1 and shall be free from defects in design, materials and workmanship for a period of 24 months from delivery.  
4.2 The Buyer may reject any Component found to be non-conforming within 14 days of delivery, and the Company shall, at its own cost, replace or remedy the defect without delay.  

5. Intellectual Property  
5.1 All intellectual property rights in or arising from the Components shall vest exclusively in the Buyer upon delivery and payment in full.  
5.2 The Company grants to the Buyer a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use any background intellectual property necessary to exploit the Components as intended under this Agreement.  

6. Limitation of Liability  
6.1 Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential or special damages arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.  
6.2 The Company’s total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed 150% of the aggregate Price paid by the Buyer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.  

7. Term and Termination  
7.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause 7.  
7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:  
(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not cured within 30 days of receiving written notice;  
(b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, or has a receiver or administrator appointed; or  
(c) undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the other party.  

14. Miscellaneous  
14.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the receiving party as set out in the preamble.  
14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of Kestrel Engineering Ltd.  
14.3 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations or understandings, whether written or oral.  
14.4 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.  
14.5 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

10.2 The obligations in this Clause 10 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.
