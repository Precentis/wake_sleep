ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 12 January 2026 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Foxley Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14540473, whose registered office is at 51 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Arden Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14142805, whose registered office is at 80 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, whose principal office is at [Address of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "SPA") for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Lowther Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11089899, whose registered office is at 5 Fleet Street, London (the "Company"), from the Seller.

(B) In connection with the SPA, a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller is to be deposited into an escrow account to secure potential indemnity and warranty claims, and the Parties have agreed to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer such funds on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 18 March 2026.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of GBP 2,750,000, representing a portion of the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under the SPA, which has been deposited by the Buyer into an account held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement.

"Parties" and "Party" have the meaning given in the preamble.

"SPA" means the share purchase agreement dated on or about the Execution Date between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the Company.

"Written Notice" means a notice in writing delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email (provided that confirmation of receipt is obtained) to the addresses specified in Clause 10.

2. Escrow Deposit

2.1 The Buyer has delivered the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent, which shall be held in a designated interest-bearing client account in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount except in strict accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be held by the Escrow Agent as security for any claims properly made by the Buyer under the SPA.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

5. Claims Notification

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by submitting a Written Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Disputes

6.1 In the event of any dispute between the Buyer and the Seller concerning a claim against the Escrow Amount, the Escrow Agent shall not release any disputed portion of the Escrow Amount until such dispute is resolved in writing by both Parties or by decision of a competent authority.

10. Notices

10.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in Writing and sent to the registered office of the relevant Party.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce, seated in New York, and conducted in the English language.
