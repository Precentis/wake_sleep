# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (EXTRACT)

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 15 June 2024 between:

(1) **Foxley Engineering Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number **10022566**, registered office at 62 King William Street, London) (the "Employer"); and  
(2) **the Founder** (the "Employee").

The parties agree as follows:

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, a subsidiary or holding company of that person, or another subsidiary of that holding company;  
(b) "Business" means the engineering design, development, and technical consultancy services carried out by the Company and its group as at the date of this Agreement;  
(c) "Client" means any person, firm, or corporation that has entered into a contract with the Employer or any member of the Group for the provision of goods or services at any time during the 12 months preceding the termination of the Employee's employment;  
(d) "Group" means the Employer and any of its Affiliates from time to time;  
(e) "Termination Date" means the date on which the Employee's employment with the Employer is terminated, whether with or without notice and whether lawfully or unlawfully.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

---

**2. Employment and Duties**

2.1 The Employer shall employ the Employee and the Employee shall serve the Employer as Founder and Principal Engineer.

2.2 The Employee shall perform such duties and hold such responsibilities as are normally associated with such position and as may be lawfully assigned by the board of directors of the Employer.

---

**3. Remuneration**

3.1 The Employee shall be entitled to a base salary of £85,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in such bonus schemes as the Employer may operate from time to time, subject to the terms and conditions of any such scheme.

---

**4. Confidentiality**

4.1 During the term of employment and thereafter, the Employee shall not, without prior written consent of the Employer, directly or indirectly disclose or use for any purpose any Confidential Information.

4.2 "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, trade secrets, client lists, pricing structures, technical data, software, business strategies, and information relating to the operations, finances, or personnel of the Employer or any member of the Group.

4.3 This obligation shall survive termination of employment regardless of how that termination arises.

---

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address of the relevant party set out in this Agreement or as otherwise notified.

5.2 Notices to the Employer shall be sent to: 62 King William Street, London, to the attention of the Company Secretary.

5.3 Notices to the Employee shall be sent to the last known residential address on file with the Employer.

---

**18. Non-Compete Obligation**

18.1 The Employee acknowledges that by reason of the Employee's role and access to Confidential Information, it is reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Employer and the Group that the Employee be subject to post-termination restrictions.

18.2 The Employee undertakes that, for a period of **18 months** following the Termination Date, the Employee shall not, whether directly or indirectly, and whether as principal, agent, consultant, employee, shareholder, or otherwise, carry on, be engaged in, concerned with, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the Business anywhere in England and Wales.

18.3 This restriction applies specifically, but without limitation, to any business providing engineering design, technical analysis, or consultancy services to clients such as **Dunmore Analytics Ltd** (company number **13690436**, registered office 53 Gresham Street, London).

---

**19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

19.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

19.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation.
