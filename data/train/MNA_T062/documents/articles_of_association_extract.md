# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF FOXLEY ENGINEERING LTD  
*(As amended and restated on 26 June 2026)*

**1. Interpretation**

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Delamere Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11195564, whose registered office is at 102 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
“Company” means Foxley Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10022566, whose registered office is at 62 King William Street, London;  
“Customer” means Dunmore Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13690436, whose registered office is at 53 Gresham Street, London;  
“Seller” means Eskdale Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11738008, whose registered office is at 24 Cannon Street, London;  
“Shares” means the issued share capital of the Company;  
“Transfer” means the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller to the Buyer pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 28 July 2026.

1.2 References to any statutory provision include any statutory modification, extension, re-enactment or replacement thereof, whether by statute or otherwise.

**2. Share Capital and Rights of Shares**

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company as at the date of these Articles amounts to £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

2.2 Each ordinary share carries the right to one vote at any general meeting of the Company and to participate equally in any dividends or distributions declared by the Company.

2.3 The Company shall not issue any new shares without the prior written consent of the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed, for a period of twelve (12) months following the completion of the Transfer.

**3. Board of Directors**

3.1 The Board shall consist of not fewer than two (2) and not more than five (5) directors, appointed in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles.

3.2 The powers of the Board may be exercised in accordance with these Articles and applicable law, including in relation to the management of the Company’s business and affairs.

3.3 A director may be removed from office by ordinary resolution of the shareholders, provided that such removal shall not affect any rights under any contract of employment.

**4. Meetings of the Board**

4.1 The Board may meet and adjourn its meetings and regulate its proceedings as it sees fit, subject to these Articles.

4.2 Notice of a board meeting shall be given not less than 48 hours prior to the meeting, unless all directors waive such notice in writing.

4.3 Resolutions in writing signed by all directors entitled to vote on a matter shall be as valid as if passed at a meeting of the Board duly convened and held.

**11. Quorum for Board Meetings**

11.1 The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.

11.2 If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the time appointed for the meeting, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other time and place as the Board may determine.

**5. Payment and Financial Provisions**

5.1 All payments due under any contract to which the Company is party shall be made in pounds sterling to a bank account designated in writing by the recipient.

5.2 The Company shall maintain audited financial records in accordance with the requirements of the Companies Act 2006 and applicable accounting standards.

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice required or permitted under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or sent by first-class post, pre-paid, or by email to the most recently notified address or email address.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be validly served if delivered to or sent to its registered office at 62 King William Street, London.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information relating to the business, financial condition, customers (including the Customer), suppliers and operations of the Company, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Buyer.

7.2 This obligation shall continue to apply for a period of five (5) years after cessation of office or employment.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
