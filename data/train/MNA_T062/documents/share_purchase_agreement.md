# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated **11 July 2026**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Eskdale Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11738008**, whose registered office is at **24 Cannon Street, London** (the “**Seller**”).

(2) **Delamere Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **11195564**, whose registered office is at **102 Queen Victoria Street, London** (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of **Foxley Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10022566**, whose registered office is at **62 King William Street, London** (the “**Company**”), on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED** as follows:

**1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including the Recitals and any schedules and appendices hereto;

“**Buyer**” has the meaning given to it in the preamble;

“**Closing**” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with this Agreement;

“**Closing Date**” means **11 October 2026**;

“**Closing Working Capital**” means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with GAAP and the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1;

“**EBITDA**” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in respect of the financial year ending 31 December 2026 (“**FY2026**”) in accordance with the accounting policies consistently applied by the Company;

“**Earn-out Payment**” has the meaning given in Section 3.4;

“**Party**” means a party to this Agreement and “**Parties**” means the Seller and the Buyer;

“**Purchase Price**” has the meaning given in Section 2.1;

“**Seller**” has the meaning given in the preamble;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company comprising 100 ordinary shares of £1 each;

“**Target Working Capital**” means **$2,000,000**.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect the construction or interpretation of this Agreement.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, the Shares, for a consideration of **$205,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in accordance with Section 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cash on the Closing Date by irrevocable wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$2,000,000**. If Closing Working Capital exceeds Target Working Capital, no upward adjustment shall be made.

**3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT**

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional consideration (the “**Earn-out Payment**”) equal to **2 times** the amount by which the Company’s FY2026 EBITDA exceeds **$4,000,000**, provided that the Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed **$5,000,000** in aggregate. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within 90 days following the final audited financial statements for FY2026, subject to the Buyer’s right to dispute such calculation in good faith.

**12. RESTRICTIONS ON SELLER**

12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of **4 years** following the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company as conducted on the Closing Date, anywhere in the United Kingdom.

**13. NOTICES**

13.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by fax, or sent by pre-paid first class post or express delivery service, to the address of the relevant Party set out in the preamble, or such other address as may be notified in writing.

**14. CONFIDENTIALITY**

14.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party or the Company in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

15.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
