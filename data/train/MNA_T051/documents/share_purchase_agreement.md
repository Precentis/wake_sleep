SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 16 February 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15694794, whose registered office is at 159 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Ivybridge Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11697157, whose registered office is at 32 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Delamere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15149332 and whose registered office is at 152 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions have the following meanings:

"Closing Date" means 28 May 2026;

"Company" means Delamere Technologies Ltd, company number 15149332;

"Purchase Price" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

"Seller" means Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, company number 15694794;

"Buyer" means Ivybridge Medical Ltd, company number 11697157.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase all of the issued shares in the Company from the Seller for a total consideration of $220,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in full on the Closing Date in cleared funds by bank transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. COMPLETION

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, when the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer the share transfer documents, share certificates and such other documents as are reasonably required to vest legal and beneficial ownership of all of the shares in the Company in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as a party may from time to time notify to the other in accordance with this Clause.

4.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been received at the time of delivery if delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by first class pre-paid post, or upon transmission if sent by email, provided that if a notice is sent by email, confirmation of receipt is returned within 24 hours.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that as at the date of this Agreement and as at the Closing Date, the warranties set out in Schedule 1 are true and accurate in all respects.

9. INDEMNITIES AND CLAIMS

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under or in connection with any breach of warranty shall not exceed an amount equal to 20% of the Purchase Price, being $39,600,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $220,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

12. RESTRICTIONS ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller undertakes that for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

13.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
