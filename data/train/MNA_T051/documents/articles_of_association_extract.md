# ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF DELAMERE TECHNOLOGIES LTD  
*(As amended and in force on 28 February 2026)*

**1. Interpretation**  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Buyer” means Ivybridge Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11697157, whose registered office is at 32 Gresham Street, London;  
(b) “Seller” means Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15694794, whose registered office is at 159 Cheapside, London;  
(c) “Company” means Delamere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15149332, whose registered office is at 152 Bishopsgate, London;  
(d) “Holloway Technologies” means Holloway Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11023949, whose registered office is at 130 Cheapside, London;  
(e) any reference to a statutory provision includes any statutory modification or re-enactment of it, howsoever made.  

**2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights**  
2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The Company shall not issue any preference shares unless approved by a special resolution passed by shareholders holding not less than 75% of the voting rights.  

**3. Transfer of Shares**  
3.1 No share shall be transferred to any person other than Ivybridge Medical Ltd or a wholly owned subsidiary thereof without the prior written consent of the board of directors.  
3.2 Any transfer of shares in the Company shall be effected by means of a stock transfer form duly completed and delivered to the Company’s registered office.  

**4. Directors**  
4.1 The board of directors shall consist of no fewer than two and no more than five individuals appointed in accordance with these Articles.  
4.2 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution passed by shareholders holding a majority of the voting rights, provided that at least 21 days’ notice of such resolution is given to the Company.  

**5. Board Meetings and Proceedings**  
5.1 The board may conduct meetings in person, by video conference or by telephone, provided that all participants can hear each other in real time.  
5.2 Questions arising at a meeting of the board shall be decided by a majority of votes of directors present and entitled to vote.  
5.3 **Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.** No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the board unless the quorum is present at the time the question is put to vote. If at any time during a meeting the number of directors present falls below the quorum, the meeting shall adjourn automatically until such time as the quorum is restored.  

**6. Payment Obligations**  
6.1 All payments due to the Company under contracts with Holloway Technologies shall be made in cleared funds within 30 days of the date of invoice, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the board.  
6.2 No director shall authorise the waiver or deferral of any payment due from Holloway Technologies without the prior approval of a resolution passed by shareholders holding at least 60% of the voting rights.  

**7. Notices**  
7.1 Any notice to be given to the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to its registered office at 152 Bishopsgate, London.  
7.2 Notices to shareholders shall be sent to the addresses last known to the Company and shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting by first-class post.  

**8. Confidentiality**  
8.1 Directors, officers and employees of the Company shall not disclose any proprietary or commercially sensitive information concerning the Company, its customers or its operations to any third party without prior board approval.  
8.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of any directorship, employment or contractual relationship with the Company for a period of five years.  

**9. General Provisions**  
9.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
