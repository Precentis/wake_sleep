MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DELAMERE TECHNOLOGIES LTD  
HELD ON 1 MARCH 2026  

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The meeting of the board of directors of Delamere Technologies Ltd (the “Company”), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15149332 and having its registered office at 152 Bishopsgate, London, was convened and held on 1 March 2026 at 10:00 a.m. at the Company’s registered office.  

2. ATTENDANCE  
The following directors were present:  
- Ms. Eleanor Hartley  
- Mr. Julian Pike  

There are five directors on the board of the Company. The attendance of 2 directors constituted a quorum for the purposes of this meeting. The meeting was quorate.  

3. AGENDA ITEM 1: MATTERS ARISING FROM THE PREVIOUS MEETING  
No matters arose from the previous board meeting.  

4. AGENDA ITEM 2: PRESENTATION OF DRAFT SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
The Company Secretary presented a draft of the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 28 February 2026 between Oakhurst Robotics Ltd (the “Seller”), Ivybridge Medical Ltd (the “Buyer”), and the Company. The Buyer is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11697157 and has its registered office at 32 Gresham Street, London. The Seller is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15694794 and has its registered office at 159 Cheapside, London.  

5. AGENDA ITEM 3: DISCUSSION OF PROPOSED TRANSACTION  
The directors reviewed the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, pursuant to which the Seller proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer. The directors considered the implications of the proposed transaction for the Company, including its employees, operations, and ongoing contractual relationships.  

6. AGENDA ITEM 4: MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP  
The board noted that Holloway Technologies Ltd, a customer of the Company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11023949 with its registered office at 130 Cheapside, London, accounts for 37.5% of the Company’s total annual revenue over the 15-month period ending 30 November 2025. The board acknowledged the significance of this relationship and discussed steps to ensure continuity post-completion.  

7. AGENDA ITEM 5: RESOLUTION ON SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
Following discussion, and having considered all relevant factors, including the financial and operational impact of the transaction, the board resolved as follows:  

**RESOLVED** that, pursuant to the powers conferred upon the board of directors of the Company, the Company hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 28 February 2026 between the Seller and the Buyer, and authorises the Company Secretary to take all necessary steps to execute and deliver the agreement on behalf of the Company, including affixing the common seal of the Company in accordance with section 43 of the Companies Act 2006, as required.  

8. ADJOURNMENT  
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:20 a.m.
