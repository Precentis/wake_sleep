ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Ivybridge Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11697157, whose registered office is at 32 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Oakhurst Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15694794, whose registered office is at 159 Cheapside, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of professional trustees, in its capacity as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has acquired, pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof, the entire issued share capital of Delamere Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15149332, whose registered office is at 152 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company"), from the Seller.

(B) As part of the consideration for the sale, an amount (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure certain indemnification rights of the Buyer under the share purchase agreement.

(C) The Parties have agreed upon the terms on which the Escrow Amount is to be held, administered and released.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 28 May 2026.

"Claim" means any notification by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a right to recover from the Escrow Amount under the terms of the share purchase agreement.

"Material Customer" means Holloway Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11023949, whose registered office is at 130 Cheapside, London.

"Release Date" means the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within five Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount except in accordance with Clause 4.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the Release Date, save to the extent of any pending Claims notified in accordance with Clause 5.2 prior to the Release Date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim shall remain held by the Escrow Agent until such Claim is finally resolved, waived or withdrawn.

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount only in accordance with the terms of the share purchase agreement and this Agreement.

5.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the Claim.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, two Business Days after posting, or upon transmission if sent by email (with confirmation of receipt).

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and shall not disclose them to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
