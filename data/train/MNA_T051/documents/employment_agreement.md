2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

3.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any bonus scheme established by the Company, at the sole discretion of the board of directors.

18. Non-Compete

18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by reason of their role and access to confidential information, the restrictions set out in this clause are reasonably necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company, the Seller, and the Buyer.

18.2 During the period of 12 months immediately following the Termination Date, the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person or entity, engage or be concerned in any business which carries on or is engaged in activities that are in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the Termination Date.

18.3 For the purposes of this clause, “business” includes any entity, partnership, sole trader or corporate body engaged in the development, sale or support of medical diagnostic software, including entities providing services to customers such as Holloway Technologies Ltd.

18.4 This restriction applies within a radius of 50 miles from the Company’s registered office and extends to any customer of the Company with whom the Founder had material contact during the 12 months prior to the Termination Date.
