**ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF GALLOWAY MEDIA LTD**  
(Extract – As in force prior to 28 September 2026)

The following is an authorised extract from the Articles of Association of Galloway Media Ltd, a private company limited by shares incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14506852, with registered office at 135 Moorgate, London.

**1. Interpretation**

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
(b) "Company" means Galloway Media Ltd;  
(c) "Director" means a director of the Company;  
(d) "Ordinary Resolution" means a resolution passed by a simple majority of the votes cast by or on behalf of members entitled to vote at a general meeting;  
(e) "Secretary" means any person appointed by the Board to perform the duties of secretary of the Company;  
(f) "Writing" includes any form of non-transient electronic or digital communication capable of being reproduced in hard copy.

**2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights**

2.1 The Company's share capital consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The rights attached to any class of shares may only be varied with the consent in writing of the holders of not less than 75% in nominal value of the issued shares of that class.

**3. Transfer of Shares**

3.1 Subject to these Articles, any member may transfer all or any of the member’s shares by instrument in writing in any usual or common form or in such other form as the Board may approve.  
3.2 No transfer of shares shall be registered unless accompanied by the relevant share certificate and such evidence of title and compliance with applicable restrictions as the Board may reasonably require.

**4. Directors**

4.1 The Board shall manage the business of the Company and may exercise all the powers of the Company unless by statute or by the Articles or by resolutions of the members, a particular power is required to be exercised by the members.  
4.2 The number of Directors shall not be less than two nor more than six.  
4.3 A Director may be appointed by Ordinary Resolution of the members.  
4.4 A Director may be removed by Ordinary Resolution of the members, notwithstanding anything in any service contract.

**5. Proceedings of the Board**

5.1 The Board may regulate its own proceedings as it deems fit, including the appointment of a chairman and the frequency and location of meetings.  
5.2 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes. In the case of equality of votes, the chairman of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.  
5.3 A Director may participate in a meeting of the Board by means of a conference call or other communication equipment allowing all persons participating to hear each other, and such participation shall constitute presence in person at the meeting.

**6. Quorum**

6.1 Article 11: The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.

**7. Payment**

7.1 Any payment due under any agreement to which the Company is a party shall be made in sterling, by cleared funds, to such bank account as the recipient may designate in writing, unless otherwise agreed in Writing between the parties.  
7.2 All payments shall be made no later than 30 days following the date of a valid invoice.

**8. Notices**

8.1 Any notice to be given to a member, Director or the Company under these Articles shall be in Writing and may be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the last known address, email address, or registered office of the relevant party.  
8.2 Notices to the Company shall be validly served if delivered to its registered office at 135 Moorgate, London.  
8.3 A notice shall be deemed served 48 hours after transmission by email, or 48 hours after posting, unless received earlier.

**9. Confidentiality**

9.1 Every Director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the Company’s business, operations, financial affairs, and relationships with third parties, including but not limited to contracts with Vantage Holdings Ltd (company number 15577764), and shall not use such information for personal benefit or disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.  
9.2 The obligations under this Article 9 shall survive the termination of any appointment or office.

**10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

10.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
10.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
