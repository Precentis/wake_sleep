## Approval of Share Purchase Agreement
4. The board considered the draft Share Purchase Agreement (the "SPA") dated 26 September 2026 between Foxley Energy plc (the Seller), Lowther Foods Ltd (the Buyer) and Galloway Media Ltd (the Company) pursuant to which the Buyer will acquire the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller.

   The board reviewed the terms of the SPA, including representations, warranties, covenants, and post-completion obligations. Legal counsel provided by Harlowe & Co. Solicitors advised on the implications and confirmations required under English law.

   It was confirmed that the Seller, Foxley Energy plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10441381, registered office: 138 King William Street, London), holds 100% of the issued shares in the Company. The Buyer, Lowther Foods Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13060157, registered office: 173 Moorgate, London), intends to complete the acquisition on or before 30 September 2026, subject to satisfaction of conditions precedent.

   The board acknowledged that the transaction is governed by English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

   RESOLVED that the board of Galloway Media Ltd approve the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement and that the Company Secretary, Ms. J. L. Moss, be authorised to execute the SPA on behalf of the Company as required.

## Legal Correspondence
5. The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 25 September 2026 from Pemberton Energy plc, addressed to the Company, threatening proceedings for approximately $2,750,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a contract for services dated 14 March 2024.

   The board received a summary from management regarding the substance of the claim and current status. It was confirmed that no formal proceedings had been issued as of the date of the meeting.

   RESOLVED that management continue to monitor the matter closely and report to the board on any developments, including any formal claim, within seven days of receipt.

## Adjournment
6. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
