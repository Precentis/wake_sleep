**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **21 September 2026**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Foxley Energy plc**, incorporated in England and Wales, company number **10441381**, whose registered office is at **138 King William Street, London** (the “**Seller**”).

(2) **Lowther Foods Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales, company number **13060157**, whose registered office is at **173 Moorgate, London** (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Galloway Media Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales, company number **14506852**, whose registered office is at **135 Moorgate, London** (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer and the Seller have agreed that the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “**Closing**” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   “**Closing Date**” means **14 December 2026**;

   “**Conditions**” means the conditions specified in Clause 4;

   “**Confidential Information**” means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement;

   “**General Warranties**” means the warranties set out in Schedule 1, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

   “**Fundamental Warranties**” means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as set out in Schedule 1;

   “**Purchase Price**” means **$195,000,000**;

   “**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to clauses and schedules are to clauses and schedules of this Agreement.

**2. Sale and Purchase**

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for the **Purchase Price of $195,000,000**.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Closing**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the **Closing Date** at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee; (b) resignations of the existing directors and secretaries of the Company; and (c) such other documents as are reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares.

**4. Conditions**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are conditional upon the satisfaction or valid waiver of the Conditions.

4.2 Each party shall use all reasonable endeavours to procure the fulfilment of the Conditions.

4.3 The Conditions include, but are not limited to: (a) the receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals; (b) the accuracy of the representations and warranties made by the other party; and (c) the absence of a material adverse effect on the Company.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling **120 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 20 January 2027)**, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

**5. Payment**

5.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full on the **Closing Date** by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such account as the Seller shall designate in writing not less than five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office or email address of the recipient.

6.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 138 King William Street, London, and to the Buyer: 173 Moorgate, London, or such other address as either party may designate in writing.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party other than its professional advisers or as required by law.

**10. Survival**

10.1 The **General Warranties** shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months** from the Closing Date. The **Fundamental Warranties** shall survive for **48 months** from the Closing Date. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
