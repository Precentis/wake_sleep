**Disclosure Letter – Quayside Engineering Ltd Acquisition of Redcliffe Marine Ltd**

This Disclosure Letter is made on 28 December 2026  

**Between:**  
(1) Brightwater Analytics Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 15956871, whose registered office is at 30 Fleet Street, London) (the “Seller”); and  
(2) Quayside Engineering Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 11670781, whose registered office is at 87 Fleet Street, London) (the “Buyer”).

**Background**  
(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Redcliffe Marine Ltd (registered in England and Wales under company number 15381337, whose registered office is at 43 Cannon Street, London) (the “Company”) pursuant to the terms of a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date of this letter (the “SPA”).  
(B) Pursuant to the SPA, the Seller has given certain warranties regarding the business, assets, and affairs of the Company, including as to material contracts.  
(C) This letter sets out the disclosures against the Warranties contained in the SPA.  

**1. Interpretation**  
1.1 In this letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Warranty” means any of the representations and warranties set out in the SPA;  
(b) defined terms in the SPA have the same meaning when used in this letter;  
(c) a reference to a “clause” is to a clause of this letter.  

**2. Scope of Disclosure**  
2.1 The disclosures in this letter are made solely for the purposes of qualifying the Warranties in accordance with the terms of the SPA.  
2.2 No disclosure in this letter shall constitute an admission by the Seller of any liability, claim, or breach of duty, nor shall it be admissible in any proceeding between third parties.  
2.3 Each disclosure is to be read in conjunction with the others, but no disclosure shall be deemed to qualify any Warranty not expressly referenced unless such qualification arises by necessary implication.  

**3. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
3.1 The Seller discloses that:  
(a) The Company is party to a service agreement with Netherby Capital LLP (registered in England and Wales under company number 12772149, whose registered office is at 19 Gresham Street, London) dated 14 February 2021, for the provision of marine engineering consultancy services (the “Netherby Agreement”).  
(b) The Netherby Agreement requires the Company to maintain certain performance standards and includes a clause permitting termination by Netherby Capital LLP if the Company fails to meet defined service level metrics for two consecutive quarters.  
(c) No material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision.  

3.2 The disclosure under Clause 3.1 is complete and accurate as at the date of this letter. There are no other contracts, arrangements, or understandings between the Company and Netherby Capital LLP beyond those set out in the Netherby Agreement.  

**4. General Disclosure Provisions**  
4.1 The disclosures made in this letter are based on the actual knowledge of the directors and officers of the Seller as at the date of this letter.  
4.2 The Seller does not warrant the completeness or accuracy of any information provided by third parties to the Company.  

**5. Payment**  
5.1 Any adjustment to the purchase price arising under the SPA as a result of claims for breach of Warranty shall be made in accordance with the terms of the SPA.  

**6. Notices**  
6.1 Any notice to be given under this letter shall be in writing and delivered by hand or recorded delivery to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in Recital (1) or (2) above, or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.  

**7. Confidentiality**  
7.1 The Buyer shall treat this letter and its contents as confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law, regulation, or the rules of any applicable stock exchange, or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidence.  

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
8.1 This letter is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this letter.  

**9. Entire Agreement**  
9.1 This letter, together with the SPA, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the disclosures made by the Seller against the Warranties.
