1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this contract between the Company and the Customer, including all schedules and appendices;

“Company” means Redcliffe Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15381337, whose registered office is at 43 Cannon Street, London;

“Customer” means Netherby Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12772149, whose registered office is at 19 Gresham Street, London;

“Effective Date” means the date first written above;

“Services” means the marine engineering consultancy and design services to be provided by the Company to the Customer as described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to the same as amended, re-enacted or extended from time to time.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment and Scope of Services

2.1 The Company agrees to provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Company shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence and in accordance with generally accepted professional standards and practices.

3. Term

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

4. Payment

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule 2, payable in arrears on a monthly basis.

4.2 All sums payable under this Agreement shall be paid in pounds sterling within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 If any payment is overdue, the Company may suspend performance of the Services until such time as all outstanding amounts are paid in full.

5. Invoicing

5.1 The Company shall issue invoices for the Services on a monthly basis, detailing hours worked and expenses incurred (if applicable).

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.

6.2 This obligation shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party, or which is required to be disclosed by law.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the receiving party.

7.2 Notices shall be deemed received if delivered by hand: on delivery; by post: two (2) business days after posting; by email: one (1) business day after transmission, provided that no error message is received.

8. Termination

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:

(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of being notified;

(b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the Customer. For the purposes of this clause, a “change of control” shall include the acquisition of more than fifty-one per cent (51%) of the issued share capital of the Company by a third party, including but not limited to Quayside Engineering Ltd (company number 11670781).

9. Consequences of Termination

9.1 Upon termination of this Agreement, the Customer shall pay all outstanding fees for Services rendered up to the date of termination.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
