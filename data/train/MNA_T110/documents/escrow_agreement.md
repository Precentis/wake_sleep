ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 27 December 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and among:

(1) Brightwater Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15956871, whose registered office is at 30 Fleet Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Quayside Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11670781, whose registered office is at 87 Fleet Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) Netherby Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12772149, whose registered office is at 19 Gresham Street, London (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 25 December 2026 (the “SPA”) with the Seller pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Redcliffe Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15381337 and registered office at 43 Cannon Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Buyer and the Seller have agreed that an amount equal to 18.5% of the total consideration payable under the SPA (the “Escrow Amount”) shall be deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the Seller’s indemnification obligations under the SPA.

(C) The Parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Amount shall be held, administered and released by the Escrow Agent.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Claim Notice” means a written notification delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with Clause 5.1, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

“Effective Date” has the meaning set out in the preamble.

“Escrow Amount” has the meaning set out in Recital (B).

“SPA” has the meaning set out in Recital (A).

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days of the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a separate interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Account”) maintained with a recognised financial institution in London, in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount (including any accrued interest) to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date (the “Release Date”), provided no Claim Notice has been validly served prior to such date.

3.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount not subject to a pending claim at the time of release shall be promptly transferred to the Seller by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller.

4. Payment Instructions

4.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made by same-day value wire transfer in immediately available funds to such bank account as the recipient may designate in writing.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may serve a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent at any time prior to the Release Date.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the amount specified in the Claim Notice (the “Disputed Sum”) until the earliest of: (a) written joint instructions from the Buyer and the Seller; (b) a final determination by a court of competent jurisdiction; or (c) a written direction from the Escrow Agent’s legal counsel that release is required under applicable law.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the address or email set out in the preamble or such other address as a Party may notify in writing.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Buyer and the Seller.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
