SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 20 December 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Quayside Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11670781, whose registered office is at 87 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Brightwater Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15956871, whose registered office is at 30 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

(4) The Company referred to in this Agreement is Redcliffe Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15381337, whose registered office is at 43 Cannon Street, London.

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals, schedules and appendices;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 15 February 2027;

   "Company" means Redcliffe Marine Ltd, company number 15381337;

   "Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   "Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Clause 8.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of $185,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in full in cleared funds on the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

   (b) share certificates representing the Shares, together with such other documents as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title to the Shares;

   (c) a certificate of non-foreign status under Section 1445 of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, if applicable;

   (d) such board and shareholder resolutions of the Company as may be required to approve the transfer of the Shares.

3.3 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in this Agreement.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 87 Fleet Street, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 30 Fleet Street, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 The limitations and exclusions set out in this Clause 6 apply to every representation, warranty, undertaking, liability or obligation arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

9. WARRANTIES

9.2 (Limitation of Liability) The aggregate liability of the Seller for any and all claims arising under the Warranties shall not exceed an amount equal to 25% of the Purchase Price, being $37,000,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $185,000,000, as set out in Section 2.1.

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of the Warranties must be notified to the Seller in writing within 20 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

10.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
