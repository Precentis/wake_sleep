**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**

This Agreement is made on 15 March 2023

Between:

(1) Arden Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15583519 whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London (the "Supplier"); and

(2) Corrib Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15986741 whose registered office is at 40 Bishopsgate, London (the "Customer").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this contract between the Supplier and the Customer, including its schedules and any subsequent amendments made in writing and signed by both Parties;

"Commencement Date" means 15 March 2023;

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, visually or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably ought to be regarded as confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure;

"Control", when used in relation to a company, means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management or policies of a company, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise;

"Force Majeure Event" means any circumstance not within the reasonable control of the affected Party including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, fire, explosion, flood, drought, pandemic, epidemic, civil unrest, strikes, lock-outs, industrial dispute, interruption or failure of utilities, or any law, rule, regulation, or official action of any government or competent authority;

"Goods" means the products or materials supplied by the Supplier to the Customer under this Agreement from time to time;

"Term" means the initial period of this Agreement commencing on the Commencement Date and continuing for a period of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

**2. Supply of Goods**

2.1 The Supplier shall, during the Term, supply the Goods to the Customer in accordance with the specifications, quantities, and delivery schedules set out in Purchase Orders accepted in writing by the Supplier.

2.2 The Supplier warrants that all Goods supplied under this Agreement shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship and conform to the applicable specifications for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery.

**3. Payment**

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier within sixty (60) days of the date of receipt of a valid invoice issued in respect of Goods delivered.

3.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) to such bank account as the Supplier may designate in writing from time to time.

3.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4.5% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis from the due date until the date of actual payment, whether before or after judgment.

**4. Notices**

4.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the receiving Party set out in this Agreement or to such other address as that Party may have notified in writing.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received:

(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;

(b) if sent by first-class pre-paid post, two (2) Business Days after posting;

(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a delivery confirmation is received.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information disclosed by the other Party and shall not use such information for any purpose other than the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

5.2 The obligations in Clause 5.1 shall not apply to information which:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was known to the receiving Party on a non-confidential basis prior to disclosure;

(c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without reference to the disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

**14. Termination**

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either Party immediately upon written notice if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not capable of remedy, or (if capable of remedy) fails to remedy such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving notice requiring remedy;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation, administration, receivership or analogous proceedings, or makes any arrangement with its creditors.

14.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other Party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the avoidance of doubt, the counterparty in this Agreement is Corrib Foods Ltd.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
