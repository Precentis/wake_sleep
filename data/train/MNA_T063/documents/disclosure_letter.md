**2. Purpose and Scope**  
2.1 This Disclosure Letter is given pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) entered into between the Buyer and the Seller for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
2.2 The disclosures set out in this letter are made solely for the purpose of qualifying the Warranties given by the Seller under the Agreement and do not constitute admissions or acknowledgements by the Seller of any liability or obligation beyond the terms of the Agreement.  
2.3 Nothing in this Disclosure Letter shall operate as a waiver of any right or remedy available to the Seller under the Agreement or at law, except as expressly provided herein.

**3. Disclosure Against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**  
3.1 The Seller confirms that there is no pending, threatened or outstanding litigation, arbitration, administrative or regulatory proceeding or investigation of any nature, at law or in equity, to which the Company is a party or to which any of its assets are subject, and no such matter has been pending or threatened at any time during the three years prior to the date of this letter.  
3.2 For the purposes of Warranty 7.1, the Seller makes no qualification or exception and confirms that disclosure is made as follows:  
(a) Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation): None.

**4. Disclosure Against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
4.1 The Seller confirms that the Company is not a party to any material contract or arrangement which contains any provision requiring the consent, notification or approval of any third party in connection with a change of control of the Company.  
4.2 Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the Seller confirms that:  
(a) None of the Material Contracts to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision;  
(b) The Customer Agreement dated 15 March 2021 between the Company and Corrib Foods Ltd (company number 15986741, registered office 40 Bishopsgate, London), under which Corrib Foods Ltd accounts for 24% of the Company’s annual revenue, does not contain any such provision;  
(c) No other contract or arrangement to which the Company is a party and which constitutes a Material Contract under the Agreement contains any obligation, restriction or condition triggered by a change of control.  
4.3 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3, the Seller makes the following disclosure:  
(a) Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts): no material contract contains a change of control provision.

**5. Payment and Consideration**  
5.1 The Seller acknowledges that the purchase price for the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be paid in accordance with the terms of the Agreement, without deduction, set-off or counterclaim, save as expressly permitted thereunder.  
5.2 All payments made pursuant to the Agreement shall be made in cleared funds to such bank account as may be notified in writing by the Seller to the Buyer not less than five Business Days prior to the relevant payment date.

6.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in the Agreement.  
6.2 Notices shall be deemed served in accordance with the provisions of the Agreement.
