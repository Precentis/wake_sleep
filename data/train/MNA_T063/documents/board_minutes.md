# MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF ARDEN INDUSTRIES LTD

**Date of Meeting:** 3 May 2026

**Location:** 64 Old Broad Street, London, EC2M 1S5 (Registered Office)  
**Chairman:** Mr. Timothy Greaves  
**Secretary:** Ms. Eleanor Vance  

**In attendance:**  
- Mr. Timothy Greaves (Director)

**1. Call to Order**  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The Secretary confirmed that notice of the meeting had been duly given in accordance with the Articles of Association.

**2. Apologies and Absences**  
No apologies were received.

**3. Confirmation of Previous Minutes**  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 4 April 2026 were reviewed. Upon a motion by Ms. Vance and seconded by Mr. Hart, the minutes were confirmed as a true and accurate record and signed by the Chairman.

**4. Update on Proposed Acquisition by Upton Pharma plc**  
The board received a report from external legal counsel regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Arden Industries Ltd by Upton Pharma plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11926407, registered office 97 King William Street, London) from Calloway Foods Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13043097, registered office 17 King William Street, London).  

The board noted that the draft share purchase agreement was in advanced negotiation and that due diligence by the Buyer was ongoing. All directors confirmed their support for the transaction, which is expected to complete on or before 30 June 2026, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.  

The board acknowledged that Corrib Foods Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15986741, registered office 40 Bishopsgate, London) remains a material customer, representing 38% of the Company’s total revenue over the 12-month period ending 31 March 2026.

**5. Legal and Compliance Matters**  
The board noted a letter before action dated 29 April 2026 from Juniper Capital LLP, solicitors, threatening proceedings against Arden Industries Ltd for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply contract dated 17 January 2024. The Finance Director confirmed that the matter is under internal review and that the Company denies liability. The board directed management to monitor the position closely and report back at the next scheduled meeting. Legal counsel has been instructed to prepare a formal response in accordance with English law and the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**6. Financial Performance – Q1 2026**  
The Finance Director presented unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 31 March 2026. Revenue was reported at £8.7 million, with gross profit of £2.9 million. Net profit after tax was £1.1 million. The board noted these results were in line with forecast, though subject to the pending dispute referenced in Item 5.

**7. Any Other Business**  
No other matters were raised.

**8. Adjournment**  
The meeting concluded at 11:20 a.m.
