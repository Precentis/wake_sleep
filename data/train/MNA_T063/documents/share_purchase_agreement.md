SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 18 April 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) UPTON PHARMA PLC, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11926407, whose registered office is at 97 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) CALLAWAY FOODS LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13043097, whose registered office is at 17 King William Street, London (the "Seller").

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

BACKGROUND:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Arden Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15583519, whose registered office is at 64 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 28 May 2026;

"Confidential Information" means all information (whether oral, written or in any other form) relating to the business, operations, assets, liabilities or affairs of any Party or any of its subsidiaries or associated persons;

"Parties" means the Buyer and the Seller, and "Party" means either of them;

"Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $165,000,000, to be satisfied in full in cash on the Closing Date, payable by irrevocable wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other place as the Parties may agree in writing. At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer such instruments of transfer, share certificates and board resolutions as may be required to vest legal and beneficial title to the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket) The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer for any claim under this Agreement unless the aggregate of all claims exceeds $1,650,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For the avoidance of doubt, no individual claim or series of related claims having a value of less than $1,000,000 shall be taken into account in assessing whether the threshold has been met, and liability shall only arise to the extent of the excess above $1,650,000.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its officers, employees, agents and advisers shall not, at any time during the term of this Agreement or thereafter, disclose to any person any Confidential Information, except:

(a) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

(b) with the prior written consent of the other Party;

(c) where such information is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this Clause 10.
