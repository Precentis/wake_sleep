ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 December 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Delamere Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11978152, whose registered office is at 63 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Orwell Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10486107, whose registered office is at 22 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a firm authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Background:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about 15 December 2026 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Brightwater Bioscience plc.

(B) Brightwater Bioscience plc is a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10693997, whose registered office is at 28 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(C) In connection with the SPA, the parties have agreed that a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller shall be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

(D) The Escrow Agent has agreed to accept and hold the Escrow Amount, and to disburse it in accordance with the terms set forth herein.

Now, therefore, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Claim" means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a liability arising under the SPA.

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, including financial, operational, and commercial details, except where such information is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £2,350,000 (two million three hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 7.5% of the total consideration under the SPA, which has been deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the date hereof.

"Material Customer" means Quayside Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13583396, whose registered office is at 152 Queen Victoria Street, London.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated account in sterling and to administer and disburse it in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the terms herein.

3. Payment and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent on the Effective Date.

3.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held in a designated interest-bearing account, and any interest accrued shall form part of the Escrow Amount and be subject to the same terms.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email as notified in writing.

4.2 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to its principal place of business or as otherwise specified in writing by it.

5. Claims and Disbursement

5.1 The Escrow Amount shall be held for a period of 18 months from the Effective Date (the "Escrow Period") as security for the obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim, the Escrow Agent shall promptly notify the Seller and shall not disburse any portion of the Escrow Amount in respect of such Claim unless and until:

(a) the Seller consents in writing to such disbursement; or

(b) a final binding determination is made by a court of competent jurisdiction or by arbitration under the terms of the SPA.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and any Confidential Information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations; or

(b) as required by law or regulatory authority.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
