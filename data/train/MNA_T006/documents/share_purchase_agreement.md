**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 27 November 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Orwell Energy plc**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10486107, whose registered office is at 22 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Delamere Energy plc**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11978152, whose registered office is at 63 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Brightwater Bioscience plc**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10693997, whose registered office is at 28 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer has agreed to purchase, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 15 March 2027;

"Confidential Information" means all information (whether written, oral or in any other form) disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement;

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Tax" or "Taxes" has the meaning given in the warranties relating to tax;

"Third Party" means any person, firm, company or entity other than the Parties to this Agreement.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Sale and Purchase**

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is **$65,000,000**, payable in immediately available funds at Closing.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer to the bank account specified in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. Closing**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates representing the Shares, if any;

(c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

(d) such corporate authorisations and documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to evidence the Seller’s authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

**9. Indemnities and Limitations**

9.4 **De Minimis and Basket**: The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds **$325,000** (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over that amount.

9.7 **Notification of Warranty Claims**: The Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of any claim for breach of warranty within **30 days** of becoming aware of the relevant facts giving rise to the claim, failing which the claim shall be barred.

**10. Survival and Time for Claims**

10.1 **Survival**: The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **72 months**. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period, failing which the relevant warranty shall cease to have effect and no claim may be brought in respect of it.

**11. Confidentiality**

11.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its employees, agents and advisers shall not, at any time during or after the term of this Agreement, disclose to any Third Party or use for any purpose other than the performance of its obligations under this Agreement any Confidential Information, except:

(a) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

(b) with the prior written consent of the other Party;

(c) where such information is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this Clause.

**12. Notices**

12.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above.

12.2 A notice shall be deemed served at the time of delivery if delivered personally, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or 12 hours after transmission if sent by email.

**13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
