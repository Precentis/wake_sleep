# EXTRACT OF ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF DUNMORE RETAIL GROUP PLC

**Interpretation**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:
   (a) ‘Act’ means the Companies Act 2006, as amended or re-enacted from time to time;
   (b) ‘Buyer’ means Pemberton Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10604458, whose registered office is at 140 Ludgate Hill, London;
   (c) ‘Company’ means Dunmore Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10706981, whose registered office is at 18 Moorgate, London;
   (d) ‘Seller’ means Brightwater Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12620703, whose registered office is at 89 Cannon Street, London;
   (e) ‘Whitlock Medical Ltd’ means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15192427, whose registered office is at 178 Old Broad Street, London;
   (f) ‘Board’ means the board of directors of the Company;
   (g) references to a ‘director’ include any person duly appointed as a director, alternate director, or shadow director of the Company;
   (h) references to ‘£’ or ‘pounds’ are to the lawful currency of the United Kingdom.

**Board Meetings**

2. The Board shall meet as frequently as necessary to discharge its duties, but at least four times in each calendar year.

3. The Chair of the Board may convene a meeting of the Board at any time by giving not less than 48 hours’ notice to each director.

4. Any director may call a meeting of the Board by giving not less than 24 hours’ notice to the other directors.

5. Notice of any board meeting may be given in writing, by electronic communication, or orally, provided that confirmation in writing or electronic form follows immediately thereafter.

6. A director shall be deemed to have received notice of a meeting if it was sent to the last known contact address, email address, or mobile number provided by that director to the Company.

7. Proceedings at a meeting of the Board shall not be invalidated by any defect in the appointment or notice to any director.

8. The Chair of the Board shall preside at all meetings of the Board at which they are present. In their absence, the directors present shall appoint one of their number to chair the meeting.

**Quorum**

9. Save as otherwise provided by these Articles or required by the Act, the quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be two directors.

10. No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the Board unless a quorum is present at the time the question is put to the vote.

11. The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

**Resolutions in Writing**

12. Any resolution approved in writing or by electronic means by all the directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting shall be as valid and effective as if it had been passed at a meeting of the Board duly convened and held.

13. A resolution in writing may be in one document or in several counterparts, and shall be deemed to be passed on the date on which the last director signs.

**Payment and Financial Matters**

14. All payments made by the Company exceeding £150,000 in a single transaction shall require prior written authorisation by two directors.

15. The Company shall maintain adequate accounting records in accordance with the Act and applicable accounting standards.

**Notices**

16. Any notice to be given to the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company at 18 Moorgate, London.

17. Any notice to be given to a director or shareholder shall be sufficiently given if delivered to or sent by first-class post or electronic means to their last known address, email, or contact details on record.

18. A notice shall be deemed to have been given 24 hours after transmission by electronic means, or two business days after posting.

**Confidentiality**

19. Every director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, operations, and financial affairs of the Company, including but not limited to customer relationships such as that with Whitlock Medical Ltd, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.

20. The obligations under this Article shall continue after a director or officer ceases to hold office.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

21. These Articles and all matters arising from them shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales.

22. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
