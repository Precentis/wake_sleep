ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] 2026 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Pemberton Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10604458, whose registered office is at 140 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Brightwater Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12620703, whose registered office is at 89 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number [●], whose registered office is at [●] (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10706981 and registered office at 18 Moorgate, London (the "Company"), pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof (the "Purchase Agreement").

(B) In connection with the acquisition, a portion of the consideration has been deposited into an escrow account to secure certain indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The parties have appointed the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount (as defined below) on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 27 November 2026.

"Claim Notice" means a written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 5.2 setting out in reasonable detail the nature of a claim, the basis for indemnification under the Purchase Agreement, and the amount claimed.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of [●] deposited by the Buyer into the Escrow Account pursuant to the Purchase Agreement.

"Escrow Account" means the interest-bearing account established by the Escrow Agent with [Name of Bank] under account number [●], into which the Escrow Amount has been paid.

"Purchase Agreement" has the meaning given in the Recitals.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on or prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account and shall not release any portion except in accordance with Clauses 4 or 5.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion as remains after deduction for pending claims, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending claims notified before that date. Any released funds shall be transferred by the Escrow Agent to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller.

5. Claims

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount only in accordance with this Clause 5.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, by delivery of a Claim Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall retain from the Escrow Amount an amount equal to the claim specified in the Claim Notice pending resolution of the claim in accordance with the Purchase Agreement.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the address or email set out in this Clause 6.1.

6.2 Notices to the Buyer: to its registered office.

6.3 Notices to the Seller: to its registered office.

6.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent: to its registered office or such other address as it may designate.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any claim made hereunder, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under confidentiality obligations.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
