MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DUNMORE RETAIL GROUP PLC  
Held at the registered office, 18 Moorgate, London, on 25 August 2026  

1. Attendance and Quorum  
1.1. The following directors were present:  
- Ms. Eleanor Vance  
- Mr. Daniel Whitaker  

1.2. The Chairman, Ms. Eleanor Vance, opened the meeting and confirmed that notice of this meeting had been duly given in accordance with the Articles of Association.  

1.3. The Chairman confirmed that, of the five (5) directors currently serving on the board, 2 directors were present. The Chairman further confirmed that the board rules as set out in the Company's Articles of Association require a minimum of 2 directors for the transaction of business and that this meeting was accordingly quorate.  

2. Declaration of Interest  
2.1. Ms. Eleanor Vance declared a personal interest in the matters to be considered under Agenda Item 3 on the grounds that she is also a non-executive director of Whitlock Medical Ltd (company number 15192427, registered office 178 Old Broad Street, London). She confirmed, however, that her interest did not extend to any financial or material conflict in relation to the proposed transaction and affirmed her willingness to participate fully in the deliberations.  

2.2. Mr. Daniel Whitaker confirmed that he had no interests to declare.  

3. Review of Proposed Transaction  
3.1. The Chairman introduced the matter concerning the proposed disposal of the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Retail Group plc (company number 10706981, registered office 18 Moorgate, London) by Brightwater Energy plc (the Seller, company number 12620703, registered office 89 Cannon Street, London) to Pemberton Pharma plc (the Buyer, company number 10604458, registered office 140 Ludgate Hill, London).  

3.2. The board was advised by external legal counsel that the transaction is to be effected by way of a Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) to be entered into between the Seller and the Buyer.  

3.3. The board received a summary of the SPA, including its main terms. It was confirmed that the SPA is governed by English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

3.4. The directors reviewed the implications of the transaction for the Company, including operational continuity, contractual relationships, and employee matters. Particular reference was made to the Company’s existing supply arrangement with Whitlock Medical Ltd, which accounts for 42% of the Company’s annual revenue.  

4. Resolutions  
4.1. RESOLVED THAT the board of Dunmore Retail Group plc hereby approve, for the purposes of section 205 of the Companies Act 2006 and any relevant provisions of the Company's Articles of Association, the entry by Brightwater Energy plc into the Share Purchase Agreement with Pemberton Pharma plc concerning the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

4.2. RESOLVED THAT the approval referenced in clause 4.1 is given in the best interests of the Company and based on the board’s collective assessment that the transaction is commercially sound and financially reasonable.  

5. Next Steps  
5.1. The Chairman confirmed that copies of these minutes will be retained in the Company’s statutory records.  

5.2. The meeting concluded at 16:45.
