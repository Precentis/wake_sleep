**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **19 August 2026**

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Brightwater Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **12620703**, whose registered office is at **89 Cannon Street, London** (the **"Seller"**);

(2) **Pemberton Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10604458**, whose registered office is at **140 Ludgate Hill, London** (the **"Buyer"**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Dunmore Retail Group plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10706981**, whose registered office is at **18 Moorgate, London** (the **"Company"**).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

---

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Closing Date"** means **27 November 2026**;
- **"Material Adverse Change"** means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

---

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances, for a total purchase price of **$115,000,000** (the **"Purchase Price"**), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

---

**3. PAYMENT**

**3.1** The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller may designate in writing at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3.2** Time for payment shall be of the essence.

---

**4. CLOSING**

**4.1** The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the **"Closing"**) shall take place on the **Closing Date** at 10:00 a.m. (London time), at the offices of the Buyer’s legal advisers, or at such other time and place as the parties may agree in writing.

**4.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, (b) share certificates representing the Shares, (c) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company, and (d) such corporate authorisations and consents as may be reasonably required by the Buyer.

---

**5. NOTICES**

**5.1** Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement must be in writing and signed by or on behalf of the sender.

**5.2** Notices shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in this Agreement or such other address as may be notified in writing.

**5.3** A notice given in accordance with this Clause 5 shall be deemed to be served: (a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; (b) if sent by post, two Business Days after posting; (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

---

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**6.1** Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

**6.2** This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

---

**9. WARRANTIES AND CLAIMS**

**9.7** The Buyer shall notify the Seller in writing of any claim under a warranty within **30 days** of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

---

**10. SURVIVAL AND LIMITATIONS**

**10.1** The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **24 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (being title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months**. Any claim under any warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.
