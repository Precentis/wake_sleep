SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 October 2026.

Parties:

(1) Elstree Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11731131, whose registered office is at 147 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

(2) Lowther Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15173359, whose registered office is at 5 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

Background:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Northgate Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13393666, whose registered office is at 88 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event, circumstance or condition which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, provided that none of the following shall be deemed to constitute, and none of the following shall be taken into account in determining whether there has been, a Material Adverse Change: (a) changes in general economic or political conditions in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates; (b) changes that affect the industry in which the Company operates generally and that do not disproportionately affect the Company; or (c) pandemics.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total purchase price of $165,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be subject to adjustment in accordance with the working capital mechanism set out in Schedule 3. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any amount by which the Closing Working Capital falls below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000.

3. Payment and Closing

3.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on 12 December 2026 (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Seller or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, as adjusted in accordance with Clause 2.3, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such bank account as the Seller shall have notified to the Buyer in accordance with Clause 7.

4. Conditions and Termination

4.5 If the conditions precedent set out in Schedule 2 (the "Conditions") have not been satisfied or waived in writing by the Buyer by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or liabilities which may have accrued prior to such termination.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates, officers, employees and professional advisers shall not, at any time after the date of this Agreement, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the other party or the Company, including but not limited to the terms of this Agreement, the identity of the parties, financial data, customer relationships (including, without limitation, the commercial relationship between the Company and Holloway Logistics plc, company number 14053432), or operational methods, except:

(a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

(b) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis and under an obligation of confidentiality; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.
