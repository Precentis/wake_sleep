ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into on 25 October 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and among Delamere Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12985149 and having its registered office at 14 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”), Penrith Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13590245 and having its registered office at 51 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”), and Dunmore Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12316980 and having its registered office at 90 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”), for the purposes of establishing an escrow arrangement in connection with the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller (the “Transaction”).

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Claim” means any written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller asserting a right to recover funds from the Escrow Amount;  
“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £12,500,000 (twelve million five hundred thousand pounds sterling), which has been deposited with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3;  
“Escrow Agent” means the financial institution appointed by mutual agreement of the Buyer and the Seller to hold and administer the Escrow Amount;  
“Transaction Agreement” means the Share Purchase Agreement dated 30 October 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount  
2.1 The Seller shall cause £12,500,000 to be deposited into an escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) maintained by the Escrow Agent within five (5) Business Days of the Effective Date.  
2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall be available solely for the satisfaction of any valid Claim made in accordance with Clause 5.

3. Payment Mechanism  
3.1 Disbursements from the Escrow Amount shall be made only upon receipt by the Escrow Agent of joint written instructions signed by the Buyer and the Seller or, in the absence of such instructions, pursuant to a final arbitral award issued under Clause 12.  
3.2 The Escrow Agent shall have no obligation to disburse any portion of the Escrow Amount in response to a Claim unless such Claim is delivered in accordance with Clause 5.2.

4. Notices  
4.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the addresses or email addresses set out below:  
(a) To the Buyer: 14 King William Street, London, Attn: General Counsel  
(b) To the Seller: 51 Old Broad Street, London, Attn: Company Secretary  
(c) To the Escrow Agent: as notified in writing.  
4.2 A notice delivered by email shall be deemed received on the next Business Day following transmission.

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount  
5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount only in respect of a breach of a representation, warranty, covenant or indemnity contained in the Transaction Agreement.  
5.2 Any Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, and such notification shall specify in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the Claim, the amount claimed, and any supporting documentation.

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the Escrow Amount, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution  
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York, conducted in the English language under the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce. The arbitral tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator appointed in accordance with such rules. Judgment upon the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.
