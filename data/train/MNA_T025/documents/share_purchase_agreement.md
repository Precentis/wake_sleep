**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 25 October 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) Delamere Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12985149, whose registered office is at 14 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”).

(2) Penrith Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13590245, whose registered office is at 51 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12316980, whose registered office is at 90 Gresham Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer has agreed to purchase, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the Shares on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, “Material Adverse Change” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

**1.2** The expression “$” denotes US dollars.

**1.3** References to clauses, schedules or paragraphs are to clauses, schedules or paragraphs of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

**1.4** This Agreement includes any document expressed to be supplemental or ancillary to it.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, all of the issued shares in the Company, free from all Encumbrances, for a total consideration of $255,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”).

**2.2** The purchase and sale shall take place on the Closing Date.

**3. CLOSING**

**3.1** Completion shall take place on 16 December 2026 (the “Closing Date”) at 10.00 a.m. at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors in London.

**3.2** At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share certificates in respect of the entire issued share capital of the Company;

(b) a duly executed share transfer form in favour of the Buyer;

(c) resignations of any directors or officers of the Company as requested by the Buyer; and

(d) such other documents as are reasonably required to effect the transfer of legal and beneficial title to the Shares.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

**15.2** The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) and accordingly both parties submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**16. NOTICES**

**16.1** Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the registered office or main place of business of the relevant party.

**17. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**17.1** Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except:
