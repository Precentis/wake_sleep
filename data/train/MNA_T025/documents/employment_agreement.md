1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Delamere Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12985149, whose registered office is at 14 King William Street, London;

“Company” means Dunmore Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12316980, whose registered office is at 90 Gresham Street, London;

“Seller” means Penrith Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13590245, whose registered office is at 51 Old Broad Street, London;

“Founder” means Dr. Eleanor Vance, who is employed by the Company pursuant to the terms of this Agreement;

“Material Customer” means Merrion Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under number 11805758, whose registered office is at 111 Cheapside, London.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, with effect from 1 September 2023.

2.2 The Founder’s principal place of work shall be the Company’s registered office, or such other location as the Board of the Company may determine, provided that reasonable notice is given.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £78,500 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 In addition to the base salary, the Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme as determined by the Board, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme, with a target bonus of 35% of base salary.

4. Working Hours and Leave

4.1 The Founder shall work such hours as are necessary to perform her duties, consistent with the role of Chief Scientific Officer.

4.2 The Founder shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in England.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 During the term of employment and thereafter, the Founder shall not, without prior written consent from the Board, disclose or use any confidential information concerning the Company, the Buyer, the Seller, or any of their respective clients, suppliers or Material Customers.

5.2 For the purposes of this clause, “confidential information” includes, but is not limited to, trade secrets, technical data, financial information, client lists (including but not limited to Merrion Capital LLP), and details of business operations.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the recipient’s last known address.

6.2 Notices to the Founder shall be sent to her most recent personal email on file with the Company. Notices to the Company shall be sent to its registered office.

7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving six months’ written notice to the other.

7.2 The Company may terminate the Founder’s employment summarily without notice or payment in lieu for gross misconduct.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

18. Non-Competition

18.1 The Founder undertakes that she shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as principal, partner, shareholder, director, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage or be concerned in any business or activity which is in competition with the business of the Company for a period of 12 months after termination of her employment, regardless of the reason for termination.

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the restriction in clause 18.1 applies to any entity that carries on a business substantially similar to that of the Company as conducted during the 12 months prior to termination, including but not limited to businesses engaged in the research, development or commercialisation of point-of-care diagnostic technologies.

18.3 This clause shall not apply to the ownership of not more than 3% of the issued share capital of any company whose shares are listed on a recognised stock exchange.
