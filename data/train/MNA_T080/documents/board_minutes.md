# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Larkspur Medical Ltd

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 76 Cheapside, London, EC2V 6AA, United Kingdom  

## 1. Attendance and Quorum

1.1. The following directors were present for the meeting:  
- Dr. Eleanor M. Hart (Director)  
- Mr. Julian T. Reed (Director)  

1.2. The Company has a total board of five (5) directors. The attendance of 2 directors constitutes a quorate meeting pursuant to the Company’s articles of association.  

1.3. No proxy representations were submitted.  

1.4. The Chair of the meeting was Dr. Eleanor M. Hart.  

1.5. The Secretary in attendance was Ms. Clara N. Singh, Company Secretary of Larkspur Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12412292, registered office 76 Cheapside, London.  

## 2. Confirmation of Agenda

2.1. The Chair proposed the following agenda, which was adopted unanimously:  
(i) Approval of entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Galloway Media Ltd and Fenwick Energy plc.  
(ii) Any other business.  

## 3. Approval of Entry into the Share Purchase Agreement

3.1. The Chair presented a draft Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) under which Galloway Media Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12294282, registered office 35 Cheapside, London, proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of Larkspur Medical Ltd (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12412292, registered office 76 Cheapside, London, to Fenwick Energy plc (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10891150, registered office 70 Moorgate, London.  

3.2. The directors reviewed the terms of the SPA, including the proposed transaction structure, warranties, and post-completion obligations.  

3.3. The directors noted that the Company is contractually bound under an existing supply agreement with Elstree Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12914845, registered office 58 Cannon Street, London, which constitutes 68.4% of the Company’s total revenue over the preceding 12-month period.  

3.4. Legal counsel confirmed that the SPA is drafted under English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

3.5. The directors considered the implications of the proposed transaction on the Company’s operations, staff, and ongoing contractual commitments.  

3.6. After deliberation, it was formally proposed by Dr. Eleanor M. Hart and seconded by Mr. Julian T. Reed that the Board approve the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Galloway Media Ltd and Fenwick Energy plc.  

**RESOLVED:** That the Board of Larkspur Medical Ltd hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Galloway Media Ltd (Seller) and Fenwick Energy plc (Buyer), such approval being granted pursuant to the authority of the Board under the Company’s constitutional documents and applicable law, and that the transaction involving the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company proceed in accordance with the terms thereof, with 2 directors present and the meeting being quorate.  

## 4. Any Other Business

4.1. There was no other business to report.
