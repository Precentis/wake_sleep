2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)

2.1 The Seller hereby discloses, for the purposes of qualifying Warranty 8.2 of the Share Purchase Agreement relating to the Company’s ownership of intellectual property, the following matter:

2.2 The Company's core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Orwell Industries Ltd. The development was carried out on a project basis without a formal written development agreement.

2.3 No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between Orwell Industries Ltd and the Company at any time during or following the development period. As a result, while the Company has treated the core platform software as its own proprietary asset since 2023 and has used it continuously in its operations, there is no executed document transferring ownership of the copyright or other intellectual property rights in the software from Orwell Industries Ltd to the Company.

2.4 The absence of a written assignment may affect the Company’s ability to assert full legal title to the software under English copyright law, particularly in circumstances where third-party rights are challenged or enforced.

2.5 This disclosure is made specifically in response to Warranty 8.2 and is intended to prevent any claim arising under that warranty in respect of the Company's title to the core platform software, given that the matter has been fully disclosed to the Buyer prior to completion of the acquisition.
