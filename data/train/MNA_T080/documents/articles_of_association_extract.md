ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF LARKSPUR MEDICAL LTD  
(incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12412292)  
Registered office: 76 Cheapside, London  

1. Interpretation  

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Fenwick Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10891150, registered office 70 Moorgate, London;  
“Seller” means Galloway Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12294282, registered office 35 Cheapside, London;  
“Company” means Larkspur Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12412292, registered office 76 Cheapside, London;  
“Elstree Energy plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12914845, registered office 58 Cannon Street, London;  
“person” includes any individual, body corporate, partnership, unincorporated association, joint venture, trust, or other entity;  
“subsidiary” has the meaning given in section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006;  
“these Articles” means these articles of association as amended from time to time.  

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 Any variation of the rights attached to the shares shall require a special resolution of the members.  
2.3 The Company may, subject to the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, issue shares with such rights or restrictions as the Board may determine.  

3. Transfer of Shares  

3.1 No transfer of shares shall be registered unless a proper instrument of transfer is delivered to the Company, duly executed and accompanied by the relevant share certificate.  
3.2 The Board may, in its absolute discretion, refuse to register any transfer of shares without assigning any reason.  
3.3 Following the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer from the Seller, any transfer restrictions under these Articles shall be subject to the terms of the acquisition agreement dated on or prior to 21 August 2026.  

4. Directors  

4.1 The Board may consist of such number of directors as the members may from time to time determine by ordinary resolution, provided that there shall be at least one director.  
4.2 A director may be appointed by ordinary resolution of the members or by decision of the Board.  
4.3 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution of the members, notwithstanding any agreement between the Company and such director.  
4.4 The Board may delegate any of its powers to a committee of directors or to any person, on such terms as it thinks fit.  

5. Board Meetings and Proceedings  

5.1 The Board may regulate its proceedings as it thinks fit, including the appointment of a chairman and the frequency of meetings.  
5.2 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes.  
5.3 In the case of an equality of votes, the chairman of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.  
5.4 **Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.** No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the Board unless at least three directors are present.  

6. Payment  

6.1 All payments due to or by the Company shall be made in pounds sterling, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Board.  
6.2 Any payment required under a resolution of the Board or these Articles shall be made to the Company’s designated bank account unless otherwise specified.  

7. Notices  

7.1 Any notice required to be given to a member, director or the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class post, or transmitted by electronic means to the relevant registered address or email address.  
7.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. A notice transmitted electronically shall be deemed served on transmission.  

8. Confidentiality  

8.1 Directors and officers of the Company shall not disclose any confidential information concerning the Company’s business, including but not limited to commercial relationships with third parties such as Elstree Energy plc, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.  
8.2 The obligation under this Article 8 shall continue after cessation of office.  

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  

9.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
