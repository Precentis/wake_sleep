SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 August 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Galloway Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 12294282, whose registered office is at 35 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

(2) Fenwick Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 10891150, whose registered office is at 70 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Larkspur Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 12412292, whose registered office is at 76 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 3;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares;

   "Closing Date" means 18 November 2026;

   "General Warranties" means the representations and warranties set out in Schedule 2, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

   "Fundamental Warranties" means the representations and warranties relating to title, capacity and tax;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means all the issued and outstanding shares in the capital of the Company.

1.2 Words and expressions defined in the recitals have the same meaning in the operative provisions.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price is $100,000,000, to be satisfied in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares, together with the share certificates for the Shares;

   (b) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

   (c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to effect the transfer of the Shares and control of the Company.

3. Conditions

3.1 The obligation of the Buyer to complete the purchase is conditional upon the satisfaction or (where permitted) waiver of each of the Conditions.

3.2 The Conditions include:

   (a) all necessary corporate and regulatory approvals having been obtained;

   (b) no material adverse change in the financial or operational condition of the Company;

   (c) accuracy of the Warranties in all material respects as of the Closing Date.

4. Termination

4.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party if the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by 18 November 2026.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (signed 1 August 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

10. Warranties and Indemnities

10.1 Survival

   The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the party against whom the claim is made before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

12. Notices

12.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

13.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
