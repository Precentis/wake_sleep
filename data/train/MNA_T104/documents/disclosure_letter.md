(b) “Seller” means Whitlock Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12826816, with its registered office at 121 Moorgate, London;

**2. Disclosure Against Warranties**  
2.1 This letter is delivered pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement and sets out full and specific disclosure against the Warranties as required thereunder.  

2.2 Disclosure of any matter in this letter shall not be construed as an admission that such matter falls within the scope of any Warranty, nor shall it be admissible in evidence against the disclosing party in any dispute arising under the Share Purchase Agreement other than for the purpose of determining the accuracy of the Warranties as qualified by this disclosure.  

**3. Disclosure Against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**  
3.1 The Seller discloses pursuant to Warranty 8.2 relating to intellectual property that the core platform software used by the Company in its principal operations was developed in 2023 by Hartwell Robotics Ltd, an independent contractor.  

3.2 The development work undertaken by Hartwell Robotics Ltd was performed pursuant to a verbal understanding between the Company and Hartwell Robotics Ltd. No written contract or statement of work was executed between the parties in relation to this development.  

3.3 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Hartwell Robotics Ltd at the time of development or at any time thereafter.  

3.4 The Company has used the core platform software continuously since its development in 2023 and has incorporated it into its proprietary systems and client-facing applications.  

3.5 The Company currently holds itself out as the owner of all rights, title, and interest in and to the core platform software for operational and commercial purposes.  

3.6 Hartwell Robotics Ltd has not, to the best of the Seller’s knowledge, claimed any ownership or proprietary interest in the core platform software since its delivery to the Company in 2023.  

3.7 The Company has not licensed the core platform software to any third party, except for limited internal use by authorised personnel and integration into systems used by Rothley Energy plc, a customer of the Company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11141670, with its registered office at 24 Queen Victoria Street, London.  

**4. Payment**  
4.1 Any adjustment to the purchase price arising as a result of claims under the Warranties shall be made in accordance with the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement. No separate payment mechanism is established by this letter.  

**5. Notices**  
5.1 Notices under this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as a party may notify in writing to the other.  

5.2 Notices shall be deemed served if delivered personally, on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, on the second business day after posting; or if sent by email, on the day of transmission provided confirmation of receipt is obtained.

6.2 The obligations of confidentiality under this clause shall survive termination or completion of the Share Purchase Agreement.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Disclosure Letter.
