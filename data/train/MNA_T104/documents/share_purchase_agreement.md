**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 3 March 2027.

**Parties:**

(1) **Whitlock Energy plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12826816, whose registered office is at 121 Moorgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Northgate Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15302873, whose registered office is at 12 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

**Recitals:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Rothley Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15623641, whose registered office is at 162 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**Now it is hereby agreed as follows:**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

**1.1** In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event, circumstance or condition which has, or would reasonably be expected to have, a material adverse effect on the business, assets, liabilities, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, excluding (i) changes in general economic or political conditions, (ii) changes affecting the energy or healthcare industries generally in any jurisdiction in which the Company operates, and (iii) any outbreak or escalation of hostilities or acts of terrorism, **pandemics** or epidemic conditions.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

**2.1** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$65,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be satisfied in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller not less than three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced on a dollar-for-dollar basis by any amount by which the Closing Working Capital (as defined in Schedule 3) falls short of **$2,000,000** (the "Target Working Capital"). Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 3.

**3. Earn-out**

**3.4** In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") calculated as follows: three (3) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026 EBITDA") exceeds **$6,500,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at a maximum of **$10,000,000**. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 4 and paid within ninety (90) days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026 being approved by the Buyer’s board, subject to the dispute resolution mechanism in Schedule 4.

**4. Closing**

**4.1** The completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the date falling on or before **28 April 2027** (the "Closing Date").

**5. Notices**

**5.1** Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing, addressed to the recipient at its registered office for the time being, and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter or email (confirmed by letter within 24 hours). Notices shall be deemed received at the time of delivery if by hand, 48 hours after posting if by letter, or upon confirmation of receipt if by email.

**6. Confidentiality**

**6.1** Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party or the Company in connection with this transaction (the "Confidential Information") and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

**7. General**

**7.1** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings in relation to its subject matter.

**7.2** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in relation to any disputes arising under this Agreement.
