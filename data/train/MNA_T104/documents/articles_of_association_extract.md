ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ROTHLEY CAPITAL LLP  
(Company number 15623641)  

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Northgate Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15302873;  
“Company” means Rothley Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15623641;  
“Seller” means Whitlock Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12826816;  
“Share Transfer” means the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or prior to 22 March 2027;  
“Material Customer” means Rothley Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11141670.  
1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.  

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights  
2.1 The Company's share capital shall consist of ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The rights attached to the ordinary shares shall be as set out in these Articles.  
2.3 No alteration shall be made to the rights attached to the issued shares unless approved by a special resolution of the shareholders.  

3. Payment for Shares  
3.1 All shares in the Company shall be fully paid on issue.  
3.2 The consideration for the Share Transfer shall be settled in accordance with the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement, with payment to be made in cleared funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller.  

4. Board of Directors  
4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all the powers of the Company except such as are by the Companies Act 2006 or by these Articles required to be exercised by the members.  
4.2 The Board may appoint one or more directors to serve in such capacity until the conclusion of the next annual general meeting or until their successors are duly appointed.  
4.3 The Board may delegate any of its powers to committees or individual directors as it deems appropriate.  

5. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
5.1 The quorum necessary for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three directors.  
11. For the avoidance of doubt, Article 11 of these Articles confirms that the quorum for board meetings is three directors.  
5.2 A director may participate in a meeting of the Board by means of a conference telephone or other communications equipment permitting all participants to hear one another.  
5.3 Minutes of all proceedings at Board meetings shall be recorded and maintained at the registered office of the Company, which is 162 Ludgate Hill, London.  

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice required to be given to a member, director or the Company under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class post, or transmitted by email to the address or email address last known or provided.  
6.2 A notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.  

7. Confidentiality  
7.1 Directors and officers of the Company shall not, without prior written consent from the Board, disclose to any third party any confidential information relating to the business, operations or financial affairs of the Company or the Material Customer, Rothley Energy plc.  
7.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of any directorship or employment.  

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
