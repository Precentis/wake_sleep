MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF ROTHLEY CAPITAL LLP (COMPANY NUMBER 15623641)

Date: 21 March 2027  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 162 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7HU  
Present:  
1. Mr. A. Finch (Director)  
2. Ms. R. Nkosi (Director)  

Total number of directors appointed: 5  
Number present: 2  
The meeting was quorate.  
It was confirmed that 2 directors were present, constituting a quorum under the constitutional documents of the Company.

Agenda:

1. Confirmation of quorum and approval of attendance  
   The Chair confirmed that the meeting was duly convened and that 2 directors were present, satisfying the quorum requirements for the transaction of business under the Company’s articles of association. The Company Secretary was directed to record attendance accordingly.

2. Declaration of interests  
   Each of the 2 directors present confirmed that they had no personal interest, direct or indirect, in any matter arising for discussion at the meeting that required disclosure under Section 177 of the Companies Act 2006. No conflict of interest was identified in relation to the proposed transaction.

3. Review of proposed transaction with Northgate Medical Ltd  
   The Chair provided a summary of the proposed transaction involving the Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) to be entered into between Whitlock Energy plc (the “Seller”), as the shareholder of Rothley Capital LLP, and Northgate Medical Ltd (the “Buyer”), incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15302873, whose registered office is at 12 Moorgate, London. The SPA provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company, which is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15623641 and has its registered office at 162 Ludgate Hill, London.

   The SPA is expected to be dated on or around 22 March 2027. The transaction is governed by English law, and the jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales shall apply to any disputes arising under the agreement.

   Reference was made to Rothley Energy plc (company number 11141670), a material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales and registered at 24 Queen Victoria Street, London. Its contractual arrangements with the Company are disclosed in the schedules to the SPA.

   The directors, being 2 in number and duly authorised to make decisions on behalf of the Board, reviewed the structure, terms and implications of the transaction. Legal advisers confirmed the form and validity of the SPA and confirmed that the Company’s consent to the transfer of shares is not required under the terms of the agreement, but Board awareness and internal record-keeping were necessary for corporate governance purposes.

4. Approval of the transaction  
   Following discussion, the Board resolved as follows:

   RESOLVED (1): That the Board of Rothley Capital LLP hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement, noting that 2 directors are present and voting in favour, and that the meeting is quorate, and further acknowledges the acquisition by Northgate Medical Ltd of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company from Whitlock Energy plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12826816, registered office at 121 Moorgate, London).

   RESOLVED (2): That the Company Secretary be authorised to file such notices as may be required at Companies House and to provide any necessary confirmations to the Seller’s legal representatives in connection with completion.

5. Adjournment  
   Having concluded the agenda, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
