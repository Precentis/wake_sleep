1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business” means the business of designing, developing, manufacturing and supplying robotic automation systems for marine applications carried out by Redcliffe Robotics Ltd or any member of its group, as conducted at the date of this Agreement.

“Company” means Redcliffe Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10477420, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London.

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information, whether technical, commercial or financial, relating to the Company, its subsidiaries, customers, suppliers or business operations, disclosed before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form.

“Effective Date” means the date of this Agreement, being 25 May 2027.

“Founder” means Dr. Eleanor M. Voss, Director of the Company.

“Group” means a corporate group comprising the Company and its subsidiary undertakings from time to time.

“Material Customer” means any person or entity that is a customer of the Company or any Group company and with which the Company has generated revenue exceeding £750,000 in any single financial year during the two years preceding termination of the Founder’s employment.

2. APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve, as Chief Technology Officer, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Founder shall report to the Board of Directors and perform duties commensurate with her office, including oversight of research and development, product strategy and intellectual property development.

3. REMUNERATION

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £125,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Founder shall be eligible to receive a discretionary annual bonus, subject to Board approval, with a target of 30% of base salary.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The Founder shall not, at any time during or after employment, use or disclose any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as compelled by law.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Any intellectual property created by the Founder in the course of her employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

5.2 The Founder hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all rights, title and interest in and to such intellectual property.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company at 74 Bishopsgate, London, or to the Founder at her last known address recorded in the Company’s personnel file.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after posting by first-class pre-paid post.

7. TERMINATION

7.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement on 90 days’ written notice.

7.2 The Company may summarily terminate the Agreement for gross misconduct without notice or payment in lieu.

18. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

18.1 For a period of 12 months following termination of employment, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as principal, partner, shareholder (save for holdings of less than 3% in any publicly traded company), consultant, director or employee, engage, assist or be interested in any business which competes with the Business within the United Kingdom.

18.2 The Founder shall not, during the restricted period, solicit or attempt to solicit any Material Customer of the Company.

18.3 For the purposes of this clause, Penrith Marine Ltd (company number 11585965) shall be deemed a Material Customer.

19. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising under this Agreement.
