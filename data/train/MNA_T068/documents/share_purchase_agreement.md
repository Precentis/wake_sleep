SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 17 May 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Kingsmere Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13714876 and registered office at 143 King William Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(2) Eskdale Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10088399 and registered office at 1 Gresham Street, London (the "Seller").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Redcliffe Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10477420 and registered office at 74 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Seller has agreed to sell and the Buyer has agreed to purchase the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, the Shares for a total consideration of $75,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price, subject to any adjustments under Section 2.3, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS AND CLOSING

4.1 The obligations of the parties to complete the sale and purchase of the Shares are conditional upon the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions (as defined below) on or before the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

4.6 Closing shall take place on 25 August 2027 (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Seller or such other place as the parties may agree.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 143 King William Street, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 1 Gresham Street, London.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not at any time after the date of this Agreement disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the business of the other party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $6,750,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $75,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

12. RESTRICTIONS ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliated entities shall not, carry on or be engaged in any business which is in direct competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date.

13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter.

13.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
