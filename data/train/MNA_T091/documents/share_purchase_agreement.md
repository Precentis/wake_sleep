SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 16 June 2027.

Parties:

(1) Marlow Robotics Ltd (company number 13709722), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 3 Moorgate, London (the "Seller").

(2) Orwell Marine Ltd (company number 12926622), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 116 Cannon Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Calloway Pharma plc (company number 15914570), a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 135 King William Street, London (the "Company").

Background:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

It is agreed as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, "Material Adverse Change" means any event, circumstance or condition that has had a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, excluding any such effect arising from: (i) changes in general economic or political conditions; (ii) changes affecting the pharmaceutical or life sciences industry generally; or (iii) pandemics or public health emergencies, regardless of duration or geographic scope.

1.2 Other defined terms used in this Agreement shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Recitals or elsewhere in this Agreement, and words importing the singular shall include the plural and vice versa.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, all of the issued shares in the capital of the Company (the "Shares") for a total purchase price of $45,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on 1 September 2027 (the "Closing Date"), or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

3. Payment

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to the bank account designated by the Seller in writing at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Time for payment shall be of the essence.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be addressed to: Marlow Robotics Ltd, 3 Moorgate, London, or such other address as the Seller may notify to the Buyer in writing.

4.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be addressed to: Orwell Marine Ltd, 116 Cannon Street, London, or such other address as the Buyer may notify to the Seller in writing.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its officers, employees and advisers shall not, disclose to any third party any confidential information concerning the other party or the Company acquired in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.

9. Limitation of Liability

9.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the Warranties (excluding fraud) shall not exceed an amount equal to 25% of the Purchase Price, being $10,125,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $45,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

10. Survival and Claims

10.1 The General Warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under the Warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the relevant survival period.

Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
