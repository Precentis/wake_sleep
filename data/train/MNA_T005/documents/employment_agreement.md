EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 15 May 2027

Between:

(1) Bellhaven Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10180382, whose registered office is at 61 Ludgate Hill, London ("Company"); and

(2) The Founder, an individual whose primary place of work shall be at the Company's registered office ("Employee").

1. Definitions  
In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:  
"Buyer" means Dunmore Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13210016, with registered office at 166 Old Broad Street, London.  
"Company" means Bellhaven Pharma plc.  
"Effective Date" means 17 May 2027.  
"Founder" means the Employee as defined in Recital (2) above.  
"Material Customer" means Foxley Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12196715, with registered office at 6 Old Broad Street, London.  
"Seller" means Netherby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15815054, with registered office at 58 Fleet Street, London.

2. Commencement and Term  
The Employee’s employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue unless terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3. Position and Duties  
The Employee shall serve as Founder and hold such responsibilities and perform such duties as are reasonably assigned by the board of directors of the Company, consistent with the Employee’s position.

4. Remuneration  
The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £98,500, payable in arrears by equal monthly instalments on or about the last working day of each month. Such salary shall be subject to review annually at the discretion of the board.

5. Benefits  
The Employee shall be entitled to participate in such pension, health insurance and other benefit schemes as the Company may make available to senior executives from time to time, subject to the rules and conditions of such schemes.

6. Working Time  
The Employee shall work such hours as are necessary to perform the duties of the role, which may include periods outside normal business hours. Normal office hours are considered to be 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. on weekdays.

7. Leave  
The Employee shall be entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays. Leave shall be taken at such times as approved by the board.

8. Expenses  
The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable and properly documented business expenses incurred in connection with the performance of duties under this Agreement, in accordance with the Company’s expense policy.

9. Confidentiality  
The Employee shall not, during or after the term of employment, use or disclose any confidential or proprietary information of the Company, the Buyer, the Seller, or any of their affiliates, except as required in the course of employment or as permitted by law.

10. Data Protection  
The Employee consents to the processing of personal data by the Company for purposes related to the employment relationship, in compliance with applicable data protection legislation.

11. Notices  
Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the Company or to the Employee’s last known address. Notices shall be deemed received two business days after posting by first-class post.

12. Entire Agreement  
This document constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and arrangements relating to the subject matter.

13. Severability  
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

14. Waiver  
No failure or delay by a party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver.

15. Assignment  
The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor entity, including the Buyer. The Employee shall not assign this Agreement without prior written consent.

16. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

17. Counterparts  
This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

18. Non-Competition  
The Founder shall not compete with the business for 12 months after termination of employment. For the duration of this restriction, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have a financial interest in any business that competes with the Company or any of its affiliates, including but not limited to businesses supplying products or services to the Material Customer. This restriction applies within the United Kingdom and any other territory in which the Company carries on business at the date of termination.
