**Contract for the Supply of Pharmaceutical Logistics Services**  
**Effective Date:** 15 November 2023  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Agreement” means this contract for the supply of pharmaceutical logistics services, including all schedules and appendices;  
“Buyer” means Dunmore Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13210016, with registered office at 166 Old Broad Street, London;  
“Change of Control” means any person or entity acquiring direct or indirect control of more than 50% of the voting rights or issued share capital of a party, or the acquisition of the right to appoint or remove a majority of its board of directors;  
“Company” means Bellhaven Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10180382, with registered office at 61 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information, technical data, business plans, pricing, and operational methods disclosed by either party;  
“Customer” means Foxley Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12196715, with registered office at 6 Old Broad Street, London;  
“Seller” means Netherby Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15815054, with registered office at 58 Fleet Street, London;  
“Services” means the pharmaceutical logistics services as described in Schedule A.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Appointment and Scope of Services**

2.1 The Company hereby appoints the Customer to provide the Services in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Customer undertakes to perform the Services in a professional and timely manner, in compliance with all applicable regulatory requirements for pharmaceutical handling and distribution.

**3. Term**

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of five (5) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

**4. Payment**

4.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees as specified in Schedule B, in arrears, on a monthly basis.

4.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling to the bank account designated by the Company, within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Lloyds Bank from time to time, accruing daily from the due date until payment in full.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each party shall keep the other party’s Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office or nominated contact of the receiving party.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed received at the time of hand delivery, 48 hours after posting, or upon confirmation of receipt by email.

**7. Termination**

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of notice.

7.2 Termination shall not affect any accrued rights or obligations.

**14. Miscellaneous**

14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the Customer, which in this case is Foxley Logistics plc.

14.3 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

**Schedule A – Description of Services**  
The Customer shall provide temperature-controlled logistics, warehousing, and last-mile delivery services for the Company’s pharmaceutical products within the United Kingdom.

**Schedule B – Fees**  
Monthly fee: £112,500, subject to annual review not exceeding 3.5% increase.
