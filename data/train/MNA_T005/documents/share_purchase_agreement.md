SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 22 April 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Dunmore Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13210016, whose registered office is at 166 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Netherby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15815054, whose registered office is at 58 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Company: Bellhaven Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10180382, whose registered office is at 61 Ludgate Hill, London.

RECITALS

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

AGREED TERMS

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Closing Date" means 8 July 2027.

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1.

"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to another in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, electronically or in writing.

"Group" means the Company and its subsidiaries from time to time.

"Shares" means all the issued and outstanding shares in the capital of the Company.

"Target Working Capital" means $2,000,000.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of $135,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be finalised and paid within thirty (30) days of the Closing Date, based on the audited statement of Closing Working Capital prepared by the Buyer’s accountants and subject to review by the Seller’s nominated accountants in accordance with Clause 2.4.

2.4 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller within sixty (60) days of the Closing Date a written statement of the Closing Working Capital. The Seller may, within twenty (20) Business Days of receipt, notify the Buyer in writing of any objections. Any dispute shall be referred to an independent auditor agreed upon by both parties.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all instruments of transfer, share certificates and other documents required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares, duly executed.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as a party may notify.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed received on the day of delivery if by hand, two (2) Business Days after posting if by post, or on the same day if by email (provided confirmation is received).

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

6. NON-COMPETE

12.1 The Seller covenants that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliated undertakings shall not, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company anywhere in the United Kingdom or the European Union for a period of 4 years after the Closing Date.

13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the whole agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

13.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes.
