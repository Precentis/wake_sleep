# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 5 August 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Whitlock Bioscience plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 14620830, whose registered office is at 32 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) **Foxley Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 11667567, whose registered office is at 93 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Netherby Engineering Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 11255152, whose registered office is at 142 King William Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"**Material Adverse Change**" means any event, act, omission or circumstance which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company taken as a whole, excluding (i) changes in general economic, political or market conditions affecting the United Kingdom or global economy, (ii) changes affecting the engineering or marine components industry generally, whether in the United Kingdom or internationally, and (iii) the outbreak, continuation or effects of **pandemics**.

## 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a purchase price of **$250,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

## 3. CLOSING AND EARN-OUT

**3.1** The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on **22 October 2026** (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:  
(a) share transfer forms in respect of all the Shares, duly executed in blank;  
(b) resignations of all directors and secretaries of the Company as requested by the Buyer; and  
(c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to vest full legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

**3.4 Earn-out Payment**  
The Buyer shall pay to the Seller, within 90 days of the end of the Company’s financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026"), an additional amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to **2 times** the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds **$4,000,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at **$5,000,000**. The calculation of EBITDA shall be prepared in accordance with applicable UK accounting standards and audited by the Company’s independent auditor.

## 10. WARRANTIES AND CLAIMS

**10.1 Survival**  
The General Warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of **18 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising title to the Shares, corporate capacity of the Seller to enter into this Agreement, and compliance with tax obligations) shall survive the Closing for **72 months**. Any claim under this Agreement in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees that it shall not, and shall procure that its representatives shall not, disclose to any third party any confidential information concerning the terms of this Agreement or the transactions contemplated hereby, except:  
(a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;  
(b) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis and under obligations of confidentiality; or  
(c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

## 12. NOTICES

Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or a designated email address of the relevant party.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
