**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT – EXTRACT**

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 15 January 2026 between:

(1) **Penrith Pharma plc** (registered in England and Wales under company number 11624014, whose registered office is at 13 Ludgate Hill, London) (the "Employer"); and  
(2) **Dr. Eleanor M. Hartwell** (the "Employee").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) "Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, a person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party;  
(b) "Business" means the research, development, manufacture and supply of specialty pharmaceutical products, including but not limited to oncology therapeutics;  
(c) "Confidential Information" means all non-public information (whether technical, commercial, financial or otherwise) relating to the Employer, its business, customers, suppliers or operations;  
(d) "Effective Date" means 1 February 2026;  
(e) "Founder" means Dr. Eleanor M. Hartwell in her capacity as a founder and director of the Employer prior to the acquisition by Merrion Technologies Ltd.  

1.2 References to legislation are to legislation as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

**2. Position and Duties**

2.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Scientific Officer of the Employer.  
2.2 The Employee shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as are normally incidental to that office and as may be lawfully assigned to her by the board of directors of the Employer.

**3. Remuneration and Benefits**

3.1 The Employer shall pay to the Employee a base salary of £185,000 per annum (before deductions), payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Employer’s annual bonus scheme, currently targeted at 40% of base salary, subject to the terms and conditions of the scheme as determined by the board in its sole discretion.  
3.3 The Employee shall be entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of statutory holidays.

**4. Termination**

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving twelve months’ written notice to the other party.  
4.2 The Employer may, at its discretion, elect to make a payment in lieu of all or part of the notice period, calculated based on the Employee’s base salary and benefits.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Employee shall not, during the term of this Agreement or at any time thereafter, disclose or use any Confidential Information except:  
(a) in the proper performance of her duties; or  
(b) as required by law or a regulatory authority.  
5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive termination of employment.

**6. Intellectual Property**

6.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee during the course of her employment and relating to the Business shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Employer.  
6.2 The Employee irrevocably waives any moral rights in such intellectual property to the fullest extent permitted by law.

**7. Notices**

7.1 Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post or email to the registered office of the Employer or to the last known address of the Employee.  
7.2 Notices to the Employer shall be addressed to the Company Secretary at 13 Ludgate Hill, London.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

**18. Non-Compete**

18.1 The Founder shall not, for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether such termination is lawful or otherwise, directly or indirectly:  
(a) carry on, be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business anywhere in the United Kingdom; or  
(b) solicit or entice away any customer or supplier of the Employer with whom the Employee had material contact during the 12 months prior to termination, including but not limited to Arden Logistics plc (registered in England and Wales under company number 11298310, registered office 23 Gresham Street, London).  
18.2 The parties acknowledge that the restrictions in this Clause 18 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and extent of activity restricted for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Employer, including its goodwill and customer relationships.
