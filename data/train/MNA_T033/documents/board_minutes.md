1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. and confirmed that a quorum was present. The Chair noted the agenda and invited comments or additions. No additions were proposed.

2. APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS MINUTES  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 17 January 2027 were circulated in advance. The board reviewed the draft and resolved to approve them as a true and accurate record.

3. DIRECTOR’S INTEREST IN CONTRACTS  
The Chair reminded all directors of their obligation under the Companies Act 2006 to disclose any interests in existing or proposed contracts or arrangements. No interests were declared at this time.

4. STATUS OF PROPOSED SALE OF SHARE CAPITAL  
The board considered the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Penrith Pharma plc by Merrion Technologies Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15242793, registered office 114 Cheapside, London) from Northgate Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14177172, registered office 116 King William Street, London).  

The Chief Executive Officer reported that the draft share purchase agreement had been circulated to both parties’ legal advisors. Due diligence proceedings were ongoing, with no material impediments identified to date. The board acknowledged that the transaction remains subject to customary closing conditions under English law, including third-party consents and regulatory approvals. It was confirmed that the board would reconvene prior to execution to review final documentation.

5. OUTSTANDING COMMERCIAL DISPUTES  
The board noted a letter before action received from Ashcombe Technologies Ltd, dated 14 February 2027, threatening proceedings for approximately $3,400,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement entered into on 12 April 2024. The matter relates to product batches delivered between 17 June 2025 and 3 September 2025.  

Legal counsel has reviewed the notice and advised that while liability is contested, the claim falls within the scope of existing product liability insurance coverage. The Chief Financial Officer confirmed reserves have been maintained in accordance with IFRS 9. The board resolved that management shall monitor the matter closely and report developments to the board on a bi-weekly basis until resolution.

6. MATERIAL CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP UPDATE  
The Chief Executive Officer reported that Arden Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11298310, registered office 23 Gresham Street, London) continues to account for 38% of the Company’s quarterly revenue under a distribution agreement dated 1 March 2022. No material changes to the terms or performance of the agreement were reported.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There being no other business, the Chair closed the meeting at 11:17 a.m.
