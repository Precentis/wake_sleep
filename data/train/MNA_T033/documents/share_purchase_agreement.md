SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 January 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Northgate Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14177172, whose registered office is at 116 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Merrion Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15242793, whose registered office is at 114 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Penrith Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11624014, whose registered office is at 13 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 4;

"Closing Date" means 23 March 2027;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1;

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting policies of the Company as applied in the preparation of the audited financial statements for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 ("FY2026");

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

"Parties" means the Buyer and the Seller;

"Purchase Price" means the amount specified in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means all of the issued ordinary shares in the capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $150,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash on the Closing Date by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any excess of Closing Working Capital over the Target Working Capital shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date.

3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $6,500,000, provided that the Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed $3,000,000 in aggregate.

4. COMPLETION

4.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or at such other place and time as the Parties may agree.

12. RESTRICTION ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company for a period of 4 years from and after the Closing Date.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any such dispute or claim.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or regulation.
