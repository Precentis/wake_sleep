ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 22 February 2027, between:

(1) Northgate Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14177172, whose registered office is at 116 King William Street, London ("Seller");

(2) Merrion Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15242793, whose registered office is at 114 Cheapside, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, acting in its capacity as escrow agent ("Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the parties are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 21 February 2027 (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Penrith Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11624014, whose registered office is at 13 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Buyer has agreed to deposit an amount equal to 10% of the total consideration payable under the SPA (the "Escrow Amount") into an escrow account with the Escrow Agent, to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Arbitration" means arbitration conducted in accordance with Clause 12.

"Business Day" means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London.

"Claim Notice" means a written notice from the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim for indemnification under the SPA, including the amount claimed (not to exceed the Escrow Amount).

"Effective Date" means 24 February 2027.

"Escrow Account" means the account to be opened by the Escrow Agent in the name of the Buyer, Seller and Escrow Agent in trust, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of 10% of the total consideration payable under the SPA, being £8,600,000 (eight million six hundred thousand pounds sterling), deposited by the Buyer into the Escrow Account on the Effective Date.

"Indemnification Period" means the period ending on 31 January 2028.

"SPA" has the meaning given in the Recitals.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount in writing to the Buyer and the Seller within two Business Days of receipt.

3. Release of Funds

3.1 On the expiry of the Indemnification Period, the Escrow Agent shall release the balance of the Escrow Amount, if any, to the Seller within five Business Days, unless a Claim Notice has been received prior to such expiry.

3.2 If a Claim Notice is received prior to the end of the Indemnification Period, the Escrow Agent shall retain the amount specified in the Claim Notice (up to the remaining Escrow Amount) and disburse it only upon receipt of a joint written instruction from the Buyer and the Seller or a final decision in an Arbitration.

4. Escrow Agent Responsibilities

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall act in accordance with the instructions provided under this Agreement and shall have no discretion to deviate from its terms.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith reliance on instructions believed to be genuine.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email as notified in writing.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all related information confidential and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

7. Entire Agreement

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation to its subject matter.

8. Assignment

8.1 No Party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties.

9. Third Party Rights

9.1 A person who is not a party to this Agreement has no right under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

10. Counterparts

10.1 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

11.1 This Agreement (other than Clause 12) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, except as provided in Clause 12.

12. Arbitration

12.1 This escrow agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York.
