**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **29 December 2026**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Upton Pharma plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **15773657**, whose registered office is at **174 Bishopsgate, London** (the “**Seller**”).

(2) **Foxley Technologies Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **13885482**, whose registered office is at **31 Bishopsgate, London** (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Orwell Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number **15471870**, whose registered office is at **63 Gresham Street, London** (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, “**Material Adverse Change**” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

**1.2** Capitalised terms not otherwise defined have the meanings given in the recitals or elsewhere in this Agreement.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total purchase price of **$40,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

**3. PAYMENT**

**3.1** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than **11 March 2027**.

**3.2** The obligation to pay the Purchase Price is unconditional except as otherwise expressly provided in this Agreement.

**3.3** Time for payment is of the essence.

**3.4** **Earn-out Payment**: In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the “**Earn-out Payment**”) calculated as follows: three (3) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 (“**FY2026 EBITDA**”) exceeds **$5,000,000**, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at **$5,000,000**. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be calculated based on audited financial statements of the Company for FY2026 and paid within 90 days of the date on which such audited financial statements are approved by the Buyer’s board.

**4. CLOSING**

**4.1** Completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the “**Closing**”) shall take place on **11 March 2027** (the “**Closing Date**”) at the offices of the Buyer or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

**4.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all documents and instruments reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares, free from all Encumbrances.

**10. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES**

**10.1** The General Warranties given by the Seller under this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (being title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **72 months**. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

**15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

**15.1** This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with **English law**. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **England**.

**16. NOTICES**

**16.1** Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

**16.2** Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 174 Bishopsgate, London. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 31 Bishopsgate, London.

**17. CONFIDENTIALITY**

**17.1** Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the transaction confidential, except as required by law or regulation or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis, bound by confidentiality obligations.

**17.2** This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.
