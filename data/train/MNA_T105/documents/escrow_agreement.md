1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Foxley Technologies Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13885482, whose registered office is at 31 Bishopsgate, London;

“Company” means Orwell Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15471870, whose registered office is at 63 Gresham Street, London;

“Escrow Agent” means the third-party financial institution appointed by the Buyer and the Seller, acting in its capacity as escrow agent under this Agreement;

“Purchase Price” means the total consideration of £12,500,000 payable by the Buyer to the Seller pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 18 January 2027 in respect of the entire issued share capital of the Company;

“Seller” means Upton Pharma plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15773657, whose registered office is at 174 Bishopsgate, London;

“Share Purchase Agreement” means the agreement for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company dated 18 January 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller;

“Warranties” means the representations and warranties made by the Seller in clause 8 of the Share Purchase Agreement, which are subject to the escrow mechanism detailed herein.

1.2 Words and expressions defined in the Share Purchase Agreement shall have the same meaning in this Agreement unless otherwise defined.

2. Escrow Arrangement

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being £1,875,000, in a designated interest-bearing account (the “Escrow Fund”) for the purposes of satisfying any valid claims made by the Buyer under the Warranties.

2.2 The Escrow Fund shall be funded by the Buyer on 20 January 2027, with payment made via same-day cleared funds to the account designated in writing by the Escrow Agent.

2.3 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Fund except as expressly permitted herein.

3. Claims and Release

3.1 During the period of 18 months commencing on 21 January 2027 (the “Claim Period”), the Buyer may submit to the Escrow Agent and the Seller a written notice specifying in reasonable detail any claim for indemnification under the Warranties (a “Claim Notice”).

3.2 The Seller shall have 20 Business Days from the receipt of a Claim Notice to object in writing to the claim. In the absence of such objection, the Escrow Agent shall release the amount specified in the Claim Notice to the Buyer.

3.3 If the Seller objects to a Claim Notice, the matter shall be referred to independent expert determination in accordance with clause 12 below, and the Escrow Agent shall release funds only upon receipt of a binding determination.

4. Payment and Accounting

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall provide the Buyer and the Seller with quarterly statements of the Escrow Fund, including any interest accrued.

4.2 Any interest earned on the Escrow Fund shall form part of the Escrow Fund and shall be distributed in accordance with the provisions for principal.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party as set out in clause 1.1.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received on the day of delivery if delivered by hand, two Business Days after posting if sent by post, and on transmission if sent by email with confirmation of receipt.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent, the Buyer, and the Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the terms of this Agreement and the existence and details of any Claim Notice, except as required by law or regulation or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions.

7.2 This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by all parties.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Escrow Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the International Chamber of Commerce in accordance with its Rules. The seat of arbitration shall be New York, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.
