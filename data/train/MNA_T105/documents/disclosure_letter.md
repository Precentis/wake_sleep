1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Agreement” means the share purchase agreement dated on or about 21 January 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company;  
“Buyer” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“Company” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“Disclosure Letter” means this letter, including any schedules and annexes;  
“Seller” has the meaning given in the preamble;  
“Warranties” means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in the Agreement.  
1.2 The headings in this Disclosure Letter are included for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.  
1.3 Words and expressions defined in the Agreement shall have the same meanings when used in this Disclosure Letter.  

2. General Disclosure  
2.1 This Disclosure Letter is issued pursuant to the Agreement and qualifies the Warranties as set out in the Agreement.  
2.2 The disclosures set out in this Disclosure Letter are specific and shall only qualify the particular Warranty to which they are cross-referenced.  
2.3 The information disclosed in this Disclosure Letter is disclosed solely for the purposes of limiting the liability of the Seller under the Agreement and shall not constitute disclosure for any other purpose unless expressly stated.  
2.4 The Buyer acknowledges receipt of this Disclosure Letter and agrees that the matters disclosed herein are full, fair and accurate as of the date hereof.  

3. Disclosure Against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)  
3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 8.2, the Seller discloses that the Company’s core platform software, used in its vessel tracking and fleet management services, was developed in 2023 by Larkspur Energy plc, an independent contractor.  
3.2 The development of the core platform software was carried out pursuant to a verbal understanding between the Company and Larkspur Energy plc.  
3.3 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or relating to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Larkspur Energy plc.  
3.4 The Company has used the core platform software continuously since 2023 without challenge from Larkspur Energy plc or any third party.  
3.5 Larkspur Energy plc has not asserted any ownership or proprietary interest in the software as of the date of this Disclosure Letter.
