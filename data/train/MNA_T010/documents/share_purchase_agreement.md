SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 15 July 2026.

Parties:

(1) Corrib Energy plc (company number 11395419), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 30 King William Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Kestrel Software Ltd (company number 11255173), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 44 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) The Company referred to herein is Holloway Logistics plc (company number 15330342), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 176 Queen Victoria Street, London.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including all schedules and annexes;

"Buyer" has the meaning set out in the preamble;

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

"Closing Date" means 15 October 2026;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, determined in accordance with Schedule A;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule B;

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

"Material Customer" means Quayside Robotics Ltd (company number 15157820), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 50 Cheapside, London;

"Purchase Price" means the amount specified in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000;

"Termination Right" has the meaning given in Clause 4.5.

2. Sale and Purchase of Shares

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $190,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated by the Seller.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any amount by which the Closing Working Capital falls below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with Schedule C.

3. Earn-out

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller, within 90 days following the end of FY2026, an additional amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000, provided that the Earn-out Payment shall in no event exceed $5,000,000.

4. Conditions Precedent

4.5 If the Conditions have not been satisfied or waived in writing by the Buyer and the Seller on or before the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 14 October 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

12. Non-Competition

12.1 The Seller covenants that for a period of 4 years from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, and shall procure that none of its affiliates shall, directly or indirectly carry on, engage in, finance, assist or otherwise be concerned with any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date within the United Kingdom. This restriction shall apply for the duration of 4 years and shall be deemed to be a continuing obligation throughout that period.

13. Notices

13.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in the preamble, or to such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other in accordance with this Clause.

14. Confidentiality

14.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
