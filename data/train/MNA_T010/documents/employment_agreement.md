1. Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:
- “Business” means the business of warehouse automation, logistics software development, and related technical services carried on by Holloway Logistics plc prior to and following the acquisition of its entire issued share capital by Kestrel Software Ltd;
- “Company” means Holloway Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15330342, whose registered office is at 176 Queen Victoria Street, London;
- “Confidential Information” means any non-public information disclosed to or known by the Employee in connection with the business of the Company, including but not limited to customer lists, pricing structures, technical data, software code, and operational methods;
- “Effective Date” means the date of execution of this Agreement by all parties;
- “Employee” means the individual employed under this Agreement in a senior technical or managerial capacity in relation to the Business;
- “Founder” means the individual who established the core operational model of the Company and who is party to this Agreement in such capacity;
- “Group” means the Company and any subsidiary undertakings from time to time, including any entity controlled by Kestrel Software Ltd following the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company;
- “Kestrel Software Ltd” means the Buyer, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11255173, whose registered office is at 44 Queen Victoria Street, London;
- “Seller” means Corrib Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11395419, whose registered office is at 30 King William Street, London.

2. Employment

2.1 The Company shall employ the Employee, and the Employee accepts employment, upon the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, commencing on the Effective Date.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Employee a salary at the rate specified in the schedule of particulars annexed hereto, payable in arrears on or about the last working day of each calendar month.  
3.2 The Employee shall be entitled to such bonus, pension, and other benefits as may be determined from time to time by the board of the Company, in its sole discretion.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Employee shall not, during the term of employment or thereafter, disclose or use any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of duties for the Company or as authorised in writing by the Company.  
4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment, regardless of the manner of termination.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid letter, or email to the registered office of the Company or the last known residential address of the Employee.  
5.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the attention of the Chief Executive Officer at 176 Queen Victoria Street, London.

6. Post-Termination Restrictions

6.1 The Founder acknowledges that by reason of their position and access to Confidential Information, the restrictions contained in this clause are reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Company and the Group.

6.2 The Founder shall not, for a period of 18 months after the termination of their employment with the Company, whether by resignation, dismissal, or otherwise, directly or indirectly:
   (a) carry on, manage, operate, join, control, finance, or be engaged or otherwise concerned in any business which competes with the Business within the United Kingdom;
   (b) solicit or endeavour to entice away any customer of the Company with which the Founder had material contact during the 12 months prior to termination, including but not limited to Quayside Robotics Ltd (company number 15157820, registered office 50 Cheapside, London);
   (c) solicit or attempt to influence any employee of the Company or Group to terminate their employment.

18. Non-Compete Undertaking

18.1 The Founder covenants and agrees that for a period of 18 months from the date of termination of their employment with the Company, they shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person, carry on, be engaged, concerned, or interested in any business or entity which is engaged in the Business, within the United Kingdom.

19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
19.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
