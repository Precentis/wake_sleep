SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 17 March 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Fenwick Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12204878, registered office at 180 Bishopsgate, London) (the “Seller”).

(2) Ivybridge Industries Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10777826, registered office at 132 Moorgate, London) (the “Buyer”).

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Redcliffe Engineering Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15281404, registered office at 34 Old Broad Street, London) (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing Date” means 20 May 2027;

“Conditions” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“Material Adverse Change” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

“Purchase Price” has the meaning given to it in Clause 2.1.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $165,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full, in immediately available funds, to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller, on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided that no such right to terminate shall be available to any party that is in material breach of its obligations under this Agreement.

5. CLOSING

5.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer such instruments of transfer and other documents as are reasonably required to vest the entire issued share capital of the Company in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post (in the case of addresses in the United Kingdom) or by internationally recognised overnight courier to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 180 Bishopsgate, London, and notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 132 Moorgate, London, or to such other address as a party may designate by prior written notice.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information acquired during the course of negotiations and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings between them in relation to its subject matter.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with warranties provided herein shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $13,200,000. The Purchase Price is $165,000,000 (as stated in Clause 2.1).

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a warranty breach must be notified in writing to the Seller within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim.
