ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] April 2027 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:

(1) Fenwick Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12204878 whose registered office is at 180 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller");

(2) Ivybridge Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10777826 whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a firm of solicitors or financial institution appointed as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

(each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties")

RECITALS:

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Redcliffe Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15281404 and registered office at 34 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company"), pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Effective Date (the "SPA").

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Buyer and the Seller have agreed that a portion of the consideration (the "Escrow Amount") shall be deposited into an escrow account for the purposes of securing potential indemnification claims by the Buyer.

(C) The Parties have appointed the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount under the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

   "Closing Date" means 20 May 2027.

   "Claim Notice" means a written notification from the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of money deposited into the escrow account by the Buyer pursuant to the SPA, intended to secure the Seller's indemnification obligations.

   "Material Customer" means Vantage Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13730319 and registered office at 1 Gresham Street, London.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as agent and depositary for the Escrow Amount, and the Escrow Agent hereby accepts such appointment, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within two Business Days following the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated interest-bearing account at a UK authorised deposit-taking institution, and shall have no right of set-off or lien against the Escrow Amount except for fees and expenses due under this Agreement.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending claims notified before that date. For the avoidance of doubt, if a Claim Notice has been validly delivered prior to the expiry of such 12-month period, the Escrow Agent shall retain such portion of the Escrow Amount as is reasonably necessary to satisfy the claim in question, until final resolution of the claim.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of the SPA.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim, failing which the Buyer shall be precluded from making such claim.
