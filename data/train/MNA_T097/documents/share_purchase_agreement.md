SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 23 October 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Ashcombe Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10803417, whose registered office is at 104 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Corrib Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15799754, whose registered office is at 135 King William Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Company is Rothley Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11741977, whose registered office is at 88 Bishopsgate, London.

RECITALS

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, 'Material Adverse Change' means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $165,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to such bank account as the Seller shall notify to the Buyer at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. CLOSING

4.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on 29 January 2027 (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Seller or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

16. NOTICES

16.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as a party may designate in writing.

16.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received: (a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; (b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, at 9.00 a.m. on the second Business Day following the date of posting; and (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of receipt is received.

17. CONFIDENTIALITY

17.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

17.2 This obligation shall not apply to information which is or becomes publicly available otherwise than as a result of a breach of this clause.

17.3 The provisions of this clause shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.
