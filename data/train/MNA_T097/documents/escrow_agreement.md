ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 5 November 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Ashcombe Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10803417, whose registered office is at 104 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Corrib Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15799754, whose registered office is at 135 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors duly authorised to act as escrow agent under applicable law (the "Escrow Agent").

The parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:  
"Closing Date" means the date on which the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of Rothley Pharma plc by the Seller to the Buyer completes under the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 November 2026.  
"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,750,000, representing 7.5% of the total consideration payable under the Share Purchase Agreement, which has been deposited with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3.  
"Escrow Period" means the period of 18 months commencing on the Closing Date.  
"Rothley Pharma plc" means the company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11741977, whose registered office is at 88 Bishopsgate, London.  
"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of Rothley Pharma plc between the Buyer and the Seller dated 5 November 2026.

2. Establishment of Escrow  
2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold, administer and disburse the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  
2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the provisions herein.

3. Deposit of Escrow Amount  
3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall cause the sum of £4,750,000 to be paid into an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") held by the Escrow Agent.  
3.2 All interest accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be held and disbursed under the same terms.

4. Payment and Disbursement  
4.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, unless a claim has been validly made in accordance with Clause 5.2.  
4.2 Any disbursement from the Escrow Account shall be made by the Escrow Agent by wire transfer to such account as the recipient may notify in writing in accordance with Clause 8.

5. Claims and Adjustments  
5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount in respect of any indemnifiable loss arising under the Share Purchase Agreement.  
5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it, by written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim and the amount claimed (a "Claim Notice").  
5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall not disburse any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to the claim until the matter is resolved by mutual agreement of the Buyer and the Seller or by binding decision pursuant to Clause 12.

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

8. Miscellaneous  
8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.  
8.2 This Agreement may only be amended by written agreement signed by all parties.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration  
12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.  
12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one arbitrator appointed in accordance with said rules, and such arbitration shall take place in New York.
