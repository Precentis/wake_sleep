3. **Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**

   (a) the Company is a party to a material contract dated 14 January 2022 with Saltash Marine Ltd (company number 11253662), incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 24 Cheapside, London, under which the Company supplies medical diagnostic equipment for marine vessels (the "Saltash Contract"); and

   (b) if sent by first-class pre-paid post, 48 hours after posting;  
   (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of receipt is received.
