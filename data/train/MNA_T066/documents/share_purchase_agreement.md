SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 7 July 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Northgate Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (company number 12138749), whose registered office is at 85 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Ingleby Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 12227254), whose registered office is at 64 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Arden Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 13135614), whose registered office is at 14 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions have the following meanings:

   "Closing Date" means 3 September 2026.

   "Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.

   "Purchase Price" means the aggregate amount payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares pursuant to Clause 2.1.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $200,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), to be paid in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in full, in cleared funds, to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller, no later than 5.00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.1 The obligations of the Parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or lawful waiver of the following Conditions:

   (a) all necessary corporate and regulatory approvals shall have been obtained;

   (b) no injunction or restraining order shall have been issued by any court of competent jurisdiction that would prevent the completion of the transaction;

   (c) the representations and warranties of the other Party contained in this Agreement shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement, being 1 April 2027, either the Buyer or the Seller may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other Party.

5. CLOSING

5.1 Completion shall take place at the offices of the Buyer at 10.00 a.m. on the Closing Date, or at such other time, date and place as the Parties may agree in writing.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above (or such other address or email as a Party may notify to the other Party).

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party undertakes that it shall not, and shall procure that its officers, employees and advisers shall not, disclose to any third party any confidential information concerning the other Party, the Company or the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

8.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket) The Seller shall not have any liability in respect of any individual claim or series of claims for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $1,000,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price). If such threshold is exceeded, the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over $1,000,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller shall be liable only for the excess amount and not for any portion of the claims up to or including $1,000,000.
