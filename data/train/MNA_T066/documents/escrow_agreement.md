ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on [●] August 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Northgate Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 12138749 and registered office at 85 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller");

(2) Ingleby Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 12227254 and registered office at 64 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a firm of solicitors acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Arden Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with registered number 13135614 and registered office at 14 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) As part of the arrangements under the SPA, a sum of money (the "Escrow Amount") is to be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 3 September 2026.

"Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer against the Seller under the SPA in respect of which notice has been duly given in accordance with clause 5.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount specified in the SPA to be held in escrow, representing 10% of the total consideration payable under the SPA.

"Parties" means the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent, and "Party" means any one of them.

"SPA" means the share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement do not affect its interpretation.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") with a bank in London, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited by the Buyer on or prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely as agent for the Buyer and the Seller and not as trustee, and the Escrow Agent shall have no beneficial interest in the Escrow Account or any part of its contents.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to clause 4, the Escrow Amount (together with any accrued interest) shall be released by the Escrow Agent to the Seller by wire transfer to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller.

4. Conditions to Release

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending Claims in respect of which notice has been given to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with clause 5 before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such Claim is finally resolved or withdrawn.

5. Notification of Claims

5.1 The Buyer may notify the Seller and the Escrow Agent in writing of any Claim at any time prior to the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date.

5.2 Any such notice shall specify in reasonable detail the nature of the Claim, the factual basis for it and the amount claimed.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or sent by email to the address or email address set out below (or such other address or email as a Party may notify):

(a) in the case of the Seller: 85 Ludgate Hill, London, email: [●];  
(b) in the case of the Buyer: 64 Ludgate Hill, London, email: [●];  
(c) in the case of the Escrow Agent: [●], email: [●].

6.2 A notice given in accordance with this clause shall be deemed to be received when delivered, if delivered personally; 48 hours after posting, if sent by post; and on the date of transmission if delivered by email and receipt is confirmed.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received under or in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
