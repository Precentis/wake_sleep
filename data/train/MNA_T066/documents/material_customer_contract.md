1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a party, a person that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party.

"Agreement" means this contract for the supply of medical robotic systems and associated services between Saltash Marine Ltd and Arden Medical Ltd.

"Control" means the possession, direct or indirect, of the power to direct or cause the direction of management or policies of a company, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise.

"Customer" means Saltash Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11253662 and registered office at 24 Cheapside, London.

"Effective Date" means 1 January 2021.

"Force Majeure Event" has the meaning set out in Clause 17.

"Initial Term" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and ending on 31 December 2025.

"Renewal Term" means any successive one-year period following the Initial Term, subject to Clause 13.

"Services" means the maintenance, software updates and technical support services provided by Arden Medical Ltd in respect of the Products.

"Products" means the medical robotic systems supplied by Arden Medical Ltd to Saltash Marine Ltd under this Agreement.

"Termination Notice" means written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 15.

2. SUPPLY OF PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

2.1 Arden Medical Ltd shall supply the Products to Saltash Marine Ltd in accordance with the specifications agreed in writing and deliver them to the location designated by Saltash Marine Ltd.

2.2 Arden Medical Ltd shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 Saltash Marine Ltd shall pay Arden Medical Ltd the fees set out in the Schedule, payable quarterly in advance within 30 days of the date of invoice.

3.2 All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which shall be paid by Saltash Marine Ltd at the rate prevailing on the date of supply.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party marked as confidential or which by its nature ought reasonably to be treated as confidential.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the recipient or such other address as notified in writing.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received 48 hours after transmission by recorded delivery post or email with confirmation of receipt.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for the Initial Term unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

6.2 Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive Renewal Terms unless either party gives not less than 90 days’ written notice prior to renewal.

14. TERMINATION

14.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:

(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of receiving notice;

(b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation; or

(c) ceases to carry on business.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. For the purposes of this clause, change of control includes acquisition of more than 50 per cent of the issued share capital of Arden Medical Ltd by a third party, including but not limited to Northgate Capital LLP or Ingleby Robotics Ltd. The counterparty is Saltash Marine Ltd.

15. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

15.1 Upon termination, Saltash Marine Ltd shall pay all outstanding fees for Products supplied and Services rendered up to the date of termination.

15.2 Each party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other party.

17. FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 A party shall not be liable for failure to perform its obligations if such failure results from a Force Majeure Event, provided that it notifies the other party promptly and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect.

18. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

18.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

18.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
