# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT** is made on **21 March 2026**

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Whitlock Logistics plc** (company number 13673890), a company incorporated in England and Wales, with its registered office at 44 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) **Northgate Robotics Ltd** (company number 15368415), a company incorporated in England and Wales, with its registered office at 110 Cannon Street, London (the "Buyer").

**RECITALS:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Juniper Systems Ltd** (company number 11774029), a company incorporated in England and Wales, with its registered office at 73 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES** as follows:

---

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4;
- "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;
- "Closing Date" means 24 April 2026;
- "Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under Clause 2.1;
- "Shares" means all of the issued shares in the Company;
- "Survival Period" means the period during which the warranties remain effective under Clause 10.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

1.3 This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, English law. The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

---

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares at Closing for a total consideration of **$125,000,000** (the "Purchase Price"), payable in immediately available funds on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

---

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed by the Seller in favour of the Buyer in respect of all the Shares;

(b) resignations of any directors or secretaries appointed by the Seller;

(c) such other documents and instruments as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares.

---

**4. CONDITIONS**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction (or waiver) of the following conditions:

(a) all necessary third-party consents, including but not limited to any required from **Elstree Energy plc** (company number 11234894), have been obtained;

(b) no order or injunction shall be in effect which prohibits Closing.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling **150 days after the date of this Agreement**, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

---

**8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any and all claims arising under the warranties (including the General Warranties and Fundamental Warranties) shall not exceed an amount equal to **20% of the Purchase Price**, being **$31,250,000**. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $125,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

---

**10. SURVIVAL**

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties (namely, title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of **48 months**. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable Survival Period.

---

**12. POST-CLOSING UNDERTAKINGS**

12.1 The Seller undertakes that it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company for a period of **5 years** after the Closing Date.

---

**13. NOTICES**

13.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

---

**14. CONFIDENTIALITY**

14.1 The parties shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all related information strictly confidential and shall not disclose them to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.
