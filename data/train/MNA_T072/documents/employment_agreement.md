**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT**  
between  
**JUNIPER SYSTEMS LTD** (the Employer)  
and  
**MR THOMAS VAUGHAN** (the Employee)  

This extract forms part of the employment agreement dated on or about 30 March 2025.

---

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Business” means the business of designing, manufacturing, and supplying industrial automation systems for logistics and warehousing operations, as conducted by the Employer at the date of this Agreement.  
“Competitor” means any person, firm, or company engaged in, or concerned with, the Business, including but not limited to Northgate Robotics Ltd, Whitlock Logistics plc, and Elstree Energy plc.  
“Confidential Information” includes trade secrets, customer lists, pricing data, technical specifications, software code, supplier terms, and any other non-public information relating to the Employer or its associated companies.  
“Effective Date” means 1 April 2025.  
“Founder” means Mr Thomas Vaughan, director and founder of Juniper Systems Ltd.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

**2. Employment and Duties**  
2.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Technology Officer of the Employer and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such office and as may be lawfully assigned from time to time by the board of directors of the Employer.  
2.2 The Employee shall devote his full time, attention, and ability to the performance of his duties and shall not engage in any other business or occupation without prior written consent of the Employer.  

**3. Remuneration**  
3.1 The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £145,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in such bonus schemes as may be determined by the board of directors in its sole discretion, subject to the terms of any applicable scheme rules.  

**4. Expenses**  
4.1 The Employer shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable and properly documented business expenses incurred in the performance of his duties, in accordance with the Employer’s expense policy as in force from time to time.  

**5. Hours and Place of Work**  
5.1 The Employee shall work primarily from the registered office of the Employer at 73 Ludgate Hill, London, but may be required to travel as necessary for business purposes.  

**6. Confidentiality**  
6.1 The Employee shall not, at any time during or after the termination of his employment, without the prior written consent of the Employer, use or disclose any Confidential Information, except as required by law.  
6.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment for a period of five years.  

**7. Intellectual Property**  
7.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of his employment shall belong exclusively to the Employer.  
7.2 The Employee irrevocably waives any moral rights in such intellectual property to the extent permitted by law.  

**18. Non-competition**  
18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as a principal, partner, shareholder, director, consultant, or employee, engage or be concerned in any business which competes with the Business for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether such termination is lawful or otherwise.  
18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies to any geographical area in which the Employer carries on business, including but not limited to England, Wales, and the European Economic Area.  
18.3 The restriction in Clause 18.1 shall not apply to the ownership of not more than 3% of the issued share capital of any company whose shares are listed on a recognised stock exchange.  

**19. Notices**  
19.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the last known address or email address of the party.  
19.2 Notices to the Employer shall be sent to: 73 Ludgate Hill, London, marked for the attention of the Company Secretary.  
19.3 Notices to the Employee shall be sent to: the address most recently provided in writing by the Employee to the Employer.  

**20. Entire Agreement**  
20.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes any prior agreement, representation, or arrangement (whether written or oral) relating to its subject matter.  

**21. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
21.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
21.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
