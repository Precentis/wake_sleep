**2. Purchase and Sale of Shares**

2.1 The Purchase Price is $105,000,000. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall transfer to the Buyer full legal and beneficial ownership of the Shares, free from all liens, charges, encumbrances and third-party rights.

**4. Conditions Precedent**

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived in writing by the Parties by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 26 October 2026), either party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement with immediate effect, without prejudice to any rights or remedies either Party may have under this Agreement or at law arising prior to termination.

16.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in the preamble.

16.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received when delivered personally; 48 hours after being posted by first-class pre-paid post; or, if by email, at the time of transmission if sent during business hours on a Business Day, otherwise at 9.00 a.m. on the next Business Day.

17.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.
