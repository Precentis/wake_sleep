ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is dated as of 10 August 2026 and is made between:

(1) Dunmore Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10671401 and registered office at 106 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”);

(2) Corrib Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12149432 and registered office at 88 Gresham Street, London (the “Seller”); and

(3) The London Trust Corporation plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 00987654 and registered office at 50 Fenchurch Street, London, as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated 9 August 2026 (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Orwell Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15046958 and registered office at 70 King William Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) In connection with the SPA, a portion of the purchase price amounting to £1,750,000 (the “Escrow Amount”) has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA.

(C) The parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Amount may be released, claimed, or otherwise dealt with pursuant to this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Claim” means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a right to recover from the Escrow Amount pursuant to the terms of the SPA.

“Effective Date” means 10 August 2026.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £1,750,000 held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement.

“SPA” means the Share Purchase Agreement dated 9 August 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of Orwell Foods Ltd.

2. PAYMENT

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller confirm that the Escrow Amount has been paid to the Escrow Agent, which shall hold it in a dedicated interest-bearing account at Lloyds Bank plc under reference ESDW-8871-FX.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall release funds from the Escrow Amount only upon receipt of joint written instructions signed by the Buyer and the Seller or pursuant to a final arbitration award issued under Clause 12.

3. NOTICES

3.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by pre-paid first-class post or delivered by hand to the recipient’s registered office or such other address as notified in writing.

3.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to 106 Moorgate, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 88 Gresham Street, London. Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to 50 Fenchurch Street, London.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any information received from another party in connection with it, except as required by law or by a regulatory authority.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

5. CLAIMS

5.1 A Claim may be made by the Buyer at any time prior to the date falling 18 months after the Effective Date.

5.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 The Seller shall have 30 days from receipt of a Claim to accept, reject, or negotiate in good faith.

12. ARBITRATION AND GOVERNING LAW

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce, seated in New York, New York.

12.3 The language of arbitration shall be English.

13. MISCELLANEOUS

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to its subject matter.

13.2 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
