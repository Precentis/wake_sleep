# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 29 December 2026.

**Parties**

(1) Yarrow Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14163336, whose registered office is at 55 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) Eskdale Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13963860, whose registered office is at 119 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

(3) Thornbury Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13443771, whose registered office is at 174 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

**Background**

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**Operative Provisions**

1. **Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Claims" means all claims for indemnification made by the Buyer under Clause 9;
- "Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;
- "Closing Date" means 31 March 2027;
- "Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1;
- "Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000;
- "Shares" means all of the issued and outstanding shares in the capital of the Company.

2. **Sale and Purchase of Shares**

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of $110,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. If the Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, no upward adjustment shall be made. Any reduction under this Clause 2.3 shall be applied prior to payment of the Purchase Price.

3. **Closing**

3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or at such other place as the parties may agree, at 10:00 a.m. (London time).

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all instruments of transfer, share certificates and such other documents as are necessary to transfer good and marketable title to the Shares, free from all Encumbrances.

9. **Indemnification**

9.4 The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer against all Claims arising under this Agreement; provided that the Seller shall not be liable in respect of any Claim unless the aggregate amount of all such Claims exceeds $1,100,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount. For the avoidance of doubt, the Seller shall not be liable for any amount up to the $1,100,000 threshold, and liability shall arise solely in respect of the excess above such threshold.

10. **Confidentiality**

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

10.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

11. **Notices**

11.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above, or as otherwise notified.

11.2 A notice delivered by hand shall be deemed received upon delivery; by post, two Business Days after posting; by email, upon confirmed transmission.

12. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England.

12.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
