SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 19 July 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Ingleby Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11401368, whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Northgate Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12870775, whose registered office is at 120 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

BACKGROUND:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Hartwell Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13585789, whose registered office is at 119 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

(C) The Closing is scheduled to take place on 12 September 2026 (the "Closing Date").

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

   "Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles set out in Schedule 1.

   "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 2.

   "FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026.

   "Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Clause 1 shall have the meanings given to them elsewhere in this Agreement.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The aggregate consideration for the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company (the "Purchase Price") shall be $185,000,000, payable in cash on the Closing Date, subject to adjustment under Clause 2.3.

2.2 The Seller shall deliver to the Buyer at Closing all share certificates and instruments of transfer required to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all Encumbrances.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be determined in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 3.

3. Earn-out

3.4 In addition to the Purchase Price, the Seller shall be entitled to receive an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") calculated as follows: the Earn-out Payment equals 3 times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000, such Earn-out Payment to be capped at $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller within 90 days following the final audited financial statements for FY2026.

4. Conditions and Termination

4.5 If the Conditions (as defined in Schedule 4) have not been satisfied or waived in writing by the Parties by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 19 July 2026), either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party.
