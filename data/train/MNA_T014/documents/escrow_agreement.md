ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] August 2026  

BETWEEN:  

(1) INGLEBY RETAIL GROUP PLC, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 11401368, whose registered office is at 132 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”);  

(2) NORTHGATE SYSTEMS LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 12870775, whose registered office is at 120 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”); and  

(3) [ESCROW AGENT NAME], a professional services firm acting as escrow agent (the “Escrow Agent”).  

WHEREAS:  

(A) The Buyer has acquired from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Hartwell Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 13585789, whose registered office is at 119 Cannon Street, London (the “Company”), pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] August 2026 (the “Purchase Agreement”).  

(B) In connection with the acquisition, the parties have agreed that a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer shall be held in escrow by the Escrow Agent, subject to the terms of this Agreement.  

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:  

1. Definitions and Interpretation  

1.1 In this Agreement:  
“Business Day” means a day (other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday) on which banks are open for general business in London;  
“Claim” means any claim made by the Buyer against the Escrow Amount pursuant to Clause 5;  
“Confidential Information” has the meaning given in Clause 8;  
“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £8,750,000 held by the Escrow Agent pursuant to this Agreement;  
“Material Customer” means Larkspur Robotics Ltd, company number 10294762, registered office 126 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“Purchase Agreement” has the meaning given in the recitals;  
“Termination Date” means 17 August 2027.  

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.  

2. Establishment of Escrow  

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a segregated account (the “Escrow Account”) with [Specified Bank], account number [●], in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall not be subject to any lien, set-off or counterclaim by the Escrow Agent or any third party.  

3. Payment of Escrow Amount  

3.1 On the Termination Date, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, or the unclaimed portion thereof, to the Seller, provided that no Claim has been validly notified under Clause 5.2.  

3.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a valid Claim shall be held by the Escrow Agent until such Claim is resolved by written agreement of the parties or determination by the courts of England and Wales.  

4. Notices  

4.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.  

4.2 Notices to the Buyer: 120 Gresham Street, London, Attn: Legal Department.  
Notices to the Seller: 132 Moorgate, London, Attn: Finance Director.  
Notices to the Escrow Agent: [Insert address], Attn: Escrow Services.  

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount  

5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount for any matter arising under or in connection with the Purchase Agreement.  

5.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.  

6. Confidentiality  

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other parties in connection with this Agreement (the “Confidential Information”), except where disclosure is required by law or regulation.  

6.2 The obligations under this Clause 6 shall survive termination of this Agreement.  

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  

7.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
