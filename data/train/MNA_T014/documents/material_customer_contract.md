2. Appointment and Scope of Services  
2.1 The Company hereby agrees to provide the Services to the Customer, and the Customer agrees to receive and pay for the Services, in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.  
2.2 The Company shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.  

3. Payment  
3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees for the Services as set out in Schedule B, payable quarterly in advance within 15 days of the invoice date.  
3.2 All sums due under this Agreement are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the prevailing rate.  
3.3 Without prejudice to any other right or remedy, the Company may charge interest on overdue amounts at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing on a daily basis.  

4. Confidentiality  
4.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, except as permitted by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party, disclose to any third party any confidential information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement.  
4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five years.  

5. Notices  
5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address set out in the preamble of this Agreement or such other address as a Party may notify in writing.  
5.2 A notice delivered personally shall be deemed received when delivered; if sent by post, 48 hours after posting; if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.  

6. Term and Termination  
6.1 This Agreement shall commence on 1 January 2024 and shall continue for an initial term of three years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.  
6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other Party if the other Party commits a material breach of its obligations under this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of receiving written notice specifying the breach.
