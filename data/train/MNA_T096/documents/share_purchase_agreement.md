SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 January 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Saltash Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14233531, whose registered office is at 21 King William Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Brightwater Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13909753, whose registered office is at 21 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

(4) Ashcombe Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14065016, whose registered office is at 115 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer has agreed to purchase, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing Date" means 11 March 2026.

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of $145,000,000 (the "Purchase Price") on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full, in immediately available funds, to the Seller or as it may direct in writing, on the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any excess of Closing Working Capital over the Target Working Capital shall not result in an upward adjustment to the Purchase Price.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the entire issued share capital of the Company (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms and other documents required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post (for addresses within the United Kingdom) or by internationally recognised overnight courier service (for addresses outside the United Kingdom), or sent by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out in this Agreement.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 21 King William Street, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 21 Old Broad Street, London.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information obtained in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive the Closing or any termination of this Agreement.

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES

9.7 If the Buyer becomes aware of any matter giving rise to a claim under any warranty or indemnity contained in this Agreement, it must notify the Seller in writing of such claim within 30 days of becoming aware of it. Failure to give such notice within that period shall invalidate the claim unless the failure was due to reasonable cause and the notice is given as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The Parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).
