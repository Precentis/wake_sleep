**ESCROW AGREEMENT**

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Saltash Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14233531, whose registered office is at 21 King William Street, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Brightwater Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13909753, whose registered office is at 21 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a company authorised to act as an escrow agent under English law, whose registered office is at [Address of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London.

"Closing Date" means 11 March 2026.

"Claim Notice" means a written notification delivered in accordance with Clause 5.2 setting out in reasonable detail the nature, basis and quantum of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

"Company" means Ashcombe Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14065016, whose registered office is at 115 Fleet Street, London.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount of £7,500,000 (seven million five hundred thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 10 February 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller.

"Material Customer" means Galloway Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10637111, whose registered office is at 19 King William Street, London.

"Share Purchase Agreement" means the agreement dated 10 February 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation. References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

**2. Establishment of Escrow Account**

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain an interest-bearing escrow account (the "Escrow Account") with a designated financial institution in London, into which the Escrow Amount shall be deposited on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and shall not be subject to any lien, set-off or counterclaim, save as expressly provided herein.

**3. Release of Escrow Amount**

3.1 The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be held in the Escrow Account as security for the performance of the Seller’s obligations under the Share Purchase Agreement.

**4. Release Mechanism**

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, being 27 September 2027, save to the extent that a Claim Notice has been validly served prior to that date in respect of which the matter remains unresolved ("Pending Claim"). Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until final resolution of such claim, whereupon the undisputed balance shall be released to the Seller and any portion determined to be payable to the Buyer shall be remitted accordingly.

**5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount**

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount only in respect of a breach of a warranty, indemnity or obligation under the Share Purchase Agreement.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified by delivery of a Claim Notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim. Failure to deliver a Claim Notice within this period shall result in the claim being time-barred and unenforceable against the Escrow Amount.

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by prepaid first-class post or by email to the address or email address, as applicable, set out in the preamble or such other address as a party may notify in writing.

6.2 Notices shall be deemed to be received on the earliest of actual receipt, 24 hours after transmission by email (with electronic confirmation), or two Business Days after posting by first-class post.

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
