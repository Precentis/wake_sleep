**Subject:** Disclosure under the Share Purchase Agreement dated 12 September 2026 (the "SPA") in relation to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Robotics Ltd  

1. **Interpretation**  
1.1. Terms defined in the SPA have the same meaning in this letter unless otherwise defined.  
1.2. The following terms have the meanings set out below:  
(a) "Buyer" means Penrith Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13883881;  
(b) "Seller" means Ingleby Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11974449;  
(c) "Company" means Holloway Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11890499;  
(d) "SPA" means the Share Purchase Agreement entered into between the Buyer and the Seller on or about 12 September 2026 relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company;  
(e) "Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Clause 7 of the SPA.  

2. **Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**  
2.1. The Seller hereby discloses pursuant to Clause 7.1 of the SPA as follows:  
(a) There is no litigation, arbitration, administrative, governmental or regulatory investigation, proceeding or inquiry (whether actual, pending or, to the best of the Seller’s knowledge, threatened) before any court, tribunal or public or regulatory authority affecting the Company or any of its assets, operations or financial condition.  
(b) This disclosure is made on a full, true and accurate basis and contains all information necessary to prevent any of the Warranties from being misleading in any respect.  

3. **Payment**  
3.1. The consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller under the SPA comprises a cash sum of £48,000,000 (forty-eight million pounds sterling), subject to adjustment in accordance with the terms of the SPA.  
3.2. The payment shall be made by same-day value sterling wire transfer to the bank account notified in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least three Business Days prior to the Completion Date.  

4. **Notices**  
4.1. Any notice or other communication given under or in connection with this letter shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the address or email address set out at the beginning of this letter, or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other party in writing.  
4.2. A notice delivered personally shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery. A notice sent by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting. A notice sent by email shall be deemed to have been served upon receipt of a delivery confirmation or read receipt, as applicable.  

5. **Confidentiality**  
5.1. The terms of the SPA and this disclosure letter, and all information disclosed pursuant to them, shall be kept strictly confidential by the parties and shall not be disclosed to any third party, except:  
(a) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;  
(b) to professional advisers of the parties who are bound by obligations of confidentiality;  
(c) where the information is already in the public domain (other than as a result of a breach of this clause); or  
(d) with the prior written consent of the other party.  

6. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
6.1. This disclosure letter and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.  
6.2. The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this letter.  

7. **Miscellaneous**  
7.1. This letter constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, arrangements and understandings.  
7.2. Nothing in this letter shall operate as a waiver of any right, power or remedy of either party under the SPA unless expressly stated.
