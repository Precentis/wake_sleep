1. CALL TO ORDER

2. MINUTES OF PREVIOUS MEETING  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 14 June 2026 were reviewed. There being no amendments, the minutes were confirmed as a true and accurate record and approved unanimously.  

3. DIRECTOR’S INTERESTS  
No new interests were declared by any director under Section 177 of the Companies Act 2006.  

4. TRADING UPDATE  
The board received a report from management on the Company’s trading performance for the quarter ended 31 August 2026. Revenue was reported at £8.2 million, an increase of 9.4% compared to the same period in the prior financial year. Gross profit margin remained stable at 32.1%. The board noted continued strong demand from Calloway Energy plc, a material customer incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11113521, registered office 120 Moorgate, London, which accounted for 28% of total revenue during the quarter.  

5. LITIGATION UPDATE  
The board noted a letter before action dated 8 September 2026 from Foxley Foods Ltd threatening proceedings for approximately $2,750,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The letter pertains to deliveries made between 1 March 2025 and 15 July 2025 under a supply agreement dated 10 February 2025. Legal counsel has been engaged to assess the merits of the claim. Management is to monitor developments closely and report to the board biweekly until resolution or formal commencement of proceedings. The board expressed no view on the likelihood of success of the claim at this stage and emphasised the need for factual accuracy in all external communications.  

6. PROPOSED SALE OF SHARE CAPITAL  
The board considered the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Robotics Ltd by Penrith Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13883881, registered office 124 Gresham Street, London. The Seller in the transaction is Ingleby Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11974449, registered office 78 Old Broad Street, London. The board acknowledged receipt of a draft share purchase agreement and confirmed that preliminary due diligence disclosures have been submitted. The board authorised the Company’s solicitors to continue negotiations on terms consistent with board directives previously issued.  

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS  
There being no other business, the Chair closed the meeting at 11:45 a.m.
