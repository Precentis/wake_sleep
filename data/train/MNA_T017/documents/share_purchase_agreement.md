SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 28 August 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Ingleby Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11974449, whose registered office is at 78 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Penrith Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13883881, whose registered office is at 124 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

(each a "Party" and together the "Parties")

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Holloway Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11890499, whose registered office is at 143 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

"Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, as determined in accordance with applicable accounting standards consistently applied.

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

"Closing Date" means 24 November 2026.

"Earn-out Payment" shall have the meaning given in Clause 3.4.

"Purchase Price" shall have the meaning given in Clause 2.1.

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, and the Seller shall sell to the Buyer, the Shares for a total consideration of $45,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash by the Buyer to the Seller on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Buyer shall deliver or cause to be delivered to the Seller at Closing all necessary instruments of transfer, duly executed, to effect the transfer of the Shares.

3.3 The Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

3.4 **Earn-out**: In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an amount (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be calculated based on the audited financial statements of the Company for the year ending 31 December 2026. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $3,000,000. Payment of any Earn-out Payment shall be made within ninety (90) days after the final audited financial statements are approved by the board of the Company.

4. CONDITIONS

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement, being 26 December 2026, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other Party.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with the warranties given in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to ten per cent (10%) of the Purchase Price, being $5,400,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $45,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by recorded delivery, or sent by email to the registered office or designated email address of the relevant Party.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England for the resolution of any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
