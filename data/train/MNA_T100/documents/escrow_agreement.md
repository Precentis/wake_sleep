ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 25 April 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Marlow Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15250828, whose registered office is at 121 Fleet Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Saltash Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14048513, whose registered office is at 105 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Clarke & Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Background

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "Share Purchase Agreement") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Merrion Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10100773, whose registered office is at 44 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

(B) In connection with the Share Purchase Agreement, a portion of the purchase price (the "Escrow Amount") has been deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the indemnification obligations of the Seller under the Share Purchase Agreement.

(C) The parties have agreed to the terms on which the Escrow Agent shall hold and release the Escrow Amount.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London;

   "Closing Date" means 15 July 2027;

   "Claims Notice" means a written notice delivered in accordance with Clause 8 specifying the nature and estimated amount of a claim made by the Buyer under the Share Purchase Agreement;

   "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,750,000 (one million seven hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent on the Closing Date;

   "Material Customer" means Arden Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10037748, whose registered office is at 13 Moorgate, London.

1.2 Clause headings do not affect interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to act in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Holding of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated client account separate from its own funds.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall not invest the Escrow Amount and shall have no liability in respect of lost interest or opportunity cost.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount, or such portion thereof as is not subject to any pending claim, shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending claims in respect of which a Claims Notice has been validly delivered prior to that date.

4.2 Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending claim shall be retained by the Escrow Agent until such claim is finally resolved by agreement of the parties or by binding decision of a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.3 Upon resolution of any claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the balance of the Escrow Amount, if any, to the Seller.

5. Payment

5.1 Any release of funds under this Agreement shall be made by bank transfer to the account(s) notified in writing by the recipient.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by email or pre-paid first-class post to the addresses or email addresses set out in Clause 8.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both parties.
