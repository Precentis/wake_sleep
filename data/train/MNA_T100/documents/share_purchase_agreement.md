**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **31 March 2027**.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Marlow Medical Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number **15250828**, whose registered office is at **121 Fleet Street, London** (the "Seller").

(2) **Saltash Media Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number **14048513**, whose registered office is at **105 Gresham Street, London** (the "Buyer").

(3) **Merrion Medical Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales with company number **10010773**, whose registered office is at **44 Cheapside, London** (the "Company").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. INTERPRETATION**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions shall have the following meanings:

"**Closing Date**" means **15 July 2027**;  
"**EBITDA**" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1;  
"**FY2026 EBITDA**" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ended **31 December 2026**;  
"**Target Working Capital**" means **$3,500,000**;  
"**Earn-out Payment**" has the meaning given in Section 3.4.

**2. SALE AND PURCHASE**

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$85,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced, dollar-for-dollar, by any amount by which the Closing Working Capital (as defined in Schedule 2) falls below the Target Working Capital of **$3,500,000**. Any such adjustment shall be determined in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 2.

**3. PAYMENT MECHANISM**

**3.4** In addition to the Purchase Price, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") within 90 days of the final audited financial statements of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2027. The Earn-out Payment shall equal **three times (3x)** the amount by which the **FY2026 EBITDA** exceeds **$6,500,000**, such payment being capped at a maximum of **$5,000,000**.

**4. CLOSING**

The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the **Closing Date**, being **15 July 2027**, at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or such other date and place as the parties may agree in writing.

**5. NOTICES**

Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by confirmed email to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address or email as a party may notify to the others from time to time. Notices shall be deemed received on the next business day if delivered by email or on the second business day after posting if sent by post.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each party shall keep confidential all information acquired pursuant to this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party. This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**.

**8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

**8.4** The aggregate liability of the Seller under any warranty claim arising pursuant to this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **25% of the Purchase Price**, which equals **$25,500,000**. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is **$85,000,000**, as set out in Section 2.1.
