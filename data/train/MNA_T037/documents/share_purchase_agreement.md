SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 2 December 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Quayside Foods Ltd (company number 15427261, registered office at 115 Bishopsgate, London), a company incorporated in England and Wales (the "Seller").

(2) Eskdale Energy plc (company number 10621710, registered office at 163 Gresham Street, London), a company incorporated in England and Wales (the "Buyer").

(3) Juniper Technologies Ltd (company number 12521165, registered office at 34 Queen Victoria Street, London), a company incorporated in England and Wales (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the Shares on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

(C) The acquisition will be completed on the Closing Date.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 26 January 2027;

"Conditions" means the conditions set out in Clause 4.1;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a party, whether technical, commercial or financial, disclosed in connection with this Agreement;

"Contract" has the meaning given in Clause 5.1(f);

"Claims" means all claims, demands, losses, liabilities, costs and expenses arising under or in connection with this Agreement;

"excess" means the amount by which the aggregate Claims exceed $1,600,000;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $160,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 Upon payment of the Purchase Price, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer share certificates in respect of the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock transfer forms duly completed and executed in favour of the Buyer.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At the Closing, the parties shall deliver or cause to be delivered all documents and instruments required under this Agreement.

4. CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or waiver by the Buyer of the following conditions (the "Conditions") on or before the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement:

(a) all representations and warranties of the Seller contained in this Agreement shall be true and accurate in all material respects as at the Closing Date;

(b) the Seller shall have performed or complied in all material respects with all of its covenants and obligations under this Agreement;

(c) no order, injunction or judgment shall have been made or entered by any court or governmental authority restraining or prohibiting the transaction contemplated by this Agreement.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

5.1 The Seller warrants to the Buyer that as at the date of this Agreement:

(a) it is the legal and beneficial owner of the Shares, free from any Encumbrance;

(b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement;

(c) the execution of this Agreement does not breach any contractual or legal obligation binding on the Seller;

(d) the Company has not traded with Netherby Engineering Ltd (company number 11308633) in the 12 months prior to the date of this Agreement.

9. INDEMNITIES AND LIMITATIONS

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket): The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any Claims unless the aggregate of all such Claims exceeds $1,600,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount, and the term "excess" shall have the meaning assigned in Clause 1.1.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party, except as required by law.

11. NOTICES

11.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant party.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
