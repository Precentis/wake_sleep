2. SUPPLY OF GOODS AND SERVICES

2.1 The Seller shall deliver the Goods to the Buyer’s designated site within 60 days of the Effective Date, in accordance with the delivery schedule set out in Schedule C.

2.2 The Seller shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, in compliance with all applicable industry standards and statutory requirements.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Seller the sum of £8,750,000 in instalments as follows: 20% upon signing, 30% upon delivery of the Goods, and 50% upon successful commissioning and acceptance.

3.2 All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Buyer at the prevailing rate.

3.3 Invoices shall be issued by the Seller and paid within 30 days of receipt. Late payments shall bear interest at 4% per annum above the Bank of England base rate.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall hold the other’s Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of five years.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address as notified in writing.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received if delivered by hand: on the day of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post: two business days after posting.

14. TERMINATION

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon 60 days’ written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within that period.

14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ written notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without the prior written consent of the counterparty. For the purposes of this clause, the counterparty is Netherby Engineering Ltd.
