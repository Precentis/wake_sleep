**3. Disclosure Against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**

3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 6.3 of the SPA, which states that no Material Contract contains a change of control provision that would be triggered by the transactions contemplated by the SPA, the Seller confirms that:

- There are no Material Contracts to which the Company is a party that contain any provision which would be activated, triggered or otherwise affected by reason of a change of control of the Company as a result of the acquisition of its entire issued share capital by the Buyer.

3.2 This disclosure is made on the basis that the Company has no contractual obligations with Netherby Engineering Ltd which constitute Material Contracts under the definition applied in the SPA.

3.3 The Seller further confirms that, to the best of its knowledge, information and belief, there are no side letters, side agreements or oral understandings relating to any Material Contract that impose obligations on the Company which would be affected by the change of control arising from the transaction.
