ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ORWELL MARINE LTD  
(As Amended and Restated in Connection with the Sale of the Entire Issued Share Capital to Arden Bioscience plc)

1. Interpretation  
1.1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Articles" means these articles of association;  
"Buyer" means Arden Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14252197, with registered office at 54 Fleet Street, London;  
"Company" means Orwell Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10659231, with registered office at 57 Cannon Street, London;  
"Company Secretary" means the person for the time being appointed to that office;  
"Director" means any person duly appointed as a director of the Company;  
"Material Customer" means Bellhaven Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13190232, with registered office at 51 Cannon Street, London;  
"Seller" means Elstree Retail Group plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10406680, with registered office at 132 Ludgate Hill, London;  
"Shareholder" means a person in whose name a share in the Company is registered in the register of members.

2. Share Capital and Transfer of Shares  
2.1. The Company's issued share capital immediately prior to the completion of the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital from the Seller consisted of 1,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2. All shares in the Company are fully paid and subject to these Articles and any applicable contractual arrangements entered into by the Company prior to 9 September 2026.  
2.3. No transfer of shares in the Company shall be registered unless accompanied by a duly executed instrument of transfer and such evidence as the Directors may reasonably require to demonstrate title.

3. Board of Directors  
3.1. The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board of Directors, who may exercise all the powers of the Company except such as are by the Companies Act 2006 or these Articles required to be exercised by the Shareholders.  
3.2. The number of Directors shall not be less than two nor more than six, as determined by ordinary resolution of the Shareholders.  
3.3. The initial Directors appointed following completion of the acquisition are: A. N. Smith, J. R. Patel, and M. L. Greene.

4. Meetings of the Board  
4.1. The Board may meet together for the dispatch of business, adjourn, and otherwise regulate its proceedings as it sees fit.  
4.2. Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of the votes cast. In the case of an equality of votes, the chair of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.  
4.3. A Director may, and the Company Secretary shall, when directed by a Director, convene a meeting of the Board by not less than 48 hours’ notice unless urgency requires shorter notice, in which case a meeting may be convened with not less than 12 hours’ notice.  
4.4. Any Director may participate in a meeting of the Board by means of conference telephone or other communications equipment permitting all participants to hear one another, and such participation shall constitute attendance in person.

5. Quorum  
5.1. Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. If at any time during a meeting the number of directors present falls below the quorum, no further business shall be transacted except to adjourn the meeting or to appoint additional directors.

6. Payment and Financial Provisions  
6.1. Any dividend or distribution declared by the Board shall be paid in accordance with the resolution of the Directors and in compliance with the Companies Act 2006.  
6.2. All payments due under any agreement to which the Company is a party and effective prior to 9 September 2026 shall be made in sterling, by cleared funds, to such account as specified in writing by the relevant counterparty.

7. Notices  
7.1. Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.  
7.2. Notice sent by post shall be deemed to have been served 48 hours after posting. Notice by email shall be deemed served at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.

8. Confidentiality  
8.1. All Directors and officers of the Company shall keep confidential all information relating to the business, operations, and financial affairs of the Company, the Buyer, the Seller, and the Material Customer, except as required by law or with prior written consent.  
8.2. This obligation shall survive termination of office or any change in shareholding structure, including the acquisition completed prior to 9 September 2026.

9. General  
9.1. These Articles are governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  
9.2. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
