5. **Litigation Update**  
The board noted a letter before action dated 1 September 2026 received from solicitors acting for Foxley Marine Ltd, threatening legal proceedings in the amount of approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 14 March 2023. The letter asserts breach of contract and seeks compensation for financial losses incurred. The Company denies liability and considers the claim to be without merit. The General Counsel provided a summary of the Company’s position and proposed response strategy. RESOLVED: That management shall continue to monitor the matter closely and report developments to the board on a fortnightly basis until resolution or formal withdrawal of the claim.

6. **Proposed Acquisition by Arden Bioscience plc**  
The Chief Executive Officer presented a draft Share Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) pursuant to which Elstree Retail Group plc (the “Seller”), the sole shareholder of Orwell Marine Ltd (the “Company”), proposes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company to Arden Bioscience plc (the “Buyer”). The Agreement is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. The transaction is conditional upon, among other things, board approval by the Company, confirmation of no material adverse change, and due completion of disclosures. The board reviewed the financial, operational and legal implications of the proposed transaction. Independent legal advice was obtained and a summary was provided to the board. RESOLVED: That the board of Orwell Marine Ltd hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement and authorises the Chief Executive Officer and Company Secretary to take all necessary steps to execute the Agreement and deliver any required consents or confirmations on behalf of the Company.
