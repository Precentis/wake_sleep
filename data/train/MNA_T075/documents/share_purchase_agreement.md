# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 16 August 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) Elstree Retail Group plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10406680, whose registered office is at 132 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Arden Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 14252197, whose registered office is at 54 Fleet Street, London (the "Buyer").

**BACKGROUND**

(A) Orwell Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10659231, whose registered office is at 57 Cannon Street, London (the "Company"), is the owner of all the issued share capital of which is beneficially owned by the Seller.

(B) The Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREEMENT**

In consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 9 November 2026;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as of immediately prior to the Closing, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles consistently applied as set out in the most recent audited financial statements of the Company;

"Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 8;

"Parties" means the Seller and the Buyer collectively, and "Party" means either of them;

"Purchase Price" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, at the Closing, the Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall purchase from the Seller, the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, for a total aggregate consideration of $45,000,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer of immediately available funds to such account or accounts as the Seller shall designate in writing not less than five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with a separate closing statement to be prepared by the Buyer and reasonably agreed by the Seller within thirty (30) days following the Closing Date.

**3. Closing**

3.1 The Closing shall take place remotely via electronic exchange of documents and funds on the Closing Date.

3.2 At the Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms in respect of the Shares, duly executed in blank;

(b) resignations of any directors or secretaries of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably request;

(c) such other documents and instruments as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares.

**4. Notices**

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant Party set out above, or such other address as notified in writing.

4.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, forty-eight (48) hours after posting; if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is received.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates, officers, employees and advisers shall not, disclose to any third party any Confidential Information, except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis;

(b) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

(c) with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 "Confidential Information" means all information, whether oral, written or in electronic form, disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to financial, commercial and operational information relating to the Company.

**6. General**

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

6.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
