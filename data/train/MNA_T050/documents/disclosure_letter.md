# Disclosure Letter

**Dated** 5 January 2026  

**From**: Penrith Energy plc  
**To**: Merrion Industries Ltd  
**Subject**: Disclosure against Warranties in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 5 January 2026 relating to Elstree Capital LLP  

---

**1. Definitions**

1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “**Agreement**” means the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 5 January 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller pursuant to which the Buyer agrees to purchase the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller;  
(b) “**Buyer**” means Merrion Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14625315, whose registered office is at 119 Moorgate, London;  
(c) “**Company**” means Elstree Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15430091, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London;  
(d) “**Seller**” means Penrith Energy plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12740145, whose registered office is at 23 Old Broad Street, London;  
(e) “**Warranty**” means any warranty, representation, undertaking or statement set out in the Agreement to which this Disclosure Letter is annexed.  
(f) any reference to a clause or section is a reference to a clause or section of this Disclosure Letter, unless otherwise stated.

**2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**

2.1 For the purposes of disclosure against Warranty 8.2 of the Agreement concerning intellectual property, the Seller discloses that the Company’s core platform software, which underpins its primary operational functionality, was developed in 2023 by Whitlock Systems Ltd, an independent contractor engaged by the Company for software development services.

2.2 The development of the software was carried out pursuant to a verbal arrangement between the Company and Whitlock Systems Ltd. No formal written agreement was executed governing the scope of work, deliverables, or intellectual property rights arising from the engagement.

2.3 No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between the Company and Whitlock Systems Ltd. As such, the transfer of ownership of any intellectual property developed by Whitlock Systems Ltd during the course of the 2023 development period has not been formally documented.

2.4 The Company has operated on the basis that full rights to use, modify and exploit the core platform software have been acquired through the payment of fees for services rendered and through the conduct of the parties. The software has been used continuously by the Company since its delivery without objection from Whitlock Systems Ltd or any third party.

2.5 To the knowledge of the Seller, no claim, demand or notice asserting ownership over or challenging the Company’s rights in the core platform software has been received from Whitlock Systems Ltd or any other person.

**3. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**

3.1 For the purposes of disclosure against Warranty 6.3 of the Agreement concerning material contracts, the Seller confirms that the Company is party to a material contract with Redcliffe Robotics Ltd (company number 13206093), a company incorporated in England and Wales, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London.

3.2 The contract with Redcliffe Robotics Ltd relates to the provision of data analytics and portfolio management services by the Company and is considered material in nature due to the revenue stream and strategic relationship involved.

3.3 The Seller further confirms that no material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision that would be triggered by the transaction contemplated under the Agreement.

**4. Payment**

4.1 Any reference to payment in this Disclosure Letter shall be construed in accordance with the payment terms set out in the Agreement, including any provisions relating to deferred consideration or adjustments.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice to be given under or in connection with this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as specified in clause 1.1, or to such other address as a party may have notified in writing to the other.

5.2 Notices delivered by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed received two business days after posting. Notices delivered by hand shall be deemed received at the time of delivery.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 The contents of this Disclosure Letter and any information disclosed herein shall be treated as confidential by the Buyer and its advisers and shall not be disclosed to any third party except as required by law, regulation or the rules of any applicable regulatory or listing authority.

**7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

7.1 This Disclosure Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Disclosure Letter.
