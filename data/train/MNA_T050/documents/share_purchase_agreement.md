SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 28 December 2025.

BETWEEN:

(1) Penrith Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12740145, whose registered office is at 23 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Merrion Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14625315, whose registered office is at 119 Moorgate, London (the “Buyer”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

in respect of the entire issued share capital of Elstree Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15430091, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”).

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

“Closing Date” means 13 February 2026;

“Conditions” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

“EBITDA” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied;

“FY2026” means the financial year of the Company ending on 31 December 2026;

“Earn-out Payment” has the meaning given in Clause 3.4;

“Parties” means the Buyer and the Seller;

“Purchase Price” has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $45,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price of $45,000,000 shall be paid in cash by wire transfer to an account nominated in writing by the Seller, upon Closing.

3. POST-CLOSING PAYMENTS

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the “Earn-out Payment”) equal to three (3) times the amount by which the EBITDA of the Company for FY2026 exceeds $4,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be calculated based on the audited financial statements of the Company for FY2026 and shall be paid within thirty (30) days following the date of such audit. The aggregate amount of the Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $3,000,000.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived in writing by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (being 27 April 2026), either Party may, by written notice to the other, terminate this Agreement without prejudice to any rights or remedies accrued prior to termination.

5. PAYMENT MECHANICS

5.1 All payments under this Agreement shall be made in USD, free and clear of any deductions or withholdings, unless the deduction or withholding is required by law, in which case the paying Party shall increase the payment to ensure the recipient receives the full amount it would have received had no deduction or withholding been required.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant Party.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party undertakes to keep confidential all information received from the other Party or the Company in connection with this Agreement and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

8. ASSIGNMENT

8.1 No Party may assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.3 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under or in connection with the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to fifteen per cent (15%) of the Purchase Price, being $8,100,000. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $45,000,000 as set out in Clause 2.1 and the EBITDA threshold for the Earn-out Payment is $4,000,000, with the cap on the Earn-out Payment being $3,000,000.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

10.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
