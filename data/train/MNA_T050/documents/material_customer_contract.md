**EXTRACT OF MATERIAL CUSTOMER CONTRACT**  
**Between Redcliffe Robotics Ltd and Elstree Capital LLP**  

This Agreement is made on 12 April 2023  

**Between:**  
(1) **Redcliffe Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13206093, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London (the “Customer”); and  
(2) **Elstree Capital LLP**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15430091, whose registered office is at 74 Bishopsgate, London (the “Supplier”).  

**Recitals:**  
(A) The Supplier provides financial advisory and asset management services to corporate clients.  
(B) The Customer wishes to engage the Supplier to provide certain services on the terms set out in this Agreement.  

**Now it is hereby agreed as follows:**  

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Affiliate” means, in relation to a party, a person or entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that party.  
“Change of Control” means, in relation to a company, the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting in concert of direct or indirect Control over more than 50% of the voting rights or issued share capital of such company.  
“Confidential Information” means all non-public information, whether financial, commercial, technical or otherwise, disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement.  
“Effective Date” means 12 April 2023.  
“Services” means the financial advisory and portfolio monitoring services described in Schedule A.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.  

**2. Appointment and Scope of Services**  
2.1 The Customer hereby appoints the Supplier to provide the Services, and the Supplier accepts such appointment, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.  
2.2 The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable care and skill, in accordance with generally accepted industry standards and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.  

**3. Term**  
3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of 36 months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.  
3.2 Upon expiry of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives not less than 60 days’ written notice of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term.  

**4. Payment**  
4.1 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the fees set out in Schedule B (the “Fees”) for the Services rendered.  
4.2 All Fees shall be invoiced quarterly in arrears and are due and payable within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice.  
4.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time, accruing from the due date until the date of actual payment, whether before or after judgment.  

**5. Confidentiality**  
5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party, except as required by law.  
5.2 The obligations under this Clause 5 shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.  

**6. Notices**  
6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out in this Agreement.  
6.2 Notices shall be deemed received:  
(a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by first-class post, 48 hours after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a confirmation of receipt is obtained.  

**7. Termination**  
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party:  
(a) commits a material breach of this Agreement which is not remedied within 30 days of notice specifying the breach; or  
(b) becomes insolvent or enters into liquidation.  

**14. Miscellaneous**  
14.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether written or oral, relating to its subject matter.  
14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent.  
14.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.  
14.4 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
