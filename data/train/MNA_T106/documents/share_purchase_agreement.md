**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on **23 December 2026**  

**BETWEEN:**  

(1) **Pemberton Robotics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13104764**, whose registered office is at **27 Cheapside, London** (the **Seller**); and  

(2) **Pemberton Logistics plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15277650**, whose registered office is at **161 Fleet Street, London** (the **Buyer**).  

**BACKGROUND**  

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Netherby Bioscience plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13336881**, whose registered office is at **63 Ludgate Hill, London** (the **Company**).  

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms of this Agreement.  

**IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**  

---

**1. Interpretation**  

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“**Material Adverse Change**” means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.  

---

**2. Purchase and Sale**  

**2.1** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company for a total consideration of **$210,000,000** (the **Purchase Price**).  

**2.3** The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of **Closing Working Capital** below the **Target Working Capital** of **$2,000,000**. Any such adjustment shall be determined in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 3.  

---

**3. Payment**  

The Purchase Price, subject to any adjustments under Section 2.3, shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no less than five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.  

---

**4. Closing**  

The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the **Closing**) shall take place on **28 February 2027** (the **Closing Date**) at 10:00 a.m. (London time) at the offices of the Buyer, or remotely by electronic exchange of documents, as the parties may agree.  

---

**5. Notices**  

Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or sent by confirmed email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as a party may designate by prior written notice. Notices shall be deemed received when delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting, or upon confirmed transmission by email.  

---

**6. Confidentiality**  

Each party undertakes to keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, except to the extent disclosure is required by law or regulation, or is made to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality. This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of **three years**.  

---

**8. Warranties and Liability**  

**8.3** The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to **10% of the Purchase Price**, being **$25,200,000**. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is **$210,000,000** as set out in Section 2.1.  

---

**12. Post-Completion Covenants**  

**12.1** The Seller undertakes that it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any financial interest in any business that competes with the business of the Company for a period of **5 years** after the Closing Date, within the United Kingdom.  

---

**13. General**  

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and understandings in relation to its subject matter. This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
