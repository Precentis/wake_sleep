1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“the Company” means Netherby Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13336881, with its registered office at 63 Ludgate Hill, London;  
“the Buyer” means Pemberton Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15277650, with its registered office at 161 Fleet Street, London;  
“the Seller” means Pemberton Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13104764, with its registered office at 27 Cheapside, London;  
“Bellhaven Analytics Ltd” means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11559702, with its registered office at 58 Queen Victoria Street, London, being a material customer of the Company;  
“Founder” means the individual appointed as Chief Scientific Officer of the Company, whose employment is the subject of this Agreement;  
“Termination Date” means the date upon which the Founder’s employment with the Company ceases for any reason, whether by resignation, dismissal, or expiry of term.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.  
1.3 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.  
1.4 References to any statutory provision include any amendment, re-enactment, or replacement thereof for the time being in force.

2. Employment  
2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder shall serve the Company, as Chief Scientific Officer on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, with effect from the date of execution of this Agreement.  
2.2 The Founder shall report directly to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are normally associated with such office, and as may be reasonably assigned from time to time by the Board of Directors of the Company.

3. Remuneration  
3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £120,000 per annum, payable in arrears in equal monthly instalments.  
3.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any bonus scheme applicable to senior executives of the Company, as determined by the Board in its sole discretion.  
3.3 The Founder shall be entitled to such benefits as are generally available to senior executives of the Company, including private healthcare coverage and a company car allowance of £800 per month.

4. Working Time and Leave  
4.1 The normal working hours shall be 37.5 hours per week, worked over five days, with flexibility as required by the operational needs of the Company.  
4.2 The Founder shall be entitled to 28 days of paid holiday per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays.

5. Confidentiality  
5.1 The Founder shall not at any time during or after the termination of employment, without the prior written consent of the Company, directly or indirectly disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the Company, its business, clients (including Bellhaven Analytics Ltd), suppliers, financial affairs, or technical processes.  
5.2 This obligation shall continue indefinitely after the Termination Date and shall survive any termination of this Agreement.

6. Notices  
6.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class post, or email to the registered office of the Company or the last known address of the Founder.  
6.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the Chief Executive Officer at 63 Ludgate Hill, London.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

18. Non-competition  
18.1 The Founder acknowledges that, by virtue of his position, he has access to sensitive business information and maintains substantial relationships with key clients of the Company, including Bellhaven Analytics Ltd.  
18.2 Accordingly, the Founder agrees that for a period of 18 months after the Termination Date, he shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person or entity, engage or be concerned in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.  
18.3 For the avoidance of doubt, the restricted business includes, but is not limited to, the provision of bioscience research services to commercial clients in the United Kingdom.  
18.4 This restriction shall not apply to the ownership of not more than 0.5% of the issued share capital of any company whose shares are listed or dealt in on a recognised stock exchange.
