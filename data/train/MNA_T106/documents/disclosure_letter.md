**2. Nature of Disclosure**

2.1 This Disclosure Letter is issued by the Seller pursuant to the SPA and qualifies the Warranties as permitted under the terms of the SPA.

2.2 Disclosure in this letter shall be deemed to qualify each Warranty to the extent such disclosure is fairly disclosed in a manner that enables a prudent purchaser to understand the nature and implications of the matter disclosed.

2.3 Any disclosure made in one section of this Disclosure Letter shall be deemed to qualify any other Warranty to the extent the relevance of such disclosure to such other Warranty is reasonably apparent on its face.

**3. Disclosure Against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation)**

3.1 Disclosure against Warranty 7.1 (Litigation): None.

**4. Payment**

4.1 Any payments due under the SPA, including but not limited to the consideration for the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company, shall be made in accordance with the payment mechanisms set out in the SPA.

4.2 All payments shall be made in pounds sterling (GBP) and credited to the account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer in advance of any such payment.

6.2 The obligations under this clause shall survive termination or completion of the SPA.
