The minutes of the board meeting held on 4 December 2026 were reviewed and approved as a true record.

**4. Update on Proposed Acquisition by Pemberton Logistics plc**  
The board received a report from the Chief Executive Officer regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Netherby Bioscience plc by Pemberton Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15277650, registered office at 161 Fleet Street, London) from Pemberton Robotics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13104764, registered office at 27 Cheapside, London).  

The transaction remains subject to due diligence, regulatory approvals, and execution of a definitive share purchase agreement. All directors confirmed their awareness of ongoing obligations under English law and the City Code on Takeovers and Mergers. Legal counsel has advised that the process is progressing in accordance with expected timelines. The board resolved to continue full cooperation with the incoming buyer’s advisers.

**5. Legal Matter – Letter Before Action from Fenwick Robotics Ltd**  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 30 December 2026, issued by Fenwick Robotics Ltd, alleging defective deliveries under a supply agreement. The claim seeks damages of approximately $3,400,000.  

Legal counsel has reviewed the letter and advised that while the allegations are disputed, the matter warrants close monitoring. The Chief Financial Officer confirmed that no provision has been made in the financial statements for the year ending 31 December 2026 in respect of this matter, as the outcome is currently considered uncertain.  

The board instructed management to monitor developments closely, preserve all relevant documentation, and report any material developments at the next scheduled board meeting. No admission of liability has been made, and the Company reserves all of its rights in relation to the claim.

**6. Customer Contract Renewal – Bellhaven Analytics Ltd**  
The Chief Operating Officer reported that contract negotiations with Bellhaven Analytics Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11559702, registered office at 58 Queen Victoria Street, London) for the renewal of the supply agreement are advanced. The proposed term is 36 months, commencing 1 April 2027, subject to final board approval and execution.  

The board expressed support for the continuation of the relationship and authorised the management team to finalise terms, pending legal review.
