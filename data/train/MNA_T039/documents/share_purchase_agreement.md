SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 1 March 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Yarrow Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (company number 11878268), whose registered office is at 107 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Seller");

(2) Thornbury Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales (company number 11971317), whose registered office is at 163 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) Redcliffe Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 11604924), whose registered office is at 50 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

The Buyer and the Seller are together referred to as the "Parties" and each as a "Party".

A. The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B. The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 31 May 2026;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4.1;

"EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with UK GAAP consistently applied;

"FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

"Parties" means the Buyer and the Seller;

"Purchase Price" has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $25,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash by the Buyer to the Seller on the Closing Date by irrevocable bank transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 The Buyer shall bear all bank transfer fees and related transaction costs associated with the payment of the Purchase Price.

3.3 The Purchase Price is exclusive of any value added tax or similar fiscal charge, which, if applicable, shall be borne by the Buyer.

3.4 The Earn-out Payment shall equal two times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $6,500,000, provided that in no event shall the Earn-out Payment exceed $3,000,000. Payment of the Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be made within ninety (90) days after the final audited accounts for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 have been approved by the Buyer’s board.

4. CONDITIONS AND CLOSING

4.1 The obligations of the Parties to complete the purchase and sale of the Shares are conditional upon:

(a) receipt of all third-party consents necessary to transfer the Shares, including but not limited to consents required under any material contract to which the Company is a party;

(b) the representations and warranties of each Party contained in this Agreement being true and accurate in all respects as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date;

(c) no material adverse change having occurred in the financial or trading position of the Company since the date of the latest management accounts delivered to the Buyer.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or duly waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 30 July 2026), either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party.

12. NON-COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that, for a period of 4 years from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as principal, partner, shareholder, director, employee, consultant or otherwise, engage or be concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, at any time after the date of this Agreement, disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the other Party, the Company, or the terms of this transaction, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or sent by email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to hear and determine any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
