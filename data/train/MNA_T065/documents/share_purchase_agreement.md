# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 21 March 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Brightwater Media Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10484682, whose registered office is at 168 Bishopsgate, London (the “**Seller**”).

(2) **Ingleby Analytics Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10220876, whose registered office is at 90 King William Street, London (the “**Buyer**”).

**RECITALS**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Calloway Marine Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12244931, whose registered office is at 57 Moorgate, London (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“**Agreement**” means this share purchase agreement, including the recitals and schedules;

“**Closing**” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

“**Closing Date**” means 20 May 2026;

“**Closing Working Capital**” means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 1;

“**EBITDA**” means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles applied in the Company’s audited financial statements for FY2025;

“**Earn-out Payment**” means the payment calculated in accordance with Clause 3.4;

“**excess**” means the amount by which any aggregate claim exceeds the de minimis threshold or basket, as applicable;

“**FY2026 EBITDA**” means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

“**Fundamental Warranties**” means the warranties set out in Clause 7.1 (Title), Clause 7.2 (Capacity) and Clause 7.3 (Tax);

“**General Warranties**” means the warranties set out in Clause 7.4 to Clause 7.12 inclusive;

“**Purchase Price**” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as adjusted in accordance with this Agreement;

“**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

“**Target Working Capital**” means $3,500,000.

## 2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell and the Buyer agrees to purchase the Shares for a total consideration of **$205,000,000** (the “**Purchase Price**”).

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in cash at Closing by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of **$3,500,000**. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with Schedule 1.

## 3. Earn-out

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an additional amount (the “**Earn-out Payment**”) equal to **2 times** the amount by which **FY2026 EBITDA** exceeds **$6,500,000**, payable within 90 days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at **$3,000,000**.

## 9. Limitations on Liability

9.4 **De Minimis and Basket**: The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer for any claim arising under this Agreement unless the aggregate of all such claims exceeds **$1,025,000** (being **0.5% of the Purchase Price**), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over that amount.

## 10. Survival and Notification

10.1 **Survival**: The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **12 months**. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of **36 months**. Any claim for breach of warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period, and must specify the nature of the claim and the amount of the **excess** for which liability is asserted.

## 11. Notices

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all information received in connection with the Transaction confidential, except as required by law or to professional advisers.

## 13. General

13.1 This Agreement is governed by English law and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

13.2 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
