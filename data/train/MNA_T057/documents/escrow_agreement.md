ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made on [●] December 2026 between:

(1) Juniper Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12048682 and registered office at 71 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Elstree Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14248682 and registered office at 105 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [●], as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date hereof (the "Share Purchase Agreement") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Bellhaven Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14973742 and registered office at 171 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Company").

(B) As part of the arrangements under the Share Purchase Agreement, a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller (the "Escrow Amount") is to be deposited with the Escrow Agent to secure the Buyer’s right to make claims in accordance with the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement.

(C) The parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 8 January 2027.

"Claim" means any claim for indemnification or compensation notified by the Buyer pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount of funds deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent in accordance with Clause 2, which shall be held in a segregated account pending release in accordance with this Agreement.

"Share Purchase Agreement" has the meaning given in the recitals.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Deposit of the Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account") in accordance with the instructions provided by the parties in writing.

2.3 The Escrow Agent shall not be entitled to charge any fees for the performance of its duties under this Agreement unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Buyer and the Seller.

3. Notices

3.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party.

3.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 71 Moorgate, London.

3.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 105 Bishopsgate, London.

3.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address or email specified in its appointment letter.

4. Release of the Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending Claims notified before that date in accordance with Clause 5.2.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a Claim against the Escrow Amount only by delivering to the Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice describing the nature and basis of the Claim, together with reasonable supporting documentation.

5.2 A Claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the Claim.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
