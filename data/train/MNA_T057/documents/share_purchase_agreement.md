SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 15 November 2026

PARTIES  
(1) Elstree Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14248682, whose registered office is at 105 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller").  
(2) Juniper Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12041418, whose registered office is at 71 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").  
(3) The Company is Bellhaven Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14973742, whose registered office is at 171 Ludgate Hill, London.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following expressions have the following meanings:  
"Closing Date" means 8 January 2027;  
"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;  
"Company" means Bellhaven Foods Ltd, company number 14973742;  
"Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;  
"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;  
"Warranty Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer pursuant to the warranties set out in Schedule 3.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE  
2.1 The Purchase Price is $170,000,000, which shall be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.  
2.2 Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions, on the Closing Date the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, with full title guarantee.

3. CLOSING  
3.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date.  
3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer the share certificates in respect of the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed instruments of transfer, together with such corporate documents of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably require.

4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT  
4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the fulfilment of the following Conditions:  
(a) the Buyer having obtained all necessary regulatory consents;  
(b) the Seller having provided evidence satisfactory to the Buyer that all intercompany balances between the Seller and the Company have been settled in full;  
(c) the representations and warranties of each party in this Agreement being true and accurate in all respects as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date.  
4.2 The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions as soon as reasonably practicable.  
4.3 If any Condition is not satisfied or waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement, then either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other, provided that such right of termination shall not be available to any party in breach of its obligations hereunder.

9. INDEMNITIES AND CLAIMS  
9.7 The Buyer must notify the Seller in writing of any Warranty Claim within 30 days of becoming aware of the matter giving rise to such claim. Failure to give such notice within the 30-day period shall result in the waiver of the claim, unless the delay is attributable to the Seller's concealment or misrepresentation.

10. NOTICES  
10.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above, or such other address as a party may notify in writing.  
10.2 A notice shall be deemed to be given: if delivered personally, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, at the expiry of forty-eight hours from posting; if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.

11. GENERAL  
11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and arrangements.  
11.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
