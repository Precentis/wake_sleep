ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] June 2027 (the "Execution Date") between:

(1) Eskdale Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10576470, whose registered office is at 134 Queen Victoria Street, London ("Seller");

(2) Upton Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11543647, whose registered office is at 126 Old Broad Street, London ("Buyer"); and

(3) [●], a bank or law firm appointed as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the "Parties" and individually a "Party".

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date (the "SPA") pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Elstree Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15028411, whose registered office is at 130 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) As part of the arrangements under the SPA, the Buyer has deposited certain funds (the "Escrow Amount") with the Escrow Agent to secure the obligations of the Seller in respect of certain indemnities and warranties.

(C) The Parties wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 10 September 2027.

"Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer under the SPA in respect of which the Escrow Amount may be drawn prior to release.

"Escrow Amount" means the amount of funds deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3, as specified in the SPA.

"Kingsmere Foods Ltd" means the company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15755882, whose registered office is at 128 Cheapside, London.

"SPA" means the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about the Execution Date between the Buyer and the Seller relating to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as extended, applied, amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.

1.3 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. Appointment of Escrow Agent

2.1 The Parties hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment on the terms set out herein.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall have no duties or obligations except those expressly set out in this Agreement.

3. Deposit of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within five (5) Business Days of the Closing Date.

3.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account and shall not invest or re-deposit such funds except as directed jointly in writing by the Buyer and the Seller.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any pending Claims notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent prior to that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a pending Claim shall remain held by the Escrow Agent until such Claim is finally resolved pursuant to the terms of the SPA.

4.2 Upon the resolution of any pending Claim, the Escrow Agent shall release the undisputed balance of the Escrow Amount to the Seller within five (5) Business Days of receipt of joint written instructions from the Buyer and the Seller or, in the absence of agreement, a final determination under the SPA.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the addresses or email addresses specified in the SPA.

5.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 126 Old Broad Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 134 Queen Victoria Street, London.

5.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address or email specified in writing by the Escrow Agent to the Parties.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
