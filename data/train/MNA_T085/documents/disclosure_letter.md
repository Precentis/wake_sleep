**2. General Disclosure**

2.1 The Seller discloses against the Warranties, and the Buyer acknowledges receipt of the Disclosed Matters set out in this letter. The Seller confirms that the information contained herein is true, accurate and complete in all material respects as at the date of this letter.

2.2 This Disclosure Letter is given in connection with, and forms part of, the Share Purchase Agreement dated 22 June 2027. The disclosures made herein qualify the Warranties solely to the extent expressly set out.

2.3 The disclosure of any matter shall not be deemed to constitute an admission that such matter is material or would give rise to a Claim under the Share Purchase Agreement.

---

**3. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**

3.1 The Company’s core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Netherby Industries Ltd. No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between Netherby Industries Ltd and the Company in respect of such software.

3.2 The Company currently uses the core platform software in the ordinary course of its business. The software is integral to the Company’s digital ordering and inventory management systems.

3.3 The Seller has not received any claim or notice alleging infringement, misuse or violation of any third party’s intellectual property rights in connection with the core platform software.

3.4 The Company has not registered any intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software.
