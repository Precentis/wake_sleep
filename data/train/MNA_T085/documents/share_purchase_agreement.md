SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 3 June 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Upton Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11543647, whose registered office is at 126 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(2) Eskdale Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10576470, whose registered office is at 134 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Elstree Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15028411, whose registered office is at 130 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 10 September 2027;

   "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the sale and purchase of the Shares, as set out in Section 2.1;

   "Target Working Capital" means $3,500,000.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $230,000,000, which shall be satisfied in cash on the Closing Date, subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three (3) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $3,500,000. Any excess of Closing Working Capital over the Target Working Capital shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller within ten (10) Business Days following determination of such amount.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares (the "Closing") shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors, or at such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At the Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all documents and instruments reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all liens, charges and encumbrances.

8. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

8.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims arising under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being $27,600,000. The Purchase Price is $230,000,000 (as set out in Section 2.1).

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis and under an obligation of confidentiality.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the address or email address set out above, or such other address or email address as a party may designate by notice.

10.2 Notices shall be deemed received on the business day following transmission by email or delivery in person, or three (3) business days after posting.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
