**DISCLOSURE LETTER**

This Disclosure Letter is made on 8 May 2027  

**BETWEEN:**  
(1) **Lowther Robotics Ltd** (the “Seller”), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12514606 and registered office at 125 Queen Victoria Street, London; and  
(2) **Kingsmere Industries Ltd** (the “Buyer”), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13757405 and registered office at 155 Cheapside, London.  

**RECITALS:**  
(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire, and the Seller has agreed to sell, the entire issued share capital of Kestrel Energy plc (the “Company”), a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12990482 and registered office at 105 King William Street, London, pursuant to a share purchase agreement dated on or about the date of this letter (the “SPA”).  
(B) The Seller has given certain warranties in the SPA, and this letter constitutes full and specific disclosure pursuant to Clause 10 (Disclosure) of the SPA in relation to such warranties.  

**1. Definitions**  
In this Disclosure Letter:  
“Disclosed Matter” means any matter, fact, circumstance, liability or event disclosed in this letter;  
“Warranty” means any warranty or representation given by the Seller in the SPA;  
Capitalised terms not otherwise defined have the meanings ascribed in the SPA.  

**2. Scope of Disclosure**  
This Disclosure Letter is made solely for the purposes of qualifying the Warranties in the SPA and does not constitute admission of liability, fault or obligation on the part of the Seller, nor shall it be construed as a waiver of any rights under the SPA. All Disclosed Matters are incorporated by reference into the relevant Warranties.  

**3. General Disclosure**  
The disclosures in this letter qualify only the Warranties to the extent expressly indicated. Where a matter is disclosed under one Warranty, it is deemed disclosed in relation to any other Warranty to which it is reasonably apparent that the matter is relevant.  

**4. Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**  
4.1 The Seller confirms that no material contract to which the Company is a party contains a change of control provision. No further disclosure is required under Warranty 6.3.  

**5. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**  
5.1 The Seller discloses that the Company’s core platform software, utilised in its primary operations, was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, **Northgate Pharma plc**.  
5.2 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Northgate Pharma plc. As a result, the Company does not hold documented legal title to the intellectual property in the software.  
5.3 The Company continues to operate and rely on the software in its ordinary course of business, and Northgate Pharma plc has not asserted any claim or right in relation to the software since its development.  

**6. Payments**  
Except as disclosed in the financial statements delivered prior to the date of the SPA, no payments, fees, royalties or other compensation are currently due by the Company to Northgate Pharma plc in respect of the development or use of the core platform software.  

**7. Notices**  
Any notice given under this Disclosure Letter shall be in writing and delivered to the recipient’s registered office for the time being, or such other address as may be notified in writing by either party to the other, and shall be served in accordance with Clause 17 (Notices) of the SPA.  

**8. Confidentiality**  
The contents of this Disclosure Letter and all Disclosed Matters shall be treated as confidential by the Buyer and its advisers in accordance with Clause 14 (Confidentiality) of the SPA and shall not be disclosed to any third party except as required by law or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis subject to confidentiality obligations.  

**9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
This Disclosure Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Disclosure Letter.  

**10. Entire Agreement**  
This Disclosure Letter, together with the SPA, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, correspondence and agreements, whether written or oral.
