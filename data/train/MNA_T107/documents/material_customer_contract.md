1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means this contract between Oakhurst Media Ltd and Kestrel Energy plc dated on or about 10 May 2027, including its schedules and appendices.

“Buyer” means Kingsmere Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13757405, with its registered office at 155 Cheapside, London.

“Change of Control” means, in relation to a company, the acquisition by any person or group of persons acting in concert of direct or indirect control of more than 50% of the voting rights attached to the issued share capital of that company, or the right to appoint or remove a majority of its board of directors.

“Company” means Kestrel Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12990482, with its registered office at 105 King William Street, London.

“Seller” means Lowther Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12514606, with its registered office at 125 Queen Victoria Street, London.

“Services” means the energy supply and management services provided by the Company to the Customer pursuant to Schedule 1.

“Term” means the initial period of five years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms herein.

2. Supply of Services

2.1 The Company shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with the specifications, service levels and performance standards set out in Schedule 1.

2.2 The Company shall ensure that all personnel performing the Services are suitably qualified and experienced.

3. Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Company the fees set out in Schedule 2 within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

3.2 All amounts stated are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer at the applicable rate.

3.3 Any late payment shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per annum above the base rate of Lloyds Bank plc, accruing from the due date until actual payment.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall hold in confidence all information received from the other party marked as confidential or which reasonably ought to be considered confidential.

4.2 The obligations in Clause 4.1 shall not apply to information which is publicly available, independently developed or required to be disclosed by law.

4.3 These obligations shall survive termination of the Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party as set out above.

5.2 Notice shall be deemed effective if delivered by hand on the day of delivery, or if sent by registered post, two (2) business days after posting.

6. Termination

6.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days’ written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within twenty-one (21) days of written notice.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent.

6.3 Upon termination, all outstanding fees for Services rendered shall become immediately due.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.

7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.

7.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of each party.

7.4 The rights and obligations under this Agreement may not be assigned without the prior written consent of the other party.

7.5 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force.
