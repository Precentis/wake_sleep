# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 April 2027.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Lowther Robotics Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12514606, whose registered office is at 125 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(2) **Kingsmere Industries Ltd**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13757405, whose registered office is at 155 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

The Seller and the Buyer are together referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**BACKGROUND**

(A) The Seller holds the entire issued share capital of **Kestrel Energy plc**, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12990482, whose registered office is at 105 King William Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 2;

"Closing Date" means 8 July 2027;

"Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as at the Closing Date, determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles;

"Claims" means all claims made by the Buyer against the Seller under this Agreement for breach of warranty or otherwise;

"excess" means the amount by which the aggregate of all Claims exceeds $200,000;

"Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable by the Buyer for the Shares as specified in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Target Working Capital" means $5,000,000.

1.2 The terms defined in this Clause 1 shall have the same meaning throughout this Agreement.

## 2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of **$40,000,000** (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $5,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and paid in accordance with an agreed procedure set out in Schedule 2.

## 9. Limitations on Liability

9.4 **De Minimis and Basket** – The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any Claims unless the aggregate amount of all such Claims exceeds **$200,000 (0.5% of the Purchase Price)**, in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over that amount. This threshold shall not apply to claims arising from fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

## 12. Non-Compete Obligations

12.1 The Seller undertakes that, for a period of **5 years** from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, manage, operate, join, control or be concerned with or interested in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the Closing Date, anywhere in England and Wales.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

13.2 This obligation shall survive the Closing for a period of 5 years.

## 14. Notices

14.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

14.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 155 Cheapside, London, and to the attention of the Company Secretary.

14.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 125 Queen Victoria Street, London, and to the attention of the Finance Director.

## 15. General

15.1 This Agreement (and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it) shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
