EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 8 May 2027 between:

(1) Kestrel Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12990482, whose registered office is at 105 King William Street, London (the "Company"); and

(2) Mr. Julian Hartley, residing at 22 Park Lane, London (the "Employee").

The Company and the Employee are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, any body corporate that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person;

   "Business" means the business of developing, licensing and operating advanced energy storage systems and related digital monitoring platforms carried out by the Company and any of its Affiliates as at the date of this Agreement;

   "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Clause 13;

   "Effective Date" means 10 May 2027;

   "Founder" means Mr. Julian Hartley in his capacity as founder of the Company.

2. Position and Duties

2.1 The Employee shall hold the position of Chief Innovation Officer and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer.

2.2 The Employee shall perform such duties and exercise such powers consistent with such office as may from time to time be assigned to him by the Board.

3. Commencement and Duration

3.1 The employment of the Employee shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

4. Remuneration

4.1 The Company shall pay the Employee a salary of £145,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme operated by the Company or its parent, subject to the terms of such scheme, with a target bonus opportunity of 45% of annual salary.

5. Benefits

5.1 The Employee shall be entitled to participate in the Company’s share incentive plan, subject to its terms and conditions, and shall be granted an initial award of 0.7% of the fully diluted share capital of the Company.

6. Working Time and Place

6.1 The Employee shall be based at the Company’s registered office, 105 King William Street, London, but may be required to travel as necessary for business purposes.

7. Expenses

7.1 The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses properly incurred by him in the performance of his duties, upon receipt of receipts or other appropriate evidence.

8. Holiday

8.1 The Employee shall be entitled to 28 days of paid holiday per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays.

9. Sickness and Incapacity

9.1 The Employee shall be entitled to full salary for up to 26 weeks in any period of certified sickness, in accordance with the Company’s sickness policy.

10. Termination

10.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either Party giving 12 months’ written notice to the other.

11. Payments on Termination

11.1 Upon termination of employment, the Employee shall be paid all accrued but unpaid salary and accrued holiday entitlement.

12. Notices

12.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the Company or the last known address of the Employee.

13. Confidentiality

13.1 The Employee shall not at any time during or after employment disclose or make use of any Confidential Information.

13.2 "Confidential Information" includes all non-public information concerning the Company, its Affiliates, clients (including Oakhurst Media Ltd, company number 12564141), suppliers, operations, technology, finances, or business strategy.

14. Intellectual Property

14.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of employment shall belong to the Company.

15. Governing Law

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

18. Non-Competition

18.1 The Founder shall not, for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, whether by resignation, dismissal or otherwise, engage, be employed, concerned or interested in any business which competes with the Business anywhere in the United Kingdom.
