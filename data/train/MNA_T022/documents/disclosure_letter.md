**Disclosure Letter**

1. **Interpretation**

1.1 In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Agreement” means the share purchase agreement dated on or about 29 March 2027 between Northgate Media Ltd (the Seller) and Rothley Capital LLP (the Buyer) for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Engineering Ltd (the Company).

“Buyer” means Rothley Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10823575, with its registered office at 40 Old Broad Street, London.

“Company” means Pemberton Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11455286, with its registered office at 93 Bishopsgate, London.

“Seller” means Northgate Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12725689, with its registered office at 37 King William Street, London.

“Warranties” means the representations and warranties given by the Seller to the Buyer under the Agreement.

1.2 Capitalised terms not defined in this Disclosure Letter have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

2. **Disclosure against Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts)**

2.1 The Seller confirms that, in relation to Warranty 6.3 (Material Contracts), there is no material contract to which the Company is a party that contains a change of control provision.

2.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this constitutes full and specific disclosure in respect of Warranty 6.3 and no further disclosure is required under this Warranty.

3. **Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (IP)**

3.1 The Seller discloses that the Company's core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Yarrow Systems Ltd.

3.2 The Seller further discloses that no written assignment of intellectual property rights was entered into between the Company and Yarrow Systems Ltd in respect of the core platform software.

3.3 The Company continues to use the core platform software in the ordinary course of its business and has not received any claim asserting ownership or infringement of intellectual property rights in respect of the software.

3.4 The Seller acknowledges that this disclosure is made in relation to Warranty 8.2 (IP) and qualifies the extent of the representation made under that Warranty.

3.5 It is expressly disclosed that no written assignment of intellectual property rights in the core platform software from Yarrow Systems Ltd to the Company exists.

3.6 This disclosure includes the fact that no written assignment has been executed, and accordingly, no written assignment is in place to transfer ownership of the intellectual property in the core platform software to the Company.

4.1 This Disclosure Letter is made subject to and on the terms set out in the Agreement.

4.2 Disclosure in this letter is limited to the specific matters disclosed in the relevant numbered clauses and does not constitute disclosure of any other matter, whether of a similar or dissimilar nature, unless expressly stated.

4.3 The disclosures set out in this letter are made solely for the purposes of qualifying the Warranties in the Agreement and do not create any independent right or obligation beyond that context.
