2.1 The Company shall use reasonable endeavours to provide the Services in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule 1 and in a professional and workmanlike manner.

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees in accordance with the payment schedule set out in Schedule 1.

3.2 All invoices issued by the Company shall be due and payable within 30 days of the date of invoice. Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 4% per annum above the base lending rate of Barclays Bank PLC from time to time, accruing on a daily basis.

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other party and shall not disclose it to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

4.2 The obligations under this Clause 4 shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, two business days after posting; if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that a delivery confirmation is received.

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of three years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.
