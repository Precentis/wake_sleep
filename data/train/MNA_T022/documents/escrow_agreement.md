ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on 25 March 2027 (the "Effective Date") by and among:

(1) Northgate Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12725689, whose registered office is at 37 King William Street, London (the "Seller");

(2) Rothley Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10823575, whose registered office is at 40 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a licensed escrow agent, acting as escrow agent hereunder (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

A. The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated on or about 29 March 2027 (the "SPA"), pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Pemberton Engineering Ltd.

B. As part of the arrangements under the SPA, a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller (the "Escrow Amount") is to be deposited into an escrow account to secure certain indemnities and warranties given by the Seller.

C. The Parties now wish to enter into this Agreement to establish the terms upon which the Escrow Amount shall be held, administered and released by the Escrow Agent.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London for general business.

"Closing Date" means 29 April 2027.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £2,500,000 (two million five hundred thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account established under this Agreement.

"Escrow Account" means the designated account held with the Escrow Agent for the purpose of holding the Escrow Amount.

"Parties" means collectively the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"Pending Claim" means any written notice of a claim made by the Buyer against the Seller under the SPA, delivered to the Seller and the Escrow Agent in accordance with Clause 5, which remains unresolved as of any relevant release date.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account with a financial institution acceptable to the Buyer and the Seller.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be deposited into the Escrow Account within two (2) Business Days following the Closing Date.

3. Interest and Earnings

3.1 Any interest or earnings accrued on the Escrow Amount shall form part of the Escrow Amount and shall be held and disbursed in accordance with this Agreement.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of any Pending Claim notified before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a Pending Claim shall remain in the Escrow Account until such claim is finally resolved, whereupon the undisputed balance, if any, shall be released to the Seller.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as notified in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 37 King William Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 40 Old Broad Street, London.

5.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to the address specified in its appointment letter.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received under or in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
