SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 8 March 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Northgate Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12725689, whose registered office is at 37 King William Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Rothley Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10823575, whose registered office is at 40 Old Broad Street, London (the “Buyer”).

(3) The Company is Pemberton Engineering Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11455286, whose registered office is at 93 Bishopsgate, London.

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   “Closing Date” means 29 April 2027;

   “Company” means Pemberton Engineering Ltd, company number 11455286;

   “Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the sale and purchase of the Shares, as set out in Clause 2.1;

   “Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $140,000,000. The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller in full, in cleared funds, by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The sale and purchase of the Shares shall take place on the Closing Date, at which time:

   (a) the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer or its solicitors, in form and substance satisfactory to the Buyer, all share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee, together with the share certificates (if any) representing the Shares and such other documents of title and transfer as may be reasonably required by the Buyer;

   (b) the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1;

   (c) the Seller shall deliver a certificate of non-foreign status under Section 1.1445-2 of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code (or any successor provision) if applicable;

   (d) all necessary share transfer stamps and other applicable taxes or duties shall be paid by the party customarily responsible under applicable law.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of any other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except:

   (a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis;

   (b) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

   (c) with the prior written consent of the other party.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

5. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

5.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

5.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
