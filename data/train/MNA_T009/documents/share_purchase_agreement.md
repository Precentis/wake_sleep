SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 28 July 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Elstree Holdings Ltd (company number 14122506), whose registered office is at 21 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”).

(2) Ivybridge Bioscience plc (company number 13181466), whose registered office is at 55 King William Street, London (the “Seller”).

BACKGROUND

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Marine Ltd (company number 15074292), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 29 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the shares in the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Closing” means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

“Closing Date” means 4 October 2026;

“Confidential Information” has the meaning given in Clause 11;

“General Warranties” means the warranties set out in Schedule 2 (General Warranties), excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

“Fundamental Warranties” means the warranties relating to title, capacity and tax as set out in Schedule 2;

“Purchase Price” means $185,000,000;

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell and the Buyer agrees to purchase the Shares for the Purchase Price of $185,000,000, payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to the Seller's nominated account on the Closing Date.

3.2 Bank details for payment shall be notified in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

4. CLOSING

4.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

4.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed share transfer forms;

(b) resignations of any directors and secretaries of the Company who are not to be retained, in such form as the Buyer may reasonably require; and

(c) such other documents as the Buyer may reasonably require to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party.

5.2 A notice shall be deemed received at the time of delivery if delivered personally or by email (provided that confirmation of transmission is retained), and 48 hours after posting if sent by post.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, except where disclosure is required by law or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. COUNTERPARTS

8.1 This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

9. LIMITATIONS ON LIABILITY

9.4 The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $925,000 (being 0.5% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over $925,000, and the amount of any liability shall be calculated after deducting such $925,000 threshold; provided that the excess of such aggregate claims over $925,000 shall be the sole measure of recoverable loss, and no claim shall be actionable or otherwise enforceable until such threshold is exceeded and only the excess shall be recoverable.

10. SURVIVAL

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under the warranties must be notified in writing to the Seller before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

12. NON-COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller agrees that it shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in or carry on any business which is competitive with the business of the Company for a period of 4 years following the Closing Date.
