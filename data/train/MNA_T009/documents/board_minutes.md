# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Ingleby Marine Ltd

**Company:** Ingleby Marine Ltd (company number 15074292), incorporated in England and Wales, registered office at 29 Bishopsgate, London.

**Date of Meeting:** 15 August 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** Virtual meeting conducted via secure video conference.

## Attendance

1. The following directors were present for the entire meeting:
   - Dr. Eleanor Hart
   - Mr. Julian Pike

2. The meeting was convened pursuant to notice given in accordance with the company’s articles of association. 2 directors were present. A quorum of 2 directors was present, constituting a quorate meeting under the company’s governance rules, despite the total board consisting of five directors.

## Agenda Item 1: Declaration of Interests

3. No director present had any direct or indirect interest in the matters to be considered that required disclosure under the Companies Act 2006 or the company’s internal procedures.

## Agenda Item 2: Review of Proposed Transaction with Elstree Holdings Ltd

4. The board considered a proposed transaction whereby Elstree Holdings Ltd (company number 14122506), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 21 Cheapside, London, intends to acquire the entire issued share capital of Ingleby Marine Ltd from Ivybridge Bioscience plc (company number 13181466), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 55 King William Street, London.

5. The transaction is to be effected by way of a Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) on terms consistent with standard market practice and governed by English law, to be adjudicated in the courts of England.

6. The board reviewed a summary of the SPA, including key commercial terms, post-completion arrangements, and representations and warranties. The board noted that no consideration amount, price adjustment mechanism, or indemnity provisions were subject to disclosure in these minutes under current board protocols.

7. The board acknowledged the strategic implications of the change in ownership and confirmed that all material contracts remain in full force and effect. In particular, the board confirmed that the supply agreement with Saltash Robotics Ltd (company number 13090625), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 116 Ludgate Hill, London, remains active and unaffected by the transaction.

## Agenda Item 3: Approval of Entry into Share Purchase Agreement

8. After due deliberation, the board considered whether to approve the Company’s entry into the Share Purchase Agreement as part of the proposed transaction.

9. The directors confirmed that, in their collective judgment, the terms of the SPA are in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders, and that execution of the agreement would not prejudice the Company’s ongoing operations or obligations.

10. It was RESOLVED unanimously that the Company approve entry into the Share Purchase Agreement with Elstree Holdings Ltd, as purchaser, and Ivybridge Bioscience plc, as vendor, for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

11. The directors further resolved that Dr. Eleanor Hart and Mr. Julian Pike be, and hereby are, authorised jointly to execute the Share Purchase Agreement and any ancillary documents necessary to give effect to the transaction, on behalf of the Company.

## Adjournment

12. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
