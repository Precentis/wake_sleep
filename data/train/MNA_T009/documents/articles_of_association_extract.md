ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF INGLEBY MARINE LTD

The following are extracts from the Articles of Association of Ingleby Marine Ltd (company number 15074292), incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 29 Bishopsgate, London.

1. Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;

“Buyer” means Elstree Holdings Ltd, company number 14122506, incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 21 Cheapside, London;

“Company” means Ingleby Marine Ltd, company number 15074292, incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 29 Bishopsgate, London;

“Seller” means Ivybridge Bioscience plc, company number 13181466, incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 55 King William Street, London;

“Major Customer” refers to Saltash Robotics Ltd, company number 13090625, incorporated in England and Wales, with registered office at 116 Ludgate Hill, London, responsible for 37.4% of the Company’s total revenue in the financial year ended 31 December 2025;

“Directors” means the directors of the Company for the time being;

“Meeting” means a meeting of shareholders or directors, as applicable.

1.2 Expressions defined in the Companies Act 2006 have the same meaning when used in these Articles unless otherwise defined.

2. Share Capital and Variation of Rights

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall consist of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

2.2 The Company shall not issue any preference shares or shares with differential voting or dividend rights without the prior written consent of the Buyer, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld following completion of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital by the Buyer from the Seller.

3. Payment of Dividends

3.1 Subject to the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, dividends may be declared and paid by ordinary resolution of the shareholders.

3.2 No dividend shall be paid if the Company is in material breach of any supply contract with the Major Customer as of the proposed payment date.

4. Board Conduct and Meetings

4.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all powers of the Company except those reserved to shareholders by these Articles or by statute.

4.2 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter.

4.3 Notice of a board meeting shall be given at least five Business Days in advance to each Director, unless all Directors waive such notice in writing.

4.4 Resolutions may be passed in writing with the unanimous consent of all Directors entitled to vote.

4.5 A director may appoint another Director as proxy to attend and vote at any board meeting by written instrument delivered to the Company at least 24 hours before the meeting.

5. Quorum and Voting

5.1 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

5.2 If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, and a quorum shall then be deemed present if at least two directors attend.

5.3 Decisions of the Board shall be determined by a simple majority of those Directors present and voting.

6. Notices

6.1 Any notice required under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.

6.2 Notices to the Company shall be valid if delivered to 29 Bishopsgate, London, or sent by pre-paid first-class post to that address.

6.3 Notices shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting or upon confirmed electronic receipt if by email to an official company address.

7. Confidentiality

7.1 Directors and officers shall not disclose any confidential information concerning the Company’s operations, customers, or financial condition to any third party, except as required by law or in the proper performance of their duties.

7.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of any individual’s appointment as a Director.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

8.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising under or in connection with these Articles.
