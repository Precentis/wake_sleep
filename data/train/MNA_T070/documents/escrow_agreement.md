ESCROW AGREEMENT

THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] June 2026 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Hartwell Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15454121, whose registered office is at 148 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”);

(2) Bellhaven Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12594034, whose registered office is at 171 King William Street, London (the “Seller”); and

(3) [●], a professional trustee or escrow agent appointed by the Parties (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent are referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party”.

BACKGROUND

(A) The Buyer has entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about 21 June 2026 (the “SPA”) to acquire the entire issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13941856 and registered office at 77 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), from the Seller.

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Parties have agreed that a portion of the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller shall be deposited into an escrow account to secure certain indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The Parties have appointed the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount (as defined below) in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Claim” means a written notification by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a liability of the Seller under Clause 5.

“Closing” means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares under the SPA on 22 June 2026.

“Closing Date” means 22 June 2026.

“Confidential Information” means all non-public information disclosed by any Party to the Escrow Agent or another Party in connection with this Agreement.

“Escrow Account” means the account designated by the Escrow Agent for the deposit of the Escrow Amount.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £4,850,000 (four million eight hundred and fifty thousand pounds sterling), representing 15 per cent of the total consideration under the SPA.

“Material Customer” means Yarrow Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15384000 and registered office at 40 Cheapside, London.

“Parties” means the Buyer, the Seller and the Escrow Agent, and “Party” means any one of them.

“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. DEPOSIT AND HOLDING OF ESCROW AMOUNT

2.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount except as expressly permitted herein.

3. PAYMENT

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall be held in interest-bearing trust, with any interest accrued forming part of the Escrow Amount.

3.2 All payments into or out of the Escrow Account shall be made in pounds sterling.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 148 Gresham Street, London.

4.3 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 171 King William Street, London.

4.4 Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to such address or email as the Escrow Agent may designate in writing.

5. RELEASE OF ESCROW AMOUNT

5.1 Subject to Clause 5.2, the Escrow Amount, or any balance thereof, shall be released to the Seller on the date that is 12 months after the Closing Date, being 22 June 2027.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it. Such notification must be in writing and delivered to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

6.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
