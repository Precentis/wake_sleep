SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 17 June 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Bellhaven Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12594034, whose registered office is at 171 King William Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Hartwell Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15454121, whose registered office is at 148 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13941856, whose registered office is at 77 Bishopsgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the term "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $85,000,000 (eighty-five million US dollars).

3. CLOSING

3.1 Closing shall take place on 4 September 2026 (the "Closing Date"), at which time the parties shall deliver all documents and payments required under this Agreement.

4. CONDITIONS AND TERMINATION

4.5 If the Conditions (as defined in this Agreement) are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 16 September 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

5. PAYMENT

5.1 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in full to the Seller on the Closing Date, by wire transfer in immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by reputable overnight courier, or sent by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as a party may notify to the other in accordance with this clause. Notices shall be deemed to have been received at the time of delivery if delivered personally or by courier, or if by email, upon confirmation of receipt, provided that if delivery occurs on a non-Business Day, it shall be deemed received on the next Business Day.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all terms of this Agreement and any information received in connection with the transaction contemplated herein, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or to professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

8. WARRANTIES AND LIABILITY

8.3 (Limitation of Liability) The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties set out in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $15,937,500. For the avoidance of doubt, the Purchase Price is $85,000,000 as set out in Section 2.1.

9. INDEMNITIES AND CLAIMS

9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified to the Seller in writing within 20 days of the Buyer becoming aware (or reasonably ought to have become aware) of the facts giving rise to the claim.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

10.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
