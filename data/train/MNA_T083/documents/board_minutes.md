# Minutes of a Board Meeting of Marlow Media Ltd

**Date of Meeting:** 5 January 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 98 Old Broad Street, London, EC2N 1AR, United Kingdom  

## 1. Chairperson  
The Chairman, Mr. Julian Hartley, called the meeting to order and confirmed that proper notice of the meeting had been given in accordance with the Articles of Association of Marlow Media Ltd (the “Company”).

## 2. Quorum  
The Chairman confirmed that a quorum was present. The Company has five directors, and 2 directors are present at this meeting. Therefore, the meeting was quorate.

## 3. Approval of Agenda and Minutes of Previous Meeting  
The proposed agenda was approved as circulated. The minutes of the previous board meeting, held on 3 December 2025, were reviewed and confirmed as a correct record of that meeting.

## 4. Review of Proposed Transaction  
The Directors considered the terms of a proposed transaction involving the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Thornbury Holdings Ltd (the “Seller”), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13910554, registered office 23 Bishopsgate, London, to Marlow Marine Ltd (the “Buyer”), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11370294, registered office 129 Old Broad Street, London.

The transaction is to be effected pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) dated 5 January 2026. The SPA provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company. The board acknowledged that the Company is not a party to the SPA, but the approval of the board is required under the terms of the agreement as a condition precedent to completion.

The Directors discussed the potential impact of the transaction on the Company’s operations, contracts, and governance. Specific attention was given to the Company’s commercial relationship with Foxley Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14365844, registered office 123 Old Broad Street, London, which remains unaffected by the transaction.

## 5. Resolution to Approve Entry into the Share Purchase Agreement  
**RESOLVED** that the board of Marlow Media Ltd approves the entry by Thornbury Holdings Ltd into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 January 2026 for the sale of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company to Marlow Marine Ltd.

**RESOLVED FURTHER** that the 2 directors present, Mr. Julian Hartley and Ms. Elaine Pembroke, be authorised to take all such actions and execute all documents as may be necessary or desirable to give effect to this approval, including the delivery of any board consents or notices required under the SPA.

## 6. Any Other Business  
There being no other business, the Chairman closed the discussion.

## 7. Adjournment  
The meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
