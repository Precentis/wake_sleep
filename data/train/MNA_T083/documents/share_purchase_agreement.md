SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 18 December 2025.

BETWEEN:

(1) Thornbury Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13910554, whose registered office is at 23 Bishopsgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Marlow Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11370294, whose registered office is at 129 Old Broad Street, London (the "Buyer").

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

BACKGROUND:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Marlow Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13663929, whose registered office is at 98 Old Broad Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the Shares on the terms set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Closing" means completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 10 March 2026;

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed or made available by either Party to the other in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement;

"General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 1, other than the Fundamental Warranties;

"Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Tax" or "Taxes" shall have the meaning given in the Fundamental Warranties.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is $200,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 3.

3. Payment and Closing

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3.2 Subject to receipt of the Purchase Price, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer, together with the share certificates representing the Shares and such other documents as are reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares to the Buyer free from all Encumbrances.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post (in the case of letters) or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify to the other from time to time.

4.2 A notice is deemed to be served when delivered by hand, 48 hours after posting, or upon confirmation of receipt if sent by email.

8. Limitation of Liability

8.2 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims under the warranties (excluding fraud or wilful misconduct) shall not exceed an amount equal to 12% of the Purchase Price, being $21,600,000. For the avoidance of doubt, this cap applies collectively to all valid claims notified under the General Warranties and the Fundamental Warranties.

10. Survival and Time for Claims

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months from the Closing Date. The Fundamental Warranties (being those relating to title, capacity, and tax) shall survive the Closing for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the Seller prior to the expiry of the relevant survival period.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each Party undertakes to the other that it shall not, and shall procure that its representatives shall not, at any time after the date of this Agreement, disclose to any person any Confidential Information, except:

(a) as required by law, regulation or any regulatory authority;

(b) with the prior written consent of the other Party;

(c) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis, provided such advisers are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

11.2 This obligation shall continue notwithstanding completion or termination of this Agreement.

12. General

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the whole agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, representations and agreements.

12.2 This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
