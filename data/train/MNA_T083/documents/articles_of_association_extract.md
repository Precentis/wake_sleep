ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF MARLOW MEDIA LTD  
(incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13663929)  
registered office: 98 Old Broad Street, London  

ARTICLE 1: INTERPRETATION

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Marlow Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11370294;  
“Company” means Marlow Media Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13663929;  
“Seller” means Thornbury Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13910554;  
“Foxley Engineering Ltd” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14365844;  
“person” includes a natural person, corporate or unincorporated body;  
“subsidiary” has the meaning given in the Companies Act 2006;  
“writing” includes any method of reproducing words legibly and in visible form.

ARTICLE 2: SHARE CAPITAL AND RIGHTS

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall be £100,000 divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.  
2.2 The rights attached to the ordinary shares include the right to receive dividends as declared by the Company, the right to vote at general meetings, and the right to a proportionate share of the surplus assets of the Company upon liquidation.

ARTICLE 3: PAYMENT

3.1 All payments due under any agreement involving the Company shall be made in cleared funds to such bank account as the Company may notify in writing from time to time.  
3.2 Any delay in payment exceeding 14 days beyond the due date shall attract interest at the rate of 8% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc, calculated on a daily basis until payment is made in full.

ARTICLE 4: NOTICES

4.1 Any notice required or permitted to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as may be notified in writing.  
4.2 A notice shall be deemed to have been served:  
(a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery;  
(b) if sent by pre-paid first-class post, 48 hours after posting;  
(c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is received.  
4.3 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to its registered office: 98 Old Broad Street, London.

ARTICLE 5: CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the Company which is marked as confidential or which, by its nature, ought reasonably to be treated as confidential.  
5.2 The obligations under this Article 5 shall not apply to information which:  
(a) is or becomes publicly available other than as a result of a breach of this clause;  
(b) was lawfully in the recipient’s possession prior to disclosure;  
(c) is required to be disclosed by law or by any regulatory or governmental authority.  
5.3 This obligation shall survive the termination of any agreement involving the Company for a period of five years.

ARTICLE 6: BOARD MEETINGS

6.1 The Board may convene meetings at such times and places as it determines.  
6.2 Written notice of a board meeting shall be given to all directors at least 48 hours prior to the meeting, unless all directors present waive this requirement.  
6.3 **Article 11:** The quorum for board meetings is three directors. No business shall be transacted at a board meeting unless the quorum is present at the commencement of the meeting. If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the appointed time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, and the quorum for the adjourned meeting shall be two directors.

ARTICLE 7: GENERAL PROVISIONS

7.1 These Articles are governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law.  
7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
