SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 16 August 2026.

Parties

(1) Ivybridge Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13912206, whose registered office is at 18 Cannon Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) Larkspur Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14830733, whose registered office is at 178 Fleet Street, London (the “Buyer”).

(3) The Company is Netherby Pharma plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13095686, whose registered office is at 45 Bishopsgate, London.

Background

A The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

B The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the Shares on the terms of this Agreement.

C The Closing is scheduled to take place on 11 November 2026 (the “Closing Date”).

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, “Material Adverse Change” means any event, circumstance or condition that has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, excluding (a) changes in general economic, business, political or market conditions affecting the pharmaceutical industry generally, (b) changes in applicable laws or accounting regulations, (c) acts of war, terrorism, pandemics or natural disasters, and (d) any effect arising from the announcement or pendency of this Agreement.

2. Purchase Price and Payment

2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the “Purchase Price”) is $80,000,000, which shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cash on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment in accordance with Clause 2.3.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. For this purpose, “Closing Working Capital” means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1, and “Target Working Capital” means $2,000,000. The Closing Working Capital shall be determined by the Buyer’s accountant within 90 days following the Closing Date, and the parties shall settle any adjustment within 15 days of such determination becoming final.

3. Earn-out

3.4 If the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 (“FY2026 EBITDA”) exceeds $5,000,000, the Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the “Earn-out Payment”) equal to two times (2x) the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $5,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall be capped at $5,000,000 and shall be paid within 60 days of the final audited financial statements for FY2026 being approved by the Buyer’s board.

10. Warranties and Claims

10.1 The General Warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 12 months. The Fundamental Warranties (comprising warranties as to title, capacity to enter into this Agreement, and tax) shall survive for a period of 36 months from the Closing Date. Any claim under this Agreement must be notified in writing to the party against whom the claim is made prior to the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

12. Notices

12.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as the party may designate by prior written notice.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
