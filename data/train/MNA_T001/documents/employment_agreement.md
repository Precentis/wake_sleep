EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 10 April 2026 between:

(1) Corrib Analytics Ltd (the "Company"), a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12370774, whose registered office is at 20 Old Broad Street, London; and

(2) The Founder (as defined below).

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Buyer" means Kingsmere Foods Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13907762, with registered office at 19 Cheapside, London.

"Change of Control" means the acquisition by Kingsmere Foods Ltd of the entire issued share capital of the Company from Netherby Energy plc.

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information (whether written, oral or in electronic form) relating to the business, operations, technology, clients (including but not limited to Ingleby Systems Ltd, company number 14772908), financial affairs or personnel of the Company or the Buyer, including trade secrets, customer lists, pricing policies, software, research, development projects and marketing strategies.

"Effective Date" means the date of this Agreement, being 10 April 2026.

"Founder" means the individual employed by the Company in a senior executive capacity, whose principal role includes strategic development and client relations.

"Good Reason" means a material diminution in the Founder’s duties, responsibilities or remuneration without prior written consent, or a relocation of the principal place of work by more than 30 miles.

"Termination Date" means the date on which the employment of the Founder by the Company is terminated for any reason.

2. Employment and Duties

2.1 The Company shall employ the Founder, and the Founder accepts employment, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, commencing on the Effective Date.

2.2 The Founder shall report directly to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company and shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are reasonably assigned from time to time.

3. Remuneration

3.1 The Company shall pay to the Founder a base salary of £95,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on or before the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Founder shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme operated by the Company, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme, with a target bonus of 45% of base salary.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Founder shall not, during the continuance of employment or at any time thereafter, use, disclose or permit the disclosure of any Confidential Information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board of the Company.

4.2 This obligation shall survive termination of employment, regardless of how, why or by whom such termination is effected.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the last known address of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices to the Company shall be sent to the registered office at 20 Old Broad Street, London.

5.3 Notices to the Founder shall be sent to the last residential address recorded in the Company’s personnel files.

6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

6.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

18. Non-Compete

18.1 The Founder covenants to the Company that, for a period of 18 months from and after the Termination Date, whether such termination occurs during or after a Change of Control, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, engage or be concerned in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company or the Buyer as carried on at the date of termination.

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies globally and includes (but is not limited to) any business activity involving data analytics services provided to clients in the food manufacturing or energy sectors, including but not limited to services currently provided to Ingleby Systems Ltd.

18.3 The Founder acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable and necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company and the Buyer.
