# Articles of Association of Corrib Analytics Ltd

**Interpretation**

1. In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
   (a) "Act" means the Companies Act 2006;  
   (b) "Articles" means these articles of association of Corrib Analytics Ltd;  
   (c) "Board" means the board of directors of the Company;  
   (d) "Buyer" means Kingsmere Foods Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13907762, whose registered office is at 19 Cheapside, London;  
   (e) "Company" means Corrib Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12370774, whose registered office is at 20 Old Broad Street, London;  
   (f) "Seller" means Netherby Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13088874, whose registered office is at 149 Fleet Street, London;  
   (g) "Ingleby Systems Ltd" means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14772908, whose registered office is at 77 Cheapside, London;  
   (h) "person" includes a natural person, corporate or unincorporated body (whether or not having separate legal personality);  
   (i) "subsidiary" and "holding company" have the meanings given in the Act;  
   (j) references to a provision include that provision and any sub-division of it.

**Board of Directors**

2. The Company shall have a board of directors consisting of such number of directors as may be determined from time to time by resolution of the Board or by ordinary resolution of the Company.

3. The Board shall manage the business and affairs of the Company and may exercise all powers of the Company, subject to the Act, the Company’s constitution, and any directions given by ordinary resolution.

4. The Board may appoint one of its members to be chair of the Board, and such chair shall have the right to preside at all Board meetings at which they are present.

**Meetings of the Board**

5. The Board shall meet as often as necessary for the proper management of the Company’s business, provided that no fewer than four meetings are held in each calendar year.

6. Subject to Article 11, a meeting of the Board shall be duly constituted if notice has been given in accordance with these Articles and a quorum is present.

7. The Board may from time to time determine the manner in which notice of meetings is to be given, provided that at least 48 hours’ notice is given unless all directors present waive this requirement.

8. Meetings of the Board may be held by electronic means, provided that all participants are able to hear and speak to one another, and such participation constitutes presence at the meeting.

**Article 11**

11. The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

**Resolutions in Writing**

12. Any decision that may be made at a meeting of the Board may be made without a meeting if a resolution in writing signed by all the directors who would have been entitled to vote on it is evidenced in writing and is retained as part of the Company’s records.

**Confidentiality**

13. Every director shall keep confidential the information acquired in the course of their duties, including but not limited to financial data, customer details, and strategic plans, and shall not disclose such information to any third party unless required by law or authorised by the Board.

**Notices**

14. Any notice or other communication required or permitted under these Articles shall be in writing and may be delivered by hand, post, or electronic means to the registered office of the relevant party.

**Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

15. These Articles are governed by and shall be construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with these Articles.
