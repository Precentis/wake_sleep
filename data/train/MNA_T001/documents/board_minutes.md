## 5. Review of Proposed Transaction

5.1. The Chairperson presented a summary of the proposed transaction involving the sale of the entire issued share capital of Corrib Analytics Ltd by Netherby Energy plc (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13088874, registered office 149 Fleet Street, London, to Kingsmere Foods Ltd (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13907762, registered office 19 Cheapside, London.  

5.2. The directors reviewed the draft Share Purchase Agreement dated 8 April 2026, which provides for the acquisition of 100% of the issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer.  

5.3. The directors noted that the Company is party to a material contract with Ingleby Systems Ltd (company number 14772908, registered office 77 Cheapside, London), which constitutes a significant portion of its current revenue.  

5.4. Legal counsel confirmed that all necessary corporate and regulatory disclosures have been made or will be made in accordance with English law.  

## 6. Resolutions

6.1. **RESOLVED** that the board of Corrib Analytics Ltd hereby approves the entry by Netherby Energy plc into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 8 April 2026 with Kingsmere Foods Ltd for the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  

6.2. **RESOLVED FURTHER** that the company secretary is authorised to execute, deliver, and file all documents and instruments required to give effect to the resolutions passed at this meeting, including but not limited to providing corporate consents and responses to due diligence requests, in accordance with English law and the jurisdiction of the courts of England.
