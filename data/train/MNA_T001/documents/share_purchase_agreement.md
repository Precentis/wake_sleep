**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 27 March 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Netherby Energy plc** (company number 13088874), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 149 Fleet Street, London (the “Seller”).

(2) **Kingsmere Foods Ltd** (company number 13907762), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 19 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”).

(3) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Corrib Analytics Ltd** (company number 12370774), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at 20 Old Broad Street, London (the “Company”).

**RECITALS**

A. The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

B. The parties intend that completion of the sale and purchase shall occur on the Closing Date.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “Closing” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;
- “Closing Date” means 15 May 2026;
- “Confidential Information” means all information of a confidential or proprietary nature disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement;
- “Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company;
- “this Agreement” includes all schedules and annexes.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Purchase Price for the Shares is **$130,000,000** (the “Purchase Price”), which shall be satisfied in full on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall deliver the Purchase Price to the Seller at Closing, failing which the Seller may, without prejudice to any other rights or remedies, terminate this Agreement by written notice.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and funds on the Closing Date.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee(s);

(b) resignations of the existing directors of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

(c) such corporate authorisations and consents as may be necessary to effect the lawful transfer of the Shares.

3.3 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller at Closing a confirmation of payment of the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

**4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES**

4.1 Each party represents and warrants to the other that:

(a) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement;

(b) this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms;

(c) the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate any applicable law or any agreement to which it is a party.

**5. NOTICES**

5.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or recognised overnight courier to the registered office of the relevant party.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed received: if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, two Business Days after posting; if sent by courier, one Business Day after dispatch.

**6. CONFIDENTIALITY**

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five years.

**12. NON-COMPETITION**

12.1 The Seller covenants and agrees that for a period of **5 years** after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, manage, operate, join, control or participate in any business which competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**13. GENERAL**

13.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with it.

13.2 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
