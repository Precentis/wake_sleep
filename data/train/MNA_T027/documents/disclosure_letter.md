3. **Specific Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**

3.1 For the purposes of Warranty 8.2 in the SPA relating to intellectual property rights, the Seller discloses that the core platform software utilised by the Company in its principal operations was developed in 2023 by Foxley Pharma plc, an independent contractor at the time of development.

3.2 At the time of the software’s development, the Company engaged Foxley Pharma plc on a project basis to design and implement the core platform software, which forms the basis of the Company’s proprietary technology offering.

3.3 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in respect of the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Foxley Pharma plc.

3.4 The development work was conducted pursuant to oral instructions and informal project specifications, and no formal contract or written agreement was executed between the Company and Foxley Pharma plc governing the creation, ownership, or transfer of intellectual property rights in the software.

3.5 The source code for the core platform software was delivered to the Company in 2023, and the Company has since maintained, updated, and used the software in the ordinary course of business without challenge to date.

3.6 The Company has not obtained any warranty, indemnity or assurance from Foxley Pharma plc regarding the non-infringement, title, or originality of the software.

3.7 This disclosure is made in full and without reservation and is intended to qualify Warranty 8.2 in its entirety to the extent that it relates to the ownership, enforceability, or chain of title of the core platform software.
