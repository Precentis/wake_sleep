SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 21 January 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Brightwater Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15575569 whose registered office is at 72 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer"); and

(2) Glenrock Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13773443 whose registered office is at 142 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Foxley Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10796875 whose registered office is at 157 Moorgate, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including its recitals and schedules;

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 4;

   "Closing Date" means 14 March 2027;

   "Closing Working Capital" means the working capital of the Company as of the Closing Date, calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1;

   "EBITDA" means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation, calculated in accordance with the methodology set out in Schedule 2;

   "FY2026 EBITDA" means the EBITDA of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026;

   "Earn-out Payment" means the amount calculated in accordance with Clause 3.4;

   "Parties" means the Buyer and the Seller;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

   "Target Working Capital" means $2,000,000.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a purchase price of $115,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), subject to adjustment under Clause 2.3.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in cleared funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to the bank account designated by the Seller in writing at least five (5) Business Days prior to Closing.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. Any such adjustment shall be calculated and verified in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 3.

3. EARN-OUT PAYMENT

3.4 The Buyer shall pay to the Seller an earn-out payment (the "Earn-out Payment") equal to two (2) times the amount by which FY2026 EBITDA exceeds $4,000,000. The Earn-out Payment shall not exceed $5,000,000 in aggregate. The Earn-out Payment, if any, shall be paid within ninety (90) days of the final audited financial statements for the year ending 31 December 2026 becoming available, subject to verification under Schedule 4.

4. CLOSING

4.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree, at 10:00 a.m. local time, or at such other time and place as the Parties may determine in writing.

12. RESTRICTIONS ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants that it shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in or assist any person in engaging in any business which competes with the business of the Company for a period of 5 years from the Closing Date. This restriction applies to all activities conducted within the United Kingdom and any other jurisdiction in which the Company carries on business as of the Closing Date. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the duration of 5 years is reasonable in scope, duration and geographical reach having regard to the nature of the business and the legitimate interests of the Buyer.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

13.2 The obligations under this Clause 13 shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, prepaid first-class post, or email to the address or email address set out in this Agreement or as otherwise notified. Any notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been served at the time of delivery if by hand, 48 hours after posting if sent by post, or upon confirmation of receipt if sent by email.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The Parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
