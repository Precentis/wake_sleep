**Employment Agreement Extract**

1. **Parties**  
1.1 This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 5 February 2027 between:  
(a) Foxley Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10796875, whose registered office is at 157 Moorgate, London ("Employer"); and  
(b) Mr. Julian Thorne, of 24 Windermere Avenue, Guildford ("Employee").  

2. **Definitions**  
2.1 "Business" means the business of providing proprietary software solutions and managed IT services to corporate clients, as carried out by the Employer and any of its subsidiaries at any time during the term of this Agreement.  
2.2 "Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to, client lists, pricing structures, technical data, software code, trade secrets, business strategies, and financial information relating to the Employer and its group companies.  
2.3 "Effective Date" means 8 February 2027.  
2.4 "Founder" refers to Mr. Julian Thorne in his capacity as founder and chief executive officer of the Employer.  

3. **Commencement and Term**  
3.1 The Employee’s employment shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.  

4. **Position and Duties**  
4.1 The Employee shall serve as Chief Executive Officer of the Employer.  
4.2 The Employee shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as are consistent with that office and as may be lawfully assigned to him by the board of directors of the Employer.  

5. **Remuneration**  
5.1 The Employer shall pay to the Employee a base salary of £145,000 per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.  
5.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in any annual bonus scheme established by the board of directors, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme.  

6. **Hours and Place of Work**  
6.1 The Employee shall work primarily from the registered office of the Employer at 157 Moorgate, London, but may be required to travel as necessary for business purposes.  

7. **Annual Leave**  
7.1 The Employee shall be entitled to thirty (30) working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of public holidays.  

8. **Sick Leave**  
8.1 The Employee shall be entitled to full salary during periods of certified sickness absence, in accordance with the Employer’s prevailing sickness policy.  

9. **Pension**  
9.1 The Employee shall be eligible to enrol in the Employer’s defined contribution pension scheme in accordance with statutory requirements and the rules of the scheme.  

10. **Confidentiality**  
10.1 The Employee shall not, during or after the term of his employment, disclose or use any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of his duties or as authorised in writing by the Employer.  
10.2 The obligations under this Clause 10 shall survive termination of employment.  

11. **Intellectual Property**  
11.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of his employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Employer.  

12. **Notices**  
12.1 Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and addressed to the registered office of the relevant party.  
12.2 Notices to the Employer shall be sent to: 157 Moorgate, London.  
12.3 Notices to the Employee shall be sent to the last known address on file with the Employer.  

13. **Termination**  
13.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving twelve (12) months’ written notice to the other party.  

18. **Non-Compete**  
18.1 The Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, whether as principal, partner, shareholder, director, consultant or otherwise, engage or be concerned in any business that competes with the Business in England and Wales for a period of 18 months after termination of employment, regardless of the reason for termination.  
18.2 This restriction applies to any entity including, but not limited to, Larkspur Technologies Ltd (company number 12960442), with which the Employer has a material customer relationship.  
18.3 The period of 18 months referred to in Clause 18.1 shall commence on the date the Employee’s employment terminates and shall run continuously thereafter.  

19. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
19.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
19.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to determine any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
