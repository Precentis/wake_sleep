**Disclosure Letter – Marlow Medical Ltd (Buyer) and Hartwell Medical Ltd (Seller) – Sale of Juniper Marine Ltd**

This Disclosure Letter is made on 21 June 2026 between:

(1) Hartwell Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14510775, whose registered office is at 177 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and  
(2) Marlow Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13359444, whose registered office is at 36 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
In this Disclosure Letter, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Agreement" means the share purchase agreement dated on or about 24 June 2026 between the Buyer and the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Juniper Marine Ltd;  
"Company" means Juniper Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10033592, whose registered office is at 162 Ludgate Hill, London;  
"Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in the Agreement;  
"Disclosed" means properly disclosed in this letter with sufficient detail to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed.

**2. Purpose**  
This letter sets out full, specific and truthful disclosure by the Seller pursuant to the Agreement in relation to the Warranties, to the extent required and permitted under English law, in order to qualify or limit the liability of the Seller under the Agreement.

**3. General Disclosure**  
The Buyer acknowledges receipt of all information previously supplied by or on behalf of the Company or the Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated by the Agreement, including due diligence materials, financial statements for the years ended 31 December 2023 and 31 December 2024, and all information contained in the data room made available by the Seller. Such documents are incorporated by reference and deemed part of this disclosure to the extent relevant.

**4. Disclosure Against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)**  
4.1 The Company's core platform software was developed in 2023 by an independent contractor, Kingsmere Industries Ltd.  
4.2 No written assignment of intellectual property rights in or to the core platform software was entered into between the Company and Kingsmere Industries Ltd.  
4.3 The Company has used the core platform software continuously since its development and holds itself out as the owner for all commercial and operational purposes.  
4.4 The Company has not received any claim, notice or demand asserting ownership of, or challenging the use of, the core platform software.  
4.5 The Company has no record of any agreement, invoice, or correspondence with Kingsmere Industries Ltd relating to intellectual property ownership beyond the original development engagement.
