2. **Purchase and Sale**

2.1 The Purchase Price is $40,000,000, payable in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in full on the Closing Date.

3. **Closing**

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee(s);

(b) resignations of the directors and secretary of the Company as designated by the Buyer;

(c) such other documents as may be reasonably required to transfer legal and beneficial title to the Shares free from all Encumbrances.

3.2 The Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

4. **Conditions**

4.1 The obligation of each party to complete the purchase and sale of the Shares is conditional upon the fulfilment of the Conditions.

4.2 The Conditions include:

(a) no material adverse change in the financial or trading position of the Company;

(b) no claim or challenge arising from Northgate Medical Ltd relating to existing supply contracts;

(c) receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals.

4.3 Each party shall use all reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions.

4.4 The Conditions shall be deemed waived unless raised in writing by either party by no later than 20 days after the date on which such party becomes aware of any failure.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 120 days after the date of this Agreement (signed 8 June 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.
