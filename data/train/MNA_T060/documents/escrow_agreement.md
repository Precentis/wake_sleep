---

**2. Appointment of Escrow Agent**

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts such appointment.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall not be required to act in any manner inconsistent with English law or its internal policies, and may rely on documents and statements believed in good faith to be genuine.

---

**3. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount**

3.1 On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account at a UK-regulated financial institution.

3.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely as agent, and title to the Escrow Amount remains with the Buyer and the Seller as beneficiaries under this Agreement.

---

**4. Disbursement of Escrow Amount**

4.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Agent shall release the Escrow Amount, or such portion as remains undisbursed, to the Seller on the last Business Day of the Escrow Period.

4.2 The Escrow Agent may disburse any portion of the Escrow Amount prior to the end of the Escrow Period upon receipt of a joint written instruction signed by both the Buyer and the Seller.

---

**5. Claims by the Buyer**

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount in respect of any breach of the Representations, the Tax Covenant, or any liability arising under the Disclosure Letter.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 Any such claim shall be made by written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim, the amount claimed (which shall not exceed 107% of the Escrow Amount), and supporting evidence.

---

**6. Notices**

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post, or email to the addresses specified in the preamble or as otherwise notified.

6.2 Notices to the Buyer: 36 Gresham Street, London;  
to the Seller: 177 Moorgate, London;  
to the Escrow Agent: such address as specified in its engagement letter.

---

**7. Confidentiality**

7.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other parties.

---

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under this Agreement.
