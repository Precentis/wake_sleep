SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 9 April 2026.

PARTIES

(1) Eskdale Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 10662728, whose registered office is at 97 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Ivybridge Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 11664802, whose registered office is at 29 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Company is Larkspur Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 14418810, whose registered office is at 84 King William Street, London.

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 5 June 2026;

   "Company" means Larkspur Industries Ltd, company number 14418810;

   "Material Customer" means Larkspur Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under number 15985852, whose registered office is at 178 Moorgate, London;

   "Purchase Price" means the aggregate consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company, comprising all ordinary shares in the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a purchase price of $145,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in full in cleared funds on the Closing Date.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms in respect of the Shares, duly executed in blank;

   (b) copies of board resolutions of the Company authorising the transfer of the Shares;

   (c) such other documents as are reasonably required by the Buyer to effect the transfer of the Shares into the name of the Buyer.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid letter, or by email to the registered office or last known email address of the relevant party.

4.2 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 97 Queen Victoria Street, London. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 29 Queen Victoria Street, London.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES
