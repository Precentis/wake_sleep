ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF LARKSPUR INDUSTRIES LTD  
(Extract Relating to Board Procedures and Shareholder Rights)  

1. Interpretation  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;  
“Buyer” means Eskdale Marine Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10662728;  
“Company” means Larkspur Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14418810;  
“Material Customer” means Larkspur Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15985852;  
“Seller” means Ivybridge Holdings Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11664802;  
“Shares” means the issued ordinary shares in the capital of the Company;  
“Transfer” means the transfer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller to the Buyer.  

1.2 Any reference to a statutory provision shall be construed as a reference to that provision as amended, re-enacted or replaced from time to time.  

2. Share Transfer and Ownership  
2.1 Following the Transfer, the entire issued share capital of the Company shall be held by the Buyer.  
2.2 The Transfer shall take effect on a date prior to 23 April 2026.  
2.3 The Shares constitute 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The Board shall consist of such number of directors as may be determined by ordinary resolution of the shareholders, provided that the number shall not be less than two.  
3.2 Directors shall be appointed by resolution of the shareholders or by the Board, subject to any rights conferred by the terms of issue of any class of shares.  
3.3 A director may be removed by ordinary resolution, subject to applicable statutory provisions.  

4. Board Meetings  
4.1 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter.  
4.2 Notice of a board meeting shall be given at least five Business Days in advance, unless all directors waive this requirement in writing.  
4.3 Meetings may be conducted in person, by video conference, or by such other electronic means as permits all participants to communicate adequately.  
4.4 Resolutions in writing signed by all directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting shall be valid and effective as if passed at a duly convened meeting.  

5. Quorum and Voting  
5.1 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.  
5.2 If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other time and place as the chairman may determine.  
5.3 At any meeting of the Board, every question shall be decided by a majority of votes.  
5.4 Each director shall have one vote on any matter before the Board. In the event of an equality of votes, the chairman of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.  

6. Payment and Financial Obligations  
6.1 The Company shall maintain bank accounts in its name with a UK-authorised institution.  
6.2 All payments made by the Company in excess of £50,000 must be approved in advance by a resolution of the Board.  
6.3 The Company shall invoice the Material Customer for services rendered under the existing supply agreement dated 15 November 2023.  

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address as may be notified.  
7.2 Notices addressed to the Company shall be sent to its registered office: 84 King William Street, London.  
7.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to 97 Queen Victoria Street, London.  
7.4 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to 29 Queen Victoria Street, London.  

8. Confidentiality  
8.1 Directors and officers of the Company shall not disclose any confidential information concerning the Company, its operations, customers, or financial affairs to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the Board.  
8.2 This obligation shall continue to apply after a director or officer ceases to hold office.  

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
9.1 These Articles shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.  
9.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
