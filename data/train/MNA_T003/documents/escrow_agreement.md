ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into on 15 April 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and among:

(1) Ivybridge Holdings Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11664802, whose registered office is at 29 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Eskdale Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10662728, whose registered office is at 97 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a professional fiduciary entity acting as escrow agent under this Agreement (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the parties to this Agreement are referred to as the “Parties”, and each individually as a “Party”.

WHEREAS, the Buyer and the Seller have entered into a share purchase agreement dated on or about the same date as this Agreement (the “SPA”) pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller the entire issued share capital of Larkspur Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14418810, whose registered office is at 84 King William Street, London (the “Company”), on the terms and subject to the conditions set out therein;

WHEREAS, as part of the consideration for the sale of the shares in the Company, the Buyer has agreed to deposit an amount equal to 15% of the total consideration (the “Escrow Amount”) into an interest-bearing escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) to secure certain indemnification obligations of the Seller under the SPA;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, including the recitals, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Claim” means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Escrow Agent and the Seller asserting a right to recover from the Escrow Amount pursuant to the terms of the SPA.

“Material Customer” means Larkspur Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15985852, whose registered office is at 178 Moorgate, London.

“SPA” has the meaning set forth in the recitals.

2. Establishment of Escrow Account

2.1 The Escrow Agent shall establish and maintain the Escrow Account with a financial institution acceptable to both the Buyer and the Seller, in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Amount shall be held by the Escrow Agent solely for the purposes specified herein and shall not be subject to any lien, set-off, or other claim by any creditor of any Party.

3. Payment

3.1 Upon the release conditions set forth in the SPA being satisfied or waived, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Amount or any portion thereof in accordance with written instructions jointly executed by the Buyer and the Seller or as otherwise directed by a final arbitration award.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth above, or to such other address as a Party may designate by written notice.

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount

5.1 A Claim may be made by the Buyer only in accordance with the procedures set out in the SPA.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received in connection with this Agreement, except as required by law or legal process.

7. Duties of Escrow Agent

7.1 The Escrow Agent shall act solely as a stakeholder and shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith unless such action constitutes gross negligence or willful misconduct.

12. Governing Law and Arbitration

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

12.2 Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules. The seat of arbitration shall be New York, New York, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
