**Minutes of a Board Meeting of Larkspur Industries Ltd**

**Date:** 18 April 2026  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** 84 King William Street, London, EC4N 7AN  

**1. Attendance and Quorum**  
1.1. The following directors were present:  
- Mr A. Patel  
- Ms R. Khan  

1.2. The Company has five (5) directors in total. The meeting was convened in accordance with the Articles of Association, and the presence of 2 directors confirmed that the meeting was quorate.  

1.3. The Chairman, Mr A. Patel, opened the meeting and confirmed that proper notice of the meeting had been given to all directors in accordance with the Company’s Articles of Association.  

**2. Declaration of Interests**  
2.1. Mr A. Patel declared a direct interest in the matters to be discussed under Agenda Item 3, by virtue of his position as a shareholder in Ivybridge Holdings Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11664802, registered office 29 Queen Victoria Street, London). He confirmed that he would not take part in any discussion or vote on the resolution concerning the Share Purchase Agreement.  

2.2. Ms R. Khan confirmed she had no material interest, direct or indirect, in the proposed transaction.  

**3. Share Purchase Agreement – Proposed Transaction**  
3.1. Ms R. Khan provided a detailed overview of the proposed transaction, pursuant to which Ivybridge Holdings Ltd (the Seller) will sell the entire issued share capital of Larkspur Industries Ltd (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14418810, registered office 84 King William Street, London, to Eskdale Marine Ltd (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10662728, registered office 97 Queen Victoria Street, London.  

3.2. The transaction is to be governed by a Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”) currently in draft form, which was circulated to the Board. The terms of the SPA are being finalised under English law and will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

3.3. The Board reviewed the commercial implications of the transaction, including ongoing operational arrangements and key customer relationships. Specific reference was made to Larkspur Medical Ltd (material customer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15985852, registered office 178 Moorgate, London, which accounts for 68% of the Company’s annual revenue under an existing supply agreement.  

3.4. Ms R. Khan advised that completion of the transaction is expected to occur no later than 23 April 2026, subject to final agreement of the SPA and satisfaction of any conditions precedent.  

**4. Resolution**  
4.1. A draft resolution was tabled by Ms R. Khan.  

4.2. RESOLVED, that the Board of Larkspur Industries Ltd hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement between Ivybridge Holdings Ltd and Eskdale Marine Ltd on the terms currently under negotiation and as may be finally agreed, such approval being granted in the best interests of the Company.  

4.3. The resolution was duly passed with one director voting in favour (Ms R. Khan). Mr A. Patel, present as one of 2 directors, did not vote due to his declared interest. The resolution was passed by the sole disinterested director present.  

**5. Authority to Proceed**  
5.1. The Board authorised the Company Secretary to perform all necessary actions to implement the resolution, including the provision of corporate documents and board consents required by the Buyer or its legal advisers, subject to compliance with the Company’s constitutional documents.  

**6. Adjournment**  
6.1. There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.
