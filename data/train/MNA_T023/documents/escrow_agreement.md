ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 21 September 2026, between:

(1) Quayside Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15687793, with its registered office at 64 Moorgate, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Kestrel Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12720883, with its registered office at 125 Cheapside, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) HSBC Trust Company (UK) Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 02922609, with its registered office at 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ (the “Escrow Agent”).

WHEREAS:

(A) The Buyer and the Seller have entered into a Share Purchase Agreement dated 23 September 2026 (the “SPA”), pursuant to which the Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12762740, with its registered office at 75 Fleet Street, London (the “Company”).

(B) In connection with the SPA, the Buyer and the Seller have agreed that an amount equal to 15.0% of the total consideration payable under the SPA (the “Escrow Amount”) shall be deposited into an escrow account to secure certain indemnification obligations of the Seller.

(C) The parties have appointed the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

“Business Day” means a day (other than a Saturday or Sunday) on which banks are open for general business in London.

“Claim” means any claim made by the Buyer under Clause 5 for indemnification from the Escrow Amount.

“Effective Date” means 23 September 2026.

“Escrow Account” means the account established by the Escrow Agent into which the Escrow Amount is deposited.

“Material Customer” means Corrib Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13827894, with its registered office at 176 Cheapside, London.

2. Deposit of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit, or procure the deposit of, the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account on the Effective Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Release of Escrow Amount

3.1 Subject to Clause 5, the Escrow Amount (or the balance thereof) shall be released to the Seller on the date that is 18 months after the Effective Date, unless a Claim has been validly notified in accordance with Clause 5.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class pre-paid post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email address as may be notified.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 64 Moorgate, London.  
Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 125 Cheapside, London.  
Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: 8 Canada Square, London E14 5HQ.

5. Claims Against Escrow Amount

5.1 A Claim may be made by the Buyer by delivering a written notice to the Seller and the Escrow Agent setting out in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim and the amount claimed.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and not to disclose it to any third party except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
