ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF ARDEN BIOSCIENCE PLC

Article 1: Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:
“Buyer” means Kestrel Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12720883, with registered office at 125 Cheapside, London;
“Company” means Arden Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12762740, with registered office at 75 Fleet Street, London;
“Seller” means Quayside Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15687793, with registered office at 64 Moorgate, London;
“Corrib Systems Ltd” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13827894, with registered office at 176 Cheapside, London;
“board” means the board of directors of the Company;
“director” means a director of the Company;
“shareholder” means a member of the Company holding ordinary shares.

Article 2: Share Capital

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.
2.2 All issued shares in the Company are fully paid and are currently legally and beneficially owned by the Seller.
2.3 Upon completion of the acquisition of the entire issued share capital by the Buyer from the Seller, legal and beneficial title to the shares shall transfer to the Buyer, and the register of members shall be updated accordingly.

Article 3: Transfer of Shares

3.1 Any transfer of shares in the Company shall be effected by an instrument of transfer in writing in the usual form or such other form as may be approved by the board.
3.2 The board may, at its discretion, refuse registration of a transfer of shares if such transfer would result in a breach of any agreement to which the Company is a party, including but not limited to contractual obligations with Corrib Systems Ltd.

Article 4: Board Meetings

4.1 The board may convene a meeting upon not less than 48 hours’ notice to each director, unless all directors agree to a shorter period.
4.2 A director may participate in a board meeting by electronic means, provided that all participants can hear one another.
4.3 Resolutions approved in writing by all directors entitled to receive notice of a board meeting shall be as valid as if passed at a duly convened meeting.

Article 11: Quorum

11.1 The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

Article 12: Payment

12.1 Any payment due under an agreement to which the Company is a party, including payments to or from Corrib Systems Ltd, shall be made in sterling to a bank account designated in writing by the recipient.
12.2 Payments shall be deemed made only when cleared funds are received.

Article 13: Notices

13.1 Any notice required or permitted under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to or sent by first-class pre-paid post, email, or electronic transmission to the registered office of the relevant party or such other address or email as may be notified in writing.
13.2 Notices sent by first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting. Notices sent by email shall be deemed served on the next business day following transmission, provided a delivery confirmation is received.

Article 14: Confidentiality

14.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the Company or any shareholder, director, or material customer, including Corrib Systems Ltd, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with prior written consent.
14.2 The obligations under this Article 14 shall survive any transfer of shares or change in control of the Company.

Article 15: Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.
15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
