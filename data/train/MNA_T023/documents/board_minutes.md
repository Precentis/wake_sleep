2.1. Dr. Hartley declared no direct or indirect interest in the matters to be discussed.

3. Review of Proposed Transaction  
3.1. The board received a briefing from the Company’s legal advisers regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc by Kestrel Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12720883, registered office 125 Cheapside, London.  
3.2. It was confirmed that Quayside Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15687793, registered office 64 Moorgate, London, is the legal and beneficial owner of all issued shares in the Company.  
3.3. The transaction involves the sale of 100% of the issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12762740, registered office 75 Fleet Street, London, to the Buyer.  
3.4. Legal counsel outlined the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement (the “SPA”), which is expected to be executed with effect from a date prior to 23 September 2026.  
3.5. The board reviewed the financial implications of the transaction and received confirmation that no third-party consents are required under existing material contracts, including the supply agreement with Corrib Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13827894, registered office 176 Cheapside, London.

4. Resolutions  
4.1. RESOLVED that the board of Arden Bioscience plc hereby approves the entry into the Share Purchase Agreement between Quayside Systems Ltd (as Seller) and Kestrel Capital LLP (as Buyer) for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
4.2. RESOLVED FURTHER that the directors are satisfied that the terms of the transaction are fair and reasonable insofar as they affect the Company and its position as a continuing entity prior to completion.  
4.3. RESOLVED FURTHER that Dr. Eleanor Hartley and Mr. Julian Reed are authorised to take all necessary steps to cooperate with the parties to the SPA, including providing any information reasonably required and executing ancillary documents consistent with the terms approved.

5. Next Steps  
5.1. The Chair confirmed that the transaction remains subject to standard pre-completion conditions, including confirmations from both parties regarding warranties and conduct of business.  
5.2. The board instructed the Company Secretary to retain copies of these minutes and ensure they are filed in accordance with the Company’s statutory record-keeping obligations.

6. Adjournment
