SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 3 September 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Quayside Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15687793, whose registered office is at 64 Moorgate, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) Kestrel Capital LLP, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12720883, whose registered office is at 125 Cheapside, London (the "Buyer").

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Arden Bioscience plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12762740, whose registered office is at 75 Fleet Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing" means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   "Closing Date" means 5 December 2026;

   "Company" means Arden Bioscience plc, company number 12762740;

   "General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 2, excluding the Fundamental Warranties;

   "Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax as set out in Schedule 2;

   "Purchase Price" means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares;

   "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Purchase Price is $100,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), which shall be paid in full to the Seller on the Closing Date in cleared funds by bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to Closing.

2.2 On the Closing Date, the Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the Shares, free from all Encumbrances, with full title guarantee.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Seller’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee;

   (b) the share certificates in respect of the Shares;

   (c) resignations of the current directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of Closing;

   (d) such other documents as may be reasonably required to effect the transfer of the Shares and control of the Company to the Buyer.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or email address last notified by the relevant party.

4.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 64 Moorgate, London.  
    Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 125 Cheapside, London.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received or obtained in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

6.1 The warranties set out in Schedule 2 shall survive the Closing and shall not merge on Closing.

10.1 (Survival): The General Warranties shall survive Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties (title, capacity, tax) shall survive for 48 months. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the other party before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

9.7 A claim under this Agreement for breach of warranty must be notified by the Buyer in writing within 30 days of the date on which the Buyer becomes aware, or ought reasonably to have become aware, of the matter giving rise to the claim.

GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
