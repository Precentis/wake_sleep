SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 16 February 2026.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Whitlock Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13097224, whose registered office is at 84 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Seller**”); and

(2) **Glenrock Capital LLP**, a limited liability partnership incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11993248, whose registered office is at 99 Old Broad Street, London (the “**Buyer**”).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12510800, whose registered office is at 93 Bishopsgate, London (the “**Company**”).

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

   “**Closing**” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

   “**Closing Date**” means 9 May 2026;

   “**Conditions**” means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

   “**Purchase Price**” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as determined under Clause 2;

   “**Shares**” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Purchase Price**

2.1 The purchase price for the Shares (the “**Purchase Price**”) is **$185,000,000**.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be payable in full in immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the Conditions, the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price on the Closing Date.

**3. Completion**

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Seller’s solicitors or such other location as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Completion, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed in favour of the Buyer or its nominee(s);

   (b) the share certificates in respect of the Shares;

   (c) resignations of the incumbent directors and secretary of the Company, effective as of the Closing Date;

   (d) copies of board and shareholder resolutions authorising the sale and transfer of the Shares.

3.3 The Buyer shall deliver to the Seller on the Closing Date confirmation of payment of the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.2.

**4. Conditions**

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction of the following Conditions:

   (a) receipt of all necessary regulatory and third-party consents;

   (b) accuracy of representations and warranties as of the Closing Date;

   (c) performance in all material respects of each party’s covenants under this Agreement.

4.2 The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions as soon as practicable.

4.3 If any Condition is not satisfied, the party for whose benefit the Condition exists may, by written notice to the other, waive the Condition in whole or in part.

4.4 The Conditions may be waived only by written agreement of both parties.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied or waived by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (being 16 February 2026), either party may terminate this Agreement by giving written notice to the other.

**9. Limitations on Liability**

9.4 **(De Minimis and Basket)**: The Seller shall not be liable to the Buyer in respect of any claim under this Agreement unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds **$1,850,000 (1.0% of the Purchase Price)**, in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the **excess** over that amount.

**10. Confidentiality**

10.1 Each party undertakes that it shall not, and shall procure that its affiliates and advisers shall not, disclose any information received pursuant to this Agreement to any third party except:

   (a) as required by law or any regulatory authority;

   (b) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidence;

   (c) in connection with the enforcement of its rights under this Agreement.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post or email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

12.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.
