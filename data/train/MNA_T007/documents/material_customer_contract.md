1. Definitions and Interpretation  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Agreement” means the contract for services entered into between Quayside Technologies Ltd and Pemberton Industries Ltd, dated 12 January 2020, including these terms and any subsequent amendments.  
“Commencement Date” means 12 January 2020.  
“Services” means the provision of digital infrastructure management, software integration, and technical support services as described in Schedule A.  
“Quayside” means Quayside Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12510800, with its registered office at 93 Bishopsgate, London.  
“Pemberton” means Pemberton Industries Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11178617, with its registered office at 46 Moorgate, London.  
“Confidential Information” has the meaning ascribed to it in Clause 10.  

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.  

2. Engagement and Scope of Services  
2.1 Quayside shall provide the Services to Pemberton in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A.  
2.2 The Services shall commence on the Commencement Date and continue for an initial term of 36 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein.  

3. Payment  
3.1 Pemberton shall pay Quayside the sum of £185,000 annually in advance, payable in equal quarterly instalments within 15 days of invoice date.  
3.2 All sums stated are exclusive of VAT, which shall be added at the prevailing rate.  

4. Term and Termination  
4.1 This Agreement shall continue in force for the initial term unless terminated earlier under this Clause 4.  
4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of receiving notice specifying the breach.  
4.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent.  

14. Miscellaneous  
14.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the respective party.  
14.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days’ notice if the other party undergoes a change of control without its prior written consent. The counterparty is Pemberton Industries Ltd.  
14.3 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.  

10. Confidentiality  
10.1 Each party shall treat as confidential all information received from or on behalf of the other party that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be regarded as such (“Confidential Information”).  
10.2 A party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party unless required by law or with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.  
10.3 The obligations under this Clause 10 shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.  

8. Intellectual Property  
8.1 All intellectual property rights developed prior to the Commencement Date shall remain the property of the originating party.  
8.2 Any deliverables produced under this Agreement shall be owned by Pemberton, subject to full payment of all fees due.  

9. Warranties and Liabilities  
9.1 Quayside warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care.  
9.2 The total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed 150% of the fees paid by Pemberton in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.  

12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
12.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.  
12.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
