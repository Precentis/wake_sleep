**4. Approval of Share Purchase Agreement**  
The directors considered the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Quayside Technologies Ltd by Glenrock Capital LLP (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11993248, registered office at 99 Old Broad Street, London, from Whitlock Industries Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13097224, registered office at 84 Old Broad Street, London.  

The directors reviewed a draft of the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 March 2026, pursuant to which the Seller will sell, and the Buyer will acquire, 100% of the issued share capital of the Company.  

It was confirmed that the Company is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12510800 and that its registered office is located at 93 Bishopsgate, London, EC2N 4AG.  

The directors noted that Pemberton Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11178617, registered office at 46 Moorgate, London, is a material customer of the Company.  

The directors discussed the terms of the transaction, including the transfer of control, ongoing contractual obligations, and disclosures required under English law. Legal and financial advisers had provided written advice, which was circulated to all attending directors prior to the meeting.  

After full discussion,  

**RESOLVED** that the board of directors of Quayside Technologies Ltd hereby approves entry into the Share Purchase Agreement dated 5 March 2026 between Whitlock Industries Ltd and Glenrock Capital LLP, under which the entire issued share capital of the Company is to be sold and transferred.  

**RESOLVED FURTHER** that the board confirms that the transaction is fair and reasonable from the perspective of the Company, having regard to its long-term strategic position, and that the interests of employees, creditors, and other stakeholders have been appropriately considered.  

**RESOLVED FURTHER** that Mr. Daniel Reeves and Ms. Amina Patel, being 2 directors of the Company, are hereby authorised to act on behalf of the Company to execute the Share Purchase Agreement, deliver all ancillary documents, and take all necessary actions to complete the transaction, in accordance with the terms thereof and applicable law.
