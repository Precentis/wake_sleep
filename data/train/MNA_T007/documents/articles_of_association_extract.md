ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF QUAYSIDE TECHNOLOGIES LTD  
(Extract)

Article 1: Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Buyer” means Glenrock Capital LLP, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11993248, with its registered office at 99 Old Broad Street, London;

“Company” means Quayside Technologies Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12510800, with its registered office at 93 Bishopsgate, London;

“Seller” means Whitlock Industries Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13097224, with its registered office at 84 Old Broad Street, London;

“Pemberton Industries Ltd” means the material customer of the Company, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11178617, with its registered office at 46 Moorgate, London;

“Board” means the board of directors of the Company;

“Director” means a director of the Company for the time being;

“Meeting” means a meeting of the Board or of the members of the Company, as applicable;

“Ordinary Resolution” means a resolution passed by a simple majority of votes cast in accordance with these Articles;

“Shareholder” means a person entered in the register of members of the Company as the holder of one or more shares in the Company.

Article 2: Share Capital

2.1 The issued share capital of the Company consists of 100 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

2.2 All shares in issue are fully paid.

2.3 The Company shall not issue any new shares without the prior written consent of the Buyer, which consent may be withheld at the Buyer’s absolute discretion.

Article 3: Transfer of Shares

3.1 Subject to the provisions of any agreement governing the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company by the Buyer from the Seller, shares in the Company may be transferred by means of an instrument of transfer in writing in any usual or common form or in any other form approved by the Board.

3.2 No transfer of shares shall be registered unless the instrument of transfer is lodged at the registered office of the Company accompanied by the share certificate (if any) and such other evidence of title as the Board may reasonably require.

Article 4: Board of Directors

4.1 The Board shall consist of such number of Directors as may be determined from time to time by Ordinary Resolution.

4.2 The Directors may from time to time appoint any person to be a Director, but any Director so appointed shall vacate office at the conclusion of the next ensuing general meeting unless confirmed in office by resolution of the members.

4.3 A Director may be removed from office at any time by Ordinary Resolution passed at a general meeting.

Article 5: Meetings of the Board

5.1 The quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting of the Board shall be three Directors, present in person or by means of video or audio conference facilities that permit all participants to communicate adequately.  
**Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors.**

5.2 The Chairperson of the Board shall preside at all meetings of the Board. In the absence of the Chairperson, the Directors present shall appoint one of their number to chair the meeting.

5.3 Questions arising at any meeting of the Board shall be decided by a majority of votes. In the case of equality of votes, the Chairperson of the meeting shall have a second or casting vote.

Article 6: Payment and Financial Provisions

6.1 All payments due from the Company to any third party, including but not limited to suppliers, contractors and employees, shall be made in cleared funds in British pounds sterling within 30 days of the date of invoice unless otherwise agreed in writing between the Company and the recipient.

Article 7: Notices

7.1 Any notice to be given to the Company under these Articles shall be sufficiently given if delivered by hand or sent by first-class pre-paid post to the registered office of the Company.

7.2 Any notice to be given to a Director or Shareholder shall be sufficiently given if sent to their last known address recorded in the Company’s register.

Article 8: Confidentiality

8.1 Every Director and officer of the Company shall keep confidential all information concerning the business, finances, customers (including but not limited to Pemberton Industries Ltd), suppliers and affairs of the Company acquired in their capacity as such, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Board, except as required by law.

Article 9: Governing Law and Jurisdiction

9.1 These Articles and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

9.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with these Articles.
