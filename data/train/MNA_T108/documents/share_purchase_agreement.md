**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated 24 March 2027.

**Parties**  
(1) Redcliffe Pharma plc (company number 10905491), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 26 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”).  
(2) Bellhaven Analytics Ltd (company number 15621970), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 122 Ludgate Hill, London (the “Buyer”).  

**Background**  
(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Northgate Analytics Ltd (company number 11902576), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 171 Queen Victoria Street, London (the “Company”).  
(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.  

**Operative Provisions**

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**  
1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
“Conditions” means the conditions precedent set out in Schedule 2;  
“Closing” means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;  
“Closing Date” means 8 May 2027;  
“Parties” means the Seller and the Buyer;  
“Purchase Price” means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares as set out in Clause 2.1;  
“Shares” means the entire issued share capital of the Company.  
1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Sale and Purchase**  
2.1 The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total consideration of $170,000,000 (the “Purchase Price”), payable in immediately available funds to a bank account nominated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.  
2.2 The Purchase Price shall be adjusted in accordance with the mechanism set out in Schedule 3.

**3. Closing**  
3.1 Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.  
3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer: (a) share certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by duly executed instruments of transfer; and (b) such confirmations and documents as are set out in Schedule 4.  
3.3 The Buyer shall make payment of the Purchase Price in accordance with Clause 2.1.

**4. Conditions Precedent**  
4.1 The obligations of each Party under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction or, where permissible, waiver in writing by both Parties of the Conditions.  
4.2 The Parties shall use reasonable endeavours to procure the satisfaction of the Conditions.  
4.3 The Conditions shall be deemed waived if not objected to in writing by either Party by 5:00 p.m. on the fifth Business Day following the date of this Agreement.  
4.4 If any Condition remains unsatisfied, and has not been waived, by the date falling 90 days after the date of this Agreement (being 22 June 2027), either Party may by written notice to the other terminate this Agreement without liability to the other, save for obligations which expressly survive termination.

**9. Warranties and Indemnities**  
9.4 The aggregate liability of the Seller for all claims arising under the warranties contained in Schedule 1 shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Purchase Price, being $31,875,000. The Purchase Price is $170,000,000 (see Clause 2.1).  
9.7 Any claim by the Buyer in respect of a breach of warranty must be notified to the Seller in writing within 30 days of the Buyer becoming aware, or reasonably ought to have become aware, of the matter giving rise to such claim.

**15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**  
15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter.

**16. Notices**  
16.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address or email address as a Party may notify in writing.  
16.2 A notice shall be deemed served: if delivered personally, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, two Business Days after posting; if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

**17. Confidentiality**  
17.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.  
17.2 This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three years.
