ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] 2027 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Bellhaven Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15621970, whose registered office is at 122 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Redcliffe Pharma plc, a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10905491, whose registered office is at 26 Old Broad Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(3) [Name of Escrow Agent], a company licensed to provide escrow services, having an office at [Address] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

A. Pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated [RELEVANT DATE] 2027 (the "Purchase Agreement"), the Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to acquire, the entire issued share capital of Northgate Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11902576, whose registered office is at 171 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Company").

B. As part of the Purchase Agreement, the Buyer has agreed to deposit an amount equal to 15% of the total consideration (the "Escrow Amount") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated interest-bearing account (the "Escrow Account"), to secure certain indemnities and warranties of the Seller.

C. The Parties now wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

"Claim" means a written notice delivered by the Buyer to the Seller and the Escrow Agent asserting a liability under the Purchase Agreement that is indemnifiable from the Escrow Amount.

"Indemnity Period" means the period ending 12 April 2027.

2. Escrow Deposit

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent within five (5) Business Days of the closing date under the Purchase Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account, which shall be maintained with a reputable financial institution in New York, and shall not commingle the Escrow Amount with any other funds.

3. Disbursement of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released in accordance with the written joint instructions of the Buyer and the Seller, or as otherwise ordered by an arbitral tribunal pursuant to Clause 12.

3.2 In the absence of joint instructions, the Escrow Agent shall disburse funds only upon receipt of a final and non-appealable arbitral award issued under Clause 12.

4. Escrow Agent’s Duties

4.1 The Escrow Agent shall act solely as a stakeholder and shall have no authority to exercise discretion beyond the terms of this Agreement.

4.2 The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any actions taken or omitted in good faith reliance on written instructions.

5. Claims Against Escrow

5.1 A Claim shall be valid only if it is submitted in writing to the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

5.3 The Seller shall have thirty (30) days from receipt of a Claim to respond in writing, either agreeing to the release of funds or disputing the Claim.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Parties and the Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information relating to this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Parties.

7. Notices

7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant Party or as otherwise notified in writing.

8. Costs and Expenses

8.1 The fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be shared equally by the Buyer and the Seller.

9. Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall terminate upon the earlier of: (a) the distribution of the entire Escrow Amount; or (b) the expiration of the Indemnity Period.

10. Entire Agreement

10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding among the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

11. Amendments

11.1 This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by all Parties.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

12.1 This escrow agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in New York.

12.2 Any arbitration shall be conducted in English before a single arbitrator appointed in accordance with the rules of the International Chamber of Commerce.

13. Counterparts

13.1 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
