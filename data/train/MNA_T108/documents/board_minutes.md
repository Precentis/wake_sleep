4. Update on Proposed Sale of Entire Issued Share Capital  
The board received an update regarding the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Northgate Analytics Ltd (the Company) by Bellhaven Analytics Ltd (the Buyer), from Redcliffe Pharma plc (the Seller).  
The Company is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11902576, with registered office at 171 Queen Victoria Street, London.  
Bellhaven Analytics Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15621970, with registered office at 122 Ludgate Hill, London.  
Redcliffe Pharma plc is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10905491, with registered office at 26 Old Broad Street, London.  
The board acknowledged that negotiations are ongoing and that legal documentation is being finalised under English law, with jurisdiction in the courts of England and Wales.  
The board resolved to continue to cooperate with due diligence requests and to ensure all material disclosures are made prior to completion.

5. Ongoing Legal Matter – Letter Before Action from Glenrock Analytics Ltd  
The board noted receipt of a letter before action dated 3 April 2027 from Glenrock Analytics Ltd, which asserts claims against the Company in respect of alleged defective deliveries. The amount claimed is approximately $2,750,000.  
The matter relates to deliveries made under a supply agreement between the Company and Glenrock Analytics Ltd during the period 15 January 2025 to 10 March 2026.  
Legal counsel has reviewed the correspondence and advised that the claim is currently under assessment.  
Resolved: Management is directed to monitor developments in this matter closely, maintain regular communication with external legal advisors, and report to the board quarterly, or more frequently if required. No admission of liability shall be made without prior board approval.

6. Material Customer Relationship – Kingsmere Engineering Ltd  
The board reviewed the current status of the commercial relationship with Kingsmere Engineering Ltd, a material customer.  
Kingsmere Engineering Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12691080, with registered office at 96 King William Street, London.  
Revenue attributable to Kingsmere Engineering Ltd in the financial year ending 31 December 2026 represented 28% of total group revenue.  
The current contract with Kingsmere Engineering Ltd runs for a term of 36 months from 1 January 2025 and includes provisions for automatic renewal unless terminated by either party on 90 days’ written notice.  
The board expressed satisfaction with the ongoing performance and strategic value of the relationship.
