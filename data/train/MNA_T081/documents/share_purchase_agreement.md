SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 21 May 2027.

PARTIES

(1) Thornbury Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15569174, whose registered office is at 72 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer").

(2) Bellhaven Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13253610, whose registered office is at 136 Cheapside, London (the "Seller").

(3) Pemberton Logistics plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12972476, whose registered office is at 111 Cheapside, London (the "Company").

RECITALS

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

   "Closing Date" means 23 June 2027;

   "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics;

   "General Warranties" means the warranties set out in Schedule 1, excluding those identified as Fundamental Warranties;

   "Fundamental Warranties" means the warranties relating to title, capacity, and tax as specified in Schedule 1.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms of this Agreement. The total consideration for the sale and purchase (the "Purchase Price") is $115,000,000.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cleared funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller no later than 3:00 p.m. on the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 Completion shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer’s solicitors or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

   (a) share transfer forms duly executed by the Seller in favour of the Buyer in respect of all the shares in the Company;

   (b) resignations of any directors and secretaries of the Company as the Buyer may reasonably request, in a form acceptable to the Buyer;

   (c) certified copies of resolutions of the board of directors and shareholders of the Company authorising the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Buyer shall make the payment referred to in Clause 2.2 by electronic bank transfer to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least three Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

5. NOTICES

5.1 Any notice or other communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the party’s registered office or such other address or email address as may be notified in writing.

5.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 136 Cheapside, London.

5.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 72 Gresham Street, London.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other party.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five years.

10. SURVIVAL OF WARRANTIES

10.1 The General Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 24 months. The Fundamental Warranties shall survive the Closing for a period of 48 months. Any claim under a warranty must be notified in writing to the party giving the warranty before the expiry of the applicable survival period.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, English law.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
