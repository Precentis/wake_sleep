2. Disclosure against Warranty 8.2 (Intellectual Property)  
2.1 For the purposes of disclosure against Warranty 8.2, which states that the Company owns or has valid rights to use all intellectual property necessary for the conduct of its business as currently conducted, the Seller hereby discloses as follows:  
2.2 The Company’s core platform software, known internally as “Pemberton Connect”, was developed in 2023 by Galloway Logistics plc, an independent contractor retained by the Company on a project basis.  
2.3 No written assignment of intellectual property was entered into between the Company and Galloway Logistics plc in connection with the development of “Pemberton Connect”.  
2.4 The development work was carried out pursuant to an informal arrangement under which Galloway Logistics plc provided software development services to the Company. Payment for such services was made in full upon completion.  
2.5 The Seller confirms that, to the best of its knowledge, Galloway Logistics plc has not asserted any ownership claim over the source code or underlying intellectual property in “Pemberton Connect”, and no third party has notified the Company of any infringement or competing claim.  
2.6 The Company continues to maintain, host and exploit “Pemberton Connect” as part of its core operations and has licensed its use to certain customers, including but not limited to Saltash Engineering Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13111616, whose registered office is at 93 Fleet Street, London.  
2.7 The Buyer acknowledges receipt of this disclosure and agrees that the matters disclosed herein constitute full and sufficient disclosure in relation to Warranty 8.2, and accordingly waives any claim arising from or connected to the absence of a formal intellectual property assignment from Galloway Logistics plc.
