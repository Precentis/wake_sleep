1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

"Agreement" means this escrow agreement dated on or before 27 May 2027 between the Seller, the Buyer, and the Escrow Agent;

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open for general business in London;

"Buyer" means Thornbury Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15569174, whose registered office is at 72 Gresham Street, London;

"Closing Date" means 23 June 2027;

"Company" means Pemberton Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12972476, whose registered office is at 111 Cheapside, London;

"Escrow Amount" means the sum agreed between the Seller and Buyer to be held in escrow pursuant to clause 4.1 of this Agreement;

"Escrow Agent" means the third-party custodian appointed to hold the Escrow Amount under the terms of this Agreement;

"Parties" means collectively the Seller, the Buyer and the Escrow Agent;

"Seller" means Bellhaven Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 13253610, whose registered office is at 136 Cheapside, London.

1.2 References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. The headings in this Agreement do not affect its interpretation.

2. Escrow Arrangement

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts such appointment and agrees to hold the Escrow Amount in a designated interest-bearing account with a recognised financial institution in London, maintaining such account in accordance with applicable financial regulations and anti-money laundering requirements.

2.3 The Escrow Amount shall not be subject to any lien, set-off, counterclaim or right of appropriation by the Escrow Agent, nor shall it form part of the Escrow Agent’s own assets.

3. Release Conditions

3.1 The release of the Escrow Amount, or any portion thereof, shall be governed exclusively by the provisions set out in clause 4.1.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 24 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. Any portion of the Escrow Amount subject to a duly notified claim prior to such date shall remain held by the Escrow Agent until final resolution of such claim in accordance with the dispute resolution mechanisms provided for in the Share Purchase Agreement dated on or before 27 May 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller in relation to the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

5. Notices

5.1 Any notice or other communication required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post, or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party as set out in clause 1.1.

5.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received if delivered personally, at the time of delivery; if sent by pre-paid first-class post, two Business Days after posting; and if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no delivery failure notification is generated.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party undertakes to the other Parties to keep confidential all information received pursuant to this Agreement and not to disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulatory authority, or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

6.2 This obligation shall not apply to information which is publicly available otherwise than as a result of a breach of this clause.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes or claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
