MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF FOXLEY INDUSTRIES LTD

Date: 2 April 2025  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Location: 10 Queen Victoria Street, London, EC4N 4SA  

Present:  
- Mr A. Trentham (Chairman)  
- Ms L. Patel (Managing Director)  
- Mr D. Greaves (Finance Director)  
- Ms C. Booth (Company Secretary)  

1. Call to Order  
The Chairman called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m.

2. Attendance and Quorum  
The Company Secretary confirmed that a quorum was present. All directors were in attendance either in person or via video conference. Ms C. Booth recorded the minutes.

3. Declaration of Interests  
No director had any disclosable interests requiring declaration under the Articles of Association.

4. Approval of Previous Minutes  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 5 December 2024 were reviewed and approved as a true and accurate record.

5. Update on Legal Correspondence  
The board noted a letter before action dated 28 March 2025 from Delamere Bioscience plc, addressed to Foxley Industries Ltd, alleging defective deliveries under Purchase Order No. DBS-7742-FXLY and threatening legal proceedings for a claimed sum of approximately $1,200,000. The letter asserts breach of contract and seeks compensation for financial losses incurred by Delamere Bioscience plc in connection with the rejection of goods supplied during the period 15 October 2024 to 8 January 2025. The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely, liaise with external legal counsel, and report on any developments at the next scheduled board meeting. No admission of liability was made by the Company, and all correspondence is being treated without prejudice.

6. Financial Performance Review  
The Finance Director presented the unaudited financial statements for the quarter ended 31 December 2024. Revenue was reported at £8.72 million, with a net profit margin of 11.4%. The board noted a 3.2% decline in turnover from the same quarter in the prior year, primarily attributed to supply delays affecting delivery schedules. A revised forecast for the financial year ending 31 March 2026 was circulated, projecting a recovery in Q4 driven by new contracts.

7. Material Customer Update  
The Managing Director reported that Lowther Engineering Ltd (company number 11643606, registered office 7 Fleet Street, London) had confirmed a new three-year supply agreement effective from 1 April 2025, representing an estimated 22% of projected annual revenue. The agreement includes performance benchmarks and liquidated damages clauses in the event of non-compliance.

8. Share Sale Transaction Update  
The board received an update on the proposed sale of the entire issued share capital of Foxley Industries Ltd (company number 10013352) to Kestrel Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15449851, registered office 138 Cheapside, London) from Calloway Logistics plc (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13566159, registered office 117 Gresham Street, London). Due diligence was ongoing, and the transaction remains on track for completion by no later than 30 June 2025, subject to standard conditions. All board members were reminded to maintain confidentiality in accordance with the terms of the confidentiality agreement dated 15 January 2025.

9. Any Other Business  
There was no other business.

10. Adjournment  
The meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
