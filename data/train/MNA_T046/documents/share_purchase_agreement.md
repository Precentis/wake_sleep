**SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is dated **6 March 2026**.

**BETWEEN:**

(1) **Calloway Logistics plc**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **13566159**, whose registered office is at 117 Gresham Street, London (the **"Seller"**); and

(2) **Kestrel Medical Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **15449851**, whose registered office is at 138 Cheapside, London (the **"Buyer"**).

**BACKGROUND:**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Foxley Industries Ltd**, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number **10013352**, whose registered office is at 10 Queen Victoria Street, London (the **"Company"**).

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Seller wishes to sell, the entire issued share capital of the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement.

**AGREED TERMS**

**1. Interpretation**

**1.1** In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

**"Material Adverse Change"** means any event, circumstance or condition which has a material adverse effect on the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, **excluding** any change or effect arising from or related to: (a) general economic, political or financial market conditions affecting the United Kingdom or global markets; (b) changes or developments affecting the engineering and industrial manufacturing industry generally; or (c) the outbreak, escalation or continuation of **pandemics**.

**"Closing"** means the completion of the sale and purchase of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3.

**"Closing Date"** means **3 June 2026**, or such other date as the parties may agree in writing.

**"Company"** means Foxley Industries Ltd, company number 10013352.

**"Shares"** means the entire issued share capital of the Company.

**"Purchase Price"** means the consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1.

**2. Purchase and Sale**

**2.1** The Seller agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, the Shares for a total aggregate consideration of **$130,000,000** (the **"Purchase Price"**).

**2.2** The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least three (3) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**2.3** Title to, and risk in, the Shares shall pass to the Buyer on the Closing Date.

**3. Closing**

**3.1** The Closing shall take place remotely via the electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the Closing Date.

**3.2** At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the Shares;

(b) share certificates representing the Shares, if any;

(c) a certified copy of the resolutions of the board of directors of the Company approving the transfer of the Shares and confirming the registration of the Buyer or its nominee as the holder of the Shares;

(d) such other documents and assurances as may reasonably be required by the Buyer to vest legal and beneficial title to the Shares in the Buyer, free from all Encumbrances.

**4. Notices**

**4.1** Any notice or other communication given under this Agreement shall be in writing, addressed to the recipient at its registered office, and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or electronic transmission (confirmed by post within 24 hours).

**5. Confidentiality**

**5.1** Each party undertakes to the other that it shall not, without the prior written consent of the other party, disclose to any third party any information concerning the terms of this Agreement, the Company, or the transactions contemplated hereby, except as required by law or regulation or to its professional advisers on a confidential basis.

**5.2** This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of **two (2) years**.

**6.1** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements and understandings in relation to its subject matter.
