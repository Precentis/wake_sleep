**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT – EXTRACT**

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 25 June 2026 between:

(1) **Kingsmere Medical Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 14111668, registered office at 151 Bishopsgate, London) (the "Company");

(2) **Elstree Robotics Ltd** (registered in England and Wales under company number 11792108, registered office at 20 King William Street, London) (the "Buyer"); and

(3) **Dr. Eleanor Galloway** (the "Employee").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, a subsidiary or holding company of that person, or a subsidiary of such a holding company, in each case as defined in the Companies Act 2006;

"Business" means the research, development, manufacture, and supply of robotic diagnostic systems for clinical use;

"Confidential Information" means all information (whether of a technical, commercial, financial or administrative nature) concerning the Company, the Buyer, or any of their respective Affiliates, including but not limited to trade secrets, customer lists, pricing policies, software, know-how, business plans and research data;

"Effective Date" means 3 July 2026;

"Founder" means Dr. Eleanor Galloway in her capacity as founder and chief scientific officer of the Company prior to the Effective Date.

**2. Employment and Duties**

2.1 The Employee shall be employed by the Company as Chief Scientific Officer and shall report to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

2.2 The Employee shall perform her duties faithfully, diligently, and in the best interests of the Company and the Buyer, and shall devote her full working time and attention to such duties.

**3. Compensation and Benefits**

3.1 The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of £145,000, payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company’s annual bonus scheme, with a target bonus of 35% of base salary, subject to achievement of performance criteria determined by the Board of the Company in consultation with the Buyer.

3.3 The Employee shall be entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, inclusive of statutory holidays.

**4. Termination**

4.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party giving six months’ written notice to the other party.

4.2 The Company may terminate this Agreement summarily for cause in the event of gross misconduct, fraud, or material breach of this Agreement by the Employee.

**5. Confidentiality**

5.1 The Employee shall not, during the term of employment or at any time thereafter, directly or indirectly disclose, disseminate, or use any Confidential Information except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as authorised in writing by the Company or the Buyer.

5.2 This obligation shall survive the termination of employment for a period of five years.

**6. Intellectual Property**

6.1 All intellectual property created by the Employee during the course of her employment, whether alone or jointly with others and whether at the workplace or otherwise, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

6.2 The Employee hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all rights, title, and interest in and to any such intellectual property.

**7. Notices**

7.1 Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or sent by email to the registered office of the relevant party or to such other address or email as a party may designate in writing.

7.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the Company Secretary at 151 Bishopsgate, London.

**8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

**18. Non-Compete**

18.1 During the period of 18 months immediately following the termination of the Employee’s employment for any reason (the "Restricted Period"), the Founder shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with others, engage, be concerned, or have any financial or other interest in any business which carries on or is preparing to carry on a business in competition with the Business within the United Kingdom or any other territory in which the Company or the Buyer operates as of the date of termination.

18.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the Restricted Period commences on the date of termination of employment and continues for 18 months thereafter.
