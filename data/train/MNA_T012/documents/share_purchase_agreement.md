SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 11 June 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) Glenrock Analytics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15922179, whose registered office is at 42 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Elstree Robotics Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11792108, whose registered office is at 20 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”);

(each a “Party” and together the “Parties”)

in respect of:

Kingsmere Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14111668, whose registered office is at 151 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”).

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to acquire, and the Seller wishes to sell, all of the issued shares in the Company, on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, “Closing Date” means 11 September 2026.

1.2 The terms “Business”, “Conditions”, “Target Working Capital”, “Closing Working Capital”, and “Purchase Price” shall have the meanings given to them in the clauses below or, where not defined, shall bear their customary meanings in an agreement of this nature.

2. PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is $160,000,000 (one hundred and sixty million US dollars) (the “Purchase Price”).

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full by the Buyer to the Seller in immediately available funds on the Closing Date by wire transfer to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

2.3 The Purchase Price shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by any shortfall of the Closing Working Capital below the Target Working Capital of $2,000,000. For the purposes of this Agreement, “Target Working Capital” shall mean $2,000,000, and “Closing Working Capital” shall mean the working capital of the Company calculated in accordance with the accounting principles set out in Schedule 1, determined as of the close of business on the Closing Date.

4. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

4.5 If the Conditions (including but not limited to regulatory approvals, third-party consents, and due diligence satisfaction) are not satisfied or waived in writing by the Parties by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement (i.e., 29 October 2026), either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party.

12. RESTRICTIONS ON COMPETITION

12.1 The Seller covenants and agrees that, for a period of 4 years from and after the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, carry on, be engaged in, or have any interest in any business which competes with the business of the Company as conducted at the date of this Agreement, within the United Kingdom or any other jurisdiction in which the Company operates.

13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from or on behalf of the other Party or the Company in connection with this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under an obligation of confidentiality.

14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the registered office or nominated email address of the relevant Party.

15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

15.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

15.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter.
