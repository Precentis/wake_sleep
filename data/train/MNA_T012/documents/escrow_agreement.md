ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [●] July 2026 (the “Execution Date”) between:

(1) Glenrock Analytics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 15922179, whose registered office is at 42 Old Broad Street, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Elstree Robotics Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 11792108, whose registered office is at 20 King William Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) [●], a firm of solicitors authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, whose principal office is at [●] (the “Escrow Agent”).

Collectively, the parties to this Agreement.

RECITALS

(A) The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of Kingsmere Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14111668, whose registered office is at 151 Bishopsgate, London (the “Company”), pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated as of even date herewith (the “Purchase Agreement”).

(B) In connection with the Purchase Agreement, the Buyer has agreed to pay an amount (the “Escrow Amount”) into an escrow account to secure the Seller’s obligations in respect of certain indemnities and warranties, to be held by the Escrow Agent under the terms of this Agreement.

(C) The parties have agreed that the Escrow Amount shall be administered pursuant to the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

“Claim” means any claim validly made by the Buyer against the Escrow Amount pursuant to the terms of the Purchase Agreement.

“Closing Date” means 11 September 2026.

“Escrow Amount” means the amount of £[●] deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account maintained by the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 2.

“Purchase Agreement” has the meaning given in Recital (A).

2. Payment and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1 The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) maintained with [●] by no later than the Closing Date.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account in accordance with this Agreement and shall not release any portion of the Escrow Amount except in accordance with the terms herein.

3. Notices

3.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered personally, sent by first-class pre-paid post, or by email to the address or email set out in the preamble to this Agreement, or to such other address or email as a party may notify in writing.

3.2 A notice given under this Agreement shall be deemed to have been received: (a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery; (b) if sent by first-class post, at 9:00 a.m. on the second Business Day after posting; and (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no error message is generated.

4. Release of Escrow Amount

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 18 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or legal process or as necessary to perform its duties under this Agreement.

6. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

6.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England in respect of any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
