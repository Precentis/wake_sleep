SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made on 2 March 2027

BETWEEN:

(1) WHITLOCK SYSTEMS LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14419323, whose registered office is at 115 Cannon Street, London (the "Seller"); and

(2) PEMBERSON SYSTEMS LTD, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13462871, whose registered office is at 53 Moorgate, London (the "Buyer").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14192220, whose registered office is at 2 Cannon Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller wishes to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Agreement" means this share purchase agreement, including all schedules and annexes;

"Closing" means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Shares in accordance with Clause 3;

"Closing Date" means 2 May 2027, or such other date as the parties may agree in writing;

"Conditions" means the conditions precedent set out in Clause 4;

"excess" means the amount by which the aggregate of all claims exceeds the Basket Amount;

"Purchase Price" means the total consideration payable by the Buyer to the Seller for the Shares, as specified in Clause 2.1;

"Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company;

"Warranties" means the representations and warranties given by the Seller in Clause 8.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

2. Purchase and Sale

2.1 The Buyer agrees to purchase from the Seller, and the Seller agrees to sell to the Buyer, the Shares for a total consideration of $185,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller to the Buyer at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. Closing

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other location as the parties may agree.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms, and other documents required to effect the transfer of the Shares, duly executed.

4. Conditions Precedent

4.1 The obligations of the parties under this Agreement are subject to the satisfaction of the Conditions.

4.2 The Conditions include, without limitation: (a) the absence of any material adverse change in the Company’s financial condition; (b) receipt of all necessary third-party consents; and (c) accuracy of the Warranties as of the Closing Date.

4.3 The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions.

4.4 The Closing shall occur only if the Conditions are satisfied or waived in writing by the Buyer.

4.5 If the Conditions are not satisfied by the date falling 150 days after the date of this Agreement, being 1 May 2027, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other.

9. Limitations on Liability

9.4 (De Minimis and Basket) The Seller shall not be liable in respect of any claim for breach of Warranty unless the aggregate amount of all such claims exceeds $1,850,000 (being 1.0% of the Purchase Price), in which case the Seller shall be liable only for the excess over that amount.

9.7 Any claim for breach of Warranty must be notified by the Buyer to the Seller in writing within 30 days of the date on which the Buyer first becomes aware of the matter giving rise to the claim. The notice shall specify in reasonable detail the nature of the claim, the factual basis for it and the amount of the excess claimed.

10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this transaction and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except as required by law or to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

10.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years.

11. Notices

11.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant party.

11.2 Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 115 Cannon Street, London.

11.3 Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 53 Moorgate, London.

11.4 Notices shall be deemed received when delivered (if by hand), 48 hours after posting (if by post), or upon electronic confirmation (if by email).

12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

12.2 The parties irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
