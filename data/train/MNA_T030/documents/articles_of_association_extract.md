# EXTRACT OF ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF DUNMORE ENERGY PLC

The following is an extract of the Articles of Association of Dunmore Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14192220, whose registered office is at 2 Cannon Street, London.

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

- “Board” means the board of directors of the Company;
- “Buyer” means Pemberton Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13462871, whose registered office is at 53 Moorgate, London;
- “Company” means Dunmore Energy plc, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14192220, whose registered office is at 2 Cannon Street, London;
- “Seller” means Whitlock Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14419323, whose registered office is at 115 Cannon Street, London;
- “Pemberton Logistics plc” means the entity incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15667852, whose registered office is at 80 Cannon Street, London;
- “Shareholder” means a person for the time being entered in the register of members of the Company as the holder of one or more shares;
- “Written Resolution” means a resolution in writing signed by all the directors for the time being entitled to receive notice of meetings of the Board.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.3 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. Share Capital and Dividends**

2.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is £100,000, divided into 100,000 ordinary shares of £1.00 each.

2.2 The Company may, by ordinary resolution, increase, divide, consolidate or re-divide its share capital or any part of it into shares of a larger or smaller amount.

2.3 Subject to the Companies Act 2006 and these Articles, dividends may be declared and paid by the Company in accordance with the recommendation of the Board.

**3. Board of Directors**

3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the Board, which may exercise all powers of the Company except such as are by the Companies Act 2006 or by these Articles required to be exercised by the Shareholders in general meeting.

3.2 The Board may delegate any of its powers to committees or individual directors, subject to any restrictions imposed by these Articles or by resolution of the Board.

3.3 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter.

**11. Quorum and Proceedings of the Board**

11.1 No business shall be transacted at any meeting of the Board unless a quorum is present.

11.2 The quorum for board meetings is three directors.

11.3 If within fifteen minutes after the time appointed for the meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting shall stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and place, or to such other day, time and place as the Board may determine.

11.4 A resolution in writing signed by all the directors for the time being entitled to receive notice of meetings of the Board shall be as valid and effective as if it had been passed at a meeting of the Board.

**4. Payment and Financial Provisions**

4.1 All payments made by or to the Company shall be made in accordance with the payment terms specified in relevant contracts, including without limitation any agreement between the Company and Pemberton Logistics plc.

4.2 The Company shall ensure that all invoices issued to Pemberton Logistics plc are settled within 60 days of the date of invoice.

**5. Notices**

5.1 Any notice required to be given to a director, Shareholder or officer of the Company shall be in writing and may be delivered personally, sent by first-class post or transmitted by email to the address or email address last notified in writing to the Company.

5.2 A notice sent by post shall be deemed to be served 48 hours after posting.

**6. Confidentiality**

6.1 Every director and officer of the Company shall maintain the confidentiality of all information concerning the Company’s business, including but not limited to contracts with Pemberton Logistics plc, financial data, and commercial arrangements with the Buyer and the Seller.

6.2 The obligations under this Article 6 shall continue after cessation of office.

**7. General**

7.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising under or in connection with these Articles.
