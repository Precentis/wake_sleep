MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF DUNMORE ENERGY PLC

HELD AT THE REGISTERED OFFICE OF THE COMPANY ON 16 MARCH 2027

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m.  

2. ATTENDANCE  
The following directors were present:  
- Mr. A. Finch, Director  
- Ms. R. Walsh, Director  

There are five directors on the board of Dunmore Energy plc. The presence of 2 directors constituted a quorate meeting pursuant to the company’s articles of association.  

3. CONFLICT OF INTEREST DISCLOSURE  
No declarations of interest in any agenda item were required.  

4. REVIEW OF PROPOSED TRANSACTION  
The Directors reviewed the proposed transaction whereby Whitlock Systems Ltd (the Seller), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14419323, registered office 115 Cannon Street, London, intends to sell the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Energy plc (the Company), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14192220, registered office 2 Cannon Street, London, to Pemberton Systems Ltd (the Buyer), incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13462871, registered office 53 Moorgate, London.  

The Directors were briefed on the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement dated 18 March 2027, which provides for the sale and purchase of 100% of the issued shares in the Company.  

5. RESOLUTION TO APPROVE ENTRY INTO SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
RESOLVED THAT the entry by the Company into the Share Purchase Agreement as contemplated for the acquisition of its entire issued share capital by the Buyer from the Seller be and is hereby approved.  

6. MATTERS RELATING TO MATERIAL CUSTOMER  
The Directors noted that Pemberton Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales, company number 15667852, registered office 80 Cannon Street, London, is a material customer of the Company.  

7. FURTHER ASSURANCES  
RESOLVED THAT any director or officer of the Company be, and hereby is, authorised to take all necessary or desirable steps to give effect to the resolutions passed at this meeting, including but not limited to executing documents, making filings, and instructing professional advisors, all in accordance with English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

8. ADJOURNMENT  
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:45 a.m.
