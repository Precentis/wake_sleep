ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 15 March 2027 (the “Effective Date”) between:

(1) Pemberton Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13462871, whose registered office is at 53 Moorgate, London (“Buyer”);

(2) Whitlock Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14419323, whose registered office is at 115 Cannon Street, London (“Seller”); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm of solicitors authorised in England and Wales, acting as escrow agent (“Escrow Agent”).

The Buyer, Seller and Escrow Agent are each referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below:

“Claim Notice” means a written notice served by the Buyer on the Seller and the Escrow Agent, specifying in reasonable detail the nature and basis of a claim against the Escrow Amount.

“Closing Date” means 18 March 2027.

“Company” means Dunmore Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14192220, whose registered office is at 2 Cannon Street, London.

“Escrow Amount” means the sum of £7,500,000 (seven million five hundred thousand pounds sterling) deposited by the Buyer with the Escrow Agent pursuant to Clause 3.

“Material Customer” means Pemberton Logistics plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 15667852, whose registered office is at 80 Cannon Street, London.

2. Escrow Deposit

2.1 On or prior to the Closing Date, the Buyer shall pay the Escrow Amount into an interest-bearing escrow account (the “Escrow Account”) held by the Escrow Agent for the benefit of the Parties in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in the Escrow Account and shall disburse it only in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

3. Disbursement of Escrow Amount

3.1 The Escrow Amount, or any part thereof, shall be released no earlier than 12 months after the Closing Date, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Buyer and the Seller.

3.2 Any release of funds prior to the expiry of such 12-month period shall require a joint written instruction signed by the Buyer and the Seller delivered to the Escrow Agent.

4. Notices

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, sent by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices shall be deemed to have been received: if delivered personally, at the time of delivery; if sent by post, 48 hours after posting; if by email, at the time of transmission, provided that no automated delivery failure is received.

5. Claims Against the Escrow Amount

5.1 The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by serving a Claim Notice on the Seller and the Escrow Agent.

5.2 A Claim Notice must be served within 45 days of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

5.3 Upon receipt of a Claim Notice, the Escrow Agent shall notify the Seller and hold the amount claimed pending resolution between the Parties or determination by a court of competent jurisdiction.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Escrow Agent shall keep confidential all information received in connection with this Agreement unless disclosure is required by law or by a regulatory authority.

6.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall survive termination of this Agreement.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

7.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
