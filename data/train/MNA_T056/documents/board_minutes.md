MINUTES OF A BOARD MEETING OF WHITLOCK MEDIA LTD  
Date: 10 June 2027  
Time: 10:00 a.m.  
Place: 109 King William Street, London, EC4R 9EN  

1. ATTENDANCE AND QUORUM  
1.1. The following directors were present:  
- Mr. A. N. Thompson  
- Ms. R. L. Carter  

1.2. The board noted that the Company has five (5) directors in total. The attendance of 2 directors constituted a quorum pursuant to the Company’s Articles of Association.  

1.3. The meeting was convened in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act 2006 and the Company’s Articles of Association.  

2. WELCOME AND APPROVAL OF AGENDA  
2.1. The Chair welcomed those present and confirmed the agenda had been circulated in due course.  
2.2. The agenda was approved as circulated.  

3. MINUTES OF PREVIOUS MEETING  
3.1. The minutes of the previous board meeting held on 12 May 2027 were reviewed.  
3.2. It was RESOLVED that the minutes be confirmed as a true and accurate record.  

4. TRADING UPDATE  
4.1. The Chief Executive Officer provided an update on current trading performance.  
4.2. Revenue for the quarter ending 31 March 2027 remained broadly in line with forecasts, with a 3.2% increase compared to the same period in the prior financial year.  
4.3. The board noted that 68% of revenue for the quarter was derived from contracts with Upton Media Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10531275, registered office 24 King William Street, London).  

5. LEGAL MATTERS  
5.1. The General Counsel reported that on 7 June 2027, the Company received a letter before action from Corrib Retail Group plc.  
5.2. The letter asserted a claim for approximately $1,200,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 14 February 2025.  
5.3. The board noted the contents of the letter and directed management to monitor the matter closely and report developments at the next board meeting.  

6. PROPOSED SALE OF SHARES  
6.1. The board considered a proposed transaction involving the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company by Holloway Systems Ltd (the Seller, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 12108743, registered office 58 Cheapside, London) to Ingleby Marine Ltd (the Buyer, incorporated in England and Wales, company number 14134882, registered office 67 Gresham Street, London).  
6.2. Legal and financial advisers provided a summary of the terms of the proposed Share Purchase Agreement, which is governed by English law and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.  
6.3. The board discussed the implications of the proposed transaction for the Company’s operations, employees, and commercial relationships.  

7. RESOLUTIONS  
7.1. It was unanimously RESOLVED:  
(a) That the board of Whitlock Media Ltd approve the entry by the Seller into the Share Purchase Agreement for the sale of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer;  
(b) That the officers of the Company be authorised to take all necessary steps to facilitate the transaction, including providing any required corporate documentation and co-operating with due diligence requests;  
(c) That the transaction be conducted under English law and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of England.  

8. ADJOURNMENT  
8.1. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
