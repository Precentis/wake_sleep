ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [●] June 2027 (the "Effective Date") between:

(1) Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 12108743, whose registered office is at 58 Cheapside, London (the "Seller");

(2) Ingleby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 14134882, whose registered office is at 67 Gresham Street, London (the "Buyer"); and

(3) [Escrow Agent], a firm authorised and regulated by [●], acting as escrow agent (the "Escrow Agent").

The parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday or Sunday on which banks are open in London.

"Closing Date" means 22 July 2027.

"Company" means Whitlock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10930406, whose registered office is at 109 King William Street, London.

"Escrow Amount" means the sum of £1,750,000 held by the Escrow Agent under this Agreement.

"Material Customer" means Upton Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 10531275, whose registered office is at 24 King William Street, London.

"Relevant Period" means the period of 12 months beginning on the Closing Date.

2. Establishment of Escrow

2.1 The Buyer and the Seller hereby appoint the Escrow Agent to hold the Escrow Amount on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Escrow Agent accepts appointment and agrees to hold and administer the Escrow Amount in accordance with the terms set out herein.

3. Purpose and Release Conditions

3.1 The Escrow Amount is held as security for the performance by the Seller of its indemnification obligations under the Share Purchase Agreement dated [●] June 2027 between the Buyer and the Seller for the acquisition of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

3.2 No release of the Escrow Amount shall occur except in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

4. Release Mechanism

4.1 The Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date falling 12 months after the Closing Date, save to the extent of pending claims notified before that date. For the avoidance of doubt, any amount subject to a bona fide claim properly notified in writing to the Escrow Agent and the Seller prior to such date shall remain held until final resolution or settlement of such claim.

4.2 Upon the later of (a) the end of the Relevant Period and (b) the resolution of all pending claims notified in accordance with Clause 4.1, the Escrow Agent shall release the balance of the Escrow Amount, if any, to the Seller by bank transfer to the account notified in writing by the Seller to the Escrow Agent.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential any information received under or in connection with this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or regulation, or with the prior written consent of the other parties.

6.2 This obligation shall survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.
