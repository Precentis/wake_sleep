SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 15 May 2027.

BETWEEN:

(1) Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12108743, whose registered office is at 58 Cheapside, London (the “Seller”);

(2) Ingleby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14134882, whose registered office is at 67 Gresham Street, London (the “Buyer”); and

(3) Whitlock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10930406, whose registered office is at 109 King William Street, London (the “Company”).

RECITALS:

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company.

(B) The Buyer wishes to purchase and the Seller wishes to sell the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

“Closing Date” means 22 July 2027;

“Confidential Information” means all information of a proprietary or confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether orally, electronically, visually or in writing;

“Material Adverse Effect” means any change, event or effect that, individually or in the aggregate with any other changes, events or effects, is materially adverse to the business, assets, financial condition or results of operations of the Company, but excluding any change, event or effect to the extent resulting from general economic, regulatory or market conditions;

“Purchase Price” means the aggregate sum of $200,000,000, to be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in accordance with Clause 2.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

2. SALE AND PURCHASE

2.1 The Seller hereby agrees to sell, and the Buyer hereby agrees to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company, free from all liens, charges, encumbrances and equities, for the Purchase Price of $200,000,000.

2.2 The sale and purchase of the shares shall take place at the Closing on the Closing Date, at which time the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price to the Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by the Seller at least five (5) Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

3. CLOSING

3.1 The Closing shall take place on the Closing Date at the offices of the Buyer or at such other location as the parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At the Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer:

(a) duly executed instruments of transfer in respect of the shares in the Company, properly stamped where required;

(b) original share certificates (if any) in respect of the shares in the Company;

(c) a certificate of non-foreign status under Section 1445 of the Internal Revenue Code, if applicable; and

(d) such other documents and instruments as the Buyer may reasonably require to evidence the transfer of the shares and the authority of the Seller to execute this Agreement.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Any notice or other communication required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class prepaid post or by email to the address or email address of the relevant party set out above, or such other address or email as a party may notify in writing.

4.2 Any notice delivered by hand shall be deemed received at the time of delivery. Any notice sent by first-class post shall be deemed received two (2) Business Days after posting. Any notice sent by email shall be deemed received at the time of electronic confirmation of receipt.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall not disclose such information to any third party, except:

(a) to its professional advisers on a need-to-know basis under a duty of confidentiality;

(b) as required by law or regulation; or

(c) with the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive the termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

6.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England.

6.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
