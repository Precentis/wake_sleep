ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF WHITLOCK MEDIA LTD  
(Extract Relating to Shareholder and Board Governance Provisions)

1. Interpretation and Definitions  
1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:  
(a) “Buyer” means Ingleby Marine Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14134882, with its registered office at 67 Gresham Street, London;  
(b) “Seller” means Holloway Systems Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 12108743, with its registered office at 58 Cheapside, London;  
(c) “Company” means Whitlock Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10930406, with its registered office at 109 King William Street, London;  
(d) “Share Sale” means the acquisition by the Buyer of the entire issued share capital of the Company from the Seller;  
(e) “Material Customer” means Upton Media Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10531275, with its registered office at 24 King William Street, London;  
(f) any reference to a statutory provision includes a reference to that provision as re-enacted, amended or replaced from time to time; and  
(g) words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

2. Share Capital and Transfer  
2.1 The issued share capital of the Company shall consist of ordinary shares, each ranked pari passu in all respects.  
2.2 Subject to compliance with applicable law and regulatory approvals, any transfer of shares in the Company shall be effected by a duly executed instrument of transfer and delivered to the Company’s registered office.  
2.3 The directors may, at their discretion, refuse registration of a transfer of shares without assigning any reason.

3. Board of Directors  
3.1 The business of the Company shall be managed by the board of directors (the “Board”), which shall have the authority to exercise all powers of the Company, subject to any limitations imposed by these Articles or by the Companies Act 2006.  
3.2 The Board may appoint such committees as it considers necessary and delegate any of its powers to such committees, provided that such delegation is in writing and recorded in the minutes of the Board.

4. Board Meetings and Proceedings  
4.1 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter to conduct the affairs of the Company.  
4.2 Written notice of not less than five business days shall be given to each director prior to any Board meeting, unless the meeting is convened as an emergency, in which case 48 hours’ notice shall suffice.  
4.3 Resolutions may be passed in writing by unanimous agreement of all directors entitled to vote, provided such written resolution is signed by each director or otherwise authenticated in accordance with the Company’s internal procedures.  
4.4 Article 11: The quorum for board meetings is three directors. If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, the meeting shall be adjourned to a date not more than seven days later, at which time the directors present shall constitute a quorum.

5. Financial Matters and Payments  
5.1 The Company shall maintain adequate financial records in accordance with the Companies Act 2006 and applicable accounting standards.  
5.2 All payments made by the Company in connection with contracts exceeding £100,000 in value shall require prior written approval of the Board.  
5.3 The Company shall not enter into any contract with a party that has a beneficial interest of more than 15% held by a director or a connected person without full disclosure and approval by disinterested directors.

6. Confidentiality  
6.1 All directors and officers of the Company shall maintain the confidentiality of proprietary information, including customer relationships, pricing strategies, and technical data, during and after their tenure.  
6.2 This obligation shall extend to information concerning the Material Customer and any contracts between the Company and the Material Customer, including but not limited to contracts dated on or about 14 June 2027.

7. Notices  
7.1 Any notice required to be given under these Articles shall be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the relevant party.  
7.2 Notices sent by pre-paid first-class post shall be deemed served 48 hours after posting.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction  
8.1 These Articles shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.  
8.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Articles.

9. Miscellaneous  
9.1 No variation of these Articles shall be effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of the Buyer and the Seller.  
9.2 These Articles, as modified by the Share Sale, shall remain in full force and effect unless otherwise terminated by written agreement of the parties.
