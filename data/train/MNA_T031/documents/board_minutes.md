MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF DUNMORE ENERGY PLC  
HELD AT THE REGISTERED OFFICE ON 6 JANUARY 2027  

1. CALL TO ORDER  
The meeting was convened at 10:00 a.m. at the registered office of Dunmore Energy plc, 27 King William Street, London. The Chairman, Mr. A. Patel, called the meeting to order and confirmed that notice had been duly given in accordance with the company’s articles of association.  

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF PREVIOUS MEETING  
The minutes of the board meeting held on 4 December 2026 were reviewed. Upon motion by Mr. Patel and seconded by Ms. L. Grant, the minutes were approved as a true and accurate record.  

3. DIRECTORS’ ATTENDANCE  
Present:  
- Mr. A. Patel (Chairman)  
- Ms. L. Grant  
- Mr. J. T. Reynolds  
- Dr. E. M. Clarke  

Apologies were received from Mr. R. S. Naylor.  

4. CHANGES IN SHAREHOLDING STRUCTURE  
The board reviewed the proposed acquisition of the entire issued share capital of Dunmore Energy plc by Kingsmere Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 11527399, registered office 97 Ludgate Hill, London) from Orwell Medical Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 10954318, registered office 71 Queen Victoria Street, London). The transaction remains subject to due diligence and expected completion by no later than 31 March 2027. The board acknowledged the change in control is expected to occur without disruption to operations.  

5. LEGAL CORRESPONDENCE  
The board noted a letter before action dated 3 January 2027 from Redcliffe Systems Ltd, addressed to Dunmore Energy plc, threatening legal proceedings for approximately $450,000 in respect of alleged defective deliveries under a supply agreement dated 14 June 2025. The letter asserts claims under English law and references potential proceedings in the courts of England. The board instructed management to monitor the matter closely, liaise with external counsel, and report at the next scheduled board meeting. No admission of liability has been made.  

6. CUSTOMER CONTRACT REVIEW  
The board reviewed ongoing contractual commitments with key customers. Particular attention was given to the supply agreement with Brightwater Systems Ltd (incorporated in England and Wales, company number 13275652, registered office 46 Bishopsgate, London), which remains in full force and extends for a further 18 months under its current terms. The board confirmed that all performance metrics under the agreement are being met, with no outstanding disputes reported.  

7. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE UPDATE  
The Chief Financial Officer presented unaudited financial results for the quarter ended 31 December 2026. Revenue was reported at £8.2 million, reflecting a 7% increase compared to the prior quarter. Operating expenditure remained within budgeted parameters.  

8. NEXT BOARD MEETING  
The next board meeting was scheduled for 3 February 2027 at 10:00 a.m. at the registered office.  

9. ADJOURNMENT  
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:45 a.m.
