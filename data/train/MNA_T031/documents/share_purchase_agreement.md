SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is dated 27 December 2026.

**PARTIES**

(1) **Kingsmere Medical Ltd** (company number 11527399), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 97 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer").

(2) **Orwell Medical Ltd** (company number 10954318), a company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 71 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller").

(3) The Buyer and the Seller are collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

**RECITALS**

(A) The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of **Dunmore Energy plc** (company number 14282416), a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales with its registered office at 27 King William Street, London (the "Company").

(B) The Seller has agreed to sell, and the Buyer has agreed to purchase, the entire issued share capital of the Company on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

**1. INTERPRETATION**

1.1 In this Agreement, the term "Material Adverse Change" means any event which has a material adverse effect on the business of the Company, excluding changes in general economic conditions, changes affecting the industry generally, and pandemics.

1.2 The terms "subsidiary", "holding company" and "company" have the meanings given in the Companies Act 2006.

1.3 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

**2. PURCHASE AND SALE**

2.1 The Purchase Price for the entire issued share capital of the Company is **$210,000,000** (two hundred and ten million US dollars), to be paid in accordance with Clause 2.2.

2.2 The Purchase Price shall be paid by the Buyer to the Seller in full and in cleared funds on the Closing Date to a bank account designated in writing by the Seller at least five Business Days prior to the Closing Date.

**3. CLOSING**

3.1 Closing shall take place on **31 March 2027** (the "Closing Date") at the offices of the Buyer or such other place as the Parties may agree in writing.

3.2 At Closing, the Seller shall deliver to the Buyer all share certificates, stock transfer forms and such other documents as are necessary to transfer legal and beneficial ownership of the entire issued share capital of the Company to the Buyer, free from all liens, charges, equities and third-party rights.

**4. NOTICES**

4.1 Any notice or communication under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first class post or email to the registered office of the relevant Party.

4.2 Notices sent by hand shall be deemed served upon delivery; notices sent by post shall be deemed served two Business Days after posting; notices sent by email shall be deemed served on the same day if sent during business hours, or the next Business Day if sent thereafter.

**5. CONFIDENTIALITY**

5.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all information received from the other Party pursuant to this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of the other Party.

5.2 This obligation shall survive termination or completion of this Agreement for a period of six years.

**6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES**

6.1 The Seller warrants that it is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the entire issued share capital of the Company and has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.2 All representations and warranties given under this Agreement shall survive the Closing for a period of 18 months.

**7. INDEMNITIES**

7.1 The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer against all losses arising directly from any breach of the warranties set out in Clause 6.

**8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

8.5 The aggregate liability of the Seller for any claims under the warranties contained in this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 10% of the Purchase Price, being **$25,200,000**. The Purchase Price is **$210,000,000** (see Section 2.1).

**9. CLAIMS PROCEDURE**

9.7 Any claim for breach of warranty must be notified in writing by the Buyer to the Seller within **30 days** of the Buyer becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the claim.

**10. NON-COMPETITION**

12.1 The Seller agrees that for a period of **5 years** from the Closing Date, it shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in or support any business that competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of this Agreement.

**11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
