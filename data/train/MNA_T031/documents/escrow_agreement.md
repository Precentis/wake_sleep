ESCROW AGREEMENT

This Escrow Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 5 January 2027 (the "Effective Date") by and among:

(1) Kingsmere Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11527399, whose registered office is at 97 Ludgate Hill, London (the "Buyer");

(2) Orwell Medical Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10954318, whose registered office is at 71 Queen Victoria Street, London (the "Seller");

(3) Dunmore Energy plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14282416, whose registered office is at 27 King William Street, London (the "Company");

(4) [Name of Escrow Agent], a firm duly authorised to act as escrow agent under English law, whose registered office is at [Address of Escrow Agent] (the "Escrow Agent").

Collectively, the Buyer, the Seller and the Company are referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

A. The Buyer has agreed to acquire from the Seller, and the Seller has agreed to sell to the Buyer, the entire issued share capital of the Company pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement dated as of even date herewith (the "SPA").

B. In connection with the SPA, the Parties have agreed that a portion of the purchase consideration shall be deposited into an escrow account to secure, inter alia, the Seller’s indemnification obligations under the SPA.

C. The Escrow Agent has agreed to hold and administer such funds in accordance with the terms set out herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1. "Closing Date" means 8 January 2027.

1.2. "Escrow Amount" means the sum of £4,200,000 (four million two hundred thousand pounds sterling), representing 7.5% of the total purchase consideration, which has been deposited by the Buyer into the escrow account maintained by the Escrow Agent.

1.3. "Escrow Account" means the designated interest-bearing account established by the Escrow Agent for the sole purpose of holding the Escrow Amount.

1.4. "Indemnification Claim" means any claim made by the Buyer pursuant to the indemnification provisions of the SPA.

2. Deposit and Holding of Escrow Amount

2.1. On the Closing Date, the Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount into the Escrow Account.

2.2. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Amount in accordance with this Agreement and disburse it only upon written instructions signed by both Buyer and Seller, or as otherwise directed by a final judgment of the courts of England.

3. Disbursement

3.1. The Escrow Amount, together with any accrued interest, shall be released in accordance with the terms of the SPA and this Agreement.

3.2. In the absence of any Indemnification Claim, the Escrow Amount shall be released to the Seller on the date which is 18 months after the Closing Date.

4. Notices

4.1. Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, pre-paid first-class post, or email to the registered office of the relevant Party or such other address as notified in writing.

4.2. Notices to the Buyer shall be sent to: 97 Ludgate Hill, London.

4.3. Notices to the Seller shall be sent to: 71 Queen Victoria Street, London.

4.4. Notices to the Escrow Agent shall be sent to: [Address of Escrow Agent].

5. Claims

5.1. The Buyer may make a claim against the Escrow Amount by delivering to the Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice specifying the nature and amount of the claim.

5.2. A claim against the Escrow Amount must be notified within 60 days of the Buyer becoming aware of it.

6. Confidentiality

6.1. The Escrow Agent shall maintain the confidentiality of all information received pursuant to this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party except as required by law or with the prior written consent of both the Buyer and the Seller.

7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

7.1. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

7.2. The courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
