**EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT EXTRACT**

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 5 January 2027 between:

(1) **Dunmore Energy plc** (the "Company"), incorporated in England and Wales under company number 14282416, whose registered office is at 27 King William Street, London; and  
(2) **The Founder** (the "Employee").

**1. Definitions and Interpretation**

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:  
"Affiliate" means, in relation to a person, any company or undertaking which is from time to time a subsidiary or parent undertaking of such person, or a subsidiary of such a parent undertaking, within the meaning of section 1161 of the Companies Act 2006;  
"Buyer" means Kingsmere Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 11527399, whose registered office is at 97 Ludgate Hill, London;  
"Company" means Dunmore Energy plc, as defined above;  
"Seller" means Orwell Medical Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 10954318, whose registered office is at 71 Queen Victoria Street, London;  
"Material Customer" means Brightwater Systems Ltd, incorporated in England and Wales under company number 13275652, whose registered office is at 46 Bishopsgate, London.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its construction.

**2. Employment**

2.1 The Company shall employ the Employee, and the Employee shall serve the Company, on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement, commencing on 8 January 2027 (the "Start Date").

**4. Remuneration**

4.1 The Company shall pay to the Employee a basic salary of £85,000 per annum (less all applicable deductions and withholdings), payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears on the last working day of each month.

4.2 In addition to the basic salary, the Employee shall be entitled to participate in any annual discretionary bonus scheme operated by the Company, subject to the terms and conditions of such scheme as may be in effect from time to time.

**7. Benefits**

7.1 The Employee shall be entitled to participate in such pension, health insurance, and other benefit schemes as the Company may make available to its senior executives, subject to the rules of such schemes.

**9. Confidentiality**

9.1 The Employee shall not at any time, whether during or after the termination of employment, without the prior written consent of the Company, divulge, use, or communicate to any third party any confidential information concerning the business, clients, suppliers, technology, financial affairs, or affairs of the Company or any of its Affiliates, including but not limited to information relating to the Material Customer.

9.2 This obligation shall continue after the termination of employment, save to the extent that such information becomes publicly available through no fault of the Employee.

**11. Notices**

11.1 Any notice required to be given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class post, or email to the registered office of the Company or to the last known residential address of the Employee.

11.2 Notices to the Company shall be addressed to the attention of the Chief Executive Officer at 27 King William Street, London.

**14. Termination**

14.1 This Agreement may be terminated by either party giving six months’ written notice to the other party.

14.2 The Company may terminate the employment summarily for gross misconduct without notice or payment in lieu.

**18. Non-competition**

18.1 The Employee acknowledges that, by virtue of their position as Founder, they have had access to confidential information and established significant relationships with customers and suppliers of the Company, including the Material Customer.

18.2 Accordingly, the Employee covenants that for a period of 18 months after the termination of their employment with the Company, however such termination arises, they shall not, whether directly or indirectly, alone or in conjunction with any other person, carry on or be engaged, concerned or interested in any business which is in competition with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of termination.

18.3 This restriction shall apply within a radius of 50 miles of any location in which the Company carries on business at the date of termination.

**19. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

19.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales.

19.2 The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
